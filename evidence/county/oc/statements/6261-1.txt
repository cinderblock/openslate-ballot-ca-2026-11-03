GLENN ACOSTA
Director, Trabuco Canyon Water District
My wife and I have lived in Trabuco Canyon for over 25 years and
raised our family here. Like many residents, my family has felt the
impact of rising water costs.
Much of TCWD’s water and sewer infrastructure is decades old and
requires significant reinvestment to maintain reliable service and
protect public health.
With infrastructure costs rising, we must explore consolidation with
SMWD and IRWD to determine whether economies of scale can
provide meaningful, long-term rate relief.
As a water resources engineer with 30 years of experience and
a master's degree in civil engineering from UC Irvine, I have the
expertise to guide the district through the important decisions
ahead.
I advocated repurposing underutilized district property for a
potential OCFA firefighting helicopter facility to strengthen wildfire
protection for our communities—at no cost to ratepayers.
It would be an honor to serve our community with the experience,
urgency, and commitment it deserves.
