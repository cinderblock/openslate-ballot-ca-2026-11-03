DONALD TORRES
AGE: 26
Councilmember
My name is Donald. I’m 26 and have lived in Stanton my entire
life. My parents are labor organizers, and I grew up on picket lines
and at negotiating tables. Watching workers stand together taught
me that change happens when people unite. That belief guides my
decisions on the City Council.
Serving Stanton as your Councilmember has been the privilege of
my life. At Western High School, I support at-risk students facing
depression and trauma, helping them stay in school and graduate.
I’ve spent my life serving the community that raised me, and I’ve
proven I’ll show up when Stanton needs me. On the Council, I
cast the deciding vote to make livestreamed meetings permanent,
helped place mayoral term limits before voters, and pushed to
increase Sheriff’s deputy staffing.
When immigrant neighbors faced uncertainty, I helped create a city
immigration resource webpage. During the chemical evacuation, I
went door-to-door helping neighbors get to safety and advocated
for accountability.
My priorities remain practical: strong public safety, a balanced
budget, and a diversified economy.
Stanton is my home, and its future is my priority. I’m asking
for your vote to keep fighting for every family. Learn more at
www.torresforstanton.org.
