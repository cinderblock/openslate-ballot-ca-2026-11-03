CHRIS LOWE
Educator/Businessowner/Parent
My children all graduated from or currently attend Placentia-Yorba
Linda Unified School District schools, just like I did. At Chapman
University, I currently teach graduate students and have served as:
Assistant Dean
Director of Executive Education
Founding Director of a master’s program in the College of
Business and Economics
At the Richard Nixon Foundation, my work includes developing
civics education platforms for students from 3rd grade to college
and launching the new American Civics Academy to expand civics
education nationally. I worked for Governor Pete Wilson, helping
launch California’s landmark 1996 Class Size Reduction Initiative.
As a former Mayor, I championed reopening an elementary school
and authored a civic history book and engagement program for 3rd
graders. Even in college, I cared deeply about education policy,
serving on the California State University System Board of Trustees.
I’m a concerned parent, educator, taxpayer, and small business
owner who is profoundly worried about the state of the Placentia-
Yorba Linda Unified School District: outstanding teachers leaving,
ongoing budget deficits, and frequent leadership turnover. Cal
State Fullerton is so concerned that they’ve stopped placing
student teachers in Placentia-Yorba Linda Unified School District
schools.
If elected as your Placentia-Yorba Linda Unified School District
Trustee, I will:
Protect academic opportunities
Recruit and retain great teachers
Prioritize special education
Strengthen school safety
Restore fiscal stability
