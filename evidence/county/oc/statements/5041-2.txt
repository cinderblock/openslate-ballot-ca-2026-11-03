ANNE HERTZ MALLARI
AGE: 63
Non-Profit CEO
As a former Mayor and City Council member, Non-Profit CEO, and
proud 32-year Cypress resident, I have dedicated my career to
serving our community through fiscal responsibility, collaboration,
and practical problem-solving.
As your Council member, I worked to keep Cypress one of the safest
cities in Orange County, invested in our streets and infrastructure,
supported local businesses, and ensured residents had a voice at
City Hall.
Today, Cypress faces important decisions about the future of
the racecourse property, economic development along Lincoln
Avenue, funding for public safety, and protecting the quality of
life that makes Cypress special. District 1 deserves leadership that
respectfully listens, responds, builds consensus and serves the
needs of the residents.
I chose to raise my family in Cypress because neighbors care for one
another. That same spirit guides my work every day as I help local
families remain safely housed and nourished, and it will continue to
guide my service on the City Council.
I will protect the character of our neighborhood as zoning decisions
arrive, support our police and first responders, strengthen our local
economy, and safeguard every taxpayer dollar.
I would be honored to earn your vote. To learn more, please visit
anneforcypress.com.
