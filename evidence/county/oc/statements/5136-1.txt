BENJAMIN YU
Councilmember, City of Lake Forest/Businessman
Serving as your City Councilmember has been one of the greatest
honors of my life. Over the past term, I have worked hard to be
accessible, accountable, and committed to the residents of Lake
Forest. Every decision I make is guided by a simple principle: put our
community first.
Together, we have continued to strengthen public safety, maintain
responsible fiscal stewardship, and preserve the high quality of life
that makes Lake Forest such a wonderful place to live, work and
play. I have remained focused on listening to residents, addressing
neighborhood concerns, supporting local businesses, and ensuring
that City Hall remains transparent and responsive.
While we have made meaningful progress, there is still
important work ahead. Our city continues to face challenges and
opportunities that require experienced, thoughtful leadership. I
am committed to building on the progress we have made—not
changing course, but continuing the steady, responsible leadership
that has served our community well.
As a U.S. Army veteran, entrepreneur, and your Councilmember,
I believe in service, accountability, and delivering results. I
respectfully ask for your vote so we can continue working together
to keep Lake Forest safe, fiscally strong, and an exceptional
community for current and future generations.
