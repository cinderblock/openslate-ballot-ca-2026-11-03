QUAN VUONG
Attorney/Educational Coach
I grew up in GGUSD. I went to Monroe Elementary, Ethan Allen
Elementary, Fitz Intermediate, and Los Amigos high school. I’m a
lawyer and Certified Public Accountant by profession. I am grateful
to the district that helped me become who I am today.
For over eight years, as a parent, I have served our school
community as Allen’s PTO Secretary and Treasurer and now as
Fitz's PTO President. Being on campus regularly gives me firsthand
perspective on how to better serve students and parents.
Alongside my wife, a GGUSD teacher, I have seen what makes
our schools work: teachers who give everything, staff who hold it
together, and families who show up for their children.
Professionally, I bring legal and financial expertise and a
commitment to accountability and transparency to every decision.
I am running because GGUSD gave me everything, and now it’s
my turn to give back. Our students deserve a board member who
has walked these halls, sat in these classrooms, and never stopped
showing up.
I am supported by three current board members, Bob Harden, Teri
Rocco and Lan Nguyen. I ask for your vote — not just to represent
our schools, but to serve the community that raised me.
