ELIZABETH "LIZ" DORN PARKER
Governing Board Member Coast Community College
District, Trustee Area 5
The district has a stellar reputation as a leading community college
district in the United States. The district’s finances are in the best
shape in decades. All colleges were reaffirmed accreditation for
their academic excellence. Orange Coast College was awarded
an additional accommodation for its outstanding programs and
leadership.
The district has expanded associate degree pathways, career
technical education and certificate programs. The colleges are
now providing additional opportunities for career advancement,
personal empowerment and transfer opportunities to our diverse
student populations.
I am dedicated to keeping our district focused on student success,
relying on our top-quality faculty, staff and administrators to move
us forward in the new AI world.
I will continue to engage with our community and business
partners to facilitate student success and maintain our institutional
effectiveness and continued fiscal stability.
I have deep roots in Newport/Costa Mesa communities. I was raised
in the district, my sons attended Newport-Mesa schools, I volunteer
as a commissioner with the city, and I have been in leadership and
governance roles at the local, state, and national levels.
As your elected governing board member, with your vote, I will
continue the outstanding work and financial excellence on behalf
of the students, the residents and taxpayers.
