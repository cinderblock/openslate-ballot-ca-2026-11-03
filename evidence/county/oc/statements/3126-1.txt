TRACY LA
Nonprofit Executive Director
Please join the teachers, nurses, and librarians of the Garden Grove
Education Association in supporting our campaign for a stronger,
more responsible Garden Grove Unified School District.
As a nonprofit director, county housing commissioner, and longtime
community organizer, I have spent over a decade working
to support Garden Grove Unified students and families. I’m
committed to fighting for our public education by investing in safe,
modern school facilities, supporting educators, expanding student
resources, and ensuring taxpayer dollars are spent responsibly.
I’m the proud daughter of Vietnamese refugees and a product of
public schools. As the first in my family to graduate from college, I
know firsthand how public education changes lives. Public schools
gave me the skills to propel my public service career and build a
nonprofit that empowers youth and fights for immigrant families.
I will always advocate for students to have the resources they need
to succeed. As Trustee, I will fight to expand:
Mental health and basic needs services,
Dual-immersion language programs,
Resources for students with special needs.
Our students, families, and school staff deserve leaders who will
fight for strong public schools. That’s why I’m running to represent
Trustee Area 4 on the GGUSD Board of Education.
