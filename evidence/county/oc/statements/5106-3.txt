STEVEN ALVES
Small Businessowner/Dad
With traffic getting worse, roads in need of repair, and a growing
budget deficit, we need to bring the focus back to what really
matters to the residents of District 5.
As your councilmember, I will bring disciplined, fiscally responsible
leadership, professionalism, and bridge-building skills honed over
years of doing business around the world. My priorities: balancing
the budget without cutting essential services; protecting public
safety; maintaining infrastructure; and keeping the University Park
Library open.
I have called Woodbridge home for 31 years, raising two children,
and volunteering for over 14 years in local Scouting. My wife and I
built our life together in this community, and we will celebrate our
30th anniversary in November.
I served as Chief of Staff to our Vice Mayor, working on Irvine's major
issues, including the budget deficit. I built strong relationships with
city staff and department directors, engaged with residents, and
supported local businesses.
I have successfully run my own international business since 2005,
and I serve on the Irvine Community Alliance Fund board.
District 5 deserves a representative with deep community roots,
real experience, and the judgment to protect what makes Irvine
work. I am ready to serve on day one.
