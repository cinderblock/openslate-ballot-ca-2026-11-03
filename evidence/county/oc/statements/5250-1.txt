LETITIA CLARK
Community College Director
Dear Neighbors,
As your Mayor and City Councilmember from 2016 to 2024, I led
Tustin through COVID-19, protected small businesses, supported
frontline workers, approved more than 2,100 new homes at
Tustin Legacy, and made City Hall more accessible through online
meetings and the Engage Tustin app.
Tustin is home. I graduated from Tustin High School, built my career
through education, culminating in earning my doctorate, and with
my husband we’ve proudly raised our three children here. Those
experiences shape every decision I make and keep me focused on
building a Tustin where every family can thrive.
I'm running for Mayor to put that experience to work again
with a clear plan for Tustin's future. That means supporting
local businesses, investing in parks, roads, housing, public safety,
emergency preparedness, and mental health services, while
partnering with our schools to expand mentorship and career
opportunities for young people. My goal is to make Tustin the
best city it can be for today's residents and the next generation,
including my own children.
You know my record, my values, and my commitment to this
community. I respectfully ask for your vote for Mayor on or before
November 3, 2026.
