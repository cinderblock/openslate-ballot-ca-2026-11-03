JESSICA GUERRERO
Governing Board Member, Anaheim Union High School
District, Trustee Area 1
I am a lifelong Anaheim resident, graduate of Anaheim public
schools, daughter of an AUHSD classified employee, and proud to
serve as your Trustee.
Together, we have strengthened our schools, expanded
opportunities for students, and built a brighter future for every child
in AUHSD.
We strengthened Career Technical Education pathways,
implemented the district's first-ever agreement to reduce class
sizes, expanded student resources, including laundry services
for students in need, opened school-based mental health and
wellness centers, and launched the Parent Ambassador Program to
strengthen family engagement.
None of this progress would have been possible without the
dedication of our teachers, classified employees, staff, parents,
families, and community partners.
I am committed to continuing this work by strengthening
partnerships that support the whole child, ensuring every student
feels safe, supported, and seen, and expanding 21st-century
learning opportunities that prepare students for today's workforce
and tomorrow's opportunities.
My career has been dedicated to education. I graduated from CSU
Fullerton and UC Irvine and now serve as a Senior Policy Advisor
for the County of Orange, connecting children and families with
educational resources.
I am proud to be From Our Schools, For Our Schools.
