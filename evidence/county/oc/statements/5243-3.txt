DEBORA DONATHAN
Educator
I am running to represent District 3 because residents deserve
safe neighborhoods, clean streets, responsive government, and
leadership that follows through.
I am an educator, parent, longtime family caregiver, and community
advocate. I hold a Bachelor of Social Work and a Master of
Education. These experiences have taught me to listen carefully,
navigate complex systems, solve problems, and stand up for people
when government is not working as it should.
My priorities are practical: improve neglected areas such as Katella
Avenue; strengthen public safety through visible Sheriff patrols and
better coordination among law enforcement, code enforcement,
businesses, and residents; address recurring problems along Beach
Boulevard; and ensure responsible development benefits the
neighborhoods already here.
I will scrutinize contracts and publicly funded programs, ask who
benefits, and challenge waste, conflicts of interest, and spending
that does not produce measurable results. Public money should
create real pathways to safety, employment, treatment, stability,
and independence while protecting individual rights.
City Hall should listen, explain its decisions, and treat every resident
with dignity. I am asking for your vote to bring independent
judgment, practical oversight, and determined advocacy to the
Stanton City Council.
