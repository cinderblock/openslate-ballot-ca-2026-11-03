MILES C. MATIAS
AGE: 33
IT Analyst/Instructor
I am a husband, father, educator, and information technology
professional who believes public service should begin with giving,
not taking. My family and I proudly call La Habra home, and I am
running for City Council to bring practical experience, fresh ideas,
and accountable leadership to City Hall.
I support reasonable term limits because elected office should be
temporary public service, not a lifelong career. New voices and fair
opportunities to serve strengthen local government. I will advocate
for a meaningful term-limits measure.
If elected, I will not personally keep City Council compensation. I am
running because I love this community and believe public service
should be rooted in sacrifice, integrity, and commitment rather than
personal gain.
I hold bachelor's and master's degrees in information technology
and management, teach college technology courses, and have
experience in private industry, state government, healthcare,
cybersecurity, and public service. I will focus on fiscal responsibility,
public safety, reliable infrastructure, transparent government,
strong local businesses, quality parks, support for families, and
responsive city services.
I would be honored to earn your trust and respectfully ask for your
vote.
