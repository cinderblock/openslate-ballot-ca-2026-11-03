DAN HAY
Orange County Commissioner/Businessowner
For over 30 years, Fountain Valley has been my home. My wife,
Carrie, and I raised our four children here. I got my start coaching
kids in Fountain Valley Pony Baseball, and spent decades coaching
young athletes and building local businesses. My business gives
young athletes a platform to earn college scholarships while
bringing jobs and economic growth to Fountain Valley.
I'm running for City Council because I want to keep Fountain Valley
the safe, family-friendly city we all love.
I will support our police and firefighters, ensuring they have the
resources to keep our neighborhoods and schools safe, while
investing in neighborhood watch and youth outreach programs.
I will champion our small businesses by cutting red tape and
streamlining city permits, and work to revitalize our commercial
corridors so Fountain Valley remains a place where families and
businesses can thrive.
I will fight to preserve the quality of life that makes Fountain
Valley special, including stronger oversight of sober living homes
to protect our neighborhoods and ensure responsible development
that fits our community.
As a business owner, coach, and lifelong community volunteer,
I've spent my life building things that last. I'll bring that same
commitment to City Hall.
I respectfully ask for your vote.
