CARLOS RODRIGUEZ
AGE: 55
Mayor/Business Executive
Yorba Linda has been my home for over two decades and I’m
honored to serve the people of our community.
As your Mayor, I’ve worked hard to deliver results that strengthen
the family-friendly quality of life which makes Yorba Linda such an
amazing community. Our successes include:
Public Safety: Supporting the highest level of law enforcement
protection for our neighborhoods has helped Yorba Linda become
one of California’s safest communities.
Fire Protection: Expanding our city’s access to advanced fire-
fighting technologies, such as aerial heli-hydrants, to improve
wildfire prevention.
Education and Parks: Opening our exceptional City Library and
Cultural Arts Center. Expanding youth recreational amenities like
the Adventure Playground.
Affordability and Public Health: As your representative on the Air
Quality Management Board, I have worked successfully to reduce
pollution while stopping excessive regulations that increase the
cost of living for families and seniors.
Housing: I oppose California’s flawed high-density housing
requirements. Local residents, not Sacramento politicians, should
determine the housing needs of our city.
On a personal note, I’m a husband and father of two children
attending Yorba Linda High School. I have been active in coaching
youth sports. I respectfully ask for your vote to continue serving
our community. www.Carlos4YL.com
