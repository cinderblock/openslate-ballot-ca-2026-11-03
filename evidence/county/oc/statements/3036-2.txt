CHLOE SERRANO
Education Policy Advisor
My experience working with community colleges, public policy, and
local government will help guide our district through the challenges
facing higher education while keeping students at the center of
every decision.
As your community college trustee, I will work to:
Expand workforce development and career education
programs that prepare students for well-paying careers while
strengthening partnerships with local employers.
Address declining enrollment by reaching out to former students,
adult learners, and working families, making it easier to return to
college and complete a degree or certificate.
Support student success by expanding academic support,
transfer opportunities, mental health resources, and basic needs
programs that help students stay enrolled and graduate.
Champion first-generation college students by improving access
to counseling, financial aid, and mentoring so every student has
the opportunity to succeed.
Strengthen services for veterans by supporting programs that
help them transition to college, earn credentials, and enter the
workforce.
Ensure the district remains financially responsible while
maintaining high-quality instruction and preparing students for
the changing workforce of tomorrow.
As a UCLA graduate, former Fullerton College Student Trustee, and
California higher education policy advocate, I have dedicated my
career to expanding educational opportunity and helping students
succeed.
