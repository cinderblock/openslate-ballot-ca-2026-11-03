NARGES RAKIN
AGE: 56
College Dean/Educator
I'm Narges Rakin — an educator, a professor, a parent, and an
advocate who has spent nearly three decades working inside
California's community colleges, putting students first as a tenured
professor, a faculty union negotiator, and now a Dean. That depth
of experience is an asset, not just a resume line because I know this
system inside and out, from the classroom to the negotiating table
to the administration building, and I know where the gaps between
promises and practice tend to show up.
I'm running for the SOCCCD Board of Trustees because I believe
our district's budget and decisions belong to the public, not just
the boardroom. Too often, families are asked to trust institutions
without being shown the reasoning behind them. I don't think that's
good enough.
As a board member, I will push for open, plain-language budget
reporting that any parent or taxpayer can actually read. I will
prioritize outside grant and workforce-development funding to
grow academic programs, protecting the general fund as a last
resort. And I will insist that every decision can stand up to a direct
question from a student, a parent, or the public — asked openly,
and answered honestly.
I ask for your vote.
