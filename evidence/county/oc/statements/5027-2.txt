SHARENA WALKER
Director of Marketing
A strong community begins with neighbors who feel connected.
For more than 20 years, I have called Buena Park home. In serving
eight years as a Commissioner, I have helped support programs and
community traditions that bring neighbors together, celebrate our
diversity, and enrich the quality of life that makes Buena Park a
special place to live.
Professionally, I serve as Director of Marketing Communications
for Joneca Company, where I've learned to think strategically,
bring ideas to life, build consensus among diverse stakeholders,
and manage projects with accountability and attention to detail.
Just as important, my lifelong passion for the arts has taught me
that creativity and innovation often reveal solutions that others
overlook. I believe the strongest leadership combines practical
thinking with the courage to imagine new possibilities.
I am passionate about enhancing our quality of life, strengthening
public safety and our city's emergency preparedness, and
cultivating a community where people feel connected and valued.
I see serving on the City Council as an opportunity to give back to
the city that has given me so much. I believe the best ideas begin
with listening, and I will always lead with integrity, collaboration,
and a genuine commitment to Buena Park residents.
