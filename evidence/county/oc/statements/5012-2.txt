BLAKE PEREZ
AGE: 35
Public Affairs Manager
My name is Blake Perez, and I respectfully ask for your vote for Brea
City Council.
I am a homegrown candidate. For more than 26 years, Brea
has been my home. I attended our public schools, from Arovista
Elementary to Brea Olinda High School. I earned my Eagle
Scout award with a local troop. I am completely shaped by this
community. And now I am working hard to raise my daughter in this
community too. And I am running to make sure her future can be
shaped by a Brea just as great as I had.
My priorities are safe neighborhoods, a strong local economy,
and livable infrastructure, all grounded in fiscal responsibility,
transparency, and meaningful community engagement. Those are
the fundamentals of good local government, and they will guide
every decision I make.
Professionally, I serve as a Public Affairs Manager, working with
local governments, businesses, and community organizations to
solve problems, build partnerships, and deliver practical results. I
have also served Brea for the past eight years on the Cultural Arts
Commission and Planning Commission.
Together, we can prepare Brea for the future while protecting what
makes it special today.
