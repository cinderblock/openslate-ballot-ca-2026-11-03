GEORGE AZER
Small Business Owner
I’ve made Lake Forest my home. As the owner of a local small
business I know what actually takes to meet a budget without
cutting corners. That’s the perspective I’ll bring to the city council.
I’ll fight for real solutions to traffic congestion and infrastructure,
not just studies. I’ll support our sheriff's deputies and push for
smart, compassionate approaches to homelessness that keep our
neighborhoods safe. And I will insist on the same fiscal discipline
I use running my own business-no wasteful spending, no empty
promises.
I’m not a career politician. I’m a neighbor, a small business owner,
And a dad of three kids who wants District 2 to stay the kind of place
where families choose to put down roots, because mine did.
I’d be honored to earn your vote and put my experience and what I
learned in Law school to work for you and our community.
“George” Giurguis Azer
