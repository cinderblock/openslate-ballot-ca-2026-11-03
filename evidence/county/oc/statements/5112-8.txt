EDWARD BAYUK
Retired Business Entrepreneur
Laguna Beach has been my home for more than 30 years. During
that time, I have been committed to serving our community through
business leadership, public service, and civic involvement.
My career has included executive leadership in the energy,
communications, and technology industries, where I gained
experience in strategic planning, responsible budgeting, and
organizational leadership. I have also been honored to serve
through appointments to the California New Motor Vehicle Board,
the California Housing Finance Agency Board of Directors, and the
Nevada-California High Speed Rail Board. Locally, I have proudly
served as a Trustee and Board Member of the Laguna Art Museum.
I am running for City Council because I believe Laguna
Beach deserves experienced, independent leadership that values
transparency, fiscal responsibility, public safety, emergency
preparedness, environmental stewardship, and thoughtful
planning. Our city is unique, and I am committed to preserving
its character while ensuring it remains financially strong,
environmentally responsible, and an exceptional place to live, work,
and visit.
Together, we can preserve what makes Laguna Beach extraordinary
while planning responsibly for its future. I would be honored to earn
your trust, your support, and your vote.
