MARY PANINGSORO
AGE: 67
Community Volunteer
I’m a proud La Palma resident of 32 years. My husband Michael and
I have 3 children, Cody, Cheyenne, and Dillon. All were educated
here in La Palma schools. Supporting the youth of La Palma has
been volunteer role for over 25 years. I have held leadership roles
during 16 years of being involved in the Parent Teacher Student
Organization and Parent Teacher Association. I served as a Board
Member for 8 years on the John F. Kennedy Scholarship Foundation.
I’ve also been committed to serving our city. My dedication to
public service includes 6 years on the La Palma Pageant Committee
and presently a Community Activities & Beautification Committee
Member for 4 years. I’ve supported community traditions such as
the La Palma Parade, The Festival of Nations, Concert’s in the
Park and many others. These experiences have strengthened my
commitment to want to give back and help future generations
thrive.
On City Council I would strive to maintain infrastructure, safeguard
our city’s fiscal health assuring our taxpayers money is spent
wisely and fight to keep our city and schools safe! As a longtime
resident, parent, volunteer and community advocate, I’d be honored
to continue serving the residents of La Palma as a La Palma City
Council Member.
