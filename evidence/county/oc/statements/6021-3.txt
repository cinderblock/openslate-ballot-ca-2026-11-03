KONYA VIVANTI
Environmental Programs Manager
As a Board Member for the Rossmoor Community Services
District Board, I bring experienced, accountable, and transparent
leadership that puts our residents first.
With 32 years of experience in local government, I have worked
successfully with county departments and other public agencies
to deliver effective solutions through strong intergovernmental
cooperation. My background in public works operations includes,
Parks
Urban Forestry
Street Sweeping
Solid Waste and Recycling
Budget Administration
Grant Management
Giving Rossmoor the practical experience needed to oversee
essential community services.
I will work to improve transparency, strengthen communication
between the Rossmoor Community Services District Board and
residents, and ensure that decisions are made openly and with
meaningful public input.
I believe every tax dollar should be spent responsibly and
efficiently. I will promote sound financial management, careful
budgeting, and cost-effective solutions that protect our community
while maintaining the high level of services Rossmoor residents
expect.
I am also committed to expanding community involvement
by working cooperatively with the Rossmoor Homeowners
Association, local schools, youth sports organizations, and other
community partners. Together, we can preserve Rossmoor's
outstanding quality of life, strengthen community relationships,
and ensure our district remains a safe, attractive, and well-managed
place to live for generations to come.
