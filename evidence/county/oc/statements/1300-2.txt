VICTOR HERNANDEZ
GREEN
Account Sales Manager
Victor Hernandez is a lifelong resident of Orange County, born
in Santa Ana, now living in Brea. Victor's vision places working
families over corporate greed and ensures resident status never
dictates quality of life. He fiercely advocates for affordable housing,
healthcare for all, defending our communities, taking money out of
politics, and protecting our natural resources. He is ready to amplify
our community's voice at the State Assembly. And Victor will serve
his constituents without taking a dime of corporate or SuperPAC
money—ever.
The son of immigrants, he co-developed a low-cost mobile
healthcare service with his wife to support under-served children
and adults, prioritizing and protecting their health and safety. As
a board member of the California United for Single Payer (CUSP),
Victor advocates for the CalCare universal healthcare bill. He
builds coalitions by coordinating and supporting campaigns, direct
actions, and mutual aid with multiple community organizations.
Victor helped revive the Orange County Green Party, and his
activism and community-building include reaching out across the
political spectrum to find common ground.
District 59 deserves a State Assemblymember who will work to
earn votes, never taking them for granted.Victor strongly believes
elected officials should represent, not ignore, the people they
serve–Victor is fully ready to show District 59 what true service
looks like! He'll use his years of organizing experience across a wide
range of goals and interests to build a coalition that listens and
answers to the wonderful communities which comprise our district.
