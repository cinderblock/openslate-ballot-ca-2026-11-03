MATTHEW TERRY
AGE: 37
Architect
I've lived my whole life in District 1. My family has lived in the district
since the '60s, and now I'm raising three kids here. This place means
a lot to me.
When the gas station on PCH wanted to expand into the residential
neighborhood, I was at the hearings fighting it. When it came before
the Council for a vote, I helped send out mailers and built a website
that directed over 60 emails and over 125 signatures to the Council
against it.
When the city held hearings on water rate increases, I was there too,
trying to convince the Council of a better way to apply rates that
would lower residents' bills from what was being proposed, without
deferring maintenance.
We are at a crossroads as a city. For the past ten years the city's
go-to solution for additional revenue to cover an ever-increasing
budget has been sales tax increases. State law won't let them
raise sales tax any further, so now City Hall's top idea for new
revenue is billboards. If billboards are the best they can come up
with, they have hit rock bottom. I have better ideas. They're at
mattforsealbeach.com.
