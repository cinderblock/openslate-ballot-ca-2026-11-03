DAVID BURKE
AGE: 45
Cypress City Councilmember
Cypress is a great place to live, and you deserve leaders who get
the big things right, but don't let the small things slide.
As your representative, I supported investments in police personnel
and technology to keep Cypress safe. As mayor, I presided over a
budget that resulted in a $3 million surplus.
Under my leadership, we have also delivered more responsive,
transparent government. We significantly increased free bulky item
pickups for residents. We passed laws to protect our elections from
dark money. And we restored civility to our council after years of
turmoil and lawsuits.
In District 1, I have worked to improve neighborhoods with street
and traffic safety enhancements, and stronger penalties for illegal
dumping.
But together we can do even more, including streamlined
city services, improving park playgrounds, smart infrastructure
upgrades, and bringing new dining, retail, and entertainment
options to our city. I am running to continue to serve as your voice
as we build a brighter future for Cypress!
I am honored to be endorsed by current and former mayors, council
members, school board members, community leaders, and our
Orange County Professional Firefighters.
