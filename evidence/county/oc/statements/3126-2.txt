JOHN GIANG NGUYEN
Small Businessowner/Parent
As a father, business owner, and longtime community volunteer,
I am committed to ensuring every Garden Grove Unified student
receives an excellent education in a safe environment that prepares
them for college, careers, military service, and life.
I have volunteered in the Garden Grove Unified School District
for over 13 years, including seven years as Parent Association
President at La Quinta High School. Working alongside parents,
teachers, and administrators has given me firsthand experience
with the opportunities and challenges facing our schools.
For more than 30 years, I have mentored young people through the
Naval Sea Cadet Corps, where I served 10 years as a Lieutenant
Junior Grade, as well as the Boy Scouts, Girl Scouts, TNTT, GDPT,
and other youth organizations, helping instill leadership, discipline,
and character.
As your Trustee, I will focus on academic excellence, safe schools,
fiscal responsibility, and meaningful parental involvement. I will
support career education, internships, mentorships, and workforce
partnerships while supporting our teachers and neighborhood
schools, ensuring every student has the opportunity to succeed.
I am proud to have the support of Orange County Supervisor Janet
Nguyen and Fountain Valley Mayor Ted Bui, and I respectfully ask
for your vote.
