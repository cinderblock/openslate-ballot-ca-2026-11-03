RAMA R. MALINE
State Prosecutor/Professor
It's time for a leader who listens. Elect Rama Maline to the NOCCCD
Board of Trustees.
The NOCCCD Board has too often ignored the community it serves.
I'm running to represent Cypress, Los Alamitos, Stanton, Seal Beach,
Garden Grove, Westminster, and Anaheim with a more logical
approach that puts students and community first.
For nearly 30 years, I've served California as a state prosecutor,
taking on organized crime and fraud, securing more than thirty
published legal opinions. That same commitment to accountability
and the rule of law will guide my work on the Board.
For nearly 20 years, I've also taught legal courses, mentoring
students into the workforce and law school. I know what students
need: rigorous instruction, real-world skills, meaningful career
opportunities, and a fiscally sound institution.
As Trustee, I'll prioritize student success and workforce readiness at
Fullerton College, Cypress College, and North Orange Continuing
Education, insist on fiscal responsibility and transparency, and
expand career and technical training.
As former Treasurer of my statewide attorneys' association, I grew
our reserves from $500,000 to over $6,000,000 — a 1,000%
increase — proof I deliver results, answering only to students,
faculty, and taxpayers.
I respectfully ask for your vote.
