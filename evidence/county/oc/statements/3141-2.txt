SHAHEEN SHEIK-SADHAL
Attorney/Parent
I am running for the Laguna Beach Unified School District Board
because our schools deserve steady, thoughtful leadership focused
on students, educational excellence, transparency, and ethical
governance. I am deeply grateful for the opportunities public
education gave me as I experienced first-hand how strong public
schools can change the trajectory of a child’s life.
Laguna Beach is my home. My husband and I chose this community
because we believed in its schools, its creativity, and its deep
commitment to public education. I am the proud parent of two
LBUSD students.
Over the past eight years, I have served our district through
PTA leadership at both the elementary and middle school levels,
SchoolPower, district committees, and countless hours working
alongside teachers, administrators, and families. Those experiences
have given me a deep understanding of both what makes LBUSD
exceptional and what is required to protect and strengthen this
treasured public good for future generations.
Professionally, I am a corporate attorney and founder of a law
practice advising growing companies on future-forward strategy,
governance, and innovative leadership. My work requires listening
carefully, building consensus among diverse stakeholders, solving
complex problems, and making decisions grounded in both facts
and long-term thinking.
I believe our district must build a thoughtful long-term vision for
success for students of all capabilities, one that supports excellent
teachers, strengthens curriculum coherence across every stage
of a child’s education, activates a child’s inherent curiosities, and
prepares students not only for academic achievement, but for
meaningful lives as communicators, collaborators, critical thinkers,
and engaged citizens in a rapidly changing world. That can only
happen on a foundation of trust and coalition-building between and
among personnel, students, parents, and community stakeholders,
which will be my highest priority.
School board service should put students above politics and
steward tax dollars responsibly by shaping a world-class public
education system, because strong schools produce strong
communities. Our children benefit when adults lead with integrity,
stability, collaboration, and a shared commitment to public
education. Laguna Beach schools have long been a source of pride
for our community, and I believe we must protect that legacy while
thoughtfully preparing students for the future.
As a parent deeply invested in this district and a professional
experienced in governance and strategic planning, I will bring a
calm, solutions-oriented, and student-centered approach to the
board.
I would be honored to earn your support.
Shaheen Sheik-Sadhal
Candidate for LBUSD School Board
