DAVE MIN
DEMOCRATIC
United States Representative/Father
Why have superPACs spent over $4.5 million opposing Dave Min?
Maybe it’s because Congressman Dave Min refuses to take
corporate PAC donations and has spent his career fighting
corruption and working to expand economic opportunity. Dave
began his career prosecuting Wall Street fraud at the U.S. Securities
and Exchange Commission. He later taught at UC Irvine School of
Law and testified six times before Congress on issues related to
financial fairness and stopping fraud.
Or perhaps it’s that since joining Congress in January 2025,
Dave’s led the efforts to stand up to the Trump administration’s
unprecedented attacks on our democracy and the rule of law, while
fighting to lower costs. Dave opposed Congressional Republicans'
efforts to raise healthcare costs and has repeatedly voted against
ICE funding, called for criminal investigations into ICE misconduct
and opposed ICE’s efforts to open an office in Irvine.
It could be because Dave has been a relentless force against
corruption in our government. Dave chairs the Anti-Corruption Task
Force and sponsored legislation to end stock trading by members of
Congress and White House officials. As part of the House Oversight
Committee, Dave helped force the release of the Epstein files.
Dave is backed by dozens of local Orange County leaders you
trust. Police officers and firefighters have endorsed Dave because
of his strong pro-safety record. He's also endorsed by Planned
Parenthood and Sierra Club and teachers, nurses, and healthcare
workers.
Dave and his wife are raising their three kids here in the OC and
working every day to make sure every child gets to grow up with
the same opportunities and freedoms he had.
