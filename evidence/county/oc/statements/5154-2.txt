SHELLEY HASSELBRINK
City of Los Alamitos, Councilwoman/Businesswoman
It has been an honor to serve Los Alamitos, the community I have
proudly called home since 1993, where my husband and I raised our
two sons. I respectfully ask for your vote to continue that work.
I have served on the City Council since 2014 and was entrusted
by my colleagues to serve as Mayor in 2017, 2022, and 2026. As a
member of the Orange County Fire Authority Board of Directors, I
help safeguard the emergency services our families depend on. As
founder of the Los Alamitos Community Foundation, I have worked
to strengthen the programs and partnerships that make our city
thrive.
My background in finance, along with an MBA and doctoral studies
in Organizational and Leadership Development, gives me the
experience to protect our city's financial stability, fund essential
services, and ensure every taxpayer dollar is spent wisely.
I believe in responsible growth, public safety, and preserving the
small-town character we cherish. My priorities remain balanced
budgets, well-maintained infrastructure, and a government that
listens and responds to you.
Thank you for your trust and support.
