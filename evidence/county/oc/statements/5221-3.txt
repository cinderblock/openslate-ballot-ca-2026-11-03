VALERIE AMEZCUA
Mayor
Valerie Amezcua ran for Mayor four years ago with the promise to
listen to Santa Ana residents and work hard every day to improve
and protect the quality of life in our neighborhoods.
As our Mayor, Valerie’s delivered on her promises and successfully
worked to:
Combat crime, improve 911 response times, and strengthen police
oversight.
Enhance parks and expand safe routes to school programs.
Reduce homelessness by focusing on mental health and housing.
Help Santa Ana businesses grow and create family-wage jobs.
Re-open the Newhope Library and open the new Delhi Library.
Repair streets and clean up blighted properties.
Ensure transparency at City Hall and responsibly manage tax
dollars.
Prior to her election as Mayor, Valerie served two terms on the
Santa Ana Unified School Board where she improved graduation
rates and opened Wellness Centers at all 56 of our neighborhood
schools. She’s a mom and grandmother who worked for 30 years as
a Supervising Probation Officer in Orange County.
“Santa Ana residents deserve a Mayor who will listen to them and
work hard to deliver results at City Hall. I hope to earn your vote and
continue serving our city.”
