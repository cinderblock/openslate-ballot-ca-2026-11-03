DANIEL MORGAN
AGE: 43
Business Owner/Parent
I'm Daniel Morgan, a small-business owner, husband, and father of
two daughters in our local schools. I've called Costa Mesa home
for almost 20 years, and I believe local government should solve
problems, spend responsibly, and deliver results.
As Managing Principal of Marterra, I help local families and
homeowners every day. On Costa Mesa's Finance and Pension
Advisory Committee, I've advocated for responsible budgeting and
long-term financial stability without asking taxpayers to pay more.
I've also served as a county planning commissioner, a Big Brother,
and on the board of Let's Be Kind.
I'm running to keep Costa Mesa safe, affordable, and fiscally
responsible. That means reducing property crime, strengthening
community policing, streamlining permitting so businesses can
open faster, and investing in the parks, streets, and infrastructure
that improve everyday quality of life.
I believe City Hall should deliver results, solve problems, and be
accountable to the people it serves. I'd be honored to earn your
vote.
