JO SHADE
AGE: 60
Incumbent
30 years in Rossmoor I have dedicated myself to our community
on many levels.
I am married with one daughter and she was the motivation for
my working to preserve and engage in our beautiful community.
I spent 9 years on the St. Hedwig School Board including serving
as PTO President and the Autumfest Executive and Church Boards.
We moved to Los Al High School and I served on several Boards,
including the PTA, and as Booster President notably. I also served
on Los Alamitos Chamber Board of Directors and the Rossmoor
Homeowners Assoc .Board of Directors, I can say I am the only
current Director that has served on both the RHA and RCSD, and
I am a member of the Rossmoor Women's Club. I only have the
interest of our community at heart and serve diligently. You will find
me at our local meetings and events for Rossmoor, Los Alamitos
City and School District, supporting all and staying informed on all
levels to ensure Rossmoor continues the excellence level we are
accustomed to. I wish to continue my service on the RCSD Board of
Directors.
