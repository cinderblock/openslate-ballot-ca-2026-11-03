AVELINO VALENCIA
DEMOCRATIC
California State Assemblymember
Dear Neighbor,
I’m running for State Senate to empower working families, uplift
our most vulnerable neighbors, and ensure state government is
focused on doing the most good for the greatest number of people.
My agenda includes working to:
Build more housing and help first-time homebuyers.
Improve public schools and lower the costs of college.
Lower homelessness by focusing on mental health and addiction.
Protect our environment and ensure cleaner air.
Help Orange County businesses create good-paying jobs.
Improve transparency and combat political corruption.
I grew up in Orange County as the son of immigrant parents
who built a successful small business. After playing and coaching
college football, I returned home to devote my life to public service.
Prior to my election to the State Assembly, I was an Anaheim City
Councilmember and served in various roles in state government.
I earned a bachelor’s degree from San Jose State University and
a master’s degree in Public Management from Johns Hopkins
University. My high school sweetheart and now wife, Dr. Monica
Valencia, is a local elementary school principal.
My endorsers include the California Teachers Association,
California Professional Firefighters, and United Nurses Associations
of California. I’m also honored to have the support of elected,
community, education, and business leaders from across Southern
California.
You can learn more about me at www.AvelinoValencia.com and
contact me at Avelino@AvelinoValencia.com with any questions or
ideas. I hope to earn your vote.
In Service,
Avelino
