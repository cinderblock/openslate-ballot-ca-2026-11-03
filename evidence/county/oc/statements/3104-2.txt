WENDY CRINER
Teacher
My name is Wendy Criner, and I am running for Brea Olinda School
Board, Area 4. My entire career has been centered on supporting
our youth–in the classroom as a public school teacher, on the pool
deck and the field as a coach, and at the decision-making table on
the School Site Council. With your support, I’m ready to bring that
experience to the school board.
My husband and I moved to Brea over 15 years ago for its
exceptional schools. Our two daughters attended Falcon Academy,
Brea Junior High, and Brea Olinda High School. I believe in practical,
thoughtful solutions that support student growth and foster critical
thinking, problem-solving, collaboration, and global readiness.
As a district, we must address complex challenges responsibly. As
we move forward with Measure H projects, it is vital that funds
are invested with fiscal prudence to ensure safe, healthy, and
supportive learning environments for all students.
Our schools are the heart of Brea. Our community deserves a
leader who understands both the classroom and local values. I am
committed to protecting our investments, empowering teachers,
and putting children first. I respectfully ask for your vote.
