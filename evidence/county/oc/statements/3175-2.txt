CHIP AHLSWEDE
Parent/Businessman
Our Orange Unified School District Board of Directors has been
through a lot recently, swinging from extremes and taking its eyes
off its purpose, to educate school kids, keep them safe while at
school, and prepare them for an increasingly challenging world.
I am running for OUSD School Board for these reasons only:
to get our District focused back on these priorities,
stop the politics, and
give our children the best opportunity for the life they deserve.
I am a lifelong Orange County resident. My wife and I have raised our
three sons here in the community. They are all the product of our
local schools. These schools have been very important to our family,
which is why I have served on the School Site Council at elementary
and middle schools and the high school football booster club. I am
also a local businessman, working to make housing affordable and
attainable for working families.
I believe:
It’s not acceptable that OUSD test scores rank near the worst in
Orange County.
It’s not right that the current Board has mismanaged public
dollars without accountability or transparency to parents and
taxpayers.
We can do better. I’d be honored to earn your vote.
Find out more at Chip4OUSD.com.
