JAMES BURANDT
Public Service Professional
James Burandt is a Marine Corps veteran, Management Analyst,
and public servant with a Master of Public Administration and
experience across local, county, and federal government.
I have spent my career learning how different levels of government
can work better for the people they serve. My background includes
legislative and policy work in Congress in Washington, D.C., local
government experience here in Orange County, and my current
work as a Management Analyst for the County of Los Angeles.
Here in Placentia, I serve as Chair of the City’s Veterans
Advisory Committee, where I’ve worked to strengthen outreach,
improve communication, and increase visibility for veterans in our
community.
As your Councilmember, I would focus on fiscal responsibility and
transparency, emergency preparedness, improving city services
through the better use of data, and making sure residents receive
value for every tax dollar.
I believe public service should be practical, accessible, and focused
on results. I want Placentia to remain a safe, financially responsible
community where residents feel heard and city government earns
their trust. Placentia is our home, and I would be honored to bring
my experience, energy, and commitment to the City Council.
