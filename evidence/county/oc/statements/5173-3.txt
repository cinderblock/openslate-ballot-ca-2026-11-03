ERIK WEIGAND
Newport Beach City Councilmember, District 3
Four years ago, you entrusted me with the honor of representing
you on the Newport Beach City Council.
My top priority was ensuring our Police and Fire Departments had
the resources needed to keep Newport Beach safe, while being a
responsible steward of your tax dollars.
Over the past four years, that commitment has led to:
Crime at an all-time low. This year alone, crime is down 12.7%.
Decreased the number of people living on our streets from nearly
100 to just six.
Invested in public safety technology that has helped lead to
hundreds of arrests and the recovery of millions of dollars in
stolen property.
Added a third fully staffed paramedic unit, improving emergency
response times throughout the city.
Fully staffed our first responders while maintaining budget
surpluses and protecting the city’s top AAA credit rating.
These results are why I am proudly endorsed by Newport Beach
Firefighters; Lifeguards; Police Officers; and Orange County Sheriff,
Don Barnes. They know I will continue fighting to protect our
community and preserve the values that make our city such a
special place.
If re-elected, I will continue to work tirelessly for you and our city.
I respectfully ask for your vote.
