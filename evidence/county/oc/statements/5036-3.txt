CARA STEWART
Businessowner/Startup Adviser
I’m a business leader and entrepreneur, not a politician. I’ve called
Costa Mesa home for a decade and have seen the progress and
challenges that come with growth. I’ll put District 3 residents first.
My background gives me a practical advantage.
As a former Orange County Register reporter, I told local stories
and uncovered fraud, abuse and corruption.
Inside city government, I learned how good ideas move forward
and where they get stuck.
As a small-business owner, I learned how delays, red tape, and
uncertainty hit livelihoods.
Costa Mesa is at a crossroads. Housing for our next generation is
unaffordable. I pledge to work alongside you to address our housing
needs while protecting neighborhood character.
I will prioritize public safety in our parks and neighborhoods;
pothole repairs and safer pedestrian and bicycle routes; removing
graffiti and trash; and strengthening our economy by cutting red
tape, speeding up permits, filling vacant storefronts, and attracting
good-paying jobs.
My endorsements include District 3 residents and community
leaders: Orange County Supervisor Katrina Foley and
Councilmember Andrea Marr.
I’ll protect Costa Mesa’s character while building a safer, stronger
future for everyone.
I respectfully ask for your vote.
