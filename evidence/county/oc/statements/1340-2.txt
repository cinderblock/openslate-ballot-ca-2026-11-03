TRI TA
REPUBLICAN
California Assemblyman/Businessman
My American Dream began when my family immigrated from Saigon
in search of freedom. The adjustment wasn’t easy, but through hard
work, I became the first in my family to graduate from college.
In 2012, I was honored to become the first Vietnamese-American
elected mayor. As Mayor, I focused on the basics—public safety,
fixing roads, and improving quality of life—and delivered real
results.
Four years ago, I ran for Assembly to help more families achieve
a middle-class life, prioritize public safety, and give back to the
country that gave me so much.
As your Assemblyman, I have passed legislation to strengthen
public safety and push back against pro-criminal policies coming
out of Sacramento. When insurance companies wrongly denied
care to autistic children, I authored tough legislation and overcame
a Governor’s veto to protect families.
I have consistently stood up for taxpayers and opposed tax
increases. California is already the highest-taxed state, and family
budgets are stretched thin. I also authored legislation to rein in
rising utility costs, fought corruption, and secured $1 million in new
park funding for our district.
I have lived in this district for over 20 years, and my wife and I are
proud to have raised our daughters here.
I am honored to be endorsed by the Howard Jarvis Taxpayers
Association Pac, California Association of Highway Patrolmen,
California Professional Firefighters, and California Women’s
Leadership Association.
I respectfully ask for your vote.
