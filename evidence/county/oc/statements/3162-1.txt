CRAIG HOLIDAY
Attorney
I am running for the Newport-Mesa School Board because I want
to improve academic outcomes for all students. Every child should
leave elementary school, be able to read fluently, write clearly, and
with basic math skills. These fundamentals are key to opening every
door that comes later.
I will bring the perspective of a parent, an attorney, a coach, and an
educator to the school board. My priorities are less screen time in
classrooms, more instructional time on fundamentals, and honest
public reporting of how our students are performing. Math and
Science programs should be chosen because evidence shows they
work.
My wife and I have been married for 29 years, and we’ve lived in
Costa Mesa for 27 years. We raised our four children here who
all went to public schools. I have been actively involved as an
educator, teaching junior high and high school. I coached wrestling
and helped lead several teams to the CIF state championships. I
have practiced law since 2002 in business, real estate, insurance,
and professional athlete representation. I earned my law degree
by attending night classes while teaching during the day.
My educational background has prepared me to enhance
educational opportunities for our children, bringing the best
courses in Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Math. Every
child should be provided with the skills to achieve their chosen
dream in college or career.
As an attorney, I will bring skills this board needs. I will examine
agreements, purchases, and other spending line by line. The vast
majority of your tax dollars need to be spent in the classroom, not
on bloated administration. Budgeting efficiently, we can get better
results without higher taxes.
I believe the board must listen to parents, students, and our
teachers. I will always make myself available to them and always
work to improve the academic opportunities and outcomes for all.
