JEFF COLE
Public School Educator
I am a teacher, educational leader, and former school board
president with over 30 years serving students across Orange
County. A product of California public schools, I hold a Doctor
of Education in Educational Leadership from CSU Fullerton, plus
advanced degrees in special education and social sciences.
I've taught elementary, middle, and high school, plus adult
education and juvenile detention programs, and spent two decades
as a special education teacher, co-teaching leader, and department
chair supporting English learners and students with disabilities.
I served eight years as an elected trustee of the Anaheim
Elementary School District, including two terms as Board President.
I led passage of Measure J, a $318 million bond approved by 73%
of voters to rebuild aging schools—delivered on time and within
budget. I worked to streamline administrative spending, direct
resources to classrooms, and helped establish a Budget Advisory
Committee that cut costs while expanding bilingual education and
enrichment programs.
I'm running for Area 2 Trustee to keep public education
focused on students, families, and teachers—not administrative
bloat. Schools should be community-centered, transparent, and
accountable. My priorities: empowering educators, strengthening
parent partnerships, and ensuring every child has safe schools,
quality instruction, and the chance to thrive.
