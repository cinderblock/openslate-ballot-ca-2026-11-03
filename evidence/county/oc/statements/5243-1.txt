DEWAYNE A. NORMAND
AGE: 57
City of Stanton Planning Commissioner
I’m running for the District 3 City Council to bring a strong,
committed voice to our often overlooked neighborhoods. As a
Stanton resident since 1975, I’ve dedicated my life to serving
our community—six years on Parks and Recreation, current
Planning Commissioner, Lions Club member, Boys & Girls Club
board member, Neighborhood Watch participant, and a volunteer
wherever help is needed.
In my work with Rady Children’s Health (CHOC), I helped bring
the Wellness on Wheels program to Stanton, expanding medical
support for children and families in our city.
Walking every block of District 3, I listened to residents
and identified key concerns: homelessness, traffic and parking
challenges, and the need for greater leadership transparency.
These priorities will guide my work from day one.
Your voice matters. I will ensure your concerns are heard,
addressed, and acted on. I will hold city leadership accountable and
push for timely, meaningful solutions.
I’m ready to fight for our community and for you.
