YOUNG KIM
REPUBLICAN
United States Representative, 40th District
I first ran for Congress because, like you, I believed our country
was heading in the wrong direction. Career politicians put special
interests and personal gain ahead of hardworking families. We
deserve better.
In Congress, I've broken through the gridlock to deliver real results
for families facing skyrocketing inflation, rising costs, and increased
crime.
Lowering Costs, Cutting Taxes: Inflation has driven up the cost of
gas, groceries, and everyday necessities. I supported the largest
tax cut in American history, fought to increase the SALT deduction
making life more affordable, and helped return more than $33
million owed to hardworking taxpayers.
Keeping our Families Safe: I will always back law enforcement and
fight to keep dangerous criminals off our streets. That’s why I’m
endorsed by the Association of Orange County Deputy Sheriffs.
Holding Congress Accountable: Too many career politicians have
used public office for personal gain instead of public service. I’m
the only candidate in this race who supports term limits and has
championed legislation to ban Members of Congress from trading
stocks. I’ll hold career politicians, both Democrat and Republican,
accountable to the American people.
I have a strong record of being an independent voice, and am
ranked among the most bipartisan and effective members of
Congress.
That’s why nearly 100 local, county, and state elected officials
and education leaders have endorsed my campaign. I would
be honored to earn your vote so I can continue fighting to
lower costs, keep families safe, and restore the American Dream.
