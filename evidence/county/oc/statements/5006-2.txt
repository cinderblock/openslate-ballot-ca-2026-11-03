ELIA RENTERIA-GARCIA
Nonprofit Development Director
Anaheim is my home. For more than two decades, I have served
survivors of family violence, families, seniors, young people, and
community partners. That work taught me that public service
should be compassionate, accessible, and focused on results
residents can see.
As Councilmember, I will listen, communicate honestly, and follow
through. My priority is to focus on safer neighborhoods, stable
housing, stronger city services, and opportunity for families at every
stage of life. I will improve reliable transportation to make programs
for seniors and residents more accessible. Support responsible
funding for police and fire. I am committed to strengthening mental
health response, improving safety and street lighting, and ensuring
responsive code enforcement.
I plan to help local entrepreneurs access permits, training,
and financing so they can build thriving businesses and create
local jobs. By strengthening partnerships with schools, libraries,
employers, and community organizations, we can expand after-
school programs, improve parks, and create more internships, trade
opportunities, and pathways to local careers. City information and
services must also be accessible to residents in both English and
Spanish.
Residents deserve clear budgets, honest communication and real
follow-through. I respectfully ask for your vote.
