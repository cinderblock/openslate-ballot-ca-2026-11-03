MAX DUNCAN
Mayor of The City of Aliso Viejo
Aliso Viejo is one of the greatest cities in our country, consistently
ranked among the safest, with a close, welcoming community. It has
been the honor of a lifetime to serve as your City Councilmember
since 2022, and as your Mayor since 2026.
My accomplishments include:
Enabled Aliso Viejo to become the most financially sound city of
all 34 cities in Orange County.
Enacted a unique ebike ordinance to curb unsafe and illegal
riding.
Led the effort to bring safer, modernized bike lanes throughout
our city.
Stopped a dense 8-story development at The Commons that
would have forever changed Aliso Viejo.
Brought meetings back to council chambers for greater
transparency.
Support the efforts our local Sheriff's and Fire Fighters.
Leading the effort to bring a world-class pump track to Aliso Viejo
promoting healthy activities for our community
I am an Orange County native, entrepreneur, and IT professional. My
wife of 24 years and I are proud to raise our two kids here in Aliso
Viejo. Aliso Viejo is special, and we need to protect it. With proven,
steady leadership, I will keep working to maintain and improve our
quality of life. I would be honored to earn your vote this November.
