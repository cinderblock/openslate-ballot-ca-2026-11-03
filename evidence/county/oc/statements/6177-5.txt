PATRICIA "TRISH" KELLEY
City Councilmember
I’ve lived in our community since 1977, when my husband, Jack, a
United States Marine Corps pilot, was assigned to Marine Corps Air
Station El Toro. We raised our three children here and have nine
young grandchildren.
As a City Councilmember, I’ve delivered results:
Protected water quality by fighting water waste and urban runoff
90% of all City irrigation uses recycled water and drip irrigation
Smart irrigation controllers ensure the City’s watering schedule
adjusts based on weather conditions, reducing water waste
Balanced budgets
Maintained strong reserves, AAA bond ratings
Reformed pension formulas, reduced unfunded liabilities
Ended an unfair taxation program
I deeply believe in serving our community, having been the
American Cancer Society’s Relay for Life Co-Chair, PTA President
at 3 schools, a Saddleback College Foundation Governor, and
volunteer at Compass Bible Church. I’d like to continue serving our
community at the Moulton Niguel Water District.
If you elect me as your Moulton Niguel Water District Director, I’ll
bring my results-oriented leadership to:
Protect our water supply
Maintain low water rates
Improve efficiency to keep costs down
Expand our local water storage
Enhance our critical water infrastructure
Pay down pension debt to protect retirees, employees, and
ratepayers
