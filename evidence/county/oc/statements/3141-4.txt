ELIZABETH BATES
Fiduciary/Businesswoman/Mother
Raised in Laguna since '79. LBHS Class of '92. I raised four children
through this district; my youngest is about to start her senior year.
Local Realtor since 2004, entrepreneur, founder of a 2,600-agent
network, fiduciary trustee. I can read a budget. Run an organization.
Answer to the people I serve.
I spearheaded the campaign that restored our 70-year graduation
tradition at Irvine Bowl. I faced a wall of bureaucracy and didn't
waver. I won't waver for you. I served on the LBUSD Facilities Master
Plan Committee — new kitchens so kids get fresh meals daily, not
packaged food, is my top priority for the November bond.
We are running on Students First, Integrity Always. That means an
open door, my direct line, and no retaliation for parents who speak
up. It means residents can bring concerns to the agenda. It means
arts, sciences, and less screen time K-5.
My third grade teacher at Top of the World is my running mate. From
the classroom to the Board Room. That's Laguna raising its own.
When decisions are left to a small group, it is never fair or equitable.
I will stand up for every student, teacher, parent, and constituent
voice.
