YONG "CHUY" CHOI
Construction Businessowner
For many years, Buena Park has been my home. I have proudly
served our community as a small business owner, Taekwondo
Grandmaster, pastor, former Planning Commissioner, nonprofit
leader, and volunteer. My life has been guided by faith, hard work,
integrity, and a commitment to serving others.
I am running for City Council because every family deserves
safe neighborhoods, clean parks and streets, responsible financial
leadership, and a city government that listens, responds, and
delivers results. As an immigrant who achieved the American
Dream through perseverance and determination, I understand the
challenges facing working families, seniors, and small businesses.
My priorities are strengthening public safety, supporting our
police officers and first responders, helping local businesses
grow, maintaining clean neighborhoods, addressing homelessness
responsibly, protecting taxpayers, improving city services, and
ensuring every public dollar is spent wisely.
I believe effective leadership means bringing people together,
listening respectfully, and making decisions based on what is best
for Buena Park. I will work to earn your trust through honesty,
accountability, and practical leadership while always putting Buena
Park residents first. I respectfully ask for your vote.
Yong "Chuy" Choi
Candidate for Buena Park City Council – District 2
