DAISY TONG
AGE: 42
Rancho Santiago Community College District Governing
Board Member, Trustee Area 4
It has been an honor to serve as your Trustee for the past four years,
including the privilege of serving as President of the Board. I believe
education transforms lives by expanding opportunities, increasing
earning potential, and strengthening our entire community.
During my term, our district maintained a strong financial position,
increased reserves, expanded enrollment, strengthened dual
enrollment and adult education, and grew nationally recognized
apprenticeship programs. From the beginning, I have been
committed to helping our young students successfully transfer to
four-year universities while also creating more opportunities for
working adults to return to college, earn certificates, degrees, and
build better careers.
Under our leadership, Rancho Santiago Community College District
expanded one of California’s largest selections of bachelor’s
degree programs, opened Santa Ana College’s new Health Sciences
Building, secured more than $29 million in new state funding
through enrollment growth, and brought in millions of federal
grants to expand educational opportunities.
I believe public service begins with listening. I maintain an open-
door policy and welcome your questions, ideas, and concerns at
daisyxtong@gmail.com. I respectfully ask for your vote for Daisy
Tong to continue building on this success by keeping student
success our top priority while remaining a responsible steward of
taxpayer dollars.
