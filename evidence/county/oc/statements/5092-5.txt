BRENDA GLIM
Business Woman
Brenda has proudly called Huntington Beach home for 37
years. She raised her family here and is now helping raise her
grandchildren. She is committed to keeping Huntington Beach safe,
strong, and full of opportunity for future generations.
Brenda began her career with the General Accountability Office
and the United States Air Force, where she developed a strong
respect for accountability and public service. She later worked at
McDonnell Douglas on advanced space technology and managed
multi-million-dollar projects for companies including Experian and
IBM, overseeing budgets, timelines, and results.
For 22 years, Brenda has been an active member of the Rotary Club
of Huntington Beach, serving as President, Secretary, Treasurer,
Assistant District Governor, and currently Membership Chair.
Her Rotary service reflects her long-standing commitment to
community, leadership, and collaboration.
Brenda is running for City Council to focus on practical priorities:
maintaining infrastructure, protecting public services, respecting
taxpayers, and restoring trust in local government.
