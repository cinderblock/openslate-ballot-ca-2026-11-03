MARLON-RAY VELASCO
City of Stanton Commissioner/Supervisor
I have dedicated my professional and personal life to public
service because improving the lives of others and strengthening the
community is at the heart of who I am.
This is why I am running for Stanton City Council.
Professionally, I work for the Orange County Health Care Agency,
where I oversee programs that provide essential services to some
of our most vulnerable neighbors, people living with HIV. I advocate
for underserved populations by building strong partnerships with
community-based organizations.
Locally, I have the honor of serving the City of Stanton as Vice
Chair of the Stanton Community Services Commission. Since my
appointment, I have worked to improve our parks and recreation
facilities, most notably the Stanton Central Park tennis courts.
In my neighborhood, I am my Homeowners Association’s President.
In this role, I oversee the day-to-day operations, balance the
annual budget, and lead efforts to identify opportunities to enhance
neighborhood safety.
From countywide to citywide to my own backyard, I have a proven
track record of helping those in need, ensuring fiscal responsibility,
and building coalitions to advance the public good.
I hope to earn your vote so I can bring these skills to City Hall and
improve the quality of life for all Stanton residents.
