NATALIE JOHNSON
Appointed Governing Board Member, Brea Olinda
Unified School District, Area 4
As the current Trustee for Brea Olinda Unified School District Area
4, appointed by the Board of Education to fill the Area 4 vacancy,
it has been an honor to serve the students, families, educators,
and community of Brea. As both a parent of two children attending
BOUSD schools and an educational leader with more than a decade
of experience, I bring the perspective of someone who understands
the importance of strong public schools from both the family and
professional viewpoints.
Since joining the Board of Education, I have been committed
to thoughtful, transparent decision-making, supporting student
achievement, and working collaboratively with fellow trustees,
district leadership, staff, families, and the community. I remain
focused on maintaining safe and welcoming schools, supporting
our dedicated educators, ensuring responsible stewardship of
district resources, and providing every student with the opportunity
to succeed.
I respectfully ask for your support so I can continue serving Trustee
Area 4 with integrity, collaboration, and a strong commitment to
excellence for all students.
