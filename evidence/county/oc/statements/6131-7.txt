ANH "ANDY" NGUYEN
AGE: 60
Businessowner/Planning Commissioner
For more than 30 years, I have proudly lived and worked in
Westminster as a local business owner, long-time resident, and
dedicated community volunteer. I am honored to serve as a
Westminster Planning Commissioner, where I promote responsible
growth, thoughtful development, and decisions that strengthen our
neighborhoods and support local businesses.
Throughout the years, I have donated my time and resources
to charitable organizations, community events, and local causes
because I believe strong communities are built by people who are
willing to serve and give back.
I am running for Director of the Midway City Sanitary District
to continue serving our community and protecting the essential
services residents rely on every day.
My priorities are to:
Keep rates affordable,
Maintain reliable trash and sewer services,
Plan wisely for future infrastructure needs,
Protect our environment, and
Ensure taxpayer dollars are managed with honesty, transparency,
and accountability.
As a business owner, I understand responsible budgeting,
customer service, and practical decision-making. As a Planning
Commissioner, I listen to residents, work collaboratively, and
understand the importance of planning for our community’s future.
I respectfully ask for your vote so we can continue building
a cleaner, safer, stronger, and more financially responsible
community for everyone.
