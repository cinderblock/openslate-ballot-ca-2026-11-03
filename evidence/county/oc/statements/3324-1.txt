ADAM ROGERS
AGE: 44
Governing Board Member, La Habra City School District
My name is Adam Rogers, an academic research director at USC,
parent of two former La Habra students, and husband of a public
school teacher. For eight years, I have been honored to serve on the
La Habra City School Board, working to strengthen our schools and
support students, families, teachers, and staff.
During my service, I helped hire our superintendent, Dr. Carlos,
supported policies that protect and welcome immigrant families,
and worked collaboratively to help our educational community
become stronger, more stable, and successful. I have advocated
for responsible use of taxpayer dollars, high-quality programs,
inclusive schools, and decisions focused on student achievement
and well-being.
My family has directly benefited from the dedicated personnel and
programs at Washington Middle School, Sierra Vista, Ladera Palma,
El Cerrito, Las Lomas, and KidZone. I have helped support programs,
including the amazing International Baccalaureate programs at
Imperial and Las Positas.
I bring 25 years of management and community service experience,
a master’s degree from USC, and a bachelor’s degree from Cal State
LA. I remain committed to excellence, innovation, transparency, and
ensuring La Habra’s diverse students receive the opportunities and
support they deserve. I would be honored to earn your vote for re-
election.
