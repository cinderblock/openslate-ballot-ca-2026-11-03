CHRIS KLUWE
DEMOCRATIC
Businessman/Coach/Father
AGE: 44
Are you fed up with politicians unwilling to fight for you? I am.
I’ve had enough of politicians who put extreme ideology or
corporate special interests ahead of people. I’ve had enough of
Sacramento gridlock preventing us from addressing the cost of
living and the solutions California needs. And I’ve had enough of
corruption in government, especially when our tax money is used
to enrich political donors.
We need leaders willing to stand up for people, even when it costs
them personally. I’ve done that my entire life. In the NFL, I spoke
out for marriage equality when it wasn’t popular, and it cost me my
career. I fought book bans in Huntington Beach’s libraries, faced
personal attacks, and we won. I’d do it again in a heartbeat because
standing up for what’s right matters.
I’m not a politician. Raised in Seal Beach in a middle-class family,
I attended public schools and earned an athletic scholarship to
UCLA. Now, as a small businessman and youth coach raising two
kids in Huntington Beach with my wife, I’m concerned the American
dream is out of reach for too many working families in California.
In the Assembly, I’ll be independent and focused on lowering costs,
ensuring education is fully funded so every child can succeed,
making healthcare affordable, protecting our freedoms, tackling
homelessness, and stopping politicians from profiting from their
positions.
I’m proudly backed by nurses, teachers, and blue-collar workers.
I’ll fight for you. I’d be honored to earn your vote.
