EDGAR MONTES
AGE: 45
Parent/Educator/Counselor
I'm Dr. Edgar Montes, School Board candidate for Saddleback
Valley USD. I'm running because I believe every child deserves
an outstanding education, every family deserves to be heard, and
every decision should begin with one simple question: What is in
the best interest of our students?
As a first-generation college graduate, I know firsthand how a high-
quality public education can change the trajectory of a family's
future. That experience inspired me to dedicate my career to serving
students, families, and educators.
My academic journey, including earning a Doctorate in Education
from USC, has prepared me to thoughtfully analyze complex issues
and make informed decisions. For more than 23 years, I have
worked in education as a school counselor, administrator, nonprofit
executive, and education leader.
As a proud parent of two SVUSD students, I have a personal stake
in fostering an educational environment where every student can
flourish academically, socially, and emotionally.
We are grateful for the teachers, counselors, administrators,
classified staff, coaches, parents, and volunteers who invest so
much in helping our children—and every student—thrive. I believe
strong schools are built through trust, respect, and thoughtful
leadership.
It would be an honor to serve the SVUSD community.
