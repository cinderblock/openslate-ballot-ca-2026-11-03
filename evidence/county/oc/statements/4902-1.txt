My name is Steven Von der Porten, and I'm running to represent
District 2 on the Aliso Viejo City Council to focus on ensuring you
have:
Safe Streets
Clean Water
Affordable Homes
Wildfire Mitigation

I love this city. During the 15 years I've lived in Aliso Viejo, I got
married, welcomed my daughter, and purchased my first home. I'm
a father and an Aerospace Engineer, but never expected to run for
office.
My family left Germany just before World War II. Those who
remained did not survive. I heard firsthand how fear, division, and
the failure of good people to stand together can have devastating
consequences.
The challenges we face now require thoughtful problem-solving,
not politics as usual. Aliso Viejo deserves leaders who listen,
respect independent oversight and make decisions based on facts,
evidence and sound judgement. As an engineer, I solve problems
by listening to many points of view, analyzing data, and finding
practical solutions. That's the approach we need and it is the
approach I’ll bring to serving you.
I would be humbled if you visited my website at steven4av.com and
I would be honored to earn your vote.
