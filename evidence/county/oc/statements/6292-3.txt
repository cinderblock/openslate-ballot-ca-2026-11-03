BOBBY LAPOINTE
Retail Investor
As a husband, father of three, Army veteran, and longtime
Orange County resident, I understand public service begins with
responsibility. I am running for Division 2 Director of the Municipal
Water District of Orange County to help secure safe, reliable,
affordable water for our communities.
My Army work in information technology and network security
taught me to protect critical systems, evaluate risks, and respond
under pressure. I earned bachelor’s degrees in History and
Women and Gender Studies from California State University,
Fullerton, along with associate degrees in Mathematics and
Nutrition from Fullerton College. My education has been rooted
in community needs—combining analytical thinking, health, and
social understanding to better serve the public. As a retail investor
managing my investment business, I evaluate risk, scrutinize costs,
plan for the long term, and understand every dollar must serve a
purpose.
I will apply those skills to protecting groundwater, strengthening
infrastructure, expanding water recycling, improving wildfire
preparedness, and reducing dependence on imported water. I will
ask questions, explain decisions openly, and treat public money
with care.
I will bring integrity, judgment, financial discipline, and a
commitment to dependable water for every community I serve.
Thank you for your consideration.
