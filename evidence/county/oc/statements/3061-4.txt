GILA JONES
Board Member, Capistrano Unified School District
Tested Leadership – Future Focus – Transparency
During my twelve-year tenure on your Capistrano Unified School
District board, I successfully advocated for the establishment of
a new Academy for CUSD students, located on the Saddleback
College campus, where students are able to take both high school
and college courses, and may simultaneously earn CUSD and
Saddleback diplomas.
I was part of the team that hired a new superintendent; added to
the already-strong Honors, Advanced Placement, and International
Baccalaureate curricula; improved public transparency and
communication with parents; and many other accomplishments.
It’s critical that we prepare students for a very rapidly changing
global economy and job market. I’ll urgently advocate for
coursework in emerging fields, as well as high-demand careers with
apprenticeship options, both for young people and those pursuing
new fields of work or study.
Public trust requires stringent oversight. The current South
Orange County Community College District board failed to
protect residents and taxpayers when they voted to accept a
$750,000 developer inducement tied to a potentially hazardous
Battery Energy Storage System (BESS), near Saddleback College,
without proper scrutiny. Trustees must independently vet staff
recommendations rather than rubber-stamping staff proposals
that may compromise community safety.
If elected, I’ll bring governance experience, future-driven vision, and
