CASEY MCKEON
AGE: 47
Mayor of Huntington Beach/Businessowner
As a 3rd generation Huntington Beach resident, small business
owner and current Mayor of Huntington Beach, I passionately love
this City. I am extremely proud that we fulfilled our campaign
promises when I was elected in 2022.
We continue to fight for local control against unjust and unfair
High Density housing mandates to protect Huntington Beach from
predatory developers to ensure our City remains a suburban beach
community that protects our open spaces.
During the last four years, we have achieved the greatest homeless
reduction numbers in our City’s history! We were the number one
city in Orange County this year for homelessness reduction, and I
will continue to prioritize tackling this challenging issue.
During the last four years, Crime is down 27% across all categories.
I am supported by Police and Fire and will ensure Public Safety is a
priority in Huntington Beach.
Our Streamline Program cut the red tape at City hall and increased
permits issued by over 23% in the first year. Huntington Beach won
first place this year out of fourteen other government agencies for
our Streamline program.
I respectfully request your vote for re-election to Huntington Beach
City Council to secure our future.
