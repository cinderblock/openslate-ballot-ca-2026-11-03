BONNIE PEAT
Council Member
Cypress is a wonderful place to live because we have leaders who
are invested in our community. As your council member, everything
I do is for the progress of Cypress with no hidden agendas, no self-
gain, just Cypress.
For generations, my family has proudly called Cypress home.
Through my volunteer work within the city, I remain deeply
committed to supporting Cypress families and local businesses.
On City Council, I delivered results for our community and will
continue to do so. I have supported funding for our police and
emergency services, voted to approve the redevelopment of Arnold
Cypress Park, fought for further transparency for all council
meetings, and stood proudly with youth sports and family programs
that bring us together.
If re-elected, I will continue delivering results for Cypress by
investing in public safety resources and equipment, expanding
practical and affordable solutions within our city, making
fiscally responsible and transparent decisions, keeping taxes low,
supporting local businesses, and remaining responsive to the needs
of our community. My door will always be open because Cypress is
strongest when every resident has a voice.
I hope to earn your vote. For more information, visit
www.bonniepeatforcypress.com.
