ALEJANDRA RODRIGUEZ
Mother
I'm Alejandra Rodriguez, running for the Santa Ana Unified School
District Board of Education, Trustee Area 5, because every child
deserves the opportunity to succeed, every parent deserves to be
heard, and every school deserves strong, transparent leadership.
I am a Santa Ana native who attended Washington Elementary,
McFadden Institute of Technology, MacArthur Fundamental
Intermediate, and Saddleback High School. I have children
attending SAUSD schools, giving me experience as both a former
student and a parent.
I have been involved in my children’s education through the School
Site Council, PBIS, the Community School Council, the PTO, and
school committees. These roles have allowed me to listen to
families, work with educators, and understand our schools’ needs.
I have served as a City of Santa Ana Commissioner for Historic
Resources and Parks and Recreation and as a Neighborhood
Association leader.
As your trustee, I will focus on improving reading, writing, and
math, supporting teachers, keeping schools safe, and preparing
students for college, careers, and life. I will advocate for students
with disabilities and their families.
Santa Ana is my home, and our children deserve leadership that
puts students first and works with families.
I respectfully ask for your vote.
