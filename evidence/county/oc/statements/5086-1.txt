AMANDA NGUYEN
AGE: 36
Senior Paralegal
Amanda Nguyen is a career public servant. For nearly a decade,
she has worked inside Orange County's criminal legal system and
is currently a Senior Paralegal with the Orange County Alternate
Public Defender. She holds a Master's degree in Criminal Justice and
has spent her career seeing how government decisions affect real
people's lives.
Amanda is running for City Council because Garden Grove families
deserve a government that works for them.
She will fully staff our police and fire departments and keep
our neighborhoods, parks, and schools safe. She will address
homelessness with real accountability, connecting people in crisis
to mental health, addiction, and employment services while
keeping our public spaces safe for families and small business
owners. And she will fight for an affordable Garden Grove, investing
in Little Saigon and Main Street so our local economy works for
working families.
She is a lifelong resident of Little Saigon and the proud daughter
of Vietnamese refugees who believes every family deserves a safe,
affordable community.
Amanda is proud to be endorsed by Congressman Derek Tran.
Garden Grove deserves leadership that is accountable,
experienced, and focused on results. Amanda Nguyen will deliver
that.
