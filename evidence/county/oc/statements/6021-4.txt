JODY L. ROUBANIS
Professor
I am committed to preserving Rossmoor's outstanding quality of
life through thoughtful, ethical, and fiscally responsible leadership. I
hold a Doctor of Education from North Carolina State University and
have more than 35 years of experience in education, public service,
program administration, policy development, and organizational
leadership.
As a faculty member at Point Loma Nazarene University and former
consultant to the American Association of Family and Consumer
Sciences, I have worked extensively in budgeting, governance,
strategic planning, and collaborative problem-solving. Throughout
my career, I have built consensus, developed public policy, and led
organizations at the local, state, and national levels.
My community service includes leadership with the Los Alamitos
Unified School District PTA Council, Orange County PTA, the
Rossmoor Homeowners Association, and local bicycle safety
initiatives. I authored the Rossmoor Homeowners Association's
resolution supporting safe and responsible e-bike use and have
advocated for neighborhood safety, youth well-being, and strong
public schools.
If elected, I will listen carefully to residents, make informed
decisions based on facts, encourage transparency and fiscal
responsibility, and work collaboratively to protect Rossmoor's
parks, services, safety, and unique community character.
I respectfully ask for your vote.
