JON PEAT
Community Volunteer
I'm Jon Peat, and I'm running for the Cypress school board because
our schools work best when the community is involved.
Many parents and residents feel shut out. I'll change that with open
communication, real opportunities to be heard, and decisions made
in the open.
Our district needs to work as one team. Departments have become
siloed, we've lost three principals in a single year, and that
instability impacts our students. I'll rebuild trust among staff,
support our new superintendent, and make Cypress a place where
good people want to stay.
We also face serious budget challenges. A thirty-year career in
financial management and strategic planning, plus eight years on
the city council, gives me the experience and the understanding
needed to work with all our stakeholders, make the right decisions
and create a sustainable budget that achieves our objectives.
And I'll keep our schools focused on their core mission: educating
our children and serving our families. Curriculum should be age-
appropriate and transparent to families — not a vehicle for political
agendas.
I’ve spent years serving Cypress families, through Champions
Baseball, the Boys & Girls Club and founding a preschool at SCG
Church. I'd be honored to earn your vote.
