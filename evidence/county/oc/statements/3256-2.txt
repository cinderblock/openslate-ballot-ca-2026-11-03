MARK LOPEZ
AGE: 43
Special Education Teacher
It has been an honor serving as a special education classroom
teacher for almost a decade, supporting student literacy and
math skills. Living and working in Anaheim, I see the needs of
our community's students first-hand and collaborate with families
daily to support their success. I have attended nearly every board
meeting for almost two years, providing public comment. My
priorities include:
Leadership: I commit to holding district leaders to high standards
and compliance with the law. In September 2025, the District
Attorney's Office investigated a possible Brown Act violation,
California's open meeting law, regarding the board's voting
process. AESD then spent thousands of dollars on attorneys and
re-voted to ratify the entire agenda. The board also compensated
trustees who didn't attend meetings with a stipend without
approving a resolution, in violation of AESD's own bylaws and
education code. Any board member found to have been paid in
error should repay the district. Any board member or employee who
the district pays to attend a conference, but fails to do so, should
reimburse the district. Our limited tax dollars should be spent on
classroom instruction and infrastructure.
Transparency and Access: Government must be accountable to
the people it serves. I support sharing taxpayer-funded survey
results publicly and allowing community members who work long
hours to provide public comment, even if arriving after a board
meeting starts. All stakeholders should receive notice of district
town hall meetings, including taxpayers who may not currently
have children in the district. Holding regular community coffee
chats and meetings with stakeholders is a must for responsiveness,
gathering input, and resolving complaints before they escalate.
Fiscal Stewardship: I opposed the February board vote of a 300%
raise, to $24,000 per year, while holding only one meeting per
month. There should be offsetting revenue or budget savings to
justify this expense. AESD should eliminate Trustee healthcare
benefits and establish travel budgets. Trustees don't need full-time
benefits or unlimited travel, especially during these financial times.
We need school-run kitchens for local control and quality student
meals. Textbooks need review for cost-efficiency, accessibility for
our diverse student populations, and supplemental use to the
expertise of our teachers’ instruction.
I would be honored to earn your vote to advocate for innovative
and effective policies that benefit our students and community.

lopez4schoolboard@gmail.com. Thank you.
