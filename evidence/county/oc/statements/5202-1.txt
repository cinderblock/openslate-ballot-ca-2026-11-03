MARK ENMEIER
AGE: 43
Teacher/Councilmember
I am a San Clemente resident, born and raised, and a history
teacher at San Clemente High School. I now have the privilege
of raising my own family in this slice of heaven. In 2022, I was
elected to the San Clemente City Council. It has been my honor to
serve the citizens of this great city. While in office, I have worked to
bring more sand to our beaches, increase our trolley lines, reduce
homelessness, and create safer roads for our e-bike community. I
have also worked with partners to expand recreational amenities,
such as new pickleball courts, a pump track, a minigolf course, and
a goat grazing program.
Going forward, I will make sure that San Clemente stays focused
on local issues. This includes fire safety and mitigation, continuing
sand nourishment and retention, supporting our small businesses
and non-profits, keeping our kids safe on our streets, reducing our
carbon footprint, and protecting our community from pesticides,
landfill expansion, and battery farms. I believe that our city is at its
best when we focus on what brings us together as a community in
order to enhance our quality of life in this amazing place we call
home.
