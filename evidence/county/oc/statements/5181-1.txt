ARIANNA BARRIOS
AGE: 56
City Councilmember, City of Orange
Orange residents deserve leaders who will fight for them. Decades
of fiscal instability and scant economic growth have sent Orange in
the wrong direction. As Mayor, I want to change that.
We need City government that works for everyone with new ideas,
disciplined management, and strong fiscal controls to ensure our
future. We need fundamental change that puts residents first and
challenges the status quo.
As a businessowner and recipient of Orange Chamber of
Commerce’s Small Business of the Year award, I know how to
balance a budget and prioritize the essentials such as Police, Fire,
roads, water, sewers, and parks.
As Councilmember, I’ve gotten things done:
Lowered the crime rate
Strengthened code enforcement
Fought illegal smoke shops and massage parlors
Introduced strong fiscal policy
But we can do better.
As Mayor, I will:
Prioritize City debt reduction
Stop deficit spending
Tackle homelessness with compassion and enforcement
Ensure Chapman University pays their fair share
My record has earned endorsements from trusted leaders:
Orange County Supervisor Vincente Sarmiento, Orange City
Councilmember Ana Gutierrez, former Orange Mayor Tita Smith,
and Orange Unified School District Trustees Kris Erikson, Andrea
Yamasaki, and Sara Pelly.
Together, can we get Orange back on track.
I respectfully ask for your vote for Mayor.
