JOE STAPLETON
AGE: 42
Newport Beach Councilmember/Businessman
Four years ago, Newport Beach's Police Officers, Firefighters, and
Lifeguards, Sheriff Don Barnes, and Mayors spanning three decades
endorsed me. Today, they support me again because my focus
hasn't changed. It's about Keeping Newport, Newport.
Since you elected me, we have:
Fully funded public safety, launched a real-time crime center,
deployed American-made first-responder drones, and earned
our Fire Department's Class 1 rating, held by just 1% of
departments nationwide.
Cut unsheltered homelessness more than 90% since 2022, a
record low, with tougher anti-camping laws and dedicated social
services.
Passed tougher e-bike rules and expanded Peninsula patrols.
Renewed our Harbor: the largest dredging in decades now
underway, the West Coast's first Trash Interceptor, and the
nation's first all-electric public patrol vessel.
Secured water independence. 100% local groundwater by 2028.
Delivered consistent budget surpluses, protected our AAA credit
rating, and put $45 million each year toward pension debt.
After four years, promises made have become promises kept. That's
a lot, as it should be. Newport Beach deserves nothing less.
What's next? Finishing the Peninsula's new fire station and library,
retiring our pension debt for good, and defending our 40-year
airport curfew.
I’m asking for your vote.
