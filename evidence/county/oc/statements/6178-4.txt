JOEL BISHOP
Business Owner/Consultant
For 14 years, I have proudly called Laguna Niguel home and
have actively served our community through volunteer leadership
with the City and the Orange County Sheriff’s Department. Public
service, to me, is about giving back—not politics. I am independent,
have no political agenda, and seek no higher office.
I previously served ten years as an elected Director of the South
Coast Water District, including three terms as Board President.
During my tenure, I focused on long-range planning, fiscal
responsibility, and major infrastructure projects requiring years of
engineering, financing, and construction.
As a Director of the Moulton Niguel Water District, I will put
that experience to work for you. My priorities are straightforward:
provide excellent, reliable service while keeping rates in check;
protect the quality and reliability of our water supply; strengthen
regional cooperation to ensure adequate water during droughts
and emergencies; and safeguard our critical infrastructure against
cyberattacks and physical threats.
Reliable water is essential to our homes, businesses, and quality
of life. I have the experience, judgment, and proven record to help
keep our District financially strong, secure, and prepared for the
future.
I respectfully ask for your vote.
Joel Bishop
