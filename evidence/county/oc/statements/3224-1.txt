ANNEMARIE RANDLE-TREJO
Governing Board Member Anaheim Union High School
District
For more than 30 years, I have dedicated my life to serving our
students, families, and community. As a Behavior Interventionist
with the Anaheim Elementary School District, employed since 1995
and an elected Anaheim Union High School District Trustee since
2012, my commitment has always been ensuring every student has
the opportunity to discover their strengths and succeed.
Experience and steady leadership matter, especially during
uncertain times. As a three-time Board President, I helped to
guide our district through unprecedented challenges—including
the COVID-19 pandemic, fears impacting our immigrant families
and community—while keeping our focus clear: protecting rights,
elevating student voice, engaging families, and ensuring every
student feels safe, valued, and supported.
During my tenure, we have transformed our schools by modernizing
classrooms, facilities, athletic fields; restored historic Cook
Auditorium & championed environmental sustainability through
Katella’s DROPS water project.
We expanded innovative pathways in Cybersecurity, Magnolia
Agriscience, Esports, and Visual and Performing Arts.
Beyond the classroom, our signature AIME program provides year-
round mentorships–including paid, and career preparation.
I bring proven leadership, deep institutional knowledge, and a
commitment to collaborative governance. I respectfully ask for
your continued trust and support as we build a future where every
Anaheim student thrives.
Thank you
