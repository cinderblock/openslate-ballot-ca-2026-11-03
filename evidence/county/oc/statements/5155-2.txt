JOHN M. DELAVIGNE
Consultant
My name is John Delavigne and I am running to be your City Council
representative for the 5th District. Many of you already know me
through community events and the work I have been doing with the
Los Alamitos Community Foundation, and I hope to bring this same
love and dedication I have for our community to the City Council.
We live in an amazing community, and my goal is to work with
the community and City Council in a collaborative way to continue
making Los Alamitos a city where people want to live, raise families,
and open businesses.
My wife, Jessica, and I have two children, ages 13 and 10. We have
lived in the Highlands since 2019, and both children have benefitted
from attending our wonderful Los Alamitos Unified schools, and
participated in various sports and activities.
Since moving here, my family and I have felt the sense of
community, and have been involved with neighborhood events,
sports, school events and volunteer work. In particular, I have been
on the board of the LACF since its inception, which is a non-profit
organization that supports philanthropy to improve the lives of the
people in Los Alamitos!
