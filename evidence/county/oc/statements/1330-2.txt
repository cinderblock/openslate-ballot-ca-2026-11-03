Dear Neighbor,
I’m a City Councilmember, businessman, husband, father, and
lifelong Orange County resident. The broad coalition supporting
me includes local firefighters, nurses, police officers, teachers,
construction workers, and small business owners. Full list of
endorsements is available at DavidPenaloza.com/Endorsements.
After my father was killed by a drunk driver when I was three, I
learned early that nothing in life would come easy. Thanks to my
immigrant mother's sacrifice and determination and a community
that helped us, my family and I persevered. From Saddleback High
School to Memorial Park and our regional libraries, public services
shaped my future. I'm running for State Assembly to ensure every
child has the opportunity to thrive, every family can live in a safe
neighborhood, and every person has the support they need.
As your State Assemblymember, I’ll fight to:
Build middle-class housing working families can afford.
Keep neighborhoods safe and improve 911 response times.
Invest in schools, universal pre-K, college, and career training.
Invest in and expand access to quality healthcare.
Support our local businesses to create good-paying jobs.
Combat pollution and fight for clean air and water.
Lower the cost of living and protect working families.
Prevent tax increases and ensure our tax dollars stay in California.
Professionally, I have worked for the same manufacturing company
for 17 years. My wife, Kim Nguyen-Penaloza, and I are proud parents
of two wonderful kids.
I’d be honored to earn your vote for California State Assembly.
If you have questions, don’t hesitate to call me at (714) 623-7308
or email Hello@DavidPenaloza.com.
Thank you for your consideration!
