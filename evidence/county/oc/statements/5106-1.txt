BETTY MARTINEZ FRANCO
Councilmember/Businessowner
When I took the oath to serve on the Irvine City Council, I promised
to put Irvine first and deliver results. I have kept that promise
by strengthening public safety through my service on the Orange
County Fire Authority Board, expanding support for seniors and
working families, protecting our beloved University Park Library,
hosting Irvine's first Women's Conference, and launching our city's
bike share pilot.
More than 30 years ago, I came to America picking apples and
cleaning hotel rooms. After surviving domestic violence, Human
Options helped my two daughters and me find safe, affordable
housing in Irvine. I worked hard, earned a Master of Public
Administration from USC, built a successful small business, and
never forgot the opportunities this community gave my family.
I am running for reelection to keep our neighborhoods safe, our
city financially strong, and our local businesses thriving. I want to
make Irvine more affordable and ensure seniors, working families,
and financially vulnerable residents are not left behind.
Irvine gave my family a new beginning. I will keep working every
day to protect the opportunities that make our city one of America's
best places to live. I respectfully ask for your vote.
Learn more at bettyforirvine.com.
