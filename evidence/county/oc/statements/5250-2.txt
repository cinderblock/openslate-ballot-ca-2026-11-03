AUSTIN LUMBARD
Tustin turns 100 years old next year, and our community is full of
energy and optimism. When you elected me four years ago as your
mayor, I made commitments to you. Those promises have been
kept. Together, we have invested in Tustin’s future, increasing police
and fire resources, strengthening our finances, and cleaning up our
streets and parks.
My work is dedicated to you. My family plays in our parks and visits
our shops and restaurants. My children attend Tustin public schools.
We volunteer with our wonderful non-profits. Most of our evenings
and weekends are spent on our fields for baseball, football, soccer,
or basketball. Everywhere we go, I meet folks who love our town
and care deeply about protecting and strengthening our city. And I
am honored to serve as your mayor and to help shape a safer, more
resilient, and prosperous Tustin for every resident.
Strengthening public safety: I am proud to be endorsed by
OC Sheriff Don Barnes, Tustin Police, and OC Firefighters who
recognize our success in expanding public safety resources to every
neighborhood, business, and school. Our commitments resulted in
reduced crime - 35% reduction in violent crime and 17% reduction
in property crime - increasing Tustin’s home values and keeping
Tustin residents safe.
Reinforcing financial discipline: we delivered strict budget policies
that reduced our city’s unfunded pension liabilities and increased
rainy-day funds. We are leveraging strategic partnerships and
attracting new businesses to increase revenue, instead of
increasing taxes.
Cleaning up our community: we are refreshing our beautiful parks
and cleaning up our tired streets and medians. We are reducing
homelessness and blight by partnering with experts and securing
state and federal funding, including creating a facility for families
with children experiencing homelessness.
Promoting Tustin’s character: we are investing in Old Town
infrastructure and amenities; we are increasing public art; we
opened Tustin’s first dog park; and we added to our thousands
of city-maintained trees. We are expanding public facilities and
promoting sustainable development. Together, we have focused on
what makes Tustin special and invested in our future.
I am humbled to be endorsed by a long list of Tustin’s leaders
including current Tustin Councilmen Ryan Gallagher, John Nielsen,
and Ray Schnell, and a long list of former mayors. Please join them
in supporting me.
I would be honored to receive your vote!
