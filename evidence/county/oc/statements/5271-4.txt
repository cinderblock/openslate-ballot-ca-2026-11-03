CHI CHARLIE NGUYEN
Mayor/Business Owner
Serving as your Mayor has been the greatest honor of my life. I
respectfully ask for your vote to continue providing experienced,
honest, and accountable leadership for Westminster.
When I became Mayor, Westminster faced years of financial
challenges. Through responsible budgeting and fiscal discipline, we
restored the City's financial health. Today, Westminster has over
$60 million in financial reserves, protecting essential services and
ensuring long-term stability.
Public safety is my highest priority. Under my leadership, the
Westminster Police Department is fully staffed and prepared
to serve our community. I am proud to be endorsed by the
Westminster Police Officers Association and the Orange County
Professional Firefighters Association.
As a Vietnamese refugee, I came to America seeking freedom and
opportunity. My journey has inspired a lifelong commitment to
hard work, service, and giving back. In 2025, I was honored to be
recognized as one of Orange County's 125 Most Influential for my
dedication to public service.
My priorities are to
Protect taxpayers,
Support local businesses,
Improve infrastructure and parks,
Enhance city services and
Keep Westminster safe.
Together, we can build a safer, stronger, and more prosperous
Westminster.
I respectfully ask for your vote.
