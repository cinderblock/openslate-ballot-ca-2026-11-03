LINDA MARTINEZ QUIJANO
Parent
As a current AUHSD parent, bold voice, and dedicated educational
advocate, I am running for Anaheim Union High School District
Trustee, Area 1, to ensure every student has the opportunity
to succeed in a safe, supportive, and high-quality learning
environment.
I am a proud graduate of Sycamore Junior High School and Anaheim
High School. My older children proudly graduated from AUHSD,
and today I am the parent of three current Anaheim High School
students. As both a graduate and parent, I understand the hopes
and challenges of Anaheim families because I have experienced
them firsthand.
For 23 years, I proudly served Anaheim families in supportive roles,
including as a Community Liaison with the Anaheim Elementary
School District, helping students and parents access the resources
they needed to thrive. I have been actively engaged as a
civic community leader in Anaheim for many years and am an
established advocate for students and families.
I proudly stand with teachers, parents, students, and our community
because strong schools begin with supporting those who educate
our children.
As your Trustee, I will champion student safety, quality educational
opportunities, transparency, accountability, and meaningful parent
engagement. I am not afraid to ask the tough questions or hold our
district accountable.
I respectfully ask for your vote.
