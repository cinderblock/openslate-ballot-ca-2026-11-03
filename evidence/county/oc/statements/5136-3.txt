BECKY KEENE
Systems Analyst
Lake Forest is currently well-off financially due to major housing
developments and fees paying for new city buildings and parks.
In the future we will be more dependent on property and sales tax.
To keep up our city services, we’ll need to watch the money – no
waste on pet projects or fighting Sacramento. This means going
through budgets and contracts that cover services from wildfire
mitigation to public safety to ensure our funds and values are
supported. This is boring work.
I’ve been a CPA for 44 years. I do boring – carefully and proudly,
and in your best interest!
I have lived in and loved Lake Forest since 1992. I’m semi-retired
and would like to give back to the City. See my website below for
detail.
My husband and I have raised 2 children here. I’ve volunteered with
various organizations including the El Toro High School PSTO from
1996 through 2004 (Auditor, President) and on the Lake Forest
Race Committee until 2015.
I’ve attended the Lake Forest Civic Academy, City Council and Audit
meetings and studied the ACFR (financials).
My financial and technical background will bring complimentary
perspectives to the Council.
I look forward to working for you!
