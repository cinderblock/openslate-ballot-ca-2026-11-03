ROBERT "BOB" RUESCH
Mission Viejo Councilmember
It has been an honor to serve as your Councilman for District 1, and
I wish to continue working to keep our city great and the needs of
North Mission Viejo heard.
My accomplishments:
Mayor 2025, signed the agreement to host the Dutch Olympic
Team in Mission Viejo, worked to bring the golf course profitable,
added the Atrium meeting center, held the first Fire Insurance
Townhall, reworked the city website, championed the new
parking addition, completed Fire Station 24, streamlined city
council meetings, rebuilt relationships with major city employers
and Saddleback College, worked with the Lake Association to
begin filtering the water to bring back fishing.
My continuing goals:
Preserve the Mission Viejo lifestyle
Keep Mission Viejo’s budget balanced.
Continue working with sheriff on crime reduction and community
safety.
Fight increased traffic and high-density housing.
Continue new mosquito abatement program
Support development and growth of local businesses in Mission
Viejo.
Personal history:
Mission Viejo Councilman and 2025 Mayor.
Entrepreneur, Scout Leader, Eagle Scout advisor, and church
member for 30 years.
It would be my honor to continue to serve the people of Mission
Viejo as Councilman for District 1
