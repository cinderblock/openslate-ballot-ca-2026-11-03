MATT SMITH
Sports Broadcaster
I’d be honored to represent District 1 as your City Council
Representative. We’ve lived in the area since 2004 and in Old Town
since 2019. This is the heartbeat of our special city and I intend to
keep Seal Beach as strong as ever with many mounting challenges
ahead.
I graduated from Pepperdine University with the intent of attending
law school but dream jobs got in the way. From working on-air
and as music director at KROQ, to my 30-year career in sports
broadcasting, I’ve been blessed to wake up and do what I love every
day.
I’ve spent considerable time the past few decades around
championship DNA: Kobe Bryant, Clayton Kershaw, Phil Jackson
and Jim Harbaugh to name a few. I know what teamwork, leadership
and self-sacrifice for the greater good look like, and what a winning
culture requires.
We want Seal Beach to be the same slice of paradise for our children
that it has been for us, only better. That means clean beaches, safe
neighborhoods, balanced budgets, increased revenue, making Main
Street an iconic strip once again, and fighting for a community that
truly cares about one another.
