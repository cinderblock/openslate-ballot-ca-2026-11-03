SELICA DIAZ
Real Estate Agent
I am a proud wife and mother of four, Realtor, Cal State Fullerton
graduate, Parks & Recreation Commissioner (2022-2026), and
dedicated community leader with 30 years of service in Santa
Ana. Over three decades, I have turned advocacy into action—
from helping create Pacific Electric Park, securing $4 million for a
new community center, organizing community resource fairs, and
volunteering year-round to support families, youth, and seniors.
As an immigrant who came to the United States as a teenager in
search of greater opportunities, I understand the hopes, sacrifices,
and determination it takes to build a better future. After becoming
a proud United States citizen in 1999, I dedicated myself to giving
back through community service and advocating for hardworking
families.
My priorities are public safety, healthy neighborhoods, responsible
budgeting, support for local businesses, and timely completion of
city projects. I will strengthen communication and trust between
residents and City Hall as your city councilmember.
I bring practical experience, compassion, accountability, and a
proven commitment to public service.
Santa Ana is our home. Together, we can build a safer, healthier,
and more vibrant city. I respectfully ask for your vote and the
opportunity to continue serving our community.
