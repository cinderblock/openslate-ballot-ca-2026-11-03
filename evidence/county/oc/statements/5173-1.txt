WALTER STAHR
Author
My family moved to Newport Beach in 1972 and my parents were
immediately involved in the community. Like them I am dedicated
to preserving the great qualities of Newport Beach.
My priorities on the City Council would include:
Saving Civic Center Park: Stop the City Council from destroying a
beloved city park to build a police station. I support a new police
complex, but it must not be built on park land.
Improving Public Safety: Ensure that our citizens feel safe in their
homes and on our streets at all times. We should have both
an independent external investigation of the recent July Fourth
rioting and looting. We should identify and implement citywide
preventative strategies.
Supporting Fiscal Accountability: Ask the tough questions about
proposed spending and rein in the recent rapid growth in city
expenses.
I have extensive financial experience, including five years at the
US Securities and Exchange Commission and four years as a senior
lawyer for Fidelity Investments. I serve on the boards of the Pacific
Symphony and the Library Foundation of Newport Beach.
I would be honored to serve Newport Beach on the City Council.
