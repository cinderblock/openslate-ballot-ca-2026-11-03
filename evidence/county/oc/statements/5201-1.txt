AARON WASHINGTON
AGE: 61
Deputy Program Manager
I am a retired U.S. Navy Commander with 35- years of honorable
service. I have been a San Clemente resident since 2006. My
credentials are solid: Born at Naval Hospital Bremerton, raised
in San Diego; Enlisted two days after high school graduation in
1982; Completed my B.S. degree in 2007 without breaking service;
my highest award: Legion of Merit. This isn’t my first time! I ran
in 2020 and 2022, and since then I’ve stayed deeply involved:
San Clemente BPRCA; Human Affairs; Coastal Advisory; Board of
Directors for San Clemente Men’s Golf Club. I put in work even when
I wasn’t on council. I’m not a tourist showing up just for election
season. Platform: Public Safety Fully Funded; Pro-small business;
Oppose tax increases (protecting fixed incomes); No Toll Road;
Protect Beaches and coastal erosion without raising taxes. Support
school safety and preserve San Clemente’s “small village by the
sea” character, restrict overdevelopment. My military background
gives me real leadership and logistics experience—not theoretical,
actual command experience. The fact I still hold an active security
clearance tells you that I am not some retiree coasting on past
glory; I am still sharp and trusted.
