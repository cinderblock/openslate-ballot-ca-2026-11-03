NAMQUAN NGUYEN
AGE: 52
Council Member City of Westminster
You Voted for Me. I Delivered for You.
When I asked for your vote, I made promises. As your District 4
Councilmember, I delivered on them.
I supported hiring more police officers and continue advocating
for additional officers, park rangers—answering residents’ call for
safer neighborhoods, schools, and parks.
I helped restore Westminster’s financial strength. For the first time
in over a decade, our city has more than $60 million in reserves—a
milestone that protects essential services.
I pushed for road repairs, cleaner streets, and improved landscaping
—improvements residents can see and appreciate.
I cut red tape for small businesses, helping shops and restaurants
open, grow, and create local jobs.
I championed Safe Routes to School, youth scholarships, stronger
city-school partnerships, and police protection for our schools—
investments valued by parents and families.
I supported our city shelter, connecting people experiencing
homelessness with housing, mental-health services, and job
training—a practical solution welcomed by residents.
Westminster is my home. You trusted me, and I delivered. These
results reflect the priorities of our community. I respectfully ask
for your vote to continue building a safer, cleaner, and more
prosperous Westminster.
Vote NamQuan Nguyen for Westminster City Council, District 4.
