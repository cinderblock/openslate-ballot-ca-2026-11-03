ROGER FLANDERS
Court Investigator
I’ve lived in West Garden Grove my entire life, or at least since my
parents brought me here as a baby. As small business owners,
my parents taught me the value of hard work and commitment. I
graduated from Pacifica High School and UC Irvine. My wife and I
chose to raise our family here because we know West Garden Grove
is a special place, and our children graduated from the Garden
Grove Unified School District, just like I did.
I dedicated my career to protecting our city:
Garden Grove Police Officer for 30 years
Terrorism Liaison
Drug Abuse Resistance Education (DARE)
Founding officer, Special Resource Team for homeless and
mentally ill people
I’m passionate about giving back to our community. I’ve served as:
Garden Grove Planning Commissioner
Garden Grove Traffic Commissioner
Garden Grove Neighborhood Improvement & Conservation
Commissioner
President, Pacifica High School Boosters
Coach, Youth Baseball and Soccer
My commitment to Garden Grove has never changed, and I will
continue serving with integrity and dedication.
I am proud to be endorsed by Garden Grove Mayor Stephanie
Klopfenstein, Mayor Pro Tem George Brietigam, and Orange County
Supervisor Janet Nguyen.
If elected, I will:
Put residents first
Prioritize public safety to keep our neighborhoods safe
Support our local businesses
Protect our special West Garden Grove quality of life
Address homelessness with both services and enforcement
