JOE HASTIE
AGE: 59
Maintenance Operations Director
I have lived in Los Alamitos area since 1976 and graduated from
Los Al High in 1984. My wife Jennifer and I married in 1989 and
proudly raised our daughters, Melissa and Breezy in Los Al. The
girls and I attended the same schools Weaver, McAuliffe (Pine),
and Los Alamitos High School. Over the years, I have volunteered
extensively with local youth sports, coaching soccer, basketball,
softball, and lacrosse from U6 to high school levels.
Having watched our city change and grow, I am dedicated to
ensuring Los Alamitos remains a wonderful place to live.
Professionally, I bring over 35 years of construction experience,
including 24 years managing Maintenance and Operations for a
local public school district. I am also currently serving on the
Los Alamitos Planning Commission. As a long-time civil servant, I
respect my responsibility to be a good steward of public tax dollars.
This experience gives me a unique perspective on the planning and
impact of projects that may come before the Council.
I would be honored to earn your vote this November 3rd to
represent District 4 on the City Council.
Thank you,
