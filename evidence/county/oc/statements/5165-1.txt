STEVEN THORNTON
Defense Acquisition Contractor
Mission Viejo has been my home for more than 40 years. I grew up
here, raised my family here, and care deeply about protecting the
community we all cherish.
I believe City Hall should put Mission Viejo residents first. My
priorities are keeping our neighborhoods safe, maintaining our
roads, parks, and community facilities, supporting local businesses,
protecting taxpayer dollars, preserving our quality of life, and
defending local control when higher levels of government try to
make local decisions for us.
My service in the United States Navy, my work as a Congressional
Aide, and my experience on the Community Services Commission
have prepared me to lead with integrity, practical judgment, and
accountability. I understand that good government starts with
listening to residents, solving problems, and following through on
commitments.
I will be accessible, responsive, and committed to making decisions
based on what is best for Mission Viejo and the families who call it
home, not politics or special interests.
It would be an honor to earn your trust and your vote. Together, we
can preserve the character of Mission Viejo while preparing our city
for a strong future.
For more information, visit www.steven-thornton.com.
