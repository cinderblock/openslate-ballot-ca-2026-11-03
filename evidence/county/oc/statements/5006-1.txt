NATALIE RUBALCAVA
Anaheim City Councilmember
It’s been my honor to serve on the Anaheim City Council since 2022.
In this role, I’ve made it a priority to listen to residents and protect
the things that make our city such a special and unique place to live.
On the City Council, I’ve fought to:
Protect neighborhoods by ensuring first responders have the
resources they need.
Tackle the root causes of homelessness by addressing housing
and mental health.
Implement a first-time homebuyers program for Anaheim
residents.
Create good-paying jobs by helping local businesses and
attracting new investments.
Secure our fair share of city funding to maintain our streets,
sidewalks, and parks.
Ensure fiscal responsibility, transparency, and responsiveness at
City Hall.
I’m a small business owner, mom, longtime community leader,
and fourth-generation Anaheim resident. I’m a proud graduate
of Lincoln Elementary, Sycamore Junior High, and Anaheim High
School. I earned my bachelor’s degree from California State
University, Fullerton and law degree from University of La Verne
College of Law.
I’m proud to have the support of Anaheim firefighters, police
officers, city employees, and small business owners.
If you have any questions or ideas, feel free to contact me directly
at (714) 349-5602.
I hope to earn your vote and continue working for you on the
Anaheim City Council.
With gratitude,
