JULIE GLOSSBRENNER
Housing Specialist
Decisions made by the Irvine City Council affect our quality of life
today and our city's future. Residents deserve a councilmember
with the integrity to do what is right, the compassion to understand
people, and the sound judgment to make thoughtful, independent
decisions.
My professional experience, combined with my education at
the University of California, Irvine, has prepared me for this
responsibility. I have extensive experience evaluating proposals,
negotiating contracts, and solving complex problems. These are the
same skills needed to review budgets, development agreements,
public contracts, and the many other important issues before the
City Council. Careful analysis, thoughtful questions, and informed
decisions lead to better outcomes.
Volunteering with Irvine Meals on Wheels, Second Harvest Food
Bank of Orange County, and serving on the Board of Directors
for UCI Health Family Health Centers has strengthened my
understanding of our community and reinforced my commitment of
service.
I am dedicated to protecting public safety, preserving open
space, supporting responsible growth, and safeguarding Irvine's
exceptional quality of life.
I would be honored to earn your vote and serve the residents of
District 5.
