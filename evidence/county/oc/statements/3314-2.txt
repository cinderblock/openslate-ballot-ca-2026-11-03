LAURA COSTELLOE
AGE: 50
Technical Education Advisor
As an Edison High School graduate, mother of two children who
attended Huntington Beach City School District schools for 13
years, and longtime community advocate, I have dedicated my life
to serving Huntington Beach students and families.
Our district is finally in a strong financial position under
Basic Aid. We should protect that success by managing
taxpayer dollars responsibly, maintaining transparency, preserving
neighborhood schools, and safeguarding community assets for
future generations. I believe there is no reason to sell district land
or close neighborhood schools when sound financial stewardship
can protect both.
My commitment is proven through years of service. I served ten
years on a PTA Executive Board, including as PTA President, have
attended every School Board meeting for the past eight years,
and maintain a community information page that keeps parents
informed and engaged on important district issues and Board
actions because accountable leadership matters.
I received the Mayor's Spotlight Award for co-leading Peterson
Elementary's annual Read Across America celebration, bringing
more than 50 community leaders into classrooms each year to
promote literacy and inspire young readers. As a former Huntington
Beach Community & Library Services Commissioner, I advocated for
transparency, public engagement, and responsible stewardship of
our community resources.
Professionally, I serve as a Technical Education Advisor at an
Orange County career-connected training center, helping students
develop career skills, earn industry certifications, and prepare for
successful careers. I have secured grant funding that has opened
doors for at-risk youth and students with disabilities to receive
vocational training, industry-recognized certifications, and life-
changing opportunities.
Whether advocating for families, supporting students through
difficult circumstances, or creating opportunities that change lives, I
have always believed every child deserves someone willing to stand
beside them.
I believe our strongest schools are built through transparency,
community partnerships, and opportunities for every student to
succeed. As your Trustee, I will continue bringing people together
to protect our neighborhood schools, support academic excellence
and career pathways, and ensure every child has the opportunity
to thrive.
I would be honored to earn your vote for Huntington Beach City
School District Trustee, Area 4.
