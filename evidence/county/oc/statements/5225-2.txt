SANDRA DE ANDA
Immigrant Rights Director
I was raised in Santa Ana. My father is an immigrant and longtime
skilled laborer, and my mother is a third-generation Santanera.
Growing up on Minnie Street, I experienced many of the same
challenges our families face today. After Kennedy Elementary and
Villa Fundamental, my life changed when a social worker connected
me to the A Better Chance Program, which took me to Chatham
Hall in Virginia and later Reed College. That taught me a lesson I
still carry: talent is everywhere, but opportunity is not.
As Director of Policy and Legal Strategy for an immigrant
rights organization, my work happens in immigration courthouses,
helping families find legal representation. It means standing beside
parents afraid of losing their children, and bearing witness so no
one faces the hardest day alone.
Those experiences show me our residents carry many burdens, and
our city government can do more to protect them. We deserve
leaders who understand what families face.
I have spent my life learning from this city and showing up for it.
Now, I am asking for your vote to lead it, with compassion, courage,
and a vision for where every resident can belong and thrive. Learn
more: SandraForSantaAna.com
