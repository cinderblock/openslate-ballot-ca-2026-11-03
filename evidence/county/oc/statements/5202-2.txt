STEVE KNOBLOCK
Mayor Pro Tem
Please consider voting for Steve Knoblock for San Clemente City
Council, District 2.
My commitment has always been to protect San Clemente's
exceptional quality of life while ensuring our police officers and
firefighters have the resources they need to keep our community
safe.
I helped secure 250,000 cubic yards of sand at the Pier and another
40,000 cubic yards at North Beach to protect and restore our
beaches.
To reduce wildfire risk, I championed a pilot program using goats
instead of toxic herbicides or expensive mechanical equipment to
clear fire-fuel vegetation in our open-space canyons. The program's
success has led to its expansion into HOA canyons and other City
properties.
When Chilean burglary crews targeted our neighborhoods and
illegal panga boats used our beaches for criminal activity, I worked
with U.S. Customs and Border Protection to help bring ocean-facing
cameras to City property in support of law enforcement.
I also support creating a small dog-friendly beach south of T Street,
giving San Clemente's thousands of dog owners a place to enjoy
the coast with their pets.
I would be honored to earn your vote.
Steve Knoblock
San Clemente City Council, District 2
