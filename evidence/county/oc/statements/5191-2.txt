MATT WAUGH
Business Executive/Parent
A Placentia resident of more than 20 years with deep roots in the
community, Matt is a business executive with experience advising
organizations on financial accountability, strategic planning,
workforce development, and operational effectiveness. He helps
organizations identify problems, design solutions, and measure
success.
Matt is running for City Council to bring that disciplined
approach to serving Placentia. His priorities are responsible fiscal
stewardship, keeping our neighborhoods safe, maintaining roads
and infrastructure, and ensuring City Hall remains focused on
the people it serves. As California cities face growing budget
pressures, careful management of taxpayer dollars will be essential
to preserve public safety, protect essential services, and invest
wisely in our community.
Matt's wife has taught economics at a local high school for nearly
three decades. Together they raised two sons in Placentia, where
both attended local elementary, middle, and high schools. These
experiences give Matt a grounded understanding of the needs of
families, students, and working residents.
Matt brings a collaborative, disciplined approach to City Council:
listening first, making thoughtful decisions, and working to keep
Placentia safe, financially sound, and a community where families
and businesses can thrive.
