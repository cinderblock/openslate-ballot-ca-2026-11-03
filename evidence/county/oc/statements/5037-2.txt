ANGELY ANDRADE VALLARTA
Nonprofit Director
As a working parent raising my daughter in Costa Mesa, I
understand the challenges families face every day.
For 20+ years, I worked across government, nonprofit, and
business sectors expanding opportunity, building partnerships, and
delivering results for families.
As Planning Commissioner, I voted to approve our city code and
projects expanding housing, including opportunities for first-time
homebuyers. I advocated for streamlined permitting to encourage
responsible development.
That's why I'm running for City Council: to ensure every resident can
thrive.
As your Councilmember, I will work to improve public safety
through neighborhood partnerships, and safer streets; expand
housing opportunities and improving affordability; invest in green
spaces; support local businesses and economic opportunity;
and increase transparency, fiscal responsibility, and community
engagement.
I bring collaborative leadership, lived experience, and a
commitment to deliver results. Together, we can build a Costa Mesa
with safer streets, stronger neighborhoods, and greater opportunity
for everyone.
I’m proud of endorsements from OC Chapter Women In Leadership,
Costa Mesa Councilmembers: Reynolds, Marr, Chavez, Gameros,
NMUSD Board Trustees: Murphy, Ersoylu.
With your vote, we can achieve great things in Costa Mesa.
