BRANDON COUNCIL
AGE: 33
Mechanical Engineer
As an R&D Mechanical Engineer and long-time community
advocate, I bring a practical, data-driven approach to solving
complex problems. I have spent over a decade leading cross-
functional teams, managing long-term technical projects, and
balancing critical budgets.
As I continue to make Lake Forest a home for myself in a fresh start,
I want to join you and support you by being a voice of reason and
progress on our infrastructure challenges, public safety, and smart
economic development for local businesses.
I am running for City Council because I love making a difference
for people. I want my professional talents focused on solving the
problems that matter most, on what affects you day to day. I bring
with me the tenacity for improvement and innovation but also a
tactful humility that comes from working on diverse teams among
talented people each with their own important contribution to
make.
I’ve served as a high school youth leader in my local church for
5 years and I’ve been involved in various homeless outreach and
census programs. I believe in working as a team, using critical
thinking and common-sense leadership to solve the problems we’re
all up against.
