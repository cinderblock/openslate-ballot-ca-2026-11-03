NICHOLAS REESE
Financial Operations Manager
Since 1996, my family has called Rancho Santa Margarita home.
Then, the Santa Margarita Marketplace was still the site of the
rodeo, and a neighborhood cow greeted our residents in the
mornings. Yet, while the landscape has changed, the values of our
community certainly have not. These are the values I was raised
on: hard work, personal responsibility, and accountability. From
Robinson Elementary and Rancho Santa Margarita Intermediate to
Saddleback College and UCLA - and from serving lemonade off
Plano Trabuco to serving as a Financial Operations Manager, this
community has shaped who I am today.
At UCLA, I studied economics and public policy, and majored in
political science. While attending, I led an investigation into our
campus that brought billions of dollars in losses and the systemic
failures behind them to public attention. I also served as Chairman
of the Oversight Committee, responsible for helping ensure the
student government's multi-million-dollar budget was managed
effectively and transparently. These experiences reinforced the
values and skills that I will bring to our city: fiscal responsibility,
accountability, transparency, and a commitment to protect our tax
dollars.
Currently, I work as a Financial Operations Manager, where I
oversee and optimize my company's budget and operations. Every
day, I am entrusted with making responsible financial decisions,
controlling costs, and ensuring resources are utilized optimally - all
experiences that I will bring to City Hall.
I remained home as the Airport Fire ravaged the hills above,
witnessing firsthand the cost and risks we continue to face. Since
then, I have been determined to help keep our homes insurable,
our rates lowered, and our families, not our properties, as our
only concern during an emergency. Through my planned home-
hardening initiative, our city can pursue this by reducing property
risk, leveraging state resources, and working directly with insurers
- all without the need of our city’s finances.
As your councilmember, I will fight to preserve and build upon the
achievements and values of our community. This means ensuring
our budget stays balanced, maintaining our nationally recognized
public safety, and keeping our taxes and costs as low as possible. At
the same time, I will lead our city towards supporting our families
and properties in new and meaningful ways, working to ensure our
homes are insured, and our neighborhoods are best prepared for
any emergencies.
I respectfully ask for your vote so I may serve our community
with the responsibility, dedication, and transparency necessary to
preserve our remarkable city. reese4council.com
