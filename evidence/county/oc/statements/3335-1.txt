KAREN SHAW
Lowell Joint School District, Trustee
As a proud member of the Lowell Joint School District Board of
Trustees since 2018, it has been a privilege to serve our students,
families, educators, and community. As a former teacher, mother
of Lowell graduates, and grandmother of students in our district, I
bring both professional experience and a lifelong commitment to
public education.
During my tenure, I have helped oversee the successful
implementation of Measures LL and P, ensuring these voter-
approved investments provide safe, modern, and engaging learning
environments for our students.
My priorities remain clear: maintaining fiscal responsibility,
attracting and retaining outstanding teachers and staff, supporting
student achievement and well-being, and ensuring every school
is a safe, inclusive, and inspiring place to learn. I am committed
to transparent leadership, open communication, and strong
partnerships with parents, educators, students, and our community.
Lowell Joint has a proud tradition of excellence. I respectfully
ask for your vote so I may continue serving our community with
integrity, accessibility, and an unwavering commitment to every
student's success.
