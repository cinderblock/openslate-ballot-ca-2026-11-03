JANE WOOD
Teacher/Businesswoman
I am the candidate who understands Irvine Schools through
experience. I engage with our students, employees, and parents
every day.
I’ve been a PTA board member for 12 years, advocating for all
kids and families in our community. I was President for two years
at Woodbury Elementary and am in my third year as President at
Portola High. I’m on the board of Irvine’s Council of PTAs serving all
of IUSD’s 38,000 students. As an educational leader, I have a proven
record of implementing successful initiatives, cutting bureaucracy,
and balancing budgets.
As a credentialed teacher, I’ve taught Pre-Kindergarten to High
School, Special Education, and Adults. I have direct knowledge of
how closed-door boardroom decisions affect our classrooms. We
share priorities: safe schools, world-class education and student-
centered resources. We need transparency, accountability, and
fiscal responsibility for our community. We need engaged leaders,
not ceremonial speakers.
As your trustee, I will work to reduce class size, retain great
teachers, maintain educational excellence, and keep our students
safe, especially our most vulnerable.
I am honored to be chosen by Irvine Teachers Association and
trusted community leaders. I hope to be your choice and your
advocate for Irvine schools.
