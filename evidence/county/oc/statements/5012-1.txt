CECILIA HUPP
Councilmember/Business Owner
As your Councilmember, I have focused on protecting Brea's future
through:
Strong Public Safety
Responsible Leadership
Sound Fiscal Management
I helped arrange the purchase of three new fire engines and
one ladder truck to strengthen emergency response and ensure
our firefighters have the equipment they need to serve our
community. I also secured a grant to establish an Integrated Crime
Center, enhancing public safety by providing law enforcement with
advanced technology and real-time information to better prevent
and respond to crime.
In addition, I helped secure more than $28 million in federal and
state funding, bringing your tax dollars back to Brea to invest in
local priorities rather than leaving them elsewhere. While many
surrounding cities and others across California have sought new tax
measures, I have worked to keep Brea financially strong without
asking residents for additional taxes.
During my time on the City Council, Brea has maintained a balanced
budget and a 25% reserve, ensuring long-term fiscal stability
and preparedness for future challenges. I remain committed to
protecting taxpayers, strengthening public safety, and preserving
the high quality of life that makes Brea an exceptional place to live,
work, and raise a family.
