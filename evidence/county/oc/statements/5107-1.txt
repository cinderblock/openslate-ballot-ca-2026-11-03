KATHLEEN TRESEDER
Councilmember, City of Irvine/Educator
My name is Kathleen Treseder. I am a scientist, not a politician. As a
professor at University of California, Irvine, I work every day to find
facts and uncover truth, to find solutions and push for progress.
For the last four years, I have been honored to serve as your City
Councilmember. I have brought the same approach I bring to my
lab and my classroom to City Hall.
I have pushed for reforms to clean up City Government, including
balancing our budget and banishing corrupt lobbyists. I believe
Irvine will work better for everyone when everyone can be sure
their voice and the needs of their neighborhoods are heard. When
Live Nation wanted a sweetheart deal to profit off public spaces, I
stopped the corporate giveaway.
I have worked to expand access to green spaces and parks. As a
mom and a climate scientist, I know how important protecting our
outdoor spaces are to our health.
I am running for re-election because there is more work to do. We
must strengthen our businesses while protecting our workers. We
must solve the affordable housing crisis to ensure every family can
prosper. And we must never stop fighting for our progressive values
that keep us strong.
