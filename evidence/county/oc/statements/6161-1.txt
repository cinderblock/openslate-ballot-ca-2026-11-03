MIKE GASKINS
Director, El Toro Water District
I bring a unique combination of scientific expertise and extensive
experience to the El Toro Water District. My background in marine
biology and geology, coupled with 40+ years of experience as an
educator, chemist, labor negotiator and human resource director,
positions me as a highly qualified candidate for this position.
During my tenure as a board member, I have dedicated myself
to ensuring the district’s continued status as one of the most
efficient in Orange County. I am committed to maintaining the
district’s commitment to providing clean, safe drinking water,
effectively treating wastewater, and producing recycled water
while minimizing costs
