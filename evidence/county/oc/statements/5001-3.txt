IMRAN SHARIEF
Doctor/Businessman
I am running for Mayor to restore trust in City Hall, keep Anaheim
safe, invest in the parts of town that feel forgotten, establish deeper
partnerships with our business community to incentivize growth,
and address the homeless issue with empathy and enforcement.
These principles are rooted in our shared experience living in the
city we call home.
I’m married to my strongest supporter, and have four wonderful
children who went to school in Anaheim. I was born and raised in
Karachi, Pakistan, where my mother and father taught me early:
help people in need. Through school and training, I worked to
become a doctor, and now I spend my days ensuring families get
the help they need. As a doctor, I know how to respond quickly to
save lives, and as the next Mayor I will respond to any situation with
the same precision.
Outside the patient room, I've devoted my life to service. As the
chairman of various local non-profits and community organizations,
I’ve worked with lawmakers locally and nationally to impact policy
that betters our community and building strong coalitions. Getting
the job done for my community is not a partisan issue, it is a practical
one.
