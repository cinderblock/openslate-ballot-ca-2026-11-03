GARY SHIELDS
Retired Deputy Sheriff
Prior to previously serving on the Magnolia School District Board
of Trustees from 2014 - 2022, I served our community as a Deputy
Sheriff within the Orange County Sheriff's Department for 33 years.
During that time I worked to provide safe avenues of travel for our
students in my jurisdiction in the areas surrounding Robert M Pyles
STEM Academy, Dr. Jonas Salk School, and Esther L. Walter School.
Having been a resident in the Magnolia School District since 1977,
I have truly developed an appreciation for the community and am
proud of the services that the Magnolia School District provides
to our students. My children both attended Dr. Jonas Salk School,
Kindergarten through 6th grade and they continued on through
Dale Junior High, graduating from Magnolia High School. Currently,
all four of our grandchildren attend local public schools, with one of
them recently promoted from the Magnolia School District’s Walt
Disney School.
With my experience in law enforcement, as a parent, grandparent,
and a longstanding community member, I look forward to this
opportunity to return to the Board of Trustees to continue to foster
an environment of collaboration, integrity, and transparency for the
families here in the Magnolia School District.
