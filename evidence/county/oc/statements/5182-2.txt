JONATHAN ST. CLAIR
General Manager
I'm running for city council because Orange is my home — I'm
raising two kids in our public schools, and my wife owns a small
business here. I've seen firsthand what it takes for a family and a
business to thrive in this city, and I want to bring that perspective
to City Hall.
My priorities are straightforward: make Orange a place where
businesses want to open, grow, and hire, so we have good jobs and
a strong tax base without overburdening residents. I'll work to cut
red tape for local entrepreneurs, support smart development that
fits our neighborhoods, and make sure growth actually benefits the
people who already live here.
Child safety is personal to me. I'll push for safe routes to school,
well-lit and secure parks, and real coordination between families,
schools, first responders, and local businesses — because a safe
community is the foundation everything else is built on.
I'm not interested in partisan fights. I'm interested in practical
results: a city that works for families, welcomes business, and moves
forward.
