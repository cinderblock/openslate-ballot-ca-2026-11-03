TIM STOAKS
Retired Construction Executive
The question is not, what have I done in the past, the question is,
what is my vision for Newport Beach.
I am Tim Stoaks and I love this town.
Relieve traffic congestion in CdM by eliminating the toll booth at
Newport Coast Drive so that South County Commuters go around
CdM and not through it;
Enhance the state mandate City’s General plans requirement for
additional housing by annexation of the 114-Acre Santa Ana
Country Club to the City of Newport Beach. Grow the city;
Constructing, through strong fiscal management, a multi-
functional community center for West Newport;
Newport does not need a New Police Headquarters; it needs a
substation concept for where the challenges are. Focusing on the
Peninsula where we have consistent police and emergency services
requirement;
Protecting our Civic Center sculpture garden to include all major
parks in the city;
Fully fund and expand senior services programming like the Oasis
Senior Center;
Listen to neighborhood organizations and local residents to best
determine what the city can do for them in their neighborhoods.
Government is here to listen to the community and My job, after
listening, is to make those needs and wants into realities.
Keeping Newport, Newport.
