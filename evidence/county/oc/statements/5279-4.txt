KHAI DAO
AGE: 60
Business Owner/Administrator
It’s time to restore independent leadership and accountable
government in Westminster.
For too long, City Hall has been influenced by political factions,
special interests, and personal agendas. I am running as an
independent candidate because I believe every decision should be
based on what is best for our residents—not on political alliances
or predetermined outcomes.
As your Councilmember, I will listen to the people, carefully
review the facts, seek expert advice, and make decisions that
protect Westminster’s long-term interests. I believe every taxpayer
deserves an honest, transparent government that spends public
funds responsibly, respects the law, and remains accountable to the
community.
I will support open meetings, encourage meaningful public
participation, and ensure every resident has the opportunity to be
heard. Our City employees and professional staff should be allowed
to do their jobs without unnecessary political interference.
I will not serve any political organization, special-interest
group, or individual. My loyalty will be to the residents of
Westminster alone. I will vote my conscience, protect taxpayer
dollars, strengthen public trust, and work to improve public safety,
city services, and our quality of life.
If elected, I will be an independent voice, an advocate for
transparency, and your watchdog at City Hall.
