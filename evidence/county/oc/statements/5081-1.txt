THU-HA NGUYEN
AGE: 50
Cancer Scientist/Teacher
Thu-Ha Nguyen for Mayor of Garden Grove
Former City Councilmember & Mayor Pro Tem - Cancer Scientist -
Community Leader - Educator
Garden Grove is more than my hometown—it is where my family
found freedom, opportunity, and the chance to build a better life.
Today, I want every family to have the opportunity to live in a
safe, affordable, and thriving community. As a Vietnamese refugee,
I learned that resilience, hard work, and service can overcome any
challenge. Those values continue to guide my commitment to our
city.
For more than 25 years, I have worked as a Clinical Scientist
and educator, advancing genetic testing and cancer diagnostics to
improve lives. As your former Councilmember and Mayor Pro Tem,
I strengthened public safety, invested in parks and neighborhoods,
supported local businesses, protected taxpayers, and expanded
resources for seniors, families, and mental health services.
My vision is to make Garden Grove the Gem of Orange County
—a safer, stronger, more affordable, and more united city where
businesses thrive, neighborhoods flourish, and every resident has
the opportunity to succeed.
Together, we can build a Garden Grove every generation is proud
to call home.
