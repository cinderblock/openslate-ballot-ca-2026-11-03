CHAD WANKE
Mayor/Businessman
As the Placentia Mayor, I am proud to have lived in Placentia for
over 31 years. My wife Leonie and I could not have chosen a better
city to be raising our four children in.
As a three-time Mayor and current 4th District Councilmember,
I have worked successfully to bring dramatic improvements to
Placentia and the 4th District including:
A balanced 2026 budget based on my proposed amendments
Repaving miles of arterial roads and neighborhoods who hadn’t
had repairs for decades
Added 10 new police officers
A new playground and amenities at Parque De Arroyo Verde
A new bridge on Golden Avenue
The recent improvements have helped set Placentia on the right
track, but there is still more to be done. If you re-elect me as your
City Councilmember, my focus will be to:
Ensure that the people of Placentia are the focus of the city
government
Keep us safe by continuing to support our Police and Firefighters
Continue rebuilding our roads and infrastructure
Fight for continued balanced budgets that benefit Placentia
residents, not the bureaucracy
Please join my supporters and vote to re-elect Chad Wanke
for Placentia City Council 4th District. Call me anytime at
714-606-1973.
