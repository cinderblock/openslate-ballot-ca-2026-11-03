IVAN KOKOS
Parent/Orange County Businessman
My parents and I immigrated to the United States when I was a
toddler. A United States Marine Corps veteran, I’m the first in my
family to graduate from college.
My wife and I chose to raise our family here, and I’m the candidate
running for Trustee Area 1 who has a child attending Orange Unified
School District schools.
Seeing our schools firsthand as a parent, I’m worried about what
I see: students are not learning reading, writing, and math at their
grade level. With my professional experience managing $2.5 billion
in government loans, I’m concerned about the Orange Unified
School District’s structural deficits: its own projections show this is
unsustainable.
If you elect me to the Orange Unified School District Board, I will:
Ensure our students learn reading, writing, and math
Push for our graduates to be ready for the workforce or college
Fight for fiscal responsibility: spending should go to classrooms,
not administration
Make sure parents and teachers are partners in students’
education
Recruit and retain great teachers with excellent salaries,
supplies, and support

Anaheim Councilwoman Natalie Meeks
Orange Councilmembers Denis Bilodeau, Kathy Tavoularis, Jon
