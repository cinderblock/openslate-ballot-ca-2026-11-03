DAVID JOHNSON
AGE: 57
Governing Board Member, Westminster School District
I will focus on restoring civility and transparency at City Hall,
supporting our city staff, growing revenue without raising taxes and
fees, reducing blight, improving safety, improve ethics, and bringing
great restaurants and stores to Area 1.
As President of the Westminster School District Board, I led the
restoration of professionalism, improved accountability, hired an
award-winning superintendent, and led the development of the
district's first long-term strategic plan. As an Eagle Scout myself, I
am bringing Scouting to every WSD school. Today, Westminster is
recognized for strong leadership, academic progress, and known as
the safest school district in Orange County.
I also founded the Westminster Homeless Task Force, establishing
the city's Navigation Center while supporting practical solutions to
reduce crime and vagrancy; but we need to do much more. I led the
Adopt-A-Park program and advocated for a fully accessible park for
residents with disabilities.
I have proudly called Westminster home for more than 20 years,
where my wife Holly and I raised our two children.
I’m a management consultant, former Vice Chancellor, and I have
a proven record of bringing people together, solving problems, and
delivering results. I would be honored to earn your vote.
