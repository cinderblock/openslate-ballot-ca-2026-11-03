AMORY HANSON
AGE: 29
Historic Resources Boardman
I have been honored to have a front row seat to municipal politics
in Huntington Beach for 12 years. I truly believe that I can bring a
unique historic perspective. When elected, I will work to create a
Huntington Beach performing arts center, to provide a space for
Huntington Beach’s many performing arts societies. I will work to
create a Huntington Beach historical museum to highlight the great
history of Huntington Beach. I will ask for a study session to review
and revise Huntington Beach’s park naming ordinance to allow
more individuals to be honored at Huntington Beach’s many parks.
I will allow the full 3 minutes at city council meetings when I am
Mayor and work to codify this as an ordinance. Most importantly, I
will continue to support our environment and the fight against high
density development. I encourage a vote for Mr. Amory Hanson, a
historic choice, for Huntington Beach City Council. to learn more go
to amoryhanson2026.blogspot.com
