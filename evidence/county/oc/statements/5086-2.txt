THOMAS QUOC THAI NGUYEN
Media Businessman
I humbly ask for your vote to become the next City Council Member
for District 3.
As a media businessman with decades of experience, I have the
expertise to make sound decisions. For 40 years, I have worked
alongside business owners, laborers, and professionals throughout
Orange County. I understand the challenges our community faces.
A strong City Council focused on public safety, infrastructure,
expedited home improvement permitting, and affordable housing
will better serve our valued residents.
Throughout my life, I have committed to giving back. As
your Council Member, I will ensure public participation in city
government. I will prioritize business growth, job creation, practical
solutions to homelessness, cultural events that showcase diversity
and strengthen our vibrant community, and health and wellness
programs that improve the quality of life in District 3.
I am fully committed to accountability and transparency.I will work
tirelessly to make District 3 more prosperous while restoring trust
in government.
As your City Council Member, I will listen to every voice, serve every
constituent, and work every day to improve District 3. I respectfully
ask for the opportunity to serve and achieve these goals together.
Thank you in advance for your support and your vote.
/s/ Thomas Quoc Thai Nguyen
