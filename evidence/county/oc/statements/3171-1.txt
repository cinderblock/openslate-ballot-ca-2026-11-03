ANDREA YAMASAKI
Governing Board Member, Orange Unified School
District, Trustee Area 1
Orange Unified parents and our community deserve leaders with
an extensive history of actual service in our schools and beyond, a
proven track record as a capable leader, and the best interests of
students as their priority. As your current Governing Board Member
of Trustee Area 1, I have all these qualities, that is why I am endorsed
by our classroom teachers, as well as hundreds of parents and
community members.
My service to this district started decades ago. As a parent of three
OUSD children, I volunteered tutoring students and facilitating
school-wide programs. I then expanded my responsibilities to
include leadership; serving as PTA President at El Rancho Charter
School and Canyon High School, and as a board member for
many parent groups and booster clubs. My commitment to making
OUSD better for every child, not just my own, led me to co-
found C.A.R.E., the parent-led grassroots effort that helped pass
Measure S, providing critical repairs and upgrades for our high
school campuses.
Now that the high school projects are nearly completed, it is
time to refocus on the elementary campuses. Fiscal responsibility
enabled Orange Unified to fund $40 million in elementary
campus improvements this year. Investments in our facilities are
investments in our students and community.
The progress our district has made the past two years towards
making OUSD the district of excellence our students deserve has
been remarkable, I am grateful to be part of it. Our test scores
are climbing, graduation rates are one of the best in the county
and multiple systems have been implemented to further support
students to continue these improvements.
My priorities:
Fiscal responsibility: ensuring your tax dollars are spent
transparently and deliver visible results in classrooms.
Student safety and well-being: strengthening policies that
protect students.
Academic opportunities: maintaining and expanding a variety of
academic programs to serve students’ interests and needs, from
Advanced Placement to Career Training.
I know the importance of listening to and building relationships with
parents and community partners, and I stay involved and engaged.
I serve on the board of multiple school-site Foundations, the
Orange County School Boards Association, and serve as president
of the state-wide Asian Pacific Islander School Board Members
Association. I am readily accessible to our students, staff and
families, often visiting school sites and participating in school
events.
I respectfully ask for your vote to keep experienced, accountable
leadership on the Orange Unified School Board, leadership
grounded in community service, responsible stewardship, and an
unwavering commitment to student success.
