MARK NGUYEN
Director, Midway City Sanitary District
It has been an honor to serve as your Director on the Midway City
Sanitary District Board. I am running for re-election to continue
delivering reliable, affordable, and accountable services for our
community.
During my tenure, MCSD has maintained one of the lowest trash
rates in Orange County while keeping the District financially strong.
We have zero debt, grown our reserves, and protected taxpayer
dollars through careful planning and responsible budgeting. These
results do not happen by accident; they come from discipline,
transparency, and a commitment to putting residents first.
We are also preparing for the future. The District has completed
a comprehensive Sewer Master Plan and begun implementation
to strengthen our infrastructure, improve long-term reliability,
and guide smart investments for generations to come. We have
acquired strategic real estate holdings to support future expansion
and provide long-term value to the District.
I am proud that MCSD has earned awards for fiscal transparency
and operational innovation, recognizing our commitment to sound
financial management and forward-thinking technology.
With your support, I will continue working to keep rates low,
services dependable, finances debt-free, and decisions focused on
all residents and future generations.
Humbly, I ask for your vote. Thank you again
