GERMAINE NEUMANN
AGE: 52
Mother/Businesswoman
It’s time to put students first for a change.
I'm running for the Anaheim Union High School District Board
because every student deserves a champion who will stand up for
our classrooms, support our teachers, and make decisions based on
what's best for our community.
As a parent and longtime Anaheim resident, I've spent nearly
two decades supporting our local schools. My husband Khoi and
I proudly raised our son through the Spanish Dual Immersion
pathway at Benito Juarez Elementary, Sycamore Junior High, and
Anaheim High School because we believe in the promise of public
education.
I've served in PTA leadership, booster organizations, and district
committees, always working alongside parents, teachers, and
school staff to strengthen our schools. When the AUHSD School
Board voted to eliminate more than 250 teaching positions in 2024,
I stood with parents, teachers, students, and our community to
protect classroom resources because I believe we deserve better.
I also bring the financial experience needed to responsibly oversee
a multi-million-dollar school district. I hold a Master of Business
Administration and have extensive experience in budgeting,
financial oversight, public contracting, and accountability. I know
we can make responsible financial decisions without losing sight of
what matters most: our students.
Every Student. Every Classroom. Every Day.
