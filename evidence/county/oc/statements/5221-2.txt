DAN SHIMELL
AGE: 40
Attorney
I'm running for Mayor because Santa Ana is my home, and I believe
in the strength, resilience, and heart of our community.
As an attorney, I advocate for the rights of others and understand
that lasting solutions begin with listening. I will fight for your voice
and lead with integrity, accountability, and transparency. Working
together, we can strengthen our local economy, support small
businesses, improve access to affordable housing, and create more
opportunities for families to succeed and thrive. I will stand with you
and ensure your voice is heard.
Recently, I have felt the fear and uncertainty affecting many
members of our community — including my own family — during
ICE raids and other immigration enforcement. No one should have
to live in fear or feel disconnected from the city they call home. As
Mayor, I will work to build a local government that is compassionate,
accessible, and worthy of the public’s trust — where every resident
is treated with dignity and respect.
Together, we can strengthen public participation in government
and foster an environment where every resident feels proud to call
Santa Ana home. I would be honored to earn your trust and your
vote. I will always have your back.
