BRANDON SCHOONER
AGE: 33
City of Buena Park Commissioner
Buena Park is home. It’s where I’ve seen how local government
shapes daily life - our streets, parks, safety, and whether people feel
heard.
I serve as a Buena Park Commissioner, where I’ve brought
forward ideas for cleaner neighborhoods, native landscaping, parks,
and better use of public space. I am also a certified OCART
volunteer, helping with traffic control, clerical work, and large-
animal handling.
My background as a designer shapes how I approach public service.
It taught me to stay curious, look at problems from more than one
angle, work within real limits, and carry an idea into something
useful.
I’m proud to have earned the support of Orange County
Professional Firefighters, Mayor Connor Traut, Vice Mayor Lamiya
Hoque, and Council Members Susan Sonne and Joyce Ahn.
Families feel the cost of living every day. I will work to keep city fees
and utility costs down, support local businesses, and create good-
paying jobs close to home.
I believe good government should be practical and curious - willing
to ask why we do things the way we do, and how to do them better.
Safe neighborhoods, reliable services, and careful use of public
money come first.
I humbly ask for your vote.
