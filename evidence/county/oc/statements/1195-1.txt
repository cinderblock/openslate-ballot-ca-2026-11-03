ARMEN KURDIAN
REPUBLICAN
Retired Navy Captain
AGE: 55
I served our country for 25 years in the Navy, retiring as a Captain.
In uniform, I led military, civilian, and union teams, managed over
$100 million, and delivered vital navigation systems on time and
under budget, saving taxpayers nearly $10 million.
Congress wastes billions every year on special interest giveaways. It
gave California's high-speed rail project $2.5 billion, yet it has never
carried a passenger. Until Congress stops wasting money, it has no
business demanding more. Orange County is taxed enough already.
I will oppose every tax increase.
As a veteran, VA reform is personal to me. I will enforce wait time
standards at the VA, ensure no one who served our country spends
a night on the street, and let veterans use local providers when the
VA cannot provide timely care. I will lower drug prices by preventing
prescription-drug middlemen from charging customers more than
pharmacies.
Families chose Orange County for single-family neighborhoods and
lower density. Government should not rewrite the rules against
our wishes. Residents, not politicians or outside special interests,
should decide how our land is used and our neighborhoods grow.
Congress has no right to use federal funding to strong arm our
communities into surrendering that right.
Job creators are fleeing California. Without a strong manufacturing
sector, everyone loses. I will work with business and labor to rebuild
manufacturing and shipbuilding, expand apprenticeships and trade
education, cut regulations, and create good-paying careers close
to home.
I humbly ask for your vote.
