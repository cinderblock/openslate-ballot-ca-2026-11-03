AMY CHOI-WON
AGE: 50
Parent/Foundation Boardmember
For nearly two decades, I have proudly served the Fullerton
School District community as a parent, volunteer, advocate, and
neighbor. My commitment to public education is both personal and
professional because I believe every child deserves the opportunity
to succeed.
I earned my Ph.D. in Chemistry from UC San Diego and have
dedicated my career to helping students prepare for bright
futures. As a chemistry professor, small business owner, and
Partner Engagement Specialist with the North Orange County
Regional Occupational Program, I have connected students
with meaningful career pathways and strengthened partnerships
between education, business, and our community. I also serve on
the boards of several Fullerton nonprofit organizations.
As your Trustee, I will focus on strengthening academic
fundamentals, expanding STEM and career education, preparing
students for an AI-driven future, supporting and retaining
exceptional teachers, improving communication with families, and
ensuring responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars.
Every child deserves access to an excellent education regardless of
their ZIP code or background. Strong public schools create stronger
families, a stronger workforce, and a stronger Fullerton.
I would be honored to earn your trust and your vote.
