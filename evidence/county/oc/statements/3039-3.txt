JESUS SILVA
AGE: 62
Teacher/Business Owner
I have dedicated my career to uplifting the lives of students and
improving our community.
First, as a teacher for Fullerton School District’s Nicolas Junior High
School, where I taught math for over two decades.
Then as a Fullerton City Council Member and Mayor, where
I addressed homelessness, invested in roads, and helped
neighborhoods during the unprecedented challenges of COVID.
I am running for the North Orange County Community College
District Board to use my unique educational and civic experience
to ensure that everyone has access to high-quality and affordable
education.
On our Community College Board, I will focus on providing students
with the resources needed to earn an associate’s degree, further
their education by transferring to a four-year college, or begin their
professional career.
I will increase training programs, workforce development, student
housing and partnerships with the private sector, trade associations
& municipalities.
I am honored to be endorsed by Assemblymember Sharon Quirk-
Silva, Congressmembers Lou Correa & Linda Sanchez, State Senator
Tom Umberg, Assemblymember Avelino Valencia, Orange County
Supervisors Katrina Foley & Vince Sarmiento, hardworking union
families, and many more school board members, elected officials
and community leaders.
I hope to earn your support as well. With your vote I will be a Board
member who opens doors, creates opportunities, and speaks for all
of North Orange County.
