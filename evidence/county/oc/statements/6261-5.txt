JILL COSS
Financial Service Professional
For five years, I have been proud to call Trabuco Canyon home.
Like many residents, I have watched our water and sewer bills rise
sharply while families are asked to absorb repeated increases and
growing long-term debt.
For more than 15 years as a financial service professional, I have
helped families evaluate complex financial decisions, understand
costs and risks, and choose solutions that protect their long-term
interests. I will bring that same financial discipline, careful analysis,
and accountability to the TCWD Board.
I support consolidation with Irvine Ranch Water District as the
strongest path toward meaningful and lasting rate relief. Any
final agreement must protect reliable service, responsibly address
existing debt and infrastructure, and preserve fair representation
for our community. This opportunity should be pursued with
urgency and should not be undermined by unnecessary borrowing
or financial commitments that could make consolidation more
difficult.
As your Director, I will advocate for affordable rates, responsible
budgeting, open communication, and full public disclosure before
major decisions are made. I will ask tough questions, listen to
residents, and put ratepayers first.
