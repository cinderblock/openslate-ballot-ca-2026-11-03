REGINA CUADRA
Parent/Teacher
As an Elementary Special Education teacher in La Habra, a longtime
PTA leader, and most importantly, a parent of children who have
spent their entire educational journey in our public schools, I am
deeply rooted in this district. I share similar hopes, concerns, and
aspirations as many parents in our community and will bring this
unique blend of professional expertise and personal investment to
the board.
Our schools deserve leadership that understands the daily realities
of our district from every angle: in the classroom, at the PTA
meeting, and around the family dinner table.
As a teacher, I work daily to ensure all students receive the
tailored support, dignity, and high-quality education they deserve.
My commitment to our schools led me to serve in the PTA, where
I steered successful grant-writing initiatives that brought vital new
funding and resources to our students.
I know what makes our schools great, and where we can improve
because I live it every day as both an educator and a parent. I
earned my BA, MA and credential at California State University,
Fullerton. I respectfully ask for your support to bring a practical,
compassionate, and deeply invested voice to our School Board.
