SHAWN DEWANE
Director, Mesa Water District, Division 5
It’s a privilege to serve. Pure, plentiful, affordable water is
fundamental to our quality of life and key to the economic
development of our community.
Maintaining our critical
infrastructure is a big job, over the last 4 years we’ve made a lot of
progress.
AAA Credit Rating: Mesa Water began a $70 million capital
improvement program to rebuild the distribution system for the
next generation. Our AAA credit rating allows us to do this.
Safe and Reliable: We have upgraded cyber security, fire hydrants
and valves, reservoirs, built two new wells, replaced mainline pipes,
which provide fire protection, and improved quality of life for our
community.
30,000 Water Quality Tests: The system delivers over 5 billion
gallons of water to 110,000 local residents and millions that travel
through the service area, including John Wayne Airport. Water
quality engineers complete more than 30,000 water quality tests
per year to ensure our water is safe to drink.
Local Control: We advocate for local control to ensure
accountability, foster local relationships, and to ensure your dollars
stay local. The US Bureau of Reclamation granted the District $250K
to support local Water Supply Improvement Projects.
With your vote, I’ll continue the fight for ratepayers.
