ASHLEIGH AITKEN
Mayor, City of Anaheim
Dear Neighbor,
Anaheim is where I grew up, where my husband and I are raising our
family, and where I’ve found strength and community.
It has been my greatest honor to serve as your Mayor. Four years
ago, I promised to listen to residents, restore integrity to City Hall,
and focus on what matters most to Anaheim families.
As your Mayor, I have worked to:
Enact transparency reforms, restrict lobbying, reset stadium
negotiations, and appoint a city ethics officer.
Improve public safety, including a 14% drop in crime, reduce
emergency response times and build two new fire stations.
Reduce homelessness by 11%, expand outreach and street
medicine, and connect people to shelter, services, and housing.
Build affordable housing and support more than 6,300
households with rent assistance.
Revitalize Beach Boulevard, improve parks and libraries, repair
infrastructure, and bring major investments that create jobs.
I’m an attorney, mom, and longtime community leader with deep
roots in Anaheim. I’m running for re-election to build on our
successes and focus on kitchen table issues like affordability, public
safety, housing, and road repair.
I will be honest, responsive, and focused on residents first.
I’d be honored to earn your vote and continue serving Anaheim.
Ashleigh Aitken
