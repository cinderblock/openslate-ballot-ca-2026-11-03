JAMES "JIM" GOMEZ
Businessman/Councilmember
When I first ran for city council, it was because many of you believed
our community was ready for change. You wanted business growth,
community events and a stronger future. Over the years, I have
worked hard to honor that trust and turn those goals into real
progress.
Today, I am standing before you again, not just because of what we
have accomplished, but because you have asked me to continue
this work. That not only means a lot to me, but what it could mean
for our community.
Together, we have made meaningful strides. We have expanded
opportunities for local businesses, bringing in more places to shop.
Places such as Costco, Walmart, Hobby Lobby and Trader Joe's
are a few businesses that have strengthened our local economy
while creating local jobs. We have prioritized open spaces that give
families and neighbors places to gather and connect, especially
with the recent development of Vin Scully Centennial Park. We have
taken important steps toward building a safer community where
people feel secure, making us one of the safest cities in Orange
County.
At the same time, we have invested in the rehabilitation of our
streets, improving roads, building water pump stations, ensuring we
have an adequate water supply and accessible sidewalks, ensuring
safer travel for everyone in our community. I accomplished all this
while maintaining a balanced budget each year.
I was honored to be recognized as City Elected Leader of the Year
by the Association of California Cities.
These accomplishments did not happen overnight and they were
not achieved alone. They are the result of listening to you, working
alongside you and staying committed to what matters most for our
city.
But there is still more to do. On the heels of our Centennial comes
new opportunities and new challenges, but I know that we can
overcome them. I am ready to keep building on the progress we
have made, to keep pushing for smart development, to protect what
makes our city special and to ensure that every resident benefits
from the work we are doing.
I am proud of the progress that we have made together and am
ready to keep moving our community forward. With your support,
I will continue to work hard to deliver the results you deserve and
build a future that reflects our potential as a city.
I’m proudly endorsed by our police, firefighters, teachers and
parents.
