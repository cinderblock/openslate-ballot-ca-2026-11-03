ISURI S. RAMOS
Nonprofit Executive Director
I'm the daughter of Mexican immigrants who laid their roots on
Minnie Street. Growing up, my family lost our housing and we relied
on public transportation. The library was my second home, and the
boxing club at the Jerome Center taught me discipline. I served as
a Youth Commissioner and graduated from Santa Ana High School
and UC San Diego.
After college, I organized with Lacy Neighborhood residents.
Together, we won 100% affordable housing at Legacy Square,
pushed the city to build Mariposa Park as the community
envisioned, and fought for Santa Ana’s rent control ordinance,
capping rent at 3%. As a Planning Commissioner, I’ve championed
development projects that provide good jobs and prioritize
affordable housing; and I led the effort to keep illegal smoke shops
out of our neighborhoods.
I’m running to continue fighting for Santa Ana residents. When
elected, my priorities will be to address our housing costs,
neighborhood safety, and to protect our immigrant and mixed-
status families by ensuring our city is a champion for our
constitutional rights, not another obstacle to overcome.
I respectfully ask for your vote.
Endorsements: National Union of Healthcare Workers, Esperanza
Tenants Union, Supervisor Vicente Sarmiento, and Councilwoman
Jessie Lopez.
