JOSE MEDRANO
AGE: 56
Mayor City of La Habra
My name is Jose Medrano, I currently serve as Mayor of La Habra.
My wife and I have been fortunate enough to raise our daughters
in our community and local schools. As a public safety advocate
and law enforcement officer, I feel it is important to be engaged
in our community and help keep it safe. As a Mexican Immigrant
and U.S. Citizen, I find it important to include public service in
my life and help those who seek the same opportunity. I have
over 30 years of Law Enforcement experience. I am a retired
Detective for the Los Angeles County District Attorney’s office
having investigated Human Trafficking, Juvenile crimes and other
criminal investigations. I have a B.A. in Business Administration
from CSUF. As a father I strongly believe in education. Two of my
daughters are graduates of University of California schools and one
attends USD as a student athlete.
I have been committed to making local government more efficient,
responsive and accountable to everyone.I continue to make Public
Safety a priority through my in person “Crime Watch” meetings in
various neighborhoods throughout the City of La Habra. Through
my leadership on the council I continue to support the city’s
fiscally responsible direction resulting in balanced budgets. The city
has completed significant capital improvement projects and road
improvements. My support of open spaces and the revitalization
of parks continues with the recent opening of the Dog Park and
other amenities at Centennial Park and improvements at other
parks. As Mayor I continue to support our efforts to address traffic
through signal synchronization on Imperial Highway, Lambert Road,
Hacienda Blvd, Whittier Blvd and traffic calming measures in
various neighborhoods. I have addressed the homeless issue in La
Habra by supporting our efforts with our two homeless shelters in
neighboring cities and staffing two homeless outreach liaisons. I
continue to support our La Habra Businesses through my weekly
Business of the Week promotion on social media. I am endorsed by
the La Habra Police Association, LA County Firefighters IAFF 1014,
Teachers and Community advocates in La Habra.
