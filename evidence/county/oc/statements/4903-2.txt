GARRETT DWYER
Appointed Incumbent
My wife and I moved to Aliso Viejo in 2004 and bought our home
here in 2005. We have raised our kids in this community, and for 23
years I’ve worked as a mortgage advisor, helping local families with
their home financing.
In October 2025 I was honored to be appointed to the City Council
for District 3, and since then I immediately worked to create our
Know Your Dough financial literacy program, a collaboration with
Junior Achievement, two chambers of commerce and the CTE
program at Aliso Niguel High School to benefit every student in the
city. This program is now funded for three years.
I led our ad hoc committee that found our new city manager and I
voted to approve our new pump track. I voted to lower speed limits
throughout the city to improve safety for residents and I volunteer
weekly at our food bank. I am also encouraging the measured
growth of our city with new homes for our citizens to purchase.
I have a proven commitment to keep Aliso Viejo safe, family-friendly
and fiscally sound, and I’d be grateful for your vote to continue
serving the city. Please go to www.votegarrettdwyer.com for more
information.
