ANIL D. PATEL
AGE: 53
Project Manager/Businessowner
For the past 18 years, Lake Forest has been my family's home, and
I’m running for City Council because I believe in giving back to the
community that has given us so much. My wife, Alka, and I have
raised our two children here, operated a small business in our city,
and have been actively involved in serving our neighbors.
I serve on the City’s Traffic and Parking Commission, and as
a Board Member of my homeowners association, where I work
collaboratively with neighbors to address community concerns. I
have also completed the City’s Civic Academy, and volunteer as a
CERT member, a Volunteer in Parks participant, and as an Assistant
Scoutmaster for a local troop.
Professionally, I bring nearly 30 years of experience helping public
agencies manage budgets, improve infrastructure, and deliver cost-
effective solutions. I hold a degree in Building Engineering and an
MBA from UCLA, combining technical expertise with sound financial
management.
As your Councilmember, I will focus on keeping Lake Forest safe,
protecting our neighborhoods, supporting local businesses, and
ensuring taxpayer dollars are spent wisely. I will listen to residents,
make thoughtful decisions, and always put our community first.
I respectfully ask for your support and consideration.
