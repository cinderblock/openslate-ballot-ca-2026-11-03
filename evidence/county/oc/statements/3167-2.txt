ANDY GODINEZ
AGE: 44
Parent/Code Officer
I am a proud Newport-Mesa Unified alum and lifelong Westside
Costa Mesa resident, and I am honored and excited for the
opportunity to serve as your Board of Education Trustee for Area 7.
I attended Whittier Elementary, Ensign Intermediate, and Newport
Harbor High. The first in my family to attend college, I chose a career
in public service that allows me to give back to the community that
shaped me.
I worked for five years for NMUSD and understand our school
district facilities and operations. I have now worked for 18 years
with the City of Costa Mesa (including roles with Parks and
Community Services, City Clerk’s office, and Code Enforcement)
through which I have developed broad community relationships
and a deep understanding of how government works including
budgets, processes, and partnerships. I know how to get things
done while staying rooted in our community and finding ways to
open doors and uplift others.
Throughout my life, I have remained active in youth development
and local leadership:
Board Member - Leadership Tomorrow
Board Member - Costa Mesa City Employee Association
Youth Mentor - Bright Youth
Youth Sports Coach and Program Volunteer Lead - MIKA CDC
Volunteer - Think Together, Love Costa Mesa, and CM Mutual Aid
My personal and professional experience combined with
longstanding community relationships and passion for serving
our neighborhood makes me uniquely qualified to be your Board
Trustee. When homegrown community leaders are present and
engaged, young people feel more empowered to believe in
themselves. As someone who has walked in their shoes, overcome
challenges, and paved my own path, I want to empower and support
our students to do the same.
This year, our daughter will start her education in Newport-Mesa.
My wife and I are determined that she and her Westside CM peers
have better access to high quality education, best-in-class facilities,
and enriching extracurricular opportunities that inspire them to
dream. Having attended schools in both Westside Costa Mesa
and Newport Beach, I have seen the disparities in facilities and
resources firsthand. I want to meet that challenge head on with out-
of-the-box solutions. I’ll partner hand-in-hand with our parents,
teachers, administrators, and community organizations to make
sure every student is given an opportunity to thrive.
I’m honored to continue serving the community that shaped me. I’m
ready to help all NMUSD students achieve their full potential.
I ask for your support and your vote.
