ANDREW GRUEL
AGE: 46
Huntington Beach City Councilmember
Appointed City Councilmember
Serving on the Huntington Beach City Council has been the honor
of my life.
My wife and I are raising our four children here while owning
and operating a local restaurant. Before public service, I built
businesses, created jobs, balanced budgets, and learned that every
dollar matters. That's the perspective I bring to City Hall.
Since joining the City Council, I've fought to protect taxpayers,
support our police and first responders, defend local control,
preserve our beaches, parks, and neighborhoods, and keep
Huntington Beach safe, financially responsible, and accountable
to the people who live here. I am honored to have earned the
endorsement of the Huntington Beach Police Officers' Association
for my commitment to public safety.
I believe government should live within its means, be transparent
with every tax dollar, and focus on delivering the essential services
residents deserve while protecting the character of our community.
I will continue to lead with integrity, common sense, and an
unwavering commitment to putting Huntington Beach first.
I respectfully ask for your vote to continue serving our community.
