LAUREN KLEIMAN
Mayor/Businesswoman
Serving as your Mayor has been an honor and a privilege.
I have worked tirelessly over my first four years, demanding
accountability, fighting for quality of life and public safety for the
residents of our beautiful city.
My efforts to overhaul the city’s approach to homelessness have
resulted in our unsheltered Point in Time Count dropping from 96
to 8, and cutting taxpayer spending.
Crime has fallen 12.7%, with burglaries down 16%. I have
championed new technology, more resources and wraparound
support for the men and women who dedicate their lives to keeping
our city safe, and am proud to have earned the endorsement of
the Newport Beach Firefighters Association and the Newport Beach
Police Association.
Some of my other successful initiatives include expansion of the
Safety Enhancement Zone, doubling the NBPD Horseback Mounted
Unit, banning of Kratom, nitrous oxide, and adoption of new smoke
shop regulations, calling for dedicated officers for Corona del Mar
and Fashion Island, and auditing the State, resulting in the closure
of over a dozen Sober Living homes citywide.
As the Mayor who brought you “3rd Thursdays" and the mural
program, I respectfully ask for your vote so that I can build on the
progress I have made, for your family and mine.
