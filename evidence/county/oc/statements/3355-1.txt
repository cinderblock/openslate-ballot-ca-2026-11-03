DAVID CLIFFORD
AGE: 42
Planning Commissioner/Businessowner
As a 3rd generation Huntington Beach resident and homeowner,
I understand the needs of the community. Our suburban
neighborhoods value public schools that are safe and focus on
student achievement. I am running for the Ocean View School
District Board of Trustees for one reason; our children.
My education credentials include a BS in Aerospace Engineering
and an MS in Supply Chain Management where I continue to serve
CSULB in an advisory role. My career in Transportation has allowed
me to give back to local communities where I donate time at
colleges and high schools to mentor and educate youth about
college and career opportunities in the Transportation sector.
I have a passion for public service and being actively involved in
my childrens school, where I serve as Chairman of the Village View
Elementary School Site Council. Also serving the city of Huntington
Beach as a Planning Commissioner and formerly as a Public Works
Commissioner, I value the importance of good governance and
want to extend my community contributions districtwide to affect
positive change in what matters most, our children.
When the district tried closing schools I worked closely with parents
in a grass-roots effort and succeeded in keeping our schools open.
As an advocate for taxpayers, I will promote fiscal responsibility and
address the structural budget deficit without raising your taxes. Let
me repeat, I will not raise your taxes.
My goal is for the district to get back to the basics including math,
science, reading, writing, and history.
I will support curriculum that emphasizes STEM/STEAM principles
and programs like the Environmental Science Farm at Golden View
Elementary.
I will advocate for policies that prioritize student achievement.
I will keep parents involved in their childrens education.
While parents are asked to donate money for field trips, district
Administration continues to receive raises. While test scores
continue to decline, the board is focusing on building workforce
housing on closed school sites.
It's time for change.
The district needs a community-focused leader who will promote
accountability and make informed decisions. I am invested in our
community and dedicated to creating a positive lasting impact on
our children's education and well-being.
Please vote for David Clifford.
Visit www.clifford4ovsd.com to read my full story.
