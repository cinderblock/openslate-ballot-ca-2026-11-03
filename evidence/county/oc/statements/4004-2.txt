CONNOR TRAUT
Mayor/Business Owner
Dear Neighbor,
Orange County has long been the epitome of the American Dream –
a place where working people can secure a good job that supports
a family, with parks and schools that serve our next generation,
and public services that help our seniors and most vulnerable
neighbors.
I’m running for Supervisor to solve problems and deliver results. My
experience gives me the tools to fulfill Orange County’s promise,
and I’ll work hard to:
Address affordability to help working families stay in O.C.
Reduce homelessness and focus on mental health and addiction.
Combat crime and ensure fast emergency response times.
Enhance our parks and repair our roads and infrastructure.
Build housing and lower barriers for first-time homebuyers.
I have served as a local mayor, city council member, and school
board president. I’m also a business owner, dad, and fourth-
generation Orange County resident. I currently serve as a board
director of the Orange County Fire Authority and board member of
the Boys and Girls Clubs of Central O.C. I earned a bachelor’s degree
and a law degree from Chapman University, and I became an Eagle
Scout with Troop 767.
As a mayor and city council member, I have worked to deliver
meaningful results for the people I serve. I am proud to have:
Improved ambulance response times by adding units.
Lowered energy bills with additional local control.
Created low-cost childcare options for families.
Limited the fees food delivery apps can charge.
Shut down an unsafe residential rehabilitation facility.
Expanded free mobility programs for senior residents.
Built a food pantry for struggling families.
Balanced the city budget every year I have served.
I’m grateful to have the support of groups representing local
firefighters, police officers, nurses, grocery workers, small business
owners, construction workers, and veterans. You can find a full list
of my endorsements at ConnorTraut.com.
The President of the Orange County Professional Firefighters,
Chris Hamm, said: “Firefighters trust Connor Traut because he’s an
experienced leader who always thinks of public safety first.”
I believe high-quality schools, strong community services, and safe
neighborhoods are the key building blocks for Orange County’s
future. My wife, Cheyenne, is a local elementary school teacher
and doctoral candidate, and we are proud that our son is a fifth-
generation Orange County resident.
You can learn more at www.ConnorTraut.com or contact me
directly at (714) 257-5447 or Team@ConnorTraut.com. I’d be
honored to earn your vote.
In Service,
Connor Traut
