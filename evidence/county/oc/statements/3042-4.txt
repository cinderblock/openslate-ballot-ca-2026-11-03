AUSTIN J. EDSELL
Educator/Finance Commissioner
As a proud Coast Community College District alumnus, I know
firsthand how our colleges transform lives. For more than 20 years,
this community has been my home.
As an educator, I prepare students for the future. As a Huntington
Beach Finance Commissioner, I promote fiscal responsibility,
accountability, and transparency. I have also hosted Career Fairs
and Children's Resource Fairs connecting students, families, and job
seekers with valuable opportunities and resources.
As your Trustee, I will:
Expand career education, technical training, and workforce
partnerships while supporting traditional four-year university
pathways, ensuring every student has a roadmap to success.
Strengthen campus safety by working with law enforcement and
first responders to improve emergency planning, preparedness, and
response times.
Keep education affordable through responsible budgeting, long-
term financial planning, and transparency so taxpayer dollars
directly support students and classrooms.
Demand measurable improvements in graduation, university
transfer, and employment rates so every program delivers results
and opportunities for students.
I am endorsed by Orange County Supervisor Janet Nguyen,
Assemblymember Diane Dixon, State Senator Tony Strickland,
Huntington Beach Mayor Casey McKeon, Seal Beach Mayor Lisa
Landau, Garden Grove Mayor Stephanie Klopfenstein, and Orange
County Board of Education Trustee Mari Barke.
