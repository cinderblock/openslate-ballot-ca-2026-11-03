JOAN MALCZEWSKI
Professor/Parent
It has been an honor to serve on the Laguna Beach Unified School
District Board of Education. I bring to this work a deep commitment
to public education, years of experience working with teachers and
school leaders, financial expertise, and a personal commitment as
an LBUSD parent.
My time on the Board has reinforced what I have believed
throughout my career, that strong schools are built on trust,
collaboration, and a shared commitment to students. Every decision
should begin with an effort to create opportunity for students,
support outstanding teachers, and ensure every child has the
opportunity to learn, grow, and thrive. Excellence does not
happen by accident. It requires thoughtful leadership, respect
for professional expertise, strong partnerships with teachers and
families, and a commitment to continuous improvement.
I am a tenured faculty member in history at the University
of California, Irvine, where my research focuses on education
policy and the relationship between schools and democracy.
Prior to moving to Laguna Beach, I served as an administrator
at Columbia University’s Teachers College and at New York
University’s Steinhardt School of Education, where I also served as
a faculty member and prepared and mentored future teachers.
Before entering education, I earned a degree in economics from
the University of Michigan and worked as a CPA in New York.
That experience helps me approach district decisions with careful
analysis, long-term planning, and a commitment to ensuring public
resources support the educational mission of our schools.
I believe public schools are among the most important institutions
in any community. They prepare students not only for college and
careers, but also help them to develop the curiosity, judgment,
and critical thinking skills needed to navigate a changing world. I
am committed to protecting what makes LBUSD exceptional while
continuing to improve student outcomes by strengthening reading
and deep thinking, supporting outstanding educators, ensuring
technology enhances learning, and creating opportunities that
allow every student to thrive.
I will continue working to bring together parents, teachers, staff,
administrators, students, and community members to support our
schools. Strong schools build strong communities, and I remain
committed to thoughtful leadership, trusted relationships, and
keeping students at the center of every decision.
