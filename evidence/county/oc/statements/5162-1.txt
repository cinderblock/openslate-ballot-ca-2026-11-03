SAGA CONROY
AGE: 47
Community Outreach Director
I love Mission Viejo. I lived under oppressive communist rule during
my childhood and immigrated to the United States to pursue
freedom. I earned my citizenship and settled in this beautiful city
with my husband and our two children. I am committed to giving
back to the community that made my American Dream possible.
I currently serve on the Mission Viejo Community Services
Commission and work as Community Outreach Director for Orange
County Supervisor Don Wagner. As a commissioner, I foster events
that contribute to our local economy and oversee the upkeep of our
parks and facilities. In my role with the county, I have gained years of
firsthand experience in municipal budgeting and local government
operations. I have a proven record of solving problems and working
collaboratively to deliver results.
As your Councilmember, I will:
Keep Mission Viejo safe by fully funding our law enforcement and
firefighters.
Maintain fiscal responsibility through prudent budgeting, without
raising taxes.
Protect open spaces and maintain our parks and trails for all
residents to enjoy.
I am honored to have the endorsements of Supervisor Don Wagner,
outgoing Mission Viejo Mayor Pro Tem Brian Goodell, former Mayor
Trish Kelley, and Orange County Board of Education Trustee Lisa
Sparks, Ph.D. To preserve our quality of life and safeguard our
future, I humbly ask for your vote.
