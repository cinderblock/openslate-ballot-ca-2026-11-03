ROBERT GARCIA
DEMOCRATIC
United States Congressman
From Long Beach to Orange County, everyone in our community
wants the same things: A safe community. A better future for our
kids. Leaders who focus on helping working families.
During my two terms in Congress, I have kept these principles at the
center of every vote I have taken and every bill I have introduced.
My priorities include:
Creating good paying jobs and lowering costs
Keeping our communities safe
Improving public education
Protecting the environment and our coast
Affordable housing & health care
Fighting government corruption
My record of results:
Working with local governments, I have helped secure over $500
million in federal grants for programs and projects including
infrastructure improvements, environmental initiatives, community
health care programs, and public safety support.
I have assisted more than 20,000 local residents who needed help
with federal agencies including Passport Services, Veterans Affairs,
Social Security, Immigration support and the IRS.
As the new lead Democrat on the House Oversight and Government
Reform Committee, I help lead investigations into government
corruption, including the release of the Epstein Files. We are
also focused on modernizing government services so the Federal
Government can serve our community better and more efficiently.
My endorsements include California’s firefighters, teachers, health
care workers and over 100 members of Congress and local officials.
I would be honored to receive your vote. Thank you.
