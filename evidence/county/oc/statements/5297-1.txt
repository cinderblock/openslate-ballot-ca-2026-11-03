BRAD MCGIRR
Councilmember, City of Rancho Santa Margarita
Rancho Santa Margarita is safe, clean, and financially secure. To
keep it this way, we need proven leadership.
I am incredibly proud to have served RSM as your Councilmember,
Mayor Pro Tem, and Mayor. Throughout my tenure on the City
Council, I have consistently worked to protect and enhance our
community by:
Prioritizing Public Safety: Fully funding law enforcement to keep
RSM recognized as one of the safest cities in California and
supporting the Orange County Fire Authority as a member of the
OCFA Board of Directors.
Ensuring Fiscal Responsibility: Helping achieve a balanced
budget annually and maintaining extremely healthy financial
reserves.
Enhancing Parks & Community Spaces: Supporting the
construction of the Central Park Splash Pad, the RSM Dog Park
renovation, and capital improvement projects such as our new
Veterans Memorial.
Promoting Environmental Stewardship: Initiating the volunteer-
led ZeroTrash program, protecting our Chiquita Ridge open
space, and supporting RSM’s transition to a "No Kill" animal
shelter.
Boosting Local Business: Supporting the RSM Chamber of
Commerce and fostering a thriving business community.
My commitment to RSM runs deep. Since our city’s inception,
I coached youth sports for 15 years, worked with the Special
Olympics and Big Brothers of Orange County, and started the
ZeroTrash initiative that has removed thousands of pounds of trash
from our city and has provided young people an opportunity to
serve our community – without spending one taxpayer dollar.
Professionally, I earned my Bachelor’s Degree from Cal State
Fullerton, majoring in Geography and Land Use Analysis/Urban
Development. I thereafter completed law school and have
practiced law since 1986. After 37 years in private practice, I
currently serve as an Arbitrator and Mediator.
Moving forward, I remain dedicated to:
Managing our budget with cautious fiscal conservatism.
Fully supporting our law enforcement and firefighters to maintain
our excellent safety status.
Fighting to preserve Chiquita Ridge as open space.
Attracting new businesses and vibrant dining options to our city.
Fostering rewarding volunteer opportunities for all ages.
Rancho Santa Margarita has been my home since 1986. It’s where
my wife, Julie, and I enjoyed our first date—a picnic by the lake—
and where we raised our three children. I love this city, and it’s been
the honor of my life to help it mature into the finest community in
California.
I thank you for your continued support and would be honored to
receive your vote.
