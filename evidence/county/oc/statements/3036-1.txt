PAUL GONZALES
Staff Specialist/Businessman
I have been a resident of Orange County for 45 years and local
small business owner for the past 35 years. I live in Buena Park
with my daughters Paulina, attending Chapman University and
Emily, attending Kennedy High School. I am currently employed
by the Orange County District Attorney’s Office and have been
working there for over 25 years. I am a graduate of California State
University Long Beach with a degree in Public Administration and
have applied it to my profession as a Safety Representative for
Orange County, looking after the safety and well-being of many
employees.
I have served as a Member of the Buena Park Planning
Commission for over 8 years and Member of the Buena Park Traffic
Commission for 8 years. I am a Tier II Volunteer for the Anaheim
Union High School District, working with the Walker and Kennedy
High School Show Choirs.
I am also a small business owner working for over 35 years at the
Cypress College Swap Meet selling watch batteries and bands on
Saturdays and Sundays.
Our community colleges are a tremendous benefit to our
residents seeking further education, enhancing job training skills,
and offering career changing pathways. I’ll work hard to ensure our
colleges stay ahead of the rapidly changing demands of today’s
society.
I sincerely appreciate your vote!
