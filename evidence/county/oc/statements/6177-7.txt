KURT LIND
AGE: 61
Water Business Manager
As a water industry professional and long time Aliso Viejo resident
and ratepayer, I am running for the Moulton Niguel Water District
Board to help deliver reliable, high-quality water at the lowest
practical cost.
I serve as Water Business Manager at Mesa Water District,
focusing on accountability and efficiency for ratepayers. I oversee
independent audits of finance, customer service, information
technology, safety, and performance—helping keep the agency
among Orange County’s most efficient.
I have previously served on MNWD’s Citizens Advisory Committee,
as a Board Member for the Aliso Viejo Community Association
and Aliso Viejo Little League, and was appointed Planning
Commissioner for the City of Aliso Viejo. I currently serve as a
volunteer umpire for Aliso Viejo Little League, supporting fairness
and positive experiences for local youth.
Guided by the call to serve others, I will work to steward MNWD’s
resources faithfully for every ratepayer.
If elected, I will prioritize:
Transparency, efficiency, and accountability
Water-supply reliability through a strong portfolio of supply
options
Strong fiscal oversight to protect ratepayers and keep service
affordable
I bring hands-on water industry experience, community
involvement, and a commitment to serve you, the ratepayer. I
respectfully ask for your vote.
