ASHLEY CINTAS
Local Business Owner
For nearly two decades, I've led large, multi-department operations,
managed complex budgets, created jobs, and built businesses by
bringing people together to solve difficult problems. Choosing to
open Oto Sushi in Laguna Beach wasn't just a business decision; it
was a commitment to this community and its future.
I'm running for City Council because I believe Laguna Beach
deserves leadership that asks the right questions, listens to the
community, and plans for the future. My experience has taught me
that the best decisions come from understanding the full picture,
weighing tradeoffs, and focusing on long-term results, not short-
term reactions.
My priorities are fiscal responsibility, public safety with a strong
focus on wildfire preparedness, and building a strong local
economy while preserving the character that makes Laguna Beach
so special.
Whether you've lived here for decades or are new to Laguna,
whether you're raising a family, building a career, running a
business, creating art, or enjoying retirement, you deserve to have
your voice heard. You deserve a City Council that listens before it
decides, asks the right questions, and plans for the future. That's the
change in leadership I will bring to Laguna Beach.
