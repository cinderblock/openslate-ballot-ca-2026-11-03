KATHRYN FRESHLEY
Director, El Toro Water District
I am a Licensed Professional Engineer with graduate management
education and worked in executive management, engineering and
business/strategic planning.
I presently serve as the President of the board of directors and have
served on the board for 8+ years. With the other members of the
board, we have made good progress ensuring that the district has a
quality executive team and the necessary capital funding to address
the water and sewer infrastructure challenges. Quality leadership
provides employment stability and ensures quality services to our
customers.
I also represent the agency on the South Orange County
Wastewater Authority Board. This authority is critical to the
successful processing and disposal of the solid waste processed at
our treatment plant. The authority is also responsible for ensuring
that any wastewater released to the ocean meets all federal and
state regulations.
I also represent the 35 Orange County Special Districts on the Local
Agency Formulation Commission (OCLAFCO). The Local Agency
Formation Commission of Orange County (OC LAFCO) strives to
ensure the delivery of effective and efficient public services such
as water, sewer, public safety, and parks by local governments to
Orange County residents.
