BERTHA MANZANARES
Social Services Manager
La Habra has been my home for more than 22 years, where my
husband and I proudly raised our two children. They are both
graduates of the La Habra City School District and have become
productive citizens in their communities.
I recently retired after 35 years of public service, including 25 years
with the California Department of Social Services, where I served
in leadership roles dedicated to protecting the health, safety, and
well-being of children in both Los Angeles & Orange County.
Throughout my career, I have partnered with families, educators,
and community organizations to help children learn, grow, and
thrive.
As your School Board Trustee, I will focus on three priorities:
Strengthening academic excellence by ensuring every student
builds a strong foundation in reading, writing, mathematics, and
science
Supporting parents by protecting their rights to be involved in their
children's education and expanding educational opportunities.
Promoting transparency, accountability, and fiscal responsibility.
I will bring integrity, experience, and a commitment to putting
students first, supporting our teachers, and preparing every child
for lifelong success.
Guided by Faith. Dedicated to Families. Committed to Students.
