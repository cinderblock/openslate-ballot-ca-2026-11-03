ERIN SPIVEY
AGE: 45
Retired Librarian
Born and raised in Huntington Beach, I attended local schools and
played local sports. In high school, I volunteered at Oak View Library
and my first job was at the Central Library coffee cart. I love this
city so much that I’m raising my family here. Today, my kids enjoy
the same beaches and play sports on the same fields.
I spent 15 years of public service in schools and libraries. I worked
on government budgets, oversaw dozens of employees, worked
with contractors and volunteers. I built programs from the ground
up that engaged thousands of residents and still exist today.
Now I want to get Huntington Beach back to being Surf City. In a
time of growing deficits, cuts to public safety, and losing lawsuits,
I will instead focus on local improvements, bringing new and old
businesses back to Huntington Beach, and bettering our local
government. I will repair our tattered reputation and invite the
community to come back together.
It's time for change. It’s time to get our amazing City back on track.
It’s time to rebuild the community I grew up with: a small town in
a big city.
I humbly ask for your vote.
