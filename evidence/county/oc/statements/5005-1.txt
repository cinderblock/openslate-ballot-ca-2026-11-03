CARLOS A. LEON
Anaheim City Councilmember
West Anaheim is home.
I grew up in these neighborhoods, attended Maxwell Elementary,
Dale Junior High, and Magnolia High School, and served as an altar
server at St. Justin Martyr Church. I’m the proud son of immigrants. I
know how hard my parents worked to achieve the American dream.
Now, I have the privilege of representing the same neighborhoods
where I was raised on the Anaheim City Council. Over the past
4 years, I have worked tirelessly to address long-standing issues,
tackle community concerns head on and ensure that city hall is
delivering results for hardworking taxpayers, like you and me.
Together, we are addressing homelessness, strengthening public
safety, securing our parks and public spaces, protecting our
neighborhoods, creating good-paying jobs, and working every day
to improve quality of life across District 2.
We live here. We pay taxes. We deserve the best. I will continue
listening to your voice. I want the best for our neighborhoods and
our city.
Join our Anaheim Firefighters, first responders, small businesses,
community leaders, and neighbors across District 2 to re-elect
Mayor Pro Tem Carlos Leon for Anaheim City Council.
I would be honored to continue working for you.
