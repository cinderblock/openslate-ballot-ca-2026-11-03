BRYAN ZAHN
Retired City of Mission Viejo Supervisor
Mission Viejo has been my home since I was 8 years old. As a single
parent, I raised three children here, who graduated from our public
schools in Mission Viejo, just like I did.
As a supervisor, I delivered for Mission Viejo:
Remodeled Matt Davis Park, Crucero Park, Vista del Lago Park,
M.M. O’Neil Park, Pavion Park
Worked with Orange County Fire Authority on fire retardant
material, weed abatement, and brush removal
Regularly coordinated after hours emergency response efforts
with Orange County Sheriff’s Department, Orange County Fire
Authority, and water districts
Ensured balanced multimillion dollar budgets
Helped plan/coordinate Mission Viejo’s Veterans Day, Memorial
Day, and 4th of July events, along with Pacific Symphony on the
Village Green
Worked with Julie Foudy Soccer Camp, public schools, and over
300 Eagle Scouts on sports fields, parks, and open spaces
I’m passionate about my hometown of Mission Viejo. I’m active in
the Mission Viejo Elks Club. I served on the Viejo Little League Board.
I was named City of Mission Viejo Employee of the Year.
If you elect me as your Councilmember, I will:
Ensure strong funding for our public safety
Strengthen wildfire preparedness
Fund school resource officers and crossing guards
Help small businesses by reducing bureaucratic red tape
Enhance parks, trails, and open spaces
I’ve earned endorsements for Council from:
Mission Viejo Mayor Wendy Bucknum and Councilmembers Brian
Goodell, Trish Kelley, Bob Ruesch
Former Mission Viejo Mayors Robert Breton, Dave Leckness
Please vote Bryan Zahn for City Council. zahnforcitycouncil.com
