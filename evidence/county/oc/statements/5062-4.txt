JIM CUNNEEN
Fountain Valley Mayor/Businessman
I’ve proudly made Fountain Valley my home for 33 years. My wife
and our 5 children were all born and raised here. I’m dedicated to
making Fountain Valley an even better place to live, so my children
can eventually raise their own children here.
I’ve worked in private business for 40 years. With my experience in
both the public and private sectors, I know how to run government
more like a business.
As your Fountain Valley Mayor and Councilmember, I’ve delivered
results since you first elected me four years ago:
Hired new Fire and Police Chiefs
New Public Safety Community Risk Assessment and Standards of
Cover
First-ever Fountain Valley Parks Master Plan
New 16-acre expansion at Fountain Valley Sports Park
Balanced budgets, including paying down pension debt
I lead “Mile with the Mayor” walks to ensure City staff and I are
accessible to residents while also encouraging everyone to be
active and enjoy Fountain Valley’s outdoors.
If you re-elect me, I’ll continue fighting for you:
Ensure our firefighters and police have what they need to keep
Fountain Valley safe
Enhance Fountain Valley parks and streets
Maintain complete accountability of our taxpayer dollars
Attract and retain good-paying jobs with healthy businesses
I respectfully ask for your vote.
