MEGHNA PARIKH
Appointed Cypress School District Board Member,
Trustee Area C
As a mother raising three children in Cypress, I know our schools are
the heart of our community. I am committed to keeping our schools
strong and ensuring every student has an opportunity to succeed.
While serving on the Cypress School Board, I provided steady
leadership during a time of change. Earlier this year, I took action to
help protect jobs, preserve important programs and services, and
support a plan to strengthen the district's finances while keeping
district decisions in local hands.
As a public service attorney, I work every day to solve complex
problems and protect public interest. This experience enables me to
ask tough questions, understand details, build consensus, and make
important decisions that put students first.
Looking ahead, I will advocate to expand after-school programs,
support academic excellence, increase learning opportunities in
STEM, arts, and music, strengthen the schools' relationships with
parents, and manage taxpayer dollars responsibly.
I care deeply about the future of our schools and the success of
every Cypress student. I respectfully ask for your vote so we can
continue working to provide the high-quality education Cypress
families expect and every child deserves.
