CAROL MOORE
Council Member City of Laguna Woods
As a Village resident for almost 20 years and Council Member, I
believe I have a good grounding in the issues. I have an MA from the
University of Denver, managed my own industrial supply business
and believe my education and training provide the skills needed
to address City issues. As a Council Member, I serve on SCAG
Transportation and Emerging Technologies Committees as well as
the OC Library Advisory Board, South Orange County Watershed
Management and LAFCO.
Public service is important to me and as the former president of
Third Laguna Hills Mutual as well as serving on GRF, I worked
with others to help keep our community economically viable.
As a council member I continued to tackle challenging issues.
Understanding how the relation of service can enhance not only
quality of life but also security is paramount. My goal continues to
be to effectively represent all members of the community to ensure
that City services operate in a safe, secure and environmentally
appropriate manner. I respectfully ask for your vote.
