RICK NICHOLS
AGE: 42
School Principal/Parent
I am grateful to call Fountain Valley home and am committed to
ensuring our schools remain among the very best for every child.
For 23 years, I have dedicated my career to public education.
I began as a classified instructional aide later serving as a
special education teacher, athletic coach, assistant principal, and,
currently, a middle school principal. These experiences have given
me a deep understanding of what students, teachers, and families
need to succeed. My passion for quality education is why I want to
serve.
Our three children attend Fountain Valley schools because my
wife and I believe in the strength of this community and the top-
notch quality of its schools. I am an active PTA member, Assistant
Scoutmaster, coach of youth sport teams, and a strong advocate for
family partnerships in students’ education.
As your Trustee, I will prioritize:
Student and campus safety
Maintaining high academic standards for all students
Attracting and retaining highly qualified teachers and staff
Ensuring fiscal responsibility
Promoting local control of education
Vote for me, and together we can preserve what makes Fountain
Valley schools exceptional, while supporting the dedicated
educators who serve our children and ensuring every student is
prepared for a successful future.
