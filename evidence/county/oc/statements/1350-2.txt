KATE SANCHEZ
REPUBLICAN
California State Assemblywoman
I’m proud to have served as your State Assemblywoman for the past
three years — and of all we’ve accomplished.
California families and business owners are paying more, feeling
less safe, and growing weary of progressive Sacramento politicians
moving further out of touch with the real world. That’s why we need
a strong, proven, conservative leader that will stand firm and deliver
results.
I have successfully authored legislation to improve your safety
and quality of life, including bills to: make sex trafficking a serious
felony, make any AI-generated child pornography a felony, and to
ensure crime victims are notified before the criminal who harmed
them is considered for parole.
I’m a bold leader bringing conservative common-sense solutions to
California.
As your Assemblywoman, I’ll continue to support policies that:
prohibit spraying dangerous pesticides in our neighborhoods,
oppose illegal immigration, lower taxes, reduce the high cost of
living, increase wildfire preparedness and availability of affordable
homeowner insurance; eliminate fraud and wasteful government
spending, and prohibit the early release of felons.
I’m honored to have earned an “A” rating from the Howard Jarvis
Taxpayers Association. I’ve never voted to raise your taxes (and
never will) - and I’ll always defend Proposition 13.
As a mother, I’ll protect parental rights and fight to keep males out
of female sports and private spaces.
I’m proud to be endorsed by the California Republican Party and
respected law enforcement leaders, including Sheriff Don Barnes
and Sheriff Chad Bianco.
I respectfully ask for your vote and continued support.
