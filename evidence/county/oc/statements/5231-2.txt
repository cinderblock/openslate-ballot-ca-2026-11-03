SCOTT DAVIS
AGE: 48
Real Estate Entrepreneur
Born and raised in Seal Beach, I am Scott Davis, 48, a real estate
entrepreneur running for District 1.
My education included McGaugh, McAuliffe, and Los Alamitos High;
I also participated in Seal Beach Junior Lifeguards. My parents
met and married here. In the 1980s, they owned John’s Food King
Market on Main Street, where I learned service and the value of
local business. My dad volunteered as a firefighter at Seal Beach
Fire Station 44, instilling respect for service.
After graduating from the University of Arizona, I built a 20-year
media and entertainment career with Paramount Pictures, 20th
Century Fox, and Amazon, across the United States and overseas.
Returning home in 2023 renewed my appreciation. I serve on the
board of Save Our Beach’s Seal Beach chapter, helped launch a kids’
surf camp, and am a Seal Beach Boardriders Club member.
I am running to give back with practical leadership: protect
public safety; maintain our beach, pier, streets, and drainage;
keep Main Street local; and preserve Seal Beach’s character. I
support transparent spending, balanced budgets, strong reserves,
and responsible cost reductions before asking more of residents. I
will listen, engage, and pursue smart improvements for today and
future generations.
