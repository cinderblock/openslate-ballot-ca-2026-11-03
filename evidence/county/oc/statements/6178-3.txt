DAVID PETTY
Retired Cybersecurity Professional
I am running for the Moulton Niguel Water District Board of
Directors because I believe in fiscal responsibility, transparent
decision-making, and reliable public infrastructure.
For more than 25 years, I have worked in cyber operations,
cybersecurity, and digital forensics, managing, leading, and
mentoring teams responsible for protecting critical systems and
delivering complex projects. As water infrastructure becomes
increasingly connected, safeguarding critical systems from cyber
threats is an important part of protecting reliable public services.
I hold a Bachelor’s degree from the University of California, Irvine
and a Master’s degree in Cybersecurity from Fordham University in
New York.
Over the past 10 years, I have also managed residential real estate,
gaining practical experience in budgeting, long-term planning, and
responsible stewardship.
If elected, I will work to ensure reliable, affordable water
service today while planning responsibly for the District’s
long-term water reliability and infrastructure needs. I will
focus on protecting taxpayer resources, promoting transparency,
strengthening resilience, and helping ensure our water system
remains dependable for future generations.
I would be honored to earn your support.
