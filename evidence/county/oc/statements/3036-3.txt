My Top Priority, Student Success.
High-paying skilled jobs have been leaving California. One of the
best ways to bring those jobs back is to build a highly trained
workforce. That begins with education.
Not every student follows the same path. Some will earn a university
degree. Others will build successful careers through career
and technical education, apprenticeships, certificate programs,
or workforce training. Hundreds of rewarding careers require
education beyond high school, and every path deserves respect,
support, and opportunity.
Our community colleges must prepare students for college transfer
and high-demand careers. This means expanding career education,
strengthening partnerships with local businesses, industry, labor
organizations, and providing training that leads directly to good-
paying careers.
California needs more skilled workers in construction,
manufacturing, healthcare, technology, transportation, public
safety, and many other essential industries. Our colleges must
provide the knowledge, technical skills, hands-on experience, and
problem-solving abilities employers need.
As Trustee, I will work with local, state, and federal elected officials
to secure funding to build modern campuses, expand career
training facilities, and provide the classes, equipment, and student
support services needed for success.
Together we can build stronger students, stronger families,
a stronger workforce, stronger communities, and a stronger
California tomorrow.
