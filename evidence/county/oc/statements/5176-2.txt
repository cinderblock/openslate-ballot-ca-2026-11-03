ANDREW P. GERKEN
Retired Surgeon
I have been privileged to live in Newport Beach since my father
moved Pacific Life to the city in 1972.
I believe we deserve a city council that reflects our long-established
values. These include protecting our parks and open space, a limit
on high rise development and respect for the people’s right to vote
on key issues, such as development.
I oppose the costly plan to put the police station/jail in the park.
when there are better, less expensive alternatives.
I oppose the plan to redevelop the current police station into a 300
ft. apartment tower.
I support public safety and I understand the way to provide more
public safety resources is to not overspend on buildings.
As a fiscal conservative, I believe we should treat each tax dollar as
if it were our own. I will rein in excess spending, and I will say No
to new taxes.
I attended Corona del Mar High, Harvard University and UCI Medical
School. For twenty years, I was the CDM Football team doctor and
was the Hoag Orthopedic Department Chair. I serve on the Library
Foundation board.
I will work for you for a better Newport.
