PATRICIA C. "PAT" BATES
Water Policy Advisor
I started my public service on my neighborhood community services
district, a special district just like the Moulton Niguel Water District.
I went on to incorporate the City of Laguna Niguel, served as its first
mayor, and led the fight against the El Toro Airport.
As an Orange County Supervisor, California State Assemblywoman,
and California State Senator, I represented every community within
Moulton Niguel Water District's service area.
I'm proud of my record as a taxfighter:
Defended Proposition 13 and Proposition 218, your right to
challenge water rates
Received the Howard Jarvis Taxpayers Association’s highest
rating
I've never stopped serving the community I love: in addition to
being the President of the Laguna Niguel Historical Society, I am
a Director for the Howard Jarvis Taxpayers Association, protecting
taxpayers throughout California.
Now, I am focused on water. Whether you’re a renter or homeowner,
a young family or a retiree, we all need safe, reliable water at a price
we can afford.
As your Moulton Niguel Water District director, water quality and
safety will always be my first priority.
I will invest in the pipes, pumps, and reservoirs that keep
water flowing through drought and emergencies, at the lowest
responsible cost, with a full public accounting of how our money is
being spent.
I would be honored to receive your vote. Please visit
votepatbates.com.
