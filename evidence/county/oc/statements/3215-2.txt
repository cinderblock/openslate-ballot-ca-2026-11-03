ZIGGY MCBRIDE
Educator/Librarian/Parent
I am running for the Tustin Unified School District Board of
Education because every decision should begin with one question:
What is best for students?
For more than seven years I have served TUSD as a Library Media
Technician and Literacy Specialist, helping students develop a love
of reading, strengthen critical thinking, and build confidence. By
modernizing library programs, leading family literacy initiatives,
and supporting parents, I have seen firsthand how strong public
schools change lives.
My experience also includes securing grants, managing budgets,
coordinating volunteers, directing youth camps, and building
community partnerships. These roles have prepared me to make
thoughtful, responsible decisions that support students, educators,
and families.
As a Board member I will keep students at the center of
every decision, support our teachers and staff, promote academic
excellence and be responsible with fiscal stewardship. I will work
to strengthen parent partnerships, and ensure transparency, open
communication, safe schools, and balanced technology use.
Tustin Unified is exceptional because of its outstanding students,
dedicated educators, and engaged families. I would be honored to
help our district continue to thrive for generations to come.
