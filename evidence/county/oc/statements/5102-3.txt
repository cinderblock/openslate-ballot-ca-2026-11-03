SUKHEE KANG
Small Businessman/Educator
Irvine has been my home for more than three decades. My wife,
Joanne and I raised our family here, and I have dedicated my career
to serving our community with integrity, experience, and results.
I served two terms as Irvine Mayor and Irvine City Councilmember,
leading our city through the Great Recession while maintaining
balanced budgets without furloughs or core service cuts and
while strengthening financial reserves. During my tenure, I secured
more than $121 million in transportation funding, expanded
community services, invested in education, and helped preserve
Irvine’s reputation as one of America’s safest and best-managed
cities.
Most recently, I served as a Presidential Appointee and Regional
Administrator for the U.S. General Services Administration (GSA),
overseeing billions of dollars in federal assets and capital projects
across eight states. This experience reinforced my commitment to
fiscal responsibility, accountability, transparency, and protecting
taxpayer dollars.
I am running for Irvine City Council District 1 to ensure responsible
spending, strengthen public safety, support educational excellence,
improve public transportation, promote local businesses, safeguard
open spaces and promote thoughtful growth. Irvine deserves
experienced leadership that puts Irvine residents first and delivers
results.
I respectfully ask for your vote by November 3.
