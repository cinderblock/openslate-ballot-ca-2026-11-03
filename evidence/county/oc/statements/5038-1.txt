STEVE SMITH

I'm running for City Council to change business as usual and refocus
City Hall on you.
Professionally, I'm a technology consultant focused on cloud
computing. Personally, I'm a Costa Mesa enthusiast, resident,
advocate, renter and member of your Animal Services Committee.
I've spent my career solving complex problems.
I'm running on the Five Commitments for District 5:
Protect Fairview Park for families, preserve Glider flying,
“Concerts in the Park” and the recreational uses that define it.
Fairview Park is for you.
Staff it. Fund it. Equip it. Let’s focus on our police and public safety
in Costa Mesa.
Fix our finances: smart cuts, smarter growth, and a real plan
to close the deficit — without new taxes or shortchanging our
community.
Let's end the standoff between City Hall and local business. Red
tape shouldn't outlast a good idea — and it shouldn't empty our
storefronts.
A transparent city council – one that shows its work, holds open
meetings, listens and answers to you.
Costa Mesa deserves experienced, accountable leadership that
cares about you and our city. I'd be honored to earn your vote and
put these Commitments into action.
