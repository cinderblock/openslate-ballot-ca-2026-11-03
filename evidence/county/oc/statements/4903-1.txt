MARIE TSAASAN
Teacher/Mother
I want all our neighbors to feel welcome and have the city services
we need to thrive here, at all stages of life. I want all our children
to have the opportunity to buy a home and raise their families here,
to live and work in this beautiful city, to play and grow in a safe,
supportive and nurturing environment.
I was born on the Navy base in San Diego into a family dedicated to
public service. I graduated from Redlands High, earned my Master's
in Informatics from the University of California, Irvine and have
published peer-reviewed research with internationally recognized
scholars in top conferences in my field. My family has lived in Aliso
Viejo for more than 20 years and I have raised my two children
with the same strong moral compass and work ethic centered in
community that my family instilled in me.
I will work to:
Increase community input in City spending priorities;
Support local businesses so they prosper while meeting the
needs of our residents;
Build housing that serves the needs of residents at all stages of
life;
Improve safety conditions on our sidewalks and roadways.
Support neighbors helping neighbors by voting Marie Tsaasan for
District 3. Visit www.Marie4AV.com to learn more.
