ROSALIND "ROZ" PRICE
AGE: 48
Operations Manager
Huntington Beach has been my home for over 40 years. I am
honored to serve as a City of Huntington Beach Community &
Library Services Commissioner, working to enhance the quality of
life for our residents while preserving the character that makes our
city special.
For more than 25 years, I have led organizations through
executive-level operations, budgeting, project management, and
strategic planning. My unique combination of executive-level
leadership, official City service as a Community & Library
Services Commissioner, and decades of hands-on volunteer work
throughout Huntington Beach has prepared me to lead with
experience, integrity, and accountability. I have proudly served local
families, veterans, active-duty military, seniors, youth, churches,
and nonprofit organizations because I believe strong communities
are built through acts of service.
I am running for City Council to protect public safety, support
local businesses, maintain our beaches, parks, libraries, and
neighborhoods, and ensure taxpayer dollars are managed
responsibly. I believe in transparent government, fiscal
responsibility, and listening to residents before making important
decisions.
My commitment is simple: to listen, lead with integrity, and work
every day to strengthen Huntington Beach for today's families and
future generations. It would be my honor to serve as your City
Council Member.
