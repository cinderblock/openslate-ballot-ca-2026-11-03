ELIZABETH ANDRADE
Community Services Executive
My family is proud to call Fountain Valley home. My husband and
I chose to raise our two sons here because it is a safe community
with great schools, beautiful parks, and neighbors who genuinely
care about one another. I'm running for City Council because I want
to help preserve what makes Fountain Valley such a special place
to live.
For more than 20 years, I've worked in public service, helping
people find solutions during some of the most difficult times in
their lives. Today, as Executive Director of a countywide nonprofit, I
manage multimillion-dollar budgets, work with local governments
and community organizations, and lead teams that serve thousands
of Orange County residents every year.
I'll bring that same practical experience to City Hall. My priorities
are keeping our neighborhoods safe, supporting our police and
firefighters, maintaining our parks and streets, being fiscally
responsible with taxpayer dollars, and ensuring City Hall is
transparent and responsive.
I'm honored to have earned the endorsement of the Fountain
Valley Police Officers Association. Most importantly, I will listen
to residents, make thoughtful decisions, and always put Fountain
Valley first.
I respectfully ask for your vote.
