RON RUEF
Youth Sports Director
Every student deserves a high quality education, every parent
deserves a voice, and every taxpayer deserves responsible
stewardship of public funds.
As your Capistrano Unified School District Trustee, I will focus
on putting students first, preparing them for the careers of
tomorrow, maintaining safe and welcoming schools, practicing
transparent and fiscally responsible budgeting, supporting and
retaining outstanding educators, and building strong partnerships
with parents and our community.
My commitment to education and public service has been a
constant throughout my life. While my sons attended Capistrano
Unified schools, I volunteered with the PTA and was elected to
school site councils, where I reviewed school budgets and helped
align financial resources with student achievement.
I helped lead the successful transformation of Carl Hankey School
from a K-5 campus into Orange County's first K-8 International
Baccalaureate World School and helped establish the CHIEF
Foundation, which continues to support students today. I also had
the privilege of serving as Chair of the Capistrano Unified Facilities
and Finance Advisory Committee for five years, gaining valuable
experience in district budgeting, long-range planning, and school
safety.
My community service includes serving as President of the Mission
Viejo Community Services Commission, the Long Beach Parks and
Recreation Commission, the California Association of Park and
Recreation Commissioners and Board Members, and the Pacific
Water Quality Association. Today, I continue serving youth and
families as Area Director for AYSO Area 11L after 29 years as a
volunteer referee and youth sports leader.
My wife, Janice, and I have been married for 43 years and
raised our two sons in Capistrano Unified schools. Today, we
are proud grandparents watching the next generation grow up
in our community. I earned a bachelor's degree from California
State University, Long Beach, proudly served in the U.S. Army, and
retired after more than 34 years from Morton Salt. As a District
Manager with Morton Salt, I led teams, managed budgets, and was
responsible for financial accountability.
I would be honored to earn your vote and the opportunity to serve
our students, families, educators, and community.
