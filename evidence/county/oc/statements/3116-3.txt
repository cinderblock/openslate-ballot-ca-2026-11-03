JEANNA EMMONS
Parent/Educator/Businessowner
As a mom with a K-8 student and a high schooler in Capistrano
Unified, I'm committed to ensuring every student, at every level,
has a clear path to reach their full potential. I'm running to bring
transparent and student-centered leadership to our community.
I've been showing up for our youth and our community, focused on
building relationships, and listening to families.
My commitment extends to child advocacy. In my role as a former
PTA President and as a parent leader, I’ve traveled to Sacramento
to advocate directly to legislators about preserving critical mental
health services, as well as increasing our public school funding. I'll
bring my passionate leadership to district oversight.
As a 12-year small business owner, I have experience in managing
resources wisely and balancing a budget. On the board, I'll respect
taxpayer dollars by cutting unnecessary overhead and prioritizing
the classroom experience.
I believe our district must prepare students for a rapidly
changing global economy and job market. I'll advocate for
modern coursework in emerging fields, including expanded Career
Technical Education (CTE) pathways with apprenticeship options,
and more opportunities for students to earn college credits
before graduation. This combination will provide our students
with academic advantages and professional skills, so they’re
exceptionally competitive for college and high-demand careers.
Although my professional background is in science, I believe
courses in music and other arts subjects are essential for student
engagement. They help build discipline and enrich learning across
all areas. I'm committed to protecting our arts and music programs,
so our children receive a rich, well-rounded education.
Every child must feel safe to learn at school. Campus security
requires foresight, clear protocols, and active listening. But true
safety goes well beyond physical security: it also means protecting
our students by strengthening anti-bullying initiatives and creating
welcoming classrooms. I’ll champion safety plans that value both
the overall well-being of students and their physical security.
An effective trustee doesn’t make decisions in a vacuum — they
listen to the community. I'll push for clearer, proactive district
communications so families are never left in the dark about
decisions that affect their children.
I’ll keep political agendas out of our classrooms and board
meetings so we can focus on what truly matters: academic
standards, safe schools, fiscal accountability, and well-rounded
opportunities for every child.
I would be honored to earn your vote. EmmonsForCUSD.com
