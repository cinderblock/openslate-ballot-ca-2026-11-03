CHARLENE CUELLAR
Community Volunteer
I’m your neighbor, a Garden Grove Unified Council of PTA’s former
president, family advocate, nurse, lifelong volunteer, and mom
to three wonderful GGUSD-enrolled kids. I’m running for Garden
Grove Unified School Board to be a voice for parents, students, and
deliver much-needed change to the board of education.
Starting in business but shifting to a nursing career, like many
parents, I became more involved in my children’s education when
the pandemic forced in-home learning.
Elected PTA Council President, I managed budgets and brought
fresh ideas. Together, we revamped libraries, funded field trips,
bought classroom supplies, paid for new sports equipment and
chess sets, brought families together, and strengthened parent
participation across the district.
Parents’ voices are vital to the success of our schools. I’ll use my
experience building communities to deliver change that puts our
kids first.
We must get back to fundamentals, rethinking kids’ relationships
with screens, focusing on literacy, building social skills, and
bolstering student mental health.
As your trustee, I’ll use my business background to protect our tax
dollars, my nursing experience to ensure student safety, and my life
as a parent to fight every day for the bright future our kids deserve.
Our students are worth it!
