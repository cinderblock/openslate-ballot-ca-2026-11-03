CYNTHIA VASQUEZ
AGE: 48
Mission Viejo City Council Member
As long-time residents, my family and I are proud to call Mission
Viejo home. I’m honored to serve on the City Council and seek re-
election.
Throughout my term, I've used my business and MBA background
to put residents first by protecting taxpayer dollars, improving
transparency, and making thoughtful decisions.
I put our community first by:
Supporting improvements to Sierra Recreation Center and Napoli
Park
Working to keep taxpayer dollars from subsidizing private
organizations
Advocating for thorough research before approving multimillion-
dollar projects
Sponsoring a much-needed refuge lane at Marguerite/Sierra
Expanding community events without taxpayer dollars
Providing residents with better access to information about City
decisions
Encouraging safety and traffic improvements from Oso to Avery
Parkways
When re-elected, I'll continue to:
Require thorough review before major projects move forward
Protect tax dollars through diligent oversight, ensuring wise
spending
Keep Oso Creek Golf Course self-sustaining
Support seniors, working families and individuals, local
businesses, and public safety
Advocate for our City Library
Mission Viejo deserves transparent, accountable leadership
focused on residents. I will continue working to keep Mission Viejo
safe, financially responsible, and thriving. Let’s move forward
together. Re-Elect Cynthia Vasquez to District 3!
