PETER MOLINARI
Small Business Owner
Born and raised in Orange County, I have spent nearly twenty
years building my life, career, and family as a Mission Viejo resident
and homeowner, raising my daughter and supporting local schools
and community organizations. These experiences have given me a
strong understanding of the values and priorities that make Mission
Viejo a special place to live.
Currently serving on the Mission Viejo Planning and Transportation
Commission, I have dedicated virtually six years to evaluating
land use, transportation, and development issues. As Chairman
and Vice Chairman, I have worked with residents, city staff, and
public safety professionals to protect neighborhood character and
enhance quality of life.
I bring more than two decades of private sector
leadership experience in educational publishing, marketing, and
entertainment. This background strengthened my skills in strategic
planning, fiscal responsibility, and problem solving.
As a Commissioner, I have worked with the Sheriff's Department
and city engineers on traffic safety priorities, including school area
improvements and speeding concerns. I supported rebuilding Fire
Station 24, preserving parks and open space, opposing proposed
rezoning of the Garden Center Plaza property, and attracting new
businesses to strengthen city revenues.
Committed to leadership focused on integrity, accountability,
public safety, and responsible growth.
