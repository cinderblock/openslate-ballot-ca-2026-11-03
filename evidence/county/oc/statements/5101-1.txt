LARRY AGRAN
Mayor of Irvine
It has been my privilege to serve as your Mayor, united by a shared
vision to make Irvine the Safest, Smartest, Greenest, Healthiest, and
Kindest City in America.
Together, we've made remarkable progress toward that goal. Now,
I ask for your vote to continue our work. Specifically, we must:
Invest in public safety to maintain our ranking as America's Safest
Big City.
Expand our newly established Irvine Public Library System with
new programs, enhanced services, and 100,000 additional books.
Build-out the Great Park; maintain our top-ranked Irvine park
system; and add the 700-acre Gateway Preserve to our 10,000-
acre Irvine Open Space Preserve.
Implement our Urban Forest Master Plan, planting another
100,000 trees.
Equip our City Hall with cost-saving solar panels and battery
backup systems.
Improve mobility and reduce traffic and pollution by expanding
our free, non-polluting Irvine Connect shuttle bus service
throughout Irvine.
Expand our public health and social support initiatives, creating
a City of Irvine Department of Health and Wellness.
Let's do all this . . . and more, while continuing to adopt balanced
budgets with $100 million in reserves to guarantee the delivery of
Irvine's top-notch city services.
I ask for your vote so we can continue to move forward . . . together.
Thank you!
Larry Agran
