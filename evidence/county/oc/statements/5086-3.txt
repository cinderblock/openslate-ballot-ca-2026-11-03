CINDY NGOC TRAN
Garden Grove Councilwoman/Engineer
As your Garden Grove Councilwoman, U.S. veteran, engineer, small
business owner, teacher, and mother, I am proud to seek your
support for re-election. I believe local government should focus
on safe neighborhoods, responsible spending, supporting local
businesses, and delivering results for District 3 residents.
During my first term, I have worked to strengthen public safety
by supporting our police and firefighters, helping keep our
neighborhoods safe, and connecting individuals experiencing
homelessness with services while protecting our community. I
have supported local businesses by expanding access to grants
and city resources, helped families through first-time homebuyer
programs, and advocated for improvements to traffic, streets, and
neighborhood infrastructure.
I believe strong communities are built through service. That is
why I have organized neighborhood cleanups, food distributions,
and community events that bring residents together. Each year, I
also provide hundreds of holiday gifts, donate backpacks to local
students, and award scholarships because investing in our youth
strengthens Garden Grove’s future.
Serving our community has been the greatest honor of my life. With
your support, I will be honor to continue to work to keep Garden
Grove safe, financially responsible, and a wonderful place to live,
work, and raise a family.
