JOHN R. HANNA
Governing Board Member, Rancho Santiago Community
College District, Trustee Area 2
I am John R. Hanna, and it has been an honor to serve you
and our community as the Trustee for Area 2 of the Rancho
Santiago Community College District. My wife, Cynthia, and our
seven children have lived in Santa Ana and Orange for 37 years. I
am a long-time parishioner at Holy Family Church in Orange and
have volunteered thousands of hours with local youth sports and
community organizations. Professionally, I have served as General
Counsel for two California agencies and handled government
affairs for the 12-state Western States Carpenters before my
retirement.
As your Trustee, I have worked to maintain balanced budgets
while building strong partnerships with local businesses. Santa
Ana and Santiago Canyon colleges continue to have strong
enrollment because of our dedicated educators and employees and
our outstanding Career Technical Education and Apprenticeship
programs, many of which allow our students to earn while they
learn.
Our District contributes $2.4 billion to the Orange County
economy, while providing students with affordable pathways to
universities and good-paying careers. I remain committed to fiscal
responsibility, accountability and addressing challenges legally,
responsibly, and transparently.
I humbly ask for your vote.
