ASHTON KAUWE
Office Administrator
My name is Ashton Kauwe, and I am running to be a Coast
Community College District Trustee because as a graduate of
Golden West College, I believe that our community colleges can
be more than just a stepping stone to other institutions. I want
to expand the District’s technical and trade offerings so that our
community colleges can be the hub to train and certify the next
generation of welders, electricians, and other trade workers that
will be needed as our world continues to change and reshoring
is prioritized. There are currently hundreds of thousands of job
openings for the trades that can result in six-figure salaries with a
little time and work, but there are not enough people to fill them.
I believe that the District can be at the forefront of training the
next generation of high-paying trade workers. We need to expand
our offerings and work with our high schools to show students
the opportunities before them. I still believe it is important for
the District to be able to help advance students who are looking
to move on to higher education, but I also believe we have the
opportunity to become so much more.
