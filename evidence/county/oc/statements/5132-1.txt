SUSAN PRITCHARD
Retired Teacher
La Habra is my home. My husband and I bought our house 43
years ago and we never left! Fondly known as Dr.P., I taught at
Washington Middle School in the La Habra City School District
for nearly 33 years, teaching more than 7000 students. I hold
a Bachelors, Masters, and Doctorate degree as well as several
teaching credentials.
Since retiring. I served as a Trustee on the La Habra City School
District School Board and help La Habra non-profits. Serving both
our residents and our local businesses gives me unique insights for
prioritizing our La Habra’s needs.
Unfortunately, we have seen our City Council campaign on pledges
of “no new taxes” only to have them push for higher sales taxes.
Our City Council failed to get a housing element approved in a
timely manner, opening the door to “builder’s remedy” housing
development the city can’t stop.
Incredibly, the City Council sent our tax dollars to a Chamber of
Commerce in Buena Park while refusing to support our La Habra
Chamber of Commerce.
I will restore trust in our City Council by supporting term limits,
keeping my promises, and putting La Habra first. I promise all my
Council stipends will go to La Habra non-profits!
I would be honored to have your vote. www.electpritchard.com
