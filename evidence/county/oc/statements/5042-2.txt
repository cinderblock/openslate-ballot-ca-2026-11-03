HELEN LE
Community Volunteer
I made Cypress my home as a young parent, drawn to its quality
schools and economic opportunities available to our family. As a
25-year resident, I want to keep Cypress a city where every family
can thrive and build a strong foundation for the future.
I’m the daughter of Korean immigrants – my mother a nurse and
my father a Vietnam War veteran – who taught me the values of
hard work, personal responsibility, and service. I graduated from
UCLA with a Mathematics degree and spent ten years in financial
and wealth management services.
I’m a proud parent of Cypress school graduates, my daughter
is an aerospace engineer and my son attends Cypress College.
I’ve supported Cypress youth sports as Team Parent/Manager
and brought parents, students, and teachers together to form
Cambridge Virtual Academy PTSA to foster student leadership.
As a longtime homeowners association leader, I advocate
for my neighbors through fiscal responsibility and sustainable
community reinvestment – ensuring every dollar delivers real
value. As your Councilmember, I will bring a fresh perspective,
transparency, accountability, and collaborative leadership to
strengthen neighborhood safety, support our local economy,
address cost-of-living pressures, and ensure access to quality city
services.
I respectfully ask for your vote. Learn more at
