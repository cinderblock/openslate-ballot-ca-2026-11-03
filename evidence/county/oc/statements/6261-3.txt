CHAD BROWN
AGE: 59
Landuse Planner/Developer
As a concerned neighbor, I am running for the position of Trabuco
Canyon Water District Director. Our community deserves a full
board that puts efficiency, transparency, and long-term value first.
As an 18-year resident within the district, I have watched our water
and sewer rates climb from ($85/month 2008 to $225/month
2026) and understand how that strains household budgets. My
priority is straightforward: run this district as efficiently as possible
so we can hold the line on costs, rather than passing every expense
on to ratepayers.
I also support continuing the pursuit of consolidation with
Santa Margarita Water District. Combining resources, staff, and
infrastructure with a neighboring agency can eliminate duplicated
overhead and create real economies of scale for savings that
should pass directly back to residents.
Finally, I'm committed to real transparency. Ratepayers deserve
clear, accessible information about how their money is spent, easy-
to-follow board meetings, and a district that welcomes questions
instead of deflecting them.
I am not a career politician — I'm someone who pays the same bills
as you and want this district run responsibly, openly, and in service
of the people it serves.
I would appreciate and value your vote this November.
