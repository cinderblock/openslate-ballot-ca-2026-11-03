TIFFANY BOCCIERI
Attorney/Mother
I'm Tiffany Boccieri, and I'm running for Laguna Hills City Council
Member, District 4.
I believe transparent, effective leadership is essential to preserving
the qualities that make Laguna Hills a safe, welcoming, and vibrant
community for current residents and future generations.
As a California-licensed attorney and mother, I understand that
sound decisions require careful analysis, active listening, and
accountability. Whether reviewing a city budget, evaluating a
development proposal, or analyzing a traffic study, I will approach
every issue with precision, integrity, and empathy.
I grew up in South Orange County and chose to raise my family
here because I value the safe neighborhoods, parks, and close-knit
communities that make this area unique. I believe every resident
deserves a strong voice in the decisions that shape the future of
Laguna Hills, and I will work to ensure every perspective is heard.
My volunteer leadership and legal experience have prepared me to
formulate effective solutions and deliver measurable outcomes. I
will bring those values of service, integrity, and accountability to the
City Council.
I ask for your vote this November and the opportunity to be a strong
voice for District 4 while serving the entire Laguna Hills community.
