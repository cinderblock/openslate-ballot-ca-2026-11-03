STEPHANIE KLOPFENSTEIN
Garden Grove Mayor/Mom
I’m one of five generations born and raised in Garden Grove. My
husband and I are raising our daughter here: she’s a Garden Grove
Unified School District student, just like I was. I actively volunteer
in our community: Garden Grove Community Foundation, Boys &
Girls Club, Children’s Hospital of Orange County, Garden Grove
Neighborhood Association, and Downtown Business Association.
Since becoming Mayor in 2024, I’ve moved Garden Grove forward:
Rapid 911 response times
Fire Station 84 upgrades, new state of the art Police Station
Neighborhood park security program
Little Saigon Business Community District promoting cultural
tourism and local businesses
First time homebuyer down payment assistance up to $110,000
Promote pet adoption at animal shelter
New playground equipment (Jardin de los Ninos Park, Haster
Basin Park), restrooms (Westhaven Park)
Woodbury Park expansion: pool, skatepark, walking trail
Historic Main Street beautification
Major investments in repairing streets, sidewalks, and curbs
Graffiti prevention/removal
Install/upgrade neighborhood safety lighting
Community Passport program with Supervisor Janet Nguyen and
Clerk-Recorder Hugh Nguyen
Reduced deficit by $61 million, improving Garden Grove’s fiscal
ranking among Orange County cities by 6 places
Streamline code enforcement
If you re-elect me as Mayor, I’ll continue fighting for all Garden
Grove residents: ensure our police and firefighters have what they
need to keep our neighborhoods safe, address homelessness with
both services and enforcement, and ensure fiscal responsibility.

Grove Police Association, and Garden Grove Police Management
