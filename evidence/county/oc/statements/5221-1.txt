DWAYNE SHIPP
AGE: 51
Caregiver/City Commissioner
As a Community Development Commissioner, caregiver, and
community organizer, I am running for Mayor because I believe
Santa Ana deserves leadership that listen, leads with integrity,
and puts residents first. Our city is filled with hardworking
families, thriving businesses, and young people who deserve every
opportunity to succeed.
Through years of community service, I have worked alongside
residents to strengthen our neighborhoods, support local
businesses, mentor youth, and bring people together through
action – not just words. I believe leadership begins by showing up,
listening, and finding practical solutions that improve everyday life.
As Mayor, I will prioritize public safety, economic growth,
neighborhood revitalization, government transparency, and
practical solutions to homelessness while investing in opportunities
for our youth, seniors, and working families. My goal is to build a
cleaner, safer, and more united Santa Ana where every resident has
the opportunity to thrive.
I love this city because it is my home, and I believe our greatest
days are still ahead. Together, we can move Santa Ana forward
with leadership that is accountable, compassionate, and committed
to every neighborhood. I respectfully ask for your vote and the
opportunity to serve as your next Mayor and work alongside every
resident every day.
