BOB ARCHULETA
DEMOCRATIC
State Senator
Senator Bob Archuleta is a former Obama Presidential Appointee
to the United States West Point Military Academy, an Army combat
veteran and former paratrooper with the 82nd Airborne Division,
who also served with the Montebello Police Department, and has
valuable local governmental experience as the former Mayor and
councilmember for the City of Pico Rivera.
Senator Archuleta has been able to successfully work across party
lines to help secure more than $100 million in state funding for local
infrastructure projects, including parks, libraries, transportation,
water and public safety projects, while supporting education,
veteran services, clean energy initiatives, and supporting our first
responders.
Senator Archuleta has been a strong fighter for veterans, seniors,
working families, women, equal and fair rights for all; public
safety, small businesses, while promoting economic growth,
environmental responsibility and good-paying local jobs.
Senator Archuleta believes the government should be accountable,
responsive, and transparent to the public, while focusing on
practical solutions that improve the quality of life for the residents
and tax-payers.
Senator Archuleta’s steadfast leadership and commitment to the
public have earned him the trust of thousands of residents,
community and regional leaders, organizations, and local law
enforcement and first responder associations throughout the
district.
Senator Archuleta respectfully asks for your vote and support so he
can continue working to keep neighborhoods safe, strengthen the
local economy, and protect the quality of life for all families across
the district.
