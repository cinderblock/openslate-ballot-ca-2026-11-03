JENNIFER L RANK
AGE: 47
Appointed Governing Board Member, Ocean View School
District
This campaign isn’t about politics. It’s about Service, Stewardship,
and Results—doing what is needed to ensure every student is safe,
every family is heard, and every school has the support it needs to
succeed.
Hello, my name is Jennifer Rank. I am the wife of Adam Rank, an on-
air analyst for the NFL Network, and we are the proud parents of
two Ocean View School District students. My family has deep roots
in education. My mother is a retired teacher at Bethany Christian
Academy and principal who served our community for more than
30 years. Growing up in a household shaped by an educator’s
dedication, I began my own path.
I care deeply about our schools and am honored to have been
appointed by unanimous decision to serve as an Ocean View
School District Board Member. With Your Support—Your Vote, I look
forward to continuing to serve.
Our school district is recognized throughout California for its
educational excellence. Beyond party labels, families want the
highest quality educational experience for their children: strong
fundamental skills in reading, math, and science, access to arts,
music, and physical education, and leaders who use taxpayer
dollars responsibly.
Through my roles as PTA President at Harbour View Elementary and
President of the Ocean View School District PTA/PTO Roundtable, I
have collaborated with PTA/PTO leaders across all 13 elementary
and middle schools. This experience has provided me with firsthand
insight into both the hurdles our schools encounter and the
positive impact that collaborative community efforts can achieve.
My mission is straightforward: provide a safe learning space for
our children by addressing their needs, supporting their experience,
and investing in their success.
Having received the unanimous endorsement of the Ocean View
Teachers Association, I am truly humbled and deeply proud to
have the backing of our community leaders, families, teachers, and
support staff.
My intent now is to earn your trust and your vote.
Whether questions arise or you’re texting to let me know I can count
on your vote, I look forward to hearing from you.
