LOREN GAMEROS
AGE: 55
Instructor/Councilmember
For more than 40 years, Costa Mesa has been the place I’m proud
to call home. It’s where I grew up, where my wife and I are raising
our family, and where I’ve dedicated my life to serving others.
For more than three decades, I’ve dedicated my career to
educating and mentoring the next generation of building inspection
professionals in the construction industry. Throughout my career,
I've helped countless students develop successful careers
emphasizing safety, integrity, accountability, and craftsmanship
because investing in people helps build stronger communities.
For the past five and a half years, I’ve had the privilege of serving
on the Costa Mesa City Council. My philosophy has always been
simple: keep my boots on the ground, listen to residents, and work
collaboratively to solve problems. I believe the best decisions are
made by being present, accessible, and accountable to the people
I serve.
I remain committed to strengthening public safety, supporting
youth sports, planning responsibly, investing in our city’s future,
and preserving what makes Costa Mesa an exceptional place to
live, work, and raise a family. Together, we can continue building a
community that future generations will be proud to call home.
