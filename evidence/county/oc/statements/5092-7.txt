BRIAN THIENES
Small Business Owner
Huntington Beach has been my home for 38 years. My family
and I built our lives here while I spent 40 years building a
successful civil engineering and land surveying company, managing
complex infrastructure projects across Southern California,
meeting payrolls, and delivering on time and on budget. That real-
world experience is exactly what I’ll bring to City Hall.
State policies threaten Huntington Beach’s community character
by forcing high-density development into our neighborhoods.
When a massive six-story project threatened our community, I
organized my neighbors and forced the developer to shrink it. Today
I serve on the Huntington Beach Planning Commission, standing
up for our neighborhoods. On the City Council, I’ll keep fighting
overdevelopment and Sacramento overreach, because decisions
about Huntington Beach should be made here, by the people who
live here.
I’ll oppose new taxes, balance the budget, fully fund our police and
fire, support real solutions to homelessness, and partner with our
local schools.
I’m not a career politician. I’m a small business owner, husband,
father, and your neighbor who will fight against new taxes, support
safe neighborhoods, oppose overdevelopment, and ensure local
decisions are made locally.
I would be honored to earn your vote for Huntington Beach City
Council.
