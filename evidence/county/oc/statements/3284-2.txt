JACK TOROSSIAN
AGE: 37
Father
Cypress is a wonderful community, and my family and I are grateful
to call it home. As a husband, father of three children, Morris
Elementary PTA Treasurer, and longtime resident, I understand the
importance of maintaining the high standards that have made our
schools among the best. I am running for the Cypress School District
Board of Trustees because I believe every student deserves a safe,
supportive, and academically excellent learning environment.
As PTA Treasurer, I am working with my fellow board members
to strengthen our organization’s financial position, achieving
higher retained earnings while continuing to invest in programs
that benefit students, teachers, and families. My professional
background in finance, logistics, entrepreneurship, consumer
advocacy, and contract negotiation has prepared me to evaluate
complex issues, make transparent decisions, and responsibly
manage our resources.
At a time when our district faces significant challenges, I am
committed to providing exceptional educational opportunities for
every student. I will work collaboratively with parents, teachers,
staff, and fellow trustees to ensure that every decision reflects
accountability, fiscal responsibility, and a commitment to putting
students first.
