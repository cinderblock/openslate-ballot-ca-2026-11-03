ANDREW KIM
AGE: 31
Public Safety Manager
Public service has been the focus of my career. I have served
our community for more than 14 years with the La Palma Police
Department, including over five years as a La Palma Police Officer.
Today, I serve as a local government manager for a nearby city and
have experience working with communities throughout Orange,
Riverside, and Los Angeles Counties.
My grandparents and father immigrated from Korea with very little
and chose La Palma to build a life, opening the city's first Korean
market. Growing up in La Palma and attending its schools, I am
proud to call this community home. I also earned a Ph.D. in Public
Administration, researching strategies to improve public sector
employee recruitment and retention.
As a husband and father raising my young family in La Palma, I am
committed to preserving the safe neighborhoods, strong sense of
community, and quality of life that make our city special.
If elected, I will provide honest, accountable, and responsive
leadership. My priorities include public safety, supporting local
businesses, enhancing parks and neighborhoods, responsible fiscal
stewardship, and preserving La Palma's small-town character.
I would be honored to earn your vote. AndrewKimForLaPalma.com
