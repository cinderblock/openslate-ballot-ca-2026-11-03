LAN QUOC NGUYEN
Governing Board Member, Garden Grove Unified School
District, Trustee Area 2
GGUSD is one of the top-performing school districts for academic
excellence. I’m proud to be a member of the board that focuses on
student successes like laser beam.
GGUSD was the first and only school district in Orange County
recognized as the Exemplary District for its model programs. All
seven high schools have been ranked America’s Best High Schools
by the U.S. News and World Report. We even received the Best
Communities for Music Education Award for many years in a row.
Our college readiness rate has overcome and greatly exceeded the
County and State from 25% in 2002 to 65% today. Our graduates
are more ready to excel in college and future careers than ever.
We’ve greatly expanded our technology infrastructure and
achieved a 1-to-1 computer-per-student ratio at all school sites,
and invested over one billion dollars to modernize all 68 schools
to meet students’ needs in the digital age. This investment has
also greatly increased property values in and around GGUSD
neighborhoods.
This trend must continue for our students and community. I humbly
ask for your vote. For more information, visit www.lannguyen.org
