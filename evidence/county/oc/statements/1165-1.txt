DEREK TRAN
DEMOCRATIC
Representative/Businessowner/Father
As the son of refugees who fled the communist regime in Vietnam,
I believe everyone deserves a shot at the American Dream and a
chance to get ahead. At 18, I enlisted in the Army to give back
to the country that had given my family so much. Using the G.I.
bill, I worked my way through college and law school to become a
workers’ rights attorney, fighting for immigrants, workers, survivors
of sexual harassment, and consumers. I know what it means to fight
for people when the odds are stacked against them.
Orange County isn’t just where I serve, it’s home. It's where we raise
our three children, who attend public school, and where my wife
runs the small business we opened together, a local pharmacy.
In Congress, I’ve stood up to special interests and delivered real
results for our community. I’ve secured resources law enforcement
needs to keep our communities safe, worked to lower the price
of prescription drugs, and pushed for affordable housing. I’ve also
fought to protect Medicaid, and voted against the war in Iran which
is raising prices by the day.
But there’s more work ahead. I’m working to ban Members of
Congress, government officials, and political insiders from trading
stocks. I’m fighting for lower costs and will work to protect Medicare
and Social Security in Washington.
I’m honored to be endorsed by Fire Fighters, Orange County Deputy
Sheriffs, Planned Parenthood Action, Nurses, California Teachers,
and Veterans.
I hope to earn your vote. www.derektranforcongress.com
