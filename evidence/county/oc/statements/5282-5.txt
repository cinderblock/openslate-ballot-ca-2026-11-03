RAYMOND POTTER
AGE: 49
Entrepreneur
My name is Raymond Potter, and I have proudly called Yorba Linda
home for more than 26 years. As the owner and operator of an
automotive business, I understand the importance of hard work,
fiscal responsibility, accountability, and dependable service.
I am running for Yorba Linda City Council because I want to preserve
the qualities that make our city an exceptional place to live, work,
and raise a family. My priorities include opposing additional high-
density affordable housing projects that threaten Yorba Linda’s
character, promoting responsible development, maintaining a
strong local economy, supporting public safety, and addressing
growing concerns involving e-bikes on our streets and sidewalks.
As a longtime resident and business owner, I take great pride
in our city. I believe council members should listen carefully to
residents, respect differing viewpoints, and work collaboratively
toward practical solutions.
I would be honored to represent the people of Yorba Linda and
respectfully ask for your vote.
