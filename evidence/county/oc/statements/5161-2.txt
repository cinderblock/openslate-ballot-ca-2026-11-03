KRISTEN WILCOX
AGE: 40
Writer
Public safety must remain a top priority. I will provide our sheriff’s
department with the resources it needs while also ensuring
residents have the transparency they deserve through a public
police blotter and appropriate oversight of Flock license plate
reader technology.
I’m a fiscal conservative who believes taxpayer dollars should be
spent carefully, responsibly, and with accountability.
As Mission Viejo continues to evolve, we need thoughtful solutions.
We need to carefully review state housing mandates and their
effects on our city’s character, infrastructure, traffic, and parking.
I grew up here, and like many of you, I’ve watched our city change
while still holding onto the qualities that make it such a special
place to live. I’m running for city council because I want to protect
what we love about Mission Viejo.
No donations. No endorsements. Beholden to none, responsive to
all. I say the quiet parts out loud and make decisions that benefit
you.
