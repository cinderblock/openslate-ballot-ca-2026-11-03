DONALD J. LANK
Retired EEO Manager
Donald J. Lank – DonaldJLank.com
As an Orange County resident since 1995 and a proud Westminster
resident since 1998, I have dedicated my career and community
service to integrity, accountability, and public service.
I served the United States Postal Service for more than 43 years,
retiring as Headquarters Area Manager of Equal Employment
Opportunity Dispute Resolution, overseeing programs supporting
nearly 89,000 employees. I then served as Chief Equal Employment
Officer for the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Los Angeles District,
until retiring from federal service in 2022.
Today, I serve as President of the Springarden Homeowners
Association and as an appointed Commissioner on the Westminster
Parks and Recreation Commission and the Westminster Traffic
Commission.
I am seeking your vote for the Midway City Sanitary District Board to
provide experienced leadership, fiscal responsibility, transparency,
and accountability.
Looking ahead, I will support efforts to preserve the capacity
of Orange County’s three active landfills—Frank R. Bowerman,
Olinda Alpha, and Prima Deshecha. Separating leftover food and
other organic waste reduces methane emissions, conserves landfill
space, helps control future disposal costs, and promotes a cleaner,
more sustainable community.
I will protect taxpayer dollars, maintain reliable infrastructure, and
ensure responsive, dependable public service.
I respectfully ask for your vote and the opportunity to serve our
community.
