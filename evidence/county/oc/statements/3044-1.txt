KIM CARR
AGE: 56
Appointed Trustee, Coast Community College District
Governing Board
As Trustee for Coast Community College District, Area 3, it is an
honor and a privilege to serve you. I am a lifelong West Orange
County resident and former Mayor of Huntington Beach who
understands and cares deeply about our community’s educational
needs. Golden West College, Orange Coast College, and Coastline
College are among California’s finest community colleges, and
strengthening and protecting these esteemed higher-education
institutions are my priorities.
I am committed to ensuring our community colleges provide
an affordable, accessible, and relevant education that meets
current and future workforce demands. I will expand our dual-
enrollment programs, giving high school students a jump on their
college degrees. I will increase our career technical education
and apprenticeship programs, providing graduates with training
and opportunities for well-paying jobs. Additionally, I'll grow
Golden West College's Encore program, providing lifelong learning
opportunities for older adults in our community.
Every dollar in our budget is critical, and I have a track record of
managing taxpayers’ money responsibly and transparently. I will
keep the budget balanced, build cash reserves, advocate for state
funding, and uphold our Fiscal Stability Plan. Aligning spending with
revenue will keep our colleges top-ranked year after year.
I am grateful to be your Trustee for Area 3 and humbly ask for your
vote.
