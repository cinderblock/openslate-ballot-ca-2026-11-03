MIKE VIGLIOTTA
Huntington Beach City Attorney
As your City Attorney, I am dedicated to preserving the quality of
life that makes Huntington Beach such a special community.
Professional Experience: 26 years of City Attorney practice with
18 years in the Huntington Beach City Attorney’s Office. In
February 2025, City Council unanimously appointed me to serve as
Huntington Beach’s new City Attorney.
Public Safety: My top priority is keeping Huntington Beach safe.
I work closely with our Police Department to support the highest
level of public safety. We prosecute quality of life crimes, such
as illegal camping which jeopardize the peace and security of our
neighborhoods. Endorsed by the Huntington Beach Police Officers
Association.
Financial: I’m dedicated to saving taxpayer dollars by avoiding
litigation and advising the Mayor and City Council to prevent
unnecessary spending and ensure that our city tax dollars are used
for community services like fire, police, parks and beaches.
Finally, I vigorously defend the City’s local control over issues such
as elections and housing development, which impact the character
of Huntington Beach.
On a personal note, I’m an avid surfer and Huntington Beach
resident since 1998 and am dedicated to our City.
I would be honored to earn your vote and continue serving the
people of our community.
