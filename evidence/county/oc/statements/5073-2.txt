DANIEL KIM
Fullerton Commissioner/Businessman
My family and I immigrated to the United States when I was 4 years
old. We achieved the American Dream: I graduated from UCLA and
Harvard, and started a successful career. I married, becoming a
father of two children.
This was shattered on August 14, 2015, when a falling tree killed
our son in his sleep during our family Yosemite trip.
We turned this tragedy into a way to honor our son, who had
planned to start a program providing musical instruments and
lessons to underprivileged children: we created the Dragon Kim
Foundation to fulfill his dream. We’ve grown to also provide
leadership training to help students launch service programs and
award college scholarships. 100% of our students go on to college.
Professionally, I’m a turnaround specialist: when companies are
failing, I move them back to profitability. As I learned about
Fullerton’s multimillion dollar budget deficit, I knew I needed to
help.
If you elect me as our Councilmember, I will:
Eliminate waste, make cuts, and increase revenue to fix
Fullerton’s budget crisis
Ensure police and firefighters have what they need to protect
Fullerton neighborhoods
Repair Fullerton’s roads after years of neglect
I request your vote to help turn Fullerton around!
