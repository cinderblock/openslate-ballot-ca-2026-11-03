STEPHEN GLASS
AGE: 58
Governing Board Member, Orange Unified School
District, Trustee Area 7
As President of the Orange Unified School District Board of
Education, I keep students at the center of every decision.
This community is my home. My wife, a school principal, and I chose
Orange Unified schools for our children because we believe in
the opportunities this district provides. Like every parent, we want
schools that are safe, academically rigorous, fiscally responsible,
and welcoming to every student. I am invested in Orange Unified’s
success.
For nearly three decades, I have dedicated my professional
life to public education. I have served as a classroom teacher,
principal, district administrator, and university professor. These
experiences have prepared me to ask difficult questions, evaluate
complex decisions, and keep teaching and learning at the center
of district governance. I know successful schools require strong
leadership, sound financial management, meaningful partnerships
with families, and exceptional teachers and staff.
As your elected trustee and Board President, I have worked
to strengthen transparency, promote respectful and effective
governance, support stable district leadership, and ensure
responsible stewardship of taxpayer funds. I have supported
investments in safe campuses, high-quality instruction, and the
employees who serve our students every day. Drawing on my
experience as a principal and district administrator, I helped guide
the thoughtful selection of a new superintendent, emphasizing
stable leadership, accountability, and long-term planning while
keeping students and classrooms at the center of our work.
I am especially proud when I see our students recognized for
academic achievement, artistic talent, athletic excellence, service,
and perseverance. Those moments remind me that board decisions
ultimately affect real children, families, classrooms, and futures.
Public education is one of our community’s most important
investments. Strong schools strengthen neighborhoods, support
local businesses, protect property values, and prepare tomorrow’s
workforce. This responsibility demands experienced leadership
grounded in integrity, accountability, collaboration, and service.
Orange Unified has made progress, but important work remains. We
must accelerate student achievement, maintain safe and modern
campuses, recruit and retain exceptional employees, preserve
valuable programs, and exercise prudent fiscal oversight. We must
also listen to families, support educators, and ensure every student
graduates prepared for college, career, and civic life.
I am honored to serve this community and grateful for the trust you
have placed in me. I respectfully ask for your vote for re-election
so we can continue moving Orange Unified forward and build a
stronger future for every student, family, and school.
