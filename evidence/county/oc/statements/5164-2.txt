JAMES MORRISON
Human Resources Professional
Preserving our roots. Living the mission. Preparing for the future.
Mission Viejo has always been more than a place to live, it is
a community built on strong neighborhoods and families who
care deeply for our city. As your candidate for City Council, I am
committed to preserving the values that have made Mission Viejo
one of the best places to call home while ensuring we are prepared
for the opportunities and challenges of tomorrow.
As a husband, father, Army veteran, executive HR leader, and
community volunteer, I have spent my career bringing people
together, solving complex problems, and making thoughtful
decisions that put people first. I believe local government works
best when it listens before it leads, remains fiscally responsible, and
delivers practical results for residents.
My priorities are clear: keep our neighborhoods safe, protect
taxpayer dollars, maintain our parks, roads, and public spaces, and
prepare Mission Viejo for the future through smart technology,
economic resilience, emergency preparedness, and efficient city
services.
I am running because I believe we can honor Mission Viejo's proud
legacy while embracing thoughtful innovation that benefits every
generation. Together, we can preserve what makes Mission Viejo
special, live the mission that inspired our city, and confidently
prepare for the future.
