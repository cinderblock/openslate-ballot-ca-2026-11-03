BENJAMIN VAZQUEZ
Teacher/Councilmember
I’m Benjamin Vazquez, and I’m asking for your vote to
continue delivering results for Santa Ana families. As your City
Councilmember, I’ve fought to protect rent control and just-cause
eviction protections so working families can stay in their homes.
I led the fight for voters to put rent stabilization into the city’s
municipal code.
I’ve worked to reduce homelessness by 15% by expanding the hours
of our Quality of Life Team (QOLT) and investing in family services
that help keep people housed before they become homeless. We
also cleared dangerous encampments along Bristol Street while
connecting people to services, making our neighborhoods safer and
cleaner.
I’ve brought new investment to Ward 2 by opening a new park on
Bristol and supporting a library center in Pacific Park that will serve
children, for years to come. As a public school teacher, I know the
challenges working families face because I live them every day. I
believe in practical solutions, fiscal responsibility, and putting our
community first.
I’m committed to keeping Santa Ana affordable, safe, and a place
where every family has the opportunity to succeed. I would be
honored to earn your vote.
