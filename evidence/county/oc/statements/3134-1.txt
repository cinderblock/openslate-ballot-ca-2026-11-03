JEFF KIM
AGE: 51
Governing Board Member, Irvine Unified School District,
Trustee Area 4
As your current school board president, I am honored to serve our
community and work hard to keep Irvine Unified School District one
of the most respected and best school districts in the nation.
My five children attend or recently graduated from IUSD Schools.
We are a proud IUSD Family. I am running for re-election because
of my commitment for our next generation – that they have
opportunities to discover their passion so that they can do good
with it.
For over 20 years in education, I have served as a teacher,
instructional coach, administrator, and as teacher educator at Cal
State University Fullerton and Vanguard University. I received my
teaching credential from the University of California Irvine, my
educational doctorate from the University of Southern California,
and I am a National Board Certified Teacher.
As your board member, I’ll:
Encourage belonging and the well-being of our students in
positive and safe learning environments
Support the high academic achievement of our students by
learning 21st Century Skills in state-of-the-art schools
Strengthen university, school and community partnerships
Retain and attract top teachers
Practice responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars
