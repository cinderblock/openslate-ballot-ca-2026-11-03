SHANA CHARLES
AGE: 50
City of Fullerton Councilmember/Professor
Four years ago, I ran for City Council to ensure every neighborhood
in Fullerton is healthy, heard, and heading in the right direction.
As your representative in City Hall, I led successful efforts to repave
our streets and restore community events like First Night & 4th of
July Fireworks.
I was the driving force behind Fullerton joining the lawsuit against
U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement's racial profiling and
other illegal practices.
I secured funding for our Fire & Police Departments and helped
create the Fiscal Sustainability Committee to ensure Fullerton’s
financial sustainability.
I am the only Councilmember who holds weekly office hours
because I will always listen to the community.
I am running for re-election to build on this progress. As your
Councilmember, I will work to pave 100% of our streets, increase
road safety for all, restore our parks and renew our commitment to
a sustainably equitable future.
I am honored to have the support of Congressmembers Lou Correa
& Derek Tran, California State Senator Tom Umberg, California
Assemblymembers Sharon Quirk-Silva & Avelino Valencia, Orange
County Supervisors Doug Chaffee & Vicente Sarmiento, Fullerton
City Councilmember Ahmad Zahra, and the hard-working families
of the National Union of Healthcare Workers, United Food and
Commercial Workers & Orange County Employees Association.
I hope to earn your support as well, together we can continue to
improve Fullerton’s roads, parks, and finances.
