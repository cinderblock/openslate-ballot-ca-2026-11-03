MELINDA LIU
City of Irvine Councilmember/Attorney
Dear Neighbor,
For the past two years, I have had the privilege of serving as your
City of Irvine Council District 1 Councilmember. When I ran as a new
candidate backed by grassroots support in our community, I made
our shared goals my policy priorities. We have delivered practical
solutions to meet your needs in Council District 1 and Irvine.
In my first short term, I have accomplished critical services and
infrastructures for public safety, education, and quality of life:
Initiated the first community center and library in Council
District 1.
Expanded Irvine Connect to alleviate Northwood High School
traffic.
Improved pedestrian safety citywide.
Ensured new housing projects meet strict fire standards.
Promoted economic development for small and start-up
businesses.
Created accessible housing options for young professionals.
Developed a vital childcare program for working parents.
Started an AAPI focused senior nutrition program using private
funding.
I have listened closely to your concerns and delivered solutions with
thoughtful deliberation, fiscal responsibility, and full transparency.
Together, we can continue to ensure that Irvine remains the best
place for families to live, work, and play.
Thank you for your continued trust and I humbly ask for your
support to serve our community for the next four years.
Melinda Liu
