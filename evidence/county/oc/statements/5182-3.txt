BRANDY ROMERO
Business Owner
My life is rooted in Orange. My husband and I are raising our children
here. I own a small business in Old Towne Orange. I know what
families and local businesses are up against, I live it every day.
As Chair of the City of Orange Park Planning and Community Events
Commission, and a graduate of Leadership Orange and Citizens
Police Academy, I’ve seen what City Hall does well and where
residents deserve better. When Treats in the Streets needed help, I
went to work, helping raise over $40,000 to save it and $10,000 for
the Orange City Firefighters Foundation. I’ve received the Woman
of Impact and Woman of the Year awards for my commitment and
service in our community.
As your City Councilmember, I’ll:
Keep Orange safe by supporting our police, firefighters, and
emergency responders.
Bring fiscal responsibility back to City Hall by protecting essential
services while addressing Orange’s budget challenges.
Protect Old Towne Orange’s historic character and help small
businesses thrive.
Put Orange residents first by increasing transparency,
accountability and responsiveness in city government.
I’m honored to be endorsed by the Orange City Firefighters
Association, Mayor Dan Slater, and Councilwoman Arianna Barrios.
There’s no place like Orange: I will fight to preserve our quality of
life. I respectfully ask for your vote.
