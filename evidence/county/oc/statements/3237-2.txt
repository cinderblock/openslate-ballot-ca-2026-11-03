AMY DUDICH
AGE: 48
Operations Manager/Parent
As a South Korean immigrant adopted at eight months old, I came
to America to live the American dream. That dream has always been
to help others. In earning a Human Services degree from Cal State
Fullerton I strengthened my commitment to serving others in our
community.
Now is the time to pass the baton of leadership in Area 5.
Being a connected parent and volunteer, I listen to families and am
committed to ensuring our students’ needs are met.
As a mother of two Fullerton public school students, PTA leader,
and 15-year resident, I am deeply committed to our schools and
community and understand the needs of today’s students and
families.
Through serving on School Site Council, I have worked alongside
students, teachers, and families. As a trustee, I will support teachers,
expand student opportunities by preparing them for college or the
workforce, ensure our schools remain safe, and advocate for access
to the mental health resources students need to thrive.
Serving my community is both a privilege and a responsibility. I ask
for your vote and support as we unite to build a stronger FJUHSD -
one where every student has the opportunity to succeed and reach
their full potential.
