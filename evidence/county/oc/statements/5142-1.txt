NITESH P. PATEL
AGE: 47
Council Member, City of La Palma
It has been a privilege and an honor to serve and represent you on
the La Palma City Council for the past eight years.
I have been fortunate to call La Palma my home for nearly 25 years.
My wife and I have raised our two daughters, who are products of La
Palma schools. I’ve also believed in giving back to our community by
volunteering with the La Palma Community Foundation, sponsoring
scholarships for graduating seniors at Kennedy High, and providing
meals to our elderly.
I am running for reelection for the City Council because I want to
continue to help our City by:
Advocating for the La Palma Police Department to continue
keeping our families safe,
Being a voice for our residents to deliver on the needs of our
community,
Model responsible fiscal stewardship,
And maintaining La Palma’s status as one of the best places to
live by investing in our infrastructure and afterschool recreation
programs.
I am proud to have always done what is best for La Palma. We’ve
made tremendous progress, and I am optimistic about the future of
La Palma. With your support, I look forward to continuing to give
back and serve you and our City.
I respectfully ask for your vote.
