LONG NGUYEN
Urgent Care Doctor
I’m proud to call Garden Grove my hometown and West Grove my
neighborhood, where my family set roots.
I grew up in Garden Grove and attended GGUSD schools from
elementary through high school. Since attending medical school
and completing my residency, I’ve strived to improve the quality of
life for each of my patients.
My career as an urgent care doctor is based on listening to my
patients and seeking solutions before symptoms worsen. Like in
medicine, I believe taking a preventative approach towards our
city’s issues, like our aging infrastructure and homelessness, can
help prepare us for the problems of tomorrow.
As your District 1 representative, I will continue supporting our local
economy and work to revitalize the business community in key
areas like the Valley View corridor and attract more investment in
the City of Garden Grove.
Together with the City Council, I will work to ensure our families feel
safe in their neighborhoods, our budget stays balanced, and policy
decisions are fiscally responsible.
Like any parent, I want my children to grow up in a neighborhood
that is safe, clean, and spacious.
Join me to ensure District 1 remains a place we are proud to call
home!
