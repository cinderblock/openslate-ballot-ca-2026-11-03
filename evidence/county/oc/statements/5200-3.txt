SHANE HIRSCHMAN
Art Director
My name is Shane Hirschman, and I'm running for Mayor at a
pivotal moment in San Clemente's history. This November, residents
will elect our first directly elected mayor, giving our community a
unique opportunity to shape its future.
As San Clemente approaches its Centennial in 2028 and prepares
to welcome the world as the host of Olympic surfing at Trestles,
we have an opportunity to honor our history while protecting our
iconic, pristine coastline.
I am running a grassroots campaign built on approachability,
transparency, and public service. As your mayor, I will listen, work
collaboratively, and earn the trust of every resident. My goal is
to build on what makes San Clemente exceptional while finding
practical, creative ways to strengthen our community.
As an Art Director and member of the Arts and Culture Committee,
I have helped organize community events that bring residents
together and support local businesses. Those experiences have
reinforced my belief that strong communities are built through
unity, creativity, and civic engagement.
The best solutions don't always come from tradition; they come
from listening, inclusion, and a willingness to think differently.
Together, we can ensure San Clemente remains an extraordinary
place to call home for generations to come.
