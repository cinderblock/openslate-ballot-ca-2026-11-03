CONRAD MAZEIKA
AGE: 42
Nonprofit Executive
I am running for Coast Community College District Trustee
to ensure our community colleges prepare students for the
opportunities of tomorrow.
I earned my degree in History from DePauw University and
have built a career helping people and organizations succeed
through financial planning, business development, real estate, and
civic leadership. As President of the Baltic American Freedom
League, I have worked with community leaders, educators, and
elected officials to advocate for freedom, education, and strong
partnerships.
Our community colleges are critical to our region’s future. As
Trustee, I will champion a Tech Renaissance in Huntington Beach by
expanding partnerships with local employers, strengthening career
pathways, and preparing students for high-demand fields including
artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, advanced manufacturing,
healthcare, and skilled trades.
I believe in responsible stewardship of taxpayer resources,
accountability, innovation, and ensuring students have access to
affordable education that leads to meaningful careers.
Together, we can build a stronger connection between
education and opportunity, empowering students, supporting local
businesses, and keeping our community at the forefront of
innovation.
I respectfully ask for your vote.
