PAULETTE CHAFFEE
Educator/Nonprofit Boardmember
My name is Paulette Chaffee, and I've dedicated much of my life to
improving public education in North Orange County. I'm running for
College Board to expand access to higher education and advocate
for our students, as I believe firmly in both the power of education
and the potential of our students.
I'm a mother, educator, attorney, and longtime community leader
who has been active in dozens of local organizations. I'm also a
former public school teacher and speech therapist with decades of
experience in our schools. I grew up in Fullerton, raised my children
here, and have deep roots in our community.
As your trustee, I'll work to:
Increase job-training programs.
Make transferring easier.
Improve counseling programs.
Support and enhance programs for our veterans.
Responsibly manage the district's budget and precious tax dollars.
I currently serve on the North Orange County Community College
District's Oversight Committee where I work to ensure our tax
dollars are being appropriately spent. I've also raised scholarship
funds for Fullerton College students.
Our community colleges are centers of opportunity, and I am
committed to seeing them thrive. I'm honored to be endorsed by
many local leaders, and I would be honored to receive your vote.
Learn more at www.paulettechaffee.com.
