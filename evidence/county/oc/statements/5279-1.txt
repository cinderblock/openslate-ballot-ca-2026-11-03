KELLY DANIELS
AGE: 50
Businesswoman
I am a proud Vietnamese American, a business owner, an MBA
graduate, and a community advocate.
My priorities are public safety, supporting our police and fire
departments, assisting small businesses succeed, assisting with
relocating the homeless off the streets into supportive services,
supporting financial transparency and ensuring your tax dollars are
spent in the best interest of Westminster.
Since 2012, I have helped immigrant residents’ access food
assistance and other essential programs. I have legally assisted
thousands of people to learn English, complete citizenship
applications, family sponsorship, and other lawful immigration
services. I also support homeless individuals and connect them with
available community resources.
I volunteer as Chief of Staff for a civilian nonprofit supporting the
U.S. Armed Forces through military funeral honors for veterans and
disaster relief efforts too.
These extensive experiences have equipped me with strong
planning, budgeting, and resource management skills to serve
Westminster with integrity, transparency, and fiscal responsibility.
As your City Council Member, I will protect our resident`s quality
of life by investing in our streets, parks and public spaces while
supporting business growth and responsible government.
Let us build a Safer, Stronger Westminster!
