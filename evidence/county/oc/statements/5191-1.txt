SANTOSH SABNIS
Business Owner/Father
As a Placentia resident, business owner, and father, I have
spent over twenty years helping organizations eliminate waste,
improve operations, and make financial decisions using data and
technology. I am running for City Council because City Hall should
manage your hard-earned tax dollars with the same care you use
at your own kitchen table.
Just like any major family investment, asking tough questions
before money is spent prevents costly financial surprises down the
road. Every public expenditure must meet a strict standard: What
are we paying for? What value are we getting? What happens if
things go wrong? Will this still benefit our community ten years
from now?
By aggressively eliminating waste, we can reinvest savings into
protecting home values, helping families call Placentia home,
supporting local job-creating businesses and responsible new
development, investing in our city employees, maintaining safe
streets and parks, strengthening public safety, and providing
opportunities for our children and seniors.
I built the Placentia Civic Vault, a free public tool, so you can
easily understand where your tax dollars go. As a dad raising my
daughters here, keeping Placentia safe, thriving, and affordable is
personal. I would be honored to earn your trust and vote.
