ERIN BEYER
AGE: 61
Business Operations Manager
I have proudly called San Juan Capistrano home for nearly 30 years
and I raised my three children here. Professionally, I have been a
Realtor for more than 40 years, a Certified Real Estate Appraiser
since 2002, and currently manage a local business.
Giving back to our community has always been a priority. I have
volunteered extensively with the PTA, serving at several schools,
including as a Legislative Representative and on the school opening
committee for San Juan Hills High School. My community service
also includes President of my Homeowner’s Association, member
of the Board of Directors for Capistrano Animal Rescue, a regular
columnist for The Capistrano Dispatch, appointee to the City's
Transportation Commission, Coffee Chat co-host for 16 years, and
the City's 50th Anniversary Committee in 2011.
In 2023, I was honored to be named San Juan Capistrano's Woman
of the Year.
As your Councilmember, I will work to protect our unique heritage,
preserve our open spaces, support local businesses, enhance public
safety, and maintain the exceptional quality of life. I know the
history of our town and how important it is to protect what makes
San Juan Capistrano such a special place to call home.
I respectfully ask for your vote.
Thank you.
