VERNON MILLER
Realtor
As a product of this school district, I know firsthand what makes
it special. I walked these campuses, made lifelong friends, and
learned from teachers who shaped who I am today. Today's
students deserve a board that understands this district and has a
vision to help them thrive.
I'm running on three principles: Educate our students to the
highest standard, Empower parents to be partners in their children's
education, and Energize our community with renewed trust and
respect for the board.
Our district faces real challenges that require a fresh perspective.
We must close a $3 million budget deficit with practical, forward
thinking solutions. Recent turmoil has strained trust with our
families and we must rebuild faith in our board by making decisions
openly and keeping our community informed, while retaining
strong school leadership because stability helps students succeed.
I'm not running to bring politics into the classroom. I'm running to
keep the focus on academics, responsible leadership, and preparing
every student for success after graduation.
This district invested in me, and now I'm ready to give back by
serving the next generation of Cypress students. I'd be honored to
earn your vote.
