AMY PHAN WEST
Councilwoman/Businessowner
I am not a career politician. I am a wife, mother, and small business
owner. I am a survivor of the Communist regime in Vietnam. My
father served alongside the United States military in our common
fight to preserve freedom. We made our home in Orange County
over four decades ago. We love America and the values this nation’s
Founding Fathers embraced—family, faith, and freedom.
Four years ago I promised to fight for our city, focus on
safety, balance budgets, and support small businesses. As your
councilmember I delivered: fully funded police and fire, advanced
west-side revitalization, and worked with state and local agencies
to address homelessness while keeping the focus on neighborhood
safety and local businesses.
For the next four years I will continue fighting for stronger public
safety, further west-side revitalization, expanded homelessness
solutions, relief from crushing regulations, and protecting residents
from rising costs to keep Westminster affordable for families.
Blessed to live the American dream, I remain determined to pay it
forward so our children have a brighter future. I humbly ask for your
vote to keep giving the west side of Westminster a strong voice at
City Hall.
