CARRIE BUCK
Governing Board Member, Placentia-Yorba Linda Unified
School District
Public education is about more than reading, writing, and
arithmetic. It’s about preparing students to think critically, adapt
confidently, and thrive in a rapidly changing world. I will continue to
build on PYLUSD’s tradition of excellence while preparing students
for tomorrow.
Under my leadership the district has pivoted from wasteful
spending to responsible budgeting benefiting all students. I
am proud of the progress we are achieving; strengthening
our district’s financial future, building trust through integrity
and transparency, advancing academic excellence, strengthening
special education, expanding career technical education, athletics,
& arts opportunities, and supporting every student.
Our community deserves transparent, accountable governance
where every decision is guided by what is best for students.
As the daughter of two special educators, a mother of three PYLUSD
graduates, and a nonprofit leader dedicated to ending youth
and family homelessness, I understand students’ strengths and
challenges. My record reflects a commitment to students, families,
community partnerships, fiscal responsibility, and transparent,
accountable governance that puts students first.
The choices we make today will shape our students’ futures for
years to come. Experience matters, so does a clear vision for the
future.
I’m asking for your vote for re-election to the PYLUSD School Board.
