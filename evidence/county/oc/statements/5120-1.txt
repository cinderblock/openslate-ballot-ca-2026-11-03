SCOTT MILLER
Laguna Hills Traffic Commissioner
Laguna Hills is my home. I care deeply about our city, our
neighborhoods, and the future we share.
My public service experience includes serving as a mayor, city
council member, and regional government leader in another
California community. I also served our country as a United States
Marine and built a career as a cybersecurity officer. Today, I am
honored to serve Laguna Hills as the current Chairman of the
Laguna Hills Traffic Commission.
My priorities are safe neighborhoods, strong support for law
enforcement, a thriving local business community, responsible
stewardship of taxpayer dollars, thoughtful planning, and
responding to residents.
I am prepared to serve from day one. I will ask difficult questions,
study the facts, listen respectfully, and pursue practical solutions
that improve daily life. I bring independent judgment, transparency,
accountability, and commitment to preserving the character and
quality of life in Laguna Hills.
Public service is a responsibility, not a title. I respectfully ask
for your vote to represent District 4 while working for the best
interests of all Laguna Hills residents with integrity, experience, and
dedication to our community and it’s future.
Respectfully serving you,
Scott Miller
