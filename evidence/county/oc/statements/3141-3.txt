DEE PERRY
Incumbent
I'm Dee Perry, and I am honored to have served our Laguna Beach
Unified School District for many years. Students First. Integrity
Always. These values guide my service in public education.
I have had the privilege of teaching at each Laguna Beach
school as a special education teacher, general education teacher,
and speech-language pathologist, working with students from
preschool through 12th grade. These experiences gave me a deep
understanding of what it takes to help every student succeed.
Serving three terms on the School Board has expanded that
perspective. Through training in school finance, governance,
facilities planning, and Basic Aid districts — and service on nearly
every Board committee — I have developed the experience to
make thoughtful, informed decisions. I bring the rare combination
of having taught in our schools and served on the Board, and a deep
commitment to Laguna Beach.
Every decision I make begins with one question: What is best
for our students? I do the research, listen to other perspectives,
collaborate with constituents and colleagues, and follow through
with action. During my time on the Board, I have championed:
Hiring specialized science and art teachers for our elementary
schools
Installing video recording of Board meetings — not just audio —
to preserve integrity and accessibility
Expanding college and career readiness opportunities
Improving student transportation services
Increasing support for English learners and our exceptional
learners with special needs
Championing the arts and rebuilding our community's
connection to our schools
Changes are only possible when the Board puts students first and
sets politics aside.
I thought carefully about running again. I decided to because our
community deserves to choose its representatives at the ballot box.
One of the greatest joys of this campaign has been having a former
student step forward to run with me. Seeing that full circle — from
the classroom to the Board Room — has been one of the most
rewarding experiences of my career.
Our community will not always agree, and that is healthy. What
matters is how we work through our differences — with respect,
civility, transparency, and a shared commitment to providing every
child with an exceptional education.
It would be an honor to earn your vote. Together, we can continue
putting students first and preparing every child for a bright future.
Thank you.
