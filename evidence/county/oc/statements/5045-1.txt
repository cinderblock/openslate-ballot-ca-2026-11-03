RANDY HARPER
AGE: 63
Emergency Manager/Firefighter
Effective representation matters, especially on our City Council. For
over 40 years, I served our communities as a firefighter, retiring as
a Battalion Chief. Today I serve as an Emergency Manager for the
County of Orange currently assigned to the Social Services Agency.
My wife and I raised our family in District 5 for almost 30 years. We
chose Cypress for simple reasons: great schools, safe streets, and
a family-focused community.
I'm running because I understand how local government works and
will stay Cypress focused guaranteeing that District 5 concerns
are heard and addressed. I will ensure that our city returns to a
financially frugal philosophy that placed us in the strong position
we now enjoy.
As your councilman, I will make sure we build smartly, fighting major
zoning changes that don't fit our neighborhoods. I will oppose plans
to open Holder Street, disrupting our beloved walking path. I’ll work
diligently on homeless concerns city wide, keeping our recreational
areas safe.
When disaster hits, our best leaders rise. I've spent my career
being ready for these moments: calm under pressure, focused on
solutions, and always answering the call.
I hope to earn your vote.
