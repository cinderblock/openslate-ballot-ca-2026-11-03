MELISSA CALDWELL
AGE: 46
Parent/Businesswoman
Students and families in the Capistrano Unified School District
deserve trustees with a proven record of service, meaningful
advocacy, and professional experience managing complex systems
and budgets. With over 15 years serving as a PTA volunteer
and leader, 6 years of community service supporting our nation’s
military and their families with care packages, holiday toy drives,
newborn essentials and family events, and 25 years as a Chief
Operating Officer for a successful law firm, I bring the experience
and commitment our community deserves.
As a longtime Laguna Niguel resident, school volunteer, and leader
of large volunteer organizations, I’ve spent years working alongside
parents, educators and local partners to support students across
CUSD. I’ve seen firsthand how strong community collaboration
strengthens our schools and I’m prepared to bring that same spirit
of partnership to our school board.
My background as a Chief Operating Officer combined with real
world experience as a PTA leader has prepared me to deliver
results. I will apply my operational expertise to ensure responsible
fiscal policies that prioritize classrooms and protect taxpayer
investments. I will ask tough, informed questions to ensure our
schools are safe, secure, and operating effectively for every
student. I will support our educators and administrators as they
work to create learning environments that meet students where
they are, providing the targeted resources each child needs to
succeed.
Clear communication and strong community relationships are
essential to keeping our schools exceptional. I’m ready to put
my professional experience and dedication to work for students,
families, and the future of the Capistrano Unified School District. I
would be honored to serve as your next Area 4 CUSD Trustee.
