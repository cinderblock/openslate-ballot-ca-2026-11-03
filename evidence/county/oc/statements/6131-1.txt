SERGIO CONTRERAS
Director, Midway City Sanitary District
Dear Neighbor,
Thank you for the trust you’ve placed in me as a school board
member, city councilman, and now on the Midway City Sanitary
District board.
As a father, homeowner, and taxpayer, I’ve spent the last four years
working to keep our community healthy, safe, and clean.
Here’s what I’ve done for you on the Sanitary District board:
Kept our trash collection service rates one of the lowest in
Orange County;
Helped keep our sewer system spill-free for over 1000 days,
protecting our neighborhoods, streets, and local waterways.
Championed the District’s first-ever 5-year plan to invest in our
sewer system, prevent backups, protect against emergencies,
and keep our service reliable for years to come;
Grew community outreach so more residents know what district
services are available and how to use them; and
Held 20 free bulky-item clean-up events that helped residents
clear over 400 tons of junk off our streets;
With your support, I’ll keep working hard to protect our community,
keep rates low, and deliver great service.
Please vote to re-elect me, Sergio Contreras, for Midway City
Sanitary District.
