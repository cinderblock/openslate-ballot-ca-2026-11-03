HILDA SOLIS
DEMOCRATIC
County Supervisor
The working people of Southern California deserve a strong
advocate in Congress. County Supervisor Hilda Solis is a proven
fighter, and we can count on her to fight to create local jobs and
economic growth, make neighborhoods safer, and tackle inflation
and the high cost of health care, groceries, gasoline, insurance,
housing and rent.
That’s why Hilda Solis is the candidate in the 38th District endorsed
by nurses, firefighters, deputies, small business owners, Planned
Parenthood Action Fund, and the L.A. / Orange County Building
and Construction Trades Council, representing tens of thousands of
working families.
Hilda Solis has demonstrated that she stands up for the
people – not the powerful – as an educator, lawmaker, U.S.
Representative, President Barack Obama’s Secretary of Labor, and
county supervisor. She works across party lines to get things done.
Raised in the San Gabriel Valley as the daughter of immigrants,
Hilda Solis fights for affordable healthcare for all. She’s fighting
back against the Trump Administration as they slash Medi-Cal
for thousands of Orange County residents. Hilda Solis fights
for solutions to save the health and emergency care systems
throughout Southern California. That’s why she’s supported by
United Healthcare Workers.
Hilda Solis works to improve our local economy so that people can
earn more at work and get a fair chance to buy or rent an affordable
home. She secured grants for small businesses to create local jobs.
And Hilda Solis fights to lower the cost of living for all.
Leaders who have represented Orange County including
Congressmembers Lou Correa, Gil Cisneros, and Robert Garcia
unite with the National Organization for Women in support of Hilda
Solis for Congress. Visit HildaSolis.com
