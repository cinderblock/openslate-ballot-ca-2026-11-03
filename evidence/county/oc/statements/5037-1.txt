FISHER DERDERIAN
Business Owner/Parent
Costa Mesa has always done things a little differently. Our mix of
cultures, creativity, entrepreneurship, and independent spirit has
given this city an identity all its own.
I’ve spent my career building organizations, founding nonprofits,
and bringing people together across the United States, Latin
America, and Europe. Now I want to bring that experience home. My
wife and I are raising our five children here, and I care deeply about
the Costa Mesa their generation will inherit.
We live in a beautiful place. We should build like it. As the City of
the Arts, we should have a vision for growth that makes Costa Mesa
more beautiful, walkable, affordable, and full of opportunity.
Whether you rent or own, City Hall should work for you. I’ll
keep neighborhoods safe, support first responders, bring common
sense to homelessness, simplify permits and approvals, help local
businesses thrive, and work to make daily life more affordable.
District 4 should share fully in that future. I want 19th Street
and Harbor Boulevard to become safer, more inviting places for
business, food, arts, and community life.
For every decision, I’ll ask one question: Does this make Costa Mesa
better for the people who call it home?
