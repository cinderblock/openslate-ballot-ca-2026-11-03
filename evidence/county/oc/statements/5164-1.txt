LAURA LACKO
AGE: 52
Community Volunteer
Mission Viejo is a special community because people care about
their neighborhoods and are willing to show up to protect what
makes our city work. As a community volunteer, I have seen what
can be accomplished when residents listen to one another, work
together, and put the community first.
I am running for City Council to bring fresh eyes and a fresh
perspective to City Hall. I will listen to residents and make sure
community voices are heard before important decisions are made
—not after.
Neighborhoods matter. I will work to protect public safety, maintain
our streets, parks, and infrastructure, support seniors who want to
remain independent in the community they love, and ensure City
Hall stays focused on the reliable services residents expect.
I also believe taxpayers deserve responsible budgeting, clear
priorities, and straightforward information about how public dollars
are spent. Residents should not have to repeatedly follow up to get
answers or have basic problems addressed.
Leadership is about more than attending meetings. It means being
accessible, doing the work, following through, and showing up for
the people you represent.
I would be honored to earn your vote for Mission Viejo City Council,
District 4.
