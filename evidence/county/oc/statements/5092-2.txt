PAT BURNS
Retired Policeman/Councilmember
As a 33 year resident of Huntington Beach, I believe the City
Council should serve the residents and businesses with integrity,
transparency and fiscal responsibility to improve the quality of
life. Community service always comes first and never self-service.
Supported by residents, business owners, public safety officers, I am
committed to serving.
I am currently on the Huntington Beach City Council and was
the previous mayor. I am a retired Long Beach Police Lieutenant
where I served for 30 years. I worked Patrol, flew the helicopter,
was an academy instructor, an undercover Narcotics Investigator,
Sergeant and Lieutenant. During my career I volunteered as coach
and referee for AYSO Soccer, St. Bonaventure School Board and
Finance Committee. I also served the City of Huntington Beach
on the Citizen Participation Advisory Board and Community and
Library Service Commission. I currently serve on the Long Beach
City Employees Federal Credit Union Board of Directors.
I would like to stay another 4 years on the City Council and serve
the citizens and businesses of Huntington Beach focused on Public
Safety, fiscal responsibility, local control and to preserve our beach
community and quality of life.
This is our community and my home.
I love this city and care for its future.
