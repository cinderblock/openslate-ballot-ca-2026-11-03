JORDAN ABUSHAWISH
Parent/Healthcare Advocate
Jordan Abushawish is the father of two Fullerton School District
students and lives one block from their school. He understands
how difficult it can be for families to balance work and household
responsibilities, stay informed, access school resources, and
advocate for their children. For three years, he has served
on the Rolling Hills School Site Council, working with parents,
teachers, and administrators to support students and improve
communication.
Jordan Abushawish is a parent and healthcare advocate running for
School Board Trustee Area 3 to bring a parent’s voice and practical
leadership to the Fullerton School Board. Jordan loves serving his
community, from volunteering at community backpack and local
food distribution events.
As our School Boardmember, Jordan Abushawish will strengthen
campus safety, expand behavioral health support, improve
communication, and ensure every family understands the resources
available to help their children succeed. He will work to reduce
absenteeism, improve student achievement, and support teachers
and school employees.
With a career in public policy and healthcare, Jordan has the
experience to bring people together, manage issues, and make
responsible decisions for students and families. Jordan Abushawish
will listen to parents, support educators, protect our public schools,
and help Fullerton remain a leader in educational excellence.
