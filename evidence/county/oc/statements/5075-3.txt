DAVID FISCHBEIN
AGE: 54
Quality Assurance Inspector
My name is David Fischbein and I am running for Fullerton City
Council to bring proven leadership, accountability, and practical
problem-solving to District 5. As a retired U.S. Navy veteran, I
spent years leading people, managing resources, solving problems
under pressure, and completing missions where preparation and
accountability mattered.
My military service included leadership in aviation and maritime
operations, including overseas deployments supporting U.S.
missions. I was responsible for personnel, equipment, safety,
maintenance, and operational readiness. In one aviation
assignment, I supervised a 25-sailor work center supporting 12
helicopters and helped reduce aircraft downtime by 25%.
My civilian career has built on those skills through purchasing,
supply chain management, quality assurance and government
acquisition. I've managed relationships with dozens of vendors,
supported millions of dollars in annual sales, and handled
government purchases ranging from $10,000 to $250,000. I also
hold a B.S. in Business Administration.
Fullerton deserves a councilmember who will focus on
responsible spending, safer streets, well-maintained infrastructure,
responsive city services, and transparent government. I'll bring
a mission-focused approach to District 5: listen to residents,
understand the facts, manage resources responsibly, and deliver
measurable results. I'll work with residents, staff, businesses, and
councilmembers to deliver results.
