JOSH BASTIAN
Planning Commissioner, City of Rancho Santa Margarita
We need dedicated, experienced, proven leaders on our City
Council, especially when our city and residents face significant
economic and social challenges.
Today, Rancho Santa Margarita is thriving.
RSM is safe: Ranked by the FBI as #1 safest city in Orange County!
RSM is financially strong: Our city maintains a balanced budget,
finished last year with a surplus, and has saved over $30 Million in
reserves.
RSM protects taxpayers: Our city earned the prestigious
Government Finance Officers Association Certificate of
Achievement for Excellence in Financial Reporting.
RSM is beautiful: Our City constructed the beautiful RSM monument
sign at the Alicia gateway into Rancho – and this year, the gorgeous
RSM monument sign, decorative fencing/median improvements
along the Antonio gateway into Rancho. Enhanced street lighting/
median improvements have been installed all across our City.
RSM provides a special quality of life: Our city opened the children’s
Splash Pad in Central Park, synchronized traffic signals to reduce
congestion, upgraded animal services to join a local no kill shelter,
sponsored free summer concerts and the annual Rancho Family
Fest community celebration.
I’m proud to serve as your Planning Commissioner -- and of
everything our city has accomplished.
Thanks to years of hard work and responsible leadership, Rancho
Santa Margarita is strong and its future bright.
Upon my election, my primary goals are to maintain our city’s
financial security and protect its award-winning master plan.
I will ensure residents continue to be protected by the finest fully
funded police and firefighting services.
I will support policies promoting lower taxes, conservative fiscal
policies, strong families, vibrant local businesses, reduced traffic,
wildfire preparedness, and safe neighborhoods and parks.
I’m well qualified to serve.
I’m a Regional Vice President in the emergency recovery and
restoration industry, overseeing operations, budgets, personnel
development, and multimillion dollar performance goals across
multiple states.
For years I served as President of the Rancho Santa Margarita
Chamber of Commerce and as Chairman of Rancho Family Fest. I’m
also involved with our local church, schools and youth activities,
RSM Cares and other community organizations.
My wife, Amanda, and I have lived in Rancho Santa Margarita for
more than twenty years. Our son serves our country in the United
States Coast Guard, and we are raising our two younger daughters
in this community we proudly call home.
I’m a dedicated, experienced, proven leader -- and proud to be
endorsed by Mayor Tony Beall.
I respectfully ask for your vote.
