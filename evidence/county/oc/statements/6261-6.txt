MIKE SAFRANSKI
Director, Trabuco Canyon Water District
As a Director of the Trabuco Canyon Water District, my highest
priority is ensuring that our customers continue to receive safe,
reliable, and responsibly managed water and sewer services. I am
committed to supporting policies that maintain and upgrade our
ageing infrastructure so the District can operate effectively for
years to come.
In recent years, we have faced significant challenges-including
state-mandated revenue reductions, a nationwide pandemic, and
rising consulting and construction costs. Despite these pressures,
I remain focused on keeping our water and sewer rates as low as
practical while still protecting the reliability of our operations.
I believe the best way to serve our customers is to listen carefully
to their input and make decisions that balance affordability with
long-term reliability. Rate adjustments are an ongoing reality in
today's environment, but I will continue seeking ways to offset costs
through grant funding, operational efficiencies, and collaboration
with neighboring water agencies.
While it may be tempting to promise rate reductions, I will
not support commitments that jeopardize the District's ability to
operate safely and dependably. Instead, I will continue working to
keep rates as low as possible without compromising the reliability
of the services our community depends on.
