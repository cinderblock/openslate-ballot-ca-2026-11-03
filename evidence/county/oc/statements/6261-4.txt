JOHN CASARIETTI
CFO
I've called Trabuco Canyon home for 22 years, where my family has
built our life and invested in this community.
As a CFO, I bring direct experience managing budgets,
infrastructure investment, and long-term financial planning.
Water districts face real pressures: aging infrastructure, rate
sustainability, and environmental compliance. These aren't abstract
issues; they affect every household's water bill and service
reliability.
My background isn't theoretical. I've overseen capital allocation,
vendor negotiations, and operational efficiency in complex
organizations. Those skills translate directly to the boardroom:
ensuring ratepayers get maximum value, prioritizing infrastructure
investments correctly, and ensuring the district operates without
waste.
I've also served this community for 20 years as a Little League
coach, soccer coach, and board member of multiple nonprofits.
That experience taught me to listen and represent what people
need, not what I assume they want.
A board is only effective when it listens. Whether it's reliable service,
predictable rates, or environmental stewardship, I will represent
your interests.
Not every problem requires more spending; some require better
decisions. That's where I can contribute.
I'm asking for your vote.
