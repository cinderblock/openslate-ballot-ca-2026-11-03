JANICE LIM
City Councilmember/Businesswoman
Serving as your Mayor (2025) and Yorba Linda Councilmember, I
have worked to preserve the hometown values that make our city
the 5th safest city in California.
On the City Council, I have prioritized public safety, fiscal
responsibility and protecting our quality of life. Together, we have:
Balanced every city budget while protecting reserves
Maintained the City's prestigious AAA credit rating
Opposed high-density housing projects
Invested in roads, parks, and infrastructure
Expanded public safety staff and invested in fire detection
technology
Added two new Heli-Hydrants for fire response
I have partnered with the Sheriff’s Department to promote Scam
Stopper programs for our seniors, continued my commitment to
Love Yorba Linda, the city’s largest community service day, and
served on the Placentia Navigation Center Board, achieving more
than a threefold increase in successful housing outcomes.
If re-elected, I will continue to use my professional background in
business and finance to advocate for our city and fight the unfunded
mandates coming out of Sacramento. I will fight to preserve Yorba
Linda’s unique character, for my children as well as yours, to
protect our neighborhoods and ensure local residents—not state
politicians—shape our community's future.
I respectfully ask for your vote for re-election to the Yorba Linda
City Council.
