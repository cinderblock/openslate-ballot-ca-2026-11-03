ANDREW HERRERA
AGE: 44
Consumer Relations Manager
Hello Brea, my name is Andrew Herrera or, as some of you may know
me, the Enjoy Brea guy.
This is my second time running for City Council. My first campaign
was a great learning experience, and I had the opportunity to meet
many wonderful people. One piece of feedback I received was that
I didn't know enough about the city. Another was that, while my
platform focused on supporting small businesses, people wanted
to know how I planned to do that.
Currently, I serve as vice president of the Brea Historical Society,
where I have the opportunity to learn about our city's history from
day one. Every day, I learn something new that makes me love Brea
even more. Small businesses are the backbone of our economy,
and for the past four years, I have spent my free time getting to
know Brea business owners and sharing their services and stories
with tens of thousands of people through my various volunteer
platforms.
As a council member, my approach would be to gather input from
the community before making decisions that affect us all. I want
everyone to feel comfortable coming to me, knowing that I will
listen.
