BEN DAVIS
AGE: 53
Civil Rights Attorney
I've spent my life showing up for people. First, I helped manufacture
medicines, including a groundbreaking cancer vaccine. I later
earned my JD/MBA, and I’ve spent the past several years fighting
for civil rights and housing — work that gave me practical
experience with systems needed in our local government.
When I’m on City Council, I will manage public funds responsibly,
invest in the infrastructure and core services our community
relies on, and represent all of our residents. I'll bring a practical,
community-first approach to housing that actually works for
the people who live here. I'm committed to careful, transparent
budgeting and to being a steady voice for safety and good
governance — because this city's name should shine the way we
all know is possible.
Since moving to Huntington Beach with my wife, Jill, nearly 20 years
ago, I have come to regard this city's future as inseparable from my
own. I've spent 30 years working on difficult problems because they
needed doing. I’m ready to do the same here and make real change
for Huntington Beach. I humbly ask for your vote for City Council by
November 3rd.
