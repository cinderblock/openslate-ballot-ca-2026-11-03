KEN CALVERT
U.S. Representative
It is an honor to represent our region in the U.S. House of
Representatives. I have a proven record of fighting for you and
delivering results for southern California. I was honored to receive
the most votes in the June Primary Election in this district and I am
eager to earn your support once again.
As a Representative for Orange County and southern California,
I have helped secure several billion dollars in federal funding for
critical Orange County transportation, flood control, and water
supply projects. I am honored to be endorsed by many Orange
County leaders, including Orange County Supervisor Don Wagner
and Rancho Santa Margarita Mayor Tony Beall.
In Congress, I am leading efforts to lower costs for American
families, including lowering energy and gasoline prices by
increasing energy supplies here in California. I am also
spearheading the crackdown on fraud by introducing the Zero
Tolerance for Fraudsters Act, which will impose mandatory
minimum prison sentences for convicted fraudsters.
Your family’s safety is one of my highest priorities. I have
consistently supported a strong border, our law enforcement
officers, and tough on crime policies. I have earned the
endorsement of local law enforcement organizations, sheriffs, and
district attorneys.
I strongly believe we should improve the integrity of our elections
by making Voter ID mandatory. That is why I have been a leader on
Prop 39, the California Voter ID initiative.
As a congressman with a proven and effective record of delivering
for Orange County, I humbly ask for your vote.
Ken Calvert
U.S. Representative
