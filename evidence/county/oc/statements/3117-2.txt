LINDA SHEPARD
Educator/Orange County Businesswoman
I am passionate about education. I earned degrees from Saint
Mary’s College and Northwestern University’s Kellogg Graduate
School of Business.
My work for young people has included:
Teaching accounting/finance at three universities
Teaching TK-3 students
Tutoring K-8 students
Tutoring high school geometry
Leading a Gifted and Talented Education career program
Helping build Discovery Science Center
13 years organizing math and science family nights for
Capistrano Unified School District schools
24 years working with Scouting America families
As a financial professional, I have been:
Chief Financial Officer, Habitat for Humanity of Orange County
Accounting Director, Taco Bell Corporation
Senior Manager, Finance, Los Angeles Market, Verizon
Certified Public Accountant (CPA), KPMG
Capistrano Unified School District is facing years of declining
enrollment and significant budget challenges. As an educator and
CPA, I will work tirelessly to get us back on track. As your trustee on
the Capistrano Unified School District board, I will:
Fight for a high-quality education for every student
Restore strong foundations in core subjects and American civics
Protect parental rights: ensure transparent access to curriculum
Defend local control and focus classroom resources on students
Demand complete accountability of taxpayer dollars
I will bring an educator’s experience, mother’s perspective, and
small business owner’s financial discipline to every decision. I am
honored to be endorsed by Capistrano Unified School District
Trustees Judy Bullockus, Lisa Zollinger, Jennifer Adnams, and Lisa
Davis. I respectfully ask for your vote. www.lindaforcusd.com
