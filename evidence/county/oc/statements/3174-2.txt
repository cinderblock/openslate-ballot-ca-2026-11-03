CRYSTAL K. MILES
AGE: 57
Councilmember/Business Owner
Transparency, accountability, and earning people's trust aren't new
goals for me — they're how I've always led, as a city councilmember
and small business owner. Families deserve a school system that
treats every taxpayer dollar with the same discipline and care you
would in running your household or business.
I strongly support parent rights and full access to curriculum
information. Parents are partners in their children's education, not
outsiders, and deserve a real seat at the table.
If elected, I will make sure teachers have the support they
need, open district spending to real public scrutiny, and focus
on improving academic outcomes for every student regardless of
background or ability.
My experience on the City Council, running a business, and raising
my own sons in this district has taught me how to manage budgets
responsibly and hold organizations accountable.
On the Villa Park City Council, I initiated our city's e-bike ordinance
to help keep kids safe on our streets, proof I get results, not just talk.
Only 46% of our students are proficient in math. Our children
deserve better!
I’d be honored to have your vote.
