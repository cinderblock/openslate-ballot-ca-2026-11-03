MICHAEL CAPUTO
AGE: 56
Traffic Commissioner/Businessman
“Keep it Simple” is my guiding principle for effective governance.
I have no political agenda other than to serve the residents of
Laguna Hills. As a three-term Chairman of the Laguna Hills Traffic
Commission, my dedication to our community is clear. My family has
called Laguna Hills home since 1980; I bought my first house here
in 2007. My wife and daughter both attended Valencia Elementary
and Laguna Hills High School, reinforcing my deep ties with our
community.
In addition to my involvement with several HOA boards, I currently
serve as President of my community’s HOA. Professionally, I
manage a seven-person sales team in the building materials
industry for a $2 billion company, giving me the experience to
handle complex projects.
My main goal as a City Council member is to ensure a safe
environment while enhancing the city’s beauty, thereby raising
property values and improving quality of life. If elected, I promise to
listen to the residents of Laguna Hills and serve with honesty and
integrity. Together, we can make our city an even better place to
live.
