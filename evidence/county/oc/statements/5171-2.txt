TINA BABAJANIANS
AGE: 41
Business Owner
When City Hall stops listening, you can keep complaining or put
your name on the ballot. So here I am.
I earned a master’s degree in Speech-Language Pathology and
spent 15 years as a clinician, helping people find their voice. That
work taught me that being heard is a two-way street and too many
Newport residents no longer feel heard by City Hall.
I never expected to own a restaurant, either. I entered the industry
without a roadmap and learned to build a business, lead a team, and
solve problems in real time.
Then came Spring Break and the Fourth of July and the toll they
took on our neighborhoods and sense of safety. Once again, I faced
a choice: stay on the sidelines or become part of the solution.
Leaders make hard choices, accept responsibility, and remain
present when things get uncomfortable. That’s what I’ll bring to the
Newport Beach City Council.
I’ll protect public safety, preserve our quality of life, and ensure City
Hall listens to the people it serves.
I’m ready to listen, lead, and work for you. Let’s get to work,
Newport!
