KIMBERLY SMITH
AGE: 43
Tech CEO/Parent
I am running for the Laguna Beach Unified School District Board
because our schools should reflect the very best of our community:
excellence, integrity, responsible stewardship, and an unwavering
commitment to our future generation - students.
Laguna Beach schools have long been one of our community's
greatest strengths. They attract families, enrich our city, and reflect
the values we share. I am committed to preserving that legacy while
thoughtfully preparing our students for the future.
I am the mother of two LBUSD students and experience this district
as a parent and a community member who understands that strong
schools are at the heart of a strong community. Our children should
be challenged, encouraged, and truly known. Those experiences
prepare them to lead fulfilling lives and help create the community
we all want to call home.
Professionally, I serve as CEO of a national Public Benefit
technology company that has built the infrastructure to redistribute
surplus resources to people who need them. Leading an
organization at the intersection of technology, operations, and
public good has reinforced my belief that innovation succeeds
when it is implemented thoughtfully, guided by clear objectives,
and measured by outcomes. Those same principles belong in
public education, where responsible governance, sound financial
stewardship, and thoughtful decision-making create the conditions
for students and teachers to succeed.
Technology plays an important role in our children's future,
and our schools have a responsibility to prepare students
for that reality. I support its thoughtful integration where it
strengthens learning, enhances instruction, and equips students
with the skills they need in college, careers, and life. Technology
should enhance, not replace, the essential human skills that
remain at the heart of exceptional education: critical thinking,
communication, collaboration, creativity, sound judgment, and
meaningful relationships with teachers and classmates.
Every student deserves an outstanding education and the
opportunity to discover their strengths. For some, that path
is advanced academics. For others, it is found through other
important curricular choices that include the arts, athletics, Career
Technical Education, leadership, and service. A great school district
recognizes that excellence takes many forms and prepares every
student for a successful future.
I believe strong governance creates stability. It builds trust,
supports educators, and allows district leadership and our teachers
and staff to remain focused on what matters most: helping students
thrive.
My family is proud to call Laguna Beach home, and I would be
honored to earn your support.
