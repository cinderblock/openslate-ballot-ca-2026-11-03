LARRY D. DICK
Director Division 2, Municipal Water District of Orange
County
Clean, reliable and affordable water doesn't just happen, it takes
experienced leadership. As Division 2 Director on the Municipal
Water District of Orange County (MWDOC) Board and currently
serving as Board President, I remain devoted to developing reliable
and economical supplies of safe, high quality drinking water for our
communities.
For twenty years I represented Orange County as a Director of the
Metropolitan Water District of Southern California (MET), serving 19
million Californians.
During my service on MET and MWDOC my top priorities were
water quality and reliability, alongside environmental stewardship
and water sustainability. Our region faces real challenges ahead:
Uncertainty in Colorado River negotiations, growing impacts of
climate change and continuing issues on the State Water Project,
all requiring experienced leadership.
My experience extends across the water community, including the
Executive Committee of the Special Districts of Orange County,
member of the Water Advisory Committee of Orange County
including two terms as President and a member of the Executive
Committee of the Urban Water Institute.
I ask for your support in continuing this important work by casting
your vote for me. Thank you for your consideration.
Respectfully
Larry Dick
