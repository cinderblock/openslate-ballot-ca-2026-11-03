JOE DOVINH
Garden Grove Council Member
Dear Residents,
It has been an honor to serve you as Councilmember and Mayor Pro
Tem. Please accept my deepest appreciation. I seek your Vote for a
second term to bring to fruition many important projects that begun
under my watch since 2022. Last year, we opened our Navigation
Center to provide much needed shelter. This September, our Public
Safety Building will be inaugurated. We wrap-up our fiscal year
with a balanced budget while the State of our City remains Strong. I
have been a constant advocate for Immigrant Rights as we navigate
policy challenges. I have promoted development of multiple
communities. I’m always reminding our development partners of
building with an eye for affordable housing and honoring our
commitment to hire locally and respect labor contracts. I have
supported our employees and city staff as we renew their contracts
in-line with inflation and increased needs. I stand with our residents
to demand accountability, transparency and responsibility in times
of crises as exemplified in the case of GKN chemical tank crisis. I
vow to double my efforts to serve you even better in the next term.
Joe DoVinh
Garden Grove Council Member
