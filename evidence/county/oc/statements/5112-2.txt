SUE KEMPF
Laguna Beach City Councilmember
During my time on the Council, I have done my best to be thoughtful,
open-minded, and effective. I’ve worked alongside a leadership
team for whom I have enormous respect. We are not afraid to
differ, and have used our differences to make better decisions
for our community. And that’s how we will find good answers to
hard questions. How can we keep our life-saving emergency room?
Can we meet Sacramento's drastic housing mandates without
sacrificing our autonomy? Can we further ramp up our efforts
to police visitors who disregard the “house rules”. And, in an
increasingly tough economy, can we live within our means more
efficiently so that we can protect everything that makes Laguna
Beach so unique? We continue to invest heavily in fire safety,
implemented a stewardship plan to address visitor impacts like our
Clean Laguna program, drone policing and a ban on canopies, and
planned for local control of water availability with the addition of
a new groundwater well in partnership with Newport Beach. These
daunting challenges require the kind of collaborative work I love
doing and — with your support and trust — pledge to continue
doing for the hometown we love.
