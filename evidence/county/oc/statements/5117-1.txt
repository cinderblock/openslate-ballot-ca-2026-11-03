LAURA THIES
AGE: 57
Business Administrator
Laguna Hills has been our family’s home for 28 years. Our
neighborhood, lovingly know as the “neighborhood behind the DMV
and Polly’s” is a tight knit and supportive community. I would be
proud to represent District 1 while serving all residents of Laguna
Hills. I am an Orange County native with my BS in Mathematics
with a cognate in Philosophy from CSULB. My husband and I
began growing our family of 5 children while growing a business.
Some of my past and current volunteer work includes, serving San
Joaquin Elementary as member of the PTA Board, helping at the
Laguna Food Pantry, team parent for Hawk softball, and working
as a Catechist at our Church. I will work hard to help implement
solutions for the common good of our city and all residents.
