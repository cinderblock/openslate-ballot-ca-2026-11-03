I’m Jenny Rae Le Roux - a business owner, mom, and government
watchdog running for Congress because career politicians have
broken California - spending too much, delivering too little, and
serving special interests first. I’m not a politician. I’m an outsider
fighting to restore accountability to Congress.
California was once a beacon of freedom and opportunity. Today,
families are crushed by inflation, unaffordable housing, high gas
prices, rising taxes, failing schools, and crumbling infrastructure.
While hardworking Californians struggle, politicians keep spending
more with little to show for it. Billions have been wasted on
failed homelessness programs and over-budget projects that never
deliver. Orange County deserves leaders who solve problems, keep
promises, and respect taxpayers.
As a government watchdog, I lead a fraud task force that has
exposed over $2 billion in government waste and abuse. Every
day, I work to cut bureaucracy, increase transparency, and protect
taxpayers. That’s the passion I’ll bring to Congress.
As a business leader, I’ve built startups into global companies
by eliminating waste, reducing costs, and demanding results.
Government should be run with the same efficiency.
As a mom with children in public school, I’m invested in building a
future where families can afford to live, work, and thrive.
Congress doesn’t need another career politician. It needs leaders
with real-world experience who know how to fix broken systems.
I’ll fight for safe communities, secure borders, excellent schools, a
stronger economy, and a government that works for us. I would be
honored to earn your vote.
