KIMBERLY HO
Entrepreneur
I’m a longtime Westminster resident and a thirty-five-year business
owner in the city of Westminster. Coming from a hardworking
immigrant family, I started my first business with only five hundred
dollars. While working two jobs to support myself, I sacrificed and
struggled for years, facing many challenges along the way. Through
determination and hard work, I eventually grew that small business
into a successful multi-million dollar company.
For decades, I have also quietly served as a dedicated
philanthropist, supporting numerous local charities and families in
need. I have lived the real struggles of small business owners. I
understand the difficulties caused by rising costs, tight budgets, and
long hours. I know what it’s like to fight every day just to survive and
grow a business in this city.
If elected Mayor, I will bring the same discipline, accountability,
and results-oriented approach to City Hall that I used to build my
own successful business. I am committed to making housing more
affordable, supporting local small businesses, reducing wasteful
spending, improving public safety, maintaining clean streets, and
creating a better quality of life for all families and seniors in
Westminster.
I also earned a Doctor of Pharmacy and an Executive MBA.
