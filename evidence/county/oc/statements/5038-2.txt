KELLY BROWN
AGE: 49
Communications Director/Educator
I believe in the potential of Costa Mesa for all of us: families, single
folk, seniors, small business owners, and creatives. I’m a mom driven
by local community needs, not politics, to face problems head on.
As Chair of Costa Mesa’s Parks Commission, I have a track record
of working collaboratively to solve problems and increase public
engagement including:
Prioritizing westside park funding
Enhancing outreach for senior transportation needs
Expanding community gardens citywide
Leading the creation of our city’s first values framework for park
investments
On Council I’ll continue to be a proactive leader who:
Ensures responsible city budgets
Improves transparency and community engagement, including
on issues like Flock
Advocates for balanced development
Enhances street safety through bike paths, walkability, and street
calming
Increases access to parks, gardens, and nature
Supports working families struggling with high costs of living
My husband, daughter, two dogs, and I love living on Costa Mesa’s
Westside. And with 25 years of experience working in the public
sector at all levels of government, including 5 years as a Parks
Commissioner, I’m ready to serve you on Day 1. I’m endorsed
by Councilwoman Arlis Reynolds and Parks Commissioners
Liz Dorn-Parker, Cristian Garcia-Arcos, Brandice Leger, and
Shayanne Wright. Let’s Connect! Visit: kellyforcostamesa.com or
@kellyforcostamesa.
