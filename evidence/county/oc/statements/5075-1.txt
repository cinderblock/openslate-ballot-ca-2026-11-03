OSCAR VALADEZ
Fullerton Commissioner/Businessowner
The son of immigrants, I was born and raised in Fullerton. My
parents taught me the value of education: I graduated from
Fullerton schools, and I earned my Civil Engineering degree at
Stanford University.
My wife is a public school counselor, and we decided to raise our
two children here in my hometown of Fullerton, where I’ve grown
our family’s construction business.
I love Fullerton. My service to our city includes:
Chair, Fullerton Planning Commission
Fullerton Parks and Recreation Commissioner
Fullerton Transportation and Circulation Commissioner
Team Leader, Love Fullerton
Fullerton Sunrise Rotary
That volunteer work includes:
Monthly Truslow Park cleanup
Native garden at Ford Park
Adopting Union Pacific Park
Mobile food pantry at Fullerton churches and parks
Upcoming mobile dental clinic
If you elect me to represent our community on Fullerton’s City
Council, I will:
Ensure Fullerton police and firefighters have the resources they
need to protect our safety
Improve Fullerton roads
Enhance Fullerton parks and public spaces
Balance and audit Fullerton’s budget with more transparency
Build senior citizen and workforce housing in Fullerton
Protect the character of our neighborhoods and our quality of life
