VICTOR CABRAL
Councilmember/Attorney/Businessman
As Mayor and City Councilmember, I prioritized enhancing public
safety by hiring additional sheriff officers and creating a Rangers
program.
I continue to fight to preserve our beach community, and as
Mayor I helped restore 450,000 cubic yards of sand to our beaches.
I worked with the Downtown Business Association to bring in new
restaurants and shops. I fought to preserve outdoor dining facilities
on Del Mar and worked with the North Beach HOA to develop a
vibrant Miramar Food Court complex.
I worked to bring a pickleball complex to San Clemente, add a
soccer field, replace turf, and add playground equipment, and I am
focused on revitalizing the San Clemente Municipal Golf Course.
As a member of the Orange County Fire Authority, I helped station
a helicopter in San Clemente to ensure we can respond to fires
quickly, and I supported the goat grazing program to clear our
hillsides of flammable brush that endangers homes.
As Vicechair of the San Onofre Nuclear Generating Station CEP, I
worked with federal and state governments to identify suitable sites
outside California to move the stored nuclear fuel. And today I am
fighting to slow down the landfill expansion adversely impacting
Talega.
A native of California, I earned degrees from the University of
California and Harvard.
