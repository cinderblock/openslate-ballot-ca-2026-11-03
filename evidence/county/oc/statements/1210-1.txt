KELLY SEYARTO
REPUBLICAN
State Senator
AGE: 66
California has always been a place of opportunity, but today too
many families are struggling with the high cost of living, rising taxes,
and an increasingly difficult environment for businesses, both small
and large. The checks and balances that have been the hallmark
of our government have been eroded by one-party, supermajority
rule in the California Legislature.
Now, after years of record spending, Californians are still waiting
for the results they were promised. We need to improve housing
affordability, keep our communities safe, make vast improvements
in our infrastructure, and ensure that every student has the tools to
succeed after high school. We can build a more affordable, safer,
and more prosperous California by setting clear priorities, spending
taxpayer dollars responsibly, and demanding accountability from
our state government.
I am proud to have passed legislation supporting military families,
college students, seniors, crime victims, and homeowners. My
proposal to exempt military retirement and survivor benefits
from state income tax was included in the state budget. I
have also authored laws that strengthen public safety and
consumer protections, improve government transparency, and
protect homeowners from property fraud.
I bring 35 years of service as a firefighter, 13 years as a local
elected official, and the perspective of someone who understands
the challenges facing working families. My wife is a small business
owner, and together we raised our family right here in this
community.
I would be honored to once again earn your vote.
