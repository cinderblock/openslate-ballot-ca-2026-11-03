DIANA MARKS
Incumbent
It has been an honor to serve on the Huntington Beach City School
Board for the past eight years. As a 51-year resident of Huntington
Beach I have dedicated my life to HBCSD, as a teacher for 39 years,
and a proud parent of three children who have attended HB City
Schools. I believe in my core that the needs of students must be
prioritized above any other agenda. As a teacher, I served as a
teacher of the year, PTA liaison, coach, and athletic director. I also
served as part of the team that earned the California Distinguished
School and National Blue-Ribbon School awards.
As a member of the board, I have worked productively with our
governance team. We have worked to improve communication
with all stakeholders, transparency and accountability in budget
decisions, finishing our modernization project and building a new
Sowers. Since we are now community funded, we have been able
to lower class sizes and offer competitive salaries in order to
keep the best teachers and staff. We have instituted science-based
teaching strategies and have seen an increase in our students'
achievement. Some of the awards our schools have received in the
past four years include; California Distinguished Schools, California
School to Watch, Gold PBIS Recognition, and Scholar Designation
Performance.
In the next four years we will have facilities projects to finish such
as bus storage along with maintenance and operations. We will be
starting our next five-year strategic plan cycle that will set the goals
that will further the advancement of our students. I ask for your
continued support in order to finish these projects and to provide
the best learning environment for our students. I appreciate your
faith and support in the past and hope you will vote for me again.
