SCOTTY HONG
AGE: 34
City of Irvine Sustainability Commissioner
As our district elects its own Councilmember for the first time, I ask
for your vote. I'm running a grassroots campaign to ensure that
our district — which includes UCI and the Jamboree/MacArthur
Corridor — has a strong advocate at Irvine City Hall.
I earned my undergraduate degree from Harvard University and a
Master of Public Administration from Cornell University. I currently
serve as an Irvine Sustainability Commissioner and as Chief Policy
Advisor to the Mayor, helping to shape our City's policies on public
safety, transportation, sustainability, housing, and quality of life.
As your Councilmember, I will:
Expand the free Irvine Connect shuttle bus system throughout
our district, connecting UCI and our residential communities.
Reduce traffic and improve lighting, sidewalks, and walking trails.
Increase neighborhood policing and strengthen fire and
emergency services.
Support affordable housing for working families, professionals,
and students.
Lower electricity costs by withdrawing from the Orange County
Power Authority, which charges Irvine residents the county's
highest rates and relies primarily on fossil fuels. Instead,
I'll expand rooftop solar and battery storage programs; plant
another 100,000 trees; and speed the transition to greener
transportation.
With your support, we can build an even brighter future for our
district and all Irvine residents!
Thank you,
Scotty Hong
