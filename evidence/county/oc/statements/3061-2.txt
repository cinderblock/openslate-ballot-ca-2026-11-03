KARIMA FELDHUS
AGE: 62
Executive Dean
It is an honor to seek your vote for the South Orange County
Community College District Board of Trustees.
For more than 30 years, I have dedicated my career to public higher
education. I have had the privilege of serving as a dean at both
Irvine Valley College and Saddleback College, working alongside
faculty, staff, administrators, and community partners to expand
educational opportunities and strengthen our colleges.
As Executive Dean of Extended Learning at Saddleback College,
I oversee programs that provide lifelong learning, workforce
training, adult education, and community education to thousands
of students each year. I have managed multimillion-dollar budgets,
secured grant funding, and developed partnerships that prepare
students for meaningful careers while meeting local workforce
needs.
As your trustee, I will promote academic excellence, student
success, fiscal responsibility, and accountability. I believe public
service means listening, making thoughtful decisions, respecting
diverse perspectives, and ensuring responsible stewardship of
taxpayer dollars. My commitment is to preserve the district’s
tradition of excellence while expanding opportunities that benefit
students, employees, employers, and our community.
I bring experience, integrity, and a collaborative approach to
governance. I would be honored to earn your trust and your vote.
