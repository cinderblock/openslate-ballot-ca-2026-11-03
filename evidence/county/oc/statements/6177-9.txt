LEENA RAY
Family Caregiver
For more than 22 years, I have proudly called Aliso Viejo home. As
a longtime resident, parent, ratepayer, and business professional,
I have a personal investment in our community and the essential
services we rely on every day.
Water is more than a utility; it is essential to our families, our
health, and our future. Residents deserve clear information, timely
communication, responsible stewardship, and accountability.
I bring more than 20 years of experience in corporate marketing,
digital strategy, project management, and business consulting. My
career has required me to analyze data, manage budgets, measure
results, solve complex problems, and adapt thoughtfully to change.
Today, my greatest responsibility is serving as a full-time family
caregiver, which has deepened my commitment to protecting the
health and future of our community.
If elected, I will advocate for transparency, fiscal responsibility,
and meaningful community engagement. I will ask thoughtful
questions, evaluate the facts, and work to ensure residents
understand water quality, infrastructure investments, and how
ratepayer dollars are spent.
It would be an honor to earn your trust and serve the community my
family and I have proudly called home for more than two decades.
