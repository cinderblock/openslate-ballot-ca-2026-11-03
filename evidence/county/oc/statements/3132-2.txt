RON SCOLESDANG
Businessowner/Youth Coach
As a father, youth coach, Irvine Public Works & Sustainability
Commissioner, business owner, and nonprofit leader, I have
dedicated more than 20 years to serving young people and our
community.
I am running for the Irvine Unified School District Board because
every student deserves a safe learning environment, academic
excellence, and opportunities to succeed in school and in life. I will
advocate for strong core academics, greater parent engagement,
responsible fiscal stewardship, and transparent decision-making.
I believe schools should prepare students not only for college, but
also for careers and life by supporting financial literacy, career
exploration, leadership, and practical life skills. Every taxpayer
dollar should be spent wisely, prioritizing classrooms, teachers, and
student success.
I will work collaboratively with parents, educators, and the
community to preserve IUSD's tradition of excellence while
ensuring every decision puts students first.
Together, we can continue building schools that prepare the
next generation with the knowledge, character, and confidence to
succeed.
