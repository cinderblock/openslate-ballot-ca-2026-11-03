MIKE MARKUS
Water Resource Engineer
Orange County’s imported water supply is growing more expensive
and less dependable. Hundreds of millions of dollars are being
wasted on projects supported by the MWDOC Board that are
destined to fail. I will stop wasteful spending and bring practical
solutions to help solve our water issues.
I dedicated 35 years to serving the Orange County Water District,
leading the organization as its General Manager for 16 years.
During that time, I built the world's largest water reuse project,
the Groundwater Replenishment System, and developed the first
program in the nation to remove PFAS from groundwater, saving
over 200 Orange County water wells from closure. These projects
provide long-term reliable water supplies while protecting human
health.
I am an Engineer, not a politician. I have built my career in sound
science and fiscal accountability.
I am running because I have real experience building and managing
large infrastructure projects. I know what water projects should
cost, which questions to ask and understand when the numbers
don’t add up. I will hold every MWDOC decision to a high standard
and make sure ratepayers get what they pay for.
I respectfully ask for your vote. I would be honored to serve you.
