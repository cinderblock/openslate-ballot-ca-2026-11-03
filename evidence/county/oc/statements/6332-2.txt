BRIAN CARVER
Firefighter/Teacher
I am proud to have grown up in Buena Park, where I learned the
values of service, hard work, and community. Buena Park has always
been home, and I believe our libraries play an essential role in
bringing people together, inspiring lifelong learning, and creating
opportunities for residents. As a father, I understand the importance
of welcoming spaces where children can discover a love of reading
and families can learn, grow, and connect.
My career has been dedicated to public service. As a firefighter,
I have spent my life protecting others, making decisions under
pressure, and putting the needs of our community first. As an
educator, I have helped students succeed by embracing California's
educational standards and preparing them for opportunities. I
have also contributed to the development of California's Career
Technical Education standards, helping ensure students are
equipped with knowledge and skills to thrive.
These experiences have taught me that strong communities are
built through education, access to information, and public service.
As your Library Trustee, I will work to support programs, promote
literacy and lifelong learning, strengthen community partnerships,
and ensure our library remains a welcoming resource for residents
of all ages.
I respectfully ask for your vote.
