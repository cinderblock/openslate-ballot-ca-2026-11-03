STEVE CAMP
AGE: 68
Architect/Business Owner
For more than 31 years, Steve Camp and his family have called San
Clemente home. A lifelong surfer, Steve and his wife of 42 years
designed and built their home here and raised their three children
in this community.
After more than five years of service on the City’s Planning
Commission and Design Review Subcommittee, Steve is running for
City Council to give back to the community that has given his family
so much.
As a Planning Commissioner and Design Review Chair, Steve has
worked to protect San Clemente’s neighborhood character while
navigating state housing mandates and reviewing major hotel,
mixed-use, and community projects.
A local architect for over 36 years, Steve founded a firm employing
ten professionals. His experience leading a business through
multiple recessions, managing complex projects, and working with
police, fire, and public safety agencies has prepared him to make
sound, fiscally responsible decisions.
On the City Council, Steve will bring that same practical, thoughtful
approach to protecting our neighborhoods, strengthening public
safety, maintaining fiscal responsibility, and preserving the
character and quality of life that make San Clemente special.
Steve has the experience and commitment to help protect the
community we all love.
Steve Camp, AIA
