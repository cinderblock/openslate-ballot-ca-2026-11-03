MAKSIM V EGOROV
Software Engineer/Businessowner
I am blessed and proud to call Irvine home. My passion for
protecting Irvine’s open space, serving every resident, and planning
responsibly for the future inspired me to run for Mayor.
As a software engineer, I have built my career solving problems,
improving quality, and delivering results. I will bring a practical,
transparent, and accountable approach to City Hall.
I serve on my neighborhood HOA Board and have volunteered on
the School Site Council and English Learner Advisory Committee
(ELAC) at my children’s Irvine school. These experiences have
strengthened my belief that the best decisions come from listening
and working together.
As Mayor, I will work collaboratively with my fellow Council
members to strengthen fiscal responsibility, provide strong budget
oversight, restore long-term budget stability, protect our open
space, support public safety, encourage innovation, and give
residents the opportunity to vote on the future of our open space.
As a husband and father, I want Irvine to remain one of the safest
and best places to live, work, and raise a family by protecting
our quality of life, planning responsibly, and improving mobility
throughout our city.
I will lead with integrity and be a Mayor for all of Irvine.
