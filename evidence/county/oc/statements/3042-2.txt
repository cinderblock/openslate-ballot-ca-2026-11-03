JOE KALMICK
City of Seal Beach City Councilmember
As I complete my 8 years of service as a Councilmember for the
City of Seal Beach, and as a board member for various agencies
in Orange County, I still have the desire to continue in public
service. I believe that the Coast Colleges Board of Trustees would
be a great fit for the skills that I have developed. Working with
my Council colleagues, we have produced a multiyear balanced
budget, and our financial position remains strong. And as Vice
Chair of the Orange County Fire Authority, I have had experience
managing a $600M budget. In addition, working closely with Staff
and the various labor groups, negotiations have yielded satisfactory
results for all concerned. Therefore, I am confident that I can be a
positive influence on the Board of Trustees in maintaining positive
communications between faculty, staff and the Chancellor’s Office.
I believe that education is the cornerstone of a strong society.
The Community College system provides a quality education for
virtually everyone. The Coast Community Colleges are a shining
example of the benefits that they provide.
I have a B.A. in Psychology from UCLA and owned a small business
in Seal Beach for 40 years. I am now retired.
