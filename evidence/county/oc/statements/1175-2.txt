DAVID PAN
REPUBLICAN
Professor
I am running for Congress to provide freedom and opportunity to
all Americans.
I have a plan to improve affordability for working families, eliminate
government fraud and waste, and reduce our national debt. We
can accomplish all of these goals by phasing out our welfare and
entitlement programs. The money saved, over $4 trillion per year,
can pay every American $16,000 per year starting at age 21 and still
reduce our budget deficit by over $1 trillion per year. Rather than
have our money go into a massive government bureaucracy, we can
invest this money in ourselves, giving all Americans the freedom to
plan their own futures.
As an educator, I support the rights of parents and their choice of
the best schools for their children, with success being measured in
determination and excellence rather than accident of birth.
I support investing in more police and firefighters, enforcing our
laws, and reforming our immigration system.
A son of immigrants from Taiwan, I was born in Chicago, received
my B.A. from Stanford and my Ph.D. from Columbia. Having taught
at UC Irvine since 2006, I also served on the U.S. State Department’s
Commission on Unalienable Rights. I have been endorsed by the
Orange County Congress of Republicans, the Republican Party of
Orange County, the California Republican Party, and dozens of
elected officials. I would be honored to receive your vote and work
toward a better future for our district and for all Americans.
Please visit my website at davidpanforcongress.com.
