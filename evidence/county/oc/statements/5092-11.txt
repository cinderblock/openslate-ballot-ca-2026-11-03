TARYN TADDEO PALUMBO
AGE: 41
Executive Director
I believe the future of Huntington Beach matters now more than
ever.
Raised in this community, I’m a parent, attorney, and commonsense
problem solver. As the Executive Director of a nonprofit
organization, I have spent my career bringing people together
to get things done, helping connect people and businesses to
secure the funding they need in order to improve lives and make a
difference for families across Orange County.
I know I’m not alone in saying enough is enough. As your City
Councilmember, I will work tirelessly to focus on the everyday
issues that matter most:
Protect our libraries and public spaces,
Invest in infrastructure upgrades and street repairs,
End the waste of taxpayer dollars in endless lawsuits,
Restore Huntington Beach’s reputation.
I believe our future is worth fighting for, and I want my children
to experience the same pride in our city that I did growing up. I
would be honored to earn your vote to bring the change we need
in Huntington Beach.
