TOM GRABLE
AGE: 64
Planning Commissioner/Businessowner
For 27 years, Lake Forest has been the place my family proudly calls
home. As a current Lake Forest Planning Commissioner, business
leader and community volunteer, I want to help keep Lake Forest
the kind of city we're proud to pass on to the next generation.
Throughout my 40-year career, I've brought people together to
solve difficult problems, managed large budgets responsibly, and
made thoughtful decisions that delivered successful results.
As your City Councilmember, I'll lead to keep our neighborhoods
safe, repair and maintain our roads and parks, support our local
businesses so they can thrive, and make sure our city spends wisely,
lives within its means, and saves for the future.
I'll listen to residents, treat everyone with respect, and make
decisions based on what's best for Lake Forest—not politics or
special interests.
My goal is simple: protect what makes our city special while
planning carefully for the future. I respectfully ask for your vote and
the opportunity to serve our community with integrity, experience
and common-sense leadership.
Tom Grable
