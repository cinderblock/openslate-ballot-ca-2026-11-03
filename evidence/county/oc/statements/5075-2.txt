JOZEF MALDONADO
AGE: 30
Chemist
Growing up in a working-class family during the 2008 financial
collapse, I saw firsthand how quickly a family can go from
comfortable to crisis. After working two jobs while earning
my degree in Chemistry from UC Davis, I joined the Fullerton
community knowing the importance of hard work and cooperation.
I ask for your vote as a neighbor with experience making the city
work for all of us.
Over the past few years, I’ve volunteered helping families access
food and school supplies and served on the Parks and Recreation
Commission. That experience has taught me that good governance
involves listening to residents, solving problems, and delivering
results.
Once elected, I promise to:
Protect public safety funding to ensure strong response times.
Expand funding for our Senior Mobility Program and increase
access to community centers and services.
Invest in small business development and social spaces throughout
downtown to streamline permitting and increase foot traffic for
businesses along Harbor.
I’m honored to be endorsed by:
Councilmember Ahmad Zahra
Councilmember Shana Charles
Fullerton School Board Trustee Vanesa Estrella
