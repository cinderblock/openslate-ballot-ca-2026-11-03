COTTIE PETRIE-NORRIS
DEMOCRATIC
California State Assemblymember
AGE: 50
Dear Neighbor,
It is my honor to represent Costa Mesa, Irvine, and Tustin in the State
Assembly. As your representative, I’ve done my best to listen to the
residents of our district and work with leaders of both parties to find
commonsense solutions to the problems facing California.
In the State Assembly, I’m fighting to:
Lower utility costs and ensure a stable power grid.
Encourage innovation and entrepreneurship to create jobs.
Build housing and lower barriers to home ownership.
Keep neighborhoods safe and ensure fast 911 response times.
Reduce homelessness with a focus on mental health.
Responsibly manage our state budget and prevent tax increases.
My leadership has been recognized with awards from a number of
organizations, including CalAsian Chamber of Commerce, Orange
County United Way, Orange County Taxpayers Association, Planned
Parenthood Affiliates of California, and Sierra Club California.
Prior to my election to the State Assembly, I had a 20-year career in
finance and technology. I helped build businesses and lead teams
at Fortune 500 corporations, small companies, and start-ups. I’m a
graduate of Yale University, where I double majored in Economics
and English. My husband, Colin, and I are the proud parents of two
sons and a rescue dog.
My campaign has been endorsed by local firefighters, teachers,
police officers, nurses, and small business owners.
If you have any questions, or ideas you would like to share, please
don’t hesitate to contact me at Info@VoteCottie.com. I would be
honored to earn your vote.
Cottie Petrie-Norris
