ALEX ROUNAGHI
Laguna Beach City Councilmember
As a lifelong resident, I'm honored to serve the town three
generations of my family call home. Laguna Beach is the product of
visionaries who planned boldly for the future.
Public safety is our highest priority. As Mayor during the Rancho
Fire, I witnessed our City at its best: prepared, coordinated, and
resilient. Our investments in wildfire preparedness paid off. We
fully funded our police; Laguna Beach has the lowest violent crime
among Orange County beach cities.
Together, we’ve embraced long-term stewardship of our
infrastructure and finances. Last year, I negotiated a historic
agreement transitioning Laguna Beach from 100% imported water
toward a majority-local supply. We cut permit times by nearly 30%
and launched efficiency initiatives resulting in over $1.5 million in
cuts.
The next four years will shape our next century. If re-elected, I will
continue to lead the effort to retain an Emergency Room; expand
our state-of-the-art wildfire defenses; implement a water resources
strategy to strengthen resiliency and protect our ocean from sewer
spills; and enhance our artistic culture with year-round live music
at the Irvine Bowl.
There is no place like Laguna Beach. We inherited something
extraordinary; our job is to leave it even stronger.
I respectfully ask for your vote.
