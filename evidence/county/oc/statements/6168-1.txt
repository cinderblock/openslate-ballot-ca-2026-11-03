PEER A. SWAN
Director, Irvine Ranch Water District, Area 5
As a Director of the Irvine Ranch Water District for 46 years,
I have played a large role in making the District a nationally
recognized leader. We pioneered the use of recycled water,
promoted conservation, funded a program to replace worn-out
plant and equipment, fully funded retirement, and built a large
emergency groundwater storage facility.
IRWD has gone from 95% dependency on expensive imported water
to where more than 85% of our water is local, more reliable and
less costly.
We have achieved this high level of service and reliability while
maintaining rates that are by far the lowest in the County.
I spearheaded the creation of the San Joaquin Wildlife Sanctuary
where urban runoff is naturally treated, while providing needed
habitat and accessible public open space.
As a Founding Director and Chairman for four years of the National
Water Research Institute we funded research to improve water
quality.
You have my continued commitment to keep the District an
innovative leader dedicated to quality service that is low priced,
environmentally sensitive, and consistent with the high standards
of the community we live in.
