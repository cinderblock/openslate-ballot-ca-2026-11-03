PATRICK PHAT BUI
Businessowner/Nonprofit Chairman
Garden Grove has been my family’s home for more than 40 years.
As your City Councilmember from 2014 to 2022, I worked every day
to protect our neighborhoods, support local businesses, and serve
our community with integrity. I am asking for your vote to continue
that commitment.
As a business owner, CEO, former Planning Commissioner, and
current Orange County Housing and Community Development
Commissioner, I have the experience to deliver results from day
one.
My top priority is public safety. I will work to fully staff our Police
Department, support our officers, enforce city laws to remove
illegal homeless encampments from our parks, neighborhoods, and
business districts, and hold homeless programs accountable for
measurable results. I will support local businesses by reducing
unnecessary regulations, oppose new taxes and fee increases,
modernize City Hall to improve efficiency and customer service
and ensure taxpayer dollars are spent wisely, responsibly, and
transparently.
As a refugee who came to America with nothing, I have never
forgotten the opportunities Garden Grove gave my family. Public
service is my way of giving back to the community that has given
us so much.
I'm endorsed by Orange County Supervisor Janet Nguyen and
Garden Grove Mayor Stephanie Klopfenstein, and respectfully ask
for your vote.
