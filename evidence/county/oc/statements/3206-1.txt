HECTOR BUSTOS
Governing Board Member, Santa Ana Unified School
District, Trustee Area 5
As a proud product of the Santa Ana Unified School District, serving
on the School Board has been the honor of my life. When I was
elected four years ago, I was a recent college graduate who ran for
office because I believed our schools deserved student-centered
leadership.
As School Board President, we delivered the district's strongest
academic gains in over a decade, protected essential student
services despite severe underfunding, strengthened college and
career programs that prepare our students for the future, and
partnered with UC Irvine to create a model elementary school that
brings the latest research into the classroom.
I'm proud to be the candidate in this race endorsed by the Santa
Ana Educators Association and the California School Employees
Association. These associations represent thousands of employees,
including our teachers, counselors, cafeteria workers, special
education professionals, and campus safety officers. Their support
reflects the trust we've built and my commitment to the workers
who serve our students daily.
Parents have always played a critical role in the decisions I make.
Santa Ana families support my re-election because we've made
responsible use of their taxpayer dollars by creating safer schools,
expanding mental health support for students, and modernizing
aging school facilities.
With your support, we’ll continue building the high-performing
schools our students deserve.
