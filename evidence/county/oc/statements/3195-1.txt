KRIS SPOWART
Community Volunteer
I believe strong public schools change lives. They prepare students
for the future, strengthen our community, and are made possible
by outstanding teachers, engaged families, and students who are
supported to reach their full potential.
I grew up attending Saddleback Valley USD schools, and my
husband and I chose to raise our children here. For the past 16
years, I have served our district as a parent volunteer, PTA leader,
Community Advisory Committee Chair, and Special Education
Advocacy Chair, working alongside teachers, administrators, staff,
families, and students to support student success.
As the parent of a son who received special education services
from preschool through graduation, I was once told he might
never earn a high school diploma. This year, he graduated. That
journey reinforced my belief that when students are surrounded
by dedicated educators, high expectations, and meaningful
collaboration between schools and families, they can achieve more
than anyone imagined.
I am running for Saddleback Valley Unified School District to build
on our district’s strong foundation and ensure our schools continue
to prepare future generations.
As your trustee, I will listen, ask thoughtful and sometimes difficult
questions, bring people together, and keep students at the center
of every decision.
