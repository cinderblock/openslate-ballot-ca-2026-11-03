JOHN BLACK
Military Officer/Educator
I, John Black, serving years as a Lieutenant Colonel, U.S. Marine
Corps, believe South Orange County's community colleges should
open doors to good careers, four-year universities, skilled trades,
military, public service, and entrepreneurship. I am running for
the Board of Trustees to help students succeed while protecting
taxpayer dollars.
I have spent my career as an educator, teaching, coaching, and
mentoring students, including Marines, and serving as adjunct
faculty, a distinguished fellow at MIT, and guest lecturer at
universities. Learning changes lives; I have spent decades helping
people learn, lead, and grow.
I have led large organizations and managed the public funds
that support them. As Senior Emergency Management Program
Coordinator for the Orange County Health Care Agency, I
strengthened disaster preparedness, improved accountability for
funded programs, and coordinated planning serving millions of
residents. I understand budgets, oversight, and stewardship of
public resources.
I co-lead The JASSD Group and support veterans, first responders,
and their families through Veterans FORWARD. My wife, Dr. Aime
Black, our family, and I live in San Clemente.
As a Trustee, I will focus on student success, academic excellence,
workforce partnerships, safe campuses, and transparent
governance.
I would be honored to serve and respectfully ask for your vote.
