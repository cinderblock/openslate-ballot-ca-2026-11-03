SHARI RHODES
Lake Forest Business Owner
I love Lake Forest, which I made my home 16 years ago. I want to
make Lake Forest an even better place to live, so my children will
be able to buy their own homes to raise their families here.
During the challenging times of 2020, I launched two businesses in
Lake Forest, which are thriving today.
I’m not a politician, and I have never run for elected office. I’m a
concerned citizen and business owner passionate about giving back
to my community, as I did when I served as the PTA President at my
children’s schools and the women’s ministries administrator at our
church.
If you elect me to represent our community on the Lake Forest City
Council, I will fight to:
Provide our firefighters and Sheriff’s deputies with what they need
to keep Lake Forest safe
Develop an anonymous tipline to report gang activity
Ensure Lake Forest maintains a responsible, balanced budget
Enhance our Lake Forest parks and community activities
Grow our economy and lower the cost of living to help young
families and senior citizens afford their own homes in Lake Forest
I am honored to be endorsed by:
Lake Forest Mayor Robert Pequeno
Lake Forest Councilmember Scott Voigts
Former Lake Forest Mayor Dwight Robinson
I respectfully ask for your vote.
