ANDREW NGUYEN
Director, Midway City Sanitary District
I am honored to have served the residents of the Midway City
Sanitary District and remain committed to providing reliable sewer
and sanitation services, protecting our environment, and serving
our community.
During my service, I have supported infrastructure improvements,
cybersecurity initiatives, grant opportunities, long-term planning,
and expanded communication with residents through radio, social
media, community events, and educational outreach. I have also
supported programs that promote recycling, waste reduction, and
environmental awareness.
My commitment to public service includes serving as
Vice President of the Westminster School District, Planning
Commissioner and Traffic Commissioner for the City of
Westminster, and a classified employee with the Garden Grove
Unified School District. These experiences have provided me with
valuable knowledge of local government, education, and the needs
of our community.
Moving forward, I will continue to focus on maintaining
and improving our infrastructure, protecting our environment,
strengthening community outreach, and ensuring clear
communication with residents. I am proud of the progress we have
made and grateful for the opportunity to continue serving the
community.
