BRENDON MOELLER
Small Businessman
I’m a third-generation resident of Orange. I was a Captain in
the United States Army, serving our country on three combat
deployments in the Middle East and East Africa. I graduated from
Orange Unified School District schools, the United States Military
Academy at West Point, and the University of Southern California.
Today, I continue my service here in my hometown of Orange
as Chair of the Santiago Creek Commission and a Member of
the Orange County Transportation Authority Citizens Advisory
Committee.
If you elect me to represent our community on the Orange City
Council, I will:
Strengthen funding for Orange firefighters and police
Grow Orange’s economy by attracting new businesses and
retaining existing businesses
Ensuring complete accountability with our taxpayer dollars
Improve efficiency and reduce waste
Protect the character of our neighborhoods and Orange's quality
of life
Preserve our open space and local waterways
I’m not a politician. Orange and the Army gave me my values and
work ethic, which I’ll bring to the City Council.
I have earned the endorsements of our City of Orange leaders for
City Council:
Orange City Firefighters
Mayor Dan Slater
Former Mayor Carolyn Cavecche
Our retiring District 6 Councilmember John Gyllenhammer
Majority of the Orange City Council
I would be honored to have your vote. moellerfororange.com
