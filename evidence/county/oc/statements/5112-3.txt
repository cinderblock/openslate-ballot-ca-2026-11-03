MARK ORGILL
City Council Member, Laguna Beach
For generations, the people of Laguna Beach have shaped
this extraordinary community through thoughtful decisions that
protected its coast, unique neighborhoods, vibrant arts community,
and natural beauty. Those choices created the Laguna Beach we
enjoy today.
Working collaboratively with my City Council colleagues, we’ve
strengthened wildfire preparedness, improved long-term water
reliability, maintained one of California’s safest communities, and
made City government more efficient and fiscally responsible.
My focus has always been Laguna Beach. I’ve worked to build on our
community’s tradition of stewardship by bringing people together
around a simple belief: our culture, heritage, and environment
are interconnected, and our best decisions strengthen all three.
That approach has helped support our arts community, protect
our natural environment, strengthen our neighborhoods, and solve
complex challenges with civility and respect.
Serving Laguna Beach has been one of the greatest honors of
my life. I respectfully ask for your support so together we can
continue protecting what makes Laguna Beach extraordinary while
thoughtfully preparing for the opportunities and challenges of the
next generation.
