JUSTIN AGUIRRE
Parent
It’s time to bring common-sense leadership back!
I’m Justin Aguirre, a lifelong Placentia resident, PYLUSD graduate,
and father of three children currently attending our district schools.
For years, I have been actively involved in PYLUSD, advocating for
students, parents, and our community. I’m running because I believe
our schools must return their focus to what matters most: academic
excellence, parental involvement, school safety, and preparing
every student for future success.
I will fight to protect parental rights, protect girls’ sports
and spaces, keep inappropriate political ideology out of our
classrooms, and ensure parents remain the primary decision-
makers in their children’s education. I will demand transparency
and fiscal responsibility, prioritize classroom spending, and oppose
unnecessary debt and bonds that place additional burdens on
taxpayers.
I will work to expand partnerships with our local colleges. However,
college is not the only path to success. We must also expand
career technical education programs, including skilled trades,
apprenticeships, and other pathways that prepare students to meet
workforce demands.
When elected, I’ll be the trustee with children currently attending
PYLUSD schools. I’m personally invested in the future of our district
and respectfully ask for your vote.
