DENNIS COLE
AGE: 55
Governing Board Member, Fountain Valley School
District
As an educator for 31 years and current President of the Fountain
Valley School Board, I work to serve students and families. I
have been a teacher, school principal, Director of Education, and
currently serve as Chief of Staff for the Orange County Department
of Education. Utilizing my area of expertise, I have helped to build
solid, thriving school communities.
During my four years as a Governing Board Member, I have placed
a major emphasis on:
Safe schools and healthy, happy kids - installed perimeter fencing
to further enhance our school safety
High academic standards propelling our students to rank first in
reading, math, and science in Orange County
Expanding STEM, the arts, and music opportunities for students
Fiscal responsibility, ensuring yearly that FVSD has a positive
financial certification with stable and sustainable budgets
I continue to serve our community on the Board of Directors
for Fountain Valley Schools Foundation, Orange County Sheriff’s
Advisory Council, Junior Achievement Orange County, and
Coastline College Foundation.
My wife and I have raised five children in Fountain Valley and have
been foster parents for 11 children. We work together, as a family,
to help our city and our schools be a great place for everyone.
I look forward to your vote.
