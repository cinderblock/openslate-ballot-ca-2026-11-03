LOAN NGUYEN
AGE: 60
Financial Consultant
My husband and I have called Westminster home for 35 years,
raising our three sons here through local public schools: a CPA,
an Army Captain and dentist, and a violinist. As a mother, small
business owner, and community advocate, I will bring people
together to restore civility, solve problems, and deliver results.
They say gray hair comes with experience, and after a 40-year
financial career, including 28 years as a Financial Controller
managing diverse teams and multi-million-dollar budgets, I will put
that experience to work for you. As a Chapman University Executive
MBA graduate who earned a CPA license, I will strengthen fiscal
accountability at City Hall. I regularly attend City Council meetings
to monitor city spending and advocate for residents.
My "Make Westminster Shine" initiative focuses on:
Ensuring fiscal responsibility by scrutinizing city spending and
prioritizing neighborhood needs.
Supporting seniors and mobile home residents through housing
stability and rent stabilization.
Expanding youth programs and safe after-school care for local
families.
Driving economic growth and addressing homelessness
responsibly.
Westminster deserves prepared, accountable, responsive
leadership that listens to residents and delivers results. Learn more
at voteloannguyen.com. I respectfully ask for your vote for City
Council, District 4.
