KATIE MCEWEN
Governing Board Member, Irvine Unified School District,
Trustee Area 2
As your Irvine Unified School District Board Member, I am proud
to have spent the past four years working to keep IUSD among
California’s highest-performing school districts while supporting
the success and well-being of every student.
During my service, including a year as Board President, I helped lead
the search and hiring of our Superintendent, strengthened student
mental health supports, expanded career and technical education
opportunities, and advocated for fair school funding.
My priorities:
Fully fund Irvine schools;
Support student academic excellence and mental health
services;
Attract and retain outstanding teachers, administrators and
classified employees; and
Maintain safe and supportive schools.
As a 22-year Irvine resident, Stanford graduate, credentialed
teacher, substitute teacher, PTA leader, and parent of three Irvine
public school graduates, I bring valuable firsthand experience to
the Board. As the parent of a child who received special education
services in IUSD, I understand the importance of ensuring every
student has the support needed to succeed.
I am proud to be endorsed by current and former IUSD Trustees:
Lauren Brooks
Cyril Yu
Jeff Kim
Connie Stone
Sharon Wallin (Ret.)
Paul Bakota (Ret.)
and by the Irvine Teachers Association (ITA)
I respectfully ask for your vote.
