MICHELLE MURPHY
AGE: 54
Newport-Mesa Unified School District Trustee, Area 2
As your elected Trustee and current Vice President of the Board, I
have worked to make our public schools the first choice for local
families. Parent of an 11th and 8th grader who have been attending
Newport-Mesa schools, I see firsthand the academic, social, and
emotional challenges families are navigating today. Students need
thoughtful leaders who are willing to tackle complex problems and
ensure they are educated and cared for in safe and welcoming
spaces.
Our family has called College Park home for more than 20 years,
volunteering with CMLL, flag football and Costa Mesa High School
drama and choir programs. A proud product of public schools
myself, I earned a master's degree in social work from USC and lead
public affairs at United Way, where I advocate every day to improve
the lives of Orange County residents. I have served our community
in the following roles:
Member, OC Fair and Event Center Board of Directors
Vice President, Costa Mesa High School Foundation
Commissioner, Costa Mesa Parks and Community Services
President, Kaiser-Woodland Parent Faculty Organization
At Newport-Mesa, I have focused on improving educational
outcomes for all students while making progress in district-
wide community engagement, infrastructure, safety, and fiscal
responsibility. My accomplishments include:
Educational Excellence: Ensured the math curriculum is aligned
through K - 12th, creating a stronger pathway for college and
career readiness. Implemented a new reading curriculum that has
already shown positive results, increasing STAR Reading growth by
more than 10 points in some schools.
Facilities: Voted for the first-ever Facilities Master Plan, making
infrastructure decisions more proactive and helping avoid wasteful
spending. Advocated for expanding the construction of additional
shade structures and new playground equipment across our
campuses.
Safety: Supported integrated security systems on all campuses and
voted for a strong cell phone policy. Backed the county's first ban
on e-bikes and e-motos for K - 8 students along with a requirement
that students complete an E-bike/bicycle safety training program.
Fiscal Stewardship: Ensured NMUSD remains fiscally strong with a
Moody's Aa1 bond rating. Our reserves exceed the state required
minimum, and our budget reflects our values by putting our
students first without compromising educational quality.
I am proud to be endorsed by a bipartisan coalition of
parents, teachers and community leaders. My record reflects my
commitment to putting students and families first while considering
all perspectives before making decisions.
Respectfully, I ask for your vote.
