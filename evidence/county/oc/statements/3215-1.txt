JONATHAN ABELOVE
Governing Board Member, Tustin Unified School District,
Trustee Area 5
As a longtime Tustin Unified Trustee, I've helped guide our students
to some of the strongest results of any school district in California:
a 97% graduation rate, faster learning gains than the state average,
and reading and math scores among the top 20% in the nation.
I'm leading our expanding workforce and career pathways so every
graduate leaves ready for what's next.
I also worked with my colleagues to save taxpayers over $48 million
through smart bond restructuring, and championed districtwide
modernization, ensuring Tustin Unified schools benefit from
upgraded classrooms, new facilities, and enhanced safety and
security features.
I believe strong schools are built through partnership, which is why
my service to Tustin doesn't stop at the dais. I serve as President of
Tustin Scholarships and Executive Vice President of the Boys & Girls
Clubs of Tustin, working alongside educators, staff, and families
every day.
It's also why I'm proud to carry the sole endorsement of the
Tustin Educators Association, representing our classroom teachers,
along with support from local leaders like Congressman Dave Min,
Assemblymember Cottie Petrie-Norris, former Tustin Mayor Letitia
Clark, and dozens more.
I'm running for re-election to keep this momentum going for every
Tustin Unified student, and I look forward to earning your vote on
or before November 3, 2026.
