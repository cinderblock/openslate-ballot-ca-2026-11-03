LAURA BASSETT
REPUBLICAN
Small Business Owner
I'm Laura Bassett, a lifelong Oceanside resident running to make
California affordable for working families.
This coastal community is home. My husband Taylor and I have been
married for 33 years and raised our six children here, staying deeply
involved in local theater, service, and the life of our district.
After earning my accounting degree from the University of
Southern California, I built a career grounded in trust and
responsibility, working as a licensed real estate professional,
a licensed professional fiduciary, and the owner of two local
small businesses. I serve as a San Diego County Civil Service
Commissioner, a Chamber of Commerce Ambassador, and a
Rotarian.
I'm running because the California that families built their lives on
has become a California that families can no longer afford. Gas,
groceries, rent, insurance, electricity — every cost keeps climbing
while Sacramento argues about everything except the issues sitting
on our kitchen tables.
In the State Senate, I'll fight to make life more affordable: lower
taxes and fees, smarter regulations that bring down the cost of
housing and insurance, and real accountability for how our money
is spent. I'll support law enforcement and safe neighborhoods,
protect our coastline for the next generation, and return decisions
to the communities that know themselves best.
This district doesn't need more partisanship. It needs balance,
and a senator focused on keeping California Safe, Strong, and
Sustainable.
I'd be honored to earn your vote.
