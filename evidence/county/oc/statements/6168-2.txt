JEFFREY HARLAN
Attorney/Planning Commissioner
After 45 years of the same, we need new leadership for Division 5.
I bring not only the experience and expertise, but also a fresh
perspective to guide the Irvine Ranch Water District through this
critical time.
For more than 20 years, I have been a dedicated Orange County
public servant and community volunteer. As the first City
Councilmember representing Eastside Costa Mesa, I helped create
and renovate neighborhood parks, deliver new bike lanes, upgrade
streets and alleys, and expand police and fire services. With my
leadership, our Council delivered balanced budgets every year.
As a land use attorney and Chair of the Planning Commission,
I have committed my 30-year career to working with governments
to improve communities and protect our environment. I understand
all aspects of responsible development, including preserving our
water supply, conserving natural resources and open space, and
investing in our infrastructure.
I graduated from the University of Pennsylvania,
and earned a law degree focusing on environmental law from
Vermont Law School.
As your Director, I will ensure our precious resources are protected
so you can enjoy a safe, reliable, and affordable water supply.
I appreciate your consideration and hope to earn your vote.
