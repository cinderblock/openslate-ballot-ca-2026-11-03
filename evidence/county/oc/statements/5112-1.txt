NICK ARONOFF
Small Business Owner
Laguna Beach is home. My family put down roots here in the late
1940s, and I am proud to be a fourth-generation local. I attended
Anneliese, El Morro, Thurston, and Laguna Beach High School, and
I grew up on our beaches. My career in business and operations,
managing teams and budgets worldwide, taught me practical,
results-focused problem solving. I believe every generation has a
responsibility to help steward its hometown, and I am ready to do
my part.
Laguna is at a crossroads. Too many residents feel decisions
are made without them. I have spent the past year at council
meetings, town halls and listening to neighbors. The message is
clear: residents want to be heard.
As your Council member, I will put residents first, with honest
communication and real public input. Protect Laguna's small-
town character, heritage, beaches, trees, and open space. Exercise
fiscal discipline, prioritizing public safety, infrastructure, and core
services over vanity projects and new taxes. Strengthen beach
management and enforcement to address over-tourism. Support
the businesses, artists, workers, and families who keep Laguna
thriving year-round.
We only get one chance to be stewards of Laguna Beach. From
Laguna, For Laguna.
