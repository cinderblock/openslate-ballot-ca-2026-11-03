NATALIE MEEKS
Anaheim City Council Member, District 6
My American dream came true in Anaheim Hills. As a single mother,
I worked hard and saved money to buy a home here to ensure my
daughter would grow up in a safe community with a wonderful
quality of life. She graduated from public schools in Anaheim Hills,
becoming a local teacher.
Four years ago, I was honored to be elected your Councilmember.
Since then, I’ve delivered results for Anaheim Hills:
12 additional firefighters stationed in Anaheim Hills
Increased the number of police officers to the most in City history
Reopened the Anaheim Police Department East Substation to the
public
New playgrounds at 2 Anaheim Hills parks
Prohibited the sale of kratom and nitrous oxide to our youth
Based on wildfire and evacuation concerns: defeated the
development proposal in Deer Canyon
Launched new penalties for those who allow illegal fireworks on
their property
Reduced the number of unsheltered homeless by 10% in 2 years
Established e-bike regulations
If you re-elect me to the Anaheim City Council, I’ll continue to fight
for you:
Wildfire and evacuation preparedness
Enhance parks, playgrounds, and sports fields
Improve East Anaheim Community Center, and add space for
seniors
Additional landscaping and pedestrian improvements on Santa
Ana Canyon Road
I respectfully ask for your vote for a second four years as your
Councilmember.
