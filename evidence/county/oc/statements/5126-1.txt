ANNIE MCCARY
Mayor City of Laguna Woods
It has been my great honor and privilege to serve on the City
Council, especially this year as Mayor. I have deepened my
understanding of our community through council meetings, and by
participating with you in community events, celebrating residents’
milestones, listening to concerns, and working together to address
the issues that matter most.
I am proud of the City’s accomplishments during the past four
years. We have completed our planning for a new fire station on
Moulton Parkway, and increased pedestrian safety at crosswalks,
with better curb cuts and lighting and audible crossing directions.
We are committed to public safety, with nearly half of our general
fund budget going to police services. We have mobilized county,
state and federal resources to respond to our coyote problem.
Our Council exercises fiscal prudence and responsibility. We
consistently have balanced budgets, clean audits, no debt, and
no unfunded pension liability. Our financial reporting has received
numerous awards.
I want to continue making the decisions that shape the future of
Laguna Woods, the welcoming, safe, and vibrant community we all
value. I welcome and appreciate your vote.
