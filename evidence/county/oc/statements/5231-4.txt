PERLA MENDOZA
CEO/Nonprofit Boardmember
As your neighbor, I understand why so many of us choose to
call District 1 home, from Old Town to Surfside. Our small-
town character, beautiful coastline, welcoming neighborhoods, and
locally owned businesses make our community special and deserve
protection for future generations.
I have spent years building trusted partnerships with local,
state, and federal agencies, law enforcement, schools, businesses,
nonprofits, and residents. I believe trust is earned by listening,
bringing people together, and following through.
I am running for Seal Beach City Council because local government
should be accessible, accountable, and responsive to the people
it serves. District 1 deserves a council member who is available,
answers the phone, shows up at community events, and welcomes
conversations about the issues residents care about most.
My priorities are protecting public safety, preserving our
neighborhoods and coastline, securing long-term beach sand
replenishment, revitalizing Main Street and preserving the small-
town character that makes District 1 so special.
I’ve been endorsed by Seal Beach Mayor Lisa Landau, Orange
County Supervisor Janet Nguyen, Orange County Sheriff Don
Barnes, and Assemblymember Diane Dixon.
I respectfully ask for your vote.
