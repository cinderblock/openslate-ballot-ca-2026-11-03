TIM SHAW
AGE: 49
Orange County Board of Education Trustee
My wife Shannon and I grew up in North Orange County, and we
are raising our family of six sons here. We want our quality of life
to be even better in the future for our kids and our grandchildren,
which is why I am running to be your Orange County Supervisor.
I understand the challenges faced by your family: higher grocery,
rent and fuel costs, on top of the high taxes we pay.
With six growing boys I understand your challenges. I have a
personal stake in keeping our communities safe, strengthening our
schools, creating better jobs, and making life affordable. We need
someone who has elected experience at the county level and knows
how to get things done quickly, not on the job training. I have the
county experience necessary to get the job done right.
Currently I serve as the President of the Orange County Board
of Education. I have approved schools and programs to enhance
academic achievement in our county, increased school choice, and
authorized the “Parents Bill of Rights” to have parents be a partner
in their children’s education.
While serving as Mayor of La Habra I balanced our budget,
implemented a pavement management plan to fix our streets,
and supported law enforcement. When I was the Chairman of
the Orange County Transportation Authority (OCTA), I was able to
widen the 57 and 91 freeways to reduce traffic congestion and
provide local road maintenance grants to fix streets.
The challenges we have now require strong leadership. We need
to have a total homelessness solution. We need to help families
with affordability, as well as encouraging business growth to create
more jobs. We need fiscal responsibility and transparency from
county government. Because of my record on these issues, I
earned the endorsement of the Orange County Register editorial
board. In their endorsement, the Register wrote “Shaw’s history of
protecting the taxpayers’ dollars has earned voters’ trust and our
endorsement”.
I look forward to working with Orange County Sheriff Don Barnes
to help make our communities safer by recruiting the best officers
and giving them all the tools they need to do the job. I am proud to
have the endorsement of Sheriff Barnes.
Don’t gamble with our future, vote County experience, Vote Tim
Shaw for Supervisor.
