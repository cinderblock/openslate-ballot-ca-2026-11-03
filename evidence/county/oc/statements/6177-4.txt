BRIAN PARKER
Appointed Moulton Niguel Water District Director
As your Director on the Moulton Niguel Water District Board, I am
committed to:
Safeguarding our vital water resources
Maintaining the lowest rates possible
Ensuring financial accountability
Throughout my service on the Board, I have applied my 30+ years
of business experience to champion operational efficiency and
fiscal responsibility. A longtime Laguna Niguel resident, I intimately
understand the importance of delivering safe, high-quality, and
reliable drinking water and wastewater services to our community.
My leadership on the District Board builds on a deep foundation
of public service, including my time serving on the 2023-24
Orange County Grand Jury, where I co-authored a landmark
report on optimizing South County water and wastewater systems.
That critical work laid the groundwork for regional realignments
that save taxpayers money and improve local infrastructure
stewardship.
I respectfully ask for your vote to continue serving as your Moulton
Niguel Water District Director.
