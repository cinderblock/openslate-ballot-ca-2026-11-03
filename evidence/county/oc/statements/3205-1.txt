SYLVIA IGLESIAS
Parent/Teacher/Educator
I’m an Elementary Public School Teacher and Parent. Homegrown
Leader, lived in Santa Ana since 1979 having immigrated from El
Salvador; resided in Trustee Area-4 since 1990. My children and I
are products of Santa Ana Unified School District (SAUSD). I proudly
graduated from Santa Ana Valley High School, Santa Ana College
and California State University-Fullerton.
With over 30 years of public service from Orange County Social
Services Agency, gained experience reviewing budget, policy
making, trained staff and connected residents to services.
As a first-generation college graduate, Parent and Teacher, I
believe education is important to our families and is society’s great
equalizer. I will Always:
Put Students First
Ensure All Students are prepared for College or Workforce
Spend money directly in classrooms, benefiting Students-Not
Bureaucrats. Ensure Accountability and Transparency.
Provide Safe and Strong Schools
Defend Tax-payer dollars and Not increase Your Taxes
Champion Parents' Rights and support Educational Options
Increase College Dual-Enrollment
Support great teachers/staff; ensure they have resources/tools
to support students.
Please join Parents, Community/Faith-Based Leaders, Teachers,
Law Enforcement Officers and Students to elect Sylvia Iglesias for
SAUSD Trustee Area-4.
With your vote, together, we Will Put Students First, build strong
future leaders and make SAUSD Great.
