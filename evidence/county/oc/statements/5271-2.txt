CARLOS MANZO
AGE: 54
Westminster City Councilmember
It has been an honor to serve our community on the City Council.
Throughout my service, I have worked to uphold the integrity of this
nonpartisan office by putting people before politics and focusing
on what matters most to our residents.
I believe effective local leadership is rooted in transparency,
accountability, and respect. That means listening to every voice,
treating every resident fairly, and making thoughtful decisions that
strengthen our entire community.
I have worked to strengthen public safety by supporting
additional Homeless Liaison Officers, Park Rangers, and the
Central Cities Navigation Center, giving first responders better
tools to address homelessness with compassion, accountability.
I have also supported investments in neighborhood parks, local
infrastructure, and economic development to keep Westminster
safe and financially strong.
I will continue focusing on public safety, responsible budgeting,
revitalizing our commercial corridors, maintaining our streets and
parks, and expanding opportunities for local businesses.
While we have made meaningful progress, there is still important
work ahead. As Mayor, I will continue bringing people together,
encouraging open dialogue, and pursuing practical solutions.
Together, we can build a stronger, safer, and more united
Westminster.
I respectfully ask for your support and your vote.
Learn more at votecarlosmanzo.com.
