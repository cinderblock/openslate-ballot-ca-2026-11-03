KAREN MARTIN
Urban Planning Entrepreneur
Development can move forward while protecting neighborhoods,
quality of life, and the environment if we simply follow the rules.
My graduate education is in Urban and Regional Planning.
For more than thirty years, I have led planning and policy
efforts across California, including coastal development, while
balancing environmental protection, property rights, public access,
neighborhood concerns, and economic needs.
Since moving to Laguna Beach in 1986, my service has included
working with the Open Space Committee, Affordable Housing Task
Force, and Housing and Human Services Committee.
As your Councilmember, my priority will be to listen to you and
your concerns before making a decision. My decisions will weigh
neighborhood, environmental, and economic impacts to avoid
disputes and reduce delays.
Our taxpayer dollars are precious. Even a city famous for sunshine
needs a rainy-day fund. I will scrutinize the present and future cost
of every major project and take into account what may go unfunded
because of it.
Laguna needs to find the best way to balance public safety and
infrastructure while supporting local businesses and protecting
our hillsides, trees, ocean, beaches, and the character that makes
Laguna Beach home. These will be my priorities.
I respectfully ask for your vote.
