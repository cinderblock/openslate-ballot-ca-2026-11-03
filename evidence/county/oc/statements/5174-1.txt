ROBYN GRANT
Newport Beach Council Member
Leading starts with listening. It has been an honor to serve on
the Newport Beach City Council since 2022. For four years I have
remained transparent and responsive. The best solutions come
from working together.
My experience as an attorney and business owner, along with
almost 20 years of community service including on the City
Finance Committee, Civil Service Board, Library Board, and Arts
Commission, has prepared me to serve on the City Council with
integrity, sound judgment, and a commitment to results, not
politics.
Public safety is my highest priority. I have supported game
changing policing policies like drones as first responders and
canine units, worked on a small City Council committee which
accomplished a 90% reduction in homelessness, and spearheaded
a resident funded initiative establishing the Newport Beach Animal
Shelter.
I prioritize maintaining a balanced City budget with annual
surpluses and a AAA credit rating, protecting our parks, harbor, and
beaches, and guaranteeing reliable drinking and firefighting water
capabilities.
Looking ahead, I will continue to fiercely advocate for transparent
government, responsible fiscal stewardship, and protecting our
neighborhoods.
I would be honored to receive your vote for re-election so
that, together, we continue building a Newport Beach that is safe,
financially strong, and an exceptional place to live.
