PHIL BACERRA
Santa Ana City Councilmember
Phil Bacerra is a proven leader that is dedicated to building a
cleaner, safer, and more prosperous Santa Ana. Guided by the
values of public safety, fiscal responsibility, and opportunity for
every family, Phil brings people together to deliver real results.
Endorsed by the OC Professional Firefighters Local 3631, the
Santa Ana Police Officers Association, and the Los Angeles/Orange
Counties Building and Construction Trades Council, Phil has earned
the trust of those who serve our community.
As your City Councilmember, Phil Bacerra has:
Strengthened laws to reduce public intoxication.
Opened a police substation in South Santa Ana.
Shut down homeless encampments.
Secured $4,000,000 in funding for five new traffic signals.
Installed $2,500,000 in new lighting and playground equipment
at Lillie King Park and George Upton All-Access Park.
Implemented $3,500,000 in repairs of roofs, apparatus doors, and
restrooms at our fire stations.
Stopped a proposed needle distribution program.
Installed safety fencing along the Flower Street Bike Trail.
If reelected to the Santa Ana City Council, Phil Bacerra will:
Continue reducing homelessness on our streets.
Attract investment that creates good-paying jobs for Santa Ana
families.
Improve sidewalks and streets.
Ensure police and firefighters have the resources needed to keep
our neighborhoods safe.
As proud Santa Ana homeowners, Phil and his wife, along with
Stanley Pup, are committed to keeping Santa Ana clean, safe, and
thriving for current and future generations.
