ROBERT R. PEASE II
Professional Chemical Engineer
I'm running for city council because I believe the best ideas
come from listening to residents, making informed decisions, and
planning ahead. Every voice matters, and everybody deserves to be
heard.
My commitment is simple: to be accessible, prepared, and focused
on practical solutions that keep Yorba Linda safe, financially
responsible, and an exceptional place for families, seniors, and
local businesses. I'll work to preserve the character of our
community while embracing thoughtful improvements that benefit
both today's residents and future generations. These are the same
characteristics I embodied as your Planning Commissioner for over
eleven years.
Educationally, I am a Chemical Engineer with an MBA (accounting
emphasis) and have worked for the state of California as a tax
auditor and as a renewable energy manager for the County of
Orange. My work career epitomizes my philosophy of public service.
I have also done volunteer work with the United Way and City of
Yorba Linda
This campaign isn't about politics; it's about service. It's about
earning your trust through integrity, respect, and a genuine
commitment to our community.
Together, we can build on Yorba Linda's strengths and ensure it
remains a city we're proud to call home for years to come.
Listening. Leading. Delivering.
