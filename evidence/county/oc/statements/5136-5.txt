BRITTANY THOMPSON
AGE: 40
Community Volunteer
As a wife, mother of three, nonprofit founder, and ordained minister,
I believe the strongest cities are built by strong families, connected
neighborhoods, and neighbors who serve one another.
Lake Forest is more than where we live—it is our home. I am
running for City Council to help preserve the safe, family-friendly
community we cherish while preparing our city for a strong future.
My priorities are clear: support excellent schools that help every
child succeed, champion our local businesses as the backbone of
our economy, and protect the quality of life that makes Lake Forest
an exceptional place to raise a family.
I believe leadership begins by listening. I want to bring
our neighborhoods together, encourage greater community
involvement, and build relationships that strengthen trust across
our city. When we work together with respect and a shared purpose,
our entire community benefits.
It would be an honor to serve Lake Forest with integrity,
transparency, and a servant's heart.
Learn more at www.ThompsonForLakeForest.com.
