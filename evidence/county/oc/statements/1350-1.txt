You deserve a government that works.
Housing, insurance, food, childcare, gas, and electricity outpace
wages. I see it in South Orange County, and my family feels it too.
I'm an immigrant who came here on a student visa, paid my way
through college, and proudly became a US citizen to pursue the
American Dream—the promise that hard work builds a good life.
But the cost of living is putting that dream out of reach for too many.
My career is in business—not government—13 years building tech
companies and meeting payroll. I serve as unpaid Treasurer of the
Silverado-Modjeska Recreation and Park District, where I balanced
our budget after a 30% cut while preserving core services.
In Sacramento, I will work to lower costs for building, insuring, and
fire-hardening homes, bring down rents so our youth can afford
to live where they grew up, make AI data centers (not families)
pay for their energy demands, win our share of road funding,
strengthen schools and public safety, and back entrepreneurs and
small businesses that create jobs.
I accept no corporate PAC money. My votes will serve you, not
private interests. I pledge to be an independent, data-driven,
solutions-focused decision maker.
