ANTHONY KUO
AGE: 43
City of Irvine Commissioner
I grew up in Irvine, attended our public schools, and have spent my
life serving the city that gave me so much.
I served eight years as an Irvine Commissioner and four years as
your City Councilmember, working to keep Irvine America’s Safest
City for our size, protect our quality of life, promote transparency
and accountability, and safeguard taxpayer dollars.
In my final year on the City Council, Irvine projected a budget
surplus nearing $30,000,000, and I led the effort to return nearly
$5,000,000 to taxpayers.
I will restore fiscal discipline at City Hall and build on my record
of strengthening public safety; fighting for greater affordability
while protecting our quality of life; and ensuring residents have a
meaningful voice in decisions about our neighborhoods.
Public service isn’t about titles. It’s about listening, doing
the homework, asking tough questions, working collaboratively,
making tough decisions, and delivering results.
You’ve trusted me to serve. I’ve always put Irvine first. I’ve done it
before, and I’m ready to roll up my sleeves and do it again.
I respectfully ask for your vote so together we can protect what
makes Irvine special and build a stronger, healthier future for our
city.
Anthony Kuo
