BRIAN BURLEY
REPUBLICAN
Trustee, Huntington Beach City School District
The California Republican Party, California Republican Assembly,
California Congress of Republicans and Reform California have all
endorsed my candidacy.
I am a lifelong Republican and local elected Trustee in Huntington
Beach. I have a proven conservative record as I have consistently
stood for common sense and fought for my constituents.
The 42nd District was drastically gerrymandered due to Prop 50.
I entered this race because we needed a responsible elected
official to represent the conservative cause for Congress in this new
district.
During a time when many school districts are cutting costs and
contemplating layoffs to balance budgets, Huntington Beach City
School District has set a new standard for excellence. We have
balanced budgets, built reserves, safeguarded your tax dollars and
kept our schools safe.
Californians are currently confronting the reality of absurdly high
taxes, increased crime and an affordability crisis resulting in rising
poverty and high inflation.
Tax relief for businesses and families is a top priority. I will advocate
for tax credits to help first-time homeowners. I have protected our
children from extreme political agendas and I will take that same
no-nonsense approach to Congress in the fight against socialism.
Experience matters - My background is in information technology
as I administer Learning Management Systems at the University of
Southern California. I also hold multiple degrees from USC including
a Master’s in Public Policy.
I respectfully ask for your vote in this upcoming election.
