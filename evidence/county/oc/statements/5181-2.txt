DAN SLATER
Orange Mayor/Businessman
I grew up in Orange, graduating from OLu. I proudly own both my
home and small business here.
When you first elected me Mayor in 2022, I pledged to protect
Orange taxpayers and bring common sense to city hall. Since then,
I’ve poured my heart and soul into this responsibility:
Orange is cleaner
Crime down 28%
Homelessness decreased
Illegal street vendors eliminated
Graffiti reduced
Traffic flow improved
Tackling the proliferation of massage parlors and smoke shops
Addressing poorly maintained vacant properties
45 acres adjoining our “Central Park” (Santiago Creek) dedicated
as permanent open space, with more on the way
Attracting new businesses, bringing much needed sales tax
revenue, helping to return Orange to financial stability
We’ve done this even though our City budget has been reduced
$37.5 million, and we’ve cut our workforce 10%.
My leadership has earned bipartisan support from our Orange
City Firefighters, Congressmember Young Kim, Assemblymember
Avelino Valencia, most of our City Council, and countless
community leaders.
I will honor existing term limits: this will be the last time I seek any
elected position in Orange.
Let’s continue putting Orange back on a solid foundation. I
respectfully request your vote for one last two-year term as Mayor.
