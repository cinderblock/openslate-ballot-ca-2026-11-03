TODD FRAZIER
AGE: 45
Governing Board Member, Placentia-Yorba Linda Unified
School District
As your Placentia-Yorba Linda school board trustee, a Certified
Public Accountant, and a father of three PYLUSD students, I have
worked to put students first and be consistent with the promises I
made to our families in 2022. In every decision, I always focus on
student interests first.
When I first ran, I pledged fiscal discipline, transparency, and
an education system focused on academics rather than politics.
On the board, I have applied my CPA training to scrutinize the
budget, protect classroom dollars, and hold spending accountable
to results, because every dollar counts.
There is still work to do, and I am asking for your vote to continue
confronting challenging issues.
I will keep defending parental rights, protect girls' sports, and
focus on student success in and outside the classroom. Parents
deserve transparency about what is taught in the classroom, timely
notifications, and a genuine voice in their children's education.
I believe classroom instruction should be academic and age-
appropriate so students can focus on reading, writing, math, and
critical thinking. Political agendas including CRT and DEI advocated
by some local universities have no place in PYLUSD.
Our daughters have a right to compete on a level playing field.
I oppose biological males in girls athletic competitions or in
girls locker rooms. I will fight to defend these rights, and keep
communication between the district and its families open and
honest.
As a business owner, I believe our schools should prepare all
students for success, not only those bound for a four-year
university. I will expand real career pathways in the skilled
trades, technology, entrepreneurship, and artificial intelligence, so
graduates leave PYLUSD immediately prepared to earn, build, and
lead.
For more than thirty years, Yorba Linda has been my home. My wife
and I have raised our family here, worshiped here. I have coached
and supported local youth sports. I love this community, and our
schools should reflect its values: integrity, hard work, and respect
for every family.
I would be honored to keep serving you. Thank you for your trust,
your prayers, and your vote.
Learn more at voteforfrazier.com.
