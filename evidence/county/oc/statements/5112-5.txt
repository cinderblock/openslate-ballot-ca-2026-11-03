JOY BERRY
Small Business Owner
The Laguna Beach city council is under increasing pressure to
approve questionable development projects, even in areas where
it is prohibited, even if it means breaking our planning and
environmental rules. I will resist that pressure.
Since moving here almost 30 years ago, I have worked tirelessly to
protect our environment. That includes taking legal action when
our council greenlights encroachment of development into our
iconic hillsides and precious watercourses.
Our town is attracting ever more tourists and the council is investing
the revenue to bring in even more people. I will work to redirect
some of those funds toward critical resident needs such as sewer,
water, and public safety infrastructure that has been neglected.
As a successful small business owner and developer who
understands the importance of community buy-in, long-term
planning, and fiscal discipline, I will ensure that our city’s budget
is balanced without relying on out-of-scale developments and
unchecked growth to pay our bills.
Laguna Beach is at a vital crossroad. Our town’s unique character,
natural beauty, and financial stability are at stake. It’s time to put
residents first, uphold our rules, and protect the Laguna we love. I
ask for your vote.
