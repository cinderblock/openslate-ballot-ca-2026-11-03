ALFREDO RODRIGUEZ
General Manager
Government exists to serve the people. That's the principle that
inspired me to run for Mayor of Anaheim.
I came from humble beginnings, where I learned that hard work,
integrity, and personal responsibility create opportunity. Those
values have guided me throughout my life and career.
Today, as the General Manager of an environmental services
company, I lead teams across California, create jobs, solve
complex challenges, and work with homeowners, businesses, and
government agencies to deliver results. Every day, I bring people
together, make difficult decisions, and solve problems.
As your Mayor, I will put Anaheim residents first by strengthening
public safety, addressing homelessness with compassion and
accountability, supporting local businesses, investing responsibly
in our infrastructure, and ensuring City Hall is transparent, fiscally
responsible, and accountable to the people it serves.
I'll be a Mayor who listens, shows up, and isn't afraid to do the hard
work. My commitment is simple: to earn your trust through action,
not promises, and to make sure every resident has a voice in the
future of our city.
It would be an honor to earn your trust and respectfully ask for your
vote.
