DONNA VIDRINE
Nurse/Business Owner
My name is Donna Vidrine, and I am running to represent you on
the San Clemente City Council. I believe government should serve
the public interest. Our community deserves thoughtful leadership
focused on local priorities. I will work to improve transportation
and parking, support local businesses, expand opportunities for
working families, make first-time homeownership more attainable,
recognize the needs of seniors, protect our coastline, reduce
wildfire risk, and responsibly advance the removal of nuclear waste
from San Onofre.
For decades, I have developed my leadership skills as an Air
Force Captain, healthcare professional, nursing administrator, and
small business owner. These experiences have taught me to
listen carefully, solve complex problems, make difficult decisions,
and bring people together to achieve practical, implementable
solutions. The decisions we make today will shape San Clemente’s
future for generations.
Public service is a calling. You deserve a council member who is
accessible, transparent, ethical, and committed to serving every
resident. I am beholden to no special interests and will work every
day to preserve, protect, and enhance the quality of life that makes
San Clemente such a special place to call home.
I respectfully ask for your vote.
Donna Vidrine
