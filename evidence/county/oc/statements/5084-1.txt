WILLIAM ZYLLA
AGE: 55
Retired Emergency Technician
Thank you for your consideration to represent Garden Grove
City Council District 1. A resident since 2001, I bring a unique
combination of healthcare experience and local government
expertise to serve our community. My background includes 11
years with UCLA Health as a Clinical Care Partner and Emergency
Trauma Technician, and 16 years in city government managing
vital public safety, neighborhood preservation, and emergency
management services. I hold a Bachelor’s Degree from UC Irvine
and have completed graduate coursework in Public Administration
and Conflict Resolution. If elected, my top priorities for District
1 are supporting our first responders to keep our neighborhood
safe, strengthening emergency preparedness to build a resilient
community, enhancing neighborhood preservation, ensuring fiscal
responsibility, and boosting local business growth and supporting
existing businesses. I will also hold GKN and others strictly
accountable to protect our residents and mitigate future impacts.
Let’s keep Garden Grove a great place to live, learn, work, and play!
