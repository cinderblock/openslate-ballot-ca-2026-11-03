JULIE SUCHARD
Community Volunteer
I’ve lived in the city of Placentia for 26 years. I’m not a politician. I
am blunt, truthful, and independent. I’m a person who rolls up their
sleeves and gets things done.
While raising my two daughters in Placentia, I volunteered for
PTA, Booster Clubs, and served as Treasurer for a local non-profit
organization serving the homeless. I’ve been Project Leader for
"Love Placentia" and helped with local school board campaigns.
I’m a stalwart supporter of our public services, the Placentia Police
and Fire Departments, and the small businesses that help our
community thrive.
I’m for fiscal responsibility and transparency. Using my expertise
in accounting and public records requests, I've shed light on
fiscally irresponsible actions within city management, resulting
in an outside investigation and substantial changes. For this, I’ve
received the thanks of many working for, and living in, Placentia.
I’m concerned about the financial stability of the city, and will
ensure that our funds are used in a fiscally responsible and
transparent way.
If elected, I won’t accept salary or benefits from the city. I'm offering
my time because I love Placentia and am deeply concerned about
our city’s financial solvency. Please vote for Julie Suchard for City
Council.
