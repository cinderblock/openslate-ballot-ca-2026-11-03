RAY SCHNELL
Councilmember/Business Executive
Tustin is my home. My wife Cheryl and I have raised our two
daughters here and watched them thrive in this wonderful city.
Over the past twenty years, I have come to deeply value the small-
town character and quality of life that makes Tustin so very special.
My background as a business executive and entrepreneur, along
with my MBA from Chapman University, has shaped how I approach
public service: practical, honest, accountable, and focused on
results that matter most to our residents.
Four years ago, I asked the good people of Tustin for the opportunity
to serve with three clear priorities: public safety as job number one,
economic vitality paired with fiscal responsibility, as well as honest,
transparent government. As the city’s current Mayor Pro Tem, and
with your support, I have delivered on every one of them.
On public safety, police funding and officer staffing have
increased, technology and equipment upgraded, and school safety
strengthened. The results are clear – total crime has fallen 46
percent over the past four years. I am proud of this work and have
earned the endorsements of the Tustin Police Officers Association,
the Tustin Police Support Services Association, the Orange County
Sheriff, and the Orange County Firefighters Association Local
3631 because they know I will continue fighting to keep our
neighborhoods and schools safe.
On economic vitality and fiscal responsibility, I have supported
and delivered balanced city budgets every year. Voted to
advance smart, thoughtful development that grows revenue while
preserving Tustin’s unique small-town character. I have also
kept city fees and taxes low, reduced unnecessary regulations,
accelerated our city pension paydown to retire this debt early,
and increased investments in public parks, roads, traffic relief,
and essential infrastructure. These steps protect affordability and
ensure we can fund the critical services and quality of life measures
residents depend on and enjoy.
On transparency, I have insisted on open financial disclosures,
public budget workshops, and meaningful community engagement
so residents can see and have a say in how their hard earned tax
dollars are being spent. These same priorities continue to guide
my work. With proven results and principled leadership, I will keep
working tirelessly to ensure Tustin remains a safe, strong, and
prosperous place for our families and the generations to follow.
I respectfully ask for your vote to continue serving the great city of
Tustin. Learn more at www.rayfortustin.com.
