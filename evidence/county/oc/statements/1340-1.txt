PAULA SWIFT
DEMOCRATIC
Small Business Owner
I’m Dr. Paula Swift, and I’m running for Assembly because Orange
County families deserve a representative who understands what it
means to stretch a paycheck, navigate a broken healthcare system,
and worry about whether the next generation can afford to stay in
the community they grew up in.
I know what resources our communities need, and I know what is at
stake when Sacramento gets it wrong.
I put myself through school while raising my family. I earned my
bachelor's degree from Azusa Pacific University and a Master’s and
Doctorate in Leadership from the University of Southern California.
After surviving a tragic act of domestic violence, I've dedicated my
work to protecting those overlooked by a government meant to
protect them.
Today I own a small business, have taught at a local college,
and spent over two decades serving Southern California families,
supporting children in foster care and fighting to improve access to
mental health care and educational opportunities.
As your Assemblymember, I will fight to make healthcare more
accessible and affordable. I will push for housing solutions
that offer pathways to homeownership. I will work to expand
opportunities for our students, protect our seniors’ retirement
security, and increase support for the small businesses that give our
communities their character.
We need leadership focused on lowering the cost of living
and bringing accountability to our State Capitol. I have the life
experience to deliver it.
A future shaped by opportunity, not circumstance.
I ask for your vote. Learn more: votepaulaswift.com
