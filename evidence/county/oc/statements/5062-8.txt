JOHN NGUYEN
Engineer/Environmentalist
Although I was born in Kansas, I was raised in Fountain Valley,
attending Ethan Allen Elementary School in the 1980s and 1990s.
I know what living in Fountain Valley is all about. People here want
to live in a safe, crime-free community where families can raise kids.
After obtaining my undergraduate degree in electrical engineering
and my graduate degree in chemical engineering, I worked to
prevent harmful emissions that can cause serious health effects.
I believe people should be able to live without the fear of the
environment harming them or their loved ones.
I also believe community safety is a fundamental right. Key to safety
is having a strong community working harmoniously together to
prevent crimes. As a city council member, I will work to foster
a stronger community by harnessing and embracing Fountain
Valley’s diversity, while refraining from divisive politics.
I respectfully ask for your support and vow to listen to everyone.
Together, we will make Fountain Valley better.
/s/ John Nguyen
