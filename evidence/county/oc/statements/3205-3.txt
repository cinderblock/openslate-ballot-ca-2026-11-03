KATELYN BRAZER ACEVES
Governing Board Member, Santa Ana Unified School
District, Trustee Area 4
As President of the Santa Ana Unified School District Board
of Education and a school employee with the Orange County
Department of Education who works directly with students and
parents every day, I've applied my experience as an educator
and proven leadership to help improve our schools. Additionally,
I am the candidate officially endorsed by the Santa Ana
Educators’ Association (SAEA) and the California School Employees
Association (CSEA), Santa Ana Chapter 41.
During my first term, SAUSD achieved academic gains across every
California School Dashboard indicator for the first time.
Together, we placed reading coaches at every elementary school,
strengthened support for English Learners through new courses
and targeted interventions, and increased college and career
readiness by creating new internship opportunities in high-demand
fields.
I led the creation of our districtwide Immigration Support Plan to
help students safely attend school and families access resources,
created two Student Board Member positions, and established the
SAUSD Community Budget Advisory Committee to strengthen fiscal
transparency.
Through building no-cost community partnerships, I've increased
resources to students and families without relying on district
general funds. This allows more money to be invested in classroom
instruction where it matters most.
Under my leadership, voters overwhelmingly approved the largest
K–12 school bond in Orange County in 2024, securing over $355
million to modernize aging schools and provide safe, high-quality
learning environments for every student.
