SUE QUAM
I will use my four years of LWV Board leadership, 30 years of
government experience and master’s in public administration to
give back to my community through practical, accountable action.
Protect cannabis tax revenue by directing it to resident services
—not unrelated general expenses. Priorities should include
items such as subsidized transportation for taxi rides, window
replacements, expanded Meals on Wheels, and other programs for
qualifying seniors and low-income families.
Create and fund safety programs for residents walking pets,
grandchildren or living with disabilities in coyote-prone areas. I will
support a small dog park or adjust existing dog-park hours for small
pets.
As coyote populations grow, promote responsible wildlife
management that balances ecology, controls rodents, prevents
feeding, uses professional hazing, and keeps wildlife at a safe
distance.
Work with regional partners to strengthen fire and police
protection, water, sewer, electrical grid services, and environmental
safeguards. I will vote to maintain golf cart paths, improve traffic
lights, and expand technology for remote public participation in
council meetings.
Advocate tirelessly to protect residents’ federal, state, and county
social safety nets.
I respectfully ask for your vote to pursue these priorities and help
run an efficient, responsive city government.
Sue Quam
