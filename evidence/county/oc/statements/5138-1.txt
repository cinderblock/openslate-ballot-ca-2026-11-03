AMY STEVENS
Mother/Communications Consultant
Lake Forest is where my family’s life happens. It’s school drop-
off, neighborhood walks, community events, beautiful parks, and
people we love and rely on. I’m running for City Council because
decisions made at City Hall shape our everyday moments.
Hi, I’m Amy! A Lake Forest mom, communications professional,
former La Madera PTA president, and CERT-trained through the
city’s Community Emergency Response Team program. Serving in
our local schools and community has shown me how much can be
accomplished when people feel heard and have a meaningful role
in solving problems.
That same approach has guided my work for 20+ years. I have
helped public agencies and community organizations navigate
difficult issues, communicate during emergencies, and bring people
with different perspectives together to find practical solutions.
As a councilmember, my priorities will include safer streets
and e-bike education, stronger emergency preparedness, well-
maintained open spaces, thoughtful planning that respects
neighborhoods and infrastructure, and attracting quality
businesses to vacant storefronts so residents have more local
options and the city strengthens its tax base.
I will show up throughout the community, listen carefully, and make
choices with District 4 residents in mind. I would be honored to have
your vote.
