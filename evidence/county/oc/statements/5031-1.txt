MIKE BULEY
Councilmember/Attorney/Parent
I was honored to be elected to the City Council in 2024, and
since then, I’ve worked hard to be a common sense voice for our
community. Serving on the Council has deepened my appreciation
for Costa Mesa. I pride myself on transparency, asking hard
questions, and making informed decisions in the best interest of our
City. As a neighbor, parent, attorney, and businessman, I ask for your
vote for Mayor.
My wife and our three children have been blessed to live, work, play,
and grow in Costa Mesa for 24 years. I have also volunteered at St.
John the Baptist, Costa Mesa Little League, Hoops Unlimited, and
local food drives and outreach efforts.
As a Councilmember, I have worked to protect funding for
infrastructure and parks, champion public safety, ensure taxpayer
dollars are spent responsibly, preserve public access to Fairview
Park while protecting its natural beauty, and strengthen the voice
of our volunteer Committees and Commissions in City decision-
making.
I have also opposed government overreach that burdens residents
and local businesses, including a proposed rental registry and
mandates that interfere with private business decisions.
I sincerely ask for your vote.
Endorsements: OC Sheriff Don Barnes, State Senator Steven Choi,
Assemblymember Diane Dixon, OC Supervisors Janet Nguyen and
Don Wagner, and Councilmember Jeff Pettis.
