SARA J. PELLY
AGE: 61
Governing Board Member, Orange Unified School
District, Trustee Area 4
I was an elementary school teacher and principal for 34 years,
I know what it takes to help our students succeed both today
and tomorrow. I am a champion for public education who works
with parents, teachers, and the community. As your school
board member, I find common ground, build trust, and form
strong partnerships to move our schools forward. Building these
relationships is exactly why I have the endorsement of all of my
fellow Orange Unified board members, our classroom teachers, and
4 out of 5 Villa Park City Council members, the city where I live.
I believe every child deserves a world‑class education, and am
committed to providing a variety of programs to serve the unique
needs of each student. It is critical that we start our young learners
on the right path, and continue to provide learning opportunities
as unique as our students. I’m proud of Orange Unified’s extensive
Career Readiness programs for students who are not university-
bound as well as a broad range of Advanced Placement classes. As
a former principal, I understand the complexities of implementing
innovative programs.
My priorities are:
Updated Facilities - Every student deserves a safe, modern, and
welcoming place to learn. This year I voted to approve a $40 million
dollar investment in facilities improvements to our elementary
schools.
Resource Allocation - A true commitment to students means
directing every dollar where it matters most - into the classroom.
I have a unique perspective as the only board member with
elementary classroom experience, and I successfully grew OUSD’s
award winning Mandarin Immersion program. Using my four
decades of diverse expertise, I am able to navigate complex
educational issues. I know where to send your tax dollars for
maximum benefit. By ensuring thoughtful distribution of resources,
I will continue to safeguard fiscal responsibility.
Fiscal Responsibility and Transparency - Taxpayers deserve to know
how funds are used and to see clear benefits for students. I am also
dedicated to bringing renewed focus on accountability and student
outcomes, so that every decision we make reflects what matters
most - our students.
I hope you’ll stand with me in protecting our district, maintaining
stability on our board, and continuing the upward trend of success.
Together, we can build a future where every child is supported,
every classroom thrives, and our community benefits.
I would be honored to have your vote.
