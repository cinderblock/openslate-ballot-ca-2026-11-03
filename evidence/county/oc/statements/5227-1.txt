GIOVANNI CHAVEZ
AGE: 31
Housing Commissioner/Lawyer
I am running for Santa Ana City Council to fight for you. As a son of
immigrants, product of public schools, and first generation college
graduate, I have dedicated my adult life to fighting for working
people in Santa Ana.
As an attorney, I have advocated for tenants facing eviction,
employees facing wage theft, and accident victims. I am now
a Rental Housing Board Commissioner, where I work directly
to enforce the City’s Rent Stabilization and Just Cause Eviction
Ordinance.
Our city faces unprecedented challenges: an affordability crisis,
public safety concerns, the highest homeless rate in the County,
budget issues, and city services being cut. However, we also have
limitless potential. We have a strong, diverse working class that can
fulfill the promise of our city, but only if we invest in the residents
that keep our city running. My commitment is to invest in you. I will
invest in jobs, housing, public safety, and parks for our residents.
I have the honor of being endorsed by Congressmember Lou
Correa, State Assemblymember Avelino Valencia, First Responders,
and over 50 labor unions. And I ask you to also support me.
Together, we will fulfill the promise of our city.
