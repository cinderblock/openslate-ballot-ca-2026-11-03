GRACEY VAN DER MARK
REPUBLICAN
Councilwoman/Business Owner
AGE: 52
My life story is proof that the American Dream still exists. I grew
up in rough neighborhoods, experienced homelessness and have
been a survivor of domestic violence. I overcame those challenges
to become a small-business owner, City Councilmember, and the
first Latina Mayor.
I delivered results. My city is one of the few in Orange County where
homelessness has declined in recent years, our streets are safer,
and our city is consistently recognized as one of California's best-
run communities.
Families are being torn apart and fleeing California because life
has become unaffordable. I’ve raised my children locally and sent
them to public schools and that’s when I realized we need to hold
politicians accountable to do better.
For too long, Sacramento has been dominated by one-party rule
and the political class like Gavin Newsom, who put ideology over
the people they represent. California needs reform. I will work every
day to deliver common-sense solutions. That is why I’m proudly
endorsed by Reform California.
I’m endorsed by the Howard Jarvis Taxpayers Association PAC
because I will fight to eliminate the fraud, waste, and abuse of your
tax dollars while working to lower the cost of living on hardworking
families.
Keeping our communities safe is my top priority. That’s why I’m
endorsed by Orange County Sheriff Don Barnes, the Association of
Orange County Deputy Sheriffs, and the Huntington Beach Police
Officers' Association.
I am a business owner, wife, and mother. It would be an honor to
earn your vote. Learn more at www.GraceyForAssembly.com
