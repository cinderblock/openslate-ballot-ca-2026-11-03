HIEU NGUYEN
Social Worker/Businessman
Raised Here. Growing Forward.
Garden Grove gave my family the opportunity to get ahead, and
now I’m running for City Council to help Garden Grove grow
forward.
As a Councilmember, I will fight to make life more affordable,
address homelessness, strengthen city services, expand parks and
open space for children and families, and invest in community
programs that bring residents together.
I immigrated here from Vietnam as a child and attended Garden
Grove public schools. My family worked long hours in local garment
factories, and like many working families, we relied on programs
like Medi-Cal, SNAP, and Section 8 to get by. Those experiences
taught me the value of hard work, resilience, and community.
For nearly 20 years, I’ve spent my career as a social worker,
community organizer, small business owner, and healthcare leader.
I’ve worked directly with people experiencing homelessness,
mental health, and substance use issues while bringing together
local leaders and community organizations to solve problems and
strengthen the services families rely on.
Today, I’m proudly raising my son in the same neighborhood where
I grew up. I’m ready to help build a stronger, more affordable, and
connected Garden Grove for every resident. I respectfully ask for
your vote.
