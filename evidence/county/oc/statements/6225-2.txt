MIKELLE FISH
Business Owner
As the owner of a small business management firm serving for profit
and nonprofit organizations for over 20 years, I have dedicated
my career to helping organizations manage their finances with
integrity, accountability, and transparency. I will bring that same
commitment to Mesa Water District.
Protecting our water supply requires responsible financial
stewardship, transparent governance, and preparation for
tomorrow's challenges. I support recording and publishing Board
meetings, providing clear and accessible water quality information,
and ensuring residents have meaningful access to district decisions.
Cybersecurity must be a top priority. Water systems across
the country have become targets of increasingly sophisticated
cyberattacks, making it essential that Mesa Water invests in strong
cybersecurity infrastructure, regular security audits, employee
training, and emergency response planning to protect our critical
water systems.
I support practical investments in conservation, groundwater
recharge, stormwater capture, recycled water, and water-efficient
technologies to strengthen our long-term water reliability. Any
future infrastructure decisions should be guided by independent
science, fiscal responsibility, environmental stewardship, and the
public interest.
I believe well-managed local water agencies provide the
accountability and expertise our community deserves. Together,
we can ensure a safe, reliable, and sustainable water future for
generations to come.
