BRAD WEISKITTEL
Professional Engineer/Educator
As a Trabuco Canyon resident, Engineer, and Father of three
sons, I am committed to protecting the reliable water services
and infrastructure our community depends on. I understand the
importance of responsible leadership, long-term planning, and
careful stewardship of ratepayer dollars.
Professionally, I bring 25 years of engineering and leadership
experience managing complex technical programs, budgets,
and schedules. As an Aerospace Engineering Manager and
California-licensed Professional Engineer, I understand the
importance of infrastructure reliability, preventative maintenance,
risk management, and making sound investments that serve
current and future needs.
I also serve as a U.S. Navy Reserve Commander and local educator.
These roles have reinforced my commitment to accountability,
transparency, and public service. My participation in this election
does not imply endorsement by the Department of Defense.
As Director, I will prioritize reliable and affordable water service,
responsible financial oversight, resilient infrastructure, emergency
preparedness, and transparent decision-making. I will work
collaboratively with fellow board members, district professionals,
ratepayers, and community partners to address water supply
challenges and plan responsibly for the future.
Together, we can protect our water resources, strengthen essential
infrastructure, and ensure Trabuco Canyon Water District remains
accountable, resilient, and responsive to the communities it serves.
