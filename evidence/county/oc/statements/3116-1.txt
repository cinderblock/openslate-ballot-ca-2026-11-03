MANDY BOURNE
Community Volunteer
After practicing law, I’ve focused on our family and serving our
schools. I’ve taught reading in elementary classrooms, helped
families with college planning and applications, served on district
curriculum and accreditation committees, and directed elementary
school field days and senior nights for high school sports. I
currently teach high-school study and life skills classes, previously
teaching writing and music. For twenty years, I’ve loved serving in
Capistrano Unified. My husband and I raised six children in CUSD,
all of whom enjoyed its exceptional Spanish-immersion program.
We’re still experiencing the district alongside our youngest. From
APs to navigating IEPs, we’ve successfully managed many concerns
parents have with their CUSD kids. As a longtime volunteer and
current Capistrano Unified parent, I remain deeply invested in every
student’s success.
I am running to prioritize:
Academic excellence
Strong partnerships with families
Safe schools
Attracting and supporting outstanding teachers.
Classrooms should prepare students for success in college, careers,
and life. Keep the focus on learning, not on issues that divide us as
neighbors. I am honored to have earned the support of teachers,
trustees, and community leaders. As Trustee, I will bring experience,
common sense, civility, and collaboration to every decision, always
putting students first.
