BYRON SALVATIERRA
Cybersecurity Manager/Father
I'm an Iraq War veteran. I've called Irvine home for 40 years. I
grew up here going to Springbrook Elementary School, Lakeside
Middle School, Woodbridge High School, and Irvine Valley College. I
worked as a Public Safety Assistant for the Irvine Police Department
and served as a reserve firefighter with the Orange County Fire
Authority.
After Iraq, I found another way to serve close to home. I'm the
Cubmaster for our local scout pack, 50 families strong. I built
the bridge our scouts cross at graduation. I also built the safety
team for Orange County's pro-democracy rallies. I helped keep our
neighbors safe as they exercised their right to speak out.
I rode my bike to the University Park Library growing up, and my
kids have too. Irvine now runs its own library system, and families
drive to Tustin for better selection. I'll get our community involved
in building an Irvine library system that brings those families back.
Irvine also faces a real budget shortfall. We deserve a council that
works toward real transparency and accountability, and one that
listens and follows through for every resident.
I'm running because this city gave my family a chance, and I want
to keep building that chance for the next one. It would be an honor
to earn your vote.
