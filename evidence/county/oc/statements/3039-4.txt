ZONYA TOWNSEND
Educator/Parent/Nurse
Education changed the course of my life, and I believe every student
deserves that same opportunity to discover their potential and
build a brighter future through education. Higher education opened
doors I never imagined, leading to a lifetime of service, leadership,
and a passion for helping others succeed.
For more than 30 years, I have served as a Registered Nurse, Nurse
Manager, Adjunct Clinical Nursing Professor, educator, and mentor.
From teaching middle school science and health to preparing
future nurses and investing in young people through church
and community programs, I have seen firsthand how education
transforms lives.
My commitment extends beyond the classroom. As a Fullerton
Public Library Trustee, nonprofit board member, and president of a
statewide professional nursing organization, I have developed the
leadership, governance, financial stewardship, and collaborative
decision-making skills needed to serve our community effectively.
As your North Orange County Community College District Trustee,
I will champion academic excellence, career and technical
education, workforce readiness, university transfer opportunities,
strategic planning and responsible stewardship of taxpayer
resources. I will listen thoughtfully, lead with integrity, and always
put students first.
It would be an honor to earn your trust and your vote!
Zonya Townsend
