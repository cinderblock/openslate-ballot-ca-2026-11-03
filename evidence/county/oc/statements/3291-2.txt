CARL BLANKENHORN
Math Teacher/Parent
As a former Fountain Valley School District student, proud parent
of two former FVSD students, and recently retired high school math
teacher, I bring a unique perspective to our schools—as a student,
a parent, and an educator who understands what students need to
succeed in high school and beyond.
I have long admired FVSD’s caring teachers, engaged families,
strong neighborhood schools, and tradition of helping students
grow academically, socially, and personally. I am running because I
want to contribute to that success and help ensure FVSD continues
to thrive for every student.
With 30 years of classroom experience and a Master’s Degree in
Mathematics, I have dedicated my career to improving student
outcomes and supporting great teaching. As your trustee, I will
prioritize student safety, mental wellbeing, strong academics,
appropriate class sizes, more school counselors, and meaningful
professional development for teachers.
I will listen to parents, value community and professional input,
and work to build consensus around a strong 21st-century
education for every child. I will also champion transparency, fiscal
responsibility, and careful oversight of taxpayer dollars.
I respectfully ask for your vote.
