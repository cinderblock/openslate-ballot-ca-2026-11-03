CARLOS FRANCO
AGE: 36
Buena Park Councilmember/Businessowner
Buena Park is more than where we live—it's our home. It shaped
who I am, and serving our community has been the greatest honor
of my life.
As your Councilmember, I've worked to bring people together
while delivering meaningful results. I helped champion Coyote
Park, transforming underused land into a unique gathering place
where families can connect and children can play. I have
hosted community town halls, strengthened partnerships with
local schools, nonprofits, and businesses, and expanded youth
leadership opportunities through the Buena Park High School
Interact Club because investing in young people is investing in
Buena Park's future.
I believe leadership begins with listening. Whether advocating
for responsible spending, improving neighborhood infrastructure,
supporting local businesses, or helping residents navigate City Hall,
my focus has always been practical solutions that strengthen our
community.
There is still important work ahead. Together, we can build safer
neighborhoods, a stronger local economy, preserve Buena Park's
unique character, and create opportunities for future generations.
I would be honored to earn your vote for re-election and continue
serving with integrity, transparency, and building community
through service.
