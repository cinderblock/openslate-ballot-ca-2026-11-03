CHUONG V. VO
REPUBLICAN
Retired Police Officer
28-Year Law Enforcement Officer. Former Mayor of Cerritos.
Husband and Father.
Hello, my name is Chuong Vo, and I came to America as a child
refugee from Vietnam. Our country gave my family freedom and
opportunity, so I dedicated my life to serving our community
through local government, law enforcement, and raising my family
here.
As Mayor, I helped keep our neighborhoods safe, maintained
balanced budgets, and protected taxpayer dollars. I know how to
make tough decisions, prioritize core services, and deliver results
without raising taxes.
Our families deserve safe neighborhoods, strong schools, and a
government that lives within its means. In Congress, I will put safety,
affordability, and accountability first.
Public safety is government’s first responsibility. I will stand firmly
with law enforcement, increase penalties for fentanyl traffickers,
dismantle organized retail theft rings, and ensure officers have the
training and equipment they need to keep our communities secure.
Families are struggling with rising costs caused by reckless
spending in Washington. I will fight to cut wasteful spending,
eliminate fraud and abuse, protect Social Security and Medicare for
seniors, and restore fiscal discipline.
We are a nation of immigrants and a nation of laws. I support
securing our border, enforcing our laws, and modernizing legal
immigration so families and employers can follow a fair and orderly
process.
I will work to end insider stock trading in Congress, demand
transparent budgets, and hold Washington accountable.
I am ready to serve and would be honored to earn your vote.
