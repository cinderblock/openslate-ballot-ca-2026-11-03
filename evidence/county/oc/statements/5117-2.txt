HARRY HUGGINS
AGE: 77
Laguna Hills Non-profit President
Currently serve on the City of Laguna Hills Parks &
Recreation Commission, Vice Chair and President, Laguna Terrace
Beautification Team (LTBT) nonprofit.
Graduate of CSULB, County resident 56 years, 30 years in Laguna
Hills.
Extensive public service/employment throughout various
municipalities in Orange County including: Coastal Greenbelt
Authority; former Board Member, Laguna Greenbelt; County of
Orange, Public Facilities and Resources Department, Administrative
Assistant, Cities of Irvine & Garden Grove. Also, represented
employees of the Orange County Employees Association, PAC.
Most recently, as noted above, organized non-profit (LTBT) to
educate residents in a campaign to improve City open space,
roadways and front yards through use of drought tolerant
plantings. Our volunteer recruitment brought improvements to
walls that needed painting, weed removal & various plant
installations. LTBT has held Plein Air Painting competitions and
organized park concerts to raise community awareness.
As your Council Member I would strive to:
Protect and preserve our open spaces and natural beauty
Support responsible growth and smart development
Improve public safety and city services
Enhance parks, trails, and community amenities
Strengthen local businesses and the economy
Listen to residents and bring our community together
I would be grateful for your support and vote.
