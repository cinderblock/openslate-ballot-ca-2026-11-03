ERNIE GLASGOW
Contractor/Businessman
My name is Ernie Glasgow, and I am running for Orange City
Council, District 1. I have proudly called Orange home for nearly
58 years. My wife and I, who have been married for 42 years, are
both graduates of Orange High School. Over the decades, I have
watched our city grow and change, and I believe those changes
have made Orange a better place to live. My commitment to our
community is reflected in my years of public service. I served on the
OUSD Facilities Advisory Committee (2003-2004), the YMCA Board
(2004-2010), the City Traffic Committee (2012-2015), and the
Planning Commission (2015-2023). I also serve as a board member
of the Union Roofers Contractor Association and as Co-Chairman
of the Union Roofers Trust. My goal is to preserve the small-town
character that makes Orange unique while responsibly planning
for growth. Old Towne is the heart of our community, but our
surrounding neighborhoods deserve the same care and protection.
We must also protect our neighborhoods from corporations and
LLCs purchasing single-family homes and converting them into
high-density housing compounds. With my experience, dedication,
and proven record of service, I respectfully ask for your vote.
