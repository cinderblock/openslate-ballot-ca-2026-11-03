MARILYN BUCHI
Governing Board Member
As a District leader, I have consistently championed a place where
every student is important -with a choice among schools and
programs, an education relevant to the future, and a safe and
supportive environment.
My experience on the board as a strong, ethical and fiscally
responsible leader has equipped me to protect the extraordinary
quality of our schools.
Students in the Fullerton Joint Union High School District are
attending, graduating and excelling in record numbers as they
prepare for transition to college and career.
Our district, with the financial support of our communities, has
completed such major improvements as science labs, theaters and
gyms. Our teachers, support staff and leadership personnel are
among the best.
My experience at the state level broadens my knowledge and
ultimately helps to improve our award-winning schools. I serve on
the policy-making body and as a past president of a statewide
educational organization (CSBA) dedicated to sound governance
standards for school board members.
As a long-time resident of the community, parent of district
graduates, PTA leader and community volunteer, I am committed
to making sure that our schools deliver the exceptional education
that our community demands.
Let's Continue the Excellence!
