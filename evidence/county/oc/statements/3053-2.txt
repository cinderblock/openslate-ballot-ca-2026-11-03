BEATRIZ MENDOZA
AGE: 50
Parent/Businesswoman
In 2017, I proudly served as a member of the Rancho Santiago
Community College District, Measure E Citizens’ Bond Oversight
Committee. As a former student of a community college, I
understand firsthand the importance of having access to the best
resources, a great learning environment, and infrastructure that
leads to student success.
I am a former City of Santa Ana Planning Commissioner and also
served on the 7th Santa Ana General Plan Advisory Group, where
we reviewed the City’s General Master Plan to help improve our city.
While in college, I was the District Student Council Representative
for the Coast Community College District and was the Associated
Student Representative on the President’s Cabinet and Academic
Senate. After obtaining my A.A., I transferred to UCLA and
graduated with a degree in Political Science.
As a former staff member for a congressional office, I co-chaired
the Higher Education Advisory Board, where Chancellors, Deans,
and college Presidents participated in a dialogue to improve higher
education for all students. Currently, my oldest son attends Santa
Ana College, my daughter is at UC Santa Cruz, and my youngest son
is at Segerstrom High School, where I am a member of the School
Site Council.
