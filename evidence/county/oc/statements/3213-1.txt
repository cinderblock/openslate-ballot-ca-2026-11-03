KELLY FELTON
Educator/Professor/Parent
As a parent, educator, and scientist, I am running for the Tustin
Unified School District Board of Education to ensure every student
receives an outstanding education in a safe, supportive, and
academically focused environment.
My husband and I raised our two sons in public schools, and
I understand firsthand the importance of strong schools and
engaged families. I hold a doctorate in education and two master's
degrees in science, and have spent more than 20 years teaching
science at the college and high school levels.
From cancer research labs to biotechnology classrooms, I know
the value of critical thinking, evidence-based decision-making, and
preparing students for success in college, careers, and life.
As a Board member, I will keep students at the center of every
decision, promote academic excellence, ensure fiscal responsibility
and transparency, and strengthen partnerships with parents. I will
support career technical education, arts, music, and athletics, plan
for declining enrollment and aging schools, and protect taxpayer
resources.
I will bring practical leadership, put students first, and preserve the
district our community is proud of. I would be honored to serve.
Dr. Kelly Felton
