RHONDA SHADER
REPUBLICAN
Local Small Businesswoman
I'm a community advocate with a long record of public service. I was
elected to the Placentia City Council in 2016, served two terms as
Mayor, and launched Placentia's first Fire Department.
I've been a champion for fiscal responsibility. When I took office,
our city had $49 in reserves. I made the necessary decisions to
get Placentia’s financial house in order, and that's exactly the
discipline Sacramento is missing. I encouraged a culture supportive
of local small businesses and development that brought substantial
economic improvements to our city, and I prioritized public safety
by making sure fire and police had the tools they needed to keep
residents safe, with some of the fastest response times in the
county.
I bring practical, real-world experience to public office. I am a
local small business owner. My experience in building a small
business will help me change the way Sacramento politicians do
their business, by fighting for local small business and working
families.
My priorities are clear: bringing down the cost of living, keeping our
neighborhoods safe, standing up for small businesses, and rooting
out the waste, fraud, and abuse draining our tax dollars.
I live in Placentia with my husband of 25 years and am a proud
parent and grandparent.
I'm running to make Sacramento work for you, not the other way
around. I would be honored to earn your vote for State Senate.
