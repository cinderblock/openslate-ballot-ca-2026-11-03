SEAN PHAYAKAPONG
AGE: 34
Engineer/Yorba Linda Commissioner
Elect Sean to City Council and bring back open, honest, and
accountable leadership!
Yorba Linda’s future depends on leaders who are focused on
long-term success rather than short-term political games. As your
Councilman, I will work collaboratively with residents, businesses,
and city staff to address challenges, seize opportunities, and ensure
our city remains well-managed and financially strong. Through
years of community leadership and volunteer service, I have worked
to bring people together, build consensus, and make thoughtful
decisions that serve the greater good.
As a City Commissioner, Love Yorba Linda Co-Chair, Rotarian, and
Knights of Columbus Officer, I believe strong leadership begins with
listening, being transparent, and making responsible decisions that
reflect the priorities of our residents. I am committed to protecting
taxpayer dollars, maintaining balanced budgets, supporting public
safety, investing wisely in our parks, roads, and infrastructure, and
preserving the high quality of life that our families enjoy.
As a proud Yorba Lindan, I am committed to preserving the safe,
welcoming, and vibrant community that makes our city such a
wonderful place to live. It would be an honor to continue my service
to our community and to help ensure Yorba Linda remains a city we
are proud to call home for generations to come.
