JOHN PARK
City of Irvine Commissioner/Businessowner
Every election, some candidates talk. They promise. They win.
Nothing happens.
Residents still sit in traffic on Culver, worry about fire risk in our
hillsides, and watch Irvine become less affordable. Our city council
chases costly pet projects. Meanwhile the city faces a nearly $50
million deficit in just a few short years. Enough. It is time to get back
to basics.
We have lived in District 1 continuously for 26 years, first in
Northpark and now in Orchard Hills. We are still raising our family
here, with our youngest currently in an Irvine public school. This is
not a campaign stop. This is home.
I serve on the Irvine Finance Commission, having previously served
as its Chairman, and have also served as Planning Commissioner
and Transportation Commissioner. This experience will allow me to
hit the ground running on day one.
This is not the time for nostalgia. We need forward-thinking
leadership ready to tackle today's challenges.
My priorities: Fiscal responsibility, Public safety, Traffic relief,
Fire mitigation, Open spaces and trails, Affordability, Educational
leadership
I am endorsed by Orange County Sheriff Don Barnes and numerous
elected officials who serve Irvine and Orange County.
Back to basics. Finally.
