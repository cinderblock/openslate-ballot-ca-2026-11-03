MARCO CARRALERO
Parent/Orange County Businessowner
I’m not a politician, and I’ve never run for elected office. I’m the
parent of daughters who will enter Capistrano Unified School
District schools when they reach school age. I own a family
business, where I lead, manage budgets, and deliver results. My
professional experience includes advising government agencies on
financial management. As a Laguna Niguel parent, I have a personal
stake in improving Capistrano Unified School District schools.
The Capistrano Unified School District is in crisis:
Student enrollment fell every year for the past eight years
Lowest student enrollment in nearly a quarter-century
Serious structural budget deficit
I’ll fight to reverse this trend and bring families back to Capistrano
Unified School District schools.
As Laguna Niguel’s representative on the Capistrano Unified School
District Board, I’ll:
Fight for a high-quality education for every student
Keep political agendas out of the classroom
Respect parents’ rights
Promote parental involvement
Provide safe, secure, and healthy schools for our students
Demand fiscal accountability and ensure money goes directly to
classrooms, not wasteful bureaucracy
Increase transparency to ensure a strong educational
partnership between parents, teachers, and the Capistrano
Unified School District Board

Zollinger, and Jennifer Adnams

Shaw
