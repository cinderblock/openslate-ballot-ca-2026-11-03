p2 ORDINANCE NO. __
p2 AN ORDINANCE OF PEOPLE OF THE CITY OF LAKE FOREST, CALIFORNIA, AMENDING SECTION 2.04.080 OF THE
p2 LAKE FOREST MUNICIPAL CODE TO CHANGE THE TERMS OF OFFICE ALLOWED FOR CITY COUNCILMEMBERS
p2 FROM TWO TERMS MAXIMUM TO TWO CONSECUTIVE TERMS WITH ADDITIONAL TERMS ELIGIBLE FOLLOWING
p2 A FOUR YEAR BREAK IN SERVICE
p2 THE PEOPLE OF THE CITY OF LAKE FOREST DO ORDAIN AS FOLLOWS:
p2 SECTION 1. Municipal Code Amendment. Section 2.04.080 of the Lake Forest Municipal Code is hereby amended to read as follows:
p2 Sec. 2.04.080 – Term Limits
p2 (a) No person shall be eligible to serve in the office of City Council member or Mayor for more than two consecutive four-year terms. Any
p2 person may hold office as a member of the City Council or Mayor for an additional two consecutive terms if he or she does not hold office
p2 as a member of the City Council or Mayor for four or more consecutive years immediately following the conclusion of each two consecutive
p2 terms of office as a member of the City Council or Mayor. There is no limit to the number of times a person may serve two consecutive terms
p2 with the requisite four year break in service.
p2 (b) If a person serves for at least two years of a term, that person for purposes of subsection (a) of this section shall be considered to have
p2 served a full four-year term.
p2 SECTION 2. Severability. If any section, subsection, subdivision, paragraph, sentence, clause, or phrase added by this Ordinance, or any part thereof, is
p2 for any reason held to be unconstitutional or invalid or ineffective by any court of competent jurisdiction, such decision shall not affect the validity of
p2 effectiveness of the remaining portions of this Ordinance or any part thereof.
p2 SECTION 3. Effective Date. This Ordinance shall become effective only upon adoption by a majority of the people voting on the measure to adopt this
p2 Ordinance at the general municipal election on November 3, 2026. This Ordinance shall be considered as adopted upon the date that the vote on this
p2 Ordinance is declared by the City Council for the City of Lake Forest, and shall go into effect 10 days after that date.
p2 SECTION 4. Execution. The Mayor is hereby authorized to attest to the adoption of this Ordinance by the voters of the City of Lake Forest by signing
p2 where indicated below.
p2 Impartial Analysis
p2 City of Lake Forest
p2 Measure F
p2 Lake Forest Municipal Code Section 2.04.080 currently prohibits any person from serving as a Lake Forest City Council Member or Mayor for more than
p2 two four-year terms. The City’s current term limits have been in place since the passage of Measure S in 2018.
p2 Measure F amends Section 2.04.080 to prohibit any person from serving more than two four-year terms on the Lake Forest City Council unless he or
p2 she does not hold office for four or more consecutive years immediately following the conclusion of each consecutive two terms of office. After the
p2 required four-year break, a person may again serve up to two four-year terms. Each full City Council term is four years. Measure F was placed on the
p2 ballot by the Lake Forest City Council.
p2 If Measure F is approved, the new four-year break between consecutive four-year terms would apply to all new or re-elected City Council Members
p2 beginning with the 2026 election. Adoption of Measure F would not increase the number of consecutive terms available to current City Council
p2 Members, as the current two term limit established by Section 2.04.080 of the Lake Forest Municipal Code will continue to apply. Once a City Council
p2 Member has served two full four-year terms on the City Council, the two-term limit will apply and he or she would no longer be eligible to serve on the
p2 City Council without a four or more consecutive years break in service.
p2 A “Yes” vote on Measure F will approve the Measure and prohibit City Council Members or the Mayor from serving more than two four-year terms
p2 without a four more consecutive years break in service.
p2 A “No” vote on Measure F will not approve the Measure and will prohibit the City Council members or Mayor from serving more than two four-year
p2 terms.
p2 Measure F requires simple majority approval of the voters to pass.
p2 The above statement is an impartial analysis of Measure F. If you desire a copy of the measure, please call the elections official’s office at
p2 (949) 461-3423 and a copy will be mailed at no cost to you.
