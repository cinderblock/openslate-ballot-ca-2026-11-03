p2 AN ORDINANCE OF THE PEOPLE OF THE CITY OF ORANGE AMENDING CHAPTER 5.16 OF THE ORANGE
p2 MUNICIPAL CODE TO UPDATE THE UNIFORM TRANSIENT OCCUPANCY TAX INCLUDING INCREASING THE
p2 TRANSIENT OCCUPANCY TAX PERCENTAGE
p2 WHEREAS, the City of Orange wishes to amend and update Chapter 5.16 of the Orange Municipal Code as it relates to the City’s Uniform
p2 Transient Occupancy Tax including increasing the Transient Occupancy Tax to be imposed from 10% to 14% as specified.

p2 SECTION I:
p2 Chapter 5.16 of the Orange Municipal Code is hereby amended in its entirety to read as follows:
p2 5.16.000. Title
p2 The ordinance codified in this chapter shall be known as the Transient Occupancy Tax Ordinance of the City of Orange.
p2 5.16.010 Administrative Regulations
p2 Unless specifically provided for in the Orange Municipal Code, the City Manager or their designee may adopt reasonable administrative regulations to
p2 further define processes and administration of this Chapter.
p2 5.16.020. Definitions
p2 For purposes of this chapter, the following words, terms, phrases, and the derivations and variants thereof, shall have the meanings given herein:
p2 “Accommodation(s)” means any room or other space in a hotel used for lodging or sleeping purposes, as well as other items or services provided to
p2 a transient as part of or accompanying the room or space upon which rent is charged pursuant to this chapter.
p2 “Accommodations Intermediary” means any person, corporation, entity, trust or partnership (other than an accommodations supplier) that directly
p2 or indirectly (i) facilitates the rental and/or facilitates payment processing of accommodation(s) in a hotel, and (ii) charges, collects or receives rent
p2 in connection with such rental, which may include, without limitation, a facilitation fee. “Accommodations intermediary” includes, without limitation,
p2 a travel or booking agent, a room seller or reseller, an on-line room seller or reseller, and an on-line travel agent or company of any type or nature
p2 whatsoever, but does not include employees of an accommodations intermediary or accommodations supplier.
p2 “Accommodations Supplier” means any person, corporation, entity, trust or partnership which operates or provides accommodations in a hotel,
p2 whether in the capacity of owner, proprietor, lessee, sublessee, managing agent, mortgagee in possession, debtor in possession, licensee or any other
p2 capacity, other than as an employee of an accommodations intermediary or accommodations supplier. “Accommodations supplier” does not mean an
p2 accommodations intermediary.
p2 “Orange” or “City” means the City of Orange.
p2 “Discount Room Charge” means the total consideration charged by an accommodations supplier to an accommodations intermediary, or an affiliate
p2 thereof, for furnishing accommodation(s) in a hotel.
p2 “Facilitation Fee”means the total consideration charged by an accommodations intermediary to a transient for accommodation(s), minus the discount
p2 room charge, if any, provided that the facilitation fee shall not be less than $0.
p2 “Fiscal Year” means the period commencing July 1 of one calendar year through June 30 of the immediately subsequent calendar year.
p2 “Occupancy” means the use or possession, or the right to the use or possession, of any room or rooms or other living space, or portion thereof, in any
p2 hotel for dwelling, lodging or sleeping purposes.
p2 “Hotel”means any structure or portion thereof, which is occupied by persons for lodging or sleeping purposes for periods of less than thirty consecutive
p2 days including, without limitation, any hotel, bachelor hotel, motel, lodging house, rooming house, bed and breakfast inn, short-term rental, apartment
p2 house, dormitory, vacation ownership resort, public or private club, mobilehome or house trailer at a fixed location, or other similar structure or portion
p2 thereof, and any space, lot, area or site in any trailer court, camp, park, or lot which is occupied or intended or designed for occupancy by a tent, trailer,
p2 recreational vehicle, mobilehome, motorhome, or other similar conveyance, where such structure, space, lot, area or site is occupied by persons for
p2 lodging or sleeping purposes for periods of less than thirty consecutive days.
p2 “Operator” means an accommodations supplier or accommodations intermediary. For purposes of the notice and appeal provisions of this chapter
p2 only, “operator” shall also include any managing employee or employee of an accommodations supplier or accommodations intermediary.
p2 “Qualifying Rental Agreement” means and is limited to a written contract signed by both the landlord and tenant, legally enforceable by either party,
p2 for a rental period of not less than thirty consecutive days. “Qualifying Rental Agreement” shall expressly exclude: (1) any agreement regardless of
p2 length of the rental term which is terminated for any reason by either party or by mutual consent prior to the thirtieth consecutive day of the tenancy,
p2 or (2) any agreement regardless of the length of the rental term which is for occupancy of lodging or sleeping space which is not the legal residence or
p2 principal dwelling place of the occupant, or (3) any agreement which would be unlawful or constitute a violation of law.
p2 “Rent” means the total consideration charged by an operator for accommodation(s) in a hotel, without any deduction therefrom whatsoever, including
p2 without limitation the discount room charge and/or facilitation fee, if any, as well as any (1) transaction fees, service fees, booking fees, processing
p2 fees, retail markups, commissions, cancellation fees, California Tourism Marketing Assessments, unrefunded advance reservation or other rental
p3 deposits, (2) separate charges levied for items or services which are part of or accompany such accommodations including, but not limited to, charges
p3 for overnight parking, furniture, fixtures, appliances, linens, towels, spa or fitness center usage or access, resort usage or access (commonly referred
p3 to as resort or destination fees), internet access, housekeeping or room cleaning, pet occupancy or pet-related cleaning, additional guests/transients,
p3 and/or partial days or early or late arrival or departure, (3) consideration or value received by an operator from an award or reward program, including
p3 the redemption of award/reward points, incentives or bonuses, or (4) charges reasonably attributable to any of the foregoing taxable items that are
p3 part of a special package that includes accommodation(s).
p3 “Special Package and Special Package Rate” means room accommodations and beverage services or room accommodations and at least one other
p3 benefit having an ascertainable fair market value offered for one fixed charge.
p3 “Short-term Rental” means a residential dwelling or a portion thereof that is rented by an operator to another person or group of persons for
p3 occupancy, dwelling, lodging or sleeping purposes for a period of less than thirty (30) consecutive calendar days. For purposes of this definition,
p3 residential dwelling means a building, or portion thereof, designed exclusively for residential purposes, including single-family and multiple-family
p3 dwellings.
p3 “Tax” means the amounts imposed pursuant to Section 5.16.030 of this chapter.
p3 “Tax Administrator” means the City Manager or their designee.
p3 “Transient” means any person who exercises occupancy, or is entitled to occupancy, of any room, space, lot, area or site in any hotel by reason of
p3 concession, permit, right of access, license or other agreement whether written or oral. Any such person shall be deemed to be a transient until the
p3 thirtieth consecutive day (counting partial days as full days) of such occupancy or right of occupancy, and the tax imposed by this Chapter shall be due
p3 upon all rent collected or accruing prior to said thirtieth consecutive day unless the occupancy is pursuant to a Qualifying Rental Agreement.
p3 5.16.030. Tax Imposed
p3 For the privilege of occupancy of space in any hotel with 11 accommodations or more, each transient is subject to and shall pay a tax in the amount
p3 of 14% of the rent.
p3 For the privilege of occupancy of space in any hotel with 10 accommodations or fewer, each transient is subject to and shall pay a tax in the amount
p3 of 10% of the rent.
p3 Accommodations intermediaries and accommodations suppliers shall be jointly and severally liable for collection and remittance of taxes imposed by
p3 this Chapter.
p3 5.16.040. Exemptions.
p3 No tax shall be imposed upon any person as to whom, or any occupancy as to which, it is beyond the power of Orange to impose the tax.
p3 5.16.050. Operator’s Duties.
p3 Each operator shall collect the tax imposed by this chapter to the same extent and at the same time as the rent is collected from every other transient.
p3 The amount of tax shall be separately stated from the amount of the rent charged, and each transient shall receive a receipt for payment from the
p3 operator. No operator of a hotel shall advertise or state in any manner, whether directly or indirectly, that the tax or any part thereof will be assumed
p3 or absorbed by the operator, or that it will not be added to the rent, or that, if added, any part will be refunded except in the manner provided in this
p3 chapter.
p3 5.16.060. Registration.
p3 Within 30 days after the effective date of the ordinance codified in this chapter, or within 30 days after commencing business, whichever is later,
p3 each operator of any hotel renting occupancy to transients shall register the hotel with the Tax Administrator and obtain from him or her a “Transient
p3 Occupancy Registration Certificate” to be at all times posted in a conspicuous place on the premises. Said certificate shall, among other things, state
p3 the following:
p3 A.
p3 The name of the operator;
p3 B.
p3 The address of the hotel;
p3 C.
p3 The date upon which the certificate was issued;
p3 D.
p3 “This Transient Occupancy Registration Certificate signifies that the person named on the face hereof has fulfilled the requirements of the
p3 Transient Occupancy Tax Ordinance by registering with the Tax Administrator for the purpose of collecting from transients the Transient
p3 Occupancy Tax and remitting said tax to the Tax Administrator. This certificate does not authorize any person to conduct any unlawful business
p3 or to conduct any lawful business in any unlawful manner, nor to operate a hotel without strictly complying with all local applicable laws,
p3 including but not limited to, those requiring a permit from any board, commission, department or office of this City. This certificate does not
p3 constitute a permit.”
p3 5.16.070 Reporting and Remitting.
p3 Each operator shall, on or before the last day of the month following the close of each calendar quarter, or at the close of any shorter reporting period
p3 which may be established by the Tax Administrator, make a return to the Tax Administrator, on forms provided by them, of the total rents charged and
p3 received and the amount of tax collected for transient occupancies. At the time the return is filed, the full amount of the tax collected shall be remitted
p3 to the Tax Administrator. The Tax Administrator may establish shorter reporting periods for any certificate holder if they deem it necessary in order
p3 to insure collection of the tax, and they may require further information in the return. Returns and payments are due immediately upon cessation of
p3 business for any reason. All taxes collected by operators pursuant to this chapter shall be held in trust for the account of the City until payment thereof
p3 is made to the Tax Administrator.
p4 5.16.080. Penalties and Interest.
p4 A.
p4 Original Delinquency. Any operator who fails to remit any tax imposed by this chapter within the time required shall pay a penalty of 10% of the
p4 amount of the tax in addition to the amount of the tax.
p4 Continued Delinquency. Any operator who fails to remit any delinquent remittance on or before a period of 30 days following the date on which
p4 B.
p4 the remittance first became delinquent shall pay a second delinquency penalty of 10% of the amount of the tax in addition to the amount of
p4 the tax and the 10% penalty first imposed.
p4 C.
p4 Fraud. If the Tax Administrator determines that the nonpayment of any remittance due under this chapter is due to fraud, a penalty of 25% of
p4 the amount of the tax shall be added thereto in addition to the penalties stated in subsections A and B of this section.
p4 D.
p4 Interest. In addition to the penalties imposed, any operator who fails to remit any tax imposed by this chapter shall pay interest at the rate of
p4 one-half of one percent per month or fraction thereof on the amount of the tax, exclusive of penalties, from the date on which the remittance
p4 first became delinquent, until paid.
p4 E.
p4 Penalties Merged with Tax. Every penalty imposed and such interest as accrues under the provisions of this section shall become a part of the
p4 tax herein required to be paid.
p4 5.16.090. Failure to Collect and Report Tax—Determination of Tax by Tax Administrator.
p4 If any operator fails or refuses to collect the tax and to make, within the time provided in this chapter, any report and remittance of the tax or any portion
p4 thereof required by this chapter, the Tax Administrator shall proceed in such manner as he or she may deem best to obtain facts and information on
p4 which to base his or her estimate of the tax due. As soon as the Tax Administrator shall procure such facts and information as he or she is able to obtain
p4 upon which to base the assessment of any tax imposed by this chapter and payable by any operator who has failed or refused to collect the same and
p4 to make such report and remittance, he or she shall proceed to determine and assess against such operator the tax, interest and penalties provided
p4 for by this chapter. In case such determination is made, the Tax Administrator shall give a notice of the amount so assessed by serving it personally
p4 or by depositing it in the United States mail, postage prepaid, addressed to the operator so assessed at his or her last known place of address. Such
p4 operator may within 10 days after the serving or mailing of such notice make application in writing to the Tax Administrator for a hearing on the amount
p4 assessed. If application by the operator for a hearing is not made within the time prescribed, the tax, interest and penalties, if any, determined by the
p4 Tax Administrator shall become final and conclusive and immediately due and payable. If such application is made, the Tax Administrator shall give
p4 not less than five days’ written notice in the manner prescribed herein to the operator to show cause at a time and place fixed in the notice why the
p4 amount specified therein should not be fixed for such tax, interest and penalties. At such hearing, the operator may appear and offer evidence why
p4 such specified tax, interest and penalties should not be so fixed. After such hearing, the Tax Administrator shall determine the proper tax to be remitted
p4 and shall thereafter give written notice to the person in the manner prescribed herein of such determination and the amount of such tax, interest and
p4 penalties. The amount determined to be due shall be payable after 15 days unless an appeal is taken as provided in Section 5.16.100.
p4 5.16.100. Appeal—Hearing.
p4 Any operator aggrieved by any decision of the Tax Administrator with respect to the amount of such tax, interest and penalties, if any, may appeal to
p4 the Council by filing a notice of appeal with the City Clerk within 15 days of the serving or mailing of the determination of tax due. The Council shall
p4 fix a time and place for hearing such appeal, and the City Clerk shall give notice in writing to such operator, at his or her last known place of address.
p4 The findings of the Council shall be final and conclusive and shall be served upon the appellant in the manner prescribed above for service of notice
p4 of hearing. Any amount found to be due shall be immediately due and payable upon the service of notice.
p4 5.16.110. Operator to Keep Records.
p4 It shall be the duty of every operator liable for the collection and payment to the City of any tax imposed by this chapter to keep and preserve, for a
p4 period of three years, all records as may be necessary to determine the amount of such tax as he or she may have been liable for the collection of and
p4 payment to the City, which records the Tax Administrator shall have the right to inspect at all reasonable times.
p4 5.16.120. Refunds.
p4 A.
p4 Whenever the amount of any tax, interest or penalty has been overpaid or paid more than once or has been erroneously or illegally collected or
p4 received by the City under this chapter, it may be refunded as provided in subsections B and C of this section provided a claim in writing therefor,
p4 stating under penalty of perjury the specific grounds upon which the claim is founded, is filed with the Tax Administrator within three years of
p4 the day of payment. The claim shall be on forms furnished by the Tax Administrator.
p4 B.
p4 An operator may claim a refund or take as credit against taxes collected and remitted the amount overpaid, paid more than once or erroneously
p4 or illegally collected or received when it is established in a manner prescribed by the Tax Administrator that the person from whom the tax
p4 has been collected was not a transient; provided, however, that neither a refund nor a credit shall be allowed unless the amount of the tax so
p4 collected has either been refunded to the transient or credited to rent subsequently payable by the transient to the operator.
p4 C.
p4 A transient may obtain a refund of taxes overpaid or paid more than once or erroneously or illegally collected or received by the City by filing a
p4 claim in the manner provided in subsection A of this section, but only when the tax was paid by the transient directly to the Tax Administrator,
p4 or when the transient having paid the tax to the operator, establishes to the satisfaction of the Tax Administrator that the transient has been
p4 unable to obtain a refund from the operator who collected the tax.
p4 D.
p4 No refund shall be paid under the provisions of this section unless the claimant establishes his or her right thereto by written records showing
p4 entitlement thereto.
p4 5.16.130. Actions to Collect.
p4 Any tax required to be paid by any transient under the provisions of this chapter shall be deemed a debt owed by the transient to the City. Any such tax
p5 collected by an operator which has not been paid to the City shall be deemed a debt owed by the operator to the City. Any person owing money to the
p5 City under the provisions of this chapter shall be liable to an action brought in the name of the City for the recovery of such amount.
p5 5.16.140. Funds Deposited into Special Fund.
p5 The tax collected by the City, imposed under Section 5.16.030, shall be deposited into the general fund. Funds expended for advertising and promotional
p5 purposes shall be paid or transferred from the general fund.

p5 Impartial Analysis
p5 City of Orange
p5 Measure J
p5 The City Council of the City of Orange is submitting Measure J to the voters for consideration at the November 3, 2026 General Municipal Election. If
p5 approved by a majority of the voters, Measure J would amend Chapter 5.16 of the Orange Municipal Code to update the City’s Transient Occupancy Tax
p5 (“TOT”), commonly referred to as the hotel guest tax. The TOT is imposed on the occupancy of hotel accommodations and is paid by hotel guests rather
p5 than by hotel operators. The ordinance would continue to require operators to collect and remit the tax to the City.
p5 The measure would increase the TOT rate from 10 percent to 14 percent for hotels with eleven (11) or more accommodations. Hotels with ten (10) or
p5 fewer accommodations would continue to be subject to the existing 10 percent tax rate.
p5 The ordinance would also update the City’s TOT definitions and apply the tax to the full amount charged to guests for accommodations, including
p5 amounts collected by accommodations intermediaries such as online travel or booking companies, as provided in the ordinance.
p5 Revenue generated by the measure would continue to be deposited into the City’s General Fund and may be used for any lawful municipal purpose. The
p5 ordinance does not dedicate revenues to any specific purpose or program. Revenues may be used for City services, including public safety, emergency
p5 response, street and infrastructure maintenance, parks, libraries, and other municipal services.
p5 The City estimates that the measure would generate approximately $3 million annually, although actual revenues will depend on hotel occupancy,
p5 room rates, and related taxable charges.
p5 A “Yes” vote on Measure J authorizes the City to amend the Transient Occupancy Tax by increasing the tax rate from 10 percent to 14 percent for
p5 hotels with eleven (11) or more accommodations, applying the tax online and other travel companies, and updating the tax to apply to the full amount
p5 charged to hotel guests, as provided in the ordinance.
p5 A “No” vote on Measure J does not authorize those changes to the City’s Transient Occupancy Tax.
