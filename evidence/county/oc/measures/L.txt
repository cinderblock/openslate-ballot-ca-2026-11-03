p2 City of Placentia
p2 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF PLACENTIA, CALIFORNIA, AMENDING SECTION 5.08.500 OF THE MUNICIPAL
p2 CODE TO CHANGE THE RATE OF THE TRANSIENT OCCUPANCY TAX AND ADDING SECTION 3.20.100 TO THE
p2 MUNICIPAL CODE TO REQUIRE CERTAIN ACCOUNTABILITY MEASURES
p2 WHEREAS, Section 3.20.020 of the Placentia Municipal Code requires that, for the privilege of occupancy in any hotel, each transient is subject
p2 to and shall pay a transient occupancy tax at the rate set forth in Section 5.08.500 of the Municipal Code; and
p2 WHEREAS, the transient occupancy tax is collected by the hotel operator from the transient and then remitted by the operator to the City; and
p2 WHEREAS, the transient occupancy tax is governed by Chapter 3.20 of the Municipal Code; and
p2 WHEREAS, Section 3.20.010 of the Municipal Code provides that “transient” means any person who exercises occupancy or is entitled to
p2 occupancy by reason of concession, permit, right of access, license or other agreement for a period of 30 consecutive calendar days or less, counting
p2 portions of calendar days as full days and that any such person so occupying space in a hotel shall be deemed to be a transient until the period of
p2 30 days has expired unless there is an agreement in writing between the operator and the occupant providing for a longer period of occupancy; and
p2 WHEREAS, Section 3.20.010 of the Municipal Code provides that “hotel” means any structure, or any portion of any structure, which is occupied
p2 or intended or designed for occupancy by transients for dwelling, lodging or sleeping purposes, and includes any hotel, inn, tourist home or house,
p2 motel, studio motel, bachelor hotel, lodging house, rooming house, apartment house, dormitory, public or private club, mobile home or house trailer at
p2 a fixed location, or other similar structure or portion thereof; and
p2 WHEREAS, Section 3.20.010 of the Municipal Code provides that “rent” means the consideration charged, whether or not received, for the
p2 occupancy of space in a hotel valued in money, whether to be received in money, goods, labor or otherwise, including all receipts, cash, credits and
p2 property and services of any kind or nature, without any deduction therefrom whatsoever; and
p2 WHEREAS, the rate of the Transient Occupancy Tax is currently 10%; and
p2 WHEREAS, the People of the City of Placentia desire to increase the rate of the tax to 14%, and to adopt certain accountability measures.
p2 NOW, THEREFORE, THE PEOPLE OF THE CITY OF PLACENTIA DO HEREBY ORDAIN AS FOLLOWS:
p2 SECTION 1. Section 5.08.500 of Chapter 5.08 (Finance Department—Business Licenses) of the Municipal Code is hereby amended to read:
p2 § 5.08.500 Transient Occupancy Tax
p2 The rate of the transient occupancy tax imposed by Section 3.20.020 of this Code is 14% of the rent charged by the operator.
p2 SECTION 2. Section 3.20.100 is added to Chapter 3.20 (Transient Occupancy Tax) of the Municipal Code to read as follows:
p2 §3.20.100 Accountability
p2 A. The proceeds of the transient occupancy tax as well as the expenditure thereof, shall be audited annually by an independent accounting firm.
p2 Such independent audit may be a part of any other independent audit of municipal finances. The report of the audit shall be filed with the City
p2 Council and shall be a public record of the City.
p2 B. The citizen’s oversight committee established by Section 3.32.020 of this code shall, in addition to its other duties, review the expenditure
p2 of funds generated by the transient occupancy tax. It shall prepare an annual report on such expenditures which shall be presented to the
p2 city council and shall also be published on the city’s webpage. Unless otherwise directed by resolution or ordinance of the city council, or
p2 by Chapter 3.32 or other provision of this code, the committee shall not have any function, power, or authority other than those expressly
p2 provided in this section. In no event shall a failure of the city council or the committee to meet, or to take any other action required by this
p2 section, invalidate any tax imposed or collected under this chapter; however, any court of competent jurisdiction may issue an order to the city
p2 compelling the city to comply with the requirements of this section.
p2 C. The proceeds of the transient occupancy tax shall be deposited in the general fund of the city and may be used for any lawful municipal
p2 purpose. The tax is a general tax imposed for general government purposes.
p2 SECTION 3. The City Council may, by ordinance, amend this Ordinance. However, the City Council shall not increase the rate of the tax imposed by
p2 this Ordinance above the rate set forth in this Ordinance without the approval of the increase by a vote of the Electorate.
p2 SECTION 4. If any section, subsection, sentence, clause, or phrase of this Ordinance is held invalid, such decision shall not affect the validity of the
p2 remaining provisions of this Ordinance.
p2 SECTION 5. This Ordinance shall take effect on January 1, 2027.
p3 Impartial Analysis
p3 City of Placentia
p3 Measure L
p3 The City of Placentia currently imposes a 10% transient occupancy tax on transients (hotel guests) who occupy a hotel room for 30 consecutive days or
p3 less. This tax is commonly referred to as the City’s “Hotel Tax.” It is collected by the hotel operator (or their agent) from the guest based on the “rent”
p3 charged for lodging. The hotel operator then remits the collected tax to the City.
p3 For purposes of the Hotel Tax, “hotel” is broadly defined to include “any structure, or any portion of any structure, which is occupied or intended or
p3 designed for occupancy by transients for dwelling, lodging or sleeping purposes.” Thus, short-term rentals of homes or of rooms in homes would be
p3 subject to the tax.
p3 Proceeds of the Hotel Tax are deposited in the general fund and available for any lawful municipal purpose. Examples of services funded through the
p3 general fund include neighborhood police patrols, fire protection, 911 response, and road repairs. The City’s accounts, including the general fund, are
p3 audited annually by an independent auditor.
p3 Measure L seeks voter approval of an ordinance that increases the rate of the Hotel Tax to 14%. The ordinance requires that, in addition to independent
p3 annual audits of tax proceeds and expenditures, the City’s existing Citizens’ Oversight Committee review expenditures of funds generated by the Hotel
p3 Tax and prepare an annual report of such expenditures.
p3 The measure was placed on the ballot by the City Council of the City of Placentia. To be adopted, this measure requires the approval of a majority of
p3 the voters casting ballots.
p3 A YES vote approves the ordinance increasing the rate of the Hotel Tax to 14%.
p3 A NO vote rejects the ordinance, and would leave the Hotel Tax at 10%.
p3 The above statement is an impartial analysis of Measure L . If you desire a copy of the measure, please call the elections official’s office at
p3 (714) 993-8231 and a copy will be mailed at no cost to you.

p4 Argument in Favor of Measure L
p4 Visitors Pay Their Fair Share, Residents Benefit.
p4 Vote YES on Placentia’s Hotel Tax Measure to protect essential city
p4 services, keep local dollars in Placentia, and ensure visitors pay their fair
p4 share. Visitors Pay, Residents Benefit.
p4 Placentia’s hotel tax is paid only by guests who stay in local hotels,
p4 motels, and short-term rentals. It is not a tax on Placentia residents’
p4 property, income, or everyday expenses.
p4 This measure is a fair, common-sense way to increase revenue without
p4 raising taxes on local residents. When visitors use Placentia’s roads,
p4 public safety services, parks, and infrastructure, they should help pay to
p4 maintain them.
p4 Placentia’s current hotel tax rate has not kept pace with inflation
p4 and is below other Orange County rates. Updating Placentia’s rate to
p4 the proposed 14% will keep Placentia competitive for tourism while
p4 generating approximately $715,000 each year with every dollar staying
p4 in Placentia.
p4 A YES vote also supports transparency and accountability. A Citizens’
p4 Oversight Committee will prepare annual audits to ensure funds are used
p4 to maintain the city’s infrastructure, including roads and parks; build fiscal
p4 reserves; and support public safety.
p4 The City continues to work hard to improve Placentia’s finances,
p4 invest in public safety, and maintain neighborhood services despite
p4 continuing cost increases. Without additional local revenue, the City
p4 faces harder choices in the years ahead about what services can be
p4 maintained.
p4 This measure helps Placentia control its future because the money
p4 stays local. It supports only local needs to protect the quality of life that
p4 makes Placentia a safe, family-friendly community.
p4 Please join residents, community leaders, and local taxpayers in voting
p4 YES on Placentia’s Hotel Tax Measure.
