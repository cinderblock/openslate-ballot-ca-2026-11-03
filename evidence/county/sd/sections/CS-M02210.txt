p55 PADRE DAM MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICT
p55 Member of the Board of Directors Division No. 1
p55 BRIAN FORDYCE
p55 Small Bussines Owner
p55 Santee has been my home for over 35 years. My wife and I raised our
p55 family here, serve in our church, and built Fordyce Construction, a
p55 company focused on improving local schools and public infrastructure.
p55 That’s the experience and commitment I’ve brought to the Padre Dam
p55 Board since 2022.
p55 In my first term, Padre Dam’s internal operating costs did not require a
p55 rate increase on our end. I’m committed to that same fiscal discipline going
p55 forward.
p55 I believe in maintaining infrastructure before it fails to protect reliability and
p55 avoid costly emergencies. I also support investing in local water
p55 independence through the East County Advanced Water Purification
p55 Program, which will provide a new, more reliable local supply. This will
p55 reduce our dependence on imported water and help stabilize the cost of
p55 our water service over time.
p55 Water management in our region is becoming more complex — volatile
p55 weather, rising imported water prices, changing state regulations, aging
p55 infrastructure, and major projects like the Advanced Water Purification
p55 Program. There’s no substitute for experience when you’re balancing
p55 reliability, costs, and long term obligations.
p55 I’d be honored to continue serving every Santee ratepayer with steady,
p55 commonsense leadership.
p55 WILLIAM WOODS
p55 Retired Law Enforcement
p55 East County is my home. I was raised here, built my life here, and raised
p55 my family here.
p55 For 32 years, I served in law enforcement, protecting the public,
p55 managing responsibilities, and earning trust through accountable
p55 leadership.
p55 I am running for the Padre Dam Municipal Water District Board of
p55 Directors because safe, reliable, affordable water is essential to every
p55 household and business. Families are already stretched by rising costs.
p55 I will oppose non-essential internal rate increases, examine every
p55 expenditure, and fight to ensure customers pay only what is necessary
p55 to provide dependable service. Every dollar must be spent responsibly
p55 and transparently.
p55 My law enforcement experience taught me to identify risks, prepare for
p55 emergencies, protect critical systems, and never take public safety for
p55 granted. I will make the security of our water supply and infrastructure a
p55 priority, including emergency planning and protection against physical
p55 and cyber threats.
p55 I will also support long-term planning that expands locally controlled water
p55 supplies, reduces dependence on imported water, and prepares East
p55 County for drought, emergencies, and growth.
p55 I am not a career politician. I am a public servant, husband, father, and
p55 grandfather who will protect your water, your safety, and your wallet.
