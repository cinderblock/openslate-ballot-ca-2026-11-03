p55 ROBERT ATKINS
p55 Retired Agric Comm, Sealer SDCO
p55 My wife, Anne, and I have lived in Fallbrook for 18 years. We have citrus
p55 and fig orchards and are supporters of Ag in the Classroom and FFA. I have
p55 a Master of Public Administration and am a graduate of CA Ag Leadership
p55 Program.
p55 I’m a retired SD Co Agricultural Commissioner so I am familiar with
p55 balancing budgets, managing a diverse array of programs and negotiating
p55 the laws. Also, I served 3 years as the Malibu Zone Weed and Brush
p55 Abatement Inspector, so I have personal experience with Fire, and Health &
p55 Safety Care Codes.
p55 The Rice Canyon fire burned across our farm, but fortunately, before we built
p55 our home. The second shock was when insurance costs increased 5-fold.
p55 Cal Fair Plan costs were still double the original. The insurance companies
p55 and banks won’t lose money, so we must find ways to reduce our losses and
p55 their monetary risk.
p55 Working with the Fire Department and the insurers we can better protect our
p55 homes from devastating fires. Together we can incentivize fire safety and
p55 prevention and convince the insurers to reduce our rates.

p55 Businesswoman/Gemologist
p55 Fallbrook and Rainbow are extraordinary communities, but we also face
p55 extraordinary wildfire risk. Protecting our families, homes, businesses, and
p55 firefighters requires thoughtful planning, fiscal responsibility, and leaders
p55 who prepare before disaster strikes. I am running for the North County Fire
p55 Protection District Board because our community needs leaders who plan
p55 ahead, ask tough questions, and make decisions based on facts.

p55 studied the natural systems that influence wildfire. My commitment to public
p55 service led me to the Davenport Institute’s Local Government and
p55 Community Program at Pepperdine University’s School of Public Policy. As
p55 a business owner with 18 years of community leadership and service,
p55 including serving on oversight boards for two voter-approved community
p55 college bond measures and leading nonprofit boards, I understand budgets,
p55 accountability, and delivering results.
p55 As your representative, I will prioritize responsible budgeting, emergency
p55 preparedness, evacuation planning, and investments that keep our
p55 firefighters equipped and our residents safe.
p55 I will bring scientific understanding of wildfire risk, practical business
p55 experience, and a proven record of leadership to prepare our fire district for
p55 the challenges ahead. I would be honored to earn your trust, your support,
