p54 CITY OF SOLANA BEACH
p54 Member of the City Council District No. 4
p54 JENNA WOLFE-DELLAMANO
p54 Business Owner / Director of Non-Profit
p54 I’ve lived in Solana Beach for more than 10 years and never thought I would
p54 run for Council. However, I am genuinely concerned about several
p54 upcoming major development projects that will impact eastside Solana
p54 Beach.
p54 High density redevelopment is proposed for the Vons Shopping Center.
p54 There is also concern regarding potential development of the Senior Living
p54 Facility on Genevieve Avenue and the Executive golf course for dense
p54 housing. These projects can harm the livability of our eastside
p54 neighborhoods and overwhelm City infrastructure, unless we are engaged.
p54 I am committed to advocating for livable neighborhoods, ensuring projects
p54 are designed to be compatible with and fit our neighborhoods, and
p54 addressing negative impacts.
p54 Many residents know me through my work as founder and director of
p54 “Solana Beach Backpacks for Kids” and the “Solana Beach Free Farmers
p54 Market”. I launched this nonprofit during the pandemic to help local families
p54 experiencing food insecurity. My home-based effort has grown into a
p54 completely free farmers market every Sunday at La Colonia Park. Through
p54 partnerships with local farms, grocery stores, and schools, we successfully
p54 recover and distribute thousands of pounds of fresh food every week.
p54 Prior to this, I earned my MBA degree from Northwest Missouri State
p54 University, and for more than 20 years I had a career with major financial
p54 institutions. I worked with Midwest regional banks auditing business loan
p54 portfolios to ensure the banks were fiscally healthy. For over 10 years, I
p54 worked for Union Bank as a VP. I managed investment funds for cities,
p54 counties, and states, and spent several years investing money from bond
p54 proceeds for state and local governments.
p54 My family chose to live in Solana Beach because of its small beach-town
p54 character. Our two sons attend school here. I own a business in Solana
p54 Beach. I understand firsthand the opportunities and challenges facing our
p54 local business community. I have been actively involved in our community
p54 for years, and I truly appreciate what makes Solana Beach a great place to
p54 live and work.
p54 I am fiscally conservative and independent. I support a vibrant business
p54 community.
p54 I believe residents should have the final say in how our neighborhoods
p54 develop, not Sacramento. I support responsible growth and working to
p54 ensure that development fits our community. I’m focused on maintaining our
p54 small town character and beach feel.
p54 As your councilmember, my priority will be to preserve and enhance our
p54 quality of life and what we love about our City, while we responsibly develop.
p54 I would sincerely appreciate your vote.
