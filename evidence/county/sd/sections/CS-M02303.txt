p56 SARAH TELAHUN
p56 Registered Nurse, Kaiser
p56 Sarah Telahun is a Navy veteran and experienced registered nurse who has
p56 spent nearly two decades caring for patients and families throughout San
p56 Diego County. For the past 17 years, she has worked as a labor and delivery
p56 nurse in both military and civilian healthcare settings, helping mothers and
p56 babies through some of life’s most important moments. Sarah is committed
p56 to strengthening access to quality healthcare and supporting the healthcare
p56 heroes who serve our community every day. Her experience across a wide
p56 range of healthcare environments has given her a deep understanding of
p56 patient care, teamwork, and the challenges facing local healthcare systems.
p56 Beyond her nursing career, Sarah has remained actively involved in the
p56 community through volunteer work at local schools and leadership
p56 involvement with youth athletics programs. She is also continuing her
p56 education at the University of San Diego, pursuing a dual master’s degree
p56 in healthcare analytics and informatics. Sarah is a devoted wife and mother
p56 of three who values family, service, and community. She believes strong
p56 healthcare systems are built through collaboration, compassion, and trust,
p56 and she hopes to bring a thoughtful, community centered perspective to the
p56 Palomar Health District Board.
