p56 MEASURE A
p56 A TRANSPARENT, ACCOUNTABLE, MODERN COUNTY GOVERNMENT
p56 Shall the San Diego County Charter be amended to establish an Independent
p56 Ethics Commission, Independent Budget Analyst, Independent Program Auditor,
p56 confirmation of certain senior County leaders, and optional Board of Supervisors
p56 appointment of the Public Defender; increase term limits from two to three terms
p56 for the Board of Supervisors members; require public disclosure of spending and
p56 performance data; clarify non-interference provisions; and update terminology and
p56 structure?
p56 This measure requires Simple Majority (50%+1) of those voting on the measure.
p56 COUNTY COUNSEL IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS
p56 BALLOT MEASURE TO AMEND COUNTY OF SAN DIEGO CHARTER
p56 The Board of Supervisors (“Board”) for the County of San Diego
p56 (“County”) placed this measure on the ballot. If approved by a majority of the
p56 votes cast, this measure will amend the County Charter (“Charter”) as
p56 specified in full text of the ballot measure.
p56 The Charter provides for the structure of the County’s government,
p56 including the powers and duties of its officers. The Charter can only be
p56 amended with voter approval.
p56 The following summarizes the amendments to the Charter proposed by
p56 this measure:
p56 •
p56 Increase County Supervisors’ term limits from two terms to three
p56 terms. This amendment would allow a person to serve in the role of
p56 County Supervisor for a maximum of twelve years, as opposed to
p56 the current maximum of eight years. Any current or former
p56 Supervisor’s prior time in office would count towards this three-term
p56 limit.
p56 •
p56 Require the Board to establish a procedure to review and confirm
p56 Chief Administrative Officer appointment of the Assistant Chief
p56 Administrative Officer, Deputy Chief Administrative Officers,
p56 Director of the Office of Emergency Services, Public Health Officer,
p56 and Public Defender. The Board shall have the power to remove
p56 such officers, with or without cause, by 4/5ths vote.
p56 •
p56 The Board may appoint an Independent Budget Analyst and
p56 Independent Program Auditor. These new positions would perform
p56 specified audit and budget services as directed by the Board or, for
p56 the Independent Budget Analyst a County Supervisor. The Board
p56 may appoint one person to serve in both positions.
p56 •
p56 The Board may change Public Defender to a Board appointment
p56 rather than Chief Administrative Officer appointment and may
p56 appoint additional officers that do not duplicate the Chief
p56 Administrative Officer’s functions.
p56 •
p56 Provides examples of permissible practices that County Supervisors
p56 may engage in without violating the non-interference clause.
p56 •
p56 Update Charter to state that no County Officer may influence the
p56 Director of Purchasing and Contracting for illegal personal gain or
p56 as prohibited by Charter’s non-interference clause. Currently, the
p56 Charter provides that no County Officer may influence Purchasing
p56 Agent in performance of duty and that Board may only conduct
p56 official business with Purchasing Agent at a regular Board meeting.
p56 •
p56 Change Citizen Advisory Board to Community Advisory Board and
p56 update appointment criteria.
p56 •
p56 Direct Board to establish an Ethics Commission of seven
p56 commissioners to be confirmed by the Board; one appointed by
p56 each County Supervisor and two by County Counsel.
p56 Commissioners are subject to removal, term limits, and certain
p56 qualifications as specified in the full measure text and may be
p56 granted subpoena power.
p56 •
p56 Require the Auditor and Controller to post all County spending data
p56 to the County’s website and order an annual independent financial
p56 and performance audit of County expenditures, made available to
p56 the public.
p56 •
p56 Clarify that regulation of political activities does not prohibit activities
p56 allowed by law.
p56 •
p56 Update prohibition against nepotism by specifying degree of
p56 relation.
p56 •
p56 Updates Charter structure and terminology.
p56 A “yes” vote is a vote in favor of the proposed Charter amendments.
p56 A “no” vote is a vote against amending the Charter.

p69 contracts. If the required disclosure is not complete and accurate,
p69 the contract will be voidable. This Charter section is intended to

p69 requiring timely public disclosure of the gifts and campaign
p69 contributions from contractors and their registered lobbyists when
p69 service contracts are being considered by the Board. This section is
p69 also intended to allow contracting to be conducted outside of the
p69 political arena.
p69 (b) Disclosure. Any person or entity whose service contract is to be
p69 considered by the Board of Supervisors for approval, amendment,

p69 considered for approval, amendment, or extension by the Board of
p69 Supervisors, and reference to “registered lobbyist” shall mean any
p69 person who is registered as a lobbyist with the County of San
p69 Diego. At least four (4) calendar days before the day of the meeting
p69 of the Board of Supervisors at which the approval, amendment or
p69 extension of the service contract is to be considered by the Board,
p69 the contractors and their registered lobbyists shall disclose in writing
p69 the following:
p69 (1) All gifts that are reportable pursuant to the provisions of the
p69 Political Reform Act of 1974, as amended, given by
p69 contractors and their registered lobbyists to any member of
p69 the Board of Supervisors or to the immediate family of such
p69 member during the year preceding the date of the disclosure.
p69 (2) All federal, State and local campaign contributions made by
p69 the contractors and their registered lobbyists to any member
p69 of the Board of Supervisors or his or her controlled
p69 committees during the year preceding the date of the
p69 disclosure.
p69 If there are no gifts or campaign contributions to disclose, that fact

p69 lobbyists within the time limit set forth above.
