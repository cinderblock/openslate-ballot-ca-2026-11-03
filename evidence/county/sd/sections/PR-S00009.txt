p88 COUNTY COUNSEL IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS
p88 The Board of Education for the Vista Unified School District (“District”)
p88 placed this measure on the ballot. If approved by 55% of the votes cast, this
p88 measure will authorize the District to issue and sell general obligation bonds
p88 in an amount not to exceed $543,000,000.
p88 This measure will authorize a levy on the assessed value of taxable
p88 property within the geographical boundaries of the District in an amount
p88 needed to pay the principal and interest on the bonds each year the bonds
p88 are outstanding. The District estimates the average tax rate would be $58
p88 for every $100,000 of assessed valuation, and the highest tax rate would be
p88 approximately $58 for every $100,000 of assessed valuation. The tax is
p88 anticipated to be collected through fiscal year 2062-2063.
p88 The estimated total debt service, including principal and interest, required
p88 to be repaid if the bonds are issued and sold is approximately
p88 $1,154,254,750. Actual amounts may vary depending on the timing of sales,
p88 bonds sold at each sale, interest rates, and assessed valuation.
p88 The measure identifies a list of projects at schools and facilities within the
p88 District that may be funded from the bond proceeds. Approval of this
p88 measure does not guarantee the proposed projects will be funded by the
p88 sale of the bonds. The District’s proposal for certain projects may assume
p88 receipt of matching State funds subject to appropriation by the Legislature
p88 or approval of a statewide bond measure.
p88 As required by State law, the measure states that the proceeds from the
p88 sale of the bonds may be used only for the purposes specified and that the
p88 District will establish an independent citizens’ oversight committee and
p88 conduct an annual independent performance audit to ensure funds are spent
p88 only on the projects listed in the measure. The District must also conduct an
p88 annual, independent financial audit of bond proceeds. The District states it
p88 has evaluated safety, class size reduction, and information technology
p88 needs in developing the projects listed in the measure.
p88 A “YES” vote is in favor of authorizing the District to issue and sell
p88 $543,000,000 in general obligation bonds.
p88 A “NO” vote is against authorizing the District to issue and sell
p88 $543,000,000 in general obligation bonds.
p88 TAX RATE STATEMENT
p88 An election will be held within the boundaries of Vista Unified School
p88 District (“District”) on November 3, 2026 to authorize the sale of up to $543
p88 million in bonds to finance facilities as described in the measure. If the bonds
p88 are approved, the District expects to sell the bonds in multiple series as a
p88 “bond extension” to the prior bond authorization, with bond maturities
p88 structured so that tax rate in each year is not projected to increase as
p88 compared to the current tax rate. Principal and interest on the bonds will be
p88 payable from the proceeds of tax levies made upon the taxable property
p88 located within the District. The following information is provided in
p88 compliance with Sections 9400 to 9404, inclusive, of the California Elections
p88 Code.
p88 1. The best estimate from official sources of the average annual tax
p88 rate that would be required to be levied to fund this bond issue over the entire
p88 duration of the bond debt service, based on assessed valuations available
p88 at the time of the election and a projection based on experience within the
p88 same jurisdiction and other demonstrable factors, is estimated to be $58 per
p88 $100,000 of assessed valuation. The final fiscal year in which the tax is
p88 anticipated to be collected is 2062-63.
p88 2. The best estimate from official sources of the highest tax rate that
p88 would be required to be levied to fund this bond issue, and an estimate of
p88 the year in which that rate will apply, based on assessed valuations available
p88 at the time of filing this statement, and a projection based on experience
p88 within the same jurisdiction and other demonstrable factors, is estimated to
p88 be $58 per $100,000 of assessed valuation first occurring in fiscal year
p88 2028-29.
p88 3. The best estimate from official sources of the total debt service,
p88 including the principal and interest, that would be required to be repaid if all
p88 the bonds are issued and sold is $1,154,254,750.
p88 Voters should note that the estimated tax rate is based on the
p88 ASSESSED VALUE of taxable property on the San Diego County official tax
p88 rolls, not on the property's market value. Property owners should consult
p88 their own property tax bills to determine their property's assessed value and
p88 any applicable tax exemptions.
p88 Attention of all voters is directed to the fact that the foregoing
p88 information is based upon the District's projections and estimates only, which
p88 are not binding upon the District. The actual tax rates, debt service and the
p88 years in which they will apply may vary from those presently estimated, due
p88 to variations from these estimates in the timing of bond sales, the amount of
p88 bonds sold and market interest rates at the time of each sale, and actual
p88 assessed valuations over the term of repayment of the bonds. The dates of
p88 sale and the amount of bonds sold at any given time will be determined by
p88 the District based on need for construction funds and other factors. The
p88 actual interest rates at which the bonds will be sold will depend on the bond
p88 market at the time of each sale. Actual future assessed valuation will depend

p93 SECTION IV: ADDITIONAL SPECIFICATIONS
p93 No Operating Expenses. Proceeds from the sale of Bonds authorized
p93 by this proposition shall be used only for the construction, reconstruction,
p93 rehabilitation or replacement of school facilities on the Bond Project List,
p93 including the furnishing and equipping of said school facilities, or the
p93 acquisition or lease of real property for said school facilities, and not for any
p93 other purpose, including teacher or administrator salaries and other school
p93 operating expenses in accordance with applicable law.
p93 Single Purpose. All of the purposes enumerated in this proposition
p93 shall be united and voted upon as one single proposition, pursuant to
p93 Section 15100 of the California Education Code, and all the enumerated
p93 purposes shall constitute the specific single purpose of the bonds and
p93 proceeds of the bonds shall be spent only for such purpose.
p93 Other Terms of the Bonds. The bonds may be issued and sold in
p93 several series, and in accordance with a financing plan determined by the
p93 Board pursuant to requirements of law. When sold, the bonds shall bear
p93 interest at an annual rate not exceeding the statutory maximum and with a
p93 maximum term not exceeding the statutory maximum, provided that the
p93 average useful life of bonds sold will not exceed one hundred twenty
p93 percent (120%) of the average life of the projects being financed or as
p93 otherwise provided by federal tax law. Bond funds may be used to
p93 reimburse the District for Bond Project list expenditures incurred prior to the
p93 election and bond issuance, in accordance with federal tax law.
p93 Attention of all voters is directed to the fact that the financial
p93 information contained in this measure is based upon the District's
p93 projections and estimates only, which are not binding upon the District, nor
p93 are the summary or average payment estimates, if any, provided in the Ballot
p93 Label. The actual tax rates, debt service and the years in which they will
p93 apply may vary from those presently estimated, due to variations from these
p93 estimates in the timing of bond sales, the amount of bonds sold and market
p93 interest rates at the time of each sale, and actual assessed valuations over
p93 the term of repayment of the bonds. The dates of sale and the amount of
p93 bonds sold at any given time will be determined by the District based on
p93 need for construction funds and other factors. The actual interest rates at
p93 which the bonds will be sold will depend on the bond market at the time of
p93 each sale. Actual future assessed valuation will depend upon the amount
p93 and value of taxable property within the District as determined by the County
p93 Assessor in the annual assessment and the equalization process.
p93 In preparing this information, the District obtained reasonable and
