p54 Oceanside is a city of hard-working, resilient and caring people. I will
p54 continue to serve each resident of the city we call home.
p54 Since I joined the City Council, Oceanside has invested over a million dollars
p54 a year in new youth programming and hundreds of thousands in
p54 neighborhood traffic calming so children can walk to school safely. My office
p54 led the coalition of seniors advocating for the Age-Friendly City program to
p54 make it easier for everyone to thrive. I’ve hosted dozens of workshops so
p54 our people better understand how to make local government work for their
p54 families. We’ve navigated the path to put sand back on our beaches and
p54 meaningfully disrupt the cycle of homelessness. We’ve done important work,
p54 but there’s still much more to come.
p54 This election is about the future of Oceanside and I believe we can elect a
p54 council that works to solve complex problems. We can ensure Oceanside’s
p54 multicultural heritage is uplifted and honored rather than sold as a tourist
p54 attraction. Together we can make staying in Oceanside a realistic option for
p54 working families and do life-changing work in the city we love.
