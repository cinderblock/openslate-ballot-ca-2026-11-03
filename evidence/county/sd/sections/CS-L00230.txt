p55 PRIYA BHAT-PATEL
p55 AGE: 38
p55 Mayor Pro Tem
p55 Endorsed by Carlsbad Firefighters’ and Police Officers’ Associations,
p55 Dr. Priya Bhat-Patel is a public health professional and tireless advocate
p55 dedicated to improving our quality of life.
p55 Priya grew up in Carlsbad and graduated from Carlsbad public schools.
p55 As a working mother of two, Priya knows firsthand the challenges Carlsbad
p55 families face and is focused on practical solutions that prioritize our
p55 community.
p55 Dr. Bhat-Patel has championed policies to protect taxpayer dollars,
p55 helping city reserves grow to 58% of the general fund, above the 40%
p55 minimum. She supported stronger housing programs that drove a 66%
p55 increase in permanent placements for people experiencing homelessness
p55 and a 44% drop in homelessness-related police calls. She led Carlsbad to
p55 divest from fossil fuels and invest in clean energy jobs. Priya has
p55 strengthened the local economy by expanding the city’s Small Business
p55 Liaison Program, and she’s working to launch a Family-Friendly Certified
p55 Program that helps Carlsbad businesses better serve families.
p55 As Mayor Pro Tem and Chair of the North County Transit District, Priya is
p55 bringing Carlsbad’s voice to the table and securing the regional resources
p55 we deserve.
p55 Dr. Bhat-Patel would be honored to earn your vote and continue serving
p55 the community she has always called home.
