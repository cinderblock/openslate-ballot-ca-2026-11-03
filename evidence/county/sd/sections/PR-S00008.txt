p86 COUNTY COUNSEL IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS
p86 The Board of Education for the Santee School District (“District”) placed
p86 this measure on the ballot. If approved by 55% of the votes cast, this
p86 measure will authorize the District to issue and sell general obligation bonds
p86 in an amount not to exceed $30,000,000.
p86 This measure will authorize a levy on the assessed value of taxable
p86 property within the geographical boundaries of the District in an amount
p86 needed to pay the principal and interest on the bonds each year the bonds
p86 are outstanding. The District estimates the average tax rate would be $12.62
p86 for every $100,000 of assessed valuation, and the highest tax rate would be
p86 approximately $19.14 for every $100,000 of assessed valuation. The tax is
p86 anticipated to be collected through fiscal year 2049-2050.
p86 The estimated total debt service, including principal and interest, required
p86 to be repaid if the bonds are issued and sold is approximately $49,700,000.
p86 Actual amounts may vary depending on the timing of sales, bonds sold at
p86 each sale, interest rates, and assessed valuation.
p86 The measure identifies a list of projects at schools and facilities within the
p86 District that may be funded from the bond proceeds. Approval of this
p86 measure does not guarantee the proposed projects will be funded by the
p86 sale of the bonds. The District’s proposal for certain projects may assume
p86 receipt of matching State funds subject to appropriation by the Legislature
p86 or approval of a statewide bond measure.
p86 As required by State law, the measure states that the proceeds from the
p86 sale of the bonds may be used only for the purposes specified and that the
p86 District will establish an independent citizens’ oversight committee and
p86 conduct an annual independent performance audit to ensure funds are spent
p86 only on the projects listed in the measure. The District must also conduct an
p86 annual, independent financial audit of bond proceeds. The District states it
p86 has evaluated safety, class size reduction, and information technology
p86 needs in developing the projects listed in the measure.
p86 A “YES” vote is in favor of authorizing the District to issue and sell
p86 $30,000,000 in general obligation bonds.
p86 A “NO” vote is against authorizing the District to issue and sell
p86 $30,000,000 in general obligation bonds.

p86 TAX RATE STATEMENT
p86 An election will be held in the Santee School District (the “District”) on
p86 November 3, 2026, to authorize the sale of up to $30 million in bonds of the
p86 District to finance school facilities as described in the measure. If such bonds
p86 are authorized and sold, principal and interest on the bonds will be payable
p86 only from the proceeds of ad valorem tax levies made upon the taxable
p86 property in the District. The following information is provided in compliance
p86 with Sections 9400-9404 of the Elections Code of the State of California.
p86 Such information is based upon the best estimates and projections presently
p86 available from official sources, upon experience within the District, and other
p86 demonstrable factors.
p86 Based upon the foregoing and projections of the District’s assessed
p86 valuation, the following information is provided:
p86 1. The best estimate of the average annual tax rate which would be
p86 required to be levied to fund this bond issue over the entire duration
p86 of the bond debt service, based on a projection of assessed
p86 valuations available at the time of filing of this statement, is $12.62
p86 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. The final fiscal year in which
p86 the tax is anticipated to be collected is 2049-50.
p86 2. The best estimate of the highest tax rate which would be required to
p86 be levied to fund this bond issue, based on a projection of assessed
p86 valuations available at the time of filing of this statement, is $19.14
p86 per $100,000 of assessed valuation. It is estimated that such rate
p86 would be levied starting in fiscal year 2030-31.
p86 3. The best estimate of total debt service, including principal and
p86 interest, that would be required to be repaid if all the bonds are
p86 issued and sold will be approximately $49,700,000.
p86 Voters should note the estimated tax rate is based on the assessed value
p86 (not market value) of taxable property on the County’s official tax rolls. In
p86 addition, taxpayers eligible for a property tax exemption, such as the
p86 homeowner’s exemption, will be taxed at a lower effective tax rate than
p86 described above. Property owners should consult their own property tax bills
p86 and tax advisors to determine their property’s assessed value and any
p86 applicable tax exemptions.
p86 The attention of all voters is directed to the fact that the foregoing information
p86 is based upon projections and estimates only, which amounts are not
p86 maximum amounts and are not binding upon the District. The actual debt
p86 service, tax rates and the years in which they will apply may vary from those
p86 used to provide the estimates set forth above, due to factors such as
p86 variations in the timing of bond sales, the par amount of bonds sold and
p86 market interest rates available at the time of each sale, actual assessed
p86 valuations over the term of the bonds, and other factors. The date and
p86 amount of bonds sold at any given time will be determined by the District
p86 based on the need for project funds and other considerations. The actual
p86 interest rates at which the bonds will be sold will depend on conditions in the
p86 bond market at the time of sale. Actual future assessed valuations will
p86 depend upon the amount and value of taxable property within the District as
p86 determined by the County Assessor in the annual assessment and the
p86 equalization process.

p88 and following).

p88 the project list contained in this ballot proposition.

p88 an Independent Citizens’ Oversight Committee pursuant to Education Code
p88 Section 15278 and following to ensure bond proceeds are expended only on
p88 the projects listed in this ballot proposition. The committee shall be
p88 established within 60 days of the date when the results of the election appear
p88 in the minutes of the Board.

p88 performance audit to ensure that the bond proceeds have been expended
p88 only on the projects listed in this ballot proposition.
p88 Financial Audits. The Board shall conduct an annual, independent
p88 financial audit of the bond proceeds until all of those proceeds have been
p88 spent for the projects listed in this ballot proposition.
