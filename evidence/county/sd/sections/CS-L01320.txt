p55 CITY OF ESCONDIDO
p55 Member of the City Council District No. 2
p55 ANTHONY JAMES DIMARTINO
p55 Government Affairs Director
p55 My two-year-old son’s future is tied to the future of Escondido, so I am
p55 invested in helping Escondido reach its full potential. My own childhood
p55 memories of Escondido include safe neighborhoods, well-maintained parks
p55 with recreation programs, and housing my parents could afford. When I
p55 started my own family, I noticed the city had moved away from these
p55 priorities. I am running for City Council because I believe we have to
p55 recommit to policies that make Escondido a great place to live, work and
p55 raise a family.
p55 For nearly two decades, I have built a career focused on practical problem-
p55 solving, coalition building and public service. My work in nonprofit
p55 organizations, city and state government and teaching policy advocacy to
p55 social work students have prepared me to bring people together and deliver
p55 practical results for our community.
p55 My priorities include making City Hall more responsive — especially in
p55 contracting and the stewardship of public resources. We must also confront
p55 the twin challenges of housing affordability and homelessness, make
p55 neighborhoods safer for everyone, support small businesses and invest in
p55 parks, arts and youth programs that help families and neighborhoods thrive.
p55 I would be honored to earn your vote. More information at
p55 DiMartinoForEscondido.com.
