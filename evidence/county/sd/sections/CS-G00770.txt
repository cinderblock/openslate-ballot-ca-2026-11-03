p50 Dear Neighbor,
p50 It is my honor to represent San Diego County in the California State
p50 Assembly. In this role, I’ve authored 35 laws, including historic legislation
p50 addressing the climate crisis and pollution, combating discrimination,
p50 expanding access to health care, and improving traffic safety.
p50 San Diego County is facing an affordability crisis—rising costs are hurting
p50 working families. That’s why I authored a new law to prevent unnecessary
p50 utility rate increases. I also authored a bill which would require internet
p50 companies to offer affordable service for low-income Californians. To
p50 tackle the high cost of housing, I authored The Starter Home Revitalization
p50 Act, which reduces the barriers to building homes that fit the character of
p50 our communities. There is more work to do, and I will keep fighting to make
p50 San Diego County more affordable for all.
p50 I believe the first job of government is public safety. In the State Assembly,
p50 I’ve helped secure new funding for San Diego County firefighters and
p50 lifeguards. I’m honored to have the endorsement of groups representing
p50 local firefighters and police officers, as well as teachers and nurses.
p50 I’m a businesswoman, mother of two, fourth-generation San Diego County
p50 resident, and a former City Councilmember and PTA leader. Those
p50 experiences make me who I am and give me a deep understanding of our
p50 community. As your Assemblymember, I will always have an open door to
p50 residents who need help and an open mind to my constituents’ ideas and
p50 challenges.
p50 I hope to earn your vote,
p50 Tasha Boerner
