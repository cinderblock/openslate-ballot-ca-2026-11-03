p53 KARL BRADLEY
p53 Retired Public School Administrator
p53 I am running for mayor because I love our little beach town. I have no agenda
p53 other than to do everything in my power to maintain and improve our quality
p53 of life.
p53 As your mayor:
p53 I will represent everyone who lives in Imperial Beach, regardless of their
p53 political party.
p53 I will advocate for transparency from the federal government regarding the
p53 work at the international treatment plant in the Tijuana River Valley and
p53 demand that local, state, and federal representatives provide additional
p53 funding for the work needed to re-open our beaches.
p53 I will support our firefighters and work to create and maintain a fully staffed
p53 fire department.
p53 I will promote public-private partnerships to bring more events and revenue
p53 to our community and help local businesses.
p53 I will implement innovative ideas to bring more revenue into our city to defray
p53 the anticipated increase in the cost of law enforcement.
p53 I will encourage managed growth that provides more housing while avoiding
p53 sprawling developments that drastically alter the character of our close-knit
p53 neighborhoods.
p53 Vote Karl Bradley for mayor on November 3!
