p79 MEASURE R
p79 To preserve the City’s ability to continue receiving state infrastructure funding for
p79 public works projects, shall the Oceanside City Charter be amended to conform
p79 to state laws and best practices regarding prevailing wage and fair competition,
p79 including the use of project labor agreements for City public works with mandatory
p79 provisions for taxpayer protections, transparency, and accountability in the
p79 contracting process, by amending Sections 301, 302, and 303 of the Oceanside
p79 City Charter?
p79 This measure requires Simple Majority (50%+1) of those voting on the measure.
p79 CITY ATTORNEY IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS
p79 Measure “R” was placed on the ballot by the Oceanside City Council. If
p79 approved by voters, Measure R would amend Sections 301, 302 and 303 of
p79 the Charter of the City of Oceanside (“Charter”) and align the Charter with
p79 laws governing state funding and financial assistance in support of public
p79 works projects. Specifically, Measure R would affirm prevailing wage
p79 requirements for all public works projects as defined in paragraph (1) of
p79 subdivision (d) of Section 1782 of the California Labor Code and, consistent
p79 with Public Contract Code sections 2500-2503, would affirmatively allow, but
p79 not require, the City Council to enter into or require a project labor agreement
p79 (“PLA”) for City public works projects that includes the taxpayer protection
p79 provisions of Public Contract Code section 2500.
p79 The Charter was approved by Oceanside voters in 2010. As adopted, the
p79 Charter generally exempts the City from state laws regulating public works
p79 contracting except as provided by ordinance or by agreement approved by
p79 the City Council, limiting the requirement to pay prevailing wage and the
p79 ability to enter into public works contracts that require one or more provisions
p79 commonly associated with PLAs.
p79 After the Charter was adopted in 2010, the California legislature passed
p79 Senate Bill 7 (SB 7) which enacted Labor Code Section 1782, and Senate
p79 Bills 922 and 829 (SB 922 & 829, respectively), which added Public Contract
p79 Code Sections 2500 through 2503. These laws generally disqualify local
p79 governments from receiving state funding for public works projects if they do
p79 not align with state laws governing prevailing wage and fair competition.
p79 The City has maintained its qualified status for state public works funding
p79 based on interpretations that state law and the Charter can be reconciled.
p79 The City Attorney confirmed the City’s ongoing eligibility for state funding
p79 with the Department of Industrial Relations in 2014. The City pays prevailing
p79 wage on public works projects consistent with state law under the existing
p79 Charter. The City also understood that it retained the ability to enter into a
p79 PLA consistent with the Public Contract Code. PLAs are generally pre-hire
p79 agreements between a city and labor organizations containing standard
p79 terms and conditions of employment on a public works project. PLAs
p79 typically include broad prohibitions on strikes and lockouts, uniform work
p79 rules and hours, and dispute resolution protocols. Union and non-union
p79 contractors can participate in a covered project and must adhere to the PLA
p79 requirements.
p79 Amending the Charter now aligns with state law and eliminates possible
p79 inconsistencies that generally operate to disqualify cities with charter
p79 provisions exempting prevailing wage and PLAs from receiving state funding
p79 for local infrastructure projects. If adopted, Measure R’s amendment of
p79 Sections 301, 302 and 303 would ensure the City’s continuing eligibility for
p79 state funding for infrastructure projects.
p79 A “yes” vote on Measure R favors amending the Oceanside City Charter.
p79 A “no” vote on Measure R opposes amending the Oceanside City Charter.
p79 The above statement is an impartial analysis of Measure R. If you desire a

p82 Section 301. - Public Works Contracts.
p82 The City is exempt from the provisions of all California statutes regulating
p82 public contracting and purchasing except as provided by ordinance, or by
p82 agreement approved by the City Council, or this Charter. Eligibility for state
p82 and federal grant funding and financial assistance in support of public works
p82 projects is essential for sound fiscal management of the City’s capital
p82 infrastructure programs. The City shall establish standards, procedures,
p82 rules or regulations to regulate all aspects of public contracting consistent
p82 with the legal requirements for securing state and federal grants and
p82 financial assistance as appropriate.
p82 Section 302. – Prevailing Wage
p82 No City contract shall require payment of the prevailing wage schedule
p82 unless: the prevailing wage is legally required, and constitutionally permitted
p82 to be imposed, by federal or state grants pursuant to federal or state law; or
p82 the project is considered by the City Council not to be a municipal affair of
p82 the City; or payment of the prevailing wage schedule is authorized by
p82 resolution of the City Council. Payment of the prevailing wage schedule, if
p82 authorized hereunder, shall use the pertinent rates published by the State of
p82 California. Notwithstanding any other provision of this Charter or local law,
p82 to ensure the City’s eligibility for state funding and financial assistance, all
p82 public works contracts, as defined in paragraph (1) of subdivision (d) of
p82 Section 1782 of the California Labor Code, that are awarded by the City shall
p82 comply with state law prevailing wage requirements.
p82 Section 303. – Fair and Open Competition
p82 The City shall not, in any contract for the construction, maintenance, repair,
p82 or improvement of public works, require that a contractor, subcontractor,
p82 material supplier, or carrier engaged in the construction, maintenance, repair
p82 or improvement of public works, execute or otherwise become party to any
p82 project labor agreement, collective bargaining agreement, prehire
p82 agreement, or other agreement with employees, their representatives, or
p82 any labor organization as a condition of bidding, negotiating, being awarded,
p82 or performing work on a public works contract. Nothing in this section shall
p82 be construed as prohibiting private parties from entering into individual
p82 collective bargaining relationships, or otherwise as regulating or interfering
p82 with activity protected by applicable law, including but not limited to the
p82 National labor Relations Act. Notwithstanding any other provision of this
p82 Charter or local law, to ensure the City’s eligibility for state funding or
p82 financial assistance, consistent with Public Contract Code sections 2500 -
p82 2503, the City Council may choose by majority vote whether to use, enter
p82 into, or require contractors to enter into a project labor agreement that
p82 includes all the taxpayer protection provisions of Public Contract Code
p82 section 2500 for a specific project or projects awarded by the City and
p82 whether to allocate funding to a specific project covered by such an
p82 agreement. Unless otherwise amended by state law, the minimum taxpayer
p82 protection provisions shall be included in any project labor agreement per
p82 Public Contract Code section 2500(a):
p82 (1) The agreement prohibits discrimination based on race, national
p82 origin, religion, sex, sexual orientation, political affiliation, or
p82 membership in a labor organization in hiring and dispatching workers
p82 for the project.
p82 (2) The agreement permits all qualified contractors and subcontractors
p82 to bid for and be awarded work on the project without regard to whether
p82 they are otherwise parties to collective bargaining agreements.
p82 (3) The agreement contains an agreed-upon protocol concerning drug
p82 testing for workers who will be employed on the project.
p82 (4) The agreement contains guarantees against work stoppages,
p82 strikes, lockouts, and similar disruptions of the project.
p82 (5) The agreement provides that disputes arising from the agreement
p82 shall be resolved by a neutral arbitrator.
