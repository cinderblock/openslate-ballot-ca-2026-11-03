p55 FALLBROOK PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT
p55 Member of the Board of Directors Division No. 2
p55 GERRY HOFFMAN
p55 AGE: 66
p55 Retired Chemistry Professor
p55 After spending 30 years teaching chemistry at the university level, I retired
p55 with my spouse to Fallbrook five years ago. We have fallen in love with
p55 this town, and I would like to show my appreciation through public service.
p55 I earned a Bachelor’s in Chemistry from Towson University (MD), and
p55 Master’s and Ph. D. degrees in Physical Chemistry from Cornell University
p55 (NY). Being a chemist, I understand the physical and chemical properties
p55 of water as well as methods of analysis and processes used for water
p55 purification.
p55 FPUD has recently made changes with the hope of reducing water rates,
p55 including leaving the San Diego County Water Authority to join the Eastern
p55 Municipal Water District. Unfortunately, water rates have not decreased.
p55 The purpose of FPUD is to provide water that is safe and affordable, as
p55 well as providing sewer service. Further, recent devastating fires have also
p55 shown how vital a reliable water supply is to our safety and the protection
p55 of our personal property.
p55 The decisions made by this Board greatly impact our semi-rural
p55 community. If rates are too high, agriculture and all ratepayers will suffer.
p55 I would like to be your voice on the FPUD Board.
