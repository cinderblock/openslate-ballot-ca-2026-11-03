p55 2026 is an extreme drought year. The drought of 2022 motivated me to run
p55 for Helix Water District’s Board of Directors, to address water security. I

p55 Over these 3-1/2 years, I’ve earned a certificate in Executive Education in
p55 Public Policy, attended water conferences, and toured water projects across

p55 Helix Water District on multiple regional water and infrastructure
p55 committees.
p55 My name is Andrea Beth Damsky. I am the current Director of Helix Water
p55 District Division 2, asking you to re-elect me for a second term.
p55 Water district directors oversee the district’s finances, water service, and
p55 facilities. I fulfill these duties as Chair of our Finance & Administration
p55 Committee and our Water Quality & Resources Committee. Every year I’ve
p55 served, the District has received a Clean Audit of its financial records.
p55 Next year, the Board will set water rates for the following 5 years, accounting
p55 for the rising costs of materials, chemicals, electricity, labor, infrastructure
p55 upgrades, and the water itself. This process is strictly regulated by State law:
p55 water districts may only charge for the actual cost of providing water. I will
p55 hold the line on fairness, as I promised in 2022.
p55 My background is in science: a BS in Environmental Science & Land Use
p55 Planning, an MS in Traditional Chinese Medicine, and an Acupuncture
p55 License. These disciplines inform my commitment to protecting both our
p55 water resources and public health.
p55 I joined this Board after 5 years on La Mesa’s Environmental Sustainability
p55 Commission. I proudly supported Helix Water District’s electric vehicle
p55 charging project, completed this March. The largest of its kind in California,
p55 it meets State electrification mandates, reduces pollution, and was funded
p55 90% by grants, saving our customers the cost.
p55 I also proudly support the East County Advanced Water Purification project,
p55 a multi-agency effort in which Helix will provide the final purification phase.
p55 Once operational in 2027, ECAWP will supply up to 30% of East County’s
p55 water, easing strain on the dwindling Colorado River and building local water
p55 independence.
p55 My name is Andrea Beth Damsky. I humbly ask you to re-elect me to the
p55 Helix Water District’s Board of Directors, so I can keep this work going for
