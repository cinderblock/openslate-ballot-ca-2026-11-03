p54 JENNIFER MENDOZA
p54 Lemon Grove City Councilmember
p54 Jennifer has lived in Lemon Grove for 25 years, giving her a deep
p54 understanding of the community and its needs. Over the years, her
p54 volunteer work in the community has included managing a weekly food
p54 distribution at St. John’s Church, organizing monthly food distributions at
p54 the Recreation Center, and actively volunteering with the Soroptimists and
p54 Lions Clubs. She helped organize LG Relays for Life, served on the LGA
p54 School Site Council, participated in Kaiser’s HEAL Zone initiative, and
p54 served on the Planning Commission.
p54 As a Councilmember, Jennifer has focused on improving streets and
p54 infrastructure, maintaining the city’s financial stability, pursuing grants and
p54 other funding opportunities, keeping Lemon Grove clean, attracting new
p54 businesses, addressing homelessness, and restoring recreation services.
p54 She is proud of the progress the city has made and is committed to
p54 building on that momentum.
p54 Lemon Grove is at an important crossroads. With the city preparing to hire
p54 a new City Manager and Finance Director, and with a relatively new City
p54 Council and staff, experienced leadership is more important than ever.
p54 Jennifer is running for the two-year council seat to provide stability,
p54 continuity, and steady leadership during this critical transition.
p54 Vote for Jennifer to keep Lemon Grove moving forward.
