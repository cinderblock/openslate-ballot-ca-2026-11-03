p54 CITY OF SOLANA BEACH
p54 Member of the City Council District No. 2
p54 KRISTI BECKER
p54 City Councilmember/ Retired Lawyer
p54 Like you, I care deeply about the future of Solana Beach and preserving the
p54 unique character of our small coastal community. It has been an honor to
p54 serve as your Councilmember.
p54 I am running for re-election because our City faces critical challenges,
p54 particularly new and proposed laws from Sacramento that require changes
p54 to our zoning and seriously reduce local control over development. These
p54 changes will permanently alter the character of our neighborhoods and
p54 business districts. With my experience on Council, I have the commitment,
p54 insight, and knowledge of resources needed to mitigate some of the
p54 negative impacts from Sacramento’s actions when possible, and to work
p54 toward realistic solutions to protect our City’s interests. I will continue to
p54 strongly represent our City on these crucial issues.
p54 I support practical, thoughtful solutions for affordable housing that fit
p54 appropriately within our City. I do not support luxury 8-story apartment
p54 projects in our neighborhoods that block views, lack adequate parking, and
p54 overwhelm our existing infrastructure. As your Council member, I will
p54 continue fighting for sustainable growth, local decision-making, and
p54 development policies that reflect the values and priorities of Solana Beach
p54 residents.
p54 Over the past few years on the Council, we have accomplished a great deal
p54 together. We stopped a devastating proposal to realign the train tracks that
p54 would have torn through much of Solana Beach. We improved Fletcher
p54 Cove Beach by replenishing sand, installing a new playground structure,
p54 and adding a new shower and access ramp. We replaced the tot lot at La
p54 Colonia Park, and secured funding to build the walking path and bike lane
p54 improvements on Lomas Santa Fe (east of the freeway) to make it safer for
p54 all modes of transportation.
p54 We restrained City spending, and our finances remain strong allowing us to
p54 move forward with other important projects, including the expansion and
p54 improvement of La Colonia Park. Also, the City has funded its street
p54 resurfacing program and set aside money toward future construction of the
p54 new Marine Safety Center.
p54 My experience as a trial lawyer at a large regional law firm and in the San
p54 Diego County Counsel’s Office has prepared me to analyze complex issues,
p54 communicate clearly, and solve difficult problems. Experience,
p54 qualifications, and values matter when shaping the future of our City.
p54 Together, we can preserve the character of Solana Beach while planning
p54 responsibly for the future.
p54 I respectfully ask for your vote.
