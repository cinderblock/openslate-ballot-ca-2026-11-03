p83 MEASURE S
p83 To provide locally-controlled funding for Oceanside services and infrastructure,
p83 such as keeping streets, sidewalks, parks, and public facilities safe, clean, and
p83 well-maintained; fixing potholes; improving traffic; restoring/protecting local
p83 beaches; modernizing public safety/emergency facilities to improve 911
p83 emergency response/community safety; and repairing aging infrastructure, shall
p83 City of Oceanside’s ordinance establishing a 1/2 cent sales tax be adopted,
p83 providing $20,000,000 annually for general government use for 10 years, with
p83 independent audits and citizen oversight?
p83 This measure requires Simple Majority (50%+1) of those voting on the measure.
p83 CITY ATTORNEY IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS
p83 Measure “S” was placed on the ballot by the Oceanside City Council and
p83 proposes to increase the transactions and use tax (commonly referred to as
p83 the “sales tax”) by one-half of one percent (0.5%) on retail sales within the
p83 City of Oceanside (“City”). If approved, the tax would commence on or after
p83 April 1, 2027 and would remain in place for ten years.
p83 Measure S’s sales tax would be levied under state law (Revenue and
p83 Taxation Code Section 7285.9). As such, it would be collected as a
p83 percentage of gross receipts from retail sales and other taxable uses
p83 occurring within the City. The current sales tax rate in Oceanside is 8.25%.
p83 If Measure S is approved, the sales tax rate would increase to 8.75%. The
p83 tax revenues generated from the additional 0.5% would go to the City less a
p83 state administrative fee.
p83 The City estimates that Measure S, if adopted, will generate approximately
p83 $20 million annually. These revenues are authorized to be used for any
p83 lawful municipal purpose. This includes expenditures for services and
p83 infrastructure, such as keeping streets, sidewalks, parks, and public facilities
p83 safe, clean, and well-maintained, fixing potholes, restoring/protecting local
p83 beaches, modernizing public safety and emergency facilities to improve 911
p83 emergency response and community safety, and repairing aging
p83 infrastructure. Because the revenues raised by Measure S can be used for
p83 any general governmental purpose, a majority of voters must approve
p83 Measure S for the tax to go into effect.
p83 If adopted, Measure S adds Sections 34.7.1 – 34.7.17 to the Oceanside City
p83 Code setting forth the nature and amount of the proposed increase as well
p83 as how it is required to be administered. Measure S requires a Citizens’
p83 Oversight Committee (“COC”) to be responsible for annually making
p83 recommendations to the City Council on a spending plan for the revenues
p83 generated by Measure S. The COC will also be responsible for reviewing
p83 the annual independent audit of the revenues and expenditures of funds
p83 generated by Measure S. All expenditures of Measure S fund monies will be
p83 tracked and accounted for by City’s Financial Services Department as a
p83 separate line item in the General Fund and in accordance with Generally
p83 Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP).
p83 A “yes” vote on Measure S favors increasing the sales tax by one-half of one
p83 percent for ten years.
p83 A “no” vote on Measure S opposes an increase in the sales tax by one-half
p83 of one percent for ten years.

p84 Sec. 34.7.2 - Purpose.
p84 This Ordinance is adopted to achieve the following, among other
p84 purposes, and directs that the provisions hereof be interpreted in order to
p84 accomplish those purposes:
p84 (a) To impose a retail transactions and use tax (commonly referred
p84 to as a sales tax), in accordance with the provisions of Part 1.6
p84 (commencing with Section 7251) of Division 2 of the Revenue and
p84 Taxation Code and Section 7285.9 of Part 1.7 of Division 2 which
p84 authorizes the City to adopt this tax ordinance which shall be operative
p84 if a majority vote of the electors voting on the measure vote to approve
p84 the imposition of the tax at an election called for that purpose.
p84 (b) To adopt a retail transactions and use tax ordinance that
p84 incorporates provisions identical to those of the Sales and Use Tax Law
p84 of the State of California insofar as those provisions are not inconsistent
p84 with the requirements and limitations contained in Part 1.6 of Division
p84 2 of the Revenue and Taxation Code.
p84 (c) To adopt a retail transactions and use tax ordinance that imposes
p84 a tax and provides a measure therefore that can be administered and
p84 collected by the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration
p84 in a manner that adapts itself as fully as practicable to, and requires the
p84 least possible deviation from, the existing statutory and administrative
p84 procedures followed by the California Department of Tax and Fee
p84 Administration in administering and collecting California State Sales
p84 and Use Taxes.
p84 (d) To adopt a retail transactions and use tax ordinance that can be
p84 administered in a manner that will be, to the greatest degree possible,
p84 consistent with the provisions of Part 1.6 of Division 2 of the Revenue
p84 and Taxation Code, minimize the cost of collecting the transactions and
p84 use taxes, and at the same time, minimize the burden of record keeping
p84 upon persons subject to taxation under the provisions of this
p84 Ordinance.
p84 Sec. 34.7.3 - Operative date.
p84 “Operative Date” means the first day of the first calendar quarter
p84 commencing more than 110 days after the approval of the voters of the City
p84 of a measure approving the imposition of the transactions and use tax set
p84 forth herein, unless a later Operative Date becomes effective under the
p84 provisions of section 34.7.6.
p84 Sec. 34.7.4 - Transactions and use tax imposition rate.
p84 (a) For the privilege of selling tangible personal property at retail, a
p84 tax is hereby imposed upon all retailers in the incorporated territory of
p84 the City at the rate of one-half of one percent (0.5%) of the gross
p84 receipts of any retailer from the sale of all tangible personal property
p84 sold at retail in said territory on and after the Operative Date of this
p84 Ordinance.
p84 (b) An excise tax is hereby imposed on the storage, use or other
p84 consumption in the City of tangible personal property purchased from
p84 any retailer on and after the Operative Date of this Ordinance for
p84 storage, use or other consumption in said territory at the rate of one-
p84 half of one percent (0.5%) of the sales price of the property. The sales
p84 price shall include delivery charges when such charges are subject to
p84 State sales or use tax regardless of the place to which delivery is made.
p84 Sec. 34.7.5 - Termination date.
p84 The authority to levy the tax imposed by this Ordinance shall expire on
p84 April 1, 2037.
