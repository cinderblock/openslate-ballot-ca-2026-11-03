p55 JAS GREWAL
p55 Planning Commissioner / Banker
p55 Del Mar is at a crossroads. This election will decide if we follow the
p55 Community Plan that has successfully guided our city for more than 50 years
p55 or choose expensive ideas that could create debt and weaken essential
p55 services. As a retired banker and former Finance Committee member, I
p55 know the importance of careful planning and financial discipline. Borrowing
p55 millions of dollars to speed up projects we are already paying for over time

p55 If elected, I will help rebuild strong relationships with regional agencies, find
p55 practical ways to meet State housing rules, support moving the train off the
p55 bluffs without tunneling under homes or businesses, protect our fast
p55 emergency response times, improve wildfire and sea-level-rise safety
p55 without managed retreat, and preserve Del Mar’s special character.
p55 I believe City Council members should be good stewards of our land and put
p55 residents’ interests first. With no personal agenda, I will be a leader Del Mar
p55 deserves, serving with honesty, ethics, and integrity.
