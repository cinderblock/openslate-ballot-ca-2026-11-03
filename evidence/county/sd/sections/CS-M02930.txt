p54 RINCON DEL DIABLO MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICT
p54 Member of the Board of Directors Division No. 3
p54 KENNETH HOVING
p54 Retired/Appointed Incumbent
p54 My family’s roots in Escondido date back to 1969, and I am proud to have
p54 served our community as your appointed Director for Division 3 of the
p54 Rincon del Diablo Municipal Water District. Having lived in Division 3 for the
p54 past 10 years, I consider it a privilege to represent my neighbors and
p54 respectfully ask for your support to continue serving our community.
p54 My commitment is straightforward: to ensure a dependable, safe, and
p54 reliable water supply at a fair and affordable cost for the residents and
p54 businesses of our district.
p54 I was raised in Escondido and graduated from Escondido High School and
p54 San Diego State University. My father, a retired Escondido Fire Captain, still
p54 lives in the family home purchased in 1971. As a child, I rode on the old fire
p54 truck in the annual Christmas Parade and delivered newspapers for the
p54 Times-Advocate beginning at age 10. Those experiences, along with my
p54 family’s example of public service, instilled in me the values of hard work,
p54 accountability, and giving back to the community.
p54 My 45-year business career began in entry-level positions and ultimately led
p54 to executive leadership of several publicly traded companies. Along the way,
p54 I learned the importance of fiscal responsibility, strategic planning, employee
p54 safety, and building organizations that operate efficiently and sustainably.
p54 These experiences provide valuable perspective as I fulfill my fiduciary
p54 responsibility to oversee the district’s finances, infrastructure, and long-term
p54 planning on behalf of our ratepayers.
p54 My wife, Cathy, and I raised our two children and we are now blessed with
p54 grandchildren. Protecting the quality, reliability, and affordability of our water
p54 supply matters to me.
p54 Like all water agencies, Rincon faces important challenges and
p54 opportunities, including maintaining infrastructure, planning for future water
p54 reliability, and managing costs responsibly. Thanks to a dedicated board
p54 and an exceptional staff, our district is well-positioned to meet those
p54 challenges while continuing to provide outstanding service.
p54 It has been an honor to serve the residents of Division 3. I would be grateful
p54 for the opportunity to continue working on your behalf, bringing experienced
p54 leadership, sound financial judgment, and a steadfast commitment to
p54 preserving the district’s legacy of responsible stewardship for future
p54 generations.
p54 ABELARDO (ABEL) MARTINEZ
p54 Staff Cyber Systems Engineer
p54 Water is critical infrastructure, and for over 30 years, I have made it my life’s
p54 work to protect the systems our community depends on. I served 22 years
p54 in the military and have spent more than 12 years since as a Cybersecurity
p54 Engineer defending critical infrastructure. I know what it takes to keep
p54 essential systems reliable, resilient, and accountable, because in my career,
p54 failure was never an option. I’ve been a Rincon del Diablo Division 3
p54 customer for 25 years. Over the past year, I’ve worked to earn this seat, not
p54 just seek it: attending Board meetings, completing Water Distribution
p54 Operator coursework, and finishing the Water Citizens Academy. I continue
p54 to lead veteran organizations across Escondido, building coalitions and
p54 delivering real results for our community. I hold a bachelor’s degree and a
p54 master’s degree, and I bring the same discipline to public service that I
p54 brought to protecting national infrastructure. As a husband and father, I
p54 understand what’s at stake when families depend on safe, reliable, and
p54 affordable water. If elected, my priorities will be responsible fiscal
p54 management, reliable infrastructure, and long-term water security. I will
p54 show up prepared, ask the hard questions, and keep residents informed.
p54 While also maintaining and improving the systems our community relies on
p54 every day, such as planning for drought, growth, and future challenges with
p54 sound science and practical solutions. I believe good governance means
p54 listening first. I will work collaboratively with fellow Board members, staff,
p54 and residents to make decisions grounded in facts and in the best interests
p54 of Division 3. I’ve spent my career protecting what matters to our nation.
p54 Now I’m ready to bring that same commitment to what matters most to our
p54 Escondido community: your water. I would be honored to earn your trust and
p54 your vote. Please check out my website to learn more about me and how I
p54 am ready to serve the voters of Rincon del Diablo Division 3.
