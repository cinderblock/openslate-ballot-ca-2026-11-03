p75 COUNTY COUNSEL IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS
p75 PERMANENT ROAD DIVISION ZONE 9B HARRIS ANNEX SPECIAL
p75 TAX MEASURE
p75 The Board of Supervisors for the County of San Diego placed this
p75 Measure on the ballot.
p75 If this Measure is approved by two-thirds of the votes cast, parcels within
p75 Permanent Road Division Zone 9B Harris Annex shall pay a maximum
p75 parcel tax of $1,000 per parcel for each developed parcel and $375 per
p75 parcel for each undeveloped parcel commencing in Fiscal Year 2027-2028.
p75 The stated purpose of the special tax is to fund ongoing road maintenance,
p75 which includes administrative expenses. The special tax will continue to be
p75 collected in the Permanent Road Division until amended, repealed or
p75 suspended by the Board of Supervisors. The special tax will replace the
p75 existing assessment imposed in the Permanent Road Division by repealing
p75 Ordinance No. 9165. Total estimated revenue derived from the special tax
p75 from the 53 developed and 3 undeveloped parcels currently in the
p75 Permanent Road Division for the first fiscal year is $54,125. The special tax
p75 is subject to adjustment, not to exceed 5% per fiscal year, to account for any
p75 inflation as measured by the Los Angeles Construction Cost Index or a
p75 comparable Southern California construction cost index.
p75 A “YES” vote is in favor of authorizing the County of San Diego to replace
p75 the existing assessment with a special tax of up to $1,000 per developed
p75 parcel and up to $375 per undeveloped parcel to fund ongoing road
p75 maintenance within the Permanent Road Division.
p75 A “NO” vote is against authorizing the County of San Diego to replace the
p75 existing assessment with a special tax $1,000 per developed parcel and up
p75 to $375 per undeveloped parcel to fund ongoing road maintenance within
p75 the Permanent Road Division.

p75 Measure Q. If you desire a copy of the ordinance or measure, please
