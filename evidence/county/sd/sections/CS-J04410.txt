p50 livable wage in San Diego, restoring District reserves that were impacted
p50 due to the pandemic.
p50 The District enacted dozens of new college transfer degrees for students,

p50 hundreds of certificate programs to prepare the workforce for well-paying
p50 jobs. The District produced a record number of graduates and expanded the
p50 San Diego Promise Program to make community college free for eligible
p50 students.

p50 District generates $5.6 billion annually into the San Diego economy and
p50 supports 37,508 jobs, or 1 out of every 57 jobs in San Diego. The District
p50 serves more than 10,000 veterans, active-duty military, and military
p50 dependents each year.
p50 I believe accomplishments such as these demonstrate my commitment
