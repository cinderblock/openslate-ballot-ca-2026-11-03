p55 CITY OF CARLSBAD
p55 Member of the City Council District No. 1
p55 MELANIE BURKHOLDER
p55 AGE: 54
p55 Mental Health Therapist / Council Member
p55 Serving on the Carlsbad City Council has been one of the greatest honors
p55 of my life. I have worked every day to maintain our quality of life, keep our
p55 neighborhoods safe, and ensure City Hall remains accountable to
p55 residents.
p55 I fought Sacramento’s one-size-fits-all housing mandates by traveling to
p55 testify before the Legislature, defending Carlsbad’s right to local control
p55 over land-use decisions. I supported adding police officers and firefighters
p55 to improve public safety—without raising taxes. I have consistently stood
p55 with our small businesses, championed fiscal responsibility, and worked
p55 to protect the unique character of our community.
p55 I opposed development that removes the neighborhood-serving
p55 businesses and convenient services our seniors and families rely on,
p55 especially in our Village. I believe growth should be thoughtful, balanced,
p55 and respect the needs of existing residents.
p55 My focus has always been on listening, being accessible, and making
p55 decisions that put Carlsbad residents first—not political interests or
p55 Sacramento mandates.
p55 I respectfully ask for your vote so I can continue protecting Carlsbad’s
p55 neighborhoods, strengthening public safety, supporting local businesses,
p55 and preserving the exceptional quality of life that makes our city such a
p55 special place to call home.
p55 NATE LARSON
p55 Art Director / Educator
p55 Carlsbad is where I was born and raised. Where my Marrón and Pico
p55 ancestors raised cattle in the 1800’s. Where my Frazee ancestors first
p55 covered the hills with colorful ranunculus flowers. Where I learned to
p55 ride a bike, ride a wave, be a teammate, and be a leader. Where
p55 teachers, coaches, local business owners, and civic leaders worked
p55 together to shape Carlsbad into the town we love.
p55 Their example guides me as leader of the ‘Save the Carlsbad Theatre’
p55 movement, in my work on the Barrio Public Art Subcommittee, as an
p55 educator in our Carlsbad public schools, and as director and producer
p55 of local youth theater.
p55 Today, as I raise my own family here, I see developers and outside
p55 investors reshaping our Village, destroying its character, and impacting
p55 us all. New state laws have taken local land use decisions away from
p55 us. As your city council member, I will fight Sacramento and pursue
p55 every tool available to regain local control. I also pledge to preserve our
p55 natural and historic places before they are lost forever, and be a
p55 compassionate voice for the underserved members of our community.
p55 Working together, we can once again shape Carlsbad’s future.
