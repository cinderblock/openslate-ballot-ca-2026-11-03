p56 Public service is about listening, making informed decisions, working
p56 together and planning for the future – not politics. As your Helix Water
p56 District Board Director since 2006, my record reflects this belief. I’ve
p56 consistently worked to ensure that our community’s water supply is safe and
p56 reliable while keeping a strong focus on fiscal responsibility, transparency,
p56 and excellent customer service, all while working to minimize rate increases
p56 whenever possible. I am seeking your vote for re-election to continue
p56 delivering practical, experienced and open leadership.
p56 For more than 26 years, I’ve proudly called the Helix Water District
p56 community home. I am a third-generation water professional, a Registered
p56 Civil Engineer with more than 35 years of experience in the water industry
p56 and hold a Master’s Degree in Public Health from SDSU. My experience
p56 includes serving as president of the Helix Water District Board and
p56 representing Helix on the San Diego County Water Authority Board, where
p56 I strongly advocate for reliable water supplies and responsible regional
p56 water policies.
p56 As your representative, I have supported responsible budgeting, long-term
p56 state-of-the-art infrastructure investments, and efforts to reduce costs
p56 whenever possible. I voted to slash a planned water rate increase, helped
p56 secure millions of dollars in grants and low-interest financing for critical
p56 infrastructure, supported new local water supplies, and promoted
p56 investments that strengthen reliability while protecting ratepayer dollars.
p56 I helped strengthen openness and accountability, earning statewide
p56 recognition for financial reporting and government transparency. I also
p56 support wildfire preparedness, environmental stewardship, and partnerships
p56 that benefit our communities.
p56 I’ve stood against unnecessary water supply cuts by the State of California
p56 and demanded rate relief for you, my fellow citizens and ratepayers.
p56 Beyond Helix, I serve as president of the Water Conservation Garden
p56 Authority, co-president of the Rancho Helix de Oro Fire Safe Council, an
p56 elected member of the Valle de Oro Community Planning Group, and a
p56 board member of the Grossmont-Mt. Helix Improvement Association. These
p56 partnerships and my full engagement help strengthen our neighborhoods
p56 and build a safer, more resilient community.
p56 I also believe every customer deserves respectful, responsive service. I
p56 support reasonable programs that assist my fellow community members
p56 experiencing financial hardship while ensuring the District remains
p56 financially strong for future generations.
p56 I remain committed to protecting our water supply, investing responsibly,
p56 and ensuring Helix continues to deliver exceptional service today while
p56 planning wisely for tomorrow.
p56 I respectfully ask for your vote.
