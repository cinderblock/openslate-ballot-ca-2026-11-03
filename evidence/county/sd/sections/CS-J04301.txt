p50 FRANK XU
p50 Parent/Non-Profit Executive
p50 Twenty-one years ago, I escaped Communist China to raise my family.
p50 My children are now in college, and it is deeply concerning to see many
p50 young people embrace ideas that resemble those I experienced
p50 firsthand under communism, including DEI and anti-racism initiatives.
p50 Colleges, including Palomar College, should equip students with the
p50 knowledge and skills they need to succeed. Too often, ideological
p50 agendas overshadow academic excellence, critical thinking, and career
p50 readiness. Having experienced political indoctrination in Communist
p50 China, I believe higher education should focus on education rather than
p50 ideology. Students should be encouraged to value liberty, personal
p50 responsibility.
p50 My Christian faith inspires a life of service. As the founder of several
p50 nonprofit organizations, I have worked to expand educational
p50 opportunity and advocate for equal opportunity. Every organization I
p50 founded operates with a balanced budget, achieves measurable results,
p50 and receives no taxpayer funding. Over the past two years, I have
p50 regularly attended Palomar College Board meetings to share my
p50 perspectives, and my participation has been covered by The Telescope
p50 student newspaper.
p50 As a trustee, I will strengthen fiscal responsibility, address enrollment
p50 fraud, refocus the college on academics and workforce training, and
p50 ensure graduates are well prepared to compete in today’s job market.
