p54 DANE M. WHITE
p54 Mayor, City of Escondido
p54 It has been the honor of my lifetime to serve Escondido as your Mayor.
p54 Working together, we are moving the needle on the critical issues facing
p54 our community.
p54 We adopted a “Public Safety First” approach to homelessness to
p54 compassionately remove people from the streets and get them into
p54 services, resulting in a 32.4% reduction in unsheltered homelessness.
p54 We approved historic new contracts with our police and firefighters,
p54 adding additional first responders and improving pay and benefits. We
p54 have increased our road repair budget by several million dollars,
p54 allowing us to fill more potholes and pave more roads.
p54 While all solid progress, there is still much work to do. I remain focused
p54 on getting the homeless off the streets, improving public safety, fixing
p54 our roads, supporting small businesses, and making Escondido more
p54 affordable for working families.
p54 I love Escondido. This is where I grew up, overcame my personal

p54 our two daughters. Escondido has given me so much, and as Mayor I
p54 am grateful to be able to give back and work every day t o make our
p54 community safer, stronger, and more affordable.
