p54 IAN BUTLER
p54 Physician
p54 I am a practicing physician with two children living in the hospital district.
p54 My son was born at the now-closed birth center at Poway. My aging
p54 parents sometimes need hospital services that are now gone. I coach my
p54 son’s football team and when a player gets hurt, we no longer have a
p54 nearby option for the best care.
p54 Behind every policy and financial decision is a patient counting on us.
p54 When you need emergency care, a quality local hospital is the difference
p54 between life and death. We have lost many of these needed services in
p54 the Poway area over the last decade and I believe better management
p54 decisions can restore these to our community.
p54 Over the last 20 years I have worked not only as a bedside doctor, but
p54 also in healthcare management and have used my MBA training to
p54 safeguard critical hospital programs. Furthermore collaborating with our
p54 nurses both at the bedside and in the board room is necessary to make
p54 sure patients get the care they need. If elected, I will work to ensure
p54 Palomar Health remains financially strong while bringing back access to
p54 outstanding care.
p54 LINDA C GREER RN

p54 today, I’m proud to represent Poway District 7 on the Palomar Health

p54 same families.
