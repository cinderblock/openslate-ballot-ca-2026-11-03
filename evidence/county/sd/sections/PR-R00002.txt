p80 OFFICIAL TITLE AND BALLOT SUMMARY
p80 BALLOT TITLE
p80 An Initiative Measure restricting the sale of birds in the City of Lemon
p80 Grove, requiring that birds be obtained exclusively from animal shelters,
p80 humane societies, or nonprofit rescue organizations and establishing related
p80 regulations.
p80 BALLOT SUMMARY
p80 If approved, this Initiative Measure (“Initiative”) adopts a local ordinance
p80 that restricts the sale of birds in the City of Lemon Grove (“City”) by extending
p80 California’s existing rescue-only model to the sale of birds in the City with
p80 the following key provisions:
p80 1. Adoption and Extension of State Rescue-Only Standards to Birds.
p80 Consistent with California’s rescue-only sourcing requirements for dogs,
p80 cats, and rabbits under California Health and Safety Code §122354.5, no
p80 pet store shall display, sell, deliver, offer for sale, barter, auction, give away,
p80 or otherwise transfer any bird within the City unless the bird was obtained
p80 from an animal shelter, animal control agency, humane society, or nonprofit
p80 rescue organization, and the pet store receives no direct or indirect
p80 compensation or financial benefit for such adoption.
p80 2. Exemptions. This Initiative does not regulate poultry or birds sold for
p80 agricultural or backyard flock purposes.
p80 This Initiative does not apply to a pet store that provides space and care for
p80 birds owned by an animal shelter, humane society, or nonprofit rescue
p80 organization for purposes of adoption.
p80 This Initiative does not apply to: (A) a publicly operated animal control facility
p80 or animal shelter; (B) a private, charitable, nonprofit humane society or
p80 nonprofit animal rescue organization; (C) a publicly operated animal control
p80 agency, nonprofit humane society, or nonprofit animal rescue organization
p80 that operates out of or in connection with a pet store, provided the
p80 organization has obtained City approval and the activity is consistent with
p80 the intent and purpose of this Initiative.
p80 3. Enforcement and Penalties. Each bird sold, displayed, or offered in
p80 violation of this Initiative constitutes a separate offense punishable as an
p80 infraction and enforceable by the City.
p80 4. Effective Date. This Initiative will become effective immediately upon
p80 adoption and enforceable six (6) months after adoption.

p80 Lemon Grove. The Measure becomes effective immediately upon adoption

p81 birds.

p81 FULL TEXT OF MEASURE E
p81 CHAPTER 6.10 — RESCUE-ONLY PET STORE STANDARDS; EXTENSION
p81 TO BIRDS
p81 6.10.001 Adoption of State Rescue-Only Standards
p81 The City of Lemon Grove hereby incorporates and adopts the rescue-only
p81 requirements established under California Health and Safety Code
p81 §122354.5, which regulate the sourcing of dogs, cats, and rabbits offered for
p81 sale in pet stores. These provisions establish the State of California’s
p81 rescue-only model for pet stores, requiring that covered animals be obtained
p81 exclusively from animal shelters, humane societies, or nonprofit rescue
p81 organizations.
p81 6.10.005 Purpose and Findings
p81 A. The City of Lemon Grove finds that California has adopted a statewide
p81 rescue-only pet store model applicable to dogs, cats, and rabbits (Health
p81 and Safety Code §122354.5) to address concerns relating to animal welfare,
p81 breeding practices, consumer deception, and transparency in sourcing.
p81 B. The purpose of this chapter is to extend California’s rescue-only pet
p81 store model to include birds, thereby promoting consistency with state
p81 policy, strengthening consumer protections, and ensuring that birds offered
p81 to the public in Lemon Grove pet stores are obtained exclusively from
p81 humane, noncommercial sources.
p81 C. The City further finds that complaints from Lemon Grove residents and
p81 animal control inspection records demonstrate recurring welfare concerns
p81 associated with the retail sale of commercially bred birds.
p81 D. This chapter does not regulate poultry or birds sold for agricultural or
p81 backyard flock purposes.
p81 E. The City further finds that adopting and extending the California rescue-
p81 only model to birds will reduce abandonment, protect consumers, and
p81 advance the humane treatment of companion birds within the City of Lemon
p81 Grove.
p81 6.10.010 Definitions
p81 For the purposes of this chapter, the following definitions apply:
p81 “Animal control agency” means a public agency or department of a city or
p81 county, or an entity acting under contract with a city or county, that enforces
p81 animal-related laws and regulations, including the investigation of animal
p81 welfare complaints.
p81 “Animal control facility” means a facility operated by or under contract
p81 with a city or county for the purpose of impounding, caring for, or placing
p81 stray, abandoned, or relinquished animals, including birds.
p81 “Animal rescue organization” means a nonprofit organization, as defined
p81 under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, that rescues,
p81 rehabilitates, and rehomes animals and does not breed animals.
p81 “Animal shelter” means a municipal or county-operated animal shelter, or
p81 a duly incorporated nonprofit organization engaged in the rescue, care, or
p81 adoption of stray, abandoned, or surrendered animals, and which does not
p81 breed animals.
p81 “Bird” means any member of the class Aves commonly kept as companion
p81 animals, including but not limited to parrots, parakeets, cockatiels, finches,
p81 doves, and similar species.
p81 “Bird” does not include poultry sold for agricultural or backyard flock
p81 purposes, including chickens, ducks, geese, or turkeys.
p81 “Humane society” means a nonprofit organization incorporated under
p81 California law that is dedicated to the prevention of cruelty to animals and
p81 the rescue, care, or adoption of animals, and that does not breed animals.
p81 “Pet store” means any retail establishment open to the public that sells,
p81 offers for sale, or displays for sale any live animal, whether or not
p81 compensation is involved.

p82 “Pet store operator” means any person or entity that owns, manages,
p82 controls, or operates a pet store.
p82 “Retail sale” means to sell, display for sale, offer for sale, offer for adoption
p82 with any compensation, barter, auction, give away, deliver, or otherwise

p82 involved.
p82 “Retail sale” does not include the adoption of a bird from an animal shelter
p82 or nonprofit rescue when the pet store receives no monetary or nonmonetary

p82 6.10.020 Rescue-Only Requirement for Birds
p82 A. Consistent with the rescue-only sourcing requirements established under
p82 California law for dogs, cats, and rabbits, beginning on the operative date
p82 set forth in Section 6.10.050, no pet store shall display, sell, deliver, offer for
p82 sale, barter, auction, give away, or otherwise transfer any bird within the City
p82 of Lemon Grove unless the bird was obtained from an animal shelter, animal
p82 control agency, humane society, or nonprofit rescue organization, and the
p82 pet store receives no direct or indirect compensation or financial benefit for
p82 such adoption.
p82 B. A pet store that provides space and care for birds owned by an animal
p82 shelter, humane society, or nonprofit rescue organization for purposes of
p82 adoption shall not be deemed to be engaged in retail sale under this chapter.
p82 6.10.030 Exemptions
p82 This chapter does not apply to:
p82 A. A publicly operated animal control facility or animal shelter, as defined in
p82 this chapter;
p82 B. A private, charitable, nonprofit humane society or nonprofit animal rescue
p82 organization;
p82 C. A publicly operated animal control agency, nonprofit humane society, or
p82 nonprofit animal rescue organization that operates out of or in connection
p82 with a pet store, provided the organization has obtained City approval and
p82 the activity is consistent with the intent and purpose of this chapter.
p82 6.10.040 Enforcement and Penalties
p82 A. Each bird sold, displayed, or offered in violation of this chapter constitutes
p82 a separate offense.
p82 B. Every violation of this chapter is punishable as an infraction.
p82 C. The City, any authorized animal services agency, the health officer, and
p82 any peace officer may enforce this chapter.
p82 D. Remedies under this chapter are cumulative and not exclusive.
p82 6.10.050 Effective Date
p82 This ordinance shall take effect upon adoption.
p82 The operative provisions of this chapter shall become enforceable six (6)
p82 months after the date of adoption, whether adopted by the City Council or
p82 by the voters.
p82 6.10.060 Severability
p82 If any section, subsection, sentence, clause, or phrase of this chapter is held
p82 invalid or unconstitutional, the remaining portions shall continue in full force
p82 and effect. The People of the City of Lemon Grove declare that they would
