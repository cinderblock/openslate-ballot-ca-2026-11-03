p51 Governing Board Member Trustee Area No. 1
p51 ROBERT MORENO
p51 Southwestern College Governing Board Member
p51 Serving you on the Southwestern College Governing Board has been the
p51 honor of my life.
p51 Four years ago, I promised to put students first, and I kept that promise.
p51 I voted for four consecutive balanced budgets while expanding educational
p51 opportunities for South County students. I helped bring bachelor’s degree
p51 programs closer to home, including a new degree in transborder
p51 environmental design and a public health partnership with UC San Diego.
p51 Nearly 600,000 South County residents live without access to a public
p51 university. Our students should not have to leave their families, jobs and
p51 communities to complete their education.
p51 I also supported expanding dual enrollment, allowing Sweetwater Union
p51 High School District students to earn college credit before graduation. As a
p51 proud graduate of Sweetwater High School, I understand how life-changing
p51 that opportunity can be.
p51 I am honored to be endorsed by the Southwestern College Education
p51 Association (faculty) and California School Employees Association Chapter
p51 524 (classified professionals).
p51 If reelected, I will continue fighting for affordable student housing,
p51 competitive pay that retains outstanding employees, and career education
p51 that leads directly to good-paying jobs.
p51 We have made real progress, but our work is not finished. I respectfully ask
p51 for your vote.
p51 Robert Moreno
p51 Southwestern College Governing Board
p51 MARY SALAS
p51 Retired Mayor Chula Vista
p51 I know firsthand that a community college education can change the course
p51 of a life—because it changed mine.
p51 When I enrolled at Southwestern Community College, I was a 37-year-old
p51 divorced single mother with only a high school diploma, limited job skills, and
p51 two daughters depending on me. Stepping onto campus was intimidating,
p51 but the faculty, staff, and counselors believed in me before I fully believed in
p51 myself.
p51 Southwestern was affordable, close to home, and flexible enough to fit
p51 around my work schedule, allowing me to earn my associate degree,
p51 transfer to San Diego State University, and complete my bachelor’s degree.
p51 That education changed the trajectory of my family’s future. It gave me the
p51 opportunity to build a meaningful career, provide greater stability for my
p51 daughters, and eventually serve our region as Mayor of Chula Vista and later
p51 on the California Community Colleges Board of Governors, where I worked
p51 to expand educational opportunities for students across our state.
p51 Today as technology rapidly transforms the workforce and employers
p51 demand new skills, Southwestern must continue investing in affordable,
p51 high-quality education that prepares students for in-demand careers while
p51 preserving strong transfer pathways to four-year universities.
p51 I respectfully ask for your vote.
