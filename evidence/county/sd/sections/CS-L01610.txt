p54 MOANA ALO
p54 Mother, Public Servant, Entrepreneur
p54 Aloha, La Mesa! I’m Moana Alo, and I’m running for City Council because
p54 I believe in leading with kuleana: responsibility to the community that
p54 raised me.
p54 For over ten years, I’ve called La Mesa home. As Community Services
p54 Commissioner for the City since 2020, I’ve worked directly on the issues
p54 that shape our neighborhoods. As District Representative for the California
p54 State Senate, I connect residents to real solutions. As a small business
p54 owner offering notary and consulting services, I understand firsthand the
p54 challenges local entrepreneurs face. And as President of the Hawaiian
p54 Civic Club of San Diego, I’ve organized around civic engagement,
p54 scholarship funding, and youth leadership for underserved communities.
p54 My commitment to public service is grounded in education; a master’s in
p54 education from Point Loma Nazarene University and a nearly completed
p54 PhD focused on building belonging in our communities.
p54 On Council, I will fight for stronger tenant protections, expanded senior
p54 services, support for small businesses, and real investment in our youth.
p54 La Mesa deserves a leader who listens - with aloha.
p54 I’d be honored to earn your vote, Mahalo for your support!
p54 AMANDA-DAWN NATALIA
p54 AGE: 42
p54 Attorney, Environmental Sustainability Commissioner, and Mother
p54 I am a lifelong East County resident who was born in La Mesa, grew up
p54 in Lakeside, and returned to La Mesa 21 years ago. I graduated from El
p54 Capitan High School, Grossmont College, San Diego State University,
p54 and California Western School of Law. I am a private practice attorney
p54 with a small firm headquartered in La Mesa. I am a homeowner, a
p54 grateful wife, and a proud mom of two middle schoolers in La Mesa-
p54 Spring Valley School District.
p54 I serve as Chair of La Mesa’s Environmental Sustainability Commission,
p54 as a member of La Mesa-Spring Valley School District’s Community
p54 Bond Oversight Committee, and as a member of San Diego County Bar
p54 Association’s Leadership Academy Subcommittee. I volunteer regularly
p54 throughout the community, most often at Litter Free La Mesa events. I
p54 have a heart for service, and I am running for City Council to share my
p54 time, talents, and ideas with the community I love in the city I am
p54 privileged to call home.
p54 I am endorsed by Councilmembers Lauren Cazares, Patricia Dillard, and
p54 Genevieve Suzuki, San Diego & Imperial Valley Labor Council, San
p54 Diego Building and Construction Trades Council, as well as other local
p54 trade unions, organizations, and elected officials.
p54 My guiding principle is compassion. I want La Mesa to be a place where
p54 every resident and visitor feels heard, welcomed, and valued. I am
p54 grateful for the opportunities I have had in La Mesa, and it would be my
p54 great honor to put that gratitude into action by serving as your next La
p54 Mesa City Councilmember.
