p81 MEASURE P
p81 AMENDMENT OF PROPOSITION ‘NN’ TO MODERNIZE THE ORDINANCE.
p81 Shall the voters of the Ramona Municipal Water District adopt a measure
p81 amending Proposition ‘NN’ by (1) exempting grant funding and donations from the
p81 calculation of authorized expenditure limit; (2) increasing the expenditure limit for
p81 new projects from $1,000,000 to $10,000,000 where no debt is incurred; and
p81 (3) establishing annual adjustments to the expenditure limit based on the San
p81 Diego Area Consumer Price Index beginning January 1, 2028?
p81 This measure requires Simple Majority (50%+1) of those voting on the measure.
p81 DISTRICT COUNSEL IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS
p81 The Board of Directors for the Ramona Municipal Water District (“District”)
p81 placed this measure on the ballot. If approved by a majority of the votes cast,
p81 this measure will amend Proposition “NN,” which established Ordinance No.
p81 108 (Chapter 99.04 of the District’s Regulatory Code). Because Proposition
p81 NN was a citizens’ initiative, it can only be repealed or amended by a majority
p81 vote.
p81 Why A Vote Is Required:
p81 Proposition NN’s stated purpose was to require public approval of the
p81 District’s “expenditures for major projects.” However, Proposition NN
p81 imposed an “authorized debt limit,” rather than an “authorized expenditure
p81 limit.” The initial limit was set at $1,000,000 but allowed for annual
p81 adjustments based on the San Diego Area Consumer Price Index (CPI) for
p81 all Urban Consumers. Based on those adjustments, the current limit is
p81 approximately $3,300,000. For the past 38 years, the District has treated the
p81 debt limit as an expenditure limit.
p81 Effects of the Measure:
p81 The purpose of this measure is to amend Proposition NN to (1) exempt grant
p81 funding and donations from expenditure and debt limit calculations;
p81 (2) establish an “expenditure limit,” in the initial amount of $10,000,000, with
p81 CPI adjustments beginning January 1, 2028; and (3) keep debt for major
p81 projects limited to the amounts initially established by Proposition NN as
p81 adjusted annually since 1989, currently approximately $3,300,000.
p81 The proposed measure also makes changes to Proposition NN that would
p81 clarify the meaning of “expenditure limits” and “debt limits,” without
p81 increasing the current limit on borrowing for projects.
p81 What A “Yes” Or “No” Vote Means:
p81 A “yes” vote means the following: (1) grant funding and donations would be
p81 exempt from expenditure and debt limit calculations; (2) an “expenditure
p81 limit,” will be established in the initial amount of $10,000,000, with CPI
p81 adjustments beginning January 1, 2028; and (3) debt for major projects will
p81 be limited to the amounts initially established by Proposition NN as adjusted
p81 annually since 1989, currently approximately $3,300,000.
p81 A “no” vote means that Proposition NN would remain unchanged with no
p81 expenditure limit specified and the current debt limit of approximately
p81 $3,300,000 as adjusted annually since 1989 would remain in place.

p81 ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE P
p81 In 1988, Ramona Municipal Water District voters adopted Proposition NN
p81 which prohibits the District from spending more than $1 million for new
p81 capital projects, property acquisitions and/or any improvements to real
p81 property unless first approved by voters. Proposition NN included an
p81 escalation factor, and the current limit in 2026 is approximately $3.3 million.
p81 The limit includes all sources of funds, including grants.
p81 We are asking voters to modernize Proposition NN to update the limit to
p81 $10 million and exempt grants while retaining the community’s right to vote
p81 on major projects and when the District incurs debt over $3.3 million.
p81 Construction costs have increased faster than inflation
p81 Construction costs have risen faster than inflation. In 1988, voters could not
p81 anticipate 21st century environmental, permitting, and other regulations that
p81 would exist in 2026. A replacement pipeline that was estimated to cost
p81 $1.6 million in 1988 was completed in 2023 for $5.6 million.
p81 Allows District to maximize grant and donation funds
p81 Grants and donations help reduce pressure on local ratepayers and are
p81 important to help us protect affordability. These funds were not exempted.
p81 Grants move quickly and have a firm deadline to complete projects. Holding
p81 an election prior to entering a grant contract means that the District has lost
p81 opportunities for grants.
p81 Measure P maintains the voter authority established in 1988
p81 This measure retains the community’s right to vote on major projects and on
p81 debt incurred while modernizing the language to take advantage of grant
p81 funding and to adjust the threshold for 21st century costs.
p81 Modernizing Proposition NN with Measure P makes sense. It maintains the
p81 voter’s role in authorizing District debt, while allowing the District to complete
p81 timely, necessary projects and take advantage of limited grant funding.
