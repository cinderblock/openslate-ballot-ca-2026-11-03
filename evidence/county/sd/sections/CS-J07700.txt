p53 CHRISTINE HARGRAVE
p53 Educator/Principal
p53 Proudly endorsed by the Teachers of Encinitas!
p53 I’ve been a teacher, vice principal and principal at schools across San
p53 Diego. I hold a master’s degree in curriculum and instruction and
p53 researched student assessment systems at Harvard.
p53 All five of my grandchildren attend EUSD schools and I’m running because
p53 three specific problems need fixing.
p53 First, I will push for a unified, grade level curriculum across every EUSD
p53 school. In the last 10 years, EUSD has seen a five percent drop in math
p53 and reading scores across grade levels. Without a shared curriculum,
p53 students in different classrooms learn different things, creating gaps as
p53 they advance. Second, I want to institute a district-wide policy limiting the
p53 use of tech in classrooms. Currently, technology use is up to the teacher’s
p53 discretion and may be used in place of instruction. Overreliance on tablets
p53 and devices hurts students’ ability to read, focus, and think critically. Third,
p53 I want to ensure the district uses the current budget surplus to address
p53 gaps in student achievement.
p53 I humbly ask for your vote.
