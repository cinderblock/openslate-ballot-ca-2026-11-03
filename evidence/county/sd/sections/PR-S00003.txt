p81 COUNTY COUNSEL IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS
p81 The Board of Education for the Jamul-Dulzura Union School District
p81 (“District”) placed this measure on the ballot. If approved by 55% of the votes
p81 cast, this measure will authorize the District to issue and sell general
p81 obligation bonds in an amount not to exceed $18,000,000.
p81 This measure will authorize a levy on the assessed value of taxable
p81 property within the geographical boundaries of the District in an amount
p81 needed to pay the principal and interest on the bonds each year the bonds
p81 are outstanding. The District estimates the average tax rate would be $30
p81 for every $100,000 of assessed valuation, and the highest tax rate would be
p81 approximately $30 for every $100,000 of assessed valuation. The tax is
p81 anticipated to be collected through fiscal year 2062-2063.
p81 The estimated total debt service, including principal and interest, required
p81 to be repaid if the bonds are issued and sold is approximately $43,170,000.
p81 Actual amounts may vary depending on the timing of sales, bonds sold at
p81 each sale, interest rates, and assessed valuation.
p81 The measure identifies a list of projects at schools and facilities within the
p81 District that may be funded from the bond proceeds. Approval of this
p81 measure does not guarantee the proposed projects will be funded by the
p81 sale of the bonds. The District’s proposal for certain projects may assume
p81 receipt of matching State funds subject to appropriation by the Legislature
p81 or approval of a statewide bond measure.
p81 As required by State law, the measure states that the proceeds from the
p81 sale of the bonds may be used only for the purposes specified and that the
p81 District will establish an independent citizens’ oversight committee and
p81 conduct an annual independent performance audit to ensure funds are spent
p81 only on the projects listed in the measure. The District must also conduct an
p81 annual, independent financial audit of bond proceeds. The District states it
p81 has evaluated safety, class size reduction, and information technology
p81 needs in developing the projects listed in the measure.
p81 A “YES” vote is in favor of authorizing the District to issue and sell
p81 $18,000,000 in general obligation bonds.
p81 A “NO” vote is against authorizing the District to issue and sell
p81 $18,000,000 in general obligation bonds.

p81 1. The best estimate of the average annual tax rate that would be
p81 required to fund this bond issue over the entire duration of the
p81 bond debt service, based on estimated assessed valuations
p81 available at the time of filing of this statement, is $30 per
p81 $100,000. It is currently expected that the final fiscal year in
p81 which it is anticipated that the tax will be collected is 2062-63.

p81 to fund this bond issue, based on estimated assessed
p81 valuations available at the time of filing this statement, is $30

p81 projected to apply in fiscal years 2030-31 through 2062-63.

p81 principal and interest, that would be required to be repaid if all
p81 the bonds are issued and sold is approximately $43,170,000.

p82 BOND AUTHORIZATION
p82 By approval of this measure by at least 55 percent of the registered voters
p82 voting on the measure, the Jamul-Dulzura Union School District (the
p82 “District”) of San Diego County (the “County”) will be authorized to issue and
p82 sell general obligation bonds in the aggregate principal of up to $18,000,000
p82 at interest rates not to exceed legal limits and to provide financing for the
p82 specific types of school facilities projects listed in the Bond Project List
p82 described below, subject to all the accountability requirements imposed by
p82 law and specified below.
p82 ACCOUNTABILITY REQUIREMENTS
p82 Expenditures to address specific facilities needs of the District will be in
p82 compliance with the requirements of Article XIIIA, Section 1(b)(3), of the
p82 State Constitution and the Strict Accountability in Local School Construction
p82 Bonds Act of 2000 (codified at Education Code Sections 15264 and
p82 following).
p82 Evaluation of Needs. The Board of Education of the District (the “District
p82 Board”) hereby certifies that it has evaluated safety, class size reduction,
p82 enrollment growth, and information technology needs in developing the
p82 Bond Project List shown below.
p82 Independent Citizens’ Oversight Committee. Following approval of this
p82 measure, the District Board will establish an Independent Citizens’ Oversight
p82 Committee, under Education Code Sections 15278 and following, to ensure
p82 bond proceeds are expended only on the specific types of school facilities
p82 projects listed below. The Oversight Committee will be established within 60
p82 days of the date when the results of the election appear in the minutes of the
p82 District Board. No employee or official of the District and no vendor,
p82 contractor or consultant of the District shall be appointed to the Oversight
p82 Committee.
p82 Performance Audits. The District Board will conduct annual, independent
p82 performance audits to ensure that the bond proceeds have been expended
p82 only on the school facilities projects listed below.
p82 Financial Audits. The District Board will conduct annual, independent
p82 financial audits of the bond proceeds until all of those proceeds have been
p82 spent for the school facilities projects listed below.
p82 Government Code Accountability Requirements. As required by Section
p82 53410 of the Government Code, (1) the specific purpose of the bonds is set
p82 forth in this Full Text of the Measure, (2) the proceeds from the sale of the
p82 bonds will be used only for the purposes specified in this measure, and not
p82 for any other purpose, (3) the proceeds of the bonds, when and if issued,
p82 will be deposited into a building fund to be held by the San Diego County
p82 Treasurer, as required by the California Education Code, and (4) the
p82 Superintendent of the District shall cause an annual report to be filed with
p82 the District Board, which report shall contain information regarding the
p82 amount of funds collected and expended, as well as the status of the projects
p82 listed in this measure, as required by Sections 53410 and 53411 of the
p82 Government Code.
p82 NO TEACHER OR ADMINISTRATOR SALARIES
p82 Proceeds from the sale of bonds authorized by this measure shall be used
p82 only for the purposes specified in Article XIII A, Section 1(b)(3), those being
p82 for the construction, reconstruction, rehabilitation, or replacement of school
p82 facilities, including the furnishing and equipping of school facilities, and the
p82 acquisition or lease of school facilities, and not for any other purpose,
p82 including teacher and administrator salaries and other school operating
p82 expenses.
