p54 CHUCK KAYE
p54 Retired Police Chief
p54 I will bring more than 33 years of proven leadership, public safety
p54 experience, and problem-solving to the El Cajon City Council. I served 28
p54 years with the San Diego Police Department, retiring as an Assistant Chief,
p54 and five years as Chief of Police for the City of Coronado.
p54 I’m running because I believe El Cajon’s best days are ahead. Every resident
p54 deserves to feel safe, every business deserves the opportunity to succeed,
p54 and every family deserves a city they are proud to call home.
p54 I will work to attract new businesses, expand dining and entertainment
p54 options, and create good-paying jobs that strengthen our local economy. I
p54 support redeveloping Parkway Plaza into a vibrant destination with pride of
p54 ownership housing, jobs, and new opportunities, while revitalizing downtown
p54 in a way that preserves its unique character.
p54 A strong economy provides the resources needed to maintain excellent
p54 police, fire protection, parks, roads, and other essential city services. I will
p54 bring experienced leadership, a commitment to public safety, and a
p54 common-sense approach to responsible growth to help build a safer,
p54 stronger, and more prosperous El Cajon for today and future generations.
