p54 BRUCE EHLERS
p54 Mayor, City of Encinitas
p54 Bruce will preserve the small-scale beach community that we love. He
p54 supports local land use control and opposes state mandates. He authored
p54 Proposition A, “The Encinitas Right to Vote Initiative” approved by voters in
p54 2013 to guarantee residents a voice in our city’s growth.
p54 Since elected Mayor in 2024, Bruce accelerated road paving, increased
p54 traffic enforcement, added deputies, and funded deferred infrastructure
p54 projects, fulfilling his “basic infrastructure first” commitment.
p54 Under Bruce’s leadership, homelessness dropped by 30%. More remains to
p54 be done and he will continue to provide necessary services while enforcing
p54 our laws to make public spaces safe for all.
p54 Bruce will continue to focus on beach sand replenishment to protect our
p54 bluffs and enhance beach recreational opportunities.
p54 Bruce remains committed to protecting our environment through open space
p54 preservation, native plant restoration, North Highway 101 tree canopy
p54 restoration, and greenhouse gas emission reduction.
p54 Elected Mayor in 2024 and City Council member in 2022, Bruce served twice
p54 on the Planning Commission, most recently as Chairman, where he
p54 opposed excessive development.
p54 Bruce and his wife have called Encinitas home for 42 years and raised three
p54 daughters here.
p54 Experience counts, now more than ever. Re-elect Bruce!

p54 RIMGA VISKANTA
p54 AGE: 52
p54 Accounting Manager, School Board Trustee
p54 For 20 years, I have called this coastal community home. I raised my
p54 children here. I know what makes it special.
p54 I am running for Mayor to shape Encinitas for the next generation. I
p54 believe local action is how we take control of our future.
p54 I govern through collaboration, not combativeness. Where others see
p54 divides, I find common ground.
p54 I served eight years on two school boards, including three terms as
p54 President. During the pandemic, I led Encinitas Union School District’s
p54 safe school reopening. At San Dieguito Union High School District, I
p54 restored stability after division, built new facilities, and turned deficits
p54 into a surplus.
p54 I know how cities work from the inside. I worked for the City of Solana
p54 Beach in the Finance Department and City Manager’s Office, where I
p54 turned policy into practice, accounted for taxpayer dollars, and improved
p54 city services.

p54 Make streets safer for pedestrians, bicyclists, and drivers through
p54 data-driven investments.
p54 Support small-scale housing that fits our neighborhoods and public
p54 services.
p54 Safeguard our coastline and prepare for wildfire, flooding, and extreme
p54 heat.
p54 Invest in infrastructure through disciplined budgeting.
p54 Prevent homelessness through regional collaboration while keeping public
p54 spaces safe and welcoming.
