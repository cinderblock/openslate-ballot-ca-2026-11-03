p83 COUNTY COUNSEL IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS
p83 PERMANENT ROAD DIVISION ZONE 13A PALA MESA SPECIAL TAX
p83 MEASURE
p83 The Board of Supervisors for the County of San Diego placed this
p83 Measure on the ballot.
p83 If this Measure is approved by two-thirds of the votes cast, parcels within
p83 Permanent Road Division Zone 13A Pala Mesa shall pay a maximum parcel
p83 tax of $1,786 per parcel for each developed parcel and $893 per parcel for
p83 each undeveloped parcel commencing in Fiscal Year 2027-2028. The stated
p83 purpose of the special tax is to fund ongoing road repair and maintenance,
p83 which includes administrative expenses. The special tax will continue to be
p83 collected in the Permanent Road Division until amended, repealed or
p83 suspended by the Board of Supervisors. The special tax will replace the
p83 existing assessment imposed in the Permanent Road Division by repealing
p83 Ordinance No. 9171. Total estimated revenue derived from the special tax
p83 from the 305 developed and 40 undeveloped parcels currently in the
p83 Permanent Road Division for the first fiscal year is $580,450. The special
p83 tax is subject to adjustment, not to exceed 5% per fiscal year to account for
p83 any inflation as measured by the Los Angeles Construction Cost Index or a
p83 comparable Southern California construction cost index.
p83 A “YES” vote is in favor of authorizing the County of San Diego to replace
p83 the existing assessment with a special tax of up to $1,786 per developed
p83 parcel and up to $893 per undeveloped parcel to fund ongoing road repair
p83 and maintenance within the Permanent Road Division.
p83 A “NO” vote is against authorizing the County of San Diego to replace the
p83 existing assessment with a special tax $1,786 per developed parcel and up
p83 to $893 per undeveloped parcel to fund ongoing road repair and
p83 maintenance within the Permanent Road Division.
p83 The above statement is an impartial analysis of Ordinance or
p83 Measure T. If you desire a copy of the ordinance or measure, please

p83 will be mailed at no cost to you.
