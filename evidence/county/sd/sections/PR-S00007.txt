p78 COUNTY COUNSEL IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS
p78 The Board of Education for the San Diego Unified School District
p78 (“District”) placed this measure on the ballot. If approved by 55% of the votes
p78 cast, this measure will authorize the District to issue and sell general
p78 obligation bonds in an amount not to exceed $3,500,000,000.
p78 This measure will authorize a levy on the assessed value of taxable
p78 property within the geographical boundaries of the District in an amount
p78 needed to pay the principal and interest on the bonds each year the bonds
p78 are outstanding. The District estimates the average tax rate would be $32
p78 for every $100,000 of assessed valuation, and the highest tax rate would be
p78 approximately $59.18 for every $100,000 of assessed valuation. The tax is
p78 anticipated to be collected through fiscal year 2062-63.
p78 The District states that the tax rate limitation in Proposition S, approved
p78 by voters on November 4, 2008, does not apply to the tax rate of the bonds
p78 proposed by this measure. The District states that if the bond issuance
p78 causes the District to exceed its statutory bonding limit, this measure
p78 authorizes the District to seek a waiver of the limit.
p78 The estimated total debt service, including principal and interest, required
p78 to be repaid if the bonds are issued and sold is approximately
p78 $8,100,000,000. Actual amounts may vary depending on the timing of sales,
p78 bonds sold at each sale, interest rates, and assessed valuation.
p78 The measure identifies a list of projects at schools and facilities within the
p78 District that may be funded from the bond proceeds. The District shall set
p78 aside $567,000,000 of the proceeds for charter school projects, with the
p78 amount adjusted based on the proportion of students attending charter
p78 schools. Approval of this measure does not guarantee the proposed projects
p78 will be funded by the sale of the bonds. The District’s proposal for certain
p78 projects may assume receipt of matching State funds subject to
p78 appropriation by the Legislature or approval of a statewide bond measure.
p78 As required by State law, the measure states that the proceeds from the
p78 sale of the bonds may be used only for the purposes specified and that the
p78 District will establish an independent citizens’ oversight committee and
p78 conduct an annual independent performance audit to ensure funds are spent
p78 only on the projects listed in the measure. The District must also conduct an
p78 annual, independent financial audit of bond proceeds. The District states it
p78 has evaluated safety, class size reduction, and information technology
p78 needs in developing the projects listed in the measure.
p78 A “YES” vote is in favor of authorizing the District to issue and sell
p78 $3,500,000,000 in general obligation bonds.
p78 A “NO” vote is against authorizing the District to issue and sell
p78 $3,500,000,000 in general obligation bonds.
p78 TAX INFORMATION STATEMENT
p78 An election will be held in the San Diego Unified School District (the
p78 “District”) on November 3, 2026, to authorize the sale of $3.5 billion in bonds
p78 of the District to finance school facilities as described in the measure. If the
p78 bond measure is approved by at least 55% of the voters of the District voting
p78 on the bond proposition, the District expects to issue the bonds in multiple
p78 series over time. Principal and interest on the bonds will be payable from the
p78 proceeds of tax levies made upon the taxable property in the District. The
p78 following information is provided in compliance with Sections 9400 through
p78 9405 of the California Elections Code.
p78 1. The best estimate of the average annual tax rate that would be
p78 required to be levied to fund this bond issue over the entire duration
p78 of the bond debt service, based on assessed valuations available at
p78 the time of filing of this statement, is 3.2 cents per $100 ($32.00 per
p78 $100,000) of assessed valuation. The final fiscal year in which the
p78 tax to be levied to fund this bond issue is anticipated to be collected
p78 is fiscal year 2062-63.
p78 2. The best estimate of the highest tax rate that would be required
p78 to be levied to fund this bond issue, based on estimated assessed
p78 valuations available at the time of filing of this statement, is 5.918
p78 cents per $100 ($59.18 per $100,000) of assessed valuation in fiscal
p78 year 2036-37.
p78 3. The best estimate of the total debt service, including the
p78 principal and interest that would be required to be repaid if all of the
p78 bonds are issued and sold is approximately $8,100,000,000.
p78 Voters should note that estimated tax rates are based on the
p78 ASSESSED VALUE of taxable property on the County’s official tax rolls, not
p78 on the property’s market value, which could be more or less than the
p78 assessed value. In addition, taxpayers eligible for a property tax exemption,
p78 such as the homeowner’s exemption, will be taxed at a lower effective tax
p78 rate than described above. Certain taxpayers may also be eligible to
p78 postpone payment of taxes. Property owners should consult their own
p78 property tax bills and tax advisors to determine their property’s assessed
p78 value and any applicable tax exemptions.

p79 ACCOUNTABILITY SAFEGUARDS
p79 The provisions in this section are specifically included in this proposition in
p79 order that the voters and taxpayers of the District may be assured that their
p79 money will be spent to address specific school facilities needs of the District,
p79 all in compliance with the requirements of Article XIIIA, Section 1(b)(3) of the
p79 California Constitution, and the Strict Accountability in Local School
p79 Construction Bonds Act of 2000 (codified at Sections 15264 et seq. of the
p79 California Education Code (the “Education Code”)).
p79 Evaluation of Needs. The Board of Education of the District (the “Board”)
p79 has evaluated the facilities needs of the District at each campus and facility
p79 to determine which projects to finance from a local bond at this time. In order
p79 to address the facilities needs of the District, the Board deems it necessary
p79 and advisable to fund the specific school facilities projects listed in the Bond
p79 Project List. The Board hereby certifies that it has evaluated safety, class
p79 size reduction and information technology needs in developing the Bond
p79 Project List.
p79 Limitations on Use of Bonds. Proceeds from the sale of bonds authorized
p79 by this measure shall be used only for the construction, reconstruction,
p79 rehabilitation, or replacement of school facilities, including the furnishing and
p79 equipping of school facilities, or the acquisition or lease of real property for
p79 school facilities, and not for any other purpose, including teacher and
p79 administrator salaries and other school operating expenses. More
p79 specifically, the Bond Project List provides for the specific projects the
p79 District proposes to finance with proceeds from the sale of bonds authorized
p79 by this measure and such proceeds shall be applied only to those specific
p79 purposes.
p79 Bond Issuance Limitation. The District is required by law to make its best
p79 estimates of the ad valorem property tax rate required to be levied on all
p79 taxable property in the District to pay debt service on the proposed bonds.
p79 The District’s tax rate estimates appear in the Tax Information Statement
p79 that the Registrar is required to provide in the Voter Information Pamphlet
p79 mailed to voters in connection with the election. The limitation contained in
p79 Proposition S, approved by voters on November 4, 2008, does not extend to
p79 bonds of the District authorized and issued pursuant to an election held after
p79 the date of approval of Proposition S, and the tax rate levied to meet the
p79 debt service requirements of the bonds proposed to be issued pursuant to
p79 this measure, as estimated in the Tax Information Statement, shall be in
p79 addition to and not included in projections related to the Proposition S tax
p79 rate limit.
