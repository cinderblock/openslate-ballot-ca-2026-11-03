p54 AMANDA CHRISTENSEN
p54 Registered Nurse, Scripps Health
p54 Amanda Christensen, RN, BSN, is a registered nurse with more than
p54 14 years of experience caring for patients and families throughout North
p54 County. Throughout her career, she has helped people through some of
p54 life’s most difficult moments and understands the healthcare challenges
p54 facing local families. Amanda is running for the Palomar Health Care
p54 District Board to strengthen community health, improve access to care,
p54 and help ensure patients receive high-quality, compassionate healthcare
p54 close to home. In addition to her frontline nursing experience, Amanda has
p54 served in leadership roles where she supported nursing teams,
p54 coordinated patient care during demanding shifts, and helped guide
p54 hospital staff through complex situations. These experiences have given
p54 her a strong understanding of the importance of collaboration,
p54 communication, and supporting the healthcare workforce. As a longtime
p54 North County resident, Amanda believes the Health Care District Board
p54 should serve not only as responsible stewards of the district, but also as
p54 advocates for community wellness and healthcare access. She is
p54 committed to building stronger connections between Palomar Health and
p54 the communities it serves, supporting healthcare workers, and helping
p54 ensure local families have access to trusted care for generations to come.
