p53 Chula Vista School District community embraced our family with
p53 extraordinary compassion and support. They reminded me that schools do
p53 far more than educate children, they provide hope, stability, and belonging.
p53 Their kindness inspired me to serve the district that gave so much to my
p53 family.

p53 strengthening campus safety through partnerships with the Chula Vista

p53 increased academic interventions for struggling students, expanded after-
p53 school and summer learning opportunities, and protected funding for visual
p53 and performing arts and special education programs.
p53 I believe every child deserves a safe, high-quality education, regardless of
