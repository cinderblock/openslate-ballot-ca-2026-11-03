p78 MEASURE C
p78 To provide general city services such as keeping beaches, parks, trails, and
p78 restrooms safe, clean and well-maintained; preventing trash from entering local
p78 beaches/lagoons; upgrading emergency response/safety facilities, shall City of
p78 Solana Beach’s measure updating the 20-year-old transient occupancy tax (paid
p78 only by hotel/short-term vacation rental guests as specified in ordinance) and
p78 increasing the rate by 1% be adopted, providing approximately $250,000 annually
p78 until ended by voters, with all money locally controlled?
p78 This measure requires Simple Majority (50%+1) of those voting on the measure.
p78 CITY ATTORNEY’S IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS
p78 The Solana Beach City Council placed Measure C on the ballot. If approved,
p78 Measure C would increase the City of Solana Beach’s (“City”) existing
p78 transient occupancy tax (“TOT”) and would modernize certain TOT
p78 provisions. The TOT is only paid by persons staying at a hotel or other
p78 lodging who are defined as “Transients” by existing law. ”Operator” collects
p78 the TOT from transients on the City’s behalf. TOT revenue collected by the
p78 operator is then remitted to the City. Measure C also addresses recent
p78 market changes.
p78 Currently, the City charges a 13% TOT. If approved, this Measure would
p78 increase the TOT rate by 1% for a total of 14% and would generate an
p78 estimated $250,000 per year in additional revenue. The Measure’s 1%
p78 increase is a “general tax.” Thus, revenue generated by the Measure is
p78 unrestricted and may be used for any general governmental purpose of the
p78 City.
p78 Under the Measure, “Hotel” is broadly defined by the TOT ordinance to
p78 include any structure occupied or intended or designed for occupancy by
p78 transients (30 days or less) like hotels, inns, tourist homes, and short-term
p78 rentals.
p78 The TOT paid is calculated based upon “rent” that is charged by operators.
p78 This Measure defines “Rent” to include cleaning fees, resort fees, parking
p78 fees, pet fees, extra guest fees, cancellation charges, WIFI/Internet charges,
p78 and any other fees, rates and charges as part of the total “rent” charged, but
p78 excludes fees, rates and charges not collected directly.
p78 This Measure also modernizes the TOT ordinance so that, if a web- or
p78 application-based hosting platform, online travel company, or third-party
p78 booking vendor that facilitates rentals and payments for rentals is used, the
p78 hosting platform, online travel company, or third-party booking vendor is
p78 considered an agent of the operator for the purposes of collecting and
p78 remitting the TOT to the City.
p78 This Measure also makes provisions of state law applicable within the City’s
p78 jurisdictional boundaries. Government Code Section 50990 et seq. allows
p78 the City to access certain records of short-term rental facilitators and to audit
p78 the receipt of transient occupancy tax due and payable to the City in certain
p78 circumstances.
p78 A “yes” vote favors the Measure. A “no” vote opposes the Measure. This
p78 Measure requires a simple majority of votes in favor to pass (50% + 1).
p78 The above statement is an impartial analysis of the Measure. If you desire a
p78 copy of the Measure, please call the City of Solana Beach’s City Clerk’s
p78 Office at (858) 720-2420 and a copy will be mailed at no cost to you.

p78 ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE C
p78 It seems every list of “Top Ten Best Places to Live” and “Beach Towns Not
p78 to Miss” includes Solana Beach. While it’s home to us, it’s a destination to
p78 more and more visitors each year.
p78 More visitors mean more wear on our parks, beaches and trails, and more
p78 demand on our public safety services.
p78 Solana Beach has not increased the Transient Occupancy Tax (TOT)
p78 percentage in nearly 20 years. Measure C increases the TOT on hotel and
p78 short-term lodging stays by 1%, which is paid for entirely by visitors and
p78 tourists who stay in Solana Beach hotels and short term lodging. It is not
p78 a tax on Solana Beach residents or property owners. Only visitors who
p78 rent a hotel room or a short term vacation rental pay TOT.
p78 Measure C will help the City keep lifeguards on our beaches, keep public
p78 restrooms clean and well-maintained, prevent trash from reaching our
p78 beaches and lagoons, and upgrade life-saving emergency equipment.
p78 Every dollar raised by Measure C stays here in Solana Beach. It is not
p78 shared with the Federal, State, or County governments. By law, these
p78 funds are spent here, on the facilities and essential services we all rely on.
p78 We’re asking for visitors to help pay for what they use.
p78 Measure C revenue is accountable to you. It is subject to annual
p78 independent financial audits and public spending reports, so residents can
p78 see exactly how this visitor tax is being used.
p78 Join our unanimous Solana Beach City Council, local hotel owners, the
p78 Solana Beach Chamber of Commerce, small business owners, lifeguards,
p78 and your neighbors in voting YES on Measure C.
p78 For more information, visit www.CityofSolanaBeach.CA.gov

p79 PROPOSED ORDINANCE 545
p79 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF SOLANA BEACH,
p79 CALIFORNIA, AMENDING CHAPTER 3.36 OF TITLE 3 OF
p79 THE SOLANA BEACH MUNICIPAL CODE TO UPDATE THE
p79 CITY’S TRANSIENT OCCUPANCY (HOTEL) TAX AND TO
p79 INCREASE THE TRANSIENT OCCUPANCY TAX RATE
p79 FROM 13% TO 14%
p79 WHEREAS, Solana Beach’s local economy is based in strong part on
p79 visitors and tourism and ensuring that visitors who stay in local hotels and
p79 short-term lodging contribute towards addressing the impacts of tourism, is
p79 a responsible long-term approach for the City and community’s fiscal stability
p79 and quality of life; and
p79 WHEREAS, the City cannot depend on the State or Federal
p79 Government to take care of local needs and a stable source of locally
p79 controlled funding can help to provide essential services for residents and
p79 local businesses; and
p79 WHEREAS, the City of Solana Beach (“City”) imposes, upon guests of
p79 hotels, motels, inns, tourist homes, and other temporary transient
p79 accommodations, a transient occupancy tax of 13% of the rent charged by
p79 an operator of such accommodations pursuant to Chapter 3.36 of Title 3 of
p79 the Solana Beach Municipal Code (“TOT”) wherein the revenue are spent
p79 as follows:
p79 A. TOT revenue collected pursuant to Solana Beach Municipal Code
p79 (“SBMC”) Section 3.36.010(A) is used for general governmental
p79 purposes.
p79 B. TOT revenue collected pursuant to SBMC Sections 3.36.010(B),
p79 3.36.010(C), and 3.36.010(D) is used for the purposes described
p79 in SBMC Section 3.36.015(B); and
p79 WHEREAS, this Ordinance increases the TOT tax rate by 1% and
p79 provides that this 1% increase is a general tax in which the revenue is to be
p79 used for general governmental purposes; and
p79 WHEREAS, this Ordinance updates and modernizes the TOT
p79 Ordinance; and
p79 WHEREAS, the TOT is only paid by transient guests who stay in local
p79 lodging facilities, and ensures tourists pay their fair share to use and benefit
p79 from the City’s services and amenities during their stay; and
p79 WHEREAS, TOT revenues remain in the City, are controlled by the
p79 City, and are used for unrestricted general municipal purposes; and
p79 WHEREAS, this measure will provide funding to address community
p79 identified priorities such as preventing trash from entering local beaches and
p79 lagoons, keeping beaches, parks, and trails safe clean, and well-maintained,
p79 keeping public restrooms well-maintained and clean, maintaining lifeguards
p79 at local beaches and upgrading life-saving emergency medical equipment
p79 and technology.
