p56 COLE MARTING
p56 Wildfire Policy Author
p56 My name is Cole Marting, and I am running to protect the community my
p56 family calls home. I have lived in North County for nearly two decades,
p56 and my wife, Hayley, and I are raising our family here in Fallbrook.
p56 I understand the stakes because I live them. Like many neighbors, my
p56 family was dropped by our insurer and moved onto the state FAIR Plan.
p56 Wildfire is not an abstraction to me. I have written extensively on wildfire
p56 preparedness, and my work has focused on helping families and
p56 communities recover after disaster.
p56 I believe our fire district must do three things well: support our firefighters
p56 and first responders, get ahead of wildfire through responsible brush and
p56 open-land management, and spend every public dollar with care and
p56 accountability.
p56 I am honored to be endorsed by Supervisor Jim Desmond,
p56 Assemblymember Carl DeMaio, and Fallbrook Union Elementary School
p56 District Board President Mary McBride, leaders who know our community
p56 and trust my judgment.
p56 Good government takes leadership that shows up, listens, and remembers
p56 who it answers to. I would be honored to earn your vote.

p56 CHRIS SHAW
p56 Director, North County Fire Protection District/Small
p56 Business Owner
p56 My wife and I moved to Fallbrook in 2016. We fell in love with the area and
p56 decided we’d spend the rest of our lives here in the “Friendly Village.”
p56 I’m proud of what we accomplished in my first term. We renovated two of
p56 our five stations, broke ground on a brand-new Station 4, added a 4th
p56 ambulance, and 3 additional firefighters, all without raising taxes on our
p56 constituents. These improvements have lowered response times and raised
p56 firefighter readiness, making our community safer and giving our local
p56 heroes the facilities they deserve.
p56 Challenges remain: “fire season” is now year-round, and our District must
p56 stay ready.
p56 As a small business owner, I understand balancing the best possible service
p56 with a responsible budget. In my four years on this board, the District has
p56 maintained a balanced budget, and there’s no reason we can’t continue.
p56 I’m a consensus builder: I look for common ground to represent all
p56 stakeholders. There’s a lot we agree on.
p56 Service to this community is what motivates me. I also serve on the boards
p56 of two local non-profits: the Live Oak Park Coalition and the Fallbrook Trails
p56 Council.
p56 I care about our wonderful community and want it to thrive.
