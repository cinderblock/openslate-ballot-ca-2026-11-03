p57 DELIA DOMINGUEZ CERVANTES

p57 Why do we send our children to school?
p57 To learn to read, write, and do math. Those are the fundamental building
p57 blocks for success in school and in life. Every child should have the
p57 opportunity to learn in a safe, supportive school and receive a quality
p57 education. That is what we all want for our children.
p57 Helping children succeed is why I serve as your Trustee, and it will continue
p57 to be my highest priority. As a retired administrator with the County of San
p57 Diego Health & Human Services Agency, and through years of volunteer
p57 service supporting families in our community, including families affected by
p57 domestic violence, I have seen firsthand that every child’s circumstances
p57 are different. Those experiences have shown me how important it is for
p57 families, school employees, and the community to work together.
p57 Parents are a child’s first and most important teacher. They deserve honest
p57 communication, meaningful involvement, and to be welcomed as partners
p57 in their child’s education. Our employees make a difference in the lives of
p57 our children every day and deserve the training, resources, and systems
p57 they need to do their best work. That is why I requested an independent
p57 review of our Human Resources and Business Services operations—to
p57 identify where we could improve our processes and strengthen the systems
p57 that support their work. When we support one another, children have the
p57 best opportunity to succeed.
p57 Our district has much to celebrate, and we should be proud of the many
p57 students who are thriving. At the same time, we cannot be satisfied while
p57 nearly half of our students are still not meeting California’s grade-level
p57 standards in English Language Arts, and more than half are still not meeting
p57 grade-level standards in mathematics. Behind every percentage is a child,
p57 a family, and a future. For some children, education is what changes the
p57 course of their lives.
p57 That is why every year matters. Every child matters. Every opportunity
p57 matters.
p57 Our children only get one chance at an elementary education. We owe it to
p57 them to make each one of those years count.
p57 It is an honor to serve our community on the Chula Vista Elementary School
p57 Board. I hope to earn your vote for reelection. To learn more, or get in touch,
p57 please visit www.DeliaForSchoolBoard.com
