p82 COUNTY COUNSEL IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS
p82 The Governing Board for the Grossmont-Cuyamaca Community College
p82 District (“District”) placed this measure on the ballot. If approved by 55% of
p82 the votes cast, this measure will authorize the District to issue and sell
p82 general obligation bonds in an amount not to exceed $624,000,000.
p82 This measure will authorize a levy on the assessed value of taxable
p82 property within the geographical boundaries of the District in an amount
p82 needed to pay the principal and interest on the bonds each year the bonds
p82 are outstanding. The District estimates the average tax rate would be $24.67
p82 for every $100,000 of assessed valuation, and the highest tax rate would be
p82 approximately $24.98 for every $100,000 of assessed valuation. The tax is
p82 anticipated to be collected through fiscal year 2058-2059.
p82 The estimated total debt service, including principal and interest, required
p82 to be repaid if the bonds are issued and sold is approximately
p82 $1,246,250,216. Actual amounts may vary depending on the timing of sales,
p82 bonds sold at each sale, interest rates, and assessed valuation.
p82 The measure identifies a list of projects at schools and facilities within the
p82 District that may be funded from the bond proceeds. Approval of this
p82 measure does not guarantee the proposed projects will be funded by the
p82 sale of the bonds. The District’s proposal for certain projects may assume
p82 receipt of matching State funds subject to appropriation by the Legislature
p82 or approval of a statewide bond measure.
p82 As required by State law, the measure states that the proceeds from the
p82 sale of the bonds may be used only for the purposes specified and that the
p82 District will establish an independent citizens’ oversight committee and
p82 conduct an annual independent performance audit to ensure funds are spent
p82 only on the projects listed in the measure. The District must also conduct an
p82 annual, independent financial audit of bond proceeds. The District states it
p82 has evaluated safety, class size reduction, and information technology
p82 needs in developing the projects listed in the measure.
p82 A “YES” vote is in favor of authorizing the District to issue and sell
p82 $624,000,000 in general obligation bonds.
p82 A “NO” vote is against authorizing the District to issue and sell
p82 $624,000,000 in general obligation bonds.

p83 ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE G
p83 URGENTLY NEEDED REPAIRS WITHOUT RAISING TAXES
p83 Measure G will NOT raise our taxes. Measure G extends the tax rate
p83 authorized by voters in 2002, enabling our Colleges to make fire safety
p83 repairs and urgently needed renovations.
p83 KEEP COLLEGE AFFORDABLE
p83 The cost to attend California’s public universities is six times that of community
p83 college. Tens of thousands of East County students rely on Grossmont College
p83 and Cuyamaca College to earn college credits and learn job skills. Measure
p83 G will help East County students continue attending local community
p83 colleges and transfer to four-year colleges/universities without generating
p83 crushing debt.
p83 REDUCE WILDFIRE RISK
p83 Recent wildfires burned thousands of acres in East County, and the threat
p83 keeps growing. Grossmont College needs to increase water storage to be
p83 able to fight a major fire. Measure G adds water storage, pressure, and
p83 sprinklers to protect both campuses and surrounding neighborhoods and
p83 keeps Cuyamaca College available as an East County evacuation center.
p83 REPAIR AGING BUILDINGS AND REPLACE WIRING
p83 Measure G replaces frayed wiring and leaky roofs; renovates outdated
p83 electrical/heating systems; and fixes deteriorating bathrooms and broken
p83 plumbing.
p83 TRAIN NURSES
p83 Up-to-date labs and training equipment are necessary for Grossmont
p83 College to continue providing registered nurses for East County hospitals.
p83 Facing a statewide nursing shortage, we can’t allow training facilities to fall
p83 behind. Measure G will enable these life-saving professionals to acquire
p83 these needed skills.
p83 ASSIST VETERANS
p83 The Grossmont-Cuyamaca Community College District assists Veterans
p83 seeking to learn new skills and rejoin the civilian workforce. Measure G
p83 provides new teaching facilities to upgrade programs that serve Veterans.
