Cudahy Rent Stabilization Protection Measure To safeguard tenant protections codified under Cudahy Municipal Code Chapter 5.13, including provisions that prevent excessive rent increases and stabilize rents over time while affording landlords reasonable returns, shall voter approval be required for any increase to the 3% maximum ceiling by which rents can be increased annually under Chapter 5.13, and 2/3 approval of the full City Council be required to otherwise amend or repeal Chapter 5.13? Supporters: Elizabeth Alcantar, Councilmember; Amanda Gomez, Councilmember Opponents: None submitted.

CITY ATTORNEY'S IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE H

CITY OF CUDAHY

The City Council of the City of Cudahy (“City”) placed the Cudahy Rent Stabilization Protection Measure ("Measure H") on the November 3, 2026 ballot. Measure H would require voter approval to increase or eliminate the 3% maximum limitation by which rents for covered units can be increased annually under the City’s Rent Stabilization Ordinance (“RSO”) and would require two-thirds of the full membership of the City Council to amend or repeal any other provision of the RSO.

On June 6, 2023, the City Council adopted Ordinance No. 736, which added the RSO to Chapter 5.13 (Rent Stabilization) of the Cudahy Municipal Code.

The RSO governs the City’s rent stabilization program for covered residential rental units. It allows a landlord to increase rent on a covered unit once in any 12-month period and caps that increase at the percentage change in the applicable Consumer Price Index or 3%, whichever is lower. The RSO also exempts certain units, requires landlords to register their rental units, sets procedures for landlord fair-return petitions and capital improvement cost pass-throughs, provides remedies for tenants, and establishes administrative and enforcement provisions.

Measure H would not change the rent limits, exemptions, or procedures described above. Instead, it would only change how the City Council may amend or repeal the RSO.

Currently, the City Council may amend or repeal the RSO with a majority vote. Measure H would require Cudahy voters to approve any increase to the 3% maximum ceiling on annual rent increases for covered units, or any amendment that has the effect of eliminating that ceiling.

Additionally, Measure H would require two-thirds of the full membership of the City Council to approve all other amendments to the RSO. This includes amendments that impose a maximum limitation on annual rent increases for covered units at a level below 3%.

A “Yes” vote would require voters to approve any increase to the 3% ceiling on annual rent increases for covered units and require two-thirds of the full membership of the City Council to amend or repeal any other section of the RSO.

A “No” vote would leave the RSO as it is.

Measure H passes if it receives a simple majority of “Yes” votes.
