CITY ATTORNEY'S IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE HB

CITY OF HERMOSA BEACH

MEASURE HB – ONE-HALF CENT (0.50%) LOCAL TRANSACTIONS AND USE (SALES) TAX

This Measure would adopt a one-half cent (0.50%) local transactions and use (sales) tax within the City of Hermosa Beach (“City”).

If approved, the Measure would authorize the City to impose the tax in accordance with the Transaction and Use Tax Law commencing with Section 7285.9 of the California Revenue and Taxation Code. The tax would be administered and collected by the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration.

The tax proceeds would be deposited into the City's General Fund and could be used for any lawful governmental purpose. The Measure estimates that the tax would generate approximately two million dollars ($2,000,000) annually, although actual revenues would depend on taxable sales activity within the City.

The measure would not dedicate revenues to any specific project or expenditure and would remain in effect unless repealed by the voters.

- A "yes" vote on Measure HB is a vote to authorize the City to impose a one-half cent (0.50%) local transactions and use (sales) tax for general governmental purposes.

- A "no" vote on Measure HB is a vote against authorizing the City to impose the proposed one-half cent (0.50%) local transactions and use (sales) tax.

Measure HB will be approved if it receives a majority vote (50% plus one) of the votes cast.
