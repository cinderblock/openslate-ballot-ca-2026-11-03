CHARTER AMENDMENT MEASURE CC: Shall the Burbank City Charter be amended to remove the requirement for at-large elections of City Council members and permit the City's electoral system to be specified by ordinance adopted by the City Council or by the voters by initiative? Supporters: Burbank City Council Opponents: None submitted.

FULL TEXT OF BALLOT MEASURE CC

THE VOTERS OF THE CITY OF BURBANK HEREBY AMEND THE CHARTER OF THE CITY OF BURBANK AS FOLLOWS:

(Proposed amendments are reflected in strike through text [deletion] and underlined text [additions].)

ARTICLE 3.

Officers and Employees

Section 300. OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES.

The officers of the City of Burbank shall be those provided for in this Charter. The Council may provide for such additional boards, commissions, committees, officers, and employees as may be deemed necessary and prescribe their respective powers and duties. The members of the Council shall be elected from the City at large by the qualified voters of the City and shall hold office for four (4) years or until their successors are elected and qualified. The manner of election of members of the Council shall be specified by duly adopted ordinance of the City of Burbank. All other officers, assistants, deputies, clerks, and employees shall be appointed as provided in this Charter, or as the Council may provide by ordinance in case no provision for their appointment is made. They shall hold their respective offices or positions at the pleasure of the appointing power, except as otherwise provided in this Charter. Where the power to appoint is vested in the Council, a three-fifths (3/5) vote of the Council Members shall be required to effect an appointment or removal.

Section 305. THE CITY COUNCIL.

The legislative body of the City shall consist of five (5) persons elected at large which body shall be known as the Council. The members of the Council shall be elected by the qualified voters of the City in the manner provided by ordinance and for the term provided in this Charter. Members of the Council shall receive such compensation as may be prescribed by ordinance but not to exceed the amount which Council Members of general law cities of similar population would receive under State law, nor shall any Council Member be eligible to hold any other office or employment of the City except as may otherwise be provided in this Charter. In the event of resignation such member shall not be entitled to vote on the selection of their successor. Members of the Council shall not hold any other elective public office to which compensation is attached, and the acceptance of any such office or employment shall be deemed a resignation as Council Member.

CITY ATTORNEY'S IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE CC

CITY OF BURBANK CHARTER AMENDMENT MEASURE

Measure CC amends Article 3, section 300 and section 305 of the Charter of the City of Burbank as to the method of electing members of the Burbank City Council. The City Council placed this measure on the ballot pursuant to the terms of a settlement agreement resolving litigation against the City under the California Voting Rights Act.

Current Charter Provisions: The Charter currently specifies how City Council members are elected. Specifically, Burbank voters elect members of the City Council “at large,” meaning any voter who is a resident of the City can run for election to a seat on the City Council in accordance with the provisions of state law. Two seats are open in the presidential election year; three seats are open in the gubernatorial election year. In each election, every Burbank voter can vote for as many candidates as there are open seats on the City Council. For example, in November 2026 every Burbank voter can vote for up to three candidates for City Council. In November 2028, every Burbank voter can vote for up to two candidates for City Council. Only the voters can change this method of electing Council members by amending the Charter.

What Would Change If Voters Approved Measure CC: Approving Measure CC would amend the Charter to remove the requirement that members of the City Council be elected “at large” and allow the manner of election of Council members be specified by ordinance enacted by the City Council or by the voters.

How Measure CC Operates: Burbank voters must approve Measure CC if the voters wish to have by-district elections for City Council members. For example, Measure CD is also on the November 2026 ballot. Measure CD is an ordinance for the by-district election of City Council members in single-member districts in the map that is part of that measure. If the voters approve Measure CD, it cannot go into effect unless the voters also approve Measure CC to remove the requirement in the Charter for the at-large election of City Council members.

Removing the Charter requirement for at-large elections of Council members would permit the Council or the voters to adopt a different by-district election ordinance and map, if the voters do not approve Measure CD.
