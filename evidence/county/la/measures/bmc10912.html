CITY ATTORNEY'S IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE CR

CITY OF BALDWIN PARK

At the November 8, 2022, General Municipal Election, Baldwin Park's voters adopted Measure CB. Measure CB was an initiative ordinance authorizing and regulating the retail sale of medicinal and adult-use cannabis and cannabis products within the City of Baldwin Park. Measure CB also imposed a cannabis retailer tax on the gross receipts of cannabis retailers at the rates of 0.5% (medicinal) and 4% (adult use), which rates could be adjusted by the City Council to any rate no higher than 5%.

Measure CB did not impose a cannabis business tax on other types of cannabis businesses, such as wholesalers and cultivators.

Measure CR seeks voter approval of an ordinance imposing a 4% cannabis business tax on the non-retail gross receipts of each non-retail cannabis business in the City. A non-retail cannabis business is any business involving cannabis which includes, but is not limited to, cultivating, distributing, manufacturing, transporting, compounding, converting, processing, preparing, storing, packaging, testing, and wholesaling of cannabis or cannabis products, whether or not carried on for gain or profit. A business that consists solely of retail sales of cannabis, cannabis products, and ancillary products and does not involve any of the activities specifically listed in the prior sentence, is not a non-retail cannabis business. The non-retail gross receipts of a non-retail cannabis business are separately calculated for activities conducted under each state cannabis license held by the business, and excludes gross receipts for activities conducted under any retail license. For certain non-arm's length transactions between different cannabis businesses, "gross receipts" is defined as the fair market value of the product.

The cannabis business tax will generally be administered in the same manner as the cannabis retailer tax. Measure CR does not itself authorize any cannabis business activity; it only provides for the taxation of such activities to the extent they occur in the City. Uncompensated personal cultivation or use that is exempt from state cannabis licensing requirements is exempt from the tax.

If Measure CR is adopted by the voters, the City Council will be able to amend the Measure CR ordinance, but cannot increase the tax rate above 4%. Proceeds of the tax imposed by Measure CR will be placed in the City's General Fund and will be available for any lawful municipal purpose.

To be adopted, this measure requires the approval of a majority of the voters casting ballots on the measure. The measure was placed on the ballot by the City Council of the City of Baldwin Park.

A YES vote approves the ordinance adopting the 4% non-retail cannabis business tax.

A NO vote rejects the ordinance adopting the non-retail cannabis business tax.
