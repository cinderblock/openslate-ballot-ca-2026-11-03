CITY ETHICS, ELECTIONS, AND GOVERNANCE. CHARTER AMENDMENT EE. Shall the City Charter be amended to: increase the authority of the Police Department Inspector General and Fire Department Independent Assessor; prohibit Ethics Commissioners from running for certain elected offices for five years; increase penalties for campaign finance violations; require City Council to meet at least weekly rather than the currently required three days per week; increase referendum petition signature requirements; and make other changes regarding ethics, elections and governance? Supporters: Tim McOsker, LA City Councilmember; Katy Yaroslavsky, LA City Councilwoman; Marqueece Harris-Dawson, LA City Councilmember Opponents: Monica Rodriguez, Los Angeles City Councilwoman, District 7
