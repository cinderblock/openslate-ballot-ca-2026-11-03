Shall the ordinance establishing the office of Mayor as an at-large, elected position beginning with the November 2028 general municipal election be adopted? Supporters: None submitted Opponents: None submitted.

CITY OF PALMDALE, A CHARTER CITY COUNTY OF LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA ORDINANCE NO. 1669

AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF PALMDALE, AMENDING PALMDALE MUNICIPAL CODE SECTION 2.08.010 TO ESTABLISH THE OFFICE OF MAYOR AS AN AT-LARGE ELECTED POSITION PURSUANT TO SECTION 600 OF THE PALMDALE CITY CHARTER

A. RECITALS

WHEREAS, Under Elections Code § 9222, a charter city may submit any proposed ordinance to the voters at an election.

WHEREAS, Section 600 of the Palmdale City Charter authorizes the City Council to establish by ordinance the manner of election for public offices including the office of Mayor;

WHEREAS, the City Council has proposed an ordinance establishing the office of Mayor as an at-large elected position beginning with the November 2028 general municipal election, with the Mayor serving a four-year term as a voting member of the City Council and presiding officer at Council meetings, without altering existing council districts or the council-manager form of government; and

WHEREAS, even if Government Code Section 34900 is applicable to this Charter City, that statute still provides that the City Council may submit to the voters for determination the election process and terms for an elected Mayor;

WHEREAS, the City will still retain a voting system for selecting Council members on a "by-district" basis that affords all protected classes the rights contained in the California Voting Rights Act of 2001; and

WHEREAS, the City Council of the City of Palmdale will call a General Municipal Election to be held in the City of Palmdale on November 3, 2026, for the submission to the voters of a ballot measure proposing an ordinance that establishes the office of the directly elected Mayor pursuant to Section 600 of the Palmdale City Charter.

B. ORDINANCE

THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF PALMDALE DOES ORDAIN AS FOLLOWS:

SECTION 1. Incorporation of Recitals. The above recitals are true and correct and are incorporated herein by this reference as material findings in support of this Ordinance.

SECTION 2. Section 2.08.010 of Title 2 of the City of Palmdale Municipal Code is hereby amended to read as follows:

Office of Mayor; Direct Election.

A. The office of Mayor is hereby established as an at-large, elected position pursuant to Section 600 of the Palmdale City Charter.

B. Beginning with the November 2028 General Municipal Election, and every four years thereafter, the Mayor shall be elected at-large by the voters of the City of Palmdale for a four-year term.

C. The Mayor shall serve as a voting member of the City Council and shall act as the presiding officer at City Council meetings.

D. Nothing in this section shall be construed to alter the council-manager form of government established by the Palmdale City Charter.

E. Nothing in this section shall be construed to alter or modify the number, boundaries, or election of City Council districts.

SECTION 3. This ordinance shall take effect only upon approval by a majority of voters voting on the measure at the November 3, 2026, statewide general election and shall become operative upon certification of the election results.

SECTION 4. The City Council finds the approval of this Ordinance is exempt from the California Environmental Quality Act ("CEQA") because adoption of the ordinance is not a project pursuant to Guidelines §15378(b)(2) as it is a governmental organizational action with no potential for a physical environmental impact. Alternatively, the ordinance is exempt under Guidelines §15061(b)(3) because there is no possibility it will result in a significant effect on the environment.

SECTION 5. Severability. If any section, subsection, subdivision, paragraph, sentence, clause or phrase in this Ordinance or any part thereof is for any reason held to be unconstitutional or invalid or ineffective by any court of competent jurisdiction, such decision shall not affect the validity or effectiveness of the remaining portions of this Ordinance or any part thereof. The City Council hereby declares that it would have passed each section, subsection, subdivision, paragraph, sentence, clause or phrase thereof irrespective of the fact that any one or more subsections, subdivisions, paragraphs, sentences, clauses or phrases be declared unconstitutional, or invalid, or ineffective, provided the basic purposes of this Ordinance and the benefits to the City and the public are not substantially impaired.

SECTION 6. The City Clerk shall certify to the adoption of this ordinance and shall cause this ordinance to be published or posted as required by law.
