Rancho Palos Verdes Public Safety/City Services Measure: To help prepare for and respond to wildfires/natural disasters; maintain roads/storm drains; prevent sinkholes; address landslides/land movement; and support general city services, shall the transient occupancy tax (paid only by hotel/motel guests) be adjusted from 10% to 13%, providing approximately $2,200,000 annually until ended by voters, requiring audits, spending disclosures, all funds benefiting Rancho Palos Verdes which cannot be taken by the State or Los Angeles County, be adopted? Supporters: PVP Chamber of Commerce; RPV Neighborhood Watch; Wayfarers Chapel; PVP Montessori School; ret. Judge Gregory O'Brien Opponents: None submitted.

CITY ATTORNEY'S IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE RS

Measure RS was placed on the November 3, 2026 General Municipal Election ballot by a unanimous vote of the Rancho Palos Verdes City Council. If approved by a majority of voters, this Measure will increase the Transient Occupancy Tax or “TOT” (paid only by hotel/motel guests) from 10% to 13% and provide an additional $2,200,000 annually to the City’s General Fund for use toward general City services, including but not limited to public safety, emergency preparedness and response, street maintenance, and wildfire mitigation.

The TOT is a general tax paid only by guests staying at hotels and motels in Rancho Palos Verdes for 30 days or less. All revenue generated by this Measure would be locally controlled by the City and subject to annual audits and funding disclosures.

Your YES vote means you approve of the proposed increase of the City’s TOT from 10% to 13%.

Your NO vote means you disapprove of the proposed increase to the City’s TOT at this time.

This Measure would take effect only if a majority of those registered Rancho Palos Verdes voters voting on Measure RS casts a “Yes” vote at the November 3, 2026 General Municipal Election. This new TOT rate would take effect ten (10) days after the City Council certifies the election results by resolution and would amend Section 3.16.030, of Title 3.16, of the Rancho Palos Verdes Municipal Code to reflect this change.
