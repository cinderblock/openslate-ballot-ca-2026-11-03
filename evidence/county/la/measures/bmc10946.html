Keep Cudahy Sales Tax Revenues Local Measure To preserve and continue the City's existing local funding stream, which may be used to support general City services, including, but not limited to, street, sidewalk and City building repair; park maintenance and enhancements; and youth and recreational programs, shall the City's existing 3/4% transactions (sales) and use tax be extended indefinitely, unless repealed by voters, providing approximately $1.3 million annually for local use? Supporters: Council Members, Elizabeth Alcantar; Martin U. Fuentes; Amanda Gomez; Cynthia Gonzalez, Mayor; Daisy Lomeli, Vice Mayor Opponents: None submitted.

CITY ATTORNEY'S IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE OK

KEEP CUDAHY SALES TAX REVENUES LOCAL MEASURE

The City Council of the City of Cudahy (“City”) placed the Keep Cudahy Sales Tax Revenues Local Measure (“Measure”) on the November 3, 2026, General Election ballot. If approved by a simple majority (50% plus 1) of votes cast at a November 3, 2026, General Election of the City, the Measure will continue the City’s existing ¾ percent (or ¾¢) transactions and use tax indefinitely, until repealed by the voters.

Voters approved the City’s existing transactions and use tax in 2018, with an expiration date of March 31, 2029. This Measure will remove the expiration date.

COLLECTION: The transactions and use tax (the “Tax”) is paid in addition to the existing County-wide sales tax on sales of tangible goods and is collected at the same time and in much of the same manner as the standard sales tax. The Tax applies to most over-the-counter sales of tangible goods and restaurant sales. For purchases of big-ticket items (e.g., appliances and furniture), the Tax applies only if the seller delivers the item to a Cudahy address from a point of sale located within or outside of the City. For automobile purchases, the Tax applies only if the vehicle purchased is registered to a Cudahy address.

USES: The Tax is a “general tax” per Proposition 218, meaning its proceeds may be used for any public purpose/need of the City, including but not limited to maintenance/repair of streets, sidewalks, City buildings, and parks; youth and recreational programs.

PURCHASES NOT SUBJECT TO THE TAX: The Tax does not apply to purchases of prescription drugs or to most basic grocery store food purchases for items like milk, meat, fish, vegetables, fruit, cereals, coffee, sugar, baby food, bread and other bakery products, nuts, condiments, candy, juices, bottled water and the like.

NON-RESIDENTS PAY: Non-residents share in paying the Tax for most over-the-counter retail purchases they make in the City.

LOCAL RETENTION OF TAX REVENUES: Unlike the Statewide sales tax whose proceeds are shared among various public agencies, revenue from the City’s existing ¾ percent transactions and use tax goes exclusively to the City, and revenue from the Tax would continue to go to the City exclusively if the Measure is approved. Because State law limits the combined rates of city and county transactions and use taxes, if the City’s existing tax expires, the County or another regional agency could take up more of the shared cap. Approval of the Measure would mean the ¾% percent portion of the shared cap will be retained by the City and cannot be seized by another taxing agency, including the County.
