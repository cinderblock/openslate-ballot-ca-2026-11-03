Shall members of the Board of Education of the Hacienda La Puente Unified School District be limited to serving no more than two (2) four-year terms during their lifetime regardless of the trustee area represented? Supporters: None submitted Opponents: None submitted.

IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE B

By Dawyn R. Harrison, County Counsel

This measure was placed on the ballot by the governing board (Board) of the Hacienda La Puente Unified School District (District). If approved by a majority of the votes cast on the measure, this measure will establish a limit on the number of terms an elected Board member may serve to two (2) full four-year terms during their lifetime.

There is currently no limit on the number of terms that an elected member may serve on the Board. This measure establishes a limit on the maximum number of terms an elected member may serve on the Board to two (2) full four-year terms during that elected member's lifetime, regardless of the trustee area represented.

If approved, this measure will apply prospectively and any full four-year term served on or after November 3, 2026 will count toward the term limit.
