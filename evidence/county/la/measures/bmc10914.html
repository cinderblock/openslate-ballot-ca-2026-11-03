Ordinance To Eliminate Advertising on Public Right-Of-Way. Shall the ordinance to amend the General Plan and Inglewood Municipal Code to eliminate the City's authority to allow advertising on the public right-of-way and certain available properties, resulting in a loss of general fund revenues, be approved? Supporters: SoFi Stadium; Kia Forum; Intuit Dome; Former State Senator Steven Bradford; Pastor KW Tulloss; Edna Cabrera; Charles Braggs Opponents: None submitted.
