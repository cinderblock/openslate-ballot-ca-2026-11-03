STATEMENT OF TIZOC CRUZ-GONZALEZ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 5

SAN GABRIEL VALLEY MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICT

I am running for the SGVMWD board to ensure our community has SAFE, SUSTAINABLE, and AFFORDABLE water. Climate change and other growing threats are pushing our system beyond what it was originally designed to handle.

For the past century, our water infrastructure has served us well. Now we must strengthen it for the future without driving up rates. We need a reliable and resilient system that can adapt to whatever comes next.

As a fifth-generation Azusan, I want to ensure that future generations can live sustainably and affordably in our community. I have a Bachelor of Science degree from MIT, a Doctorate in mechanical engineering from the University of Michigan, and work as an aerospace engineer on the next human lunar lander.

That technical background helps me evaluate complex systems, ask the right questions, and focus on practical solutions. The future decisions of the water board will have real impacts on our lives and how we live.

I will bring new, fresh, and innovative ideas to maintain our water supply without sacrificing affordability.

I would be honored to earn your vote on election day.

- Dr. Tizoc Cruz-Gonzalez
