STATEMENT OF KAREN K. DAVIS

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
4th District

CITY OF GLENDORA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 64

Occupation: Pastor

My family and I have enjoyed being in Glendora for more than 30 years. For the past 21 years, it has been my privilege to serve on your City Council, always putting our community first. During that time, we've strengthened Glendora by investing in what matters most: public safety, maintaining streets and renovating parks, responsible budgeting, and projects that improve everyday life, including the Glendora Public Market, Urban Trails, Village enhancements, transportation connections to the A Line, and the Diamond Ridge Center. I believe a strong community is one where people of every age and every neighborhood have the opportunity to thrive. That's why I've supported quality parks, local businesses, open space preservation, improved city facilities, and a variety of housing options so that more people can build their future in Glendora. As your councilmember, I will continue listening, leading with integrity and compassion, and making thoughtful decisions that protect what makes Glendora unique while preparing for the future. I respectfully ask for your vote on November 3, 2026, so together we can continue building a safe, welcoming, and thriving Glendora for generations to come.
