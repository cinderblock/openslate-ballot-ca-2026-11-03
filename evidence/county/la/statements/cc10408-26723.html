STATEMENT OF AMY J. WONG

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
First District

CITY OF ALHAMBRA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

I'm Amy J. Wong and I'm running for Alhambra City Council to use my experience bringing people together, solving problems, and delivering results to help Alhambra preserve what makes our city special and prepare for the opportunities ahead.

I'm the daughter of immigrants and a first-generation college graduate. After studying Public Health at UC Berkeley, I dedicated my career to building healthier, more vibrant communities and currently work as an advisor with South Coast Air Quality Management District to protect public health. With ActiveSGV, I've worked to make San Gabriel Valley communities more resilient by helping deliver millions in public investments to improve our streets, parks, and air quality.

Recently, I helped lead the successful campaign to ban data centers in Monterey Park to protect our region's health and quality of life.

As an Alhambra Historic Preservation Commissioner and former Planning Commissioner in our region, I've focused on improving public engagement, ensuring residents have a voice in shaping our community's future.

As City Councilmember, I'll continue to work with residents, schools, local businesses, and regional leaders to improve neighborhood safety, focus on affordability, and invest in solutions that create healthier communities.

I humbly ask for your vote.
