STATEMENT OF LAURA OLHASSO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 4

FOOTHILL MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICT

Occupation: Appointed Incumbent

The guaranteed delivery of sufficient safe water to our homes faces increasing threats. As supply from the stressed Colorado River becomes less reliable, there will be increased pressure to find alternative sources of water. Those water sources will be more expensive. Aging infrastructure must be replaced. Our resilience during emergencies such as fire and earthquake must be ensured. All of which needs to be accomplished with an eye toward impacts on us taxpayers.

Addressing these serious issues requires both expertise and experience. Each of the four FMWD directors with whom I serve possess water expertise, having been involved in the water community for years. But a robust board is a well-rounded one utilizing all the necessary skills needed to work together successfully. My twelve years on the LCF City Council, including three terms as your mayor, have given me the knowledge and relationships to work effectively with the Council and staff. My background as a government affairs consultant will be invaluable as we petition the state and the federal governments for funding.

It is my broader experience that was the deciding factor in my appointment to this seat. Now I ask for your vote to continue working on your behalf.
