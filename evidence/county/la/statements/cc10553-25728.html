STATEMENT OF ROBERT GONZALES

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 5

SAN GABRIEL VALLEY MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICT

As Vice Chair of the Water Quality Authority and a 19-year member of the Azusa City Council, I have dedicated my public service to protecting our communities, strengthening infrastructure, and ensuring responsible stewardship of public resources.

Throughout my service, including as Mayor from 2020 to 2025, I have worked to strengthen our community through sound financial stewardship, economic growth, and investments that improve quality of life.

As Vice Chair of the Water Quality Authority, I have partnered with local, state, and federal agencies to protect the San Gabriel Valley's water supply and secure millions of dollars for water infrastructure and groundwater cleanup projects. Among these efforts is restoring Azusa's Aspen Well, returning an important local water source to service after years of contamination. I also worked with the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency to advance initiatives that protect the San Gabriel River and strengthen our region's environmental health.

I am committed to ensuring safe, reliable, and affordable water, protecting ratepayer dollars, and planning responsibly for our region's future. I would be honored to earn your support and serve as your representative on the San Gabriel Valley Municipal Water District Board.

Vice Chair, Water Quality Authority/Former Azusa Mayor
