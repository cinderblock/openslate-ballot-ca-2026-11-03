STATEMENT OF LINDSAY DAVIS

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF SANTA MONICA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 44

Occupation: Yoga Instructor

I am Lindsay Davis, also known as Daisy the Venice Healer.

I have been a member of this community for most of my life, and for nearly a decade I have dedicated myself to creating spaces rooted in connection, healing, service, and environmental stewardship.

My vision for Santa Monica is built on three pillars: Awakening, Biodiversity, and Fellowships. I believe thriving communities begin with personal growth, healthy ecosystems, and meaningful human relationships.

Through community-led and volunteer-run biodiverse food forests, healing circles, beach cleanups, mentoring and service, I have worked to nurture the heart of our community. I bring a spirit of care, compassion and a mothering presence focused on helping people feel connected and valued.

Together, we can restore our environment, strengthen neighborhood bonds, reduce isolation, and create a Santa Monica where every resident has the opportunity to belong, contribute, and flourish.
