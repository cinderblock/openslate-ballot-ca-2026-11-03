STATEMENT OF CHARLIE J. CHAVEZ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS

LA PUENTE VALLEY COUNTY WATER DISTRICT

Occupation: Public Servant

My name is Charlie Chavez. I am a resident in the City of La Puente, a father, a public servant, and a longtime community volunteer. I am invested in our community and care deeply about the future of our city and the quality of life of its residents.

I hold a Master of Science in Emergency Management from the University of Southern California, a Bachelor of Arts in Communication from California State University, Los Angeles, and an Associate of Arts in Communication from Rio Hondo College. My professional experience in public service has provided me with leadership, problem-solving, and emergency management skills, along with an understanding of accountability and responsible management of public resources.

I have served on the Parks and Recreation Commission and volunteered with youth and family programs, including AYSO, CASA, and local after-school programs. These experiences have strengthened my commitment to community service and responsive leadership.

As a resident and homeowner, I understand the importance of dependable infrastructure, transparency, and responsible decision-making. I am seeking election to the La Puente Valley County Water District Board to listen to residents, provide accountable leadership, and help ensure the district serves our community professionally and effectively.

I RESPECTFULLY ASK FOR YOUR VOTE.
