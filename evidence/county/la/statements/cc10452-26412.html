STATEMENT OF RYAN W. BALDINO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF EL SEGUNDO GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 51

Occupation: Council Member/Attorney

Twenty-five years ago. I fell in love with El Segundo when my wife began teaching music at Richmond Street School. We moved here soon after and raised our two sons in this remarkable community. I'm running for re-election to continue serving the town that's given us so much.

As an attorney with more than two decades of experience resolving complex disputes, I bring practical, independent judgment to every decision. Before being elected to City Council, I served 14 years on the Planning Commission and chaired the School Bond Citizens Oversight Committee. In 2024, my fellow Council Members honored me by selecting me as Mayor Pro Tem.

As your Council Member, I've kept our budget balanced, supported our first responders, and spent taxpayers' dollars responsibly. I created the Hyperion Ad Hoc Subcommittee, helping pass AB 1216 to require permanent air quality monitoring and public reporting. I've championed long-overdue park improvements, including the plunge renovation and adding new green space at Hilltop Park.

I'm proud of what we've accomplished together, and I respectfully ask for your vote so I can continue working for El Segundo.
