STATEMENT OF NOOSHA RAZAVIAN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 5

FOOTHILL MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICT

Age: 31

Occupation: Resource Specialist

As a Water Resource Specialist at the Metropolitan Water District of Southern California, I spend my days working on solving the most difficult water problems throughout the region. I began my decade-long career in the water sector after graduating from the University of California, Berkeley with a degree in Environmental Economics. My husband and I have lived in the Foothill region for over six years. We cherish the Foothill community, and look forward to many more years here.

With the growing impacts of climate change, it is critical that our community develops infrastructure and supply management plans for responding to both emergencies and drought periods. These plans must be financially prudent and respect the rate-payers who fund them. My professional and personal experience has prepared me to navigate the complex economic, political, and legal challenges facing the Foothill Municipal Water District Board. My goal is to ensure safe, reliable, and equitable access to water at the lowest cost for the residents of the Foothill region.

I humbly request your support as I seek to serve you on the Foothill Municipal Water District Board and guide our community to dependable and cost effective access to water for years to come.
