STATEMENT OF ANGELICA GARCIA

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

VALLE LINDO SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 50

Occupation: High School Counselor

My name is Angelica Garcia, a lifelong resident of South El Monte. As a High School Counselor, I see the impact of education daily. My educational background includes a B.A. in Psychology with a Minor in Education from UCLA and a M.A. in Counseling, Pupil Services Credential, Child Welfare & Attendance Credential and a Behavior Intervention Case Management Certification from CSULA.

Our public schools are the heart of our community. Every child in the Valle Lindo School District deserves a high-quality education in a safe, supportive environment. My vision centers on four key pillars:

- Parent & Community Engagement: Giving families and educators a real seat at the table where their voices are heard.
- Academic Excellence: Providing access to a rigorous curriculum, modern technology, expanded STEM and arts programs.
- Teacher & Staff Support: Empowering educators with resources and professional development to thrive in the classroom.
- Facilities and Infrastructure: Having an educational environment that is well-maintained and built for the 21st century.

Strong schools build strong communities. I pledge to listen to parents, collaborate with staff, and keep our students' best interests at the forefront. I would be honored to earn your vote and help shape the next generation of leaders.
