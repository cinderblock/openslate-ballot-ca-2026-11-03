STATEMENT OF MONICA GARCIA

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 2

GLENDORA UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

It has been an honor to serve as your Glendora Unified School District Trustee. As both a GUSD parent and your elected Trustee, I understand that outstanding schools strengthen our community, protect our investment in Glendora, and prepare the next generation for success.

As your Trustee, my priorities will be:

- Building on Excellence - Preserving GUSD's tradition of academic achievement and preparing every student for college, careers, and life.

- Responsible Stewardship - Making transparent, accountable decisions that protect taxpayer dollars, invest wisely in our schools, and ensure every dollar benefits students.

- Students First - Supporting outstanding teachers, maintaining safe schools, expanding opportunities for every student to succeed, and partnering with parents to help every child reach their full potential.

Experience matters. Proven leadership matters. I have served our community with integrity, listened carefully, worked collaboratively, and made thoughtful decisions focused on what is best for Glendora.

I will continue to lead with honesty, transparency, and a steadfast commitment to preserving the excellence that makes GUSD a source of pride for our entire community.

I respectfully ask for your vote so I can continue building on excellence and serving our community with integrity, experience, and an unwavering commitment to every student.
