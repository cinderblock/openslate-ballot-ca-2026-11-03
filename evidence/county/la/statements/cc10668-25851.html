STATEMENT OF FALYNN LOVE THOMPSON

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

ROWLAND UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

I'm Falynn Love Thompson, a Rowland Unified parent, business owner, and community member for over 20 years. I chose to raise my family in the community my husband grew up in, and I am a proud parent of a Rowland High School graduate and a daughter still navigating Rowland Unified. I'm running for the Rowland Unified School District Board of Education, Trustee Area 5, to build a comprehensive support system for our students. I will work to elevate RUSD's status as a competitive school district while ensuring students' academic, mental, and behavioral needs are met. I will advocate for competitive compensation, benefits, and growth opportunities for certificated and classified staff. I will nurture a partnership between the district and families rooted in two-way communication, accountability, and opportunities for families to be part of their students' education. I will work to make sure every school receives the same standard of care to promote positive learning environments. I will work to ensure district spending aligns with community priorities and has maximum positive impact. I'm not a career politician and have no political aspirations beyond serving our Rowland Unified community. I'm running to shape a RUSD we can all be proud of.
