STATEMENT OF SONJA STUMP

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
3rd District

CITY OF CLAREMONT GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Community Volunteer

I love Claremont and think of my neighbors as extended family. While we may not always agree on every issue, we share a common goal: preserving the qualities that make Claremont a wonderful place to live, work, and raise a family.

I support thoughtful planning that respects our community values, protects neighborhood character, preserves our history, and encourages local decision-making. As a longtime small business owner and co-founder of the Claremont Village Marketing Group, I understand the challenges local businesses face with online shopping. I will support efforts to attract businesses that complement Claremont's unique character, including those that provide experience-based retail shopping.

Public safety is essential. I value our outstanding Police Department and its partnerships through CERT, Neighborhood Watch, the Explorer Program, and Community Patrol. I also support bringing back the Citizens Police Academy to strengthen community engagement. Our partnership with Claremont Unified School District is vital. It's time to update the Youth Master Plan to meet the needs of today's Children and families. As your Councilmember, I will provide responsible fiscal oversight, maintain our infrastructure, protect our tree canopy, support public safety and community service while seeking innovative ways to make every taxpayer dollar count. I will serve Claremont with integrity, transparency and dedication. Visit me at Sonja4Claremont.com
