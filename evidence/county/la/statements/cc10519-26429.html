STATEMENT OF KIMBERLY "KIM" VALENCIA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF SOUTH EL MONTE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Continuing Education Manager

I was born and raised in South El Monte, and this community has shaped the person I am today. As a first-generation daughter of immigrants, I am running for City Council because I believe our residents deserve leadership with integrity that always puts our community first.

Professionally, I work in continuing medical education at the UCLA David Geffen School of Medicine, where I help ensure healthcare professionals receive education that meets the highest standards of quality and integrity. I have earned national certifications, mentored professionals across multiple states, and presented at national conferences. I also founded the San Gabriel Valley Health Alliance to expand educational opportunities in our region.

My commitment to service extends beyond my profession. I proudly serve on our Citizens' Oversight Committee and Parent Advisory Committee and have spent more than a decade supporting local youth and nonprofit organizations because I believe strong communities are built by neighbors who invest in one another.

As your Councilmember, I will work to protect our neighborhoods, support public safety, strengthen local businesses, and ensure every decision is guided by the best interests of South El Monte residents. Our community deserves responsible development, a healthy environment, and a future where every family can thrive.

Ethical leadership is possible. I would be honored to earn your trust.
