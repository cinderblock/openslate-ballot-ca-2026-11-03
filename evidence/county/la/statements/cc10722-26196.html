STATEMENT OF KEVIN KIM

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 1

SAUGUS UNION SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 49

Occupation: Attorney

As Chairman of the Bridgeport Elementary School Site Council, a member of the Asset Management Committee for the Saugus Union School District, and father of two alums and one current student- I have seen firsthand the hard work put in by parents, teachers and administrators to provide a great education for our children. But I have also seen issues that need improving.

As a member of the Board, I will work hard to ensure transparency in our decision making process so everyone understands why we make the choices that we do. I will ask questions of everyone working with the Board so I understand where they are coming from. I will focus on sustainability and making sure that we are wise with our resources. Even the best programs or teachers cannot be sustained if we cannot pay for them, especially with potential budget cuts looming. These actions will create a process the can be trusted.

As a community, we have an obligation to our children to provide them with an education that will help them take on the world. I want this for my child and for your child, and I will work tirelessly to make that happen.
