STATEMENT OF ALICIA MIGNANO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF EDUCATION

SANTA MONICA-MALIBU UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 39

Occupation: School Board President

I'm Alicia Mignano, President of the Santa Monica–Malibu School Board. I was elected in 2022, and I'm excited to ask for your vote for reelection.

I'm a renter in the Pico neighborhood with my husband and our son, who started sixth grade at JAMS in August. As an immigrant from Mexico, I learned English and gained the skills to build a career and contribute to my community, all through public education. I serve because I know what education can do for a child, no matter what walk of life they come from.

Over the last four years, I've worked to put students first. During my first two months on the Board, I visited every school in Santa Monica and Malibu to better understand the diverse needs of our students and teachers. I've focused on improving outcomes for multilingual learners, protecting early reading intervention teachers from budget cuts, supporting healthier classrooms through upgraded air filtration across the district, and investing in long-overdue deferred maintenance. While I'm proud of what we've accomplished, there's still important work ahead to keep our schools strong.

I would be honored to earn your vote.

RE-ELECT ALICIA MIGNANO FOR SCHOOL BOARD

November 3, 2026!
