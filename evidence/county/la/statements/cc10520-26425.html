STATEMENT OF JIMMY OZAETA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF SOUTH GATE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 46

Occupation: Nonprofit Executive

38-Year South Gate Resident

Education: Bachelor's and Master's degrees, San Francisco State University

Jimmy Ozaeta is the son of immigrant parents from Durango, Mexico. Born and raised in South Gate, he attended Stanford Elementary, South Gate Middle School, and South Gate High School, Class of 1998.

Jimmy Ozaeta sees a City Hall facing governance gaps, fiscal mismanagement, and fractured community trust. He doesn't see problems beyond repair; he sees a role he's spent 25 years preparing to fill.

Jimmy Ozaeta has built his career turning crisis into stability. He has dedicated more than 25 years to the nonprofit sector, serving in senior executive leadership roles, including Chief Executive Officer of a YMCA Association, while serving on state, regional, and national cabinets.

Jimmy Ozaeta's service isn't a talking point; it's the thread running through every chapter of his life.

Jimmy Ozaeta is running for City Council because the city that shaped him deserves a City Hall rooted in honesty, transparency, and the same unconditional commitment he has always given the community.
