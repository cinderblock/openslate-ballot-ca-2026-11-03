STATEMENT OF MIREYA GARCIA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF COMMERCE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 58

Occupation: Mayor Pro Tem

Friends,

I am excited to serve as your Mayor Pro Tem and once again seek your vote.

Over the past term, my top priority has been the well-being of the residents who call our city home. I have always believed that a thriving community begins with a simple, foundational promise: every neighborhood must be safe, and every family must have the support they need to succeed.

During my tenure, we have successfully delivered several key initiatives and capital projects to uplift our community:

- Education & Enrichment: Established the Bandini Learning Center and expanded community arts programs.

- Public Safety & Infrastructure: Implemented administrative changes to improve public safety, enhanced traffic control measures, and upgraded the walkways at Rosewood Park.

- Community Pride & Heritage: Honored our military families by hosting the America's Freedom Tribute & Traveling Vietnam Memorial Wall and completed extensive cleanups of street medians and rights-of-way.

- Recreation & Economic Growth: Approved a new replacement structure for local sports activities and secured vital economic revenue by passing a progressive digital billboard ordinance.

There is still important work to do. I ask for your continued support to keep moving forward together—with safe streets, strong neighborhoods, and families first.

Respectfully, Mireya Garcia
