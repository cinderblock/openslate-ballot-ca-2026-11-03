STATEMENT OF MARIANNE RIGGINS

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF MALIBU GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 59

Occupation: Retired

It has been an incredible honor serving the Malibu community. When you elected me four years ago, I promised dedicated, community-first leadership. Together, we have made historic strides toward securing Malibu's local independence, enhancing our public spaces, and protecting our neighborhoods.

My top priority remains local control over our children's education. As a member of the School Separation Ad Hoc committee, I helped lead our team to a historic agreement with SMMUSD to split the district, paving a clear path toward an independent Malibu Unified School District.

Equally vital is protecting our community. Utilizing my deep knowledge of local planning and building safety, I am dedicated to streamlining our fire rebuilding efforts, ensuring residents can return home safely and efficiently. Furthermore, I have championed much-needed community amenities, helping secure approvals for our long-awaited Permanent Skate Park and collaborating with partners to develop community lands for youth soccer fields and a multi-generational recreation center.

I am running for reelection to ensure these critical safety and community projects reach the finish line. I will continue to protect our unique environment and lead with accountability. I respectfully ask for your vote on November 3, 2026.
