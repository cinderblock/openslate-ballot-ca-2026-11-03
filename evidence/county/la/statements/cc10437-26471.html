STATEMENT OF NICK PADILLA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF COMMERCE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 59

Occupation: Retired Commerce Employee

Hello, My name is Nick Padilla, was born and raised in Commerce, attended Bandini Elementary, Bell Gardens High school, and received AA degree at Cerritos College. Taking courses in management, Landscape architecture ROP program with the City of Commerce, learning and beautifying the city's parks and outdoor facilities. I was hired Full time in 1985 with the City of Commerce as Park Maintenance Worker and moved up in various positions beautifying our parks, facilities, and city medians. I retired in 2025 after 40 years of service. If elected City Council Member, I will do my very best to bring back the "The Model City" by:

- Strong on Crime, Improve Public Safety, having city security Patrol all residential streets and parks every day and night as they did in the past.
- Bring back our Neighborhood watch program, with block captains as it was in past
- Support Senior services and programs.
- Evaluate safety and improve parking issues.
- Open up snack bar in the Heart of our city, Rosewood park.
- Stabilize city revenue, bring in grocery market & other restaurants, stores, not be dependent upon one main revenue producer like the casino
- Beautification of city parks, facilities, medians, streets, alleys, making our city "clean and green!"
- Work hard to produce education programs and jobs for city residents.
