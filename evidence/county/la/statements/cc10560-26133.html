STATEMENT OF CAROL RAINES BROWN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 2

WEST BASIN MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICT

Carol Raines-Brown is a community advocate, educator, parent, worship leader, and former Inglewood Unified School District Board of Education member with more than 40 years of public service supporting children, foster youth, families, and neighborhoods across Los Angeles County. As an elected trustee, she represented more than 16,000 students and families while building partnerships among schools, government agencies, businesses, faith-based organizations, and community leaders.

Carol supported the Los Angeles Department of Water and Power's Water Is Life K-8 student Art Contest education initiative, promoting clean drinking water, conservation, environmental awareness, and school bottle-filling stations. As a Community Outreach Organizer for the Los Angeles Unified School District, she advances transparency and community engagement on school modernization, ADA compliance, and environmental construction projects. She also served on the Executive Board of the Los Angeles County School Trustees Association and represented the California School Boards Association.

Water is one of our Creator's greatest gifts. As faithful stewards, we must protect clean, safe, reliable, and affordable water for future generations.

As your representative on the West Basin Municipal Water District Board, Division 2, Carol will strengthen conservation, drought preparedness, landscape efficiency, environmental education, workforce development, and ensure every community has opportunities, water resources and a voice.

Vote Carol Raines-Brown for West Basin Municipal Water District, Division 2, by November 3, 2026. Thank You.
