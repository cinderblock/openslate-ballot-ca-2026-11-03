STATEMENT OF LEAH MIRSCH

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF ROLLING HILLS GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 78

Occupation: Incumbent

I have been fortunate to have lived in Rolling Hills since 1986 and honored to have served on City Council since 2015.

When I first ran for Council, based on observations gained over years as an active community volunteer and Planning Commissioner, two of my priorities included preserving the unique characteristics of our city (open spaces, privacy, equestrian, views, vegetation, etc.) and continuing our sound fiscal policies. I emphasized several areas of focus supporting those priorities, including Fire Safety Actions, Land Use/Development Policies, Disciplined Budgeting and Spending Guidelines, and Increased Resident Communication and Involvement.

Almost twelve years later, my commitment to those priorities and to the city remains unchanged. Although new issues come up, I think those original priorities remain strong pillars for effective governance of our city.

That focus also provides consistent perspective as we deal with new issues, including recent state laws restricting local government's ability to determine our own land use policies, unfunded government mandates, land movement, property insurance cancellations, and canyon vegetation growth that contributes to our very high fire risk. I continue to work to limit the impact of these and all other challenges to our community and the superior quality of life we enjoy.

Sometimes issues before the Council are complex and require us to render extremely difficult decisions that cannot satisfy everyone. But on every issue, in accordance with our understanding of the laws and our policies, the Council works together to act in the best interests of our entire community.

I work diligently to be an effective part of that process. I serve on committees to develop recommendations for Council's consideration on some of our most challenging issues, including Rubbish Collection, Vegetation Management and Disaster Preparedness.

Like my fellow Councilmembers, I strive to make the best possible decisions for the city and our residents. I have never missed a council meeting, am accessible and responsive to residents seeking information or wishing to share their opinions, and liaison to several community groups. On issues large or small I believe I have demonstrated that I try to consider all sides of a matter, deliberate openly and honestly and work collaboratively to get things done.

I hope I have earned your support and respectfully ask for your vote for reelection to the City Council.
