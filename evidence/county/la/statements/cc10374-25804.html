STATEMENT OF DENNIS YANG

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 5
(Unexpired term ending December 1, 2028)

WALNUT VALLEY WATER DISTRICT SPECIAL ELECTION

Occupation: Chief Information Officer

As a resident of the Walnut Valley Water District service area since 2002, a technology executive, and a homeowners association board member, I understand the importance of responsible leadership, careful financial oversight, and dependable infrastructure. My goal is simple: to help ensure our community has safe, reliable, and affordable water while providing transparent and fiscally responsible leadership.

For the past nine years, I have served as the Chief Information Officer of a nonprofit trade association, I oversee complex technology operations, cybersecurity, major projects, contracts, and multimillion-dollar budgets. I have also led more than 150 technology professionals delivering IT support and infrastructure services to over 30,000 employees worldwide. This experience has taught me to evaluate long-term investments, manage risk, hold vendors accountable, and balance service quality with cost.

I am also a member of the Board of Directors of my homeowners association and currently serve as Treasurer, overseeing budgets, reserve funding, financial reporting, vendor contracts, and long-term capital planning. This hands-on experience is directly relevant to the challenges facing WVWD, including aging infrastructure, rising imported-water costs, and the need to balance necessary investments with customer affordability.

If elected, my priorities will be:

- Promoting transparency, accountability, and responsible use of public funds
- Carefully scrutinizing rate proposals and capital projects to ensure ratepayer dollars are spent wisely
- Managing reserves prudently and investing responsibly in aging infrastructure
- Strengthening emergency preparedness, cybersecurity, and operational resilience
- Supporting practical water conservation without placing unreasonable burdens on residents and businesses
- Listening and responding to the needs of everyone in Division 5

Water is one of our community's most essential resources. Decisions made today will affect its reliability, affordability, and infrastructure for decades. I will review the facts, ask hard questions, and make decisions based on our community's best interests.

Public officials should be accessible, transparent, and accountable. I will bring an independent perspective, strong business and technology experience, and a collaborative approach to the Board.

My wife and I have been married for 21 years and are raising our two children in this community, where they attend local public schools.

It would be an honor to represent Division 5. I respectfully ask for your vote.
