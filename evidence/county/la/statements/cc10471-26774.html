STATEMENT OF STEVEN FRANK JOHNSON

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
1st District

CITY OF LA VERNE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 64

Occupation: Insurance Broker

I am Steven Frank Johnson, and I am asking for your vote for La Verne City Council District 1.

I have attended La Verne public schools, graduated from Bonita class of 1979, the University of La Verne 1983 and became a self-employed insurance Broker in 1983. I am a 42-year member of the La Verne Rotary Club serving as President Twice. I have served on the La Verne City Council 2003 to 2011 and currently serve as your District 1 council member. My mother, Nadine Johnson resides at Hillcrest Homes where I served on their Board of Directors.

My wife, Lori Castellano and I received the CALIFORNIA CONGRESS OF PARENTS, TEACHERS AND STUDENTS, INC Honorary Service Award presented by the Bonita Council (2007).

It would be my honor to serve to serve District 1 with my guiding principles,

The Rotary FOUR-WAY TEST:
1. IS IT THE TRUTH?
2. IS IT FAIR TO ALL CONCERNED?
3. WILL IT BUILD GOODWILL and BETTER FRIENDSHIPS
4. WILL IT BE BENEFICIAL TO ALL CONCERNED

Thank you for your consideration!
