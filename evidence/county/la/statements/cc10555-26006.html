STATEMENT OF JEFF HANLON

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 3

THREE VALLEYS MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICT

Elected in 2022, it has been my pleasure to serve on the board of Three Valleys Municipal Water District. In that time, I have built positive relationships with fellow board members, represented the district in regional decision-making, and budgeted for needed projects while keeping costs low. This is a critical time in which we must reinvest in our water delivery and storage infrastructure and consider the most fair and equitable funding mechanisms to do so. If elected again, I will continue to advocate for more water delivery and storage in our groundwater basins for use during times of drought. We must continue to invest in storm water capture, regional conservation, and wastewater recycling to ensure that our communities can thrive in this century and the next.
