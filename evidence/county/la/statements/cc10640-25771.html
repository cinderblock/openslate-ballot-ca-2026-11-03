STATEMENT OF BETTY LUZ MONROY

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 7

DOWNEY UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Special Education Mom/Businesswoman

My name is Betty Luz Monroy, and I bring extensive experience serving on public boards, commissions, and school-related leadership roles. I served 16 years as Chair of the Downey Library Board, four years on the Downey Parks & Recreation Commission, and 10 years as Sports Parent Liaison for Warren High School Athletics. I also supported Special Education programs as the Classroom Parent Liaison at Rio Hondo Elementary, strengthening collaboration between staff, families, and students.

My Priorities

Special Education:

As the mother of three special-needs graduates of Downey Unified, I understand IEPs, advocacy, and the importance of inclusive learning environments.

Balanced Three-Year Budget:

I support fiscal responsibility and transparency to maintain stability and avoid state intervention.

School Safety:

I will work to improve pick-up and drop-off systems to protect students and families.

Student Success Beyond Graduation:

I want to expand pathways that prepare students for college, careers, trades, and independent adulthood.
