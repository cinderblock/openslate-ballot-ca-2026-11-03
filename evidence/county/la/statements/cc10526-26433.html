STATEMENT OF STEFANIE N. LEILUA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF WALNUT GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 45

Occupation: Office Manager

My name is Stefanie Leilua, and I am running for Walnut City Council. As a proud resident for over ten years and a single parent of five wonderful children, I am deeply invested in our community. My two youngest children attend Walnut High School as an incoming freshman and sophomore, and my second-eldest daughter serves as the assistant coach for the WHS cheerleaders.

My commitment to Walnut is proven. I served as the Walnut NJB Administrator for five years and volunteered as a coach for over seven. Through these roles, I have had the privilege of watching out local children grow into remarkable young adults. I love our city, and I am running to ensure the current and future generations continue to thrive in Walnut.
