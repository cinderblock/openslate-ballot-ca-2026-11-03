STATEMENT OF JENNIFER R. BAGLEY

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 1

LAWNDALE SCHOOL DISTRICT

My husband and I have lived in Bodger Park for over a decade, and called the South Bay home for more than 15 years. We are raising our three children—ages 9, 6, and 3—right here in the Lawndale Elementary School District, so the success of our schools is deeply personal to our family.

As a parent, I've experienced our district from the inside, navigating enrollment, PTA and the IEP process firsthand. This has given me a deeper understanding of the importance of accessibility, equity, and inclusion for every student and family in our district.

I believe every child deserves access to a strong, supportive education that meets their unique needs. I deeply care about how our district can continue to grow as a resource for all parents and students—ensuring families feel supported, heard, and connected.

As we face challenges like declining enrollment and budget constraints, it's more important than ever that we focus on what matters most: manageable class sizes, strong teacher engagement, and partnerships with community resources that support student success.

I am committed to ensuring every child in LESD receives an outstanding education. Together, we can build a culture of excellence in our classrooms.
