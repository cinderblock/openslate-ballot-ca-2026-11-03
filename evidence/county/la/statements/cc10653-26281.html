STATEMENT OF JOSE LUIS PIÑA

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

LYNWOOD UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 49

Occupation: Business Owner

I am running for the Lynwood Unified School District Board to put students first, strengthen parent involvement, and bring greater accountability to our schools.

As a longtime Lynwood resident, business owner, Planning Commissioner, former PTO President, and former DELAC President, I have spent many years serving our community. I have served for over 15 years as a parent and President of the Lynwood Marlins Swim Team, and I currently serve as Vice President of the Lynwood Sports Association (LSA).

I understand the importance of creating safe schools, supporting teachers, improving academic achievement, and preparing students for college, careers, and a successful future.

I will listen to parents, ask tough questions, protect taxpayer dollars, and work to ensure district decisions are transparent and focused on students.

Our children deserve strong schools, responsible leadership, and every opportunity to succeed.

I respectfully ask for your vote.
