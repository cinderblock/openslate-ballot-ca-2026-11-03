STATEMENT OF JOHN WU

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF SAN GABRIEL GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 52

Occupation: Councilmember

I truly appreciate your trust and the privilege of serving you as your Councilmember and former Mayor.

Together, over the past four years, we have made San Gabriel stronger, safer, and more financially secure.

During my term as Mayor, working with my colleagues, dedicated City staff, and our community, we turned a budget deficit into a surplus, built more than $23 million in reserves, removed San Gabriel from California State Auditor's High-Risk Financial City list, and improved our streets to a Pavement Condition Index of 85, among the highest in the region.

I was proud to initiate annual Memorial Day, Veterans Day, and Fourth of July celebrations, help bring the Veterans Memorial Monument from vision to reality, establish two Sister City partnerships, and secure donated lanterns display through those partnerships that helped establish San Gabriel's iconic Lantern Festival.

Thank you for giving me the opportunity to serve our community. I pledge to continue serving with integrity, transparency, and the high standard of service you deserve. Let's protect what we've accomplished and continue improving our infrastructure, public safety, economic prosperity, parks, and open spaces so San Gabriel remains a city we are all proud to call home.
