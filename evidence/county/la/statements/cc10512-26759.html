STATEMENT OF CALVIN LO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF SAN MARINO GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 56

Occupation: Self-Employed

As the incumbent, it has been a privilege to serve the residents of San Marino. Since making San Marino my home in 2007, I have experienced firsthand what makes our community exceptional: outstanding schools, safe neighborhoods, beautiful parks, and engaged residents who care deeply about our city's future.

During my time on the City Council, I have worked to promote fiscal responsibility, preserve the character of our neighborhoods, strengthen public safety, and support high-quality city services. I believe local government works best when it is transparent, respectful, and focused on practical solutions.

San Marino's success is built on thoughtful planning and responsible leadership. I remain committed to protecting our quality of life, maintaining the City's long-term financial strength, investing wisely in our infrastructure, and ensuring our community remains one of the best places to live and raise a family.

I would be honored to continue serving our residents with integrity, accountability, and dedication. I respectfully ask for your support and your vote so that, together, we can continue building an even stronger future for San Marino.
