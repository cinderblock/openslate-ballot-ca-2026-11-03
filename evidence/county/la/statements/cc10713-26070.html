STATEMENT OF GABRIEL J. JIMENEZ

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 2

LITTLE LAKE CITY SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Planning Commissioner

Strong schools build strong communities. That belief has guided my commitment to Little Lake City School District and Santa Fe Springs throughout my life.

My passion for education was inspired by my mother, Rebecca Jimenez, who dedicated 31 years to serving students and families as a bilingual teacher in Little Lake City School District. My siblings and I attended Little Lake schools, where I learned the value of hard work, service, and giving back to the community that shaped me.

As a lifelong Santa Fe Springs resident and local business owner, I have proudly served as a Little Lake City School District Board Member, Chair of the Santa Fe Springs Planning Commission, Chair of the Heritage Arts Advisory Committee, Chair of the Little Lake City School District Bond Committee, Santa Fe High School varsity baseball coach, and mentor for the Santa Fe High Business Academy.

I raised three children who attended Little Lake schools, and today my grandchildren continue that tradition. I understand the priorities and challenges families face because I have experienced them myself.

I believe every decision should put students first and reflect the values of the families we serve. Together, we can build on Little Lake's strong foundation and ensure every student has the opportunity to succeed.

I would be honored to earn your vote.

Gabriel J. Jimenez
