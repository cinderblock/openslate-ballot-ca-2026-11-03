STATEMENT OF GUSTAVO VILLA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF MAYWOOD GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 71

Occupation: Retired

My name is Gustavo Villa, and I am honored to ask for your support for Maywood City Council. I have lived the values of hard work, family, and community, and I believe our city deserves leadership that listens, works together, and delivers results.

My priorities are clear. First, I will work to improve public safety by supporting our police and promoting safe neighborhoods where families and businesses can thrive. Second, I will focus on cleaner streets, better-maintained parks, and improvements to our local infrastructure so that Maywood remains a city we are proud to call home. Third, I will fight for transparent, accountable government that spends taxpayer dollars wisely and keeps residents informed about important decisions.

I also believe in creating opportunities for our youth, supporting local businesses, and ensuring City Hall is responsive to every resident. By working together, we can strengthen our community, improve our quality of life, and build a brighter future for the next generation.

I would be honored to earn your trust, your vote, and the opportunity to serve the people of Maywood.
