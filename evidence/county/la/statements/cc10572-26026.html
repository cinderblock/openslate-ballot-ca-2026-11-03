STATEMENT OF NASHON T. MITCHELL

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 4

ANTELOPE VALLEY COMMUNITY COLLEGE DISTRICT

Occupation: Career Development Prog. Specialist

Antelope Valley College transforms lives through education, workforce development, and community partnerships. I am running for the Antelope Valley College Board of Trustees because I believe every student deserves access to the education, resources, and opportunities needed to succeed.

As a Workforce Readiness Facilitator with the Los Angeles County Office of Education (LACOE), I help individuals develop the skills and confidence needed to achieve meaningful employment. This experience has strengthened my commitment to expanding educational opportunities and preparing students for successful careers.

As the founder of Solé of the Community, I have worked to support youth, families, and underserved communities through mental health advocacy, wellness initiatives, and community partnerships. I understand that student success requires strong academic programs, career pathways, and access to essential support services.

As your Trustee, I will advocate for workforce education, strengthen partnerships with local employers and community organizations, support student mental health and wellness, promote transparency and fiscal responsibility, and expand opportunities for student success.

I will bring collaborative leadership, integrity, and a student-centered approach to every decision. Together, we can strengthen Antelope Valley College and create opportunities that benefit students, families, and our entire community.

I respectfully ask for your support and your vote.
