STATEMENT OF ANDRE SPICER

CANDIDATE FOR MAYOR

CITY OF COMPTON GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 44

Occupation: Councilmember and Entrepreneur

Compton is standing at a crossroads, and the decision voters make this November will shape our city for the next decade.

I did not run for office to build a political career. I ran because Compton deserved someone willing to do the work. In four years as your District 2 Councilman, I have delivered for this community because the work needed to be done.

I have never waited for things to be easy before showing up for Compton. Real leadership is tested under pressure, and I have proven I will fight for what is right, stand firm, and keep moving forward.

This is not a typical election. It is a decision about what kind of city Compton will become. The momentum we have built is real. The opportunity ahead is real. The next four years will determine whether we seize that opportunity or return to the same cycle of stagnation.

Compton has the talent, history, and resilience to rise. What we need now is leadership willing to take us from where we are to where we know we can be.

From Now to Next. This is our moment. Let's take it.

Andre Spicer
District 2 Councilman
