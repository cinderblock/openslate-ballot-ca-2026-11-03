STATEMENT OF PAULA JOYCE CLARKE-PROVINSAL

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

LANCASTER SCHOOL DISTRICT

My name is Paula Clarke-Provinsal, and the Lancaster School District has been part of my life for more than five decades. I am a former Lancaster student, a parent, business owner, and community member, and all four of my children received their education in our local schools.

My journey began when I entered the Lancaster School District in sixth grade. That year, I was honored to be nominated as the Most Outstanding Sixth Grade Girl for Academics. I attended Piute Middle School and graduated from Antelope Valley High School. During my senior year, I participated in the ROP program and earned my cosmetology license, which helped put me through college.

I earned my AA degree from Pierce College and continued my education at Cal State Northridge, including student teaching at Montclair Private Preparatory School in Van Nuys.

After returning to Lancaster and starting a family, I continued working as a cosmetologist so I could remain actively involved in my children's education. I eventually purchased Hair Smith Salon, and my business became another way to serve our community. We held fundraisers benefiting Lincoln Elementary School, where my children attended, and I served as PTA President, supporting students, teachers, and families.

My commitment to our schools extends beyond my own family. In the 1980s, my salon helped recognize and support our local school pop librarians, and we attended a school board meeting to stand with them. That experience taught me that strong schools depend on teachers and administrators as well as parents and community members willing to get involved.

All four of my children attended Lancaster schools. They went on to attend local middle and high schools, where three served as student body presidents. All four became college graduates, attending Cal State Fresno, American InterContinental University of Los Angeles, UCLA, and Cal State Northridge.

Watching my children grow from elementary students into college graduates strengthened my belief that education can change the direction of a child's life.

That is why I am running for the Lancaster School Board.

I want to bring my experience as a student, parent, business owner, community member, and lifelong advocate for education to the board. I believe in strong teachers, involved parents, responsible leadership, and opportunities for every student to succeed.

Every child has potential. Every child has a dream. Our schools should help make those dreams possible.
