STATEMENT OF ODALYS VALLADARES

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF SANTA FE SPRINGS GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Santa Fe Springs needs leadership with the courage to act, the experience to deliver, and the accountability to put residents first.

I am not a career politician. I am a 20-year resident, working mother, small business owner, and community volunteer who has turned challenges into results.

As President of Metro Little League, I helped save a program that had fallen to 68 players and was at risk of folding. Today, it serves nearly 300 children. Over the last three seasons, we have sponsored 95 families ensuring that financial hardship would never prevent a child from playing.

I serve on the Parks and Recreation Advisory Committee and previously volunteered with the Los Angeles Police Department for two years supporting efforts to combat narcotics and alcohol abuse. Coming from a law-enforcement family, I will stand firmly for public safety.

I will push for action against speeding near our schools and neighborhoods. I will demand transparency and accountability when decisions, including water-rate increases, placing serious burdens on families, seniors, and residents on fixed incomes. I will also support a thorough review of plasma-donation centers, businesses whose operations create excess traffic, loitering, sanitation, or public-safety concerns, and help improve business retention in our community.

It is time for new energy, bold vision, and proven action. I respectfully ask for your vote.
