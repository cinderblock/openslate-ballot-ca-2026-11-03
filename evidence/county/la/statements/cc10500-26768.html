STATEMENT OF MICHELLE RICHARDSON BAILEY

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF EDUCATION,
District No. 3

PASADENA UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Board Member, Pasadena Unified School District

Dear District 3 Neighbors,

Serving as your school board representative has been one of the greatest honors of my life. Every decision I make begins with one question: Will this help our students thrive?

Student success depends on a strong instructional core, students, teachers, parents, meaningful relationships, and culturally relevant curriculum, as well as the dedication of classified staff, and every employee who supports learning. When we invest in our people through thoughtful planning, professional development, competitive compensation, and adequate staffing, our school community prospers.

To benefit our district, we passed Measure J, Bond Measures O and R, and Parcel Tax Measure EE. I have worked to promote equitable schools, provide meaningful resources for our neediest students, and stronger representation for our diverse community. I also authored unanimous Board resolutions affirming PUSD's commitment to Black and Latinx students, helping establish Ethnic Studies and the Immersive Reading Academy.

Over the next four years, I will work to prioritize classrooms, elevate student voice, strengthen accountability, and continue to advance workforce housing and vocational education, confront inequity, and champion inclusive schools where every student feels valued.

With Gratitude, I humbly ask for your vote on November 3, 2026.

Thank you!

Michelle Richardson Bailey
