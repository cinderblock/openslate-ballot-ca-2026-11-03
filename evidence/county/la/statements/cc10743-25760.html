STATEMENT OF YESENIA MARIA CUARENTA

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 4

PARAMOUNT UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: School Board Member

My name is Yesenia Cuarenta, and I am honored to seek re-election to the Board of Education.

As a lifelong Paramount resident, public school teacher, and youth services coordinator, I have dedicated my career to serving our youth and our district communities. I grew up in the neighborhoods of Paramount we call the "Sans" and graduated from Paramount High School. Coming from the Paramount School District, I want to ensure that every student has the same opportunity to graduate from college.

Throughout my elected service on the Board of Education, my commitment is that every student of diverse backgrounds will have access to equal education no matter what their immigration status is. I will continue to advocate for stronger academic achievement, improving students' mental health, increasing parent involvement and creating safer routes to school.

I believe leadership means listening, asking questions, and making decisions with compassion, integrity and transparency. Our school district has many strengths, and we must continue working to improve student outcomes and ensure our most vulnerable students receive the support they need.

As your re-elected board member, let's work together to help every student reach their full potential.

I would be honored to receive your vote.
