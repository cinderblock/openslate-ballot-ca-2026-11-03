STATEMENT OF WENDI MORSE

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
1st District

CITY OF BELLFLOWER GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 55

Occupation: Incumbent/Business Owner

As a proud Bellflower resident, City Councilmember, business owner, and longtime community volunteer, I understand the challenges and opportunities facing our city. My service with Little House Inc., and Calvary Bellflower's Celebrate Recovery Ministry, has shaped my commitment to practical, community-focused solutions.

My Priorities:
- Public safety
- Homelessness solutions
- Responsible economic development
- Senior and youth services

Strengthening Bellflower
On the City Council, I have supported projects that strengthen Bellflower, expand resident services, and encourage responsible growth, including the Senior & Youth Oak Center, Chick-fil-A, Chipotle, Firehouse Subs, and Northgate Market (2027).

Service and Experience
My experience supervising senior living facilities helps me advocate for vulnerable residents with care and respect. Through Bellflower Kiwanis, I have also supported local youth through Key Clubs and scholarship fundraising.

A Commitment to Bellflower's Future
With my husband, Allan, and our children, Gary and Alli, I am deeply invested in a safe, welcoming city with opportunities for families, seniors, young people, businesses, and every resident.

I will continue working to enhance public safety, support practical homelessness solutions, and encourage responsible economic growth so every Bellflower resident can thrive.

Your support is invaluable, and I am honored to serve you. Please vote Wendi Morse.
