STATEMENT OF WENDY WANG

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF SAN GABRIEL GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 28

Occupation: Businesswoman/Boardmember/Caregiver

Service has been at the heart of who WENDY WANG is. Raised in San Gabriel and educated locally, she learned that strong communities are built by neighbors who care and leaders willing to serve. Today, WENDY WANG is running for City Council to give back to the community that believed in her, shaped her, and inspired her to serve.

As a small business owner, nonprofit executive, and caregiver, WENDY WANG understands the needs of families, seniors, local businesses, and community organizations. She has worked alongside them to solve problems, build partnerships, and strengthen the community. For her, public service means helping people.

WENDY WANG believes San Gabriel deserves transparent, accountable, and fiscally responsible leadership. She will work to keep neighborhoods safe, strengthen trust between residents and City Hall, support police, firefighters, and other first responders, champion small businesses, create opportunities for young people through initiatives such as a Youth Commission, improve parks and infrastructure, and build bridges among San Gabriel's diverse communities while preserving the city's unique character.

With integrity, compassion, and practical leadership, WENDY WANG will bring people together to build a stronger, safer, and more united San Gabriel.

Advocacy. Leadership. Vision.

WENDY WANG respectfully asks for your vote.
