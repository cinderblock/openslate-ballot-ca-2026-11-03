STATEMENT OF CARLA D. DILLON

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS

CRESCENTA VALLEY WATER DISTRICT

Carla D. Dillon is a water leader dedicated to strengthening essential infrastructure and supporting the long-term wellbeing of the communities she serves. With experience overseeing engineering, operations, and utilities management, she has guided organizations through major capital improvements, water quality initiatives, and drought-resilience planning in a fiscally responsible manner. Throughout her career, she has prioritized clear communication, regulatory compliance, and operational efficiency to ensure public resources are managed wisely and sustainably.

Reliable water and sewer service depends on thoughtful planning, fiscal responsibility, and open dialogue with residents. Agency consolidation needs to be seriously considered to reduce administrative costs and stabilize costs to customers.

Carla remains committed to fostering trust, strengthening partnerships, and advancing solutions that reflect both the needs and the values of the community. I look forward to serving the Crescenta Valley community.
