STATEMENT OF RANDALL PUTZ

PARTY PREFERENCE: DEMOCRATIC

MEMBER OF THE STATE ASSEMBLY, 34th DISTRICT

For 25 years, my family and I have called this region home. I've spent nearly two decades serving our communities as a school board trustee, fire authority director, city council member, and three-time Mayor of Big Bear Lake. My public service has always focused on bringing people together to solve problems and strengthen the communities we love.

I hear the same concerns across our mountain and high desert communities. Families are struggling with rising costs for housing, healthcare, utilities, and everyday necessities. Too many people feel like the California dream is slipping out of reach. I'm running for State Assembly to make life more affordable and secure so everyone has the chance to build and enjoy a future here with dignity, stability, and hope.

As a small business owner and longtime local official. I understand the challenges our communities face. I believe in practical solutions that expand affordable housing, improve access to healthcare, strengthen our schools, and support good local jobs. I have worked across political lines to deliver results for our region by listening, respecting different perspectives, and focusing on what brings us together. This record of service is why my campaign has earned strong support and endorsements from leaders, educators, firefighters, and community members across our region.

Our district deserves a strong, experienced voice in Sacramento. I will fight for the resources and attention our communities need and deserve. I would be honored to earn your vote.
