STATEMENT OF CARRIE UVA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
4th District

CITY OF DOWNEY GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 61

Occupation: Estate Planning Attorney & Real Estate Broker

Hi Downey! My name is CARRIE UVA. I have called Downey home for over 60 years. As a Trust Attorney, Real Estate Broker and senior advocate, my life's work is deeply rooted in serving our community.

As Chair of the Planning Commission and Measure S & D Oversight Committees, I believe character counts in public service. I bring proven integrity, legal expertise and fiscal accountability to local government.

My priorities:

- Public Safety: Partner directly with the Downey Police Department to prevent crime, recruit and retain qualified officers and strengthen local Neighborhood Watch programs.

- Quality of Life: Protect our neighborhoods by improving street lighting, increasing community cleanliness and implementing effective traffic-calming measures on residential streets.

- Balanced Budget: Protect taxpayer dollars through vigilant financial oversight. Having monitored city expenditures for years, I will ensure Police and Fire remain fully funded essential priorities.

- Small Businesses: Utilize my experience as past Chamber of Commerce President to cut red tape, streamline permitting and foster a thriving business environment.

Active with the Community Emergency Response Team (CERT), Kiwanis, and local charities, I will ensure Downey residents have a strong voice at City Hall.

I humbly ask for your vote. CARRIE UVA for Downey City Council. 562-417-2068.
