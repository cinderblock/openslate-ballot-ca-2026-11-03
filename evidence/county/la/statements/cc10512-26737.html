STATEMENT OF JOHN DUSTIN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF SAN MARINO GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 62

Occupation: Quality Improvement Specialist

I have been proud to call San Marino home since 1969. As a graduate of Carver Elementary, Huntington Middle School, and San Marino High School, I have deep roots in this community. It has been especially meaningful to watch my daughter, Stepahanie, follow that same path and graduate from those same schools.

Public service has been a central part of my life. I have had the privilege of service three years on the Design Review Committee and eight years on the Planning Commission. I have also volunteered with the San Marino Community Emergency Response Team (CERT) since 2011 and have been a member of the San Marino Grad Night construction crew since 2013.

Professionally, I work in the health insurance industry, specializing in quality improvement and data analysis. My work requires navigating state and federal regulations and collaborating with regulatory agencies to ensure accountability and excellence.

My wife, Atlántida, and I are members of Ss. Felicitas and Perpetua Church, and we are grateful to be part of such a special community. I am committed to preserving San Marino's unique character, beauty, and small-town spirit, and I would be honored to continue serving the residents of San Marino.
