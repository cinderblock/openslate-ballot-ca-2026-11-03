STATEMENT OF ANTHONY (A.J.) BAYNE

CANDIDATE FOR JUDGE OF THE SUPERIOR COURT
Office No. 87

Age: 60

Occupation: Deputy Public Defender

Anthony "A.J." Bayne was rated "Well Qualified" by the Los Angeles County Bar Association's Judicial Elections Evaluation Committee.

Bayne has practiced law for more than 31 years, including 26 years as a Los Angeles County Deputy Public Defender. He spent roughly 19 years handling cases in the Compton courthouse and currently serves as a Deputy Public Defender IV. He has served as lead counsel in more than 100 jury trials to verdict, including murder, robbery, and other serious felony matters. Bayne previously served as a Deputy in Charge, supervising and mentoring attorneys, and has experience handling juvenile delinquency matters.

Bayne holds a J.D. and an LL.M. in Taxation, the latter earned with honors and is admitted to the State Bar of California.

Bayne is endorsed by more than 30 sitting and retired judicial officers, the Police Officers Research Association of California, UNITE HERE Local 11, the ILWU Southern California District Council, the Southern California News Group (including the Los Angeles Daily News, Daily Breeze, Long Beach Press-Telegram, Pasadena Star-News, and San Gabriel Valley Tribune), La Opinión, the Metropolitan News-Enterprise, the Mexican American Bar Association, and La Defensa.

Bayne believes the law should be applied neutrally, fairly, and with respect for everyone who appears in court.
