STATEMENT OF KRISTINE E. KWONG

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

PASADENA AREA COMMUNITY COLLEGE DISTRICT

Age: 59

Occupation: Attorney

Every student deserves the opportunity to build their future through a quality education.

As a Pasadena City College Trustee, I have championed the expansion of dual enrollment programs that empower students to earn college credit while still in high school and supported career education programs that prepare students for today's high-demand workforce.

When the devastating Eaton Fire changed so many lives across our community, I worked to ensure Pasadena City College was part of the recovery by supporting workforce training programs that help rebuild our communities while creating new opportunities for students seeking meaningful careers. I have also invested in modern classrooms, upgraded technology, and campus improvements that enhance learning.

I believe strong colleges are built through collaboration. That's why I have made it a priority to create and maintain productive partnerships with faculty, staff, students, and community members to ensure that the decisions we make always put students first. By promoting transparency, responsiveness, and inclusive leadership, I have worked to create a campus culture where every voice is valued.

I respectfully ask for your vote so I can continue expanding opportunity, strengthen Pasadena City College, and deliver meaningful results for our students and our community.
