STATEMENT OF MAGGIE BOVE-LAMONICA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF HERMOSA BEACH GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Boardmember, Hermosa Beach City School District/Non-profit Director

Hermosa residents deserve experienced, responsible, and pragmatic leadership at City Hall. Born and raised in Hermosa Beach, I have dedicated my career to public service, public policy, and law. Over the last 13 years on the Hermosa School Board, including three as Board President, I successfully managed $100M budgets, maintained strong reserves through COVID-19, and spearheaded $87M in facility bonds to modernize our schools.

My priorities for Hermosa Beach include:

- Common-Sense Governance: Streamlining permit and zoning timelines using a data-driven system to provide predictability for residents and business owners.

- Fiscal Responsibility: Using proven fiscal discipline to transition from reactive budgeting to sustainable, long-term infrastructure and financial planning.

- Protecting Our Community: Preserving our unique beach-city character while ensuring a safe, modern future that supports youth, renters, homeowners, and seniors.

I have lived every stage of life here—from renting as a young professional to buying a home, raising a family, and supporting aging parents. I know our community's needs firsthand and would be honored to earn your vote.
