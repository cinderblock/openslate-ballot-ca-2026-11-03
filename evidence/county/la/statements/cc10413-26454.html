STATEMENT OF MICHAEL CAO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
5th District

CITY OF ARCADIA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 55

Occupation: Arcadia Councilmember/Cardiologist

As an Air Force Major and USC Arcadia cardiologist, my duty and privilege was service to our country and the public.

You honored me to serve as your councilmember for four years, protecting Arcadia. Public safety is our top priority. As Mayor during the 2025 wildfires, I helped coordinate Arcadia's swift and successful efforts to protect ourselves and help our neighbors. Today, crime is low, our budget is balanced, and our community is safe. Arcadia has the highest possible bond rating, AAA. I am proudly endorsed by our partners, the Arcadia Police and Fire associations along with LA County Sheriffs and Peace Officers groups.

In 2022, I asked for your vote, promising to create a health commission, focus on safety, and build fiscal strength. But most of all, I promised to listen to your voices, reflect, learn and lead.

Promises made. Promises kept.

But our work is not done. My wife and I want our children to grow up in their lifelong home: a healthy, safe, prosperous Arcadia. We want the same for you and yours, too. Protecting our beautiful city and continuing to serve you would be a great honor.

Please vote for Dr. Michael Cao.
