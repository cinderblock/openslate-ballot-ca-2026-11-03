STATEMENT OF CHARLES FREDERICK HUGHES

PARTY PREFERENCE: REPUBLICAN

MEMBER OF THE STATE ASSEMBLY, 34th DISTRICT

My life can be summarized in one word: service.

Service to my country. Service to California. Service to the High Desert.

At 18, I followed my father and uncle into the U.S. Navy, where I served as a Damage Controlman. I then spent 26 years with the California Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation, rising to the rank of Lieutenant and keeping dangerous criminals where they belong: behind bars.

Today, I'm a small business owner, school board trustee, husband of 37 years, father of 5, and grandfather of 8. And every week, I host local veterans and deputies at my restaurant, because to me, service doesn't end when the uniform comes off.

I have seen how hard it's gotten. How much everything costs. And too often, it seems like Sacramento has forgotten communities like ours.

I'm running for State Assembly to change that. I'll fight to lower the cost of housing, gas, and groceries. I'll fight to keep violent criminals and child predators locked up, so kids can play outside and parents don't have to worry. And I'll fight to make sure the High Desert gets its fair share for our schools and our roads.

I didn't come up through Sacramento. I came up through the Navy, the prison yard, and my own restaurant kitchen. I'm a veteran, a retired peace officer, a businessman, and a grandfather. I've served this community my whole life. Now I'd like to serve you in Sacramento.

Charles Hughes
