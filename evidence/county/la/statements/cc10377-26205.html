STATEMENT OF JEFFERY W. JOHNSON

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS

CRESCENTA VALLEY WATER DISTRICT

Age: 64

Occupation: Attorney

I am a resident of and have lived in La Crescenta for the past 31 years. My wife and I were blessed to have two kids who attended and graduated from our local schools. Representing clients over the last 39 years, I have extensive experience working with state and Federal environmental law related to water quality standards and remediation of soil and ground water.

During my first term as an elected Board of Director of CVWD, I worked (1) to keep rates affordable while continuing to keep our aging pipeline infrastructure from failing; (2) to maintain the highest drinking water quality standards; (3) to improve our water system's viability and survivability in the face of increasing fire risk; and (4) to secure our sources of water and to improve our ability to use locally sourced water.

I would like to continue the work I started four years ago and would appreciate your vote so that I can keep serving our community as a Board of Director of CVWD.
