STATEMENT OF JEANNE OGAR

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

WISEBURN UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 48

Occupation: Environmental Scientist/Parent

As a 14-year Wiseburn resident, mother, and 20-year environmental scientist, I am running for Wiseburn USD School Board to bring independent oversight, transparent communication, and healthy environments to our schools.

I am deeply grateful for our community and our position next to world-class aerospace and technology leaders. As a scientist, I want to leverage these neighboring industries to expand hands-on STEM opportunities for our students. Furthermore, evaluating localized health risks reinforces my commitment to protecting campus air quality and neighborhood safety regarding heavy industrial developments, including the El Segundo cement plant and proposed data center.

My roots here run deep. My family participated in Wiseburn AYSO and Little League, my oldest son graduated from Da Vinci Design, and for 16 years I have navigated public education as a foster and adoptive parent of a child with special needs. Last October, our family faced the heartbreaking loss of my younger son to cancer. These experiences taught me firsthand how vital compassionate support, inclusive classrooms, and strong resources are for every family.

If elected, I will actively collaborate with our Citizens' Bond Oversight Committee (CBOC), Wiseburn Education Foundation (WEF), and PTAs to protect our kids and strengthen Wiseburn schools. I respectfully ask for your vote.
