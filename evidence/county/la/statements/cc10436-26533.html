STATEMENT OF LAWRENCE MUELLER

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
4th District

CITY OF CLAREMONT GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Software Engineer

I am Lawrence Mueller, a software engineer and resident of the City of Claremont.

I put myself through college delivering pizza in Claremont. I learned this city street by street, and earned my education doing it. I finished a degree in computer science and built a career writing software. From this I know that hard work and small details matter.

Software is a useful background for a city council member. My work is untangling complicated systems, solving problems under pressure, and reading the fine print most people never see. I am studying Claremont's public contracts and staff reports now, before the election rather than after it.

I am not accepting donations. I am funding this myself and knocking on every door in District 4, because I would rather hear what you need in person than mail you something you will throw away.

I cannot promise that I will always agree with you. I can however promise that you will always know where I stand and why, that I will do the reading before I vote, and that my decisions will reflect the people I represent rather than my own convenience.

I would be grateful for your vote.
