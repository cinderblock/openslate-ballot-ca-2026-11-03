STATEMENT OF PAWN KONGKOSONKICHKAN

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 4

POMONA UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

We owe every child more than a diploma. We owe them an excellent education that equips them with the knowledge, skills, and character to succeed in life.

As the daughter of an immigrant from Thailand who became a proud United States citizen, I learned that education, hard work, and personal responsibility create opportunity. Those values have shaped my life and guided my commitment to serving others.

Serving young people has been a lifelong commitment. I served as a Big Sister through Big Brothers Big Sisters, spent eight years at Genesys Works preparing students for college and careers, and today work as a technical recruiter, where I see firsthand the knowledge, skills, and character today's employers value.

Since early 2024, I have faithfully attended and spoken at Pomona Unified School District Board meetings because Board decisions have a lasting impact on children and families. Every decision should put students first.

I believe parents are their children's first and most important teachers. As your Trustee, I will champion parental rights, make decisions that prioritize academic achievement and safe learning environments, and be a responsible steward of taxpayer dollars.

I will listen, lead with integrity, and put students first in every decision.

I respectfully ask for your vote.
