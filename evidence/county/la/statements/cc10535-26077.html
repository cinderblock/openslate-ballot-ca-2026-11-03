STATEMENT OF YVETTE L. GONZALEZ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 2

PALMDALE WATER DISTRICT

Occupation: Small Business Owner

Working families, seniors, and local businesses in Palmdale work hard for every dollar. As a concerned Palmdale resident and a small business owner with 20 years of financial management experience, I know how tough it is when essential monthly bills keep going up.

Right now, residential users pay a high monthly base fee before even opening the tap. That isn't fair to those who conserve water. Low water users should pay a lower base rate, while high-volume users should pay their fair share for the demands they place on our system.

As your next Director, I will stand up for Palmdale families by fighting to:

- Advocate for reducing base charges so bills reflect actual water use.
- Ensure our water is clean, safe, and affordable without wasting ratepayer dollars.
- Promote full transparency so every ratepayer knows how public funds are spent.

The Water Board makes decisions affecting your rates and long-term water supply. You deserve a Director who manages public funds carefully and puts residential ratepayers first. I will serve with honesty and accountability, voting only for items that benefit District residents.

I respectfully request the honor of your vote.

Yvette L. González
