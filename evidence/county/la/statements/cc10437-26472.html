STATEMENT OF JAIME VALENCIA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF COMMERCE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Advanced Automotive Technician

Commerce is my lifelong home, the community that raised me, and where my family's story continues.

I'm Jaime Valencia. My wife Claudia and I have been married for 10 years, and I help care for my elderly parents, who also call Commerce home. I want Commerce to remain a place where families feel safe, supported, and proud to stay for generations.

I had the privilege of working as a Commerce lifeguard and later serving as a Planning Commissioner and Education Commissioner. I have also served as President of the Commerce Lions Club and volunteered to improve our neighborhoods.

After 30 years as an accountant, I became an advanced-certified automotive technician. Both careers taught me to examine details, solve problems responsibly, and consider how decisions affect people.

I am running for City Council because residents deserve a government that listens, responds, and earns their trust. My priorities include addressing vehicle theft and illegal fireworks, strengthening programs for families and seniors, promoting responsible development, protecting taxpayer dollars, and moving forward with the redevelopment of Veterans Park.

I respectfully ask for your trust, your vote, and the opportunity to serve you on the Commerce City Council.
