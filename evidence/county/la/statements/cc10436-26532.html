STATEMENT OF AUNDRE JOHNSON

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
4th District

CITY OF CLAREMONT GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 60

My connection to Claremont began in childhood. From an early age, I recognized what made this community such a special place. Over the years, I have watched Claremont grow while seeing some of the qualities that make our city special begin to change. My commitment to Claremont has only grown stronger, which is why I am asking for your support to serve on the City Council.

Claremont is one of California's most desirable communities because we value safe neighborhoods, excellent public services, tree-lined streets, responsible financial management, and engaged residents. As our city faces new opportunities and challenges, we need practical, experienced leadership.

I have served on the Sumner/Danbury School Site Council, a citywide Citizens Advisory Committee, and the Police Commission.

If elected, I will work to keep our neighborhoods safe, improve infrastructure, address homelessness with practical solutions, safeguard taxpayer dollars, and keep City Hall transparent and accountable.

I support thoughtful planning that preserves Claremont's unique character while encouraging responsible economic growth and strengthening local businesses. I am ready to put Claremont first by providing practical, results-oriented leadership that protects our quality of life and prepares our city for the future.

I respectfully ask for your vote.
