STATEMENT OF JEAN MCHATTON

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5
(Unexpired term ending December 8, 2028)

ABC UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT SPECIAL ELECTION

Occupation: Educator

As your ABC Unified School District Trustee for Area 5, I have focused on student achievement, fiscal responsibility, and transparent leadership. I ensured decisions about school closures and student transitions were financially responsible and educationally sound.

My career in public education spans service as a paraprofessional, teacher, elementary principal, and Director of Curriculum. Today I mentor future teachers.

I have also proudly served our community through Distinguished Young Women, the Cerritos College Board of Trustees, and Gahr Band Boosters. I will continue strengthening partnerships with our cities and promoting ABC Unified's outstanding schools to attract more families.

I remain committed to strong, proven academic programs, including STEM, English Language Development, the Arts, Career Technical Education, Special Education, language immersion, dual enrollment, and ensuring every student reads by third grade. I will continue supporting our district's vision of One Vision for All Students through responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars and the exceptional teachers and staff behind that success. My goal is to ensure every student is prepared for college, career, and life.

I respectfully ask for your vote so I can continue serving Trustee Area 5 with integrity, experience, and an unwavering commitment to educational excellence.
