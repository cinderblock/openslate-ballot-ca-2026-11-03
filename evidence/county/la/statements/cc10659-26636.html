STATEMENT OF XIAOHU "JEFF" MENG

CANDIDATE FOR CITY TREASURER

CITY OF EL MONTE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 53

Occupation: Business Owner

With more than 25 years of experience in finance, budgeting, accounting, and public service, I have dedicated my career to fiscal responsibility, transparency, and accountability. I earned bachelor's and master's degrees from Nankai University and a master's degree from UW with studies in Computer Science and Accounting. Through my professional career, business experience, and service in my homeowners association, I have managed budgets, strengthened financial oversight, and promoted responsible governance.

As your City Treasurer, I will work to protect public funds, increase financial transparency, oppose unnecessary tax increases, support affordable housing, and ensure City Hall serves residents, not special interests, with integrity and accountability. I am committed to providing clear financial reporting, promoting prudent investment of city funds, and ensuring careful oversight of taxpayer resources. Strong fiscal management is essential to safeguarding taxpayer dollars while maintaining the quality services our community depends on.

I believe government should be open, accountable, and responsive to the people it serves. Through honest leadership, sound financial management, and active community engagement, we can build a stronger, more transparent, and financially responsible city government that serves every resident with integrity. I would be honored to earn your trust and your vote for City Treasurer.
