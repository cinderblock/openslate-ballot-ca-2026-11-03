STATEMENT OF KRYSTLE PALMER

CANDIDATE FOR CITY TREASURER

CITY OF BURBANK GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 43

Occupation: City Treasurer

It has been my honor to serve as your Burbank City Treasurer since 2019. Every day, my mission is simple: to safeguard your hard-earned tax dollars and generate the investment returns that fund the vital city services that the people of Burbank deserve.

Since I took office, we have achieved extraordinary results together. Through active, disciplined management, we have grown Burbank's investment portfolio from $430 million in 2019 to $750 million today. This $320 million increase means more resources for our community.

Recognizing our track record, my peers across the state recently elected me President of the California Municipal Treasurers Association. I am proud to represent Burbank, and this role will allow me to bring innovative ideas, valuable partnerships, and proven best practices back into our treasury.

Burbank is my home and my three children attend our local public schools. Drawing on my professional background in management consulting and investments, I have worked tirelessly to earn your trust through responsible leadership, sound decisions, and measurable results. I will continue to be your disciplined steward and fiercest advocate.

Our momentum is strong, but our work is just beginning. I respectfully ask for your vote so we can continue protecting Burbank's financial future, together.
