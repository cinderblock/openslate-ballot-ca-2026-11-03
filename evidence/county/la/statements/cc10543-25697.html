STATEMENT OF BETH K. WHITTENBURY

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF TRUSTEES

PALOS VERDES LIBRARY DISTRICT

Occupation: Educator/Attorney/Author

As an incumbent, I seek to retain a job I treasure: PVLD Trustee. My 25 years as a PV resident have given me the opportunity to serve our community on several boards such the PVP Chamber of Commerce (Board and Executive Committee) and the Toberman Neighborhood Center, thus enhancing my leadership/governance skills. As an attorney, I have chaired committees at both the state and national levels. In addition, I teach Business Communications at CSU Dominquez Hills and provide strategic guidance as a member of the CBAPP Dean's Advisory Board. This service has allowed me to make meaningful contributions to our library policies, fiscal governance, and Director oversight. My first job as a library page in another system with three branch libraries gave me a staff perspective.

As a current trustee, I have attended the California Special District Association's leadership training for trustees, district ethics training, and more. This training enhanced my ability to serve you as an informed, aware, and skilled trustee.

Most importantly, I love our library system. To me, the library is the center of our community which offers something for everyone. I want to support that purpose and will always strongly advocate for those values if re-elected.
