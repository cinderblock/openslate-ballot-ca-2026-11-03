STATEMENT OF BENNIE TINSON

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
2nd District

CITY OF COMPTON GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 48

Occupation: Executive Director/Educator

Compton Community First. Progress Always.

As a longtime resident, product of the Compton Unified School District, Community College Professor, SEIU Union Organizer and City of Compton employee, my life experiences have prepared me to be your City Councilman. My experience as a Paralegal in the Compton City Attorney's Office provided me with intricate knowledge of the workings of Compton City government, ensuring I can hit the ground running and be a strong voice for residents. I am currently the Executive Director of a community-based organization working in partnership with the Los Angeles County Affordable Housing Solutions Agency advocating for housing and care to keep vulnerable residents off our streets.

I will lead with honesty, integrity and transparency.

My Platform for Progress includes:

- Public Safety & Infrastructure: Prioritize installing speed bumps to protect our children, fix streets, trim trees, and modernize drainage to prevent flooding.

- Revitalization: Transform and reclaim Long Beach Boulevard by eradicating sex trafficking and attracting new dining, shopping, and attainable housing with mixed use developments.

- Neighborhood Quality: Remove commercial trucks and RVs from residential streets, stop local industrial pollution and eliminate graffiti within 24 hours.

Together, we will unite the 2nd District and build a stronger Compton.

I humbly ask for your vote.

/s/ Bennie Tinson
