STATEMENT OF CHRIS PIMENTEL

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF EL SEGUNDO GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 49

Occupation: Technology Executive

I am the Mayor of El Segundo and led the solution to the nearly $400 million dollar pension and retiree medical crisis. I will continue to champion the safety, security, and disaster preparedness of the residents of this city. In the next four years we will advance our housing goals so that our children and young professionals have a place to call home. We will continue to grow our state-leading economy while defending El Segundo's small-town character.

The next four years will challenge us as we build solutions to the critical needs of the environment, incorporate advancing technology, and nurture our diverse community. As the delegate to many of our regional and county organizations, I will continue to shape smart policy, advocate for our city, and resist impractical or poorly considered laws.

During my term we have fixed the long-term budget, revitalized our diverse economy, built the safest city in the region, championed emergency response, rebuilt three playgrounds, completed the Plunge, welcomed an NFL team, and improved the environmental compliance of our neighbors.

You trusted me then, and I hope to earn your trust again.
