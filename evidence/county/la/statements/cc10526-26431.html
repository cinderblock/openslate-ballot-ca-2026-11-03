STATEMENT OF RICHARD "RITCHIE" CAJULIS

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF WALNUT GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Over the past four years, Walnut has grown so much and together we have accomplished a lot. This is why I have decided to run for re-election to the Walnut City Council, and I would be honored to earn your support.

By working closely with our Sheriff's Department, we have helped reduce crime and kept our neighborhoods safe. Crime in our city is trending down over the last four years. We have welcomed new developments, small businesses, and restaurants that have brought new energy and economic activity to our city. However, our work as a city is never done. If re-elected to the city council, I promise to:

Continue and strengthen our partnerships with public safety agencies

Create more opportunities and programs for families and seniors

Ensure that every tax dollar is used wisely, transparently, and with thoughtful care

Protecting the character of our community through smart, intentional development

Walnut is an amazing place to live in, and as your councilman I have fought for our residents to keep it that way. I would be honored to earn your support once again. Vote for Ritchie Cajulis for Walnut City Council.
