STATEMENT OF KATHRYN DUNN

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 1

CLAREMONT UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Appointed Incumbent

Championing for investments that improve success for all students has been the standard for every action during my first term on the CUSD Board.

I supported the Family Resource Center, establishing mental health centers at the middle and high schools, expanding Dual Language Immersion and the IB Middle Years Programme, and adding Registered Behavior Technicians to support teachers and students receiving special education services.

I approved a three-year technology improvement plan, a highly impactful middle school no-cell-phone policy and construction of a girls' softball field and locker room.

We balanced an $11 million deficit, and our teachers got well-deserved raises.

As Baldy View ROP Commission President, I believe we should invest more in career-technical education and develop partnerships with businesses so that our students gain essential career skills.

I listen attentively to parents, teachers and community members. As a special education parent, former newspaper editor, and communications consultant, my experience is rooted in thoughtful analysis, building consensus and optimizing outcomes.

When elected officials are doing great work, they deserve another term. Claremont's schools are stronger, more inclusive, and better prepared for the future. The results speak for themselves. I have served with integrity and heart, and I respectfully ask for your vote.
