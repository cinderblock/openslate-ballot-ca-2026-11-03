STATEMENT OF GEORGINA TAMAYO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
1st District

CITY OF MONTEBELLO GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Mayor/Finance Director

It has been the honor of my life to serve as your Mayor and District 1 Councilmember, representing the community where my husband and I have raised our two children for over 35 years. I bring my Bachelor's and Master's degrees from UCLA, along with my government career in development and finance, to every decision I make for Montebello.

As your Councilmember and Mayor, I have:

Secured over $3 million for the development of a new fire station in District 1.
Attracted major retailers including Tesla, Hobby Lobby, Planet Fitness, Yard House, and Round 1 Entertainment. Adopted the city's first Community Workforce Agreement, ensuring unionized labor on city funded projects.
Invested in new community amenities, including a dog park, community garden, and police resource center.
Made significant investments in our fire and police departments, expanding personnel and creating a new Public Park Safety Division.
Reinstated our Traffic & Safety Commission to improve road safety and address community traffic concerns.

Moving forward, I will:
Continue investing in infrastructure and public safety across District 1.
Support programs that serve our families, seniors, and local businesses.

Our work is far from over. As your District 1 Councilmember, I will continue fighting for a safer, stronger Montebello. I respectfully request your vote.

Georgina Tamayo
