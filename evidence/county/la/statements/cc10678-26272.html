STATEMENT OF GRACE ATHENA BOLAND BADER

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

WISEBURN UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

My name is Grace Bader, and I have proudly called Wiseburn home for over 18 years. As a wife, mother, and nurse, I've learned that healing, growth, and strength come from caring for the whole child. I want to bring that same care and holistic approach to our school district.

I envision a Wiseburn where every child feels safe, supported, and inspired. Where families and teachers are empowered, mental and physical health are prioritized, and transparency guides every decision. If elected, I will work tirelessly to build bridges, encourage open conversations, and create a learning environment filled with hope, safety, and opportunity. I will champion the mental and physical well-being of our students, families, and teachers while helping create a safe and joyful environment where every student can flourish.

We are the Community of Good Neighbors, and I know firsthand how true that is. Wiseburn is built on kindness and connection. I want everyone to feel comfortable reaching out with questions or concerns. Together, let's nurture a district where our children grow strong, healthy, and ready for bright futures. Let's cultivate a community where every child thrives and every voice matters.
