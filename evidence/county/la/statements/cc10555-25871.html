STATEMENT OF SHERRY SHAW

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 3

THREE VALLEYS MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICT

Three Valleys Municipal Water District faces significant challenges, including rising infrastructure replacement costs, rate pressures, and the emerging threat of golden mussel infestation. Experienced leadership is essential to meet these challenges while maintaining reliable, affordable water service.

As General Manager of Walnut Valley Water District, one of Three Valleys' member agencies, I worked closely with Three Valleys staff on budgets, rate planning, and long-term infrastructure investments. I understand the financial impacts of Metropolitan Water District's changing rate structure, declining water sales, and the need to balance responsible investment with rate stability.

My engineering and management experience has provided a strong understanding of capital improvement planning, infrastructure replacement, and the importance of sound financial stewardship. I have also remained actively engaged in regional water issues, including the golden mussel infestation, the Pure Water Project, the State Water Conveyance Project, and Colorado River water supply negotiations.

Earlier in my career, I prepared the City of La Verne's Water Master Plan, providing firsthand experience with long-range water planning and resource management.

With more than 35 years of engineering and water industry experience, I am committed to protecting our water supplies, maintaining reliable infrastructure, supporting the development of future leaders, and ensuring responsible stewardship of the public's resources for the communities served by Three Valleys Municipal Water District.
