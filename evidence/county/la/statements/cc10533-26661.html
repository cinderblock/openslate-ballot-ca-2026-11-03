STATEMENT OF TOM DEMILLE

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF WEST HOLLYWOOD GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

My name is Tom DeMille, a 35-year resident of West Hollywood running to give our city back to its residents. For too long, the people of our community have been ignored. As a result, residents no longer attend city meetings or help guide council members on critical decisions. When I first moved here, West Hollywood thrived on active democracy. We had passionate debates with hundreds of residents on opposing sides speaking out, compromising, and ultimately reaching a consensus where everyone felt heard. Today, that collaborative spirit is entirely absent.

Beyond governance, public safety in our city is in shambles. I firmly believe that every resident and guest has the fundamental right to sleep peacefully at night without fear of break-ins, and to feel completely safe strolling down our boulevards at any hour of the day or night.

My commitment to this city is proven by my actions when no one was looking. Following the tragedy of September 11th, West Hollywood lacked even a single flagpole. After organizing a candlelight vigil attended by 4,000 residents the next day, I spent 33 days waving the American flag on the site where the Veterans Memorial now stands (this year will be the 25th anniversary of 9/11) to offer comfort and security to our community. That effort ultimately led to the installation of roughly 100 flagpoles across our city, including the one proudly standing in front of the Sheriffs' station.

With your support, I will transform our city into a lasting legacy by creating three distinct Walk of Fame tributes: the Gay Icon Walk of Fame, the Sunset Music Icon Walk of Fame, and a memorial East of Fairfax honoring the Russian war heroes who helped defeat Nazi Germany. This will make our city vibrant for the next 100 years, ensuring we can pass down our local businesses to our children. This initiative requires minimal funding, as it can be integrated directly into necessary sidewalk repairs. Additionally, we will widen the sidewalks throughout the Rainbow District to create vibrant gathering spaces for our community rather than forcing people onto the roadside. After 35 years of marching, our residents deserve dedicated spaces to assemble.

In closing, when elected, I will defend every resident and guest who comes into our city with my life, so help me God. I ask for your vote.

Thank you,
The Legacy Candidate
