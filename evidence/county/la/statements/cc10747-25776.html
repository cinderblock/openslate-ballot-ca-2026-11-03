STATEMENT OF JENNIFER MAK

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area D

TORRANCE UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Retired School Administrator

Throughout my career, I have been guided by a simple belief - sustainable progress comes from building trust, working together, and maintaining an unwavering commitment to student success.

My family has proudly called the Torrance community home for nearly 30 years. Our three adult children graduated from Torrance Unified after attending Riviera Elementary, Richardson Middle, and South High School. As both an educator and TUSD parent, I understand the importance of strong public schools and their impact on students and the community.

During my 38-year career in public education, I have served as a teacher, instructional coach, assistant principal, principal, school support administrator, and Community of Schools administrator. As an elementary principal, I led a California Gold Ribbon and Title I Academic Achievement Award-winning school. I later partnered with 11 of the district's lowest-performing schools to strengthen instruction and improve student outcomes. As a Community of Schools Administrator, I supported 16 schools serving approximately 7,000 students, focusing on instructional leadership, school improvement, and student achievement.

I am committed to putting students first, empowering educators, and strengthening partnerships with families. It would be an honor to bring my experience and collaborative leadership to the Torrance Unified School District Board of Education.

Warm regards,

Jennifer Mak
Retired School Administrator
