STATEMENT OF REZA RASSOOL

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF TRUSTEES,
Trustee Area 1

SANTA CLARITA COMMUNITY COLLEGE DISTRICT

Age: 63

Occupation: Nonprofit Chair and CEO

I am a long-time Santa Clarita Valley resident and parent of four sons who attended our local public schools. I am running for College of the Canyons Board of Trustees, Area l, to keep our college affordable, high-quality, and truly student-centered.

CoC has recently faced serious questions about how millions of dollars in facilities contracts were awarded and whether funds were misused or misappropriated. Independent legal reviews and a forensic audit are beginning to uncover long-standing problems in contracting and oversight. I believe our community deserves strong, transparent governance, full public reporting, and clear reforms so that students, employees, and taxpayers can trust how decisions are made.

As an educator and technology leader, I teach as an affiliate professor and lead a nonprofit AI lab focused on preparing students and working adults for the jobs of the future. My experience with shared governance, budgeting, and long-term planning will help COC navigate rapid change while protecting students and taxpayers. I respectfully ask for your vote.
