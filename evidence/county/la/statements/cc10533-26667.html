STATEMENT OF KYLE BRAZEAL

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF WEST HOLLYWOOD GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

West Hollywood has shown what local leadership can accomplish by putting people first. From renter protections to civil rights to social services, our city has set an example for others to follow. As an LGBTQ+ resident, that legacy is personal. Protecting it also requires us to demand change when local government stops listening.

Today, too many residents feel decisions are made for them, not with them. They see their legitimate concerns dismissed, public meetings held after outcomes are already decided, and political theater replacing pragmatic solutions and compromise. We need a City Council that listens, not lectures, and that gives residents a meaningful voice in shaping outcomes.

That is why I am running: not to build a political career, but to bring fact-based, community-focused leadership back to West Hollywood. My career in finance has taught me to make disciplined budget decisions, weigh competing priorities, ask difficult questions, and measure results. I will bring that same discipline to each City Council decision. Every taxpayer dollar should have a purpose, every city program should have clear goals and transparent reporting, and the public should be heard before major decisions are made, not after.

Good leadership rejects the false choices that too often dominate local politics. We can address crime while protecting civil rights and privacy. We can support fair labor practices while helping local businesses open, fill empty storefronts, and succeed.

That approach already guides my advocacy on behalf of our residents before City Council. I have called for a local development plan that welcomes new housing without displacing renters, eliminating rent-stabilized homes, or ignoring neighborhood character and infrastructure. I opposed the Fountain Avenue redesign as proposed and advocated for less disruptive alternatives that improve safety and protect all road users without worsening congestion or removing needed residential parking.

Across city issues, my approach will remain consistent: listen before deciding, ground decisions in evidence and real-world impacts, measure results, and change course when a policy is not serving our community. West Hollywood's strongest leadership has combined bold values with officials that are responsive to public input. I am running to restore that balance, make City Council work with the community, and hold it accountable for results.

I respectfully ask for your vote to so we can put residents at the center of City Council decisions and deliver pragmatic, considerate, and accountable leadership for everyone who calls West Hollywood home.
