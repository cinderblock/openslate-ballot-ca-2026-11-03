STATEMENT OF CHRISTINE SARMIENTO

CANDIDATE FOR MAYOR

CITY OF EL MONTE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 43

Occupation: Public Health Nurse

My name is Christine Sarmiento, a Public Health Nurse and Operations Manager raising five boys and a proud resident of El Monte. I immigrated to the US at eighteen and have lived in different parts of California. No other city compares to El Monte. El Monte has so much hidden potential, the same way many immigrants do. We work hard, we build quietly, and it takes time to fully realize what we are capable of. I want to help El Monte create real opportunities for our residents to achieve their potential.

I see that same potential in my own five boys, and it's why I decided to give back to the city that raised me. As a nurse, I care for our community's physical and psychological health. As an operations manager, I turn plans into real results. El Monte is home to young families, yet we have just 1% of our land as parkland, crime above the national average, and streets that need repair.

I am asking for your help in getting your vote as your new Mayor, to create a space that promotes health and safety for our kids, our families, and all the residents of El Monte.
