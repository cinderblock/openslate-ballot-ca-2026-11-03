STATEMENT OF JAMES M. BISHOP SR.

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

LYNWOOD UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Educator/Coach

James Bishop, for the past 45 years, I have proudly called Lynwood home. During that time, I have dedicated more than 35 years of service to the Lynwood Unified School District as a Safety Officer, Coach, and Vocational Education Teacher.

I have had the privilege of working with students, families, teachers, and staff from every corner of our community. I understand the challenges, opportunities, and strengths that make our community special. I understand the needs of our students, the concerns of our teachers, and the expectations of our families. I have seen firsthand how decisions made at the district level impact the people we serve every day. My commitment will be to advocate for what is best for students, educators, and strengthen the connection between our schools and the community.

I believe effective leadership starts with listening, understanding, and being present. My roots in Lynwood run deep! My experience comes from decades of working side-by-side with the people who make this community great.

If elected, I will be a strong voice for students, teachers, employees, and families, always putting the needs of our community first!

Together, we can build a stronger future for Lynwood.
— James Bishop Sr.
Candidate for Lynwood Unified School District Board of Education
