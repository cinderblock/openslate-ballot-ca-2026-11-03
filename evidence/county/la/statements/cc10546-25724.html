STATEMENT OF AMY I. CHEN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 4

FOOTHILL MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICT

Are we addressing our aging water infrastructure prudently, at the lowest responsible cost? Are we prepared for the next Eaton Fire—or the next disaster?

The Foothill Municipal Water District Board is making long-term financial commitments right now that will shape water rates and taxes for decades. Water-related property taxes have more than doubled since 2024—with another 20% increase this year. For residents on fixed incomes, these are not line items. They are real hardships.

These decisions demand independent scrutiny, technical expertise, and proven water policy experience.

I have spent 40 years in California water, delivering infrastructure and advising board members on high-stakes policy and investment decisions. I bring the technical depth to understand what's at stake, the policy experience to evaluate tradeoffs, and the independent judgment to ask the hard questions before public dollars are committed.

As a Professional Engineer, a 30-year La Cañada Flintridge resident, a commissioner on the City's Public Works & Traffic Commission, and a member of its Emergency Preparedness Coordination Committee, I am running to bring real accountability to the Foothill Board.

Vote Amy Chen, Division 4. Water decisions should be earned—not rubber-stamped.
