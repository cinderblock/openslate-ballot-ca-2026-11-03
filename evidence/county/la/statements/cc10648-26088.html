STATEMENT OF CARMEN EULALIA BASSO

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 2

HACIENDA LA PUENTE UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

As a working mother, parent, and now grandparent, I understand the hopes and challenges families face every day. Balancing work while raising my children taught me responsibility, perseverance, compassion, and how to make thoughtful decisions under pressure. Today, as the grandmother of a grandson on the autism spectrum, I also understand the importance of ensuring students with special needs receive the support and opportunities they deserve to thrive.

During my 40 year career in transportation, hospitality, and travel—including aviation, Amtrak, and May Company Travel—I built strong skills in teamwork, customer service. Managing travel operations for 45 stores required organization, and the ability to work collaboratively. As a proud Transportation Union member, I successfully fought an unjust termination, reinforcing my belief in fairness, accountability, and standing up for others. Throughout my career, I challenged myself to keep learning.

Every child deserves a high-quality education, safe schools, and access to the resources they need to succeed. My lived experience as a working mother, grandmother, and community advocate, combined with my professional background in listening, conflict resolution, and collaboration, has prepared me to work with the Board of Education, educators, families, and our community to ensure every student has the opportunity to reach their full potential.
