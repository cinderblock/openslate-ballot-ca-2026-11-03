STATEMENT OF JEFFREY DE LA TORRE

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

HACIENDA LA PUENTE UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

For more than 30 years, I have dedicated my career to educating, mentoring, and advocating for children. Today, I am honored to continue that commitment as your elected representative on the Hacienda La Puente Unified School District Board of Education.

As a proud parent whose children attended our district schools, I have a personal investment in ensuring every student receives the opportunities, support, and quality education they deserve. My commitment to this district extends beyond my role as a Board member it is rooted in my family's experience and my lifelong service to public education.

As your trustee, I have worked to be a thoughtful listener, a strong advocate for all students, and a responsible steward of taxpayer dollars. I believe every decision should be guided by facts, integrity, accountability, and transparency, especially when it comes to the district's financial resources and long-term planning.

I remain committed to putting students first, supporting our dedicated educators and staff, and working collaboratively with families and the community to strengthen our schools. I respectfully ask for your continued trust and support as we build on our progress and prepare every student for a successful future.
