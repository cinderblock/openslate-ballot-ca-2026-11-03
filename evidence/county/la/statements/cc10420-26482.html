STATEMENT OF AARON K. DRAKE

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
1st District

CITY OF BELLFLOWER GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 67

Occupation: Law Enforcement Professional

Dear Bellflower voters. I would be Honored to receive your vote and represent our community as a member of the Bellflower City Council District-1. I have resided in Bellflower for over 30+ years and raised my family here. I have served Honorably on several Commissions and Committees over the past years and I have the experience to serve on the City Council.

As a member of the City Council, my goals are to work together with our community to improve our programs and to make our community even safer. I'm a strong supporter of law enforcement and I will work to address the concerns of our community. Progress has been made in many areas and new businesses are starting to open in our city. I intend to continue making recommendations that will improve our community.

On occasions, I can be found in the community meeting, talking to and listening to their concerns. I enjoy getting to know not only our Bellflower residents, but business owners as well. I'm positive we can work together to make our community even better.

I humbly ask for your vote for the Bellflower City Council District-1.
