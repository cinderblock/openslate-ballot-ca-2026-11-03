STATEMENT OF SION ROY

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF TRUSTEES,

SANTA MONICA COMMUNITY COLLEGE DISTRICT

Age: 45

Occupation: Santa Monica College Board Chair/Physician

My name is Dr. Sion Roy, and I am honored to be the current Chair of the Santa Monica College (SMC) Board of Trustees, now seeking re-election. During my two terms on the Board, I spearheaded the creation of SMC's Allied Health Taskforce to increase healthcare related curriculum at our college. This led to the development of both Certified Nurse Assistant and Home Health Aide Certificate programs, which not only benefit our students, but help LA County fill critical healthcare employment needs. Additionally, I worked with my colleagues to develop mentorship programs at the college focused on exposing our students from minority backgrounds to in-demand healthcare fields, which has led to scholarship opportunities and other rewarding experiences for our students.

Over the next four years, I'll work to expand community programs for our SMMUSD dual enrollment students, as well as in the Emeritus college and our new Malibu campus. Additionally, we will take steps to continue to help as many students as possible achieve their goals as the #1 transfer school to UC.

Along with serving as Board Chair, I'm a physician at Harbor UCLA, a safety net public hospital, and past President of the Los Angeles County Medical Association.
