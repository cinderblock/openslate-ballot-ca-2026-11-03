STATEMENT OF ROGELIO "ROGER" BAÑUELOS

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

WISEBURN UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 53

Occupation: President, Executive Leadership and Planning

I have been a resident of the Wiseburn District Community for 25 years committed to developing and providing educational opportunities for all WUSD students. As a school board member and current President, for the last 17 years I have led and participated in efforts to make our community's priorities a reality: student success, Wiseburn Unification, Choice Block Incorporation, purchase and development of our local high school and Aquatics Center site and most recently the grand opening of the Wiseburn Sports Complex for sport league and community use.

These efforts were accomplished while I remained focused on student achievement in mathematics, english language, and performing arts through the COVID pandemic and financial hardships due to decreased State funding. My wife and I raised four sons, all of whom have proudly promoted from Wiseburn K-8 schools and graduated from Da Vinci Science. We are truly grateful to the Wiseburn Community, its schools, teachers and staff for the great education they have received.

As a parent, graduate from USC in Civil Engineering, and a business leader, I ask for your vote and support so that I may continuing to serve our community as a school board member with integrity, commitment and passion.
