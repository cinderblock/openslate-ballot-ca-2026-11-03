STATEMENT OF QUI NGUYEN

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 3

EL MONTE UNION HIGH SCHOOL DISTRICT

I am running for reelection to the School Board to ensure every student in our district receives a world-class education in a safe, supportive environment. Throughout my current term, my focus has remained steadfast on academic excellence, fiscal responsibility, and student well-being.

As a UCLA graduate, retired electrical engineer, and retired Certified Financial Planner®, I bring a unique blend of analytical problem-solving and rigorous financial stewardship to our board. I am dedicated to maximizing our tax dollars, ensuring resources go directly into classrooms to support our students and hardworking staff.

Moving forward, my top priorities are: Academic Excellence: Empowering teachers and strengthening vital English Language Arts and STEM programs to build the critical thinking skills essential for the modern economy. Campus Safety: Maintaining secure schools through a proactive, positive law enforcement presence that fosters community trust, because learning requires a safe environment. Fiscal Responsibility: Ensuring transparent, efficient budgeting that aligns with our educational goals to effectively serve our community.

With a deep commitment and a proven track record, I have the experience required to navigate complex issues. I would be honored to earn your vote to continue moving our district forward together.
