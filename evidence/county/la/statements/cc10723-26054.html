STATEMENT OF ALISA GENTRY

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 2

SAUGUS UNION SCHOOL DISTRICT

As a first responder (EMT) for over twenty years, I know how to listen and solve problems under pressure. As the mother of a special needs child, I know how difficult our public education system can be.

I want to represent you on the Saugus Union School District Governing Board.

Our schools are strong thanks to the teachers, administrators and staff at each of our school sites. But we need to bring change to the district office.

I am running because I am committed to using my skills to ensure that our schools become the centerpiece of our Helmers, Foster, Northpark, Mountainview, and Tesoro communities. And to open the lines of communication between the governing board, superintendent, and our school communities.

I respectfully ask for your vote.

Alisa Gentry
