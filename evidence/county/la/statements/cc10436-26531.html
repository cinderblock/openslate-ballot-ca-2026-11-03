STATEMENT OF ANNA JACOBSEN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
4th District

CITY OF CLAREMONT GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Urban Planner

My husband and I chose Claremont to raise our three children because of its schools, parks, walkable neighborhoods, and vibrant Village. Over time, it's the people who have truly made Claremont home. I'm running for City Council to protect what our community loves about Claremont while preparing thoughtfully for the future.

I'm an urban planner and small business owner with master's degrees in public administration and planning from USC. For over 17 years, I've helped cities listen to residents, analyze complex issues, manage public funds, and turn community priorities into practical plans.

As a Claremont parent and community advocate, I founded Better Claremont Playgrounds. We brought families, neighbors, and City staff together to advocate for inclusive, sustainable parks. That work reinforced what I've seen throughout my career: the best decisions are made when residents are heard, facts are clear, and leaders work together.

On City Council, I'll support policies that promote local businesses, thoughtful housing, well-maintained parks and city services, more walkable streets, responsive public safety, and long-term financial stability. I'll listen, ask hard questions, and pursue practical, responsible solutions rooted in our community.

I'd be honored to earn your vote and serve District 4. www.annaforclaremont.com
