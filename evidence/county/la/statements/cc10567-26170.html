CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,

SANTA CLARITA VALLEY WATER AGENCY

She voted to:

- Purchase a solar farm/protect against energy shutoffs
- Protect the Santa Clara River
- Expand recycled water
- Enhance transparency

SHE VOTED AGAINST RATE INCREASES

Water Supply Goals:
- Increase recycled water

Lynne promotes open, honest government
- Meeting accessibility

Previously Held Positions:
