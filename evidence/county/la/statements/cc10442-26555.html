STATEMENT OF LEE MAO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
4th District

CITY OF DIAMOND BAR GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 60

Occupation: Small Business Owner

I am proud to be a long-time Diamond Bar resident, small business owner, and dedicated community volunteer. My wife and I moved here in 1993, where we raised our daughter and built our life in this wonderful city.

For over 30 years, I have owned and operated a business, giving me real world experience in budgeting, financial responsibility, and problem solving skills that are especially important as Diamond Bar faces ongoing fiscal challenges.

I currently serve as a Planning Commissioner and have also served on the Parks and Recreation Commission, providing hands-on experience in land use, community services, and city operations. I am also actively involved with the Diamond Bar Friends of the Library, Diamond Bar Community Foundation, and Local service organizations.

I am running for City Council to bring strong fiscal responsibility, ensure public safety, improve traffic flow, and promote a healthy, active community.

I am committed to fighting for you-listening to residents, making thoughtful decisions, and putting our community first. I respectfully ask for your vote so we can keep Diamond Bar a safe, vibrant, and thriving place to live.
