STATEMENT OF ALI TAJ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF ARTESIA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 59

Occupation: Grandfather/Businessman/Councilmember

It has been my honor to serve the residents of Artesia as your Mayor and City Councilmember. Together, we have delivered meaningful progress while preserving the small-town values that make our community special.

We have reduced crime, strengthened public safety services, maintained balanced budgets, completed independent audits, invested in parks, streets, and infrastructure improvements, and supported responsible economic growth. We secured a new Public Services Station, enhanced city services, improved public facilities, and positioned Artesia for future opportunities, including regional transportation investments and the future train station project.

My commitment has always been to put Artesia residents first. Through transparent leadership, fiscal responsibility, and a results-driven approach, I have worked to deliver improvements that strengthen our community and enhance quality of life.

While I am proud of our accomplishments, there is still work to do. My priorities remain clear: keeping neighborhoods safe, supporting local businesses, attracting investment, improving parks and recreation, protecting taxpayer dollars, and ensuring Artesia remains financially strong for future generations.

I respectfully ask for your vote so we can continue Moving Artesia Forward with proven leadership, responsible stewardship, and a vision for an even brighter future.
