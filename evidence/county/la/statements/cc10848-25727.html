STATEMENT OF JOE MESSINA

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

WILLIAM S. HART UNION HIGH SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Small Business Owner

I was elected to the Hart Board with a mandate to strengthen and expand Career Technical Education (CTE). While the challenge was greater than expected, we've made tremendous progress.

The next step is a true state-of-the-art CTE Center that prepares students for successful careers. As Artificial Intelligence changes the workforce, skilled professions such as plumbing, electrical work, welding, construction, culinary arts, cosmetology, healthcare, and others remain essential.

Together, we've achieved:
- Hired a full-time Career Technical Education Director focused on expanding opportunities for students.
- 92% of Hart District seniors have taken at a CTE course before graduation.
- Secured millions of dollars in CTE grant funding.
- Expanded to more than 30 career pathways, including construction, skilled trades, healthcare, technology, and more.

I will continue to:
- Protect your tax dollars and ensure students have the educational and mental health resources they need.
- Give parents a strong voice and meaningful input in their children's education.
- Advocate for opportunities for every student–whether they choose a college, trade, military, or another path.

Let's finish what we started and ensure every student has the opportunity to discover their passion and build a successful future.

I would be honored to earn your vote.
