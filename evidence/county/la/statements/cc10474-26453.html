STATEMENT OF ROBERT PULLEN-MILES

CANDIDATE FOR MAYOR

CITY OF LAWNDALE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Mayor, City of Lawndale

It has been an honor to serve as your Mayor. For more than 22 years, I have proudly served Lawndale as a Planning Commissioner, Councilmember, and Mayor, committed to protecting our neighborhoods, strengthening our economy, and improving our quality of life.

Together, we have attracted businesses including Target, Sprouts, Bossa Nova, Starbucks, Hirsch Pipe & Supply, WSS Shoes, and Mateo's, creating jobs and strengthening our economy. I will continue promoting quality housing, hotel development, and investment that expands city revenues.

I secured $6 million in state funding, including $2 million for road improvements, pedestrian safety and $4 million for a Youth Development Center. Working alongside residents, I successfully fought Metro's proposal to route a light rail line through our neighborhoods.

As your Mayor, I will continue prioritizing public safety, economic development, infrastructure improvements, services for seniors and youth, and preparing Lawndale for opportunities associated with the 2028 Olympics.

I'm proudly supported by first responders; Councilmembers Kearney and Cuevas; former Councilmember Rhonda Hofmann-Gorman; community leaders Chelsea Schreiber and Johnny Martinez; business owners, and residents.

On behalf of my wife, Léna, and our three daughters, thank you for your trust. I respectfully ask for your vote. I'm Pullen for You!
