STATEMENT OF YARITZA A. GONZALEZ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF EDUCATION,
Trustee Area 3

INGLEWOOD UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

My name is Yaritza Gonzalez, and I am running for Inglewood School Board, Trustee Area 3.

I am an Inglewood native and proudly attended Inglewood schools, including Worthington Elementary School, Albert Monroe Magnet Middle School, and Animo Inglewood Charter High School. As a multilingual learner, I experienced firsthand the challenges students face and understand the importance of ensuring every student has the resources they need to thrive.

I earned my bachelor's from Dartmouth College and my Master of Public Policy from the UCLA Luskin School of Public Affairs, where I concentrated on immigration and education policy. I served with the White House Initiative on Educational Excellence for Hispanics, was awarded a Fulbright Public Policy Initiative Fellowship, and now work as a Policy Manager at the Central American Resource Center (CARECEN-LA), advocating for students and their families.

As your Trustee, I will prioritize competitive teacher pay, restore local control, increase student enrollment, raise student achievement, and make sure that corporations pay their fair share to keep our schools open, safe, and welcoming learning environments.

I am committed to fighting for every student to have access to a high-quality public education. I respectfully ask for your vote.
