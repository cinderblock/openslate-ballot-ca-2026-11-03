STATEMENT OF JUANITA DOPLEMORE

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 4

COMPTON COMMUNITY COLLEGE DISTRICT

Juanita Doplemore
Incumbent, Compton Community College District Board of Trustees, Area 4
Serving as your Board of Trustee President has been one of the greatest honors of my life. Guided by the principles of duty, discipline, and service, I remain committed to expanding opportunity and ensuring Compton College provides affordable, high-quality education for students and families throughout our district.

As your incumbent Trustee, I have supported support initiatives that strengthen academic achievement, expand Career Technical Education and workforce training, modernizing our campus, enhancing dual enrollment with local school districts, improve campus safety, and build partnerships connecting education to careers. I also guided/led supported support for the Compton College Student Housing Project, a transformational investment that will reduce housing insecurity and help students remain focused on completing their education.
I believe every student deserves access to college regardless of finances. That is why I remain committed to expanding financial aid awareness, increasing college readiness, and creating pathways to high-demand careers.

If when re-elected, I will continue providing responsible fiscal stewardship, supporting academic excellence, expanding workforce opportunities, and ensuring Compton College remains a source of opportunity for families across Paramount, Bellflower, North Long Beach, Lakewood, Compton, and the communities we serve.

I respectfully ask for your vote to re-elect Juanita Doplemore for Compton Community College District Board of Trustees, Area 4.
