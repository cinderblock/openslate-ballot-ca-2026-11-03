STATEMENT OF ANGELA CUTBILL

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

LAS VIRGENES UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Governing Board Member, Las Virgenes Unified School District

I have been honored and grateful to serve the students, parents, staff, and community of LVUSD.

Since 2022:
Our schools have earned statewide recognition for academic achievement, attendance, environmental leadership, and expanded student opportunity.

-We have undergone the largest facilities modernization program in district history, including improving classrooms, science labs, career education spaces, technology, safety, accessibility, athletic facilities, HVAC, and solar.

-We maintained fiscal prudence with positive budget certifications, earned among the highest credit ratings to an LCFF-funded district, generated approximately $5M through new programs, reduced operating costs by millions, and substantially lowered long-term liabilities.

-We expanded supports for students, increased counseling and wellness services, expanded transitional kindergarten and after-school opportunities, improved services for students with disabilities, created more inclusive graduation pathways and enhanced specialized services.

Additionally, I initiated changes to our cellphone policy as well as a districtwide ban on e-bikes, as part of our continued focus on student safety and wellness.

I will continue to bring dedication, experience, and a focus on fiscal responsibility to this position.

I ask for your vote to continue to serve our students and Las Virgenes community.

Thank you!
