STATEMENT OF JORGEL CHAVEZ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 4

WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICT OF SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA

Age: 29

Occupation: Water Policy Analyst

As a Water Policy Analyst and Southeast Los Angeles resident, I am running for the Water Replenishment District to bring new, accountable leadership to our communities.

I am the first in my family to graduate from college. I earned two associate degrees from Long Beach City College before graduating cum laude from the University of Southern California with a bachelor's degree in Political Science, a minor in Communications, Policy, and Law, and a Master of Public Administration.

As Mayor of Bell Gardens, I led and advanced major infrastructure projects, including the opening of our regional aquatic center.

Our communities have been represented by the same person for 20 years, since I was 9 years old. I believe it is time for new leadership.

I will:

-Protect clean, reliable, and affordable water.

-Hold data centers accountable so they do not harm our water supply or force families to pay higher costs.

-Protect groundwater through recycling, conservation, stormwater capture, and replenishment.

-Work with cities and fire departments to strengthen water infrastructure and ensure water is available during wildfires and emergencies such as the Lineage warehouse fire.

I respectfully ask for you to vote for me, Jorgel Chavez.
