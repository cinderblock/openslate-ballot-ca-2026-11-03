STATEMENT OF LINDA L. SMALL

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

WHITTIER CITY SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 65

Occupation: Incumbent

"Together, we've accomplished much. Together, we'll achieve even more."

For 21 years, I've served the students, families, employees, and community of the Whittier City School District. I remain passionate about ensuring every child has the opportunity to learn, grow, and succeed in safe, innovative, and inspiring schools.

I believe lasting success is built through trust, collaboration, and strong relationships. I have worked diligently to build productive partnerships with our employees and labor groups, city leaders, higher education partners, and community organizations---because when we work together, our students benefit.

Together, we have achieved meaningful results: passing a a $99 million school facilities bond; earning prestigious National Blue Ribbon, California Distringuished, Apple Distinguished, AVID Showcase, and Schools to Watch recognitions; expanding Dual Enrollment, Dual Immersion, and Musical Theatre programs; securing more than $11 million in competitive grants; investing in modern classrooms, technology, playgrounds, and campus improvements; and protecting employee healthcare benefits.

While I am proud of what we have accomplished, our best work is still ahead. I am committed to providing experienced leadership during the transition to a new superintendent, ensuring continuity as we deliver our $99 million bond projects, advancing academic achievement, strengthening special education programs, and providing collaborative Board leadership focused on student success.

I respectfully ask for your vote on November 3, 2026, so together we can continue building a stronger future for every child in Whittier.
