STATEMENT OF MARIA DE LOS ANGELES GONZALEZ

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

LENNOX SCHOOL DISTRICT

Dear Community,

I am running for re-election to the Lennox School Board. As a resident of this community since 2001, my three children are a product of the Lennox School District, with great success going on to college & university. I strive to give back to my community and continue to ensure every student in the district has the resources and support that they need to be successful.

For over 14 years, I served as a parent volunteer because I was passionate about my children's educational journey. I led district-level committees, as President of DELAC (District English Learner Advisory Committee) and served as a representative for ELAC (English Learner Advisory Committee).

As a Board member for the last 8 years, I have made progress in addressing our community's outstanding needs and remained fully committed to representing the best interests of all students. I have empowered parents and encouraged them to become more involved in the school community, while also providing teachers with all available resources to ensure student success.

Please support me with your vote in the upcoming election on Tuesday, November 3rd.

See you at the polls!
María de los Ángeles González
