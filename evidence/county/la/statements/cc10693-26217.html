STATEMENT OF MIGUEL ANGEL HERNANDEZ JR.

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 4

EAST WHITTIER CITY SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Law Enforcement Officer

I am the first in my family born in America and have called Whittier home since I was twelve years old. I attended middle and high school here and became the first college graduate in my family, earning my degree from Whittier College.

After several years in the private sector, I spent eight years as a high school teacher before beginning my career in law enforcement, where I have proudly served our community for the past seventeen years.

My wife and I have raised our three children in Whittier, and all have attended schools in the East Whittier City School District. We have enjoyed being actively involved in their education through volunteering as room parents, serving in various PTA roles, and supporting school activities.

For the past six years, I have volunteered at our local high school by mentoring students in academy programs and the ELA Mentor Program, as well as photographing school sporting events. Investing in the lives of young people is a passion of mine. I deeply appreciate our educators and district employees and would be honored to earn your vote so I can support their important work and continue serving our community.
