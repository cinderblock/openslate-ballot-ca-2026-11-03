STATEMENT OF SUSANA RAMIREZ

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 1

LITTLE LAKE CITY SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 36

Occupation: Administrative Assistant of ELOP Expanding Learning Opportunities Program

My name is Susana Ramirez, and I am honored to be a candidate for Little Lake City School District School Governing Board, Trustee Area No. 1.

I have the privilege of working in education while also being the parent of children who are currently attending Little Lake City School District. As both a former district employee and a current parent, I bring a unique perspective and firsthand understanding of the needs of our students, families, and staff.

I believe every child deserves a safe, welcoming, and supportive learning environment where they have the opportunity to learn, grow, and succeed. Strong schools are built through collaboration, transparency, and a shared commitment to putting students first.

If elected, I will listen to the voices of our families, staff, and students and make thoughtful, informed decisions, bringing success and well-being to every child. I am committed to supporting academic excellence, fostering positive school cultures, and strengthening the partnerships between our schools and families.

It would be an honor to serve our community as a member of the Little Lake City School District. I respectfully ask for your support and the opportunity to help build an even stronger future for all of our students.
