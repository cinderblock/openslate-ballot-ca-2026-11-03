STATEMENT OF THERESA LEE

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 3

WALNUT VALLEY WATER DISTRICT

For more than a decade, I have been committed to strengthening Walnut Valley Water District through responsible stewardship, transparent governance, and a deep respect for the communities we serve. Our mission -- to provide a reliable, high quality water supply in a fiscally efficient and environmentally responsible manner -- guides every decision I make as a director. Over the past ten years, WVWD has invested in major infrastructure upgrades, expanded conservation programs, and modernized operations to ensure long term reliability. From reservoir and pump station improvements to leak reduction initiatives, digital customer tools, and sustainability planning, these efforts reflect a forward looking approach that protects our resources and prepares us for future challenges. I believe leadership means listening, collaborating, and acting with integrity. My focus remains on strengthening system resiliency, supporting innovation, and ensuring that every dollar is used wisely for the benefit of our ratepayers. WVWD's success is built on teamwork -- staff, board members, community partners, and residents working together to safeguard our water supply. I will continue championing reliability, sustainability, and service excellence so our District remains a model of efficiency, preparedness, and community trust.
