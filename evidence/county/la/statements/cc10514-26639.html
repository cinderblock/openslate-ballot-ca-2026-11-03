STATEMENT OF ERICKA LESLEY

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF SANTA MONICA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

I'm Ericka Lesley, and I'm running for Santa Monica City Council because I believe our city is strongest when every resident has the opportunity to succeed. As a current Rent Control Board Commissioner and longtime community advocate, I've worked to protect renters, expand affordable housing, support working families, and strengthen our local economy.

I know that affordability, economic opportunity, and thriving neighborhoods go hand in hand. We need practical solutions that help small businesses grow, create good-paying jobs, keep housing within reach, and ensure longtime residents can continue to call Santa Monica home.

My commitment to public service is rooted in lived experience and a belief that government should listen, lead with integrity, and put people first. I'm running to bring community voices to City Hall, build partnerships that deliver results, and create a Santa Monica where everyone has the opportunity to succeed.

Together, we can build a city that is more affordable, more inclusive, and full of opportunity for every resident. I'm Ericka Lesley, and I ask for your support for Santa Monica City Council.

Because this seat belongs to the community.
