STATEMENT OF NIKOLAS WOODS

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF NORWALK GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

As a 20-year resident and middle-school teacher, I am running for City Council for the City of Norwalk to ensure our local government listens first, collaborates, and acts in the best interest of our residents through strength and accountability.

We need responsible, transparent leadership to address our pressing challenges. My priorities include:

Public Safety: Support first responders in the tackling of property crime.
Traffic & lnfrastructure: Fix aging streets, sidewalks, and potholes, improve lighting, and expand transportation options.
Fiscal Responsibility: Ensure wise use of taxpayer dollars and increase spending transparency.
Housing Solutions: Support responsible housing that fits the city's character, protects neighborhoods, and meets state mandates.
Homelessness: Pursue compassionate, practical solutions connecting individuals with services while keeping public spaces clean and safe.
Youth & Education: Expand after-school programs, scholarships, and college-preparatory partnerships.
Senior Support: Protect and expand programs keeping seniors active and connected.
Small Business Growth: Encourage local investment to help small businesses thrive.
Parks & Quality of Life: Invest in parks, recreation, community events, and beautification to make this a better place to raise a family.

I humbly ask for your vote on November 3, 2026.
