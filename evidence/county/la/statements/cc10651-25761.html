STATEMENT OF OCTAVIA THUSS

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

LA CAÑADA UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Incumbent

Four years ago, I ran for LCUSD's Governing Board to ensure representation for all students. I am proud of the work I have done, the voices I have amplified, and I am eager to continue serving as a member of the LCUSD Governing Board.

During my tenure on the board, LCUSD has continued to position itself as a top-performing district, as seen in its CAASPP test results, received top ratings on Niche and US News and World Report, and an extraordinary percentage of our graduates pursue postsecondary studies and vocations. LCUSD maintains strong enrollment and an uncommon fiscal stability, which made the board's approval of going out for another bond measure that much more meaningful of an investment.

We have approved policies, new courses, new curriculum, and budgets that reflect our collective goals and support an exceptional educational experience. In addition, I look forward to a seamless transition of our new Superintendent. Lastly, I remain laser-focused on policy, procedures, and practices which ensure the safety of our students.

I look forward to continuing the work of the board as we seek the best solutions to changing needs while maintaining what works well for all LCUSD students.
