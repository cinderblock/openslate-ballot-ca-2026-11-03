STATEMENT OF LYNN ROBERT BARNES

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 1

ANTELOPE VALLEY-EAST KERN WATER AGENCY

Age: 76

Occupation: Retired Farmer

I am Lynn Barnes and I am running for District 1 board of directors of Antelope Valley East Kern water agency. My family Homesteaded in the Neenach area of the west end of the Antelope Valley in the late 1880's. I was born in Lancaster and raised here. The Barnes family has been farming and raising cattle since then. My father Bill Barnes, farmed over 70 years here. I have much knowledge of this district regarding farming, irrigation and the issues that AVEK deals with. I feel that because of my background and my family's history in this district I would make the ideal candidate for the position on the AVEK board of Directors. I would do my best to support all the agencies policies and procedures while also representing all the citizens of District 1.
