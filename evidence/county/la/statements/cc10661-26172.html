STATEMENT OF MALAIKA WARE

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 6

NORWALK-LA MIRADA UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Hi, my name is Malaika Ware. I am a woman of faith, a mother of six children, all of whom were educated in the Norwalk-La Mirada Unified School District, and a wife of a Biola professor.

I hold a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology, a Master of Science in Counseling/Counselor Education, and a California Pupil Personnel Services Credential. Having served as a university lecturer in Human Development, substitute teacher, high school coach, and family support specialist, I now prepare students for college and career as a high school counselor. I understand the academic, social, and emotional needs of children from diverse backgrounds.

My mission is simple: to seek the welfare of my community.

Our schools must partner with families to provide every student with a rigorous education in a safe, supportive environment while operating with transparency and accountability. Drawing on my professional experience and my perspective as a parent, I will work to expand opportunities for all students, support families, and advocate for the success and well-being of every child in our schools.

It would be an honor to serve the families of NLMUSD as your District 6 Trustee.
