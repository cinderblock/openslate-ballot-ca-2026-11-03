STATEMENT OF RALPH MUNOZ IV

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
4th District

CITY OF GLENDORA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 50

Occupation: Attorney/Business Owner/Community Leader

For 41 years, Glendora has been my home, where my wife and I have raised our family and where I have dedicated time serving our community. As an attorney, community volunteer, and local leader, I have dedicated my career serving others through thoughtful leadership, sound judgment, and a commitment to doing what is right.

I am seeking your support for Glendora City Council because our city deserves leadership that is accessible, accountable, fiscally responsible, and guided by both the law and our community's values. I believe every decision should be made with integrity, transparency, and a commitment to serving our residents.

One of Glendora's greatest needs is maintaining and improving our infrastructure while protecting taxpayers. As an attorney with a Master's Degree in Public Health from Loma Linda University, experience as a businessman and developer, and leadership as Chair of the Damien High School Consultative Board, I will bring sound judgment, strategic fiscal planning, and collaborative leadership to the City Council.

If elected, I will support our first responders, maintain and improve our streets, parks, and public infrastructure, encourage responsible economic growth while preserving Glendora's unique character, and always put our residents first.

I respectfully ask for your vote.
