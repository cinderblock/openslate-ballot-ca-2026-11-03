STATEMENT OF JESSICA GONYEA

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 4

ACTON-AGUA DULCE UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 46

Occupation: Chief Nursing Officer

As a parent, community volunteer, healthcare executive, and lifelong advocate for children and families, I am running for the School Board because I believe every student deserves the opportunity to succeed in a safe, supportive, and high-quality learning environment.

For many years, I have been actively involved in my community. I began volunteering as a teenager with the American Red Cross and have continued serving our community through classroom volunteering, athletic support, school activities, and community programs. As a mother of a student in our district, I have experienced firsthand the dedication of our teachers, staff, and administrators and understand both the opportunities and challenges facing our schools.

Professionally, I serve as the Chief Nursing Officer of a community hospital, where I oversee complex operations, multimillion-dollar budgets, quality improvement initiatives, regulatory compliance, and strategic planning. My career has been built on collaboration, accountability, and making data-driven decisions that improve outcomes while remaining fiscally responsible. I have also served on negotiation teams and worked closely with diverse groups to find practical solutions to complex issues.

If elected, I will focus on:

- Supporting academic excellence and programs that ensure every student has opportunities to prepare for college, career, military service, or skilled trades.

- Promoting safe and secure learning environments where students and staff can thrive.

- Strengthening partnerships between schools, families, and the community.

- Listening to students, parents, educators, and community members to make informed decisions that reflect the needs of our district.

Jessica Gonyea, MBA, BSN, RN
Candidate for School Board

"Putting Students First, Supporting Families, and Strengthening Our Community."
