STATEMENT OF JOHN HAN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
3rd District

CITY OF ARCADIA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Appointed Incumbent

Seniors strolling together along tree-lined streets. Kids and dogs playing in our parks. Neighbors looking out for neighbors. Businesses thriving. Citizens taking pride in their hometown.

That is the Arcadia I love and pledge to protect.

After a successful career in finance, I was appointed to the council in June. It is my honor to represent you.

Protecting Arcadia and representing you means:
Supporting first responders. Public safety is our top priority. Managing tax dollars wisely with discipline and transparency. Listening to and learning from you. Planning ahead and taking action.

Working hard and striving for improvement. Evaluating results.

For more than 30 years, I have counseled companies and clients through good times and bad. As an international advisor, I know the importance of following the data and making smart decisions.

Arcadia is a special place. Together we can continue to protect and enhance our beautiful hometown. I will work diligently to earn your trust.

Please vote for John Han.
