STATEMENT OF JEFFREY BERMAN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
4th District

CITY OF SANTA CLARITA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 66

Occupation: Screenwriter/Producer

Santa Clarita doesn't need someone promising change. We chose this city because we love it, and the last thing it needs is a candidate treating it like a problem to fix. What it needs is someone who wants to protect what works and improve what doesn't. That's not a slogan. That's the job.

Five years ago my family put down roots here. My grandchildren attend school here, and I want them, and every family, to enjoy a safe, responsible, and well-managed community.

My background as a writer, producer, and director has taught me how to solve problems, manage budgets, and bring people together. That's the kind of accountability I will bring to the City Council: less talk, more follow-through.

In talking with residents across District 4, I've heard concerns about traffic, responsible growth, and protecting our quality of life. My commitment is simple: listen, make informed decisions, and always put the interests of the people first.

Local government works best when it remembers why residents chose to live here in the first place.

I would be honored to earn your vote and the opportunity to serve my neighbors in District 4.

Jeffrey Berman
