STATEMENT OF JEANNINE WISNOSKY STEHLIN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF CULVER CITY GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Small Business Owner

I'm Jeannine Wisnosky Stehlin, and I'd be honored to earn your vote for City Council. My husband and I raised our three daughters here, and they attended CCUSD schools. Like so many families, we chose Culver City for our excellent schools, safe neighborhoods, and strong community spirit.

I'm running for City Council to enhance Culver City's quality of life by improving parks and neighborhoods, making our city more affordable, addressing homelessness, creating safer streets, sidewalks, intersections, and routes to school, work, and play; reducing traffic congestion; supporting schools, local businesses, and creative economy; and providing fiscally responsible leadership that listens, and ensures every neighbor has a voice.

I'm a small business owner and nonprofit executive director specializing in performing arts, access to justice, education, and student healthcare. I earned a BS from the University of Illinois and an MBA from the Illinois Institute of Technology.

I served as a Cultural Affairs Commissioner (2016-2024), on the Citizens Bond Oversight Committee for CCUSD, am a SAG-AFTRA union member, and co-founder of community organizations that strengthen our parks, schools, arts, and neighborhoods.

Endorsed by a coalition of residents and community leaders: Lt. Governor Eleni Kounalakis, State Treasurer Fiona Ma, former Culver City mayors, school board members, commissioners, and organizations representing labor, business, equality, environment, and the creative economy.
