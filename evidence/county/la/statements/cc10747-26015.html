STATEMENT OF RYAN RAUZON

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area D

TORRANCE UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

We chose Torrance for one reason: great public schools.

We're the proud parents of three wonderful kids: one a South High graduate, another starting his senior year, and our youngest still in middle school – with high hopes for the future.

When our schools need a hand, I show up. PTA? I served a term as president, along with other positions over the years. School Site Council? Yep.

As the husband of a teacher, I learned a one-size-fits-all education doesn't work for our kids. Struggling students need support to reach their goals.

We must help students make the connection between what they're learning today and the skills they'll need in college and careers.

I know that great teaching happens in classrooms, and that's where our tax dollars belong, not offices or bloated bureaucracies.

Torrance Unified can become the jewel of the South Bay, but our schools can't do it alone. Public institutions only succeed when we all do our part.

In my time as a small-business owner, consultant and community organizer, I've built a reputation for rolling up my sleeves and tackling problems.

I would be honored to earn your vote.
