STATEMENT OF AARON SIMENTAL

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

BASSETT UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Board President, Bassett Unified School District

Dear Community,

Bassett is ready for its transformation. We need leadership who'll see it through. As the candidate for re-election who graduated from our K-12 schools and a first-generation transfer graduate from Rio Hondo College and UCLA, I understand the struggles our students face and the resources they need to succeed.

During my term of service, I have championed real change for our students. We've expanded college/career pathways, invested in new textbooks and technology, and boosted mental health support. We've secured historic pay increases for our dedicated teachers and staff, all while maintaining ongoing fiscal health and reserves. We've invested in new playgrounds, athletic facilities, modernized CTE classroom spaces, and critical infrastructure repairs. Bassett has been my lifelong home. As your neighbor and board president, I lead countywide and statewide organizations to fight for the educational dollars our students deserve. Our schools need an energetic leader with vision and creativity to guide us into the future.

My values will always be integrity, service, and excellence. Together with our students, families, teachers and staff, I'll continue to be the leader the next generation is counting on. I hope I've earned your support.

With gratitude,
AARON SIMENTAL
