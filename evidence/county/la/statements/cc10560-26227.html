STATEMENT OF TYRON D. TURNER

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 2

WEST BASIN MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICT

I'm Tyron Turner, and I want to represent you on the West Basin Water Board.

This is the job I want, and the only job I want.

Affordability is the biggest issue facing our families today. For the past 5 years, water rates have climbed between 10-15% year after year. I won't support those increases.

If elected I will deliver:

- Clean, safe, affordable drinking water

- Lower water rates

- Sustainable water solutions for our future

Today, I serve as an advisor on a local water board, giving me firsthand experience protecting one of our community's most essential resources.

I'm a longtime Inglewood resident and a proud SEIU Local 2015 member.

I attended Southwest Community College and went on to receive my Bachelor's and Master's degree from California State University, Dominguez Hills.

I'm proud to have the support of WRD Board Member Joy Langford, Former County Supervisor Yvonne Brathwaite Burke, Inglewood Mayor James Butts, and Councilman Alex Padilla.

If you want someone 100% focused on clean, safe, affordable water, I'm your candidate.

I respectfully ask for your vote for West Basin Water District, Division 2, and I'll keep your rates as low as possible.
