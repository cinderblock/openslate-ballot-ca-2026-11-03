STATEMENT OF MARCOS TORRES ALVAREZ

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

PALMDALE SCHOOL DISTRICT

My name is Marcos Alvarez, I have lived here in Palmdale, CA for 30 years. As a longtime resident, district parent, and public servant, I am committed to ensuring every child has access to a high-quality education.

My priorities are reducing class sizes, supporting and retaining outstanding teachers through competitive pay, expanding science, technology, arts, and music programs, strengthening early education opportunities, and providing students with the resources and support they need to succeed. I believe every child deserves a safe, welcoming learning environment free from bullying, where academic excellence and opportunity come first.

Recent immigration enforcement actions in Southern California have caused anxiety for many students and families. Reports indicate these operations have affected school communities and increased fear among some children and parents. Regardless of immigration status or political views, every student deserves to feel safe, respected, and able to focus on learning. As a school board member, I will support policies that keep our schools focused on education, ensure families have access to accurate information and available resources, and work to maintain a positive, inclusive and safe environment where every child has the opportunity to learn and thrive.
