STATEMENT OF AGNES P. GONZALEZ

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 1

ROWLAND UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: RUSD Governing Board Member

My name is Agnes P. Gonzalez, and I am honored to seek your support for re-election to the Rowland Unified School District Board of Education, Trustee Area 1. Serving our students, families, educators, and community has been one of the greatest privileges of my life. I believe every child deserves a safe, high-quality education that prepares them for success in college, careers, and life.

I have proudly called Walnut home since 1985, where my husband, Jack Gonzalez, a proud U.S. Army Veteran, and I have raised our family and built lasting relationships within this wonderful community. I earned my Bachelor of Science in Business Administration from the University of Southern California and later became a Graduate Gemologist from the Gemological Institute of America. My educational background, combined with decades of community involvement, has prepared me to make thoughtful, responsible decisions for our district.

As your current Board Member and former President of the Rowland Unified School Board (2025–2026), I have remained committed to putting students first while working collaboratively with teachers, staff, parents, administrators, and community leaders. My priorities continue to include school safety, academic excellence, curriculum improvements, teacher and staff development, parent engagement, technology advancement, and responsible fiscal management.

Beyond the classroom, I have dedicated my life to community service. I currently serve as a Walnut Senior Commissioner, President of the Friends of the Filipino American Press Club, and Vice President of the Saint Lorenzo Filipino Association (2026). I am honored to have received the Larry Itliong Award (2025) for community leadership. In 2018, I was crowned Mrs. Philippines USA World and recognized among the 100 Outstanding Filipino Americans, reflecting my lifelong commitment to service and leadership.

As I seek another term, my mission remains clear:

- Students First

- Strong Leadership You Can Trust

- School Safety

- Accountability

- Transparency

- Respect

- Excellence

- Balanced Budget

- Community Collaboration

I remain committed to listening, leading with integrity, and ensuring that every decision is made with the best interests of our students at heart. Together, we can continue building a stronger future for Rowland Unified School District.

On November 3, 2026, I respectfully ask for your vote for Agnes P. Gonzalez, the experienced incumbent and proven leader for Rowland Unified School District Board of Education, Trustee Area 1. Thank you for your trust, your support, and the opportunity to continue serving our community.
