STATEMENT OF JANET WALL

CANDIDATE FOR CITY TREASURER

CITY OF MONROVIA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Over 40 years in Financial Services Management, along with a Colorado School of Banking degree, have served me well as City Treasure over the past 4 years. Just as in the private sector, oversight is critical. Working with the City's Director of Administrative Services to oversee the budget, I have been able to assure the City Council and the citizens of Monrovia that Monrovia's financials are sound and that there are well-established, and monitored, policies and procedures in place to ensure that our financials remain sound.

I have worked with the Director of Administrative Services to strengthen the role of City Treasurer as an internal financial reviewer. Collaborating with the City Council Audit Committee, the City's auditors and investment contractor, I have enhanced the role of City Treasurer by looking at the larger financial picture, being attentive to maximizing the use of City funds.

It has been my pleasure to be able to act as the eyes and ears of the citizens of Monrovia in monitoring the City of Monrovia's finances, ensuring the City is being a good steward of the public's money.

I ask for your vote to continue to serve on your behalf.
