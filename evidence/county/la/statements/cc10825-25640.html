STATEMENT OF ANNA SLOTKY REITANO

CANDIDATE FOR JUDGE OF THE SUPERIOR COURT
Office No. 65

Occupation: Deputy County Counsel, County of Los Angeles

ANNA REITANO has dedicated nearly two decades to public service, bringing the broad experience, sound judgment, and balanced perspective our courts deserve. She began her legal career clerking in the District Attorney's Office, represented public safety employees in labor and administrative matters, served for more than a decade as a Deputy Public Defender handling trials for adult and juvenile cases, and now serves as a Deputy County Counsel in Los Angeles County's Justice and Safety Division.

Working on multiple sides of the justice system has given Anna a unique understanding of how judicial decisions affect individuals, families, and our communities. She believes every person who enters her courtroom deserves dignity, respect, impartiality, and equal justice under the law.

Anna has earned the endorsement of the California National Organization for Women (CA NOW PAC), National Women's Political Caucus, UA Local Union 250, Citizens for Accountable Leadership, Stonewall Democratic Club, and both former opponents who endorsed her after the primary election. This broad coalition reflects confidence in her experience, integrity, and judicial temperament.
