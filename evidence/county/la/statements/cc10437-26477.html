STATEMENT OF LEONARD MENDOZA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF COMMERCE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 54

Occupation: Journey Level Painter

A lifelong resident of the City of Commerce. This is where my beloved wife Marlem, and I bought our home and are raising our 2 loving children Brandon and Leilanie.

My passion for Public Service began with 22.5 years of service to our great City of Commerce. As a City Employee, I served on:

- Employee Committees.
- Employee Unions-Safeguarding Employment.
- Commerce Urban Search & Rescue Team.

My passion to serve continued by advocating Against:

- I-710 Freeway Expansion.
- Union Pacific Railroad MOU.
- Vernon Power Plant.
- Lead contamination by Exide Technologies.

I had the honor to serve you in the Commerce City Council from June-2021-June-2022. Proudly served as Mayor from April 2021-April-2022.

Our successes were:

- Balanced budget- $7.6Million dollar surplus.
- Employee raises- No furloughs/layoffs.
- Voted to construct nearly 1,000 new homes.
- Planted 1.200 trees citywide.
- Delivered lowest crime rates in the Los Angeles County.
- Brought in Covid-19 testing and vaccine site to our Commerce Senior Center.

When elected I will tackle:

- High crime rate.
- Out-of-control spending.
- Affordable housing for our residents, seniors, and military veterans.

I am ready to roll up my sleeves and get to work for our residents and city employees. I humbly ask for your trust, support and vote.
