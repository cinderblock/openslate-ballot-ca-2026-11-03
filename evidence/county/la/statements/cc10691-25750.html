STATEMENT OF BRENDA SMITH

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 1

EAST WHITTIER CITY SCHOOL DISTRICT

Education and Qualifications: As a former Teacher in the East Whittier City School District, I want the best for our students. Through my experience at Ocean View Elementary, as both an Intervention Teacher and a TK teacher, I have seen first hand the needs of our students and the hard work and dedication of the staff.

I feel obligated and qualified to continue to support our students, staff and community. I want to put the students and staff first and know that they are always the top priority. As a parent and educator, I know that this will take a lot of community input and I am ready to be a part of the process to make East Whittier City School District better.

Please vote on November 3rd. Change can only happen when you vote.
