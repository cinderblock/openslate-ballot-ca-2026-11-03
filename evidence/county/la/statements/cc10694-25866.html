STATEMENT OF OSCAR MEJIA

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 1

EASTSIDE UNION SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 47

Occupation: Aerospace Engineering Manager

Every morning at school drop-off, I see the faces of our community: mothers dedicated to their children's future, and fathers working tirelessly to provide a better life. I see my own roots in them. I am a US Army veteran and a proud product of public education, earning my BS in Aerospace Engineering from CSULB and my Master's in Mechanical Engineering from CSUN. Because of the foundation public schools provided, I grew up to lead engineering teams building the world's most advanced aircraft. My commitment to our local schools is deeply personal; all four of my children have attended Eastside Union School District schools, and two are currently enrolled. Our children possess limitless potential. I am running for Trustee Area 1 to provide a renewed focus on classroom needs, bringing collaborative leadership, professional stability, and measurable results to our schools. My mission is to ensure every dollar is spent wisely on safe campus infrastructure, high-quality teacher retention, and advanced math and science programs. I will be your direct voice on this board- Bilingual-ensuring your daily sacrifices are honored with an education that prepares your children to lead the future. I respectfully ask for your vote.
