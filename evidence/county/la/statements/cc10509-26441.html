STATEMENT OF JORDAN GUZMAN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF SAN GABRIEL GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 28

Occupation: Music Educator/Musician

Jordan Guzman, 28, is a professional musician and educator based in San Gabriel, California. He earned his Bachelor of Music degree from the Bob Cole Conservatory of Music at California State University, Long Beach, and is currently pursuing a single-subject teaching credential at Cal State Los Angeles.

Jordan commits his time to community service and civic engagement. He volunteers with San Gabriel's Healthy Walking Hearts Club, leading morning walks that promote wellness and connection among residents. He also served as a governing board member at a private school in Sierra Madre.

Jordan values San Gabriel's rich history and is dedicated to honoring that legacy while working to help create a vibrant future for all who call it home. As a candidate for City Council, his mission is to serve the community and work to establish an annual jazz festival at the Mission Playhouse.
