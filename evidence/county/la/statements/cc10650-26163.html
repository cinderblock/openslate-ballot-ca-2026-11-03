STATEMENT OF ALITZAH WIENER

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

HACIENDA LA PUENTE UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Children's Educator/Entrepreneur

I am a lifelong Hacienda Heights resident and proud product of Hacienda La Puente Unified School District. Spanish was my first language, and my mother, an immigrant from Lima, Peru, spent 47 years at Rowland Unified. My father dedicated over five decades to HLPUSD as an ESL teacher, the longest tenure in district history.

As a children's educator, entrepreneur, and producer, I partnered with PhDs in education to create, write, and produce educational entertainment content for PBS Kids. As a producer, I manage vendors, negotiate contracts, and run budgets from ten thousand to hundreds of thousands, giving me real expertise in the visual and performing arts, a career pathway every school invests in.

As Vice President of the Hacienda Heights Improvement Association, I chaired the revival of our community's Fourth of July Parade, building sponsorships with local businesses and officials.

As your Trustee Area 5 representative, I will ask the hard questions, hold our district fiscally accountable, and champion the foundation and life lessons students need to succeed, in school and beyond, always keeping students at the center of every decision.

I do not just make promises. I do the work. I respectfully ask for your vote.
