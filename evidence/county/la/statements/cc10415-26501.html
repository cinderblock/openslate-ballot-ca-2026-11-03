STATEMENT OF ALEJANDRA AVILA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF BALDWIN PARK GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 62

Occupation: Councilmember

Baldwin Park resident for 52 years, elected Baldwin Park City Clerk for eight years, Baldwin Park Councilmember for seven years and Mayor for one year. Alejandra is a dedicated employee of the Baldwin Park Unified School District for the past 33 years where she had held various positions that involve helping Baldwin Park residents.

Alejandra Avila believes Baldwin Park needs leaders that care and engage in the community to ensure a better quality of life and more opportunities for all residents. She will continue to work to bring integrity and the trust of the people to the council.

Alejandra Avila's Top Priorities
Work diligently with the Police Department to assure safety is top priority; Continue to build and update infrastructure to continue the beautification of our city; Work side by side with the Baldwin Park School District to help our students with more educational and job opportunities; Plan and provide more services to our veterans; and work closely with the state and county officials to help the unhoused.

Help Baldwin Park continue to move forward. Vote Alejandra Avila for City Council and put family and community first.
