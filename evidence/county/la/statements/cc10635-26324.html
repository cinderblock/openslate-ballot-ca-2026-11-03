STATEMENT OF GARY C. RODRIGUEZ

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 4

COVINA-VALLEY UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

My name is Gary Rodriguez, and I have been proud to call the Covina-Valley Unified School District community home for more than 30 years. My wife and I raised our three children here, all of whom graduated from Covina-Valley Unified schools.

As a United States Marine Corps veteran, parent, and former Board of Education member, I bring a broad and informed perspective to our district. I believe every student deserves a high-quality education, and that taxpayers deserve responsible stewardship of their tax dollars.

I am running to provide experienced leadership that is willing to make the difficult but necessary decisions. I have consistently done so in the best interest of our students and district. I support responsible budgeting and do not believe our community should take on additional bond debt. We must make the most effective use of our existing resources while maintaining a strong focus on student success.

Together, we can strengthen our schools, ensure the safety of our students, teachers, and staff, and prepare every student for a successful future.

I respectfully ask for your support and your vote.

Gary C Rodriguez
