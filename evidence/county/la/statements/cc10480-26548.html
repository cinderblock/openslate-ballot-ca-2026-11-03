STATEMENT OF EDUARDO "EDDIE" DE LA RIVA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF MAYWOOD GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 48

Occupation: Councilman

Mi gente,

Since you first elected me in 2013, it has been the greatest honor of my life to serve the people of Maywood. Together, we have restored integrity, transparency, and accountability to City Hall.

When I sought re election in 2018, our city was still recovering from the damage caused by a previous administration whose members were charged with and convicted of public corruption crimes. We pledged to restore trust through honest, ethical leadership, and we have worked every day to earn your confidence by governing with transparency and fiscal responsibility.

We also inherited a city facing a severe financial crisis and on the brink of bankruptcy. Through disciplined leadership, we have passed balanced budgets every year since 2020, eliminated chronic deficits, and rebuilt Maywood's emergency reserves.

While I am proud of what we have accomplished together, our work is not finished. I am seeking reelection to complete major projects already underway, including new housing, the renovation of Riverfront Park, and a state of the art Teen Tech Center for our youth.

I humbly ask for your trust and your vote once again. Together, we can continue building a stronger, safer, and more prosperous Maywood.
