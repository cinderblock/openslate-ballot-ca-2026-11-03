STATEMENT OF CARLOS D. CORDERO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
3rd District

CITY OF PALMDALE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 35

Occupation: Sales Associate

Having lived and worked in Palmdale for the past 15 years, including owning and operating the food truck Top of Mis Papas with my brothers, I have proudly called District 3 home for much of that time. I believe our community has tremendous potential. District 3 is the heart of our city, home to one of Palmdale's largest concentrations of families and working residents. By working together, we can build a stronger sense of community, and opportunity throughout Palmdale as a whole.

I am running because I want to encourage greater civic engagement and help ensure every resident feels their voice matters. Like many people, I have felt unheard, and I want to reconnect with those who have become discouraged from participating.

I also believe our residents deserve to feel safe in their neighborhoods. Addressing public safety, and strengthening trust between the community and local government I will advocate alongside efforts to improve opportunities for District 3.

I am committed to listening, being present, and earning the trust of the community through honesty, accessibility, and hard work. When District 3 succeeds, Palmdale succeeds. Together, we can build a community where every resident feels heard, valued, and proud to call home.
