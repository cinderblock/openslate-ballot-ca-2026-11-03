STATEMENT OF TRISTON EZIDORE

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

CULVER CITY UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: School Board Member/Clean Energy Manager

Four years ago, you elected me to bring bold, student-centered leadership to Culver City schools. Since then, I've worked every day to deliver on that promise.

Together we have

- Raised graduation rates to 97%
- Passed Measure E, investing $358 million to modernize our schools
- Made historic investments in arts education and career pathways
- Balanced our budget while protecting investments in students and classrooms
- Established a Black Student Achievement goal to close persistent opportunity gaps
- Secured millions in additional City funding for our schools
- Advanced our Student Green New Deal and sustainability initiatives
- Championed stronger mental health supports and safe, welcoming schools
- Secured good-paying union jobs as we rebuild our campuses

There is more work ahead. In my next term, I'll focus on improving math and literacy, retaining exceptional educators, strengthening student mental health, and expanding arts and career opportunities.

I'm proud of our progress and ask for your vote to continue building excellent schools where every student can thrive.
