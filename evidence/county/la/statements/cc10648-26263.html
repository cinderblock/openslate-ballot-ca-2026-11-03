STATEMENT OF JOHNATHON "JOHNNY" YODER

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 2

HACIENDA LA PUENTE UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 43

Occupation: Teacher/School Administrator

For the past 14 years, La Puente has been my home. It is a community I care deeply about and one I am proud to serve.

As a teacher, school administrator, and former La Puente Planning Commissioner, I've spent my career helping young people discover their potential and helping organizations succeed through thoughtful leadership and collaboration. I've learned that the best decisions are made when people listen, treat one another with respect, and stay focused on the work that matters.

I believe every child deserves an outstanding education that includes strong academics, excellent teachers, safe schools, and meaningful opportunities in STEM and the arts. Education should prepare students not only for college and careers, but for lives of purpose and contribution.

I'm running because our students deserve a Board that is focused on education, works together with civility and respect, and earns the trust of our community.

I would be honored to earn your vote for Hacienda La Puente Unified School District Governing Board Member, Trustee Area No. 2.
