STATEMENT OF PATRICIA "TRISH" GONZALES

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
1st District

CITY OF AZUSA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 59

Occupation: Retired

Leading with heart has always been my guiding principle, and making a meaningful difference has always been my purpose. As your City Councilmember, I will prioritize transparency, meaningful public engagement, building public trust, fiscal responsibility, and inclusive leadership. I will bring proven experience, sound judgment, and a steadfast commitment to serving the residents of District 1 and the City of Azusa with integrity.

For more than 25 years, I proudly served the public at the Metropolitan Water District of Southern California, retiring as a Human Resources Principal Labor Relations Analyst. My experience in labor negotiations, ethics, workplace investigations, conflict resolution, and building collaborative solutions earned me a reputation for professionalism, integrity, and accountability.

My commitment to public service extends beyond my career. I have served as President of my HOA Board for 12 years, Planning Commissioner for the City of Monrovia, Parks and Recreation Commissioner for the City of Azusa, and currently serve my second term as President of the San Gabriel/Pomona Regional Center Board of Directors, helping oversee an organization with a budget exceeding $600 million that serves more than 19,000 individuals with developmental disabilities and their families.

Experience that Matters-Leadership you can Trust.
