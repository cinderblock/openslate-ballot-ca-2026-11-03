STATEMENT OF JOE W. BOWERS JR.

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF EDUCATION,
Trustee Area 2

INGLEWOOD UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Education Advocate

I am running for the Inglewood Unified School District Board of Education Trustee Seat 2 because every child deserves an excellent education, and our community deserves experienced leadership committed to student achievement, fiscal responsibility, and accountability.

My commitment to Inglewood Unified is personal. My son attended school in the district, and my wife served as president of the Board of Education.

For years, I have advocated for stronger public schools through community engagement and public policy. I co-founded the Education Equity Coalition, serve as the Inglewood Area Ministers Association's liaison to the school district, serve on a district committee, and work with community leaders, board members, district administrators, and state legislators to strengthen our schools.

A Stanford graduate with a B.S., M.S. in Engineering, and an MBA, I led engineering, business, and strategic planning initiatives for Fortune 500 companies, including IBM, Exxon, and The Coca-Cola Company. As a contributing editor for California Black Media, I write about education policy, school finance, and school governance.

I will listen, ask tough questions, make informed decisions, and always put the interests of students and taxpayers first.

I respectfully ask for your vote.
