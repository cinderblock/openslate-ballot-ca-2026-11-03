STATEMENT OF SCHERISE R. ACOSTA

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5
(Unexpired term ending December 12, 2028)

LITTLE LAKE CITY SCHOOL DISTRICT SPECIAL ELECTION

Age: 55

Occupation: School Attendance Clerk

I am a lifelong member of this community, where four generations of my family have attended Little Lake City School District's schools, including my granddaughter today at Cresson Elementary. I work as a school attendance clerk for the Los Angeles Unified School District and have served as a City of Norwalk Social Services Commissioner for seven years. I have volunteered extensively in local schools, including rebuilding the Santa Fe High School cheer booster club after it was lost during the pandemic, and serving as its president for two years.

I am running for the Little Lake City School Board because every child deserves a boardmember that puts students first. I am committed to ensuring our schools have the resources needed for academic success, including support for special education and manageable class sizes. I will bring transparency and real communication to the board, so parents always understand how decisions are made. I also believe in fiscal responsibility paired with modern, well-maintained schools built to serve our students for years to come.

As a parent, grandmother, and lifelong community volunteer, I understand what our schools need because I have lived it. I ask for your vote so I can bring steady, trustworthy leadership to our Governing Board.
