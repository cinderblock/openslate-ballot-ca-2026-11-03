STATEMENT OF RAFAEL GUTIERREZ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
4th District
(Unexpired term ending November 7, 2028)

CITY OF MONTEBELLO SPECIAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Teacher

My name is Rafael Gutierrez, and for over 45 years, Montebello has been my home. As a lifelong resident, educator, coach, and community volunteer, I have dedicated my life to serving our families and investing in the future of our city.

In addition I was honored to serve as your elected City Treasurer from 2018-2022.

My priorities are clear: improving our infrastructure, supporting responsible housing projects, enhancing our parks and neighborhoods, attracting new businesses, restaurants, and jobs, and making sure Montebello remains a safe and great place to live, work, and raise a family.

I believe in the values that make our community strong—family, education, integrity, teamwork, and hard work. I will be a strong voice for our district and will work collaboratively to create positive results for all Montebello residents.

I look forward to serving you on a positive path in this beautiful community we call home.

I respectfully ask for your vote.

Rafael Gutierrez
