STATEMENT OF JULIAN JACKSON

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF SANTA FE SPRINGS GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 27

Occupation: MPA

I've had the privilege of living and growing up in the City of Santa Fe Springs my entire life. The strong connection with the city has allowed me to cherish fond memories with my family at city events, build amazing neighborhood friendships, and now take on the opportunity to serve the community to the best of my ability.

I am product of the LLCSD, attending Lakeview and Lake Center, graduated with honors from SFHS, and I went on continuing my education by completing my undergraduate at CSULA and recently my graduate from CSULB. My education has led me to work within the public sector for over 8 years.

Now, my candidacy for City Council has motivated me to advocate for stronger support for our youth, greater investment in our small businesses and developing a streamlined process for them, and expanded access to programs for our older adult population, and to ensure our city develops guidelines to prevent the construction of data centers that would harm the community.

I humbly ask for your vote, providing a voice for all residents and ensuring our city's future remains strong for generations to come.

Thank you and many blessings to everyone.
