STATEMENT OF THOMAS RAMON MARTIN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF MAYWOOD GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Healthcare Industry Professional

Several residents, from Maywood, asked me to run for City Council, due to issues affecting our people. I have always helped people in need here in our city. I have an open door to help our residents. I am bilingual and Latino.

We need more trees, we need new bus shelters, we need to continue to repair our sewers, and we need to empower parents to have new programs at our parks. Graffiti and homelessness has gotten worst in our city. I will work on programs to keep our sidewalks clean. I opposed any new homeless shelter in the City, where it can bring more homeless people. We need more safety in our streets. I oppose data centers but support Public Wi-Fi.

The fire in Boyle Heights affected us but the City Council, did not do anything to help us. I will make sure, whenever something like that occurs that we help the residents. ln addition, I will work with the County and State to get us grant funding and help. We need new job programs to help our teens, young adults, and older folks. I will help make Maywood a better place to live and work at.
