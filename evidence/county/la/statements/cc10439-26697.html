STATEMENT OF KIMBERLEY "KIM" GRIFFIN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF CULVER CITY GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 63

Occupation: Public Safety Professional

I am dedicated, personally and professionally, to serving the Culver City community. As a longtime resident, public safety professional, volunteer, and civic leader, I bring 20+ years of experience working collaboratively to address public safety, homelessness, human trafficking, and quality of life.

I have always been guided by common sense, accountability, integrity, and respect for our community. Throughout my career, I have worked with government agencies, nonprofit organizations, businesses, and community members to solve problems, build partnerships, and deliver meaningful results. I believe government works best when it listens to residents, spends taxpayer dollars responsibly, plans thoughtfully for the future, and delivers exceptional public services.

As your Councilmember, I will work to:

- Connect the unhoused with critical services
- Fight for affordable housing, so seniors and young professionals can afford to live here
- Enhance mobility without creating gridlock
- Help small businesses thrive
- Protect our community
- Invest in city infrastructure
- Stop wasteful spending
- Make City Hall more transparent

Culver City is a remarkable city with an engaged and caring community. I am committed to preserving what makes our neighborhoods special while planning responsibly for the future. It would be an honor to earn your vote and serve as your representative on the Culver City City Council.
