STATEMENT OF DAVID L. MUSE

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS

VALLEY COUNTY WATER DISTRICT

I respectfully ask for your vote for re-election to the Valley County Water District Board of Directors. During my nine years of service, including my current role as Board President, I have remained committed to protecting our community's water resources through fiscal responsibility, transparency, and accountability.

Together, we have kept Valley County Water District's rates among the lowest in the San Gabriel Valley while continuing to provide drinking water that meets or exceeds all state and federal quality standards. We have also replaced more than 10 miles of aging water pipelines to strengthen the reliability of our water system.

Our Board expanded customer programs such as the Affordable Rate Program and 360 Water Tracking, helping families detect leaks and lower water bills. Through our partnership with Baldwin Park Unified School District and the District's Aqua Academy, we continue educating students about water conservation and environmental stewardship.

I am proud of what we have accomplished, but there is more to do. With your support, I will continue protecting our water supply, investing wisely in our infrastructure, and providing safe, reliable, and affordable water service for every customer.

Endorsed by local community leaders, educators, public safety professionals, and neighbors committed to responsible leadership.
