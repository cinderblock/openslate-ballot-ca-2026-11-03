STATEMENT OF RICARDO ORTEGA

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 2

ANTELOPE VALLEY COMMUNITY COLLEGE DISTRICT

My name is Ricardo Ortega, and I am running for the Antelope Valley Community College Board, Area 2, because every student deserves a clear pathway to opportunity.

As a former foster youth and first-generation college graduate, I understand how education, mentorship, and support can change a life. I have dedicated my career to youth advocacy, mental health, public service, and expanding opportunities for underserved communities. As Chief Executive Officer of Pax Imperium, I build partnerships among education, government, labor, business, and community leaders.

If elected, I will work to improve graduation and transfer rates, expand access to required classes, and strengthen tutoring, counseling, mental health, and basic-needs services. I will advocate for transparency and accountability of taxpayer dollars, safe and modern facilities, transportation assistance, housing solutions, and stronger support for foster youth, veterans, working adults, and first-generation students.

I will build partnerships with unions, apprenticeship programs, aerospace, healthcare, technology, and local employers to prepare students for good-paying careers in the Antelope Valley. I will invest in faculty and staff, support responsible budgeting, and ensure students and employees have a meaningful voice in college decisions.

I respectfully ask for your vote.

Ricardo Ortega — AV College Board, Area 2
