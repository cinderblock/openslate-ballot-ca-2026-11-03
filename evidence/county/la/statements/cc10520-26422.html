STATEMENT OF AL RIOS

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF SOUTH GATE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Councilmember/Retired Educator

For over 26 years, I have proudly served the City of South Gate as an educator, community leader, and Councilmember. My commitment has always been to improving public safety and upgrading city infrastructure, including sidewalks, streets, lighting, and water systems. I remain dedicated to expanding educational opportunities, supporting working families, and enhancing the quality of life in our neighborhoods.

As a longtime advocate for higher education, I have led efforts to increase access to college and career opportunities. Next year, we will open a newly constructed college campus in South Gate that will provide academic, workforce training, career pathways, and employment opportunities for current and future generations to come.

As a member of the HUB Cities Board, I led efforts to bring the HUB Cities WorkSource Center to South Gate on Tweedy Boulevard, connecting residents with job training and employment opportunities.

I have also advocated for safer, cleaner parks, improved city services, environmental justice, and additional funding to support elders, youth, and families.

As your Councilmember, I will continue working to strengthen public safety, improve city services, and ensure South Gate remains a safe, thriving, and family-centered community.

I respectfully ask for your support and your vote.
