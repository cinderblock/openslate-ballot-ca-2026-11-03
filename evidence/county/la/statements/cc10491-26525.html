STATEMENT OF VINH T. NGO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
5th District

CITY OF MONTEREY PARK GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 52

Occupation: Monterey Park Councilmember

Monterey Park gave my immigrant family the opportunity to prosper, and I've spent my life giving back. I grew up here attending: Monterey Vista, Garvey, Mark Keppel, then earned USC Accounting and Finance degrees.

When an industrial data center threatened our neighborhoods, I acted. I brought the emergency moratorium to the Council and launched the legislative process that became Measure NDC - delivering a permanent, 88% voter-approved ban, and making Monterey Park the first city in America to prohibit data centers.

You also elected me to keep our neighborhoods safe and our city strong. We delivered:
- Stronger police and fire departments
- Over half our streets resurfaced, plus $25 million in infrastructure, water, and sewer upgrades
- Funding for the Barnes Park Aquatic Center
- Streamlined permitting and revitalized business corners
- Three straight balanced budgets with surpluses

I donate every Council stipend back to our schools, library, police, fire, and local nonprofits.

I'll continue to empower Monterey Park, ensuring it as the best place to Live, Work and Play.

I'm proudly endorsed by Congresswoman Judy Chu, Senator Sasha Renée Pérez, Senator Maria Elena Durazo, Assemblymember Mike Fong, and Supervisor Hilda Solis.

I ask for your vote to re-elect me to City Council.
