STATEMENT OF SHAZIA HOQ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
1st District

CITY OF MONTEBELLO GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Business Woman

My name is Shazia Hoq, and I am a businesswoman, community leader, and candidate for Montebello City Council District 1.

I came to America from Bangladesh at a young age with a dream of building a better future through hard work, opportunity, and service. I earned an Associate Degree in Liberal Arts from Los Angeles Valley College and pursued Psychology at California State University Channel Islands.

With 20 years of experience in business as a President of Sarah & Brother Inc., a California retail business. My experience has taught me the importance of creating jobs, managing resources responsibly, and supporting local businesses.

I also served two terms as Vice President of the Los Angeles Wilshire Center Koreatown Neighborhood Council, working with elected officials, nonprofits, and residents to advance economic development, public safety, housing, and homelessness solutions.

I am running because District 1 deserves a strong, accessible voice at City Hall. I will fight for transparency, accountability, responsible budgeting, safer neighborhoods, economic opportunity, and a city government that respects residents, employees, labor partners, and public safety professionals.

I respectfully ask for your vote to build a stronger, safer, and more prosperous Montebello.
