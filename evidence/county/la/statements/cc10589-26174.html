STATEMENT OF ADAM BRUNO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF TRUSTEES,
Seat 4

LOS ANGELES COMMUNITY COLLEGE DISTRICT

Education and mental health have both been lifelong passions of mine. My first college experience was at Los Angeles Valley Community College, where I received my AA before continuing to a 4-year university and then graduate school. I believe community colleges are the backbone of early adult education, and a well-educated society is a successful society. If we can help community colleges thrive, we can significantly improve the quality of life in Los Angeles. All students, regardless of ethnicity, income, religion, or other background deserve an accessible and high quality education, and it would be my honor to make this happen.
