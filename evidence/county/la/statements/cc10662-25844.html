STATEMENT OF LIZ PETERSON

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

PALOS VERDES PENINSULA UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Mother/Business Owner

My name is Liz Peterson, and I'm running for the Palos Verdes Peninsula Unified School District Board because our schools shaped my family, and I want to protect what makes them work.

Both of my children went through PVPUSD; my youngest graduated this past year. I've spent those years volunteering rather than watching from the sidelines, serving in several PTA board positions and on the district's Local Control and Accountability committee from 2023 to 2025, where I helped review how the district sets priorities and spends its money.

That experience taught me the difference between a budget on paper and a classroom that has what it needs.

Our district faces real challenges. Declining enrollment is reshaping our schools and forcing hard decisions about facilities and staffing. And voters recently approved a bond that many residents opposed, which makes it more important that every dollar is spent transparently, on schedule, and on the projects promised.

I will push for honest budgeting, open communication with parents, and spending decisions this community can verify.

I'm not a politician. I'm a parent who stayed involved after graduation, because these schools belong to all of us.
