STATEMENT OF DOUG STEWART

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF MALIBU GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 78

Occupation: Councilmember/Business Owner

Malibu has been my home for more than 25 years. I built a career in finance and budgeting here, and I've spent years volunteering locally, including on the Public Safety Commission, with CERT, and eventually as your Mayor.

The fires and floods of the past year tested our city like never before. With our community's support, I stepped up to meet those challenges head on, whether it was helping open the Rebuild Center for residents in need, working with the Governor's office to accelerate like-for-like rebuilds, or supporting the city's efforts to recover what was lost through litigation and other avenues.

I ran on getting results - and we have delivered. The Highway Patrol returned to PCH after 33 years. Speed cameras are coming to our most dangerous stretches, funded without local taxes. Homelessness dropped from more than 250 people to fewer than 50. The General Fund gained over $4 million in new revenue, none from residents' pockets.

There is still important work ahead: completing the recovery, maintaining strong finances, and protecting the unique character that makes Malibu special. I would be honored to continue serving you.

Please vote to re-elect Doug Stewart!
