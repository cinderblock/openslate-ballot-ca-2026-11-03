STATEMENT OF JOHNNY GARCIA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF PICO RIVERA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

My heart and passion is Pico Rivera. It is not just my hometown; it is where I was educated, married my wife, purchased our home, raised my family, and continue to serve the community I love.

Since being elected to the City Council, I have served as Mayor and represented Pico Rivera on the Los Angeles County Sanitation Board. In both roles, I have worked with commitment, integrity, and a deep respect for the residents who trust us.

Together, we have strengthened Pico Rivera's financial future, maintained strong reserves, earned an AA credit rating, and adopted balanced budgets investing in public safety, streets, parks, and infrastructure. We have secured major grant funding, advanced improvements to Smith Park Aquatic Center, Rio Hondo Park, street resurfacing, and water system upgrades, while supporting new businesses, housing, and projects that improve life for families, seniors, and youth.

My priorities: safe streets, clean water, and parks; responsible economic development; strong public safety; and fiscal responsibility and transparency.

I will continue working hard, staying humble, listening to residents, and putting Pico Rivera first. With your vote, I would be honored to keep serving and building a stronger, safer, more prosperous Pico Rivera.
