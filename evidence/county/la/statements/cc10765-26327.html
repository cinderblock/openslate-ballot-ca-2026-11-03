STATEMENT OF TODD J. ROBERTS

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

BELLFLOWER UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 60

Occupation: Professor

I am a professor of biology at a local college with more than 30 years of experience in higher education and community partnership work. As a first-generation college graduate, I have dedicated my career to expanding access, strengthening student pathways, and supporting collaborative governance.

I hold a B.S. in Biology from UC Davis, an M.S. from the University of Georgia, and a Ph.D. in Animal and Dairy Science from the University of Georgia. I enjoy teaching, developing curriculum, and creating learning experiences that help students succeed.

My leadership experience includes serving as department chair in Natural Sciences, Health & Kinesiology, Academic Senate Vice President, and an AFT campus representative on the negotiating team. These roles have strengthened my work in curriculum development, faculty mentorship, program review, and responsible stewardship of educational resources.

I approach public service with a focus on listening, collaboration, and clear communication. Thank you for taking the time to read.

Vote Dr. Todd

Todd J. Roberts, Ph.D.
