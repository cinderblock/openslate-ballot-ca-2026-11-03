STATEMENT OF AMARPREET SINGH MALIK

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

LA CAÑADA UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 47

Occupation: Attorney

I am running for the LCUSD School Board because I believe education is the bedrock of thriving communities and I am dedicated to ensuring that our schools provide an excellent environment for learning and belonging for all students.

I attended LCUSD from elementary through high school, and when my wife and I started our family, we chose La Canada so our four children could benefit from the same exceptional schools and close-knit community that shaped me.

As an attorney and entrepreneur, I have the legal, financial, and governance experience to help ensure taxpayer dollars are managed transparently and responsibly.

I have dedicated myself to serving our community through coaching youth softball and basketball, volunteering with the PTA, supporting scouting, and serving on the District's Citizens Oversight and Parcel Tax Committees to help ensure voter-approved funds were spent as promised.

As a Trustee, I will continue to support our community by maintaining a focus on student learning and well-being, transparent and responsible governance, and supporting our new Superintendent's leadership. I believe every student deserves to feel a true sense of belonging in our schools. I know firsthand the power of an LCUSD education, and I am committed to preserving that promise.
