STATEMENT OF MESHAL "KASH" KASHIFALGHITA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
3rd District

CITY OF LA VERNE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 50

Occupation: Army Reserve Officer

As your City Councilmember, it has been my honor to serve our community with integrity, transparency, and a commitment to putting residents first. Together, we have worked to strengthen public safety, support local businesses, improve our neighborhoods, and make thoughtful decisions that protect the quality of life that makes our city such a special place to call home.

I am seeking your vote for re-election because there is still important work to do. I will continue to be an accessible, accountable leader who listens to residents, works collaboratively, and makes decisions based on what is best for our community.

My experience in public service, military leadership, and local government has prepared me to address the challenges ahead while planning responsibly for our city's future. I remain committed to fiscal responsibility, public safety, maintaining our parks and infrastructure, supporting our local economy, and preserving the character of our neighborhoods. Together, we can continue building a safe, strong, and thriving community for every resident.
