STATEMENT OF LAURIE LIEBERMAN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF EDUCATION

SANTA MONICA-MALIBU UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

I believe that strong public schools are key to preserving democracy and opportunity. As a parent activist and as a School Board member, I have been deeply committed to excellence and equity and to ensuring that all children graduate from high school prepared for success in an ever-changing world. I have used my experience in our diverse district and my legal and policy-making expertise to help make our great schools even greater by:

- Promoting high-quality Transitional Kindergarten/early childhood programs that prepare students for kindergarten;

- Supporting students with engaging and rigorous curriculum that teaches critical thinking skills;

- Ensuring strong teaching and necessary interventions that help all students attain academic mastery and address the achievement/opportunity gap;

- Evaluating and navigating the appropriate role for technology in our schools;

- Maintaining and modernizing schools to ensure staff and student safety and access to facilities that support 21st century learning;

- Enhancing our revered music and fine arts programs;

- Balancing our budget, strengthening our financial reserves, and maintaining our strong credit rating.

I am supported by:

Community for Excellent Public Schools
Congressmember Ted Lieu
Senator Ben Allen
Assemblymember Rick Zbur
All current School Board members

I would be honored to earn your support too.
