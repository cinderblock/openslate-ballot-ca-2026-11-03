STATEMENT OF GLORIA D. GRAY

CANDIDATE FOR MAYOR

CITY OF INGLEWOOD PRIMARY NOMINATING ELECTION

Age: 82

Occupation: Inglewood City Councilwoman

For generations, Inglewood has been a city built by hardworking families, resilience, and opportunity. As your City Councilwoman and after more than 30 years serving Los Angeles County in healthcare administration, I have dedicated my life to public service, accountable leadership, and delivering results.

Together, we have helped position Inglewood as one of Southern California's most dynamic cities. Now, it's time to ensure every resident and every neighborhood shares in that success as we build the next chapter of our city's future.

As your Mayor, I will focus on the priorities that matter most: safe neighborhoods, responsive city services, affordable housing, support for renters and homeowners, good-paying union jobs, thriving small businesses, quality healthcare through strong community partnerships, and a transparent, accountable City Hall that puts residents first.

I believe progress should be measured not only by new development, but by the quality of life we create. Every investment should strengthen our neighborhoods, every voice should be heard, and every family should have the opportunity to succeed.

My vision is One Inglewood—a city united by purpose, where opportunity reaches every neighborhood, families thrive, businesses grow, and future generations are proud to call Inglewood home.

I respectfully ask for your vote.
