STATEMENT OF ROB SAEMANN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF HERMOSA BEACH GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Hermosa Beach City Councilmember

My wife Katie and I have been blessed to call Hermosa Beach home for 52 years. As a general contractor for five decades. I've built and remodeled homes and businesses across Hermosa. I get things done, on time and on budget.

My qualifications:

- Your Councilmember since 2022; Mayor in 2026
- Board Member, South Bay Cities Council of Governments
- 8 years as Planning Commissioner
- 2 years as Public Works Commissioner
- President, Hermosa Beach Kiwanis Club

I worked to begin a turnaround at City Hall. Spending gets real scrutiny, stalled projects are getting finished, and we are rebuilding a culture of cooperation. There is more to do.

When re-elected, I will work for:

- Careful investment in infrastructure that keeps Hermosa safe, beautiful and economically vibrant
- Protecting public safety while building community trust
- Local control over decisions that belong here in Hermosa
- Smart financial choices that make taxpayer dollars go further

Big decisions are ahead: saving our Pier, tough budget negotiations, major infrastructure work. My unique experience with construction, land use, budgets, and governance really matters now.

I pledge to donate my entire council salary to local projects and good causes. I ask for your trust and your vote.
