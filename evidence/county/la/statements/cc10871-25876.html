STATEMENT OF DORA D. SANDOVAL

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5
(Unexpired term ending December 12, 2028)

LITTLE LAKE CITY SCHOOL DISTRICT SPECIAL ELECTION

Occupation: Economy Efficiency Commissioner

I am a former Little Lake City School Board Member and longtime community leader. My husband and I raised our three daughters in this district, and our family has always been committed to serving our community.

I am running for Little Lake City School District Trustee Area 5 to ensure fiscal responsibility, accountability, and transparency while advocating for students, parents, teachers, classified staff, and our community.

During my service on the School Board from 2015 to 2020, I helped secure more than $200,000 in funding, including $125,000 to improve William Orr Elementary School and the Sara Mendez Park playground. I believe every decision should be guided by what is best for students and by responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars.

As your Trustee, I will listen to our community, ask the tough questions, make informed decisions, protect taxpayer dollars, and stand up for students, families, teachers, and classified staff with honesty, integrity, and transparency.

I am honored to be endorsed by the Little Lake Educational Association (LLEA), the teachers who serve our students every day.

I respectfully ask for your vote on November 3.Together, we can build a stronger future for every student.

Thank you and God Bless

Dora Sandoval
