STATEMENT OF JÖEL ZALDÍVAR

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF WEST HOLLYWOOD GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Being a resident of the proudest city in America, I'm excited to run for West Hollywood City Council and address the diverse wants and needs of our residents and local business owners.

As the son of Cuban refugees, I saw firsthand that hard work, dedication and persistence led my parents to achieve a better life for our family of 7. That same work ethic drove me to start my own small business right in the heart of West Hollywood, OPVS. A neighborhood gym that evolved into a safe space for the community to come together, get strong and feel seen.

I hold a B.S. in Political Science which I'll use to shape public policy that can better the daily lives of our residents and businesses. I also hold a B.A. in International Studies and find myself in a unique position to understand the multicultural diversity that makes up our great city.

Small businesses are fundamental to a thriving community. I will spearhead the creation of a Commercial Rent Stabilization Division to tackle the ever-increasing price of goods and services, driven by a largely speculative and greedy commercial market. Small business owners are being strong armed into leases that force their hand into increasing prices to our residents. I will take immediate action to stop this endless cycle.

Safety is paramount. Safety for our seniors, our marginalized communities, our Russian speaking residents, our businesses and our tourist economy. I will champion the creation of the West Hollywood Police Department. We have become too reliant on Los Angeles for our policing. Our city's needs are multifaceted, and our police force needs to reflect the complex relationship many in our community have already experienced.

I will pioneer the creation of the UPP (Universal Parking Pass) for city residents. We need a complete overhaul of our Residential Parking Division. Aggressive ticketing practices create an unaffordable burden on our community. Our streets have become a complex web of signs that all too often act as a punishment and not a privilege to our residents.

To know me is to understand that my deep sense of personal responsibility and work ethic is rooted in my love for my partner, my children, my friends, and this beautiful city I call home. I hope to be lucky enough to garner your support and your vote in this upcoming election. Please visit www.joel4weho.com or email: vote@joel4weho.com
