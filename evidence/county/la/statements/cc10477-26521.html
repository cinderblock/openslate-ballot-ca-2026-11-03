STATEMENT OF EMILIANO ARELLANO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF LYNWOOD GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 26

Occupation: Public Safety Commissioner

I'm Emiliano Arellano, and I'm running for Lynwood City Council to draw on my legal experience to make City Hall more responsive to your needs.

I learned public service by watching my mother, who worked two jobs, and my father, a teacher, spend evenings speaking up at City Council meetings for our neighbors.

As a student at 32nd Street USC Magnet, I founded an organization to feed people experiencing homelessness and support local animal shelters. At Cal State Dominguez Hills, I earned degrees in Public Administration and Environmental Studies and built the campus's urban farm, deepening my commitment to clean, healthy neighborhoods.

At Southwestern Law School, I represented children in dependency court and helped survivors of violence navigate the immigration system during the recent wave of aggressive ICE raids.

Recently, as Chair of Lynwood's Public Safety, Parking & Traffic Commission, I turned an inactive body into one that holds the city accountable, helping fix long-ignored problems like potholes.

As your Councilmember, I'll strengthen public safety, protect immigrant families, expand access to city resources, and make City Hall more responsive and transparent.

I humbly ask for your vote.
