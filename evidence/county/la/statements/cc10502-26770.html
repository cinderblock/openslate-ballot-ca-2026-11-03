STATEMENT OF CYNTHIA TORRES

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF EDUCATION,
District No. 7

PASADENA UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 68

Occupation: Education Services Consultant

I am running for the Pasadena School Board because I believe every decision we make centers on the question: what will best serve our students?

Pasadena has a proud tradition of strong public schools, deeply engaged families, dedicated educators, and community members who care about every child's success. That spirit is one of our District's greatest strengths.

I believe I can contribute meaningfully to the District's strategy and performance. In 2018–2019, I served on the District's Bond Oversight Committee, where I contributed to careful stewardship of taxpayer-supported investments in our schools. I have previous deep experience with school district finance, an area that is especially important now.

Our District's finances are under real pressure. Declining enrollment, the end of one-time pandemic relief, rising costs, and the devastating effects of the Altadena fire have created difficult challenges. Assuring fiscal stability requires disciplined budgeting and a plan that protects students and our schools' long-term health.

It is time to think boldly about opportunity. I want to focus on revenue generation and strengthening the District's prospects through fundraising, sponsorships, deeper partnerships with our municipalities, and renewed community confidence.

Together, we can meet this moment with a shared commitment for every student's future.
