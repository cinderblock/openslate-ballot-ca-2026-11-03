STATEMENT OF CHRISTINE CHACON KENNEDY

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 3

EAST WHITTIER CITY SCHOOL DISTRICT

Serving our district has never been about recognition but always about creating opportunities, strengthening our schools, and leaving a lifelong legacy our children will be proud of as they are the heart of EWCSD. Their future deserves experienced and honest leadership.

Being a Board Member is more than a title. It is a commitment to serve, putting students first, listening to families, leading with integrity, and protecting every taxpayer dollar. Every vote I cast is guided by one question: What is best for our students, district, and community?

This commitment has guided my work to establish a Student Board Member, launch district spelling bee and speech contests, support the restoration of the historic Emma Wicker Auditorium, and create our annual Engage East Whittier event that brings our community together.

I believe leadership means preparing students for life.

That is why I continue to champion innovative opportunities, including school garden centers where students can learn about sustainability, nutrition, and caring for our environment.

Together, we can build stronger schools and brighter futures for every child. I respectfully ask for your vote. On Election Day, remember: Christine Chacon Kennedy –leading the way, right from the start.
