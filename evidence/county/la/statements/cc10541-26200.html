STATEMENT OF STEVEN M. MCCALL

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF TRUSTEES,
Trustee Area No. 3

ALTADENA LIBRARY DISTRICT

I have lived in Altadena for 39 years. I'm seeking a position on the Altadena Library District Board of Trustees because I am an ardent advocate of the importance of libraries to democratic societies and a believer in libraries' ability to provide access to information and ongoing learning freely to all who visit.

After the January 2025 Eaton Fire, when my wife and I were able to return after evacuation, naturally our first priority was to check on our residence. It was standing. Our next stop was to check on the Main Altadena Library branch, located a few blocks from our home. We were relieved to find it also was still standing.

Since retiring 10 years ago, I have served on several non-profit boards, Including the Los Angeles Conservancy, Las Angelitas del Pueblo, and the Altadena Historical Society. I believe this experience has prepared me well to serve as a trustee on the Altadena Library Board.

As Altadena rebuilds and reimagines its future, I am eager to support the Library Board, in an official capacity, as that work progresses.

I welcome your support.
