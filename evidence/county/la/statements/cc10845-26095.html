STATEMENT OF BRYAN BIGGERS

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 2

WILLIAM S. HART UNION HIGH SCHOOL DISTRICT

I'm Bryan Biggers — a Hart graduate and a Hart dad. My wife and I grew up in Santa Clarita and attended local schools. We're raising our children here, two of them in Hart district schools this year. For 15 years I've coached local kids — AYSO, SCV Flag Football, Flying Phoenix Track Club, and SCV Mountain Bike Team.

I also co-founded a local construction company, where I've spent my career managing budgets, meeting deadlines, and answering for results. The Hart board oversees nearly half a billion dollars annually. I understand balance sheets, and I'll ask the hard questions.

Strong students require strong teachers, solid opportunity, and safe schools. I'll fight for the resources our teachers need, adequate Honors, AP, and Career Technical Education so every student can excel, and campuses that are secure and staffed by those who know and care for our students — because learning doesn't happen if a child doesn't feel safe.

That's why parents need a board that listens, provides straight answers, and maintains fiscal accountability. Every student deserves to be safe, respected, and well taught —without exception. I'd be honored to do the job.
