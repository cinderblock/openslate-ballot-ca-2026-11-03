STATEMENT OF ERIC HORNE

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF HERMOSA BEACH GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Wildfire Policy Director

Dear neighbor,

We need new leadership to tackle our city's fiscal crisis and protect what makes Hermosa special.

I grew up in Hermosa Beach, attending Hermosa Valley, Mira Costa, and El Camino before starting my career in public service and eventually earning a Harvard MBA.

My experience has prepared me for City Council: as the Food and Drug Administration's liaison to Congress, I strengthened oversight over $1 billion in drug safety funding and helped save taxpayers $20 million by consolidating facilities. Today, I run the California Chapter of Megafire Action, advancing legislation to cut red tape for controlled burns and deploy technology to protect communities.

Hermosa faces real challenges: a structural deficit with increasing County Fire costs, an aging pier with no funding plan, and struggling businesses.

We need fiscal discipline and fresh ideas:

1) Save taxpayers and local businesses money: use new technologies to modernize permitting, overtime staffing, and administrative work.

2) Establish a Pier Fund: levy a surcharge tax on short-term rentals (Airbnbs) and secure state/federal dollars to fund our pier's future.

3) Protect public safety: shield police and fire from budget cuts and diversify patrols on speeding, e-bikes, and quality-of-life issues.

Together, we can make government work better for you.
