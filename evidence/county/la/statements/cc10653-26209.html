STATEMENT OF JULIAN DEL REAL-CALLEROS

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

LYNWOOD UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 36

Occupation: Teacher/Governing Board Member

Since 2021, I've had the honor of serving on the Lynwood Unified School District Governing Board of Education. My colleagues appointed me to join them in service, and in 2022 I won election to a four-year term unopposed. This year I ask for your vote to continue serving Lynwood students and families on the Lynwood School Board.

Since joining the board, I've tirelessly advocated for resources and improvements that benefit students:

- lobbying for funding to rebuild Lynwood High School on Imperial Highway
- championing recognition and support for our most vulnerable and marginalized students and families
- demanding improved classrooms and facilities.

One of my earliest priorities was renovating our athletic facilities; today a Sports Complex is underway, including a refurbished football field, a new baseball field, a new softball field, and a multipurpose field.

I believe in teamwork and measurable results. My experience playing youth baseball and soccer, combined with my work as a Restorative Justice teacher, informs my collaborative leadership and commitment to safe, supportive schools where every student can thrive. Together we can promote rigorous college and career pathways, restorative justice practices for accountability and healing, and stronger parent involvement. Vote for Julian Del Real-Calleros on November 3rd.
