STATEMENT OF RICHARD (RICK) HEVER

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 3

WHITTIER CITY SCHOOL DISTRICT

It's a tremendous honor to serve the Whittier City School District community. As an incumbent member of the Board of Education, I remain committed to ensuring that every student has the opportunity to succeed.

Together, the WCSD community successfully passed a $99 million school facilities bond, allowing us to modernize and improve our schools. During my tenure on the Board, our district has celebrated remarkable achievements, including National Blue Ribbon, (2) California Distinguished Schools, and (2) State and National Schools to Watch.

WCSD has established a Dual Enrollment Program with Rio Hondo College, expanded our Performing Arts pathway to include TK-8th grade, and enhanced our middle school elective offerings by adding more STEAM and Career Technical Education (CTE) courses.

Serving on the Whittier City School District Board is more than a position to me—it is a calling. I truly believe God has called me to lead and serve this community, and I approach every decision with humility, integrity, and a heart for our students, staff and families.

I would be honored to earn your trust and respectfully ask for your vote so that I may continue serving the Whittier City School District with dedication, compassion, and purpose.
