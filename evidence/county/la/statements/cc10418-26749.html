STATEMENT OF MIGUEL DE LA ROSA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF BELL GARDENS GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Two years ago, you entrusted me with the responsibility of serving Bell Gardens. Since then, we have made meaningful progress together.

We strengthened neighborhood partnerships, and resources that help protect our families. We advanced housing and economic development opportunities while supporting local businesses and planning responsibly for our city's future. We also delivered important investments in parks, recreation, and community facilities, creating spaces where residents of every generation can gather, grow, and thrive.

These achievements did not come without obstacles. The next four years will be critical, and Bell Gardens needs leadership that will protect our progress and keep moving us forward.

At the heart of every priority the goal is to improve the quality of life for every Bell Gardens resident.

We have built strong momentum, and more is yet to come. I respectfully ask for your vote so we can continue flourishing a safer, stronger, and more prosperous Bell Gardens!
