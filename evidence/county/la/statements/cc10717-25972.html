STATEMENT OF CINDY WU

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

MOUNTAIN VIEW SCHOOL DISTRICT

Our Mountain View School District has been taking the District to a new level. We implemented the Bond Program to improve the infrastructure of our schools, brought in free after-school programs for our kids through partnerships with local nonprofits, and are working to improve educational outcomes while ensuring continued accountability for the dollars spent by the District.

As a small businesswoman and educator, I know how important schools are for our community. Our local schools offer hope for our children. Our teachers provide our children the tools they will need to navigate a changing world, and there are many important decisions ahead that will impact our schools.

When the rest of the Board voted to eliminate school counselors, I was the lone vote to save them, because I believe our students deserve the mental health and academic support counselors provide. As someone who bridges the gap between our community and the school district, I will continue to advocate for the needs of our students and families, and make sure Mountain View School District serves their best interests. I am passionate about stronger education and financial literacy in our schools.

I respectfully ask for your vote!
