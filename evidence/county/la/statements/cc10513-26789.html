STATEMENT OF JUANITA MARTIN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF SANTA FE SPRINGS GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Dearest Residents and Friends,

I come before you as a lifelong resident of Santa Fe Springs. I was born and raised here and blessed to call this city home. I have served for the past 17 years, and I look forward to the opportunity to continue to serve the community.

While there is always work to be done in the future in our city, I am proud to have been a part of many milestones within the city. Most recently the renovation of the Aquatic Center, a new police department, and the great upgrades at our neighborhood center for seniors and families.

I hope to continue this journey to improve our financial management plan. I vow to continue to make public safety a priority with the best fire and police services, I will continue to do my part to support the growth within the community and business partners. I commit to continuing to keep the community and its residents the most important reason for my service to this city. I respectfully ask for your support with a vote on November 3,2026.
