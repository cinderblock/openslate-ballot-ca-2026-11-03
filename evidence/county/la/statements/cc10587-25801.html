STATEMENT OF HUY "HENRY" NGUYEN

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

LONG BEACH COMMUNITY COLLEGE DISTRICT

Occupation: School District Administrator

As an experienced education leader, United States Air Force veteran, son of Vietnamese refugees, and Long Beach homeowner, I know education changes lives. I am running for the Long Beach Community College District Board of Trustees, Area 5, to expand opportunities for every student, strengthen workforce training, and protect taxpayers' investments.

For more than 15 years, I have served students and families in the Los Angeles Unified School District. As a School District Administrator, I oversee programs serving thousands of students while managing multi-million-dollar budgets, supervising staff, and ensuring efficient operations. Every day, I make responsible decisions that improve services, maximize resources, and put students first.

I proudly served in the U.S. Air Force as a Staff Sergeant, completing two tours of duty in Turkey and Afghanistan. I went back to school to earn a Bachelor of Science degree from Cal Poly Pomona, a Master's Degree from Bowie State University, and an MBA in Finance from California State University, Long Beach. As an experienced educator, veteran, and Long Beach City College parent, I will bring independent leadership, fiscal accountability, transparency, and educational experience to prepare students for 21st-century careers.

I am honored to have earned endorsements from the LBCC Faculty Association, VoteVets, United Steelworkers, Teamsters, LBCC Board President Uduak-Joe Ntuk, Vice President Dr. Ennette Morton, and many respected neighborhood, small-business, labor, and community leaders.

I respectfully ask for your vote. Learn more at www.votenguyen4lbcc.com
