STATEMENT OF SAMUEL PEREZ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF BELL GARDENS GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

I am Samuel Perez, a lifelong Bell Gardens resident who is proud to have attended our local schools. I earned a bachelor's degree in Accounting from California State University, Los Angeles, College of Business and Economics, after completing a Certificate in Banking and Money.

I currently serve as Vice Chair of the Bell Gardens Traffic and Safety Commission, working to improve public safety and the quality of life in our community. Professionally, I work for the Los Angeles County Office of Education supporting payroll systems for school districts throughout Los Angeles County. My experience also includes accounting and finance with the City of Pico Rivera, serving as an office clerk for the City of Bell Gardens, and administrative work with Long Beach Unified School District.

As a Christian minister, I am committed to serving others with integrity, compassion, and accountability.

If elected to the Bell Gardens City Council, I will work to strengthen public safety, promote transparent and accountable government, support responsible fiscal management, and encourage thoughtful economic growth while preserving the values that make Bell Gardens a great place to live, work, and raise a family.
