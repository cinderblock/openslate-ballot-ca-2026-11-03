STATEMENT OF NANCY K. SMITH

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 1

PALMDALE SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Incumbent

Experience Counts! I have a broad background in education. I have been a teacher, classified Director, Deputy Superintendent of Business Services and Superintendent of Schools. I believe that my expertice in education, especially in fiscal planning and being a School Board Member for the past 13 years allows me to use my expertice in continuing to help provide excellent services to the students and families of our Palmdale School District.

I am an active member in our Antelope Valley communities involved in the various Chamber of Commerce Associations, AV School Boards Association (past President, current 1st Vice-President), Regional Director California School Boards Association and a member of the group working to improve student learning outcomes by realigning the state frameworks.

I believe that Palmdale School District has made great strides in improving our facilitiess and the programs we offer to our students and families. I would love to continue to plan and help implement even more improvements for our community.
