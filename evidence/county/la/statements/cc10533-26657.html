STATEMENT OF JONATHAN COTTRELL

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF WEST HOLLYWOOD GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

When Jonathan Cottrell first visited West Hollywood at 24 years old, he knew he had found his home. Jonathan and his husband Erik own and operate a local sports bar and community gathering spot (GYM Bar Weho) and are raising their newborn son here in the city they love.

West Hollywood is at a real inflection point, with two longtime pioneers leaving the City Council. Jonathan is running to preserve what we love about West Hollywood; address and rethink solutions to the challenges we face; and work to position our city for the future.

Jonathan will work to tackle the affordability crisis affecting all of us: renters, homeowners, small businesses, and workers alike. He believes we grow economic opportunity by attracting high-quality jobs and welcoming creative and green small businesses.

As a business owner himself, Jonathan is both pro-worker and pro-small business, because it's good for our community when everyone has the opportunity to thrive.

Jonathan is pro-housing and will support more affordable homes/apartments that are concentrated on commercial corridors, and transit nodes where the infrastructure can support it. He'll also build on our legacy of strong tenant protections and stabilization policies.

Above all, Jonathan believes public office is a public trust. At a time when confidence in government is at an all-time low across the board, Jonathan believes West Hollywood should do what we always do - raise the bar. This includes: setting clear goals, tracking outcomes, and showing residents the results. Jonathan will advance accountability, starting with himself. He'll keep his own phone off the dais so residents always have his full attention during meetings, and he hopes his colleagues will join him in that commitment. He'll also look at strengthening financial disclosures for our public officials, because residents should never have to wonder or worry about whether out-of-state billionaires are influencing decisions here at home, and they have every right to know who is funding their local government.

Please consider voting for Jonathan Cottrell for one of the three seats on the City Council. To learn more, visit cottrell4weho.com.

Jonathan Cottrell
