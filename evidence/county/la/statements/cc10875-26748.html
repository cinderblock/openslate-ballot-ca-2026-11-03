STATEMENT OF RAYMOND "RAY" JACKSON

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF HERMOSA BEACH GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Councilmember & Former Mayor, City of Hermosa Beach/California Coastal Commissioner/Colonel U.S. Army (Retired)

A lifelong public servant, I proudly wore our nation's uniform for over thirty years, retiring as an Army Colonel. For the past eleven years, I have been equally proud to serve our "Best Little Beach City" by coaching hundreds of our kids, volunteering with our School District and dozens of local organizations, and, for the past five and a half years, serving as your City Councilmember and Mayor, in elected regional leadership roles, and as your state-appointed California Coastal Commissioner.

Serving Hermosa Beach has been the honor of a lifetime. Together, we have accomplished so much, but our most important work still lies ahead.

Our city is at a pivotal moment, and the need for competent, experienced leadership has never been greater. The decisions we make today will shape Hermosa Beach for generations to come. That is why I remain committed to thoughtful leadership grounded in fiscal responsibility, public safety, smart and measured growth, and protecting the character, quality of life, and values that make our community so special.

I would be honored to earn your vote once again and continue serving with integrity, experience, common sense, and action.

Thank you for your trust, your support, and the privilege of serving our community. Together, let's keep Hermosa Beach moving onward and upward.
