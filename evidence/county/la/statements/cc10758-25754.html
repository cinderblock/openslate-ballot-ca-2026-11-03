STATEMENT OF RALPH VELADOR

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 1

PALMDALE SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 57

Occupation: Coordinator

As a proud product of California's public education system and the parent of four children who attended public schools in Palmdale, I have a deep personal commitment to ensuring every student has access to a high-quality education.

I believe every student deserves the opportunity to reach their fullest potential in a school district where parents, teachers, staff, and community members work together toward a common goal: student success. Effective leadership requires more than simply holding public meetings it means actively listening to parents, educators, and stakeholders and ensuring their voices help shape Board decisions.

I am committed to continuing to make the Board more responsive, accountable, and connected to the needs of our community. As a proud Army veteran, former Palmdale Planning Commissioner, and current school board trustee, I have the experience, dedication, and leadership necessary to continue serving our students, families, and community.

I respectfully ask for your support and the opportunity to continue serving as your school board representative.
