STATEMENT OF MIKE MURCHISON

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 3

WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICT OF SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA

Age: 61

Occupation: Water Preservation Advocate

We deserve a future with access to clean, reliable, and affordable drinking water. That's why who represents you on the Water Replenishment District is important. We need a leader with direct experience preserving and protecting Southern California's most valuable natural resource.
I'm a Long Beach native who attended Wilson High School and USC and have since dedicated my career to bringing together communities, public agencies, and environmental stakeholders to solve complex water infrastructure and conservation challenges.
I'll use my experience to:
DELIVER AFFORDABLE WATER:

Protect our groundwater basins, invest wisely in replenishment, and ensure a reliable, affordable water supply for future generations.

PROTECT TAXPAYERS:
Every dollar should be accounted for, spending should be transparent, and infrastructure investments should deliver results.

PLAN FOR TOMORROW:
As we continue to battle record droughts and climate change, we must create a sustainable system that protects our water resources.

I will bring practical problem solving, fiscal responsibility, and an independent perspective. I believe in transparent decision making, careful oversight of public funds, and working collaboratively with local cities, water agencies, businesses, and residents to develop long-term solutions that protect our water resources.

PROTECT OUR WATER. PROTECT OUR FUTURE.

I ask for your vote. Learn more at VoteMikeMurchison.com
