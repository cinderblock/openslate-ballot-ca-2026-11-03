STATEMENT OF LUIS BARRERA CASTANON

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF TRUSTEES,

SANTA MONICA COMMUNITY COLLEGE DISTRICT

Age: 45

Occupation: Founder & CEO

Twenty-five years ago, while walking down Pico Blvd, as an undocumented teenager, I was inspired by a bus ad for Santa Monica College; I enrolled soon after.

That decision changed the trajectory of my life. SMC opened a door that led to a doctorate and over twenty years of service to Los Angeles County nonprofits and communities.

I was appointed to the SMC Board of Trustees in February 2025, since then I have focused on:

Student success. Every student deserves a real path to transfer, a career, or lifelong learning.

Career pathways. Partnerships with future employers so students graduate into good jobs.

Transparency. I've published my board votes online, alongside every trustee's, so residents can see how decisions are made.

Community colleges are where working families, immigrants, veterans, and where people starting over can have a second chance. I have lived that. I commit to keep SMC affordable, accountable, and open to all.

Endorsed by Assemblymember Mike Fong (Chair, Assembly Higher Education Committee), Mayor Caroline Torosis, and SMC Trustees Nancy Greenstein, Rob Rader, and Anastasia Foster.
