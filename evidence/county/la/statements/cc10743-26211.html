STATEMENT OF ALICIA LINDEN ANDERSON

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 4

PARAMOUNT UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Incumbent/Business Owner/Parent

I am a lifelong resident of Paramount, a proud Paramount Pirate, and a dedicated PTA member for more than 20 years. Paramount is my home. My parents were teachers in the district, my children attended Paramount schools, and now my grandchildren are proud PUSD students. Serving our schools has been my family's legacy and my greatest honor.

As your Board of Education representative, I put students first by listening to parents, students, teachers, staff, and community members before making decisions. I believe the best results come through communication, collaboration, and accountability.

Together, we have strengthened academic programs, increased graduation rates, upgraded our schools with air conditioning, new flooring, and water stations, begun the unification of the West Campus and Senior Campus, expanded access to technology, and created more opportunities that prepare every student for college, careers, and life. There is still work to do.

When re-elected, I will continue advocating for safe schools, high-quality education, fiscal responsibility, equitable opportunities, and expanded Career Technical Education, Arts, and Music programs for all students.

Every child deserves the opportunity to learn, grow, and succeed. I respectfully ask for your continued support as we build an even stronger future for all PUSD students.
