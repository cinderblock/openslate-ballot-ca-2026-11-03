STATEMENT OF TINA FUNICIELLO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF RANCHO PALOS VERDES GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 48

Occupation: Attorney

My name is Tina L. Funiciello, Esq. I am honored to be a candidate for the Rancho Palos Verdes City Council.

I am passionate about serving as a City Council member because of my belief in the values our community embraces, like safety, maintaining property values, and preservation of our beautiful land.

I've lived here my entire life and have developed a deep understanding of our community, having actively involved myself in a variety of committees with my children's schools and my church, coordinating numerous events, managing funds, and organizing. I have unique experience and insight into our city's educational needs. This equipped me with the skills needed to contribute to the council effectively.

My career as an attorney gave me practical skills in problem solving, research and communication.

I'm known for my integrity, dedication, and team collaboration. I'm committed to bringing these values to this role. My approach is always to fully research issues, communicate effectively, and bring common-sense solutions.

If elected, my primary goals are:

- Upholding our General Plan;

- Implementation and creation of policy serving our public's interests;

- Keeping RPV in accordance with our founders' original intent - open spaces and under developed.

Thank you for your consideration.
