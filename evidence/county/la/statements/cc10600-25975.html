STATEMENT OF ROSAELVA LOMELI

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 3

RIO HONDO COMMUNITY COLLEGE DISTRICT

As a teacher for more than 29 years and your Rio Hondo Community College Trustee for the past eight years, I have dedicated my career to expanding educational opportunities for students. Many of my students continue their education at Rio Hondo College, giving me firsthand insight into the important role our college plays in changing lives and strengthening our community.

During my service on the Board, Rio Hondo College has remained fiscally strong while making significant investments in its future. I supported the successful passage of Measure RH to modernize our campus, including new Science and Math facilities and student housing. I also supported launching the High Road Training Partnership to expand workforce education and strengthen partnerships with local employers. Together, we have maintained healthy financial reserves while investing responsibly in student success.

My priorities are clear: fiscal responsibility, student success, workforce education, work-based learning, and strong university transfer opportunities. I remain actively engaged throughout our communities, listening to students, employees, and residents to ensure Rio Hondo College continues to meet the needs of those we serve.

I respectfully ask for your vote to continue building opportunity through responsible leadership, educational excellence, and a strong future for Rio Hondo College.
