STATEMENT OF CINDY LARAE HALLMAN

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

SAUGUS UNION SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 68

Occupation: Retired Educator/Administrator

Cindy Hallman believes every child is more than a test score. They have a name, a story, unique strengths, and challenges that deserve to be understood. As a Saugus School District Board Member and lifelong educator, she will work to ensure that every student receives the support, encouragement, and challenge they need to succeed.

Cindy is not running for a political position: she is running to continue the work that has been her passion for 40 years – as a teacher, principal, and university professor – advocating for children and helping educators give every student the opportunity to succeed. She sees the role of board members to include all stakeholders to ensure that student learning is the utmost priority.

Cindy's passion is simple; she wants every child to have the chance to thrive. She wants the stakeholders in the district to see the whole child, support the teachers, challenge the students, and never lose sight of individuals behind every desk.

"I would appreciate your vote to ensure that the educational experience for each student, parent, and educator is the highest priority of the Saugus Union School District."
