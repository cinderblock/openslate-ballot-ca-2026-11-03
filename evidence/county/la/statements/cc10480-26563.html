STATEMENT OF ALEYDA LEMUS

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF MAYWOOD GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 49

Occupation: Wife/Mother/Store Manager

Education and Qualifications: College graduate with a Bachelors Degree in Marketing and Fine Arts at AIU University. As a mother, home owner and active community member, I understand the importance of safety and unity. I volunteer at local schools and support programs that strengthen opportunities for our children. As a businesswoman, I recognize the logistics of managing people and finances.

I'm proud to announce I'm running for Maywood City Council. Our Community deserves better representation and leadership, putting community over profit. Leaders should listen, understand, follow up on their commitments and work alongside residents to address important issues.

As your Council member, I will focus on helping the community thrive, putting my time and effort to keep our city safe and prosperous by supporting our schools as well as improving public safety. Additionally I will invest in parks, libraries and community spaces. I want to promote responsible economic developments that raise home property values. I aim to make programs and services accessible for all residents, especially fixed income families. I will support local businesses and startups to generate job opportunities.

Together, we will build a stronger, safer more prosperous Maywood for all residents. I humbly ask for your support and vote in the upcoming elections. Thank you.

Aleyda Lemus
Candidate for Maywood City Council
