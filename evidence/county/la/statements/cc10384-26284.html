STATEMENT OF VINCENT M. SANTANA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS

LA PUENTE VALLEY COUNTY WATER DISTRICT

For nearly 20 years, La Puente has been my home. I attended school locally and have had the privilege of watching our community grow and develop. I have participated in local food drives, supported small businesses, and believe strongly in giving back to the community that has given me so much.

My career in healthcare has taught me the importance of responsibility, communication, accountability, and putting people first. I have worked as a Patient Scheduler and currently serve as a Radiology Assistant. Every day, I work with patients and healthcare professionals in an environment where attention to detail and responsible decision-making matter.

As a member of the La Puente Valley County Water District Board of Directors, my priorities would be ensuring our community has access to safe, reliable, high-quality water, reducing unnecessary and wasteful spending, and making responsible use of ratepayer funds.

I believe public service means listening to the community, asking difficult questions, and making decisions based on what best serves the people we represent.

I would be honored to serve the community I have called home for nearly two decades, and I respectfully ask for your vote.
