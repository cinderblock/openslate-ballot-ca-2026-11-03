STATEMENT OF RICHARD LEGASPI

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF NORWALK GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

RICHARD "RICH" LeGASPI

I have called Norwalk home for 40 years, since buying my first house at age 22. My wife Natalie and I have raised our two children, Christina and Joseph, here. We now enjoy watching our grandson grow up in our community, our home.

Following the example set by my parents, I have spent my life giving back to Norwalk. I served on the Norwalk-La Mirada School Board from 1999 to 2008, including as President, where I helped raise graduation standards and expand educational opportunities. I currently chair the Public Safety Commission and serve on the Social Service Commission. I am a past President of the Norwalk Chamber of Commerce and currently serve on the SELACO Workforce Development Board, connecting residents to good jobs.

As an Area Manager, I am responsible for a multi-million-dollar sales budget, working directly with city officials, public safety leaders, and community organizations.

If elected, I will prioritize public safety, smart development that keeps Norwalk families here, real support for local businesses and jobs, and responsible spending of your tax dollars.

Please vote Richard LeGaspi for Norwalk City Council.
