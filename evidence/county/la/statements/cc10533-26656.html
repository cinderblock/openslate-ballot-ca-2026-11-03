STATEMENT OF KODY CHRISTIANSEN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF WEST HOLLYWOOD GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

West Hollywood has been one of the defining places of my life. For more than twenty years, I have experienced this community through seasons of challenge, growth, and opportunity. Today, I am running for City Council because I believe in giving back to the city that has given me so much.

My journey has not been ordinary. I have experienced homelessness, battled addiction, and rebuilt my life one day at a time. Today, after more than eleven years of sobriety, I know that lasting change is possible. I know the dignity that comes from being seen, supported, and given the opportunity to rebuild in a community that believes people are more than the hardest chapter of their lives. I also know the importance of taking responsibility for the life you build next. Those experiences continue to shape how I listen, how I lead, and how I approach public service.

Like many of my neighbors, I rent an apartment, work in our local small business community, and experience the everyday realities of life in West Hollywood. I have spent years listening to seniors, longtime residents, local business owners, and neighbors from every walk of life. Those conversations have taught me that the best government listens first, plans thoughtfully, and earns the public's trust.

I am a retail sales associate, award-winning author, and actor. I earned degrees from New York University, Harvard University, and the University of Cambridge, and I am currently completing my Master of Public Administration at the USC Sol Price School of Public Policy. My education taught me how government works. My life taught me who government should work for.

As your Councilmember, I will work to strengthen public safety, support housing that is affordable while planning responsibly for our future, help local businesses thrive, address homelessness with compassion, accountability, real solutions, and the perspective of lived experience, and ensure City Hall is transparent, fiscally responsible, and responsive to the people it serves.

I believe our city is strongest when every resident feels heard, every neighborhood has a voice, and every decision is guided by transparency, respect, and a commitment to working together.

Together, we can build a West Hollywood that honors its past, embraces its present, and prepares responsibly for its future. It would be the honor of my life to earn your trust and your vote.
