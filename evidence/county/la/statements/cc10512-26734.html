STATEMENT OF SHELLEY BOYLE

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF SAN MARINO GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 55

Occupation: Non-Profit Executive Director

Born and raised in San Marino, I am honored to be a candidate for City Council in the community that has shaped my life. Growing up here gave me a deep appreciation for the values, traditions, and sense of community that make San Marino such a special place.

For the past eight years, I have had the privilege of serving on the San Marino Planning Commission, working to thoughtfully balance growth with preserving the character of our city. I am committed to protecting our historic homes, preserving our urban forest, supporting responsible economic development, and ensuring that future decisions reflect the priorities of our residents.

As our community evolves, we must embrace opportunities that strengthen San Marino while safeguarding the qualities that define it. I believe we can honor our history, invest wisely in our future, and maintain the exceptional services and sense of community that residents expect. I would be grateful for your support and the opportunity to serve San Marino as a member of the City Council.
