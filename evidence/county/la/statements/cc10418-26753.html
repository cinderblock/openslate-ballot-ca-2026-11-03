STATEMENT OF FRANCIS DE LEON SANCHEZ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF BELL GARDENS GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 42

Occupation: Administrator

Bell Gardens is the community that raised me, shaped my values, and inspired my commitment to public service. Serving you on the City Council has been one of the greatest honors of my life.

Since being elected in 2022, I have worked to bring responsive, transparent, and community-centered leadership to City Hall. I have advocated for safer neighborhoods, responsible use of taxpayer dollars, improved streets and public spaces, strong programs for youth and seniors, and opportunities that bring families together and strengthen community pride.

As an educator, school administrator, and Doctor of Educational Leadership, I have dedicated my career to empowering others and creating pathways to success. I bring that same heart, discipline, and accountability to every decision I make as your councilwoman.

Together, we have made meaningful progress, but our work is not finished. I will continue fighting for public safety, financial stability, economic growth, quality parks and services, and a city government that listens to residents and puts their needs first.

I am proud of how far Bell Gardens has come and hopeful about where we are going. I respectfully ask for your vote so we can continue building a safer, stronger, and more prosperous Bell Gardens together.
