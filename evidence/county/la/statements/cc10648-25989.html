STATEMENT OF NANCY LOERA

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 2

HACIENDA LA PUENTE UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 52

Occupation: Teacher

Nancy Loera has nearly 25 years of experience as a teacher and serves as the Hacienda La Puente USD Trustee for Area 2.

As a parent whose children attended HLPUSD schools, Nancy understands the importance of providing every student with a high-quality education.

Nancy is focused on keeping reading, writing, and mathematics at the center of every decision so students are prepared for college, careers, and life.

Working alongside educators, staff, and the community, Nancy:

Advocated for stronger reading and mathematics programs at Lassalette Elementary, where students are thriving.

Supported the Dual Language Program at Nelson Elementary.

Expanded music funding for La Puente HS.

Supported arts and enrichment at California Elementary and Sparks Middle through music, Youth Cinema Project, District Musical, and Mariachi.

Supported educational opportunities requested by the community: Aviation, Drones, Robotics, C-STEM, Speech and Debate, and Conga Kids.

Believes every student deserves a safe, supportive learning environment.

Supported difficult financial decisions that helped keep HLPUSD financially stable while protecting educational opportunities for students.

Nancy believes taxpayer dollars should be spent responsibly, focused on students, and educational opportunities. She respectfully asks for your vote to continue delivering responsible leadership, putting students first, and expanding opportunities for all students.
