STATEMENT OF LESLIE H. GARCIA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
2nd District

CITY OF WEST COVINA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 27

Occupation: Atmospheric Scientist

I'm Leslie Garcia, a lifelong West Covina resident and Atmospheric Scientist running to represent District 2 on our City Council. This City raised me. From volunteering at the West Covina library to founding the Best Buddies chapter at Edgewood High School, I have been serving my community since childhood. These experiences showed me that public service means uplifting people, expanding opportunity, and ensuring everyone has a voice.

At City Hall, I will protect residents from federal immigration enforcement by ending the city's contract with Flock Safety, I'll establish clear procedures for city employees, and connect families with legal and emergency aid.

We can make West Covina more affordable by opposing working - class fees like street sweeping tickets, advancing tenant protections and rent stabilization, and partnering with other levels of government to fund public services without bankrupting the city.

I will propose a ban against data centers that threaten our valuable resources, and quality of life. I will ensure small businesses survive rising costs and economic disruption. I will streamline city processes, revitalize dead spaces, and oppose deals that shift costs onto small businesses.

West Covina's District 2 deserves leadership grounded in science, integrity, and community, I would be honored to earn your vote.
