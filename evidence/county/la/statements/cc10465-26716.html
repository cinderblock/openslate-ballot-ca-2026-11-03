STATEMENT OF CHERYL SHAW WILLIAMS

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF EDUCATION,
Trustee Area 1

INGLEWOOD UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

As a lifelong resident of Inglewood and a proud product of the Inglewood Unified School District, I understand firsthand the importance of a strong public education. I attended Woodworth Elementary, Monroe Junior High, and graduated from Morningside High School before earning a Bachelor of Science in Sociology with a minor in Behavioral Science from California State University, Dominguez Hills.

For more than 15 years, I have dedicated myself to supporting Inglewood students through scholarships, educational programs, and community service. As a Founding Board Member of the Morningside Monarchs Alumni Association, I have helped provide scholarships, recognize student achievement, and expand educational opportunities. I also serve as Speech Chairperson for the Inglewood Black History Celebration, District 4 Planning Commissioner, and Scholarship Board Member for the Century Heights Neighborhood Watch Association.

I am invested in our public schools because I am invested in our families and community. As your School Board Member, I will advocate for fiscal responsibility, academic excellence, safe learning environments, and opportunities that prepare every student for college, careers, and lifelong success.

Together, we can strengthen our schools and build a brighter future for every child.
