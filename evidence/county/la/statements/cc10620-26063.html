STATEMENT OF LAURA A. FRANKLIN

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

BALDWIN PARK UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

My commitment has been to serve children, families, and the Baldwin Park community with compassion, integrity, and accountability. As a second-generation Baldwin Park resident, a parent and now a grandparent, I understand the importance of strong public schools and the lasting impact that caring educators, mentors, and community leaders have on a child's future.

For more than 30 years, I have dedicated my life to mentoring children, youth, and young adults through education, coaching, faith-based leadership, and community service. I have worked alongside students from diverse backgrounds, helping them build confidence, develop life skills, overcome challenges, and recognize their potential. I believe every student deserves to feel seen, valued, supported, and prepared for success.

As a Baldwin Park Housing Commissioner, Chair of the Baldwin Park's Oversight Committee, Board Trustee, nonprofit leader, mother of veterans, and two-time breast cancer survivor, I have learned that resilience, collaboration, and responsible leadership create stronger communities. I am committed to advocating for academic excellence, student wellness, safe learning environments, fiscal responsibility, and meaningful 1 partnerships between schools, families, and the community.

Together, we can ensure every student has the opportunity to learn, thrive, and achieve their dreams while building a stronger future for Baldwin Park.
