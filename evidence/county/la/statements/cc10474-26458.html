STATEMENT OF FRANCISCO M. TALAVERA

CANDIDATE FOR MAYOR

CITY OF LAWNDALE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 65

Occupation: Councilmember, City of Lawndale

Serving as your City Councilmember has been one of the greatest honors of my life, and I respectfully ask for your vote to continue serving as your Mayor.

As a lifelong Lawndale resident, my wife, Mabel, and I have been married for 31 years and raised our three children here. My family has lived in Lawndale since 1958, when my grandfather bought the house where I was raised.

For more than 30 years, I served our community as an educator in the South Bay and in Los Angeles before being elected to the City Council. I am committed to ensuring Lawndale's continued financial stability, supporting public safety, improving infrastructure, and encouraging responsible economic development.

As Mayor, I will continue to provide honest, accessible, and fiscally responsible leadership focused on safe streets and neighborhoods, youth and senior services, and greater economic opportunity.

I believe in public service, a stronger community, and a brighter future. Together, we can build One Lawndale–Our Community, Our Future by listening to residents, working collaboratively, and making thoughtful decisions that strengthen our city for generations to come.

It would be an honor to earn your trust and your vote. Honored to Serve. Committed to You.
