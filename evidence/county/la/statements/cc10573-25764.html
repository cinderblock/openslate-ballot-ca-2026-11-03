STATEMENT OF BARBARA GAINES

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

ANTELOPE VALLEY COMMUNITY COLLEGE DISTRICT

Barbara Gaines, current AVC Board of Trustee

For the past 13 years, I have proudly served on the Antelope Valley College Board of Trustees, building on 31 years in education and a lifelong commitment to student success. I believe effective trustees lead by listening, earning trust, making responsible decisions, and always putting students and our community first.

Together, we have achieved remarkable results. We secured a $350 million bond in 2016 to modernize our campus, expanded career pathways in aerospace, nursing, automotive, fire technology, and welding, and celebrated more than 4,000 graduates and certificate earners in 2026. Every achievement creates stable families, a stronger workforce, and a thriving Antelope Valley.

While I am proud of what we have accomplished, our best days are still ahead. I remain committed to fiscal responsibility, transparency, innovation, and ensuring every student has access to an affordable, high-quality education.

I respectfully ask for your vote and trust. The best investment is in our students. When students succeed, our entire community succeeds. Together, let's keep expanding opportunities cementing a bright future within everyone's reach.

With your support, we will continue building pathways, empowering students, strengthening partnerships, and creating viable options for generations across the Antelope Valley.
