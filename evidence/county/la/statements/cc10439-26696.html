STATEMENT OF THOMAS AUJERO SMALL

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF CULVER CITY GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 67

Occupation: Urban Development Strategist

Great cities don't happen by accident they're built through thoughtful, integrated planning. That conviction guided my career and my service as Culver City Councilmember and Mayor.

My experience has taught me that housing, transportation, economic development, infrastructure, and quality of life are not separate challenges they're interconnected. When cities plan them together, they become more affordable, prosperous, and resilient for every generation.

As Mayor, I led the City's General Plan Update, championed the Ballona Creek Bikeway Extension, strengthened the city's finances, expanded housing opportunities, and secured millions of dollars in outside funding for local priorities.

My approach is to anticipate change, integrate solutions across issues, and measure success by results residents feel: safer streets, more housing choices, stronger neighborhoods and a government that plans instead of reacting to crises.

Effective government depends on understanding how every decision affects the whole city. I believe Culver City deserves leaders who connect the dots linking housing to transportation, infrastructure to economic opportunity, and planning to everyday outcomes.

That is the experience and proven leadership I bring to Culver City: integrated thinking, disciplined execution, and results that improve everyday life for every neighborhood.
