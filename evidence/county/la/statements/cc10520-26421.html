STATEMENT OF JOSHUA BARRON

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF SOUTH GATE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 44

Occupation: Council Member/Financial Crimes Investigator

My name is Joshua Barron. I am a lifelong South Gate resident and attended Stanford Elementary, South Gate Middle School, South Gate High School, ELAC, and Cerritos College. My wife Elizabeth and I have raised our three daughters in the community we call home.

I serve as Mayor and have completed four years on the South Gate City Council, including three years as City Auditor. I continue working as a Financial Crimes Investigator for a bank in Los Angeles.

On the City Council, I have addressed residents' concerns, worked to protect essential services, developed responsible solutions to our fiscal challenges, and advocated for property tax reform. I created programs promoting local businesses and worked on regional mobility and transit solutions. I advocated for resources that helped bring Urban Orchard, Hollydale Community Park, and Circle Park to our city and championed youth sports.

As Charter President of SELA Kiwanis, I supported youth leadership, awarded college scholarships, organized food assistance during the Palisades fires and immigration enforcement actions, and led neighborhood cleanups.

In another term, I will strengthen public safety, improve infrastructure, restore financial stability, support local businesses, and expand opportunities for our youth and families.

I respectfully ask for your vote.
