STATEMENT OF NORMA NATALIE RODARTE

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 3

WHITTIER CITY SCHOOL DISTRICT

I have proudly served the Whittier City School District for 33 years, serving alongside educators, staff, students and families.

My five children attended Whittier City Schools, and now my grandchildren as well.

Most important, is my experience of serving with dedicated professionals educating our students. I believe every decision made by our District Board should be guided by what is best for our students. And that includes following our state education guidelines and not allowing inappropriate outside influences interfere with their education.

I will strive to put student interests first, by supporting academic excellence, safe and welcoming schools, responsible fiscal management, and transparent leadership.

- My goals are to ensure that teachers and staff have the resources they need to help children reach their full potential
- That Special Needs students are a priority and treated as such.
- Schools are safe academic environments

I will bring integrity, experience, accountability, and a strong work ethic to our Board of Trustees.

That's why I have been endorsed by WETA - Whittier Elementary Teachers Association; and CSEA -California School Employees Association; as well as numerous parents, trustees, and community leaders. Please support NORMA RODARTE FOR WHITTIER CITY SCHOOL DISTRICT. Thank you for your consideration!
