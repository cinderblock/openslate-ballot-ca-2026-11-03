STATEMENT OF MARIA SANTAY

CANDIDATE FOR CITY TREASURER

CITY OF EL MONTE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 27

Occupation: Licensed Insurance Agent

As a proud lifelong resident of El Monte, I can say El Monte is home for many of us. As a proud daughter of immigrants, mother of 2, and neighbor, I, Maria Santay, understand the significance of hard work and securing stability for our families.

For the past decade, I have been dedicated to serving others across the service industry. Today, as a benefits advisor and licensed insurance agent, I get to work with private and public employees, helping them protect and secure their families, financially. These past 4 years have built and strengthened my ability to serve with responsibility and earn the trust of those who I serve.

I am running for El Monte City Treasurer because I believe that our El Monte residents deserve transparent, responsible, and accountable leadership. Every El Monte resident should be confident that their hard earned, tax payer dollars are being responsibly managed and properly allocated to our city's tomorrow.

My goal is to serve the community where I was raised; with honesty and transparency while ensuring public funds are being properly allocated. This is our chance to build a stronger, more financially secure and transparent El Monte where we can continue to grow.
