STATEMENT OF DAVID CHURA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF RANCHO PALOS VERDES GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Technology CEO

Vote for Experience and Proven Leadership.

My family has proudly called Rancho Palos Verdes home for 27 years. Our daughters were educated here, and our church community is here.

Experience. I have served six years on the Planning Commission, including two as Chairman, served on key subcommittees, led traffic safety initiatives, and introduced technology to address landslide challenges. The City Council has honored me for "inspired innovation in a time of crisis."

Leadership. As a technology executive and entrepreneur, I have built successful startup companies, run diverse organizations and managed large budgets. I accept responsibility, value accountability, and deliver results.

Public service must also produce measurable results:

Fiscal Responsibility: Landslide costs continue to consume nearly 50% of the City's budget, leaving a backlog of critical programs unfunded. It is far from over. I will reduce costs through innovation, restore city services, and maximize state and federal funding.

Public Safety: I will strengthen our partnership with the Sheriff and continue improving traffic safety.

Community Character: I will defend our city's control over land use and resist state overreach. I respect our neighborhood compatibility, view preservation, and privacy ordinances.

Infrastructure: I will prioritize road safety, improve drainage, and expand wireless communications.
