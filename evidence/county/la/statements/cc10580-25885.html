STATEMENT OF PETER K. PARKER

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 4

COMPTON COMMUNITY COLLEGE DISTRICT

Age: 63

Occupation: Retired College Professor

Peter K. Parker, 63, is a candidate for Compton Community College District Board of Governors, Area 4. A proud product of California's community colleges, Peter earned degrees in liberal arts, human services, comparative literature, and English, then served eighteen years as a full-time English professor at Orange Coast College.

At OCC, Peter served as academic senate president, led semester abroad programs in Paris, and helped build a web of collaboration across campus. He understands shared governance, curriculum, student services, and the real challenges students face.

Peter's life also includes accountability and recovery. Years ago, after experiencing severe legal consequences for bad choices made, he had to rebuild his life one honest choice at a time, one day at a time. Today, with years in recovery, he knows community college can be the facility for second chances, where people rebuild their lives, discover purpose, and turn adversity into opportunity and opportunity into service.

Peter is running to put students first: strengthening instruction and services, supporting responsible innovation, expanding opportunity, and helping students complete their goals. With great responsibility comes the duty to listen, act with integrity, and serve the community. Peter will do these things, and he asks for your vote on November 3.
