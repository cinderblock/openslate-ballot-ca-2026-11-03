STATEMENT OF MATTHEW BRACH

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF RANCHO PALOS VERDES GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 55

Occupation: Small Business Owner

When my time on the RPV Planning Commission ended, I figured I was done with public service. I was looking forward to being a resident again.

That lasted about a month.

RPV has real problems ahead. The landslide is one. Wildfire risk is another. Then there are roads, burglary, development, and rules coming out of Sacramento.

I served as president of the PVPUSD Board of Education, president of SoCal ROC, and vice chair of the RPV Planning Commission. I know budgets, staff reports, public hearings, and the need to weigh both sides before making a decision.

My approach is simple. I listen first. I read the material and ask questions. Then I make the decision and own it.

I want RPV to stay RPV. That means protecting neighborhood character. Local decisions should stay local whenever they can. My record reflects that. I have supported quick local action when it was needed. I also pushed back when state rules stopped making sense for our community.

I am running because experience matters here. The job is difficult. It is not new to me. I know the work. I know RPV. I would be honored to serve on the City Council.
