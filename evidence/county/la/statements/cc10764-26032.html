STATEMENT OF RENITA ARMSTRONG

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 2

BELLFLOWER UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

I'm Renita Armstrong, running for re-election to the Bellflower Unified School District Board of Education to continue advocating for our students. As a 25-year Bellflower resident, mother of five Bellflower High graduates, and Early Childhood Special Education Specialist with over 30 years of experience, my dedication to our district is both personal and professional.

Unprecedented challenges have required courageous, innovative governance. Every decision I make prioritizes what is best for our entire District, ensuring our students are fully prepared for college and career pathways. Looking ahead, we must work collaboratively alongside our new Superintendent to make the tough decisions needed to improve educational outcomes.

Nobody knows children better than parents, and nobody works harder for them than teachers. Real progress happens when educators and families have a seat at the table. I will continue prioritizing partnership over politics—bringing parents, teachers, administrators, and community members together through mutual respect rather than conflict.

I ask for your support to remain your advocate on the governance team.

Vote Renita Armstrong for Bellflower Unified School District Board of Education.
