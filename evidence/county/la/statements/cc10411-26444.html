STATEMENT OF SHARON KWAN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
2nd District

CITY OF ARCADIA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Incumbent

Serving the people of Arcadia has been one of the greatest honors of my life. I am truly grateful for the trust you have placed in me, and I respectfully ask for your support for another term on the City Council.

Since being elected, I have worked hard to listen to residents, protect our neighborhoods, support public safety, spend taxpayer money wisely, and keep Arcadia a wonderful place to live, work, and raise a family.

I believe city government should be honest, open, and always put residents first. I will continue to work hard, make thoughtful decisions, and stand up for what is best for our community.

Arcadia has given so much to my family, and I will never take the opportunity to serve this city for granted. I would be honored to earn your trust once again and continue working every day to keep Arcadia safe, strong, and welcoming for everyone.

Thank you for your trust, your support, and your vote.

Sincerely,

Sharon Kwan
