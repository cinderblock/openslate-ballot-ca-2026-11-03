STATEMENT OF SUE HIMMELRICH

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE RENT CONTROL BOARD

CITY OF SANTA MONICA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 73

Occupation: Attorney

As a tenants' rights attorney and eight-year Santa Monica City Councilmember (including two years as Mayor), I have spent nearly twenty years fighting to keep Santa Monica renters securely and affordably in their homes.

At Western Center on Law & Poverty, I represent tenants facing displacement. On the City Council and Planning Commission, I championed lowering the cap on annual rent increases to 3% for rent controlled tenants, protecting Section 8 renters from discrimination, preventing rental units from being converted into short-term rentals like Airbnb, and shielding teachers and students from mid-year evictions. I was a proponent and funder of Measures GS-GSH (2016) and GS (2022), which together have generated more than $60 million to date for financial and legal assistance to at-risk Santa Monica renters, and for affordable housing creation.

I want to serve on the Rent Control Board to continue defending our renter neighbors against displacement, and our rent-controlled housing stock against redevelopment into luxury housing. I believe tenants should never be displaced from their homes absent a genuine safety necessity.

I do not accept, and will not accept, contributions from corporations, developers, or landlords.

I ask for your vote.
