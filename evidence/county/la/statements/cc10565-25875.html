STATEMENT OF BILL COOPER

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 1

SANTA CLARITA VALLEY WATER AGENCY

Occupation: Santa Clarita Valley Water Agency Board Vice President

BILL COOPER has worked his entire professional career in water resources managing the operations of all five Metropolitan Water treatment plants serving water to 19 million people in Southern California. Managing large water treatment and distribution facilities gives him the experience needed to protect the quality and availability of our local water supplies.

The reliable, safe and affordable supply of water to the Santa Clarita Valley is essential for our community and the quality of life we enjoy.

BILL COOPER will continue to strive for responsible water management by:

Maximizing Existing Supplies through recycling and conservation;

Requiring New Development to have an identifiable supply of water before building;

Promoting Underground Banking Storage Programs to provide water during drought years;

Removing Contaminants threatening our groundwater and requiring polluters to pay for cleanup;

Seeking Federal and State funding to reduce local project costs;

Maintaining Low Water Rates through strict budget oversight.

BILL COOPER will continue to work hard to ensure that we always have a reliable supply of high quality water at a reasonable cost and to protect our valley from future drought through responsible planning and management.
