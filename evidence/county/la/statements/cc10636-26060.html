STATEMENT OF STEPHANIE LOREDO

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

CULVER CITY UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

I am committed to ensuring every student receives a high-quality public education in an inclusive school environment. As CCUSD's board president, I lead with collaboration, transparency, and fiscal responsibility to strengthen our schools and expand opportunities for every student. Stewarding over $300 million in annual investments throughout my professional career, I have the experience to safeguard our district's financial health. I will continue building trust in our district through open communication, accountability, and engagement with the community.

BACKGROUND
- Culver City Unified School District Board President
- Vice-Chair Membership/Outreach, Filipino American Democratic Caucus of California Democratic Party
- Media and Marketing Executive

ACCOMPLISHMENTS
- Advanced Culver City's reputation as a destination district
- Expanded student wellness and learning opportunities
- Renewed funding to protect teacher salaries
- Improved the district's long-term fiscal stability
- Strengthened partnerships with Culver City government

COMMITMENTS
- Achievement: empower students to thrive through equity, support, and opportunity
- Belonging: ensure safe, inclusive schools where every student is respected and supported
- Community: build sustainable environments where students, educators, and families can thrive
