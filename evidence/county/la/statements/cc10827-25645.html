STATEMENT OF DAVID ROSS

CANDIDATE FOR JUDGE OF THE SUPERIOR COURT
Office No. 131

I have devoted my professional life to public service and strengthening confidence in our justice system. As a Deputy Public Defender for 31 years, I have litigated 113 jury trials, 2000+ preliminary hearings, and represented l0,000 people, ensuring every individual is treated with dignity, respect, and fairness. Few candidates bring experience in court, the newsroom, and in Congress. Before becoming an attorney, I was a TV reporter and documentary producer, telling stories of communities whose voices were unheard. I served on Capitol Hill, where I learned how government affects working families and the communities our courts serve. These experiences gave me a broad perspective on the law, government, and people who depend on both. Justice requires legal knowledge, integrity, compassion, and the ability to listen carefully before making decisions. I have worked with judges, prosecutors, victims, witnesses, and thousands of ordinary Angelenos, giving me a well-rounded understanding of the challenges facing our courts. I bring a lifetime of public service, extensive courtroom experience, and proven commitment to fairness, accountability, and equal justice to the Los Angeles Superior Court. I will uphold the Constitution, apply the law impartially, and work every day to earn and maintain the public's trust.
