STATEMENT OF SAMUEL MISTRANO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF TRUSTEES,
Trustee Area No. 3

ALTADENA LIBRARY DISTRICT

Age: 58

Occupation: Clinical Assoc. Professor of S.W.

Hi. I raised my daughter for 19 years in Altadena – she went to Aveson, Pasadena High, and CSArts – and we have been grateful library users. We live within walking distance of the main library, and visited several times a week for years. Now that she is at Cal State, I am excited to ask for your vote as Trustee so I can be as helpful to our libraries as they are for us.

I work as an associate professor of social work, and believe in the community-first purposes of our libraries. Whether for doing research, reading, listening to music, watching DVDs, children's groups, using the on-line computers, visiting for cultural events, getting our passports, notarizing post-fire SCE documents, even as a place for air conditioning, our libraries are an incredibly helpful public resource in our lives. As an academic, I am committed to keeping our libraries open, expansive, diverse and useful!

Our main library is, unfortunately, closed for needed renovations. These will improve the branch, but the process needs strong oversight. I am a former senior project manager for LAUSD's facilities division, and my experience is another reason I can be a helpful Trustee.

Thank you for reading this!
