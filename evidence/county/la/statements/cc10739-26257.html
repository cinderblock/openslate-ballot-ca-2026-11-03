STATEMENT OF MARYBEL MICAELA LORENZO

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 2

NORWALK-LA MIRADA UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Good morning,

Hi I am a single mother, a small businesswoman, and your Norwalk neighbor. I am the daughter of immigrants who worked in the garment industry and taught me that education opens every door. I earned my associate degree at a community college and my bachelor's in sociology at a Cal State. Today I am a small business owner, and I volunteer to provide free cosmetology services for our community.

Our board makes decisions that shape our children's lives, and too often families learn about them after the vote. I will insist that parents be told first, that costs be published plainly, and that no major decision moves without true public discussion. More financial transparency and more rubber stamps.

As a past student of speech-language pathology I believe special needs children deserve real support, not waiting lists. I will fight for the staffing and services our families were promised. I will work to make class sizes small, because a teacher who knows your child can change your child's life.

I am a working mother who reads the budget, asks hard questions, and answers to you.

I ask for your vote to help me make our schools what our kids deserve.
