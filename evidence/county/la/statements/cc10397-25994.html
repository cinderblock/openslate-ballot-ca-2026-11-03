STATEMENT OF LENET PACHECO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS

VALLEY COUNTY WATER DISTRICT

I respectfully ask for your vote to continue serving on the Valley County Water District Board of Directors.

Since first being elected in 2009, I have worked to ensure our community receives safe, reliable, high-quality, and affordable drinking water while protecting the District's long-term financial stability.

As your Director, I have supported responsible budgeting, transparent governance, infrastructure improvements, emergency preparedness, and long-range planning. My decisions have always been guided by fiscal responsibility, accountability, and the best interests of our ratepayers.

California's water systems face increasing challenges, including aging infrastructure, water supply reliability, rising costs, cybersecurity, and changing regulations. These issues require experienced leadership, careful planning, and cooperation with local and regional partners to protect our community's water future.

As a small business owner and longtime community volunteer, I understand the importance of responsible financial management, public accountability, and responsive customer service. My experience has reinforced the value of listening to residents, making informed decisions, and planning for future generations.

As your representative, I will continue working to maintain reliable water service, protect the District's financial strength, invest responsibly in our infrastructure, and serve every customer with integrity, experience, and dedication.

I would be honored to earn your vote.
