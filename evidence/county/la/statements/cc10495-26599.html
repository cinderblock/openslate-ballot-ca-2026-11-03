STATEMENT OF JEREMIAH FARRELL

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
3rd District

CITY OF PALMDALE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 39

Occupation: Teacher/Land Surveyor

Did you know the Palmdale City Council gave us the highest sales tax of any city in California and the second highest in all of America?
Did you know they took away your right to vote for mayor?
Did you know they just approved spending millions of your tax dollars, unnecessarily, to a business outside of Palmdale?

Help me stop these irresponsible decisions. I stand for:

Faith: Bringing Jesus Christ-centered values back to Palmdale.
Freedom: Stopping government overreach and protecting your rights.
Family: Helping our families get healthy and strong again.
Fiscal Responsibility: Lowering taxes, cutting waste, and stopping the flow of local dollars to people and businesses in other cities.

My family and I have lived here for most of our lives. Palmdale is our home. We need better decisions being made. Help me to bring common sense back to our city leadership.
