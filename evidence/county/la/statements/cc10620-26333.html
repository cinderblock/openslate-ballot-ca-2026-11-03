STATEMENT OF CHRISTINA LUCERO

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

BALDWIN PARK UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Having been raised in Baldwin Park, I have always believed and still do that education is the key to giving our children the best possible life. This is why experience matters.

Experience will keep our budget in control... Small decisions can hurt our students.

Experience will see that we need to plan for the future and if there are fewer students in our district we still need to provide quality education.

Experience means that we do not have all the answers and must listen to parents, employees, students and the community because as the world and our lives change we must also change.

And most of all having experience has taught me that we need to go back to some basics. But in our world today they also need skills that we did not even dream of.

We need to be sure that they become whole, intelligent, well educated adults.
