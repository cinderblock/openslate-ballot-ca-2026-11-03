STATEMENT OF JOHNNY HERNANDEZ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF SANTA FE SPRINGS GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Retiree

My name is Johnny Hernandez, and I am proud to be a candidate for Santa Fe Springs City Council.

I was raised in Santa Fe Springs and have proudly called this city home for 65 years. It is where I raised my children, now enjoy my grandchildren, attended local schools, and participated in our city's recreation programs.

My commitment to public service began early on. I served as ASB President at Pioneer High School and later earning my AA at Rio Hondo College and continued my education at California State University, Fullerton.

In 1981, I began my career with the City of Santa Fe Springs, serving our community for 33 years in Recreation and Public Works, retiring in 2015 while continuing to contribute as Planning Commissioner.

My dedication to Santa Fe Springs continues today through volunteer work supporting families in need and fostering children alongside community leaders. I was honored to receive the City's Innovation Award for my services.

I am committed to protecting our city's values, supporting public safety, strengthening our community, and ensuring Santa Fe Springs remains a great place to live, work, and raise a family.

Johnny Hernandez
