STATEMENT OF C.J. COHICK

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 2

GLENDORA UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 38

Occupation: Product Manager

I'm a Glendora dad. My wife and I chose this town in 2017 for what everyone can feel here: neighbors who know each other, families at the center of life, kids growing up with real roots. Our schools should form young people who can carry that forward. Kids of character, ready for whatever comes next.

I've spent my career as a product manager working with artificial intelligence. I know what's coming for the world our kids will inherit, and it makes character matter more, not less. Strong kids can master any new tool. No tool can substitute for purpose, honor, and strength.

Glendora Unified faces declining enrollment and real budget deficits. I'll bring professional discipline to those numbers, protect what makes our schools worth choosing, and work to bring more Glendora families back into partnership with their district.

I believe Glendora families decide what's right for their own children, and that a trustee's job is stewardship: listen to parents, back our teachers, and keep decisions here at home.

I would be honored to earn your vote.
