STATEMENT OF PAUL VIDAL

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

PALOS VERDES PENINSULA UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 49

Occupation: CEO of Healthcare Company

Our schools are why families choose Palos Verdes - keeping them excellent takes more than good intentions. As budgets tighten across California, it takes board members with real CEO experience running complex budgets, who want every student in every school getting the same great education, and who treat the role as service, not politics.

I've spent my career being accountable for results and serving. As CEO of a healthcare company, matching complex budgets to the people they serve is my daily job. As a Naval Academy graduate, I served as a carrier-based Navy pilot in wartime, and hold an MBA from Harvard Business School.

More importantly, my wife and I chose this community for its schools and have been PVPUSD parents for over a decade. Our son attends high school here; I know firsthand what our schools do well and where they must do better.

On the board, I will protect academic rigor, keep every student safe and welcome, execute transparent budgets, support our teachers, and keep politics out of our classrooms. Whether or not your children fill them today, great public schools are the promise Palos Verdes keeps to every child. I would be honored to earn your vote.
