STATEMENT OF NEWTON CHELETTE

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 2

ANTELOPE VALLEY COMMUNITY COLLEGE DISTRICT

Newton Chelette has helped students learn, compete, lead, and succeed. A retired Antelope Valley College Dean, Vice President of Student Services, Athletic Director, instructor, and coach, Newton Chelette understands education from the classroom, campus administration, and the sidelines.

At Antelope Valley College, he served as a PE instructor, men's basketball coach, golf coach, Athletic Director, Dean of Kinesiology, Dean of Visual and Performing Arts, and Vice President of Student Services. In 46 coaching years, Newton Chelette helped generations of young people develop confidence, discipline, teamwork, and belief in their potential.

His record reflects leadership and results. He is the winningest men's basketball coach in AVC history, a seven-time Foothill Conference Coach of the Year, a California Community College Basketball Coaches Hall of Fame inductee, a Community College Athletic Directors Hall of Fame inductee, a 2024 recipient of the California Commissioners Award, and the first person from Antelope Valley College inducted into the California Community College Hall of Fame.

Newton Chelette continues serving the Antelope Valley through education, athletics, and community leadership, including the AV Fair Board and AV Boosters. In 2014, Newton Chelette was honored as Citizen of the Year. He brings experience, integrity, and a lifelong commitment to strengthening local schools and helping every student and family succeed.
