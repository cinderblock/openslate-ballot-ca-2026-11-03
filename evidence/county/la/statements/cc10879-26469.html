STATEMENT OF MELISSA RAMOSO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF ARTESIA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 43

Occupation: Mayor Pro Tem

It is an honor of my life to serve as your Mayor Pro Tem of the city we all love —Artesia.

As a Gahr Gladiator and UC Irvine graduate, to 23 years working in public service, my guiding principle has always been simple: leadership means serving others.

My commitment to our community comes from my parents—a nurse and a military veteran who sacrificed everything to show me what true service looks like.

Serving as a State Assembly District Director strengthened my ability to navigate government and solve problems.

I have put that experience to work serving Artesia.

Together with hardworking city staff, I have:

Fixed roads, sidewalks and trimmed trees;
Balanced budgets;
Championed new businesses and park space,
Secured a dedicated Sheriff's Sergeant, license plate readers and roving cameras;
Initiated the Pioneer Boulevard restriping & e-bike safety ordinance;
Launched Citywide Townhalls & published a trackable Capital Improvement Plan online; and
Passed Los Angeles County's first catalytic converter theft ordinance.

City Hall should always be accessible, responsive, and accountable to YOU. I am grateful for your trust and the opportunity to serve.

I respectfully ask for your vote to keep delivering thoughtful, innovative, and fiscally responsible solutions for Artesia.
