STATEMENT OF FELITA KEALING

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF EDUCATION,
District No. 1

PASADENA UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 63

Occupation: Assistant Coordinator of Marketing/Student Recruitment/Community Relations

Felita Kealing is running for the Pasadena Unified Board of Education, District 1, because she believes strong schools build strong communities—and District 1 deserves both.

A LEADER WHO BRINGS PEOPLE TOGETHER

A proud Pasadena Unified graduate, parent of two PUSD graduates, school employee, and longtime community leader, she has long served the district that helped shape her.

For decades, Felita has united families, educators, nonprofits, and community partners to strengthen public education. She helped establish the Pasadena Unified African American Parent Council, led the Pasadena Educational Foundation Board, organized literacy and school supply initiatives, and continues to support students and families every day as Assistant Coordinator of Marketing, Student Recruitment, and Community Relations at Thurgood Marshall Secondary School.

READY TO REBUILD

After losing her family's Altadena home in the Eaton Fire, Felita understands firsthand the challenges many District 1 families face. She believes schools are at the heart of rebuilding.

Felita is committed to strengthening family engagement, supporting educators and staff, expanding opportunities for every student, and ensuring every child feels seen, supported, and inspired to succeed.

As a Board of Education Member, Felita will lead with compassion, collaboration, and an unwavering commitment to the students and families of Pasadena Unified.
