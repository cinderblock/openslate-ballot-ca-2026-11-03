STATEMENT OF JERRY VELASCO

CANDIDATE FOR CITY CLERK

CITY OF EL MONTE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

As a long-time El Monte resident, homeowner, and former Councilman, Mayor Pro Tem, Treasurer and Planning Commissioner, I am now running for City Clerk, to build on the strong record that I established serving the City for nine years, which included bringing additional resources to the City, improving community programs for children and seniors, and providing public safety services, which produced the lowest crime record in 30 years.

As a former council member, I focused on making responsible financial decisions, protecting vital city services, enhancing the arts and culture, and preparing our community to weather challenging circumstances.

As an elected City Clerk, I will help City Council to keep all our fire stations open, fully funding our police department, protecting vital services, and the creation of more city parks.

As an elected City Clerk, I will make it my priority to attend all city council meetings, prepare all official city council minutes, and will maintain all city ordinances, resolutions, and other city documents, oversee the publication of legal and public notices, public records requests, oversee the elections and administrating oaths. I respectfully ask that you consider voting for Jerry Velasco for City Clerk. Together, we will continue to thrive!

Thank you!
