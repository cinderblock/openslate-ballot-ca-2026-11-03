STATEMENT OF SHIRLEY RUDOLPH

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 2

LAWNDALE SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Incumbent

My late husband, Councilman Larry Rudolph and I lived our 55 ½ year Married life in Lawndale. We raised our Three Sons here and ALL Five of US Graduated from Lawndale Schools.

I have proven my Dedication and Commitment to Youth by donating 45+ years to Lawndale Little League Baseball; 12 ½ years to the City of Lawndale's Parks, Recreation Social Services Commission; Lawndale Kiwanis Club Charter Board Member and Lawndale High School Key Club Advisor for 14 years.

THANK YOU for allowing me to be YOUR Representative on this Board for the past years. I have served YOU well. I have proven my Accessibility to ALL and I have been YOUR VOICE for the Betterment of Education for ALL of Our Students, Our Schools and Our Community.

I am seeking Re-Election to the Lawndale Elementary School Board because of my continuing interest in the Quality of Life for Lawndale's Young People.
I need YOUR support.

TOGETHER we can SUCCESSFULLY move our CHILDREN through The 21st CENTURY.

I ASK FOR YOUR VOTE
