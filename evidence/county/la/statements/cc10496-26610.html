STATEMENT OF GETRO ELIZE

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
4th District

CITY OF PALMDALE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 45

Occupation: Analyst - Los Angeles County Department of Public Health

Getro Elize for Palmdale City Council

As a devoted husband, father of two, U.S. Army Veteran and graduate of Howard University, I've dedicated my life to serving others. As Vice Chair of the Palmdale Planning Commission, I have worked to strengthen neighborhoods, expand housing opportunities, and promote responsible economic development that benefits every resident.

Throughout my career, I have partnered with government agencies, nonprofit organizations, businesses, and community leaders to serve vulnerable populations, including veterans, seniors, individuals experiencing homelessness, justice-involved individuals reentering the community, and youth seeking a second chance. I have connected residents with housing, employment, education, healthcare, and behavioral health services, helping individuals and families achieve greater stability and independence.

If elected, I will focus on creating quality jobs, supporting small businesses, increasing housing opportunities, improving public safety, investing in infrastructure, and expanding educational and workforce opportunities for youth and working families. I believe strong communities are built through transparent leadership, fiscal responsibility, and collaboration.

With a lifetime of public service, proven leadership, and a vision for Palmdale's future, Getro Elize is the clear choice to lead our city forward and deliver practical solutions that strengthen our community and improve the quality of life for every resident.
