STATEMENT OF CECILIA REYES KEFFER

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

SAN GABRIEL UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 57

Occupation: Educator/Realtor

After 28 years working as a teacher in the San Gabriel Unified School District, I am excited to be a candidate for the School Board. My connection to the San Gabriel Unified School District, however, is not limited to teaching. I have also spent many years as an SGTA union leader, a school-site council member, a community member, and the parent of two children who completed their entire K–12 education in SGUSD.

These experiences have provided me with a unique understanding of how decisions made in the boardroom affect students, families, and employees every day. It is this understanding that will guide my decisions as a board member, helping to ensure that every dollar we allocate has the greatest possible impact on student well-being and the long-term success of our eight schools.

I am a problem solver who believes in making sensible, budget-minded decisions that prioritize student needs, protect essential programs and services, and use taxpayer dollars wisely and effectively.

I will bring a balanced perspective shaped by years of service in our schools and community. I will listen thoughtfully and ask careful questions to help ensure that our School District provides outstanding opportunities and support for all students.
