STATEMENT OF JOHN TRAN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF ROSEMEAD GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Business Owner/Commissioner

I'm humbly asking for your vote for Rosemead City Council. I've had the privilege of serving this community before and serving two terms as your Mayor. I would be honored to have your support once again.

My priorities for Rosemead are simple – improve public safety, enhance recreation opportunities, and implement a common-sense economic development plan. I have a proven track-record of getting things done and have concrete ideas for how to make our city safer, upgrade our parks, and bring new shopping, small businesses, and dining experiences to Rosemead.

It is important that I acknowledge my past. I've certainly made mistakes. I fully accept responsibility for those prior actions and I do regret having let so many people down. But I am a better leader and person today because of those experiences. It is my sincere hope that my actions and record of late, as well as the overwhelming support from so many community members and neighborhood leaders across Rosemead, has enabled me to re-earn your confidence.

Rosemead is an amazing City. Together, I know we can implement our shared vision to create a community that respects the past while fully embracing the future.

John Tran, Former Mayor
