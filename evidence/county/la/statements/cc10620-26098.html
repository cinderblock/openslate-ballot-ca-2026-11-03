STATEMENT OF JOHN B. DE LEON

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

BALDWIN PARK UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 43

Occupation: Attorney IV, State of California

I am a father of 4 BPUSD students and my focus is on ensuring student success. As a BPUSD Board Member, I've encouraged kids to read and advocated for phonics-based instruction. I've championed creating mentorship programs, expanding CTE courses, strengthening BPUSD's athletics and arts departments, and ensuring access to AP and early college classes. Every student deserves the opportunity to pursue the college or career of their choice.

As a state attorney, I know the problems our students face: social media addiction, financial illiteracy, unethical AI use, unhealthy screen time, and an economy where life's necessities are increasingly unaffordable.

This is why I support helping working parents by providing quality programs for students year-round with free after-school programs, expanded before-school care, and immersive summer field trips. Our families deserve these important supports.

I am an immigrant, raised by a single mother, and a graduate of BPUSD. I know that education is the path to prosperity. As iron sharpens iron, so too do our kids make each other better. Thank you for trusting me over these last four years and I hope you will support me over the next four because the needs of our students must always come first.
