STATEMENT OF JESSICA RUBIO

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

HACIENDA LA PUENTE UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Nurse Manager/Parent

As a mother raising two young boys in Hacienda Heights, I understand every parent wants their child to feel safe, supported, challenged, and prepared for the future. I decided to run because I realized I didn't have to wait until my children started school to help shape their future.

As a nurse manager, I lead teams, manage budgets, implement policies, promote accountability, and make thoughtful decisions every day. I will bring that same leadership, integrity, collaboration, and responsibility to our schools.

I believe parents deserve a voice, teachers deserve support, students deserve opportunities to succeed, and every decision should put children first. I will listen respectfully, work collaboratively, and make informed decisions that strengthen trust between families and our district.

I am committed to fostering safe, inclusive, and transparent schools where every student feels valued and has the opportunity to succeed.

I am not a career politician. I am a parent who cares deeply about our community and wants to help ensure every child has the opportunity to succeed.

It would be an honor to earn your trust and your vote for Hacienda La Puente Unified School District Trustee Area 5.
