STATEMENT OF LIZBETH RENOVA

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 2

MONTEBELLO UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Teacher

Lizbeth Renova is an MHS graduate and a long-time MUSD resident. Got her B.A. from Cal State Long Beach in Family Consumer Sciences with a concentration in Child Development and Family Studies.

As a lifelong member of this community and a proud mother of three children attending Montebello Unified School District, Lizbeth cares deeply about the future of our district. Watching her children walk the same hallways and learn in the same classrooms that shaped her own childhood is both meaningful and motivating. It has strengthened her commitment to ensuring every student has the opportunity to succeed in a safe, supportive, and high - quality learning environment.

Lizbeth is running for the Montebello Unified School District school board because she believes our students deserve leaders who listen, work collaboratively, and always put children first. As a parent, a former MUSD student, and an educator, she understands the hopes families have for their children and the responsibility we have to provide them with the best education possible.

Throughout her career as an educator and director, Lizbeth has achieved significant milestones, notably by fostering a secure and supportive environment for students and their families. Through her commitment to building a strong community, she has successfully empowered families in need to thrive within the Early Childhood Education sector. Furthermore, she has demonstrated exceptional leadership by establishing four Child Development Centers from the ground up. Guided by the belief that every child learns differently and possesses unique potential, Lizbeth remains dedicated to ensuring that every student enrolled in her programs is provided with the opportunity to reach their full potential. Teachers and staff make a difference every day. They deserve our respect, support, and the resources to succeed. Investing in those who educate our children strengthens our entire district.

As your elected MUSD trustee, Lizbeth will ensure that she

- Update facilities
- Bring a strong Special Education Program
- Support small classes
- Updates technology
- Support teachers, classified and administrative staff
- Increase financial responsibilities.

I believe in listening, being transparent, and making thoughtful, fiscally responsible decisions. Together, we can continue building schools where every student feels safe, supported, and inspired to achieve their dreams. It will be an honor to serve the community that has given so much to my family and to help ensure MUSD remains a place where every child can succeed.

Thank you.
