STATEMENT OF MARISOL CORTEZ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
6th District

CITY OF EL MONTE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 38

Occupation: Councilmember/School Administrator

Ballot Designation: Councilmember/School Administrator

El Monte has been my home my entire life, and I am proud of what we have achieved as a community.

As the daughter of immigrant parents, I learned nothing is given, and everything is earned. Family and striving for a better future are core values we share. Many in our community work hard yet struggle to make ends meet. As your councilmember, I supported building housing to help working families stay in El Monte.

Economic opportunities are crucial for providing critical services. We need to invest in repaving roads, bringing in retail businesses, restaurants, and entertainment to create jobs and new revenue sources.

Safety is also essential for our residents and investment in our community. As your councilmember, I voted to keep our police funded and fire stations open.

I will continue to prioritize projects in each district and be accountable to you.

We need to listen more to the community to better understand our City's needs.

Together, we can make our community better for all generations.

I respectfully ask for your vote.
