STATEMENT OF YOLANDA DAVIDSON

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
1st District

CITY OF INGLEWOOD PRIMARY NOMINATING ELECTION

Age: 53

Occupation: Business Owner

I am a wife, mother, and lifelong resident of Inglewood. I have witnessed the city's triumphs and challenges and have remained steadfast in my commitment to Inglewood. I have stood with families seeking answers, organized residents, and ensured their voices are heard. Representation means being present, accessible, and willing to listen, not only during election season. Inglewood deserves leadership focused on improving residents' quality of life. Safety, accountability, transparency, and the use of taxpayer dollars must be priorities. I will work to address the traffic issues that plague our district, advocate for cleaner air and to reduce aviation noise, support our seniors and local businesses, and promote responsible development that benefits residents.

I believe Inglewood is at its best when we work together. Our progress should strengthen neighborhoods, create opportunities, and ensure that everyone wins, not just corporations and special interests.

I will bring independent leadership, common sense, compassion, and a community voice to City Hall. I respectfully ask for your vote and the opportunity to serve District 1 while continuing to fight for the Soul of Inglewood.
