STATEMENT OF DANNY ROMAN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
4th District

CITY OF WEST COVINA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 69

Occupation: Retired LAPD Sergeant

I am a proud father, grandfather, West Covina homeowner, and a retired Los Angeles Police Department Sergeant with more than two decades of public-safety leadership experience. In my professional and personal life, I have built a reputation on professionalism, integrity, and steady decision-making under pressure. Today, I am running for West Covina's City Council District 4 seat because I believe I can play a critical role in improving organizational accountability in West Covina. I would like to protect our public resources, and expand partnerships between our residents, local businesses, and City departments. Through transparency, consistent communication, and collaboration, we can build a more vibrant City. I have seen it, first-hand, in the vulnerable neighborhoods I grew up in, and in the communities I would eventually look after as a young police officer on patrol. I'd be grateful for your trust in me and, respectfully, ask for your vote.
