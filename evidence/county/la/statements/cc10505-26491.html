STATEMENT OF JEANEVRA CALHOUN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF ROLLING HILLS GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

I am running for City Council because I believe our community deserves thoughtful leadership, practical solutions, and a genuine commitment to preserving what makes our city special. I believe I can offer a fresh perspective on familiar issues and a grounded approach to new ones. I aim to bring enthusiasm and renewed energy to the council chambers as we navigate and prepare for the future.

As a residential developer in the construction industry, I understand the practical challenges associated with city planning, zoning, and building regulations. I also recognize the importance of property values and land potential alongside preservation of the rural beauty we all enjoy. Homeownership in our community is unique and often long-term; I believe people should have the freedom to love the place they call home.

I care deeply about public safety and look toward new initiatives currently being developed by City staff. I recognize the benefits of implementing new technology systems like the Safe Network and the value of the existing Block Captain program that helps keep residents feeling safe, prepared and connected in times of crisis.

I appreciate the Council's commitment to fiscal responsibility, and I'm keen on continuing this mindful decision-making moving forward. As our state policies continue to evolve, my goal is to thoughtfully pursue progress while embracing the values and traditions that have shaped our past.

As a third-generation resident, I feel incredibly fortunate to have grown up in a community with such unique character. My childhood was spent almost entirely outdoors, exploring the trails that wind through our pastoral landscape. I want future generations to grow up with those same opportunities and to create cherished memories of their own. If elected, I strive to ensure we all can continue to experience the treasured lifestyle that defines Rolling Hills.
