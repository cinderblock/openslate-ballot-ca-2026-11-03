STATEMENT OF ANDREW ROWE

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

WESTSIDE UNION SCHOOL DISTRICT

As an incumbent Westside Union School District Board Trustee, I am running for re-election to continue delivering a safe, high-quality education that prepares every child for success. With professional experience in aerospace engineering, I bring a problem-solving, results-oriented, systems-thinking approach to board service.

Academic Excellence First

Raising student achievement remains essential. Building on our schools' distinctions, we must continue improving proficiency in reading and math through evidence-based instruction, targeted support, and high expectations for all students.

Empowering Parents

Parents are children's first teachers. I support enhanced transparency and policies that better inform families about educational materials and curriculum. I will promote greater involvement through PTAs, School Site Councils, and open communication.

Safe Campuses

Safety enables learning. I strongly support the addition of School Resource Officers in partnership with Lancaster Police to protect students and build positive relationships.

Responsible Stewardship

I will keep advocating prudent budgeting focused on classrooms while listening to families, educators, and our community.

Together, we can strengthen academic performance, parental engagement, and safety across our K-8 schools. I respectfully ask for your continued support and vote.
