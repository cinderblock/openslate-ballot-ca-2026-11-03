STATEMENT OF CELESTE RODRIGUEZ

PARTY PREFERENCE: DEMOCRATIC

MEMBER OF THE STATE ASSEMBLY, 43rd DISTRICT

I'm honored to represent you in the State Assembly and proud to share that I have been fighting for our community since day one. I grew up in the San Fernando Valley and I love that it's where my family calls home.

I've seen our struggles firsthand; not being able to meet basic needs, navigating support systems that don't work, and seeing people left behind. I went to Sacramento to make sure we are at the tables where decisions are being made because our community deserves every resource and opportunity others have.

In the Assembly, I've worked to pass legislation that keeps families together, helps people recover from crisis, and opens doors for young people to build careers. These are real changes that reach families directly.

I wrote the Family Preparedness Plan Act to protect immigrant and mixed-status families from sudden separation and helped keep schools and childcare centers safe spaces for children. I'm working with community advocates to defend California's sanctuary protections, limit harmful immigration enforcement cooperation, and fight to expand access to healthcare, food assistance, and water protections for all families, regardless of status.

I'll continue to fight for the resources the Northeast Valley needs - I'd be honored to have your vote to continue serving our community.
