STATEMENT OF JIAMIN LI

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS

VALLEY COUNTY WATER DISTRICT

Age: 34

Occupation: Medical Aesthetic Business Owner

As a small business owner, I understand the importance of responsibility, accountability, and serving the community. I came to the United States with the dream of building a better future through hard work, and I am grateful to call Baldwin Park my home.

Working with people from many different backgrounds has taught me the value of listening, respect, and compassion. I believe every resident deserves to have their voice heard and to receive fair representation.

As a member of our diverse community, I want to learn, serve, and give back. I believe responsible leadership begins with honesty, transparency, and careful decision making. If elected, I will work diligently, continue learning, and always put the interests of our residents first.

I am committed to protecting our community's resources, supporting responsible water management, maintaining reasonable water rates, and ensuring that future generations continue to have safe and reliable water service. My goal is simple: to serve with integrity and help build a stronger community for everyone.
