STATEMENT OF RUDY T. MARTINEZ

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

VALLE LINDO SCHOOL DISTRICT

Incumbent: Elected Board Member and past President of the Valle Lindo School District.

I am a true leader with experience and take pride in the positive reputation of our Award Winning Schools. Valle Lindo School District students achieve academic excellence and benefit from our strong programs. I believe that children always come first. I will remain dedicated to our students as we prepare them for the future.

I will always work in unity with all Board Members to continue the success in our Award Winning Schools.

Our Children Come First.
