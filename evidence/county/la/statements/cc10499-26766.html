STATEMENT OF KIMBERLY KENNE

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF EDUCATION,
District No. 1

PASADENA UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Board Member

I'm proud to have served you as a PUSD board member for the last 15 years.

I'm focusing on our students' academic success and positive mental health following the Eaton Fire and advocating for additional state funding for Altadena post-fire.

I have spent over 2 decades both as a parent leader and as an advocate for educational reform.

I am the best qualified and most experienced candidate.

As a PUSD board member and Altadena resident, I have worked to keep funding in the classroom. I've prioritized quality instruction with experienced teachers and the latest technology. I am a strong supporter of neighborhood schools and I put student interests first.

My own children have attended Blair Middle School, Eliot Middle School, Burbank Elementary and Longfellow Elementary.

For the last four years, I have worked to:

- Increase public access to information on district spending, especially for legal fees and consultant fees
- Base decisions for programs and expenditures on data and results

As your representative, I will continue to work to:

- Increase opportunities for all students
- Engage families as partners in their child's education
- Enforce fiscal stability of student programs so they can thrive even in challenging financial times

I'd be honored to have your vote.
