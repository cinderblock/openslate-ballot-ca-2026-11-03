STATEMENT OF WILLIAM PEREZ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
1st District

CITY OF INGLEWOOD PRIMARY NOMINATING ELECTION

Occupation: Business Owner

For 16 years I've run my own business in Inglewood meeting budgets managing resources and delivering results for my customers and my community. That hands on experience shapes how our city spends money and sets rates.

As Vice chair of Inglewood Unified's Asset Management Committee, I help oversee how public resources are managed for our students and families. As a member of GPAC/Next Level Inglewood, I work directly with residents on the issues that matter most.

I've called Inglewood home for over 20 years, and I've watched neighbors squeezed by rising utility costs, worsening traffic, and an affordability crisis pushing longtime residents out.

Elected to represent District 1, I'll bring my real-world experience to fight for fairer utility pricing, real traffic solutions, and an Inglewood we can still afford to call home.
