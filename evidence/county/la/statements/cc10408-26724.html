STATEMENT OF RON SAHU

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
First District

CITY OF ALHAMBRA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

For more than 38 years, Alhambra has been Dr. Ron Sahu's home. Now, he is running for City Council, District 1, to bring decades of local service, scientific expertise, community advocacy, and independent leadership focused on results—not politics.

EXPERIENCE ROOTED IN ALHAMBRA

A Caltech-trained environmental engineer and educator, Ron has spent his career solving complex problems through research, evidence, and accountability. He has taught at Caltech, UCLA, USC, and other universities while raising his family in Alhambra and sending his children to local public schools.

Ron has served Alhambra on the Planning Commission and Environmental Sustainability Commission, helping shape the City's General Plan and Zoning Code while advocating for responsible development and environmental protections. He serves on the board of Grassroots Alhambra, a nonpartisan community organization focused on civic engagement, transparency, and accountability.

PUTTING RESIDENTS FIRST

Ron believes Alhambra deserves a City Council that listens to residents, asks tough questions, and follows through. He will work to protect neighborhoods, strengthen public accountability, invest responsibly in Alhambra's future, and ensure residents always have a meaningful voice in the decisions that affect their community.

With decades of experience serving Alhambra, Ron is ready to get to work.
