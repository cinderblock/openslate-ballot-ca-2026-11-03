STATEMENT OF ANA TORRES

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
3rd District

CITY OF MONTEBELLO GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Montebello Business Owner

My name is Ana Torres, and Montebello has been my home for over 30 years. As a Mexican immigrant, mother of two, local business owner, and community advocate, I understand the value of hard work, perseverance, and service to others.

I earned my Associate's degree from East Los Angeles College and both my Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Sociology from California State University, Los Angeles. I spent over 14 years serving students and families through the Los Angeles Community College District before investing in my own city by opening Organica Juice Bar, Montebello's first organic juice bar. My goal has always been to create opportunities that improve the health and well-being of our community while supporting local economic growth.

Throughout my career, I have worked to bring people together, build meaningful partnerships, and serve with integrity. I am running for City Council to continue that work. Montebello deserves honest, transparent leadership that puts residents first.

My priorities include supporting public safety, strengthening local businesses, improving city services, investing in youth and seniors, and ensuring responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars.

I would be honored to earn your trust and your vote as we work together to build a better Montebello.
