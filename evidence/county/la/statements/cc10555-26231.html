STATEMENT OF MONICA ALEMAN GIBBS

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 3

THREE VALLEYS MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICT

Occupation: Water Consultant/Educator

Hello Neighbor,

Our region faces urgent water challenges: aging infrastructure, groundwater contamination, and rising costs that hit ratepayers hardest. I'm running for the Three Valleys Municipal Water District Board because we need leadership that knows our local water systems and understands their complexities, not politics as usual.

I've spent my career working across local, state, and federal agencies, bringing community voices directly into water policy decisions. From contamination compliance to watershed restoration, I've led projects throughout the San Gabriel Valley corridor.

My hands-on experience with EPA mandates, Army Corps regulations, and multi-agency funding battles gives me a rare and practical understanding of how to secure resources and hold agencies accountable.

Fiscal responsibility matters. Every dollar ratepayers contribute must go toward real solutions like contamination cleanup, infrastructure reliability, and water security—not bureaucratic waste. As a water consultant, I know the funding pipelines because I've navigated them for years. As an educator, I am committed to keeping our community informed on how tax dollars are spent.

My family put down roots here because we believe in this community. It's personal to me, and I'll fight for clean, reliable water for every family in our district.

I respectfully ask for your consideration.

Monica
