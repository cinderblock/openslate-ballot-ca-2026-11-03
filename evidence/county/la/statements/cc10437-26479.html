STATEMENT OF KEVIN LAINEZ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF COMMERCE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 44

Occupation: Mayor

The City of Commerce is where I proudly chose to raise my family, and serving our community has been such an honor. Four years ago, I ran for City Council because our City faced unprecedented challenges. The pandemic shut down programs, delayed projects, strained our budget, and tested our sense of community.

Together, we've made real progress. I've worked to improve roads and sidewalks, upgrade our parks, support economic development, expand rental assistance for seniors and veterans, launch free Wi-Fi at our parks and facilities, enhance youth and senior services, strengthen our home beautification programs, and adopt smarter building standards for City facilities that save energy and reduce long-term costs.

While I'm proud of what we've accomplished, our work isn't finished. We must continue making Commerce safer, supporting small businesses, creating new housing and economic opportunities, revitalizing underused City properties, and keeping our neighborhoods clean and well maintained.

Commerce deserves ethical leadership that delivers results while planning for the future. That is the standard I've worked to uphold every day I've had the privilege of serving you. I would be honored to earn your support so we can continue moving Commerce forward together.
