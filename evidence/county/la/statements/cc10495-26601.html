STATEMENT OF FRANZELLE LOUIS JOHNSON JR.

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
3rd District

CITY OF PALMDALE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 46

Occupation: Small Business Owner

Every resident in District 3 deserves a safe, supportive, and prosperous neighborhood to call home. As a father, small business owner, practical problem solver, and 12-year Palmdale resident, I am running for City Council to serve as an accessible advocate for everyone in the community. I believe local government should work to uplift every family, senior, worker, and youth across our district. My top priority is neighborhood safety: reducing crime and gang activity by strengthening proactive community policing, expanding youth prevention programs, and protecting our streets. As a parent, I am deeply committed into investing in youth education, vocational trade training, and strong local job pipelines so our next generation can build successful futures right here at home. Bringing a business owners result oriented mindset to local government, I will help foster economic opportunity all supporting small businesses, attracting high-quality employers, and revitalizing our commercial areas. City Hall must serve every single person in Palmdale. With deep roots and an unyielding commitment to our entire community, I humbly ask for your vote.
