STATEMENT OF MARYANN G. BARRIOS

CANDIDATE FOR CITY TREASURER

CITY OF EL MONTE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Retired Analyst

My name is Maryann Barrios. I am a retired analyst who has lived in El Monte for the better part of 45 years.

I hold a bachelor's in business administration (accounting option) and a California CPA certificate (Inactive). I have worked in the non-profit sector for the bulk of my career, and worked in various positions, at various levels of management, and have been responsible for financial statements, government reports and various analytical reports.

I would like to serve as City Treasurer because over the years I have been given a good life here in the city. My children grew up here, and my husband's businesses were/are here. El Monte has given us much, and now I have the time and ability to use my talents and pay forward what I was given.

I firmly believe that my background, education and work ethic would truly add value to the City of El Monte.

Please vote for Maryann G. Barrios for El Monte City Treasurer on or before November 3! Thank you.
