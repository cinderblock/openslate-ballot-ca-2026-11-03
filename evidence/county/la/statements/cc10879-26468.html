STATEMENT OF RENE J. TREVINO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF ARTESIA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Candidate: Rene J. Trevino
Ballot Designation: Mayor/Business Owner
Age: 58

Dear Artesians,

It is my honor and privilege to serve as Mayor of Artesia this year and as your Councilmember since 2018. I have dedicated myself to improving the quality of life for all Artesians through communication and involvement in our community.

I promised to deliver on my message, "Let's Make Change Together". I am proud to say...together we did! We made Artesia better together! And together we can do much more for our city.

Public Safety is my number one priority to keep our neighborhoods safe, and I am proud to say, crime has gone down each year. But there is still work for us to do together. Our senior programs, parks, streets, sidewalks, trees, city improvement projects and programs will continue to get better if we work TOGETHER.

City hall works for you! I will continue to watch over your taxpayer dollars as we keep Artesia moving forward.

Therefore, I am asking for your continued trust and support in me to serve all Artesia residents and businesses for the next four years.

I am committed to serve Artesia and all Artesians.

I humbly ask for your vote by November 3rd.

Respectfully,

Rene J. Trevino
