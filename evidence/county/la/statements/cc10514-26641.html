STATEMENT OF PHIL BROCK

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF SANTA MONICA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Businessperson/Community Advocate

I am your neighbor.

Born and raised in Santa Monica, I grew up on these beaches, body-surfed these waves, and played on these fields. This city is my home, my community, my life. That is why I am running.

Our city is at a crossroads.

Crime and homelessness demand urgent, practical solutions & accountability— not idle promises. I consistently fought to expand our police force and provide real safety for our residents and visitors while vocally pressing federal, state, and county officials to be more responsive. I will not stop.

We will go toe-to-toe with Sacramento to regain our right to determine our own zoning while adding the affordable housing our longtime senior and low-income residents must have.

Here is what I believe — without apology.

This election is about taking back our city from developers who want to demolish our way of life for their profit. Our green spaces are the lungs of Santa Monica — we will protect them always. Our small businesses are the soul of this city. No one gets left behind. Common Sense!

Santa Monica's best days are ahead — if we have the courage to demand them.

I am Phil Brock. Pro-resident. Pro-local business. Pro-safety. Pro-Santa Monica.
