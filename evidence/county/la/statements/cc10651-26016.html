STATEMENT OF DAN JEFFRIES

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

LA CAÑADA UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Attorney

It has been my honor to serve on the La Cañada Unified School District Governing Board for the past 13 years, including two terms as Board President.

Throughout my service, I have listened to students, parents, teachers, and staff while making thoughtful, transparent decisions that put students first.

Together, our district has achieved remarkable success LCUSD has been recognized as California's top-ranked school district and among the best in the nation. Our elementary schools earned National Blue Ribbon recognition, and our students rank first among California unified school districts in English language arts and second in mathematics on statewide assessments.

We have maintained stable enrollment, preserved a balanced budget, passed bond measures to modernize our schools and technology, and successfully welcomed the Sagebrush area into our district.

While I am proud of these accomplishments, important work remains. I remain committed to academic excellence, strong student support services, outstanding educators, modern facilities, fiscal responsibility, and transparent governance.

I care deeply about our schools and remain dedicated to ensuring every student receives a world-class education in a safe, welcoming, and supportive environment.

It would be an honor to continue serving the families of the La Cañada Unified School District.
