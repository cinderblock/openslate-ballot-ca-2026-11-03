STATEMENT OF MARIA TERESA DEL RIO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF HAWAIIAN GARDENS GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 43

Occupation: Council Member

For the past six years, I have had the honor of serving our community as a Council Member. This experience has been one of the most meaningful commitments of my life, and I am proud of the work I have done on behalf of our residents.

Throughout my six years in office, I have supported and approved initiatives that created positive changes, improved our community services, strengthened our neighborhoods, and enhanced the quality of life for our families.

I take my responsibility as an elected official seriously. I listen to residents, study the issues, and make decisions based on what I believe is best for our community and its future. Leadership requires integrity, accountability, courage, and a willingness to stand up for what is right and that is exactly what I will continue doing.

I am running for reelection because there is more work to do. I will continue advocating for our residents, supporting responsible growth, improving our city, and creating opportunities for future generations.

I am asking for your vote and your continued trust on November 3rd, 2026. Thank you.
