STATEMENT OF ANDREW ROBLE

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

WHITTIER CITY SCHOOL DISTRICT

As a proud father, Los Angeles County Behavioral Health Commissioner, and longtime community advocate, I am committed to serving the families of Whittier.

As a blue-collar worker and union leader, I have dedicated my career to improving workplace safety, strengthening our community, and advocating for working families. Through my service on the Los Angeles County Behavioral Health Commission, I have worked with local leaders to expand access to mental health resources and programs that support children, students, and families.

I am running for the Whittier City School District Board of Education because every student deserves a safe, supportive, and academically excellent learning environment. I will work to strengthen school safety, support our teachers and classified employees, promote student achievement, expand access to student mental health services, and ensure responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars.

I believe school board members must be transparent, accountable, and focused on student success. I will work collaboratively with parents, educators, staff, and our community to make thoughtful decisions that prepare students for lifelong success.

I respectfully ask for your vote for Whittier City School District Board of Education, Area 5.
