STATEMENT OF HONG "DIANA" ZHAO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF WALNUT GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 59

Occupation: Business Owner

Dear Walnut Residents,

It is a great honor to run for Walnut City Council. I am committed to serving our community with integrity, transparency, and accountability while putting residents first.

As a small business owner, licensed clinical social worker, community leader, and nonprofit volunteer, I have dedicated my career to helping individuals, families, and our community overcome challenges through compassion, collaboration, and practical solutions. These experiences have strengthened my ability to listen, bring people together, and make thoughtful decisions.

I will work to maintain safe neighborhoods, support responsible economic development, protect our city's financial stability, improve essential city services, and encourage open communication between residents and City Hall. I believe every voice matters, and our local government should be accessible, responsive, and transparent.

Walnut is a wonderful place to live, work, and raise a family. I am committed to preserving our high quality of life while planning responsibly for the future. Together, we can build a stronger, safer, and more vibrant community for current and future generations.

I would be humbled and grateful to earn your trust, your support, and your vote.

— Dr. Hong Diana Zhao, LCSW
