STATEMENT OF CHRISTINE RAQUEL CRUZ-ARVAYO

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 1

POMONA UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Retired School Administrator

With a "people first" approach, I have welcomed the opportunity to influence the culture of Pomona Unified School District schools and promote student academic achievement and personal growth for the past 30 years. I have an unrelenting passion for helping students succeed academically, socially, and personally. My work as a high school English and Health teacher, Director of Student Activities, ASB Advisor, Resource Teacher, and a Program Administrator in the Secondary Education Department has helped students make the most of their educational experience and prepare for post-secondary education or the workforce.

I have also had the privilege of serving as a member of the San Antonio ROP Board for 12 years. My qualifications combine strong work values with a solid education and work history that aligns closely with Pomona Unified School District's commitment to student achievement. I respectfully ask for your support.
