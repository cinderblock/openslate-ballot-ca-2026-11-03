STATEMENT OF MARIA A. GUTZEIT

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 3

SANTA CLARITA VALLEY WATER AGENCY

As an environmental engineer, business owner, and mom I understand the challenges facing families in the Santa Clarita Valley: balancing a budget, keeping our kids healthy, and planning for the future. I am running for re-election to ensure our water remains safe, reliable, and affordable.

During my time on the board, I have prioritized fiscal responsibility, ensuring our long-term rate plans remain predictable for local families and small businesses. We have successfully expanded our state-of-the-art water treatment facilities, guaranteeing the water flowing into our homes and schools is pristine and safe.

My focus for the next term is clear:

- Water Quality: Ensuring every drop we serve is healthy for all.
- Rate Stability: Protecting your household budget from unnecessary spikes.
- Water Reliability: Investing in local groundwater and infrastructure so we are drought-resilient without burdensome mandates.
- Transparent Leadership: Making sure you know exactly how and why decisions are made.

The Santa Clarita Valley is our home. We need a water board that works quietly, efficiently, and for you. I humbly ask for your vote to continue protecting our community's most essential resource. Let's keep our valley's water secure for our future.
