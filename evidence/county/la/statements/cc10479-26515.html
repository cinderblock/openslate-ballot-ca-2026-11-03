STATEMENT OF JOSEPH UNGOCO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF MANHATTAN BEACH GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 57

Occupation: Retired Publicist

I moved to Manhattan Beach 12 years ago, after retiring from a successful career in Strategic Communications in DC and New York City. Since moving here, I have volunteered and advocated for MB Neighborhood Watch, the Friends of Polliwog Park and our Botanical Garden. I also served on the Planning Commission for 7 years and share the concerns of residents regarding over development and state overreach.

If elected, my priorities will be instituting hard term limits for City Council, balancing the budget, restoring a culture of saving for deferred maintenance of our aging city buildings and infrastructure, and increasing the visibility of our current Police force. Additionally, I am committed to ending the 5-0 voting, "go along to get along" status quo on our council that does not accurately represent the diversity of opinions and priorities among the residents of our community.

As a leadership trainer and a civic minded resident, I firmly believe in putting my skills and experience at the service of my community and that a finite term fosters urgency, innovation and, ultimately, results. I would be honored to serve my fellow residents as we improve our community for the next generation of residents and local leaders.
