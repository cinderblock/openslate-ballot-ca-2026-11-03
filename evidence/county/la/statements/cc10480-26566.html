STATEMENT OF VERONICA PERAL BERNABE

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF MAYWOOD GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 52

Occupation: Community Insurance Professional

My name is Veronica Bernabe. It is both an honor and a privilege to ask for your support and your vote for City Council of Maywood.

I proudly called city of Maywood my home for the past three years. I was raised in the neighboring communities of Huntington Park and Bell, where I learned the importance of hard work, respect, family, faith and serving others and dedication values that continue to guide me today.

I believe our city deserves leadership built on integrity, transparency, accountability and strong values. I am committed to listening to our residents, working together, making thoughtful decisions that strengthen our community and working tirelessly to improve the quality of life for every family in our community.

As a woman of faith, I know God's will be done, and if I am given the opportunity to serve as your Councilwoman, I will work faithfully, honestly, and with dedication to represent every member of our community.

I would be honored to earn your trust, your support, and your vote. Together, we can build a stronger, safer, and more united future for our city.

Thank you for your consideration and support.
