CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
Fifth District

CITY OF ALHAMBRA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

NO MORE POLITICAL STATUS QUO

Born to a South American immigrant family, I know what it's like to struggle. I moved to Alhambra before the 2008 recession and have watched families work hard to protect their QUALITY OF LIFE.

As a college civics and ESL professor, I help immigrants and working adults build better futures.

Alhambra deserves leadership that puts RESIDENTS, SMALL BUSINESSES, and TAXPAYERS first—NOT developers, special interests, or machine politics.

I'll be a STRONG, INDEPENDENT VOICE in City Hall, focused on

Neighborhood safety and character
Helping small businesses thrive
Improving quality of life
Traffic and sustainability
Transparency and fiscal accountability

I'll listen, ask tough questions, and make decisions based on FACTS and DATA.

I've championed campaign finance reform, government accountability, and environmental stewardship; served on the city's first historic preservation commission; lead the successful effort to stop a MASSIVE luxury housing project on polluted land; and fought to save Alhambra's last urban forest.

As an ANTI-CORRUPTION CANDIDATE, I accept NO contributions from developers, corporations, or PACs because I always put #PeopleOverProfits.

I want to SERVE MY NEIGHBORS, not build a political career. I respectfully ask for your vote.
