STATEMENT OF JOHN MENDOZA

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 1

POMONA UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Tax Payer Advocate

Our educational institutions across California are currently facing significant challenges and PUSD is no exception. Conflicting state and federal economic pressures are threatening PUSD funding.

PUSD has historically submitted bond measures and property tax ballot measures initiatives to generate the loss of revenue. Trump has declared a war on education, children of immigrants and sciences.

In order to off set revenue loss PUSD regularly submits bond measure in which property tax payers have yet to see tangible improvements in school facilities. Instead in Pomona numerous school structures remain vacant and abandoned creating city blight.

As a candidate I advocate for accountability regarding our tax dollars. School district funds should be distributed equitable, as the prioritization of PUSD facilities in Diamond Bar and Philips Ranch is not fair.

PUSD Measure UU was approved by 70.56 % of PUSD voters in 2024 general election authorizing $385.000.000 in bonds leveling $60.00 per $100.000 of assessed valuation generating $22.500.000 . Measure UU provided no exemption for seniors, handicap and low in property owners struggling.

In PUSD district 88 .2% student's enrollment of 19.005 are classified as socially economic disadvantage.

As a past Water Board official and past City of Pomona commission member I ask for your vote
