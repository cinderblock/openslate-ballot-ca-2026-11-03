STATEMENT OF JOANN N. SMITH

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 3

PALMDALE SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Teacher

Joann Smith is a wife, mother, educator, community leader, and advocate who has devoted nearly 30 years to serving students, families, and communities with compassion and integrity. A proud United States Army Veteran, her commitment to service and leadership has guided her lifelong work to strengthen educational opportunities and improve lives.

Joann earned a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology from Long Island University and a Masters of Education from Pepperdine University. She has taught in New York City and California, where she has served students and families while building strong relationships within the communities she serves.

Throughout her career, Joann has been a champion for educational excellence, equity, and student success. She has served as the Teacher Union Representative, chaired the Human Rights Committee for the High Desert Service Center Council (CTA), and mentored aspiring educators through the BETR Program (The Boundless Educator Teacher Residency). Over the years, she has volunteered extensively throughout her community in support of students, families, and local organizations.

Believing strongly in supporting the whole child, Joann is committed to helping every student learn and grow while serving as an advocate for students and a collaborative leader for the Palmdale School District and our community.
