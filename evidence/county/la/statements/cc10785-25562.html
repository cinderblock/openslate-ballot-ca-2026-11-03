STATEMENT OF DEBORAH KLEIN LOPEZ

PARTY PREFERENCE: DEMOCRATIC

MEMBER OF THE STATE ASSEMBLY, 42nd DISTRICT

I'm Deborah Klein Lopez, and I'm running for State Assembly because California needs a fighter who is an experienced leader when the stakes are high. I'm the only candidate in this race with a proven record of creating jobs, lowering costs, tackling crises, reducing crime, and improving public safety.

I'm proud to be endorsed by:
- Los Angeles County Firefighters
- Sheriff Robert Luna and law enforcement leaders
- California Nurses Association
- Planned Parenthood Central Coast Action Fund
- State Senator Henry Stern
- Assemblywoman Jacqui Irwin

I was elected to the Agoura Hills City Council just two days before the Woolsey Fire. I helped lead recovery efforts, improve emergency preparedness, cut red tape, and make our neighborhoods safer and more resilient. As we face more extreme wildfires, rising costs, and a home insurance crisis, we need leadership that has been tested.

I grew up here, raised my children here, and have spent my career fighting to make this region stronger. As Chair of Clean Power Alliance, I helped create thousands of good-paying jobs, lower energy costs, and expand clean energy. I've strengthened local schools, supported public safety, and delivered results by bringing people to the table.

As a working mom determined to help residents afford to live here, I will stand up to Trump to make California more affordable. I'll focus on reducing the high cost of healthcare, prescription drugs, energy, housing, food, and gas.

I'm ready to deliver on day one, and I'd be honored to earn your vote.
