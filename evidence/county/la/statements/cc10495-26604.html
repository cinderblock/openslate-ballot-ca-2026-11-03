STATEMENT OF ANAIN ROBLES

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
3rd District

CITY OF PALMDALE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 47

Occupation: Business Owner

As a small business owner, I understand the challenges facing local businesses, working families, and our community. For years, I have built a business that creates opportunities, provides jobs, and serves families throughout Palmdale. Every day, I manage budgets, solve problems, and work to ensure my business continues to grow while giving back to the community. Through my work with local schools, youth programs, and community organizations, I have seen the incredible potential of our young people. They deserve more opportunities to learn, grow, and succeed right here in Palmdale. I believe investing in our youth today means building a stronger city for tomorrow. As your City Councilmember, I will work to support small businesses, strengthen our local economy, expand opportunities for youth and ensure city government is transparent, accountable, and responsive to residents. I am committed to listening, working hard, and making thoughtful decisions that put our community first. It would be an honor to earn your trust and your vote.
