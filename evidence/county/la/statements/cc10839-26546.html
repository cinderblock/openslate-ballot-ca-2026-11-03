STATEMENT OF KELVIN DRISCOLL

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
5th District

CITY OF SANTA CLARITA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 41

Occupation: Nonprofit Executive/Educator

For the first time, District 5 will elect its own representative. Every neighborhood deserves a Councilmember who is present, accessible, and committed to listening before leading. That's why I'm running.

As a Nonprofit Executive and graduate-level Educator, I lead one of Los Angeles County's largest nonprofit organizations and teach graduate students at the USC School of Social Work. Every day, I bring people together, solve complex challenges, and help prepare future leaders to serve our communities.

Like so many families, we chose Santa Clarita for its safe neighborhoods, outstanding schools, beautiful parks, and exceptional quality of life.

As your Councilmember, I'll work to keep our neighborhoods safe, improve traffic and mobility, support local businesses, make growth work for residents, and ensure District 5 receives the attention and investment it deserves. Together, we can build on our strengths by creating more opportunities to support local businesses, enjoy our parks and trails, and experience the best of Santa Clarita close to home.

I will be a Councilmember you can reach. I would be honored to earn your vote. When you love your city, you don't just live here – you help build it. Let's build our future together at KelvinforSCV.com.
