STATEMENT OF MANUEL "MANNY" ACOSTA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF SOUTH EL MONTE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 60

Occupation: Professor

I am honored to serve as your councilmember Manny Acosta in our great city of South El Monte. I would like to continue serving our great city as your representative. With your continued support and your vote in the upcoming election on November 3, 2026, we will continue to improve the quality of life of our residents, maintain fiscal responsibility, continue quality and responsible community and economic development, and remain committed to transparency.

During my term, I have served the community with distinction, honor, and humility. I have demonstrated competence and concern for all members of the South El Monte community. I have a proven and strong record of studying the issues impacting our residents and making well-informed decisions. I have and will continue to advocate for small businesses, promote a healthy community, oppose mega warehouse construction, and eliminate cannabis businesses in South El Monte.

I've been married for almost 40 years, raising three wonderful children in South El Monte. I've served this community for many years with South El Monte Little League baseball, Jets football, and South El Monte basketball.

Professionally, I have both a B.S. and M.S. degrees in biochemistry from California State University Los Angeles. I'm now a professor, molding young minds and preparing future leaders at Los Angeles Trade-Technical College, where I teach Chemistry and Biomanufacturing.
