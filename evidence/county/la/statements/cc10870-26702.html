STATEMENT OF RAINA CARRILLO

CANDIDATE FOR MAYOR

CITY OF INGLEWOOD PRIMARY NOMINATING ELECTION

Age: 47

Occupation: Inglewood Community Leader

I believe in the promise of Inglewood. That's why I refuse to stand idle. Born and raised in Inglewood, I graduated from Morningside High School in 1997 and earned my bachelor's at Cal State Dominguez. At 20 years old, I began my career serving our community with Stop the Violence Increase the Peace Foundation in the city of Inglewood. Today, I continue my commitment to empowering our community through prevention and intervention programs for at-risk youth and by leading educational awareness programs for youth and the community. There is so much we could accomplish for our city if we had the right leadership with the right priorities. My commitment to you when elected includes the following: transparency and accountability, support for small businesses, housing security and affordability and improved public safety and social services. As a community leader, wife, and mother, I am running for Mayor to put the people first and expand opportunity for all. We deserve a mayor that prioritize the needs of our community, not outside interests or mega corporations. I humbly ask for your vote to ensure our community has a voice at city hall.
