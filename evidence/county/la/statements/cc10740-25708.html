STATEMENT OF ANTHONY ARMENDAREZ

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 4

NORWALK-LA MIRADA UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

As a lifelong Norwalk resident and parent of four children attending Norwalk-La Mirada schools, I am committed to ensuring every student has the opportunity to succeed in a safe, supportive, and high-quality learning environment.

My service to our community includes working as a Youth Coordinator, serving as a Norwalk Parks and Recreation Commissioner, serving as a former PTA Vice President, and participating as a School Site Council member. As a volunteer with local youth programs, I have brought together families, educators, and community partners to help students reach their full potential.

As the parent of children receiving special education services, I understand the importance of listening to families and ensuring every student, regardless of background or ability, receives the resources they need to thrive. I will advocate for strong academic achievement, fiscal responsibility, safe and supportive schools, meaningful parent engagement, and transparent decision-making.

I believe our schools are strongest when students are at the center of every decision and when parents, teachers, classified employees, administrators, and the community work together. I believe in leadership that is present, accessible, and accountable. I would be honored to earn your vote and the opportunity to serve our students, families, and community with integrity, accountability, and dedication.
