STATEMENT OF CAROL TULA

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

BASSETT UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

I am running for a seat on the Bassett Unified School District Board. I care deeply about our schools, my years of working with young people and families have strengthened my commitment to public service. As a proud community member and former Bassett student, I understand the importance of effective, transparent, and accountable board leadership."

Our district faces significant challenges, including declining enrollment, and educational funding cuts. I believe we can meet these challenges by staying focused on what matters most for our students. My priorities are to:

- Improve student academics and educational opportunities.
- Encourage family and community participation.
- Promote transparency and responsible district leadership.
- Support student well-being, including mental health resources and extracurricular programs.

Every student deserves a safe, supportive, and challenging learning environment.

My candidacy is personal to me. It is rooted in service, not politics. With my experience as a professional, and a parent, I am committed to decisions that put students first so they can feel seen and supported.

"I would be honored to earn your vote. Together, we can build stronger schools, support our teachers and students, and create a brighter future for every child in Bassett Unified."

Respectfully,
Carol Tula
