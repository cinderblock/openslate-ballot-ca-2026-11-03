STATEMENT OF FRANCISCO MAGANA-HUANOSTO

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 3

PALMDALE SCHOOL DISTRICT

As a proud graduate of the Palmdale School District, where I attended school from kindergarten through eighth grade, I understand the lasting impact our schools have on students, families, and our community. The education and support I received here inspired my commitment to public service and my desire to give back by serving on the School Board. I currently work for the Los Angeles Superior Court. I believe the importance of strong schools, dedicated educators, and supportive communities in helping young people succeed. This experience has strengthened my commitment to making thoughtful, student-centered decisions.

I am also preparing to attend law school to further my ability to serve the public and advocate for children, families, and our community. As your School Board member, I will prioritize student achievement, safe and welcoming schools, fiscal responsibility, transparency, and meaningful partnerships with parents, teachers, and the community. I believe every child deserves access to a high-quality education and opportunities to reach their full potential. As someone who grew up in this district, I am committed to helping ensure today's students have the same opportunities to succeed. I respectfully ask for your vote and the opportunity to serve our community.
