STATEMENT OF JAMES BODNAR

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS

CRESCENTA VALLEY WATER DISTRICT

I appreciate the opportunity to represent you as your Director on the Crescenta Valley Water District Board. I work to ensure that our community has reliable, clean, and affordable water. I have served on the Crescenta Valley Water District Board for the last seventeen years and have thirty years of experience in the water industry. I have worked on conservation issues, local water supply projects, water storage programs, infrastructure development, and water quality issues. I have considerable experience in managing water projects and keeping within budgets.

EXPERIENCE COUNTS
- Thirty years water industry experience
- Seventeen years CVWD Director experience
- M.S., Civil Engineering, Stanford University
- B.S., Engineering, California State University Northridge
- Professional Civil Engineer
- Water Treatment and Distribution Operator

GOALS
- Control and reduce costs
- Improve local supplies
- Improve system reliability during outages and emergencies
- Properly support infrastructure
- Provide safe drinking water to our community

I am committed to managing our water supply in an economically responsible way.
Please vote James Bodnar as your representative.
