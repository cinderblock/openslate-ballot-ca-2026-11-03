STATEMENT OF KEOKIA CHILDRESS

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
1st District

CITY OF INGLEWOOD PRIMARY NOMINATING ELECTION

For more than 40 years, Inglewood has been my home. It is where I was raised, built my business, raised my family, and dedicated my life to serving our community.

For the past eight years, I have proudly served as an Inglewood Housing Commissioner, advocating for residents and protecting affordable housing opportunities. As a small business owner for over 10 years co-founder of the Taste of Inglewood, and community leader, I have worked to strengthen our local economy, created opportunities for entrepreneurs, and brought our neighborhoods together through service and civic engagement.

My campaign is built on accountability, transparency, visibility, and compassion. I believe residents deserve a Councilmember who listens, is accessible, and puts people before politics.

I understand the challenges facing District 1, from public safety and housing affordability to supporting small businesses, improving city services, and investing in our youth. I am committed to practical solutions that protect our neighborhoods while creating opportunities for future generations.

I respectfully ask for your vote for Inglewood City Council, District 1, so together we can build a stronger, safer, and more united Inglewood.
