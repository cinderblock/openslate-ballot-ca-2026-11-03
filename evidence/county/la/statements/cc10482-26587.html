STATEMENT OF MARIE LEDEZMA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
3rd District

CITY OF MONTEBELLO GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Paralegal/Business Owner

I am Marie Ledezma, and I am one of you. Fluent in Spanish, a parent, a grandparent, a hard worker (paralegal and small business owner), and proud Montebello resident. For over ten years, I've invested time, money, and energy in this city-volunteering, attending City Council meetings, and holding our leaders accountable through advocacy.

District 3 needs a leader with persistence, poise, and passion- qualities I've developed alongside other strengths. As a senior paralegal, I listen, research, and solve complex problems under pressure. As a business owner, I understand the realities of entrepreneurship. As a mother and grandmother, I know what families and seniors need to thrive. These experiences prepare me to lead with competence and compassion.

If elected, I will lead with transparency and unwavering accountability. I will make decisions that prioritize Montebello first, firmly guided by ethics and law. Advocate for ordinances that work, cut red tape, and expand economic opportunities. I will fight for a beautiful Montebello by protecting our green spaces and rebuilding aging infrastructure. I am committed to building strong partnerships with city leaders, businesses, and first responders, to better serve and protect our community.

I respectfully ask for your vote to serve District 3
