STATEMENT OF KARINA CORDERO

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

LENNOX SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 44

Occupation: Small Business Owner

It has been an honor to serve on the Lennox School District Board of Education over the past five years. As a parent of three children in the district, a graduate of Lennox schools, a small business owner, and an immigrant, I remain deeply committed to the success of every student and family in our community.

During my term, our Board has faced significant challenges, including declining enrollment and financial uncertainty. These realities required us to make difficult but necessary decisions, including the closure of a school, to preserve the long-term fiscal stability of our district.

While these decisions were never easy, they were made with thoughtful deliberation, transparency, and the best interests of our students at heart.

I am proud to have worked alongside dedicated educators, staff, administrators, and fellow board members to strengthen our schools while remaining fiscally responsible.

If reelected, I will continue to advocate for academic excellence, responsible stewardship of public funds, and safe, supportive learning environments where every child has the opportunity to succeed. It would be an honor to continue serving the community that has been my home for more than 40 years.
