STATEMENT OF CAROLINE M. TOROSIS

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF SANTA MONICA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 40

Occupation: Mayor/Consumer Protection Attorney

I'm your Mayor, Caroline Torosis, and I'm asking for your vote to keep Santa Monica moving forward.

Four years ago, our city faced a budget shortfall, a struggling downtown, and concerns about safety and affordability. Today, Santa Monica is finally turning a corner.

We're cutting red tape and waiving fees so local businesses can open and grow. We've reduced violent crime and burglary to their lowest level in over a decade, so residents and visitors feel safe. We're bringing events downtown, so everyone has more reasons to visit, shop, and dine.

I'm a workers' rights attorney who served six years on the Rent Control Board. As your Councilmember, I've expanded legal help for renters facing eviction and delivered financial assistance that kept more than 250 low-income seniors in their homes. For the first time in over 20 years, every funded police officer position is filled. And while Los Angeles' unhoused population has risen, we've reduced the people living on our streets by nearly a fifth.

There's more to do. I'll keep fighting to make Santa Monica safe, clean, affordable and fun. I'd be honored to earn your vote.

Endorsed by Congressmember Ted Lieu, Senator Ben Allen, and Assemblymember Rick Zbur.
