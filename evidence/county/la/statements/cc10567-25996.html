STATEMENT OF KENNETH "KEN" PETERSEN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 3

SANTA CLARITA VALLEY WATER AGENCY

Occupation: Water Board Director

Ken Petersen is a registered professional civil engineer who practiced for over 40 years specializing in water system management, design, construction, and operation. He has the knowledge, experience and qualifications necessary to continue serving you on the SCV Water Board.

As the elected Director of the SCV Water Board, Division 3 and a 24-year resident of the SCV, Ken Petersen understands how vitally important a reliable, safe and affordable water supply is to our valley.

Ken Petersen is committed to:

- Accomplishing SCV Water's mission of providing a reliable supply of high-quality water at a reasonable cost to the Santa Clarita Valley.

- Protecting our local underground water supplies and proactively developing additional imported supplies necessary to serve our community's needs now and into the future.

- Supporting sensible and effective water conservation and recycling programs.

- Ensuring that developers and future water users pay their fair share to expand existing facilities and resources to provide the needed services.

- Conducting all Agency business in an open, honest and accountable manner.

Ken Petersen's education (BS Engineering; MS Civil Engineering), extensive background in water system management, proven leadership and strong personal commitment to the SCV community including being a member of the Rotary Club of SCV, makes him the right choice for SCV Water Director, Division 3.
