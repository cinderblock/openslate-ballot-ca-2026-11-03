STATEMENT OF JULEE BROOKS

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 2

WILLIAM S. HART UNION HIGH SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 47

Occupation: Education Nonprofit Executive

I am running for the Hart Union High School District Board of Trustees because our schools are strongest when guided by leaders who understand our community, listen to families, and put students first. As a Hart District parent with one son at Hart High and another graduating from our district in 2033, I have a long-term stake in our schools and our community's future.

As a first-generation college graduate and the parent of a child with autism, I know every student's path is different, and I would be honored to bring my experience and collaborative approach to serving our students, families, and educators.

I serve on the Board of the Santa Clarita Valley Education Foundation, as Fundraising Chair for the Hart High Show Choir Boosters, and previously as a PTA Treasurer. Professionally, I have spent 25 years serving children and schools, currently leading a nonprofit that partners with school districts to serve 30,000 students a year, and I was appointed back to serve on California's Advisory Committee for Before and Afterschool Programs.

As your Trustee, I will support safe schools, responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars, strong support for teachers, academic standards, student mental health, and expanded college and career pathways.
