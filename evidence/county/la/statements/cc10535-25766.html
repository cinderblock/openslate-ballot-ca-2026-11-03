STATEMENT OF CHRISTINA M. FRAGA-SAENZ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 2

PALMDALE WATER DISTRICT

I've been a resident of District 2 since 1999, founding member and coordinator of Desert Dumping Task Force that has removed 5,000 tons of trash from the Antelope Valley since 2021 and held various commissioner appointments for the City of Palmdale since 2019. I've dedicated my life to serving others from volunteering at food banks to responding to our local emergencies with CERT.

Data centers cannot come at the expense of our drinking water, agricultural heritage, or public health. I will demand strict environmental impact reports, prioritize residents and local farmers and families over tech conglomerates.

I ask for your vote to safeguard our most precious resource. I will work transparently to ensure compliance with California's strict "Human Right to Water" standards without placing an unfair financial burden on it's customers. I will ensure fiscal responsibility and scientific integrity drive every decision.

Let's Build a resilient, healthy future together and last but not least let's get Littlerock Dam OPEN!
