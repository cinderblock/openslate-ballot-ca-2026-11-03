STATEMENT OF DANNY SMITH

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF MALIBU GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 44

Danny Smith is a husband, father, and neighborhood leader. Since losing his home in the Palisades fire, Danny has been on the front lines of the recovery effort as a member of the Malibu Rebuild Task Force and a zone captain for the La Costa and Carbon Beach neighborhoods. His advocacy efforts have included major victories for rebuilding homeowners such as the overturning of a regional water board policy prohibiting the reuse of functioning septic systems on the beachfronts and the defeat of state legislation SB 1229, which would have punished those who need to sell their lots after a disaster. He has been a relentless voice for accountability and transparency within city hall, a bold supporter of property rights, and a champion for Malibu's small business community. Danny vows to fight for and protect the Malibu he loves, to use the full power of the city council to spearhead efforts to empower our recovery, and to safeguard our environment and the shared values expressed in Malibu's mission and vision statements.
