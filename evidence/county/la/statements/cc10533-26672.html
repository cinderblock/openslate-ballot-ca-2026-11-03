STATEMENT OF TATYANA "TANYA" TSIKANOVSKY

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF WEST HOLLYWOOD GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

West Hollywood deserves a City Hall that puts residents first. Common sense should never be political. As a nonprofit executive, I have dedicated myself to serving others, solving problems, and bringing people together. My commitment to community began when I founded my high school's first Gay-Straight Alliance, and it continues today through public service.

I am running because local government should answer to the people of West Hollywood before Sacramento. Development must be done with a progressive mindset while respecting the current residents and their way of life. In doing so, we need to invest in bringing back our vibrant business community by cutting the red tape, and incentivizing growth and investment in our town. Public safety must be our highest priority. Every resident deserves clean streets, safe neighborhoods, thriving parks, and a city where businesses can succeed. We must support law enforcement, address homelessness with compassion and accountability, and reduce unnecessary barriers that make it harder for local businesses to grow.

I will listen, lead with integrity, and always put residents first. Together, we can build a safer, stronger, and more prosperous West Hollywood that works for everyone.

I respectfully ask for your vote.
