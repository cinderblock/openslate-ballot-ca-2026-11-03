STATEMENT OF JULIA RUEDAS

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
3rd District

CITY OF EL MONTE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 45

Occupation: Educator/Councilwoman

As a lifelong El Monte resident, I am running for City Council District 3 to continue serving our community because we deserve experienced leadership that puts people first!

My experience as an Educator, Public Servant, and Policy Analyst has prepared me to build on the progress we've made through honesty, collaboration, and accountability.

Together, we've strengthened neighborhoods, supported local businesses, expanded park improvements, improved public safety and infrastructure, and invested in families and future generations.

I believe the best decisions come from listening to residents, working collaboratively, and finding solutions that reflect our shared values and move our community forward.

As your Councilwoman, I will continue taking bold action to:

1. Public Safety: Strengthen community policing and neighborhood security.
2. Reduce Homelessness and Expand Affordable Housing: Through supportive services and stable housing opportunities.
3. Infrastructure Improvements: Invest in safer streets, better roads, and cleaner public spaces.
4. Business Growth: Attract new businesses, support small businesses, and create jobs.
5. Invest in Families: Senior services, workforce development, and reopen Pioneer Park.

I respectfully ask for your support. TOGETHER, we can continue creating meaningful change:

For our People. For our Future. For El Monte.

Vote JULIA RUEDAS for El Monte City Council, District 3.
