STATEMENT OF PAUL SEO

PARTY PREFERENCE: DEMOCRATIC

MEMBER OF THE STATE ASSEMBLY, 66th DISTRICT

Paul Seo is a corruption prosecutor, Army Veteran, and Mayor of Rancho Palos Verdes, running for State Assembly to keep our neighborhoods safe and deliver real results for working families.

Raised in Torrance by immigrant parents who ran a small business, Paul attended local public schools, graduated from South High School, and later the United States Military Academy at West Point. He proudly served his country as a U.S. Army officer, earned his law degree, and became a prosecutor to keep serving his community.

As a prosecutor, Paul has taken on corruption in all forms, whether it's from public officials, illegal gun dealers, or the polluters responsible for the 2021 Huntington Beach oil spill. Paul is protecting our coastline, communities and quality of life.

In the Assembly, Paul will crack down on corporate price gouging that drives up utility bills and the cost of gas and groceries, tackle the homelessness crisis with real accountability and results, clean up backroom deals so our government works for everyone, and keep our streets and neighborhoods safe.

Paul will deliver affordability for working families by bringing in good-paying jobs, supporting small businesses, and investing in first responders to keep us safe.
