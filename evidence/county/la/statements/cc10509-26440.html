CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF SAN GABRIEL GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 51

Occupation: Councilmember/Property Manager

Since 2022, I've been PROUDLY serving as YOUR City Councilmember and I am your current Mayor! I'm a Property Manager, a decades-long San Gabriel Resident, and active in numerous professional, cultural, and civic organizations.

It's obvious that I care about San Gabriel! It's a great place to live, and I'm doing my best to improve it every day.

Just a few of my accomplishments so far:

1. Using my Accounting background to help pull the City out of a bad fiscal situation and improve the financial stability of the City so that we now have a balanced budget and healthy reserves.

2. Bringing our Police Officers and Firefighters up to FULL Staffing levels to ensure the SAFETY of San Gabriel's Residents and Businesses.

3. Improving the Business-friendliness of San Gabriel so that it is a "Destination City" sought out by visitors worldwide who come here to enjoy San Gabriel's unique culture.

4. Starting a new Farmer's Market that is popular and thriving.

I have the EXPERIENCE, knowledge, and passion to ensure that San Gabriel continues to prosper.

Together, we can CONTINUE to improve the San Gabriel Community.

Please vote to re-elect Eric L. Chan!
