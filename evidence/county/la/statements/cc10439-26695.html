STATEMENT OF FREDDY PUZA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF CULVER CITY GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Mayor/Education Director

As your current Mayor, I am honored to seek re-election to the Culver City Council. As the city's first openly LGBTQ+ Mayor, I have worked to make Culver City more affordable, accountable, and functional for the people who live and work here.

I championed the Affordable Housing Trust Fund, launched the Affordability Subcommittee, and expanded access to childcare, helping working families, seniors, renters, and small businesses stay in the city they call home.

I also led on investments in the Mobile Crisis Team, giving residents access to trained mental health professionals during crises while ensuring our emergency response systems remain strong and reliable.

I delivered small business microgrants and fee relief to help local entrepreneurs recover and grow.

Under my leadership, City Council adopted our first balanced budget in three years while investing in the basics that matter most: repairing sidewalks, resurfacing roads, and expanding tree canopy maintenance to make our neighborhoods greener and more livable.

I'm proud to be endorsed by Congresswoman Sydney Kamlager-Dove, Supervisor Holly Mitchell, State Senator Lola Smallwood-Cuevas, Assemblymember Isaac Bryan, the Sierra Club, and dozens of other elected officials and community leaders.

I'm running for re-election to keep Culver City affordable, safe, and welcoming for everyone.
