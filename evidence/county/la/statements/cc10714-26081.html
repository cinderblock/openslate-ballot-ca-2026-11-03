STATEMENT OF EVELYN MENDEZ AVDALYAN

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 2

LOS NIETOS SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Legal Assistant

Evelyn M. Avdalyan

Candidate for the Los Nietos School District Governing Board

I am honored to seek re-election to the Los Nietos School District Governing Board.

For the past 25 years, I have proudly called Whittier home. My husband and I have one son who attended schools in the Los Nietos School District. Throughout his education, I was actively involved in our school community, serving for many years on the Parent Teacher Club and the School Site Council.

I believe every child deserves a strong educational foundation, and I remain committed to supporting the success, well-being, and future of every student in our district. If re-elected, I will continue to serve with integrity, listen to our community, and work diligently to ensure that Los Nietos students receive the quality education they deserve.

Thank you for your trust, your support and your vote.
