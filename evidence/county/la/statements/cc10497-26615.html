STATEMENT OF CYNTHIA "CYNDI" GARCIA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
5th District

CITY OF PALMDALE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 61

Occupation: Executive Assistant

For nearly 40 years, the Antelope Valley has been my home. I am the proud daughter of a highly decorated military veteran who retired after 23 years of service.

As a single mother, I worked hard to raise my two sons here, both of whom are college graduates. I have also been blessed with a granddaughter. Like so many families, I understand the importance of living in a community where people feel safe, supported, and proud to call home.

My career began as a legal secretary, commuting daily to Encino and the Mid-Wilshire area. For the past 18 years, I have served as Executive Assistant to the Superintendent of the Palmdale School District, gaining experience in leadership, collaboration, and public service.

I have volunteered with the Chamber of Commerce; Christmas parades; PTA; Football, Hockey, and Swim Boosters; Community drives; and Measure AV Oversight Committee because I believe giving back strengthens our community.

I am running for Palmdale City Council because I believe our city can do better. I will work to improve public safety, support responsible spending, expand opportunities for our youth, and help local businesses succeed. Together, we can build a safer, stronger Palmdale for generations to come.
