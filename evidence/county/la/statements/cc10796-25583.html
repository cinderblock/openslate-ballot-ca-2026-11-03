STATEMENT OF JESSICA MARTINEZ

PARTY PREFERENCE: REPUBLICAN

MEMBER OF THE STATE ASSEMBLY, 56th DISTRICT

Do NOT vote for me if you want:

High taxes

High crime

High cost of living

Homeless men, women and children on the street

Boys in girls' sports and locker rooms

Vote for me if you want:

Low taxes

Criminals off the streets

Clean streets

Good jobs

To stop the radical agenda in schools

There is a proverb that says, "When the wicked are in power, the people groan." Californians are groaning under the weight of high taxes that fund fraud and waste. Over-regulation has made housing unaffordable. We groan when we see houses burnt to the ground and a government that is unresponsive. We voted to stop criminals and our California government overruled us.

I will bring back high paying jobs. I will make housing affordable again by encouraging entrepreneurs and builders to do what they do best: create wealth, provide opportunities and build homes.

During my time as a city council member, I worked to ensure that the homeless had a place to stay, were of the sidewalks and out of the parks. I worked to reduce crime by building our police force. As a board member of the Puente Hills Habitat Authority, I was instrumental in securing the 'Heli-tank' that firefighting helicopters are using to quickly extinguish fires. I worked to ensure that our city workers were not forced to take the COVID vaccination.

We can have a society that benefits everyone. Si se puede.

Vote for a better tomorrow.
