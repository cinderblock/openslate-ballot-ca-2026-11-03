STATEMENT OF MARTINA RODRIGUEZ

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

LYNWOOD UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Mother/Community Organizer

My name is Martina Rodriguez. As a proud Lynwood resident and homeowner for over four decades, my commitment to our city and its schools runs deep. Three of my four children are graduates of the Lynwood Unified School District, and my youngest is now a freshman at Lynwood High School.

I previously served as an LUSD Board Member from 2001 to 2005, and my mission hasn't changed. As a community organizer, I have spent two decades showing up at every board meeting, working with families from the outside to cut through bureaucracy, and carrying our community's voice into rooms where decisions get made.

Our district faces real challenges today, and our children deserve leadership that meets this moment head-on. I am running for LUSD Governing Board Member again to make sure our district delivers the resources, strong academic programs, and support our students truly deserve. Every family in Lynwood deserves equity because a quality education is a right, not a privilege. I know what it takes to right this ship, because I have done it before.

I am ready to listen, lead, and fight for every student in Lynwood. I humbly ask for your vote.
