STATEMENT OF SIMONE ZULU

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

PALMDALE SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 73

Occupation: Retired Educator

For 46 years, I had the privilege of serving as a preschool educator, dedicating my career to helping children build a strong foundation for lifelong learning.

Along the way, I worked closely with families and educators to create safe, inclusive, and nurturing environments where every child could succeed. I believe in educating the whole child and know that students thrive when families, teachers, and schools work together. As a lifelong advocate for holistic, multicultural, and multilingual education, I am committed to ensuring every student feels valued, respected, and empowered to reach their full potential.

Our community's diversity is one of its greatest strengths. I will continue to champion inclusive policies that honor every voice, support academic excellence, and prepare all students for a bright future.

It would be an honor to serve on the Board of Trustees and continue giving back to the children, families, and community that have meant so much to me. I am committed to listening, leading with integrity, and making decisions that put students first.
