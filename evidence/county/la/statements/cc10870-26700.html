STATEMENT OF ARTURO MARTINEZ

CANDIDATE FOR MAYOR

CITY OF INGLEWOOD PRIMARY NOMINATING ELECTION

I am a lifelong Inglewood resident of 47 years, a homeowner, an Inglewood High School graduate (98), and a graduate of CSULB with a Bachelor of Science degree. A devoted husband and father of two, 11&5. I have worked in technology and business, managing complex budgets, teams, and systems for over 25 years. Inglewood is my home. I have watched our city grow under the bright lights of a new entertainment district. I believe that growth must lift the longtime families who built this community rather than push them out. As mayor I will fight for affordable housing, tax relief, and generational wealth workshops so our elders and children can stay rooted here. I will demand fiscal fairness so big venues contribute their fair share instead of shifting the burden onto residents. I will restore real public safety with more patrols and full police body-camera accountability so every resident can trust the officers who protect us.

I am not a career politician. I am one of you, a neighbor, a father, a homeowner, and a fighter.

Together we will keep our roots deep and our future bright.
