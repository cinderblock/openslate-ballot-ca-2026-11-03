STATEMENT OF MICHAEL BRAUN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 2

WALNUT VALLEY WATER DISTRICT

Age: 51

Occupation: Professor of Criminal Justice

Water is one of our community's most essential public services. I am running for the Walnut Valley Water District Board of Directors, Division 2, to help ensure that every household and business receives safe, reliable water at a fair and affordable rate.

As a professor of criminal justice with a Ph.D., my work requires me to evaluate evidence, examine complex systems, promote public accountability, and make decisions based on facts rather than politics. I will bring that same independent, analytical approach to the Water District.

My priorities are responsible budgeting, transparent decision-making, long-term infrastructure planning, and careful preparation for drought, emergencies, and changing water-supply conditions. I will ask hard questions before rate increases or major expenditures are approved and support investments that improve reliability, reduce waste, and protect future generations.

Public agencies work best when residents understand how decisions are made and know their concerns are being heard. I will listen, communicate clearly, and put the interests of Division 2 residents first.

I respectfully ask for your vote.
