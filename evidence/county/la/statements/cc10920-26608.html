STATEMENT OF ARTURO FLORES

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
5th District

CITY OF HUNTINGTON PARK GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Huntington Park has always been a city built on hard work, family, and resilience. During challenging times, our community has shown that when we stand together, we are stronger than any obstacle we face.

As your Councilman, I have worked to ensure our city responds with compassion, leadership, and respect for the constitutional rights of every resident. I will continue advocating for policies that protect our community from unlawful immigration enforcement actions, strengthen trust between residents and local government, and ensure families are treated with dignity and respect.

Our future also depends on investing in the people who call Huntington Park home. I remain committed to supporting public safety, revitalizing Pacific Boulevard to create jobs and economic opportunity, expanding housing options for working families, seniors, veterans, and young professionals, and developing citywide childcare programs that help parents succeed while giving every child a strong start.

The challenges we face will not divide us–they will bring us together. By supporting one another, protecting our neighbors, and investing in our future, we can build a safer, stronger, and more united Huntington Park where every family has the opportunity to thrive.

I respectfully ask for your vote so we can continue moving our city forward–together.
