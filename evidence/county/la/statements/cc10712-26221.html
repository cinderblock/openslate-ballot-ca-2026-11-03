STATEMENT OF ELIANA TAPIA

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 1

LITTLE LAKE CITY SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 56

Occupation: Retired Police Detective

My name is Eliana Tapia, and I am running for Little Lake School District Governing Board, Trustee Area 1.

I have proudly called Santa Fe Springs home for the past 29 years. I am a U.S. Navy veteran, a retired Police Detective, and the proud mother of three young boys. I am actively involved in our community by volunteering at my children's school and local youth sports.

My decision to run for the School Board was inspired by the recent 10-day teachers' strike. That experience made it clear that our community deserves greater transparency, open communication, and a stronger voice in the decisions that impact our schools. We must carefully examine district spending and future funding to ensure the Little Lake School District remains financially strong and sustainable for generations to come.

As your Board Member, I will ask the tough questions, make informed decisions, and clearly communicate those decisions to our community. I believe accountability and transparency are essential to earning the trust of the families we serve.

I am honored to be the only Area 1 candidate endorsed by the Little Lake Education Association teachers, and I respectfully ask for your support on Election Day, November 3.

Together, we can build a stronger future for every student in the Little Lake City School District.
