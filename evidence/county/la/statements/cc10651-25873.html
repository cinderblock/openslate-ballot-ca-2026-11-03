STATEMENT OF SHILPA SHINDE-GARG

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

LA CAÑADA UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Shilpa Shinde-Garg, MD, is a longtime La Cañada Flintridge resident, physician, and dedicated community volunteer committed to education and public service. She and her husband, orthopedic surgeon Dr. Rishi Garg, have raised their two children in the La Cañada Unified School District, where both have thrived.

Dr. Shinde-Garg's passion for education began at UC Berkeley, where she tutored underserved students through Break the Cycle. She later taught science in South Central Los Angeles before earning her medical degree from the USC Keck School of Medicine in 2005. She has practiced Internal Medicine ever since.

Her community leadership includes helping lead the successful Measure LCF campaign to secure long-term funding for local schools. She currently serves as President of the LCHS Flag Football Foundation and previously served as President of the LCHS Spartan Football Foundation, a director on the LCHS Boosters, and a stadium turf advisor for LCUSD.

As a physician, parent, and community leader, Dr. Shinde-Garg believes strong public schools are the foundation of a thriving community. She is committed to academic excellence, fiscal responsibility, student well-being, and strong partnerships with teachers, families, and the community to ensure every student has the opportunity to succeed.
