STATEMENT OF V. JESSE SMITH

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
5th District

CITY OF PALMDALE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 60

Occupation: Union Representative

I am excited to run for Palmdale City Council to offer bold, principled leadership that works—leadership that listens, takes action, and unites us around a safer, stronger, and more prosperous Palmdale.

Palmdale needs leadership that builds a brighter future for all, including giving residents the right to elect their mayor collectively instead of having one appointed selectively.

As your council member, I will prioritize transparency, accountability, and community input. Through our B.E.S.T. model—Building culture and community, Economic vitality and innovation, Social unity, and Transportation and infrastructure development—I will support small businesses, public safety, youth activities, seniors, housing, and veterans while working with others to advance shared solutions.

Together, we can cultivate a city that is inclusive, resilient, and forward-thinking. Through leadership that works, we can transform Palmdale Blvd, activate our Palmdale Airport, and ensure that everyone's rights are protected under the law.

I am passionate about serving you and dedicated to building our community.

I humbly ask for your support to serve as your council member in District 5 and bring leadership that works for all in Palmdale.
