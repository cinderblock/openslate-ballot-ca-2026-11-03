STATEMENT OF AL AUSTIN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 3

WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICT OF SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA

Water is our most vital resource, crucial for daily life. Southern California's future is uncertain as traditional water sources like the Colorado River and Sierra snowpacks become less dependable due to climate change.

It would be an honor to serve as your representative on the Water Replenishment District governing board to protect our groundwater and ensure a sustainable future for our communities.

As a former three-term city councilmember and current Public Utilities Commissioner in Long Beach, I have provided clean, safe, and reliable water services to nearly half a million residents for over a decade.

My priorities as your next WRD Director will include:

- Keeping rates affordable
- Making long-term sustainable investments
- Building groundwater capacity and resilience
- Creating local jobs
- Partnering with community stakeholders

I will champion smart water policies and solutions that connect our communities and guarantee reliable water for future generations.

Together, we can create a sustainable water future for Southern California.

Vote for Al Austin for Water Replenishment District, Division 3.
