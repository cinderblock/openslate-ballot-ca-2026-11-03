STATEMENT OF VANESSA HARO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF COMMERCE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 44

Occupation: Senior Quality Engineer

My name is Vanessa Haro and I am running for City of Commerce city council to bring a community-first focused perspective, where the residents' voice, health and safety are top priority.

I am a fourth-generation Commerce resident and grew up participating in many of Commerce's opportunities, including sports, swim lessons, Miss Commerce and Washington DC trips. I run a non-profit karate dojo out of the St. Marcellinus Church in Commerce where I have the honor of training many of our residents and was honored on the Commerce Wall of Champions.

I plan is to be a consistent advocate for the residents. My focus includes addressing and getting ahead of any chemical, or hazardous issues that can threaten our community. I plan to bring financial resources to the community to help us keep our homes and investments and bring new resources and opportunity to legacy residents wanting to purchase homes in Commerce.

Our community is the reason Commerce is the model city. I want the physical and financial health of our residents and businesses to flourish and thrive in Commerce, while preserving our tight-knit community and bringing back the legacy programs and clubs that originally made us the model city.
