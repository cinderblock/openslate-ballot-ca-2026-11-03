STATEMENT OF RAYMOND VERGARA

CANDIDATE FOR CITY TREASURER

CITY OF HAWTHORNE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 58

Occupation: Small Business Owner

Background:
Familyman and Business Owner
B.A., Business Administration, Cal State Los Angeles
Master of Business Administration, University of Phoenix
Endorsed by Mayor Vargas, Mayor Pro-Tem Johnson, Councilmember Valentine (ret.)

Statement:
This election will determine if we protect the City's finances, or return to the mismanagement of the past. We must focus on safeguarding our finances, rather than debate increasing treasurer's pay, perks, and pensions. PLEASE elect Raymond Vergara and send a committed voice to City Hall!

If elected, Raymond will:

Maintain Strong Financial Reserves
We must be prepared for an emergency. This means allocating increased levels of funds into our reserve accounts, in order to avoid raising taxes unexpectedly.

Protect Taxpayer Dollars
Safeguarding the City's money is crucial. Priority will be given to responsible budgeting and enacting careful financial oversight, to ensure City services to residents.

Increase Transparency
Residents need to know how City money is spent. Particular focus will be on making Hawthorne's budget reports easier for residents to understand and access online.

Improve City Investments
We need to increase the City's return on investments. By making proper allocations which are safe, we can maximize returns while minimizing risks.
