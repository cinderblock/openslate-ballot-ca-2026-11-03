STATEMENT OF LUIS ROA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF HAWAIIAN GARDENS GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 41

Occupation: Incumbent/Payroll Specialist

Four years ago, you placed your trust in me to serve on the Hawaiian Gardens City Council. It has been an honor to earn that trust every day, and I am grateful for the opportunity to represent the community my family and I are proud to call home.

Together, we've accomplished a great deal. During the COVID-19 pandemic, our city continued providing essential services while supporting residents through an unprecedented time. Since then, we've invested in street beautification, infrastructure improvements, the renovation of our community gymnasium, expanded community programs, and other projects that will benefit residents for years to come. We've also remained fiscally responsible, carefully managing taxpayer dollars while protecting the city's long-term financial stability.

Serving on the City Council has allowed me to listen to residents, understand different perspectives, and work alongside my fellow councilmembers and city staff to solve problems. I believe the best decisions come from listening first and working together.

It would be an honor to continue serving Hawaiian Gardens. Thank you for your trust, support, and the privilege of representing our community.
