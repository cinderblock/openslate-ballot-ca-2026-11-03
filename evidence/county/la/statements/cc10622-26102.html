STATEMENT OF JUDITH MANOUCHEHRI

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

BEVERLY HILLS UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

It has been an honor to serve on the Beverly Hills Unified School District Board of Education and as Board President. As a BHUSD graduate and parent of three children in our schools, I have worked to ensure every decision puts students first and strengthens our schools for the future.

During my tenure, we have raised academic achievement, expanded educational opportunities, strengthened college and career readiness, and supported our exceptional teachers and staff. I have championed educational excellence while advancing responsible, developmentally appropriate approaches to technology and preparing students for a rapidly changing world.

I have prioritized student safety and security, strengthening protections across our campuses and working to ensure every student can learn in a safe and supportive environment.

I have also remained committed to fiscal responsibility, accountability, and strong oversight of taxpayer dollars. We have brought greater discipline to District finances, strengthened our construction program, and made significant progress completing the modernization of our schools and delivering facilities our community has long been promised.

There is more work ahead. I am running to continue raising academic standards, strengthening safety, protecting taxpayers, supporting educators, modernizing our schools, and providing every student with an exceptional education and opportunities to succeed.
