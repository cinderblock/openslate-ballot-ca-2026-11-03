STATEMENT OF SERGIO ROBERTO MORTARA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF HAWTHORNE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 42

Occupation: School Board Member

Background

- Parent & Homeowner
- Board Member, Hawthorne School District
- Business Owner, Continental Gourmet
- Endorsed by Mayor Vargas and Mayor Pro-Tem Johnson

Statement

Hawthorne can become an even greater city! This election, we can move Hawthorne forward or continue with the divisive politics of the past. I believe we accomplish more by focusing on solutions, working together, and addressing the issues that matter most to our residents.

As a parent, business owner, and longtime School Board Member, I believe the best decisions are made by listening to residents, working together, and focusing on practical solutions.

Public Safety

Everyone deserves to feel safe. I will support strengthening public safety, improving school safety, addressing retail theft, and working with law enforcement to enhance the quality of life throughout Hawthorne.

Address Homelessness

We need practical, compassionate solutions that connect people with services while keeping our neighborhoods clean, safe, and welcoming.

Solve Our Parking Challenges

I will work with residents and stakeholders to develop practical, long-term parking solutions for our community.

Affordable Housing

We need thoughtful planning that expands affordable housing opportunities while protecting our neighborhoods and supporting those who need assistance.

I respectfully ask for your vote and the opportunity to serve on the City Council.
