STATEMENT OF VARUN "SUNNY" TAMRAKAR

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 2

LAWNDALE SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 39

Occupation: Educator/Parent/Father

As a South Bay father, I'm passionate about improving LESD. My girls went to Mitchell's preschool, Smith Elementary, and currently attend Addams Middle. If elected, I would bring firsthand knowledge of families' experiences in the district today. Since 2019, I've been heavily involved as a volunteer and elected parent leader, and I bring 20 years experience as an educator. With half our students performing below average in math and reading, I will passionately push for more rigorous academics, exciting classroom experiences, and robust family support.

From 2020, I was Smith's PTA President, Parliamentarian and Treasurer. District-wide, I was the Council PTA President and District Parent Advisory Committee President. Last year, I successfully advocated for a new PTA at Addams and a new district program for gifted/high achieving students.

Every day, as Academic Services Director of a nonprofit learning center, I directly support struggling and low-income students. I provide reading/math, Advanced Placement class, SAT instruction and help students apply to college.

A vote for Sunny puts a current parent/educator in the boardroom. I know how students learn and will be a passionate advocate for parents and students, determined to push LESD to do its best for all students.
