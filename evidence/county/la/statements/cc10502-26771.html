STATEMENT OF DENNIS J. MCNAMARA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF EDUCATION,
District No. 7

PASADENA UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 72

Occupation: Retired Finance Executive

Our primary District-wide objective is achieving universal academic proficiency across core subjects. Although we face serious challenges, this objective must drive every decision. Indeed, it is our obligation to the children and families of generations to come.

We must set a vision with our full community that contemplates the next 25 to 50 years, not just the next budget cycle. This will allow us to stabilize our campus infrastructure, increase funding predictability, enrich curricula, and truly focus on classroom resourcing and results.

My skill set fits this challenge. I bring decades of successful executive leadership. As an investment manager, I was transparently accountable for client results. As a senior executive, I was responsible for staffing, systems, and meeting budgets. Highly visible. Highly accountable.

My commitment to public education is deep. We sent our children to public schools, and I have served as a second grade math tutor at Washington Elementary for the past three years. I chair the Citizens' Oversight Committee for PUSD's $1.4 billion school modernization program.

I earned a BA and MBA from the University of Chicago. I am a Chartered Financial Analyst.

Thank you for your support.
