STATEMENT OF OLLIE CANTOS

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
4th District

CITY OF WEST COVINA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: West Covina Councilman/Attorney

Government exists to serve the people and not the other way around. As fellow residents, this is OUR community. We collectively shop at the same stores, eat at the same restaurants, send our kids to the same schools, enjoy the same parks and open spaces, and otherwise live our daily lives ... together.

Four years ago, you sent me to City Hall to be your voice. I have been working tirelessly ever since, standing up for commonsense solutions, win or lose.

Because of our collective efforts, solidly built on trust, mutual respect, and LISTENING to residents, "Promises made, promises kept."

Lower homelessness, better parks, strong police and fire departments, successful collaboration with schools, thriving businesses, improved infrastructure, support for seniors, higher volunteerism via community service, and more.

Best of all, it is not because of what *I* did but rather because of what WE did! Driven by a genuine spirit of caring, I KNOW we can do more.

Our town is great, because we MAKE it that way. Driven and inspired by what we KNOW we can become, we may rise to be our best, building a future of which we may all be proud.
