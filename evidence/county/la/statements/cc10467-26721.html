STATEMENT OF BRANDON GEORGE MYERS

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF EDUCATION,
Trustee Area 3

INGLEWOOD UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

My name is Brandon George Myers and I am seeking re-election to the Inglewood Unified School District Board of Education to continue building a stronger future for our students, families and schools.

I was first elected in November 2020 to represent Seat 3 and have worked to improve educational opportunities, strengthen fiscal accountability and invest in safe, modern learning environments. During my tenure, I have supported the successful completion of the Bennet Kew Tk-8 expansion which entailed adding six new classrooms and a multipurpose space, as well as the District's Child Development Center improving access to high-quality early childhood education for Inglewood families.

As a longtime resident, I bring more than eight years of experience in education and over fourteen years of serving children and families. Additionally, I founded the Inglewood South Bay Democratic Club and have dedicated my public service to advancing equity, opportunity and community engagement.

While on the Board, I have also supported responsible governance, fiscal stewardship and the progress towards restoring local control while advocating for accountability from leaders in the decision-making process.

I respectfully ask for your vote to continue strengthening Inglewood Unified and ensure every student has the opportunity to thrive.
