STATEMENT OF RUBEN TORRES

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
1st District

CITY OF DIAMOND BAR GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Banker/Planning Commissioner

I am proud to have called Diamond Bar home for more than 40 years. After attending local schools and earning my bachelor's and MBA degrees from the University of La Verne, my wife and I chose to raise our family here because of Diamond Bar's safe neighborhoods, outstanding schools, beautiful open spaces, and strong sense of community.

For more than 20 years, I have built a career in banking and financial services, leading teams, helping organizations grow, and making sound financial decisions. My commitment to our community includes serving as a Diamond Bar Planning Commissioner, a board member of the Diamond Bar Community Foundation, and a board member of the San Gabriel Valley Regional Chamber of Commerce. I have also dedicated more than two decades to supporting local youth sports programs, serving in volunteer leadership roles that help provide opportunities for our young athletes.

As a 17-year member of the Diamond Point HOA Board, including serving as its current President, I have worked to expand community programs, improve communication, and promote transparency and accountability.

If elected, my priorities will be public safety, responsible economic development, maintaining our infrastructure, addressing homelessness, and ensuring residents always have a voice. Most importantly, I will listen, lead with integrity, and work to keep Diamond Bar a community we are proud to call home.
