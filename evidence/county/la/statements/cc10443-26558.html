STATEMENT OF MARIA ZARAGOZA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
2nd District

CITY OF DOWNEY GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 50

Occupation: Fiscal Analyst/Mother

As a Downey resident for over 20 years, I have proudly raised my two children in this community. They are graduates of Downey's public schools, and our family has experienced firsthand what makes our city such a special place to call home.

As a hardworking mother and Fiscal Analyst, I understand the importance of responsible budgeting, transparency, and making every taxpayer dollar count.

I am running for Downey City Council because District 2 deserves leadership that puts residents first. Our community needs a representative who listens, leads with integrity, and focuses on the issues that matter most.

My priorities are keeping our neighborhoods safe, repairing streets and infrastructure, supporting local small businesses, protecting seniors and families, and restoring transparency and accountability at City Hall.

I believe every resident deserves to be heard. Together, we can strengthen our local economy, preserve the quality of life that makes Downey a great place to live, and ensure our city government is responsive, responsible, and focused on serving the people.

I would be honored to earn your vote. Together, we can build a stronger, safer, and more accountable Downey for current and future generations. Thank you, and God bless everyone.
