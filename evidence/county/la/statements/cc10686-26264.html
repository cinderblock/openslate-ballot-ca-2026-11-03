STATEMENT OF JACQUELINE YVETTE CASTAÑON

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 4

EL MONTE UNION HIGH SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 39

Occupation: Educator

I am JACQUELINE CASTAÑON, a Special Education educator with 14 years serving students with special needs, leader with the California School Employees Association (CSEA), and proud parent of four. My children and I are products of our public schools. I am a lifelong El Monte resident, attended Miramonte Elementary, Kranz Intermediate, and graduated from El Monte Union High School District. I earned my Associate degree in Administration of Justice from Rio Hondo College and am currently attending Grand Canyon University for my teaching degree.

My priorities are:

1. SCHOOL SAFETY: Absolute protection from sexual abuse with zero-tolerance and accountability, plus safety officers, safe classrooms, clean restrooms, bully-free campuses, and mental health support.
2. SPECIAL EDUCATION: Highly qualified teachers, ongoing professional development, smaller caseloads, inclusive classrooms, and transition plans to college, career, and independent living.
3. PARENT ENGAGEMENT: Strengthen parent advisory councils and English learner committees, improve two-way communication, and expand translation, childcare, and evening meetings.
4. CAREER AND TECHNICAL EDUCATION (CTE): Expand hands-on programs to good-paying jobs: Construction, Carpentry, Electrician, Refrigeration, Industrial Maintenance, Welding, Mechanic, Plumbing, Heating and Air Conditioning.

I respectfully ask for your support. Vote JACQUELINE CASTAÑON for El Monte Union High School District.
