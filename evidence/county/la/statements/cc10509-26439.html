STATEMENT OF DENISE MENCHACA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF SAN GABRIEL GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Councilwoman/Accountant

I am honored to serve as your Councilwoman and proud of what we have accomplished together to make San Gabriel a safer, stronger community. I respectfully ask for your vote to continue our progress together.

As an accountant, protecting your tax dollars is my personal mission. Six years ago, when the State Auditor identified our city as high risk, I helped lead our financial recovery. Through fiscally responsible leadership, we earned a clean audit, achieved seven consecutive years of financial growth, and built reserves to nearly 42 percent. That discipline allows us to invest in the services our residents deserve.

We have prioritized public safety by investing in our Police and Fire Departments, including a new fire engine. We have supported our businesses and brought the Farmers Market back to the Mission District.

We have strengthened our arts and cultural community, starting with our historic Mission Playhouse, improved our streets, parks, and services for older adults, and moved forward with the long-awaited Veterans Memorial.

There is still more to do. I will continue investing in our youth, families, older adults, and businesses while protecting the financial stability that keeps San Gabriel strong.

I ask for your vote. Learn more at DeniseMenchaca.com.
