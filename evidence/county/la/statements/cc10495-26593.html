STATEMENT OF LAURA BETTENCOURT

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
3rd District

CITY OF PALMDALE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 63

Occupation: Councilmember/Crime Analyst

Keep Palmdale Moving Forward!

It's been an honor to serve as your Palmdale Councilmember for District 3.

I am a mother, crime analyst, public servant, union member, and a 28-year Palmdale resident who loves her City.

I am proud of my efforts to:

- Create 1000s of diverse jobs with companies such as Lockheed, Northrop, Amazon, and Trader Joes.
- Expand educational choices such as the Palmdale Aerospace Academy
- Have Palmdale recognized as one of California's safest cities.
- Developed relationships on both sides of the aisle to obtain $10,000,000 dollars in federal funding and $32,000,000 in grant money to help fund projects in the City of Palmdale.
- Voted to let the public decide if they want a direct elected mayor or keep the council rotation of Mayor.

Our shared focus should be on:

- Improving our streets, parks, pools and community spaces
- Providing resources for women, children, veterans, and senior citizens
- Expanding youth programs
- Improving our local Animal Shelter
- Beautify Palmdale with public art and new landscaping
- Addressing homelessness and mental health issues

I am a proven leader who recognizes the benefits and strengths of diversity, and I have a proven record of building bridges and uniting people.

Please Vote Laura Bettencourt!!
