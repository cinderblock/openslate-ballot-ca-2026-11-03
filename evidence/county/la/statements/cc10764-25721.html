STATEMENT OF JOHN PAUL DRAYER

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 2

BELLFLOWER UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Hi, Bellflower! Four Generations of my family and I attended Bellflower Schools. I graduated from Bellflower High in 1980 and went on to Cerritos College (A.A.), CSULB (B.A.), & CSUDH with my Masters in Education, CTE, Teaching & Counseling Credentials. In 2012 & 2014 Bellflower residents elected me to the Cerritos College Board. I have over 35 years of experience in Education.

I was instrumental in requesting a full state audit of Bellflower Unified, which uncovered millions being mismanaged while Special Education, Regular Education & Gifted students lacked basic funding.

Platform:
School Safety: Implementing proactive Safety Measures at all schools to learn without fear.

Student Success: Fostering innovative programs to lead to College, Career, & Vocational success!

Balanced Budget: Work together with all Stakeholders to create a 3 year Balanced Budget to prevent a State takeover of the District.

Affordability: Working to make quality education affordable for all Bellflower Families, especially before & afterschool programs.
