STATEMENT OF WAYNE M. KALLIOMAA

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 1

EASTSIDE UNION SCHOOL DISTRICT

I was appointed to the school board in February, being selected from a pool of 9 candidates during the interview process. During my tenure I have voted to improve school safety, increase teacher pay, and to ensure that our students with special needs have the dedicated help they need. I also voted to require all law enforcement agencies, including ICE, to have the proper documentation when entering our schools or accessing our student's records. Students need to feel safe and secure in their classroom.

I am a strong advocate for education. I believe America is a better country when its people are well educated. Whether a student's path is learning a trade or going to college, it starts with the basic skills learned in our classrooms. I am a 37-year resident of Lancaster and have been married for 32 years. My wife and I have raised 2 beautiful girls. Both attended Eastside Schools and are now flourishing in college. I ask for your vote.
