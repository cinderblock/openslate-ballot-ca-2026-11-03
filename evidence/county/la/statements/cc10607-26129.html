STATEMENT OF BRIAN FERRER

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5
(Unexpired term ending December 8, 2028)

ABC UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT SPECIAL ELECTION

Age: 43

Occupation: Registered Nurse/Cerritos Commissioner

Hello Neighbors! I am a proud product and graduate of the ABC Unified School District. I attended from Kindergarten to the 12th Grade. I pride myself on being a Son of this community and working full-time as a Registered Nurse at a local hospital. I served our Veterans at the Veterans Hospital and worked directly with University Students as a School Clinic Registered Nurse at the University of Southern California (USC) and California State University, Fullerton (CSUF).

I am currently serving as Chairman of the ABC Unified School District Strategic Planning Board Advisory Committee, where we review the progress of the district's Strategic Plan and make formal recommendations to the Board of Education. As a City Appointed Commissioner for the City of Cerritos, we hold public hearings to determine if a property complies with the City's property maintenance code.

I want to bring my experience and leadership to the ABC School Board with a Fresh, New, and Innovative perspective.

I will work tirelessly to focus on;
1. Student achievement and Mental Health
2. A Balanced Budget
3. Collaboration with district staff and teachers.

I humbly ask for your vote, Brian Ferrer on November 3!
