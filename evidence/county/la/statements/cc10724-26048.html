STATEMENT OF CHRISTOPHER TRUNKEY

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

SAUGUS UNION SCHOOL DISTRICT

It has been my honor to represent you on the Saugus Union School District Governing Board since 2014.

Last year, 6 of our elementary schools were recognized by the California Department of Education as Distinguished Schools including Cedarcreek, Rosedell, and Plum Canyon.

Our district was recognized in a national Harvard study For post-covid academic achievement in both English Language Arts and Mathematics (1 of only 11 in the state of California).

New classroom buildings have been opened on the campuses of Rosedell (2025), Plum Canyon (2020), and Skyblue (2022). In addition, Cedarcreek opened a new multi-purpose room building (2021) and instructional garden with a culinary arts program (2025).

I look forward to continuing my commitment to excellence in elementary education for all students and respectfully ask for your vote.

Experience. Commitment. Results.

Christopher Trunkey
Saugus Union School District
Governing Board Member, Trustee Area 5
