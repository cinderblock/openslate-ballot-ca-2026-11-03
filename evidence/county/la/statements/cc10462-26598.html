STATEMENT OF NANCY CINDY MARTIZ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
2nd District
(Unexpired term ending December 8, 2028)

CITY OF HUNTINGTON PARK GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

I am running to represent my neighbors in District 2, within the City of Huntington Park. I've lived on Newell Street for the last decade and my neighbors will often find me at Salt Lake Park, at the local cafe, supporting the arts, planting trees, or running around HP. I am invested in the well-being of my neighbors, my city, and my region. For the past 15 years I have been a professional in Human Resources & Employee Benefits. I have a B.A in Government, and an M.A. in Public Policy & Administration. Serving as a council member is how I contribute to the community I love. I was appointed as a Huntington Park City council member in April 2025 and I am committed to finishing what I started. Over the past year as a council member I have been a loud advocate for protecting our immigrant neighbors, keeping neighbors housed, investing in our aging infrastructure, and restoring our parks. I believe Huntington Park has not reached its fullest potential and we deserve better. I will keep fighting for green spaces, clean air, fair housing policies, and safe streets for all. With your support I will continue fighting for the working families of Huntington Park.
