STATEMENT OF CHUONG V. VO

PARTY PREFERENCE: REPUBLICAN

UNITED STATES REPRESENTATIVE, 45th DISTRICT

My name is Chuong Vo, a 28-year law enforcement officer, former Mayor of Cerritos, husband, and father.

I fled Vietnam to America as a one-year-old refugee. The United States of America gave my family freedom and opportunity. Consequently, I dedicated my life to serving our community through local government, law enforcement, and raising my family here.

As Mayor, I helped keep our neighborhoods safe, maintained balanced budgets, and protected taxpayer dollars. I know how to make tough decisions, prioritize core services, and deliver results without raising taxes.

Our families deserve safe neighborhoods, strong schools, and a government that lives within its means. In Congress, I will put safety, affordability, and accountability first.

Public safety is government's first responsibility. I will stand firmly with law enforcement, increase penalties for fentanyl traffickers, dismantle organized retail theft rings, and ensure officers have the training and equipment they need to keep our communities secure.

Families are struggling with rising costs caused by reckless spending in Washington. I will fight to cut wasteful spending, eliminate fraud and abuse, protect Social Security and Medicare for seniors, and restore fiscal discipline.

We are a nation of immigrants and a nation of laws. I support securing our border, enforcing our laws, and modernizing legal immigration so families and employers can follow a fair and orderly process.

I will work to end insider stock trading in Congress, demand transparent budgets, and hold Washington accountable.

I am ready to serve and would be honored to earn your vote.
