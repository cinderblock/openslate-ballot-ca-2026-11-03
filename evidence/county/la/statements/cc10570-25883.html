STATEMENT OF TRACY BURNS

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 3

WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICT OF SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA

I am running for the Water Replenishment District to bring practical leadership, fiscal responsibility, and accountability to protecting our local water supply.

As a retired Los Angeles County Sheriff's Sergeant, local small-business owner, mother, former MOMS Club President, and PTA board member, I have a long history of public and community service. Today, I continue that commitment as a DAR board member and through volunteer organizations serving veterans and youth.

My priorities are clear: protect the quality and reliability of our groundwater, reduce dependence on expensive imported water, invest wisely in infrastructure, control unnecessary costs, and ensure taxpayer dollars are spent carefully and transparently.

I will bring independent oversight, common sense, and a strong work ethic to the Water Replenishment District, with a commitment to protecting both our water and the families who depend on it.

I would be honored to earn your vote.
