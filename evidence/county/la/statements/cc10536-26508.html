STATEMENT OF MARK A. CARO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
1st District

CITY OF AZUSA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 63

Occupation: Energy & Power Field Material Control

Over the years, I have come to appreciate what makes District One such a special place to call home. It is our neighborhoods, our families, our businesses, and most importantly, the people who care deeply about this community.

I am running because I believe representation begins in the neighborhood, not just at City Hall. A council member should be visible, accessible, and willing to listen before decisions are made.

If elected, my focus will be simple:
- Public Safety
- Neighborhood Representation
- Emergency Preparedness
- Responsible Economic Development
- Fiscal Responsibility

This campaign is not about politics; it is about service.

I would be honored to earn your trust and your vote on November 3rd, 2026.
