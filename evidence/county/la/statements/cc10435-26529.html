STATEMENT OF JENNIFER STARK

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
3rd District

CITY OF CLAREMONT GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 57

Occupation: City Councilwoman

As a lifelong Claremont resident and City Council member, I am honored to seek re-election. Since 2018, I have served with dedication and commitment, including terms as Mayor in 2020 and 2026. My public service is rooted in accessibility, responsiveness, and a deep commitment to building trust between residents, local businesses, and our renowned academic institutions.

My priorities for the upcoming term remain steadfast: fostering a vibrant local economy by attracting revenue-generating businesses that respect Claremont's unique character. I am dedicated to rigorous fiscal stewardship, public safety, and proactive environmental management. Through regional leadership, I ensure Claremont's voice is influential on critical issues such as open space preservation, sustainable water management, beneficial development, and infrastructure development, while consistently advocating for our most vulnerable community members.

Locally, I maintain hands-on community connections through my service on the boards of Claremont Heritage and Crossroads for Women, alongside volunteering with the Service Center for Independent Living, SCIL. My approach is defined by building bridges and collaborative problem-solving. Together, we can secure a sustainable future that serves every resident and preserves the Claremont we are proud to pass on to the next generation. I would be honored to earn your vote on November 3.
