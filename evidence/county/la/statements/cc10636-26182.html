STATEMENT OF BRIAN GUERRERO

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

CULVER CITY UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

As a parent, educator, union organizer and policy analyst, I've brought my unique set of skills to the Culver City Unified School Board for the last 4 years, and, as the only teacher and union member on the Board, I've driven policies that have moved CCUSD forward and resulted in positive outcomes for students.

When I ran 4 years ago, I promised to work with teachers, classified staff, students and families to:
1. Provide high-quality academic, social-emotional, artistic and athletic experiences for all students on safe, well-maintained campuses.
2. Improve access and inclusion, particularly students with disabilities and English learners.
3. Examine existing policies and practices for barriers to student success, asking difficult questions, using data, and listening to the experiences of students and families.
4. Value diversity and engage with different communities on matters that impact students, families, and employees.

I have delivered on those promises, and we've made steady progress... but we still have work to do. I believe we need to continue forward, focusing on student opportunities and outcomes, working with our labor partners, and building the confidence of the community until we've revitalized CCUSD and made it the pride of our community.
