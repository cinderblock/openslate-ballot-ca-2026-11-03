STATEMENT OF MICHAEL CACCIOTTI

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
4th District

CITY OF SOUTH PASADENA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Re-Elect South Pasadena City Councilmember Michael Cacciotti

I'm honored to have had the privilege of helping make South Pasadena an even better place to live, and I'm excited to help secure a bright future for our city.

In service to our city, I've helped:

- Prevent traffic congestion from the 710 freeway extension
- Stop the sale of our South Pasadena Nature Park
- Spearhead the Arroyo Seco Pedestrian/Bicycle path
- Improve library, senior, and recreational programs

(See MichaelCacciotti.com for my full list of accomplishments)

Each year I've been in office, we have balanced our budget and improved our fiscal reserves. While we continue to increase efficiency, I'm the only candidate who has won millions of dollars in grants and funding to reduce the burden on taxpayers.

Before I leave public service, I want to secure the achievements we've made for South Pasadena and continue improving the quality of life for our residents.

Priorities for the future:

- Maintain high levels of public safety
- Increase bike and pedestrian safety
- Improve the city services you depend on (Streets, sidewalks, water & sewer systems)
- Champion financial and environmental sustainability
- Regain local control of development to keep our small-town character

Michael Cacciotti

South Pasadena City Councilmember
