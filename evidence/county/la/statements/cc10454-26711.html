STATEMENT OF LINDSEY MORGAN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
4th District

CITY OF GLENDORA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 43

Occupation: Realtor/HS Cheer Coach

My name is Lindsey Morgan, and I am proud to call Glendora home. As a parent of two children, one completing high school in the Glendora public school system and another attending a local private school I understand the importance of strong schools, safe neighborhoods, and a connected community.

I love Glendora's small-town charm, rich history, and close-knit spirit. If elected to represent District 4, I will be a voice for every resident, no matter what side of the freeway you call home. My priorities include creating safer outdoor spaces for children and families, supporting dog parks, and improving lighting in neighborhoods and public areas to enhance safety for all.

I will also advocate for greater understanding and discussion regarding long-standing school district boundary concerns affecting the south side of Glendora and the Charter Oak area, with the goal of exploring solutions and providing clarity for families.

Glendora is a special community, and I believe we are strongest when we work together. I would be honored to serve in District 4 and help preserve the character, safety, and unity that make our city such a wonderful place to live.
