STATEMENT OF JOHN PICKHAVER

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF EL SEGUNDO GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 36

Occupation: Nonprofit CEO/Coach

As a community organizer, nonprofit leader, and Chair of the Arts & Culture Committee, I've worked to solve problems, strengthen our community, and build trust between residents and City Hall.

I led the effort to bring greater transparency to the now-withdrawn charter city proposal, helped mobilize residents to defeat a planned data center, advocated for better pedestrian safety, and organized community events that brought neighbors together. Through this work, I've sought to ensure underrepresented voices aren't just heard, but included in the decisions that shape El Segundo because I believe local government works best when the people participate.

The strongest leaders don't just make policy. They create opportunities for residents to shape it. That's the kind of leadership I'll bring to City Hall. I'll listen before I act, consider competing perspectives, and make thoughtful, transparent decisions in collaboration with residents, businesses, local organizations, and my fellow elected officials.

With your support, we'll create an El Segundo that's a community of opportunity where young people can build their future, seniors can remain in the town they love, and teachers, first responders, and other essential workers can call El Segundo home.
