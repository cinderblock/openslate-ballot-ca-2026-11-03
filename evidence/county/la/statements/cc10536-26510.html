STATEMENT OF ANDREW N. MENDEZ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
1st District

CITY OF AZUSA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 39

Occupation: Councilmember, City of Azusa

I have had the utmost pleasure of serving on the Azusa City Council since 2020.

As a tight-knit community, every penny spent at City Hall counts. As a businessman, husband and father, I believe we must be smart and disciplined to ensure our tax dollars make an impact. Today, our city boasts an incredible $52 million in General Fund reserves, while we are also actively investing in major Capital Improvement Projects, including renovations to our Library, Senior Center and our roads.

We have also created the Azusa Resource Center, creating a hyperlocal approach to addressing homelessness and connecting those in need with services.

As a leader in Azusa, I have worked to make life better for our entire community. I fight for all: Like our senior citizens living on fixed incomes; our youth who benefit from after-school programs; families who want safe neighborhoods to raise their children; and our veterans.

I will continue championing our business community, guarding our resources, strengthening public safety, promoting economic development, expanding programs for seniors, youth and veterans, and advocating for homeownership.

That is my promise to you.
