STATEMENT OF THOMAS BAIRD

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 4

EAST WHITTIER CITY SCHOOL DISTRICT

It has been an honor to serve the students, families, and educators of the East Whittier City School District as Trustee for Area 4. Since joining the Board, I have strived to make thoughtful, informed decisions with one priority: putting our students first.

I am proud to have two children attending district schools. I grew up in Whittier, and I met my wife, Frances Luna-Baird, while attending California High School. This community is our home.

As a social worker and educator, I believe every child deserves a safe, supportive learning environment where they can thrive. I am committed to transparency, accountability, and working collaboratively with families, teachers, staff, and district leadership to strengthen our schools.

I support innovative programs that prepare students for the future while providing a strong academic foundation. I also believe our teachers and staff deserve the resources, respect, and partnership necessary to help every student succeed.

There is important work ahead. I promise to lead with integrity, listen attentively, and make decisions that reflect the best interests of our students.

I respectfully ask for your vote and continued support.
