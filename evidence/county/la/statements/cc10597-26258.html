STATEMENT OF JENNIFER THOMPSON

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

PASADENA AREA COMMUNITY COLLEGE DISTRICT

Occupation: Media Marketing Executive

Education has opened doors in my life. I want to help ensure those same doors remain open for every student who walks through Pasadena City College.

For the past 25 years, South Pasadena has been home for my family. My husband is a lifelong South Pasadena resident and graduate of South Pasadena High School, and we have raised our three children here volunteering in our schools, parish, and community.

After graduating from UC Berkeley, I spent more than 30 years in media building trusted relationships, solving problems, and helping businesses and organizations connect with the communities they serve. My career taught me the importance of listening, asking questions, collaborating with others, and making informed decisions.

For me, Pasadena City College is more than a place to earn a degree. It is a place where high school students can get a head start, adults can prepare for a new career, entrepreneurs can gain new skills, and lifelong learners can continue to grow. Every student deserves the opportunity to discover their talents, receive a meaningful education, and graduate with real-world experience to build a successful future. As your Trustee, I will advocate for students, parents, employers, and community members throughout our district.
