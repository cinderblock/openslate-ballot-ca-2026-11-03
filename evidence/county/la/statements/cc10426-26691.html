STATEMENT OF KIMBERLEY CLARK

CANDIDATE FOR CITY CLERK

CITY OF BURBANK GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: City Clerk

Dear Burbank Voters,

It has been an honor serving as your City Clerk for the past four years. I am running for re-election to continue delivering accountable, efficient, and accessible service to our community.

During my first term, I successfully oversaw seamless, secure local elections and a thriving passport processing program that provides a convenient, essential service for our residents. Additionally, when a street in Burbank was erroneously redistricted into Glendale, I led the complex annexation project that officially restored our neighbors to their rightful municipality.

My commitment remains simple: transparent governance, streamlined city services, and proactive leadership. With your vote, I look forward to building on this progress and serving our city for another four years.

Thank you for your trust and support.
