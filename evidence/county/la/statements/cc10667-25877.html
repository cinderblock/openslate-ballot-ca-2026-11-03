STATEMENT OF BRANDON M. MACIAS

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 3

ROWLAND UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

I've been going to RUSD school board meetings since I was 14 years old, and now I think it's time for a change. My name is Brandon Macias, I'm a proud community council member for Rowland Heights and the school site council president of Nogales High. As a product of our schools, and after years of serving as an educational advocate, a classroom aide at Northam Elementary in Rowland Unified, and a community organizer in Rowland Heights and La Puente, I've seen how a student's background determines their outcome. I'm running to build an educational system that works for every student, regardless of language, income, or documentation.

In an unincorporated community with no mayor or city council, school board members need to be more than just bureaucrats. We need leaders capable of connecting with people and affecting real change through tangible proposals. Most importantly, we need a government that delivers. So whether it's a pot hole in your street, or a concern in the classroom, I believe in a board where every issue that is yours should be ours.

That's why I've spent the last few years fighting against developers, fighting for the movement against AI data centers across the SGV. I believe government belongs to people, not to corporate profits or big money that doesn't care about our schools, or students, or our families.

That's why I'm putting forward a concrete plan that tackles the key issues in the everyday lives of our students and families.
My plan does this by: putting aides at each of our elementary schools tackling issues of literacy and math intervention, using our in-house transportation department to add additional bus routes for student pickup and drop-off to support working families, and working with local organizations to provide free legal aid in addition to strengthening policy against the collection of student citizenship status.

As a Rowland Unified employee who stood in the rain side by side with my coworkers, fighting for a fair deal, I plan to ensure we're giving staff livable wages, fair benefits, and a life of dignity through our union contracts.

These proposals address the greatest barrier facing many students: access. Every child deserves the opportunity to succeed, and every family deserves a school district that listens, responds, and delivers.

I am running because I want to bring learning for all and a government that delivers to RUSD.
