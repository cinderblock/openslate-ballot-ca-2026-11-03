STATEMENT OF JAIME VACA

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

LENNOX SCHOOL DISTRICT

A Working Parent vs. Goliath Developers & Board Members

Powerful special interests are pulling our district away from students and families. Declining enrollment, millions in debt; Lennox lost 1/3 of student enrollment, from 7,000 to under 4,000. Felton is now closed, and without a new direction, another school could be next. Different names for Felton are being proposed to rename the school, possibly after a greedy contractor, "International Properties"

Enough is Enough!

I'm a committed advocate for our students and a working parent with two children at Moffett; I bring real-world problem-solving and accountability. Through the LEAP After School Lennox Program, I created a competitive Cross Country program for interscholastic meets and successfully trained a student to run the L.A. Marathon.

Fiscal Accountability: Demand transparent budgets, review of major expenditures, and quarterly public reporting. Strengthen classrooms, not bureaucracy.

Student Programs: Protect and restore sports, arts, and academic programs that keep OUR children engaged and prepared.

Academic Excellence: Prioritize early phonics reading, math intervention, measurable goals, and meaningful parent input before major decisions, not after.

Stand with me to restore trust, raise standards, and put children first.

Supported by educators, parents, and community leaders.
