STATEMENT OF MARGARET E. FINLAY

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
1st District

CITY OF DUARTE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 73

Occupation: Duarte City Councilmember

Experience matters – now, more than ever.

These past six years, Duarte lost three Councilmembers, who served more than 60 years. I am a regional leader who works to save our cities' tax dollars by managing liabilities and reducing expensive insurance claims. My leadership, experience and commitment helped me work with my council colleagues and staff to achieve:

- Increased Grant Funding and sales revenue, providing services like the annual Fireworks show, upgraded Duarte Teen Center, and beautified Huntington Drive while maintaining stable budgets and 106% in budget reserves.
- Reduced major crimes by 32.9% since 2023, while reducing homelessness.
- Economic Growth at City of Hope with a new parking structure, 147-room hotel, Outpatient Clinic, and upcoming Pathology, Radiation, Oncology building. Also, Vallarta supermarket and Sky Zone entertainment - coming soon.
- Housing & Infrastructure Improvements by working with Congresswoman Grace Napolitano to secure $1.6 million grant for the Royal Oaks bike path upgrade. Also developed state-mandated housing at the new Duarte Station and on Huntington Drive.

Over the next four years, we can:

- Continue lowering crime.
- Address homelessness through job training and addiction recovery programs, for safer public areas.
- Create wildfire mitigation programs to protect our neighborhoods.
- Improve communication for all residents.

Your vote for me, Margaret Finlay, will keep Duarte forging ahead.
