STATEMENT OF JEANNETTE C. ALVARADO

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 3

ROWLAND UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Teacher

Educator. Parent. Rowland Unified graduate. Lifelong Rowland Heights/La Puente resident.

My name is Jeannette C. Alvarado, and I am running for the Rowland Unified School District Board of Education, Trustee Area 3. As a graduate of Rowland Unified, the parent of two Rowland Unified elementary students, and an educator with 15 years of experience, I understand the strengths, opportunities, and challenges facing our schools.

I attended Killian Elementary School, Alvarado Intermediate School, and graduated from John A. Rowland High School in 2003. The education I received, along with the support of my family, teachers, and community, inspired my lifelong commitment to public education and service. I am dedicated to ensuring every student has access to the same opportunities to learn, grow, and succeed.

Throughout my career, I have served as a classroom teacher, instructional leader, program coordinator, and union representative. These experiences have provided me with expertise in curriculum and instructional improvement, educational program development, collaborative leadership, and student advocacy. I have worked to strengthen literacy and mathematics instruction, support College and Career readiness through Career Technical Education (CTE) Pathways and Project-Based Learning, and improve outcomes for English Learners, students with disabilities, and other diverse learners. My experience has reinforced the importance of responsible governance, fiscal accountability, and data-informed decision-making.

As a Board member, I will advocate for policies that put students first. My priorities include expanding access to social-emotional and mental health supports, promoting responsible and balanced use of technology to strengthen teaching and learning, and using state, district, and school data to identify student needs and improve academic outcomes. I believe every student deserves a safe, inclusive, and academically rigorous learning environment where they can reach their full potential.

Strong schools are built through partnerships with families, educators, and the community. I will promote transparent communication, meaningful parent engagement, and collaborative decision-making while advocating for safe, well-maintained campuses, adequate staffing, and responsible stewardship of district resources.

As a lifelong member of this community, a Rowland Unified graduate, a parent, and an educator, I am committed to serving with integrity, accountability, and respect. It would be an honor to serve the families of Trustee Area 3 and the Rowland Unified School District, working collaboratively to ensure every student receives the high-quality education they deserve.
