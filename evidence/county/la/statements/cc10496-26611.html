STATEMENT OF MARK MALDONADO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
4th District

CITY OF PALMDALE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 69

Occupation: Business Owner

I'm running for City Council in District 4 because our community deserves leadership that listens, collaborates, and puts people first. My years of volunteer service with non-profits focused on economic development, education, and family support have shown me what transparent, community-driven representation can accomplish. They've also reinforced a core truth: representation must be by the people and for the people.

My priorities reflect the values of our district:

Fiscal Stewardship: I will ensure taxpayer dollars are managed responsibly and transparently. By pursuing common-sense solutions and reducing unnecessary burdens, we can support sustainable growth and expand opportunity.

Community Engagement: A healthy democracy depends on open dialogue. I will create regular opportunities—such as monthly town halls—for residents to share ideas, voice concerns, and help shape local decisions.

Public Safety & Well-Being: Safety is a shared responsibility. I will strengthen partnerships between residents, law enforcement, and city leadership to build trust, accountability, and a welcoming environment for every family.

Infrastructure: Reliable roads and well-maintained public spaces are essential. I will prioritize improvements that support safe travel, accessibility, and long-term development.

Together, we can restore meaningful representation and build a stronger more connected District 4.
