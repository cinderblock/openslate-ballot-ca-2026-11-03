STATEMENT OF VALERIE BRADFORD

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
4th District

CITY OF SANTA CLARITA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

I am running for Santa Clarita City Council District 4 because our city needs leaders who put people over politics and listen before they lead. Our families deserve a city government that is transparent, accountable, and responsive to the people it serves.

For more than two decades, Santa Clarita has been where my husband and I have built our lives and where I have dedicated myself to serving others. That means encouraging responsible economic growth, strengthening opportunities for small businesses, and making sure residents have a voice in the decisions that affect their lives. The best solutions come from bringing people together and putting our community's interest first.

As founding President of a local nonprofit, I worked to build partnerships and strengthen community. My 24 years in Human Resources helped me solve problems and navigate challenges. My degree in Small Business Management and Entrepreneurship from College of the Canyons gave me insight into the needs of local businesses and working families.

I remain committed to serving others through volunteer work feeding the hungry and assisting individuals experiencing homelessness. I am ready to bring that same commitment, experience, and servant leadership to Santa Clarita's City Council.
