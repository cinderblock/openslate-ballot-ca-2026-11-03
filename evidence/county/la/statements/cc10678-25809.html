STATEMENT OF REBECCA HAMBURG CAPPY

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

WISEBURN UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Nonprofit Executive/Attorney

As a nonprofit executive, attorney, small-business owner, and Wiseburn parent, I am seeking reelection to the Board of Trustees. For 4.5 years, I have been honored to serve our community by bringing both professional expertise and lived experience to this work. As a Latina single mother of two Wiseburn students, I understand how important it is that every child feels seen, supported, and challenged to thrive. I will continue to ensure that our schools provide a high-quality, student-centered education, with strong support for special needs learners, expanded enrichment opportunities, and open communication with families. Having chaired the successful Measure EE bond initiative, I will continue working alongside our community to invest $98 million in improving our schools, strengthening facilities, and supporting student learning, including the new Wiseburn sports complex.

My background in nonprofit leadership, strategic planning, and fiscal oversight will allow me to continue to be a responsible steward of public resources. I believe Wiseburn's strength comes from its families, caregivers, caring staff, diversity, and strong community spirit. I will continue working to protect student academic success, health and safety, improve transparency, and ensure that all children in Wiseburn are well served alongside our amazing teachers and staff.
