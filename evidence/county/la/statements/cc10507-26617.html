STATEMENT OF DIANA LAM

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF ROSEMEAD GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Career Education Manager

My parents came here as refugees and chose Rosemead to build our future. They worked hard and from the age of four, I spoke up for my family. Opportunity isn't something families find on their own. It's built by strong schools, safe streets, and a City Hall that listens.

Every family deserves the opportunities that helped mine. I'll fight for a Rosemead where families can afford to stay, seniors can age with dignity, and small businesses can grow.

I'm proof of what happens when a community invests in its youth: public schools and a full scholarship made me a first-generation college graduate. At Bank of America, I managed budgets and analyzed risk, and I've mentored young people in STEM and civics through CAUSE and BioscienceLA.

As your Councilwoman, I'll read the City's budget line by line, ask hard questions before we spend, and compete for the state and federal dollars Rosemead is leaving on the table. I'll modernize a City Hall where you still can't pay for a permit online, expand multilingual services, and bring a real plan to encourage businesses along Valley Blvd and Garvey Ave.

With gratitude, I ask for your vote. More information: VoteDianaLam.com
