STATEMENT OF MARICELA ROMERO FRYMARK

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF IRWINDALE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Dear Neighbor,

It has been an honor to serve as the Chair of our City's Planning Commission. Thank you for your trust and support over the years. I am proud to now run for Irwindale City Council because I believe our city deserves thoughtful leadership that considers all residents and holds our City to a high standard.

My priorities are simple: encourage smart growth that brings quality businesses and local jobs, maintain balanced budgets, keep our neighborhoods safe, invest in our youth, seniors, and families, focus on housing opportunities, and make sure City departments are open, accountable, and accessible to every resident and community partner.

Professionally, I serve in an executive leadership role where I help develop education policies that affect students, solve difficult problems, and build partnerships that strengthen our schools and community. I remain active in our local schools by serving on PTA boards and district committees, advocating for our students and families.

I am running because I care deeply about Irwindale. I will listen, lead with integrity, and work collaboratively to preserve what makes Irwindale special, while building a stronger future for the next generations. I would be honored to earn your vote.

With gratitude,

Maricela
