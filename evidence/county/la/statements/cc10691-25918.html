STATEMENT OF LISA MICHELLE DABBS

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 1

EAST WHITTIER CITY SCHOOL DISTRICT

As your East Whittier City School District Trustee since 2018, I have focused on what matters most: the success of our students. As a former principal and educator with decades of experience in the classroom and school leadership, I bring proven leadership, practical experience, and a steadfast commitment to student success to every decision I make.

During my service on the Board, I have prioritized fiscal responsibility, strengthened investments in arts, technology, and innovative learning opportunities, promoted transparent communication with parents, staff, and the community, and supported the outstanding teachers and classified employees who make a difference in our students' lives every day.

I believe strong schools are the foundation of a strong community, and I have worked to ensure our district remains accountable, financially responsible, and focused on academic excellence.

I remain committed to maintaining the high standards that make East Whittier City School District one of the finest districts in our region to ensure student success!

It would be an honor to continue serving as your Trustee for Area 1 in the East Whittier City School District. Together, we can build on our successes, prepare every student for a bright future.
