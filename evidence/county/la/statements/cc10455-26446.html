STATEMENT OF DANDY DE PAULA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF HAWAIIAN GARDENS GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 51

Occupation: Incumbent/Consultant

I believe leadership is about service, accountability, and creating opportunities for every resident to succeed. As your Councilmember, I have supported initiatives in making Hawaiian Gardens a safer, stronger, and more vibrant community by prioritizing public safety, supporting violence prevention efforts, and building strong partnerships with law enforcement, schools, community organizations, and residents.

I have championed programs that support families, empower students, create opportunities for our youth, and ensure our seniors have the support, respect, and resources they deserve. I have also supported efforts to keep our streets clean, improve our neighborhoods, and create a welcoming environment that attracts businesses and encourages economic growth.

By collaborating with local businesses, community leaders, and residents, we have made meaningful progress together. I remain committed to listening to our community, addressing challenges, and delivering results. Together, we can continue building a Hawaiian Gardens where families thrive, seniors are valued, businesses prosper, and every resident feels proud to call our city home.
