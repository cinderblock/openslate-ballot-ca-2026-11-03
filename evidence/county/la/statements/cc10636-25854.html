STATEMENT OF DARREL MENTHE

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

CULVER CITY UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 54

Occupation: Parent/Attorney

As a proud CCUSD parent of two and a Culver City resident for 20 years, I'm passionate about improving the quality and reputation of Culver City schools. After earning degrees from UC San Diego, Stanford Law School, and UCLA, I began my career as a lawyer. Today, I'm the Culver City Downtown Business Association Executive Director, where I created High School Senior Day, inviting local businesses to celebrate our graduates. I also chair the Culver City Planning Commission and serve on the Measure E Bond Oversight Committee.

CCUSD is a $100 million operation serving over 6,000 students and 700 teachers and staff. Running it well takes serious focus on budgets and academics.

When elected, I will work to:
-Fix CCUSD's budget by moving dollars into classrooms, not overhead;
-Bring back honors classes, so every student who wants a challenge gets one;
-Cover AP exam costs, like LA Unified does, so price never stops a college-bound kid;
-Make sure students can meet with a counselor;
-Shrink class sizes so teachers can reach every child.

I'm endorsed by Teachers, School Board members, City Council members, and local parents.

I would be honored to earn your vote.
