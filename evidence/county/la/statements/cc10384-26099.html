STATEMENT OF GEORGENE "MRS. GEORGIE" NAVARRETE

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS

LA PUENTE VALLEY COUNTY WATER DISTRICT

My name is Georgene Navarrete, and I am honored to be a first-time candidate for the Board of Directors of the La Puente Valley County Water District.

For 29 years, I have proudly served our community as a Site Supervisor Aide with the Hacienda La Puente Unified School District. In addition, I work for the City of La Puente as a Recreation Service Leader. Throughout my career, I have worked closely with students, families, and staff, building relationships based on trust, service, and dedication.

My commitment to our community extends beyond my career. I had served as a board member of the Fairgrove Academy PTO, served as Secretary and Treasurer of Kiwanis Club of La Puente, and was President of the La Puente Warrior Field Corps. These experiences have strengthened my leadership skills, fiscal responsibility, and commitment to serving others.

I am running because I believe our community deserves dedicated, transparent, and accountable leadership. If elected, I will work to protect our water resources, ensure reliable water service, and make thoughtful decisions that serve the best interests of our residents.

Although this is my first campaign for the Water District Board, my years of public service and community involvement have prepared me to serve with integrity, respect, and dedication.

I respectfully ask for your vote and the opportunity to represent you on the La Puente Valley County Water District Board of Directors.
