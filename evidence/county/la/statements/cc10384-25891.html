STATEMENT OF JOHN P. ESCALERA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS

LA PUENTE VALLEY COUNTY WATER DISTRICT

As your water board director, it has been an honor to serve the residents of La Puente for the past 25 years. I am a lifelong resident of La Puente and have raised my three children here with my wife Betty. During my time on the water board, I have been a part of various programs to benefit our community. My goal has always been and will continue to be to provide the residents of La Puente clean and safe water at one of the lowest rates in the Valley. I look forward to continuing to serve you on the La Puente Valley Water Board.
