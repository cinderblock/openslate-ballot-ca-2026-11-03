STATEMENT OF JOHN DURAN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF WEST HOLLYWOOD GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

West Hollywood has been my home for 36 years. I built my civil rights law practice here. It was an honor to serve on your Council from 2000-2020. Today, I'm running because the city needs experienced independent leadership focused on residents – not political ambitions.

In recent years, the new council has drifted from critical priorities. Political ideology, special interests and higher office ambition have trumped protecting our neighborhoods, supporting public safety and improving residents' quality of life.

I'm running to restore common sense and accountable leadership. With longtime Councilmembers John Heilman and Lauren Meister termed out, both encouraged me to return and provide the experienced steady hand our city needs.

WeHo deserves thoughtful growth – not overdevelopment. Affordable housing includes protecting our rent-controlled apartments, preserving historic neighborhoods and keeping WeHo unique. I oppose eliminating traffic lanes on Fountain Avenue. Bike lanes belong down on Willoughby where they can better serve cyclists without reducing parking, interfering with first responders and jamming all our traffic arteries.

Public safety remains my top priority and I historically earned the endorsement of our sheriff deputies union. West Hollywood has many vulnerable communities that require protection from hatred. Reducing law enforcement funding has weakened public safety. I will work to restore the full funding of our sheriffs and fire departments.

We must rebuild the torn relationship between our city and chamber of commerce. Our restaurants, hotels, nightclubs and entertainment venues are essential to our economy and identity. This symbiotic relationship must be renewed to reduce storefront vacancies and strengthen our neighborhoods.

Finally, we must confront homelessness with both compassion and a firm hand. We should continue to help those who want services. But we must also remove those individuals with severe mental health episodes who bring criminality onto our city streets and threaten residents and businesses alike.

During my 20 years of council service, I created the West Hollywood Recovery Center site and protection for the Log Cabin 12 step meeting facility. I created the free nighttime shuttle, rainbow crosswalks, neighborhood dog parks, AIDS monument and new design for West Hollywood Park, pool and library. I tripled the budget for our fine arts programming and required adequate parking for all proposed new developments. I have a record of tenant protections under our ordinance and building long term prudent reserves for the city.

I respectfully ask for your vote to bring pragmatic common sense back to City Hall.
