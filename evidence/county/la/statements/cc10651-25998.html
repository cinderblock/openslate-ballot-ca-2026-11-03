STATEMENT OF SUSAN MOORE

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

LA CAÑADA UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

As an educator, parent, and La Cañada community member, I am running for the LCUSD School board to bring an authentic classroom perspective, deep institutional understanding, and a parent's heart to our district. Over my 18 year career as a LCHS English teacher, I have dedicated my career to empowering students to succeed inside and outside the classroom. I have served the district as English department chair, WASC coordinator, Leadership and School site council representative, negotiation team member, and advisor to numerous clubs on campus including Speech and Debate and ASB. As a parent and grandparent, my children graduated from La Cañada High School and my grandson will begin his elementary education in the next few years. Contributing to the education of children has given me the chance to understand the unique needs of our community and gain an understanding of all of its members from teachers, administrators, families, and most importantly students whose educational experience depends on the shared values of all of us. I am committed to support every student through compassionate governance ensuring the continued excellence of our schools.
