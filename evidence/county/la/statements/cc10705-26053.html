STATEMENT OF OSCAR IRAHETA

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 2

LANCASTER SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 40

Occupation: Lead Organizer

Every child deserves the opportunity to succeed.

My name is Oscar Iraheta. I am a proud father of a student attending the Lancaster School District, a local business owner, investor, and Co-Founder of a community organization dedicated to serving families and strengthening our community.

Like many parents, I want our schools to provide every student with a safe learning environment, outstanding teachers, strong academic programs, and the skills needed to succeed in college, careers, military service, or skilled trades.

I am running for the Board of Trustee because I believe our students deserve leaders who listen, collaborate, and put children—not politics—at the center of every decision.

As your Trustee, I will work to:

- Raise academic achievement and improve student outcomes.

- Support and respect our teachers and school employees.

- Keep our schools safe, welcoming, and focused on learning.

- Expand STEM, Career Technical Education, financial literacy, and workforce readiness programs.

- Strengthen partnerships between schools, parents, businesses, and community organizations.

- Ensure transparency, accountability, and responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars.

My experience as a parent, entrepreneur, and community leader has taught me that lasting success comes from listening, building trust, and working together to solve problems.

The future of Lancaster begins in our classrooms. Every decision we make today shapes the opportunities our children will have tomorrow.

Together, we can build stronger schools, stronger families, and a brighter future for every student.

I respectfully ask for your vote for Lancaster School District Governing Board, Trustee Area 2.

Oscar Iraheta

Parent - Business Owner - Community Leader
