STATEMENT OF KIMBERLY LIFSCHITZ

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

BEVERLY HILLS UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 39

Occupation: Parent

When I decided to run for a seat on the Beverly Hills Unified School District Board of Education, I found myself repeatedly returning to one simple idea: It's time for BHUSD to get back to school. Our district is well-resourced and full of talented people, and we have everything we need to be the "lighthouse district" we hear so much about – we just need the will.

My top-level priorities are a more comprehensive approach to student success that fosters academic achievement while also emphasizing personal growth, physical and mental health, and strong community connections for every single student; a restoration of trust between district leadership and the community; and a renewed focus on responsible and informed governance.

I also plan to build and improve upon existing initiatives, such as the newly-enacted educational technology policy and partnership with Special Olympics of Southern California. While encouraging on their own, these measures have the potential for even greater impact, and I look forward to turning that potential into reality.
