STATEMENT OF JESSICA PATTON VLACO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF RANCHO PALOS VERDES GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Accounting and Finance Executive

I am a lifelong resident of Rancho Palos Verdes. I attended PVPUSD schools. My husband and I raised our three children here. It is a wonderful place to live.

I have a long history of involvement in the community in leadership roles in the PTA and Booster Club. I was a long-time soccer coach and referee before switching to volleyball. I volunteered for many years as a coach and now most recently as the PV Site Director/Clinician for the United States Youth Volleyball program.

Always eager to get involved and stay current on things going on in the city, I applied for and was appointed to the city's Traffic Safety Committee and then the Finance Advisory Committee. Currently I serve on the board of CHOA.

In my professional career I have served in senior level accounting and finance positions for a variety of public and private companies.

As a council member, my priorities would include preserving public safety and the quality of life here in RPV while maintaining sound fiscal policies. I will work with staff to continue looking for technical and financial assistance for the landslide area.
