STATEMENT OF JAMES CODY BIRKEY

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 3

CERRITOS COMMUNITY COLLEGE DISTRICT

Occupation: Incumbent

In the 8 years since Trustee Birkey joined the board, Cerritos College has risen to become one of the top-performing community colleges in the nation. Working alongside campus leadership, faculty, staff, fellow trustees, and our community, we've achieved extraordinary results.

For example, last year we awarded over 9,000 degrees and certificates—the largest graduating class in the college's history. That's a 261% increase in 10 years. Today, there are more Cerritos College graduates from Bellflower than ever, and thanks to our Dual Enrollment pilot project with BUSD, some are even earning degrees alongside their diplomas.

Education from Cerritos College is now ranked the #1 Top Return on Investment (ROI) in Los Angeles County—where students recoup educational costs in just two months. Economists now estimate that Cerritos College's economic impact adds $500 million to our local economy every year by increasing earnings and professional capabilities. We're changing lives, bringing more money to Bellflower, and momentum continues to grow.

Birkey is a Harvard grad with decades of experience in higher education, a long-time Bellflower resident with a track record of transformational success. Join countless local leaders, business owners, and graduates—"Vote Birkey" for the next generation of students to thrive here.
