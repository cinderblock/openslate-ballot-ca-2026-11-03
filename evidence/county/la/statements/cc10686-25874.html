STATEMENT OF FLORENCIO BRIONES

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 4

EL MONTE UNION HIGH SCHOOL DISTRICT

I am honored to serve as your Trustee. Throughout my service, my focus has remained the same: student needs must be at the center of every discussion and every decision we make.

I expanded college access through partnerships with Rio Hondo College and Pasadena City College, enabling students to earn college credit in high school. I supported establishing wellness centers across our campuses, expanding the Board to include two Student Board Members, and strengthening services for parents and families through our Family and Community Engagement Center.

I also supported major investments in classrooms, athletic facilities, outdoor spaces, accessibility, and essential campus infrastructure. We have updated the District's Facilities Master Plan to guide future improvements across our schools.

As a graduate of Mountain View High School, Pasadena City College, and Cal Poly Pomona, my educational journey guides my commitment to every student.

Our work is not finished. I will continue expanding college and career pathways, strengthening academic and mental-health support, completing critical school improvements, and protecting the District's long-term financial stability.

Good policy, not politics, remains my priority. I am prepared to continue leading with experience, integrity, and an unwavering commitment to our students, employees, families, and community.
