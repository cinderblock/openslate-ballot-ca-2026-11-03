STATEMENT OF BRETT HADDOAK

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
2nd District

CITY OF SANTA CLARITA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 41

Occupation: Software Engineer

I've called Santa Clarita home my entire life, and my family's roots in our community span over five decades. My grandfather helped build our region's infrastructure, working on the Castaic Lake Dam and the 5 and 14 freeways. My mother spent nearly 20 years serving Santa Clarita students in our public schools. Today, my wife and I are raising our daughter in this community that has given so much to our family.

As a small business owner and software engineer for over 20 years, I've built practical solutions to complex problems. It's that perspective that will allow me to successfully serve this city that I love. Growing up here has given me a firsthand understanding of Santa Clarita's opportunities and challenges. We need better access to emergency healthcare, more quality local jobs, less time spent in traffic, and effective solutions to homelessness.

I've worked on many of these issues already, volunteering over 1,000 hours with Bridge to Home, serving on Santa Clarita's Homelessness Task Force, and developing software supporting the Shared Housing Program. I hope to continue my service to our community on the City Council, solving problems and delivering results that strengthen our community for the next generation.
