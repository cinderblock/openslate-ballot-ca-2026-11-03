STATEMENT OF GLORIA D. GRAY

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 2

WEST BASIN MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICT

I respectfully ask for your vote for the West Basin Municipal Water District Board.

I bring decades of public service and proven leadership advocating for strong, healthy, and resilient communities. At West Basin, I have worked to protect our water supply, strengthen infrastructure, promote conservation, and prepare our region for drought, climate change, and future growth.

Water is essential to every family, business, and community. I will champion reliable and affordable water while supporting responsible investments that protect our resources for future generations.

My leadership is grounded in listening, accountability, collaboration, and results. I understand the needs of local communities and the regional challenges facing Southern California's water future.

West Basin deserves experienced, independent leadership committed to protecting ratepayers and our water resources.

This November, I ask for your vote and the opportunity to serve with integrity, experience, and results.
