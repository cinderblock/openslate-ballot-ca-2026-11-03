STATEMENT OF LISA C. NASHUA

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 1

POMONA UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 55

Occupation: College Administrator

Lisa Nashua has been a Pomona resident for over two decades, including ten years developing and implementing community-based programs in the visual and performing arts in Pomona. She is committed to pipelining students from K-12 to college to receive a four-year degree or Career Technical Education of their choice. It has been her privilege to serve as a member of the PUSD Board of Education representing trustee area 1 for the last 3 ½ years. During this time the district has achieved a decade high of a 92.8% graduation rate on the state of CA school dashboard. Career and Technical education developed and implemented 39 programs, including high-paying careers in its award-winning health career ladder. As a trustee, Dr. Nashua has enthusiastically supported music program expansion across the district and cultural enriching programming. Dr. Nashua remains committed to supporting the district's teachers, staff and students with access to quality education in safe facilities. This commitment extends to continuing to implement strategies to achieve transparency and accountability to our community and their trust to build and renovate schools to benefit our children.
