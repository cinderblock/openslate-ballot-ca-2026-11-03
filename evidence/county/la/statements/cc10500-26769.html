STATEMENT OF VERONICA ELIAS

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF EDUCATION,
District No. 3

PASADENA UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Educator

I am Veronica Elias, Ed.D., a PUSD parent and 25-year educator who most recently served as a school principal, leading my school to 2026 California Distinguished School recognition.

I've spent my career making difficult decisions, balancing budgets, supporting teachers, improving student outcomes, and always putting students first. I understand how every board decision directly affects classrooms, families, and our district's future.

PUSD faces significant financial challenges, declining enrollment, and persistent absenteeism. Our community deserves leadership that is honest, transparent, and focused on long-term solutions.

I will advocate for an independent review of district administrative spending before additional classroom cuts are considered, support meaningful strategies to improve enrollment and attendance, and insist on transparent, accountable governance that earns our community's trust.

As both an educator and a parent, I'll bring experienced, accountable leadership to help PUSD move forward with transparency, stability, and an unwavering commitment to students.
