STATEMENT OF BURTON BRINK

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
2nd District

CITY OF ARCADIA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 62

Occupation: Chairman, Nonprofit Organization

I have been a proud resident of Arcadia since 1967. As a retired Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department Sergeant with nearly 40 years of law enforcement experience, I have dedicated my career to protecting our community and keeping families safe.

I served eight years as an Arcadia City Commissioner, completing my term in 2025. I currently serve as Chairman of Crime Survivors, where I advocate for victims of crime.

My priorities are clear: public safety first, restoring trust and transparency at City Hall, fiscal responsibility with taxpayer dollars, protecting property values, supporting local businesses, and preserving the high quality of life that makes Arcadia special. I will fight to maintain local control over our city's future rather than allowing overreaching state mandates to dictate how we grow.

As the father of an Asian son and a longtime member of our diverse community, I am personally committed to addressing safety concerns that affect all families, businesses, and neighborhoods in District 2.

With experience, integrity, and common-sense leadership, I will work every day to make Arcadia safer, stronger, and more prosperous for every resident.

I respectfully ask for your support and the opportunity to serve District 2. For more information, please visit www.burtonbrink.com
