STATEMENT OF JOYCE RANDALL

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF EDUCATION,
Trustee Area 1

INGLEWOOD UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Joyce Randall is a classroom teacher, education advocate, and your elected Trustee for Inglewood Unified School District, Trustee Area 1.

Joyce previously taught biology in Inglewood Unified and continues serving students today as a classroom teacher in Los Angeles Unified. She understands what students, parents, teachers, and school employees need because she has worked directly in public education and remains connected to the classroom.

During Joyce's first term, Inglewood Unified made important progress toward restoring local control after years of state receivership. Joyce has supported responsible oversight, stronger accountability, improved communication with families, and a renewed focus on student achievement.

The next term will be critical. As the district prepares to regain local governance, Trustee Area 1 needs steady, knowledgeable leadership from someone who understands both classroom instruction and the responsibilities of the school board.

Joyce will continue working to strengthen academic achievement, support teachers and staff, improve parent engagement, protect fiscal stability, expand opportunities for students, and ensure that community voices are heard.

Joyce Randall has the experience, commitment, and educational background to help lead Inglewood Unified forward with integrity, independence, and proven dedication.

Please vote again for Joyce Randall for Inglewood Unified School District Trustee, Trustee Area 1.
