STATEMENT OF MARITZA CRUZ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF MAYWOOD GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 54

Occupation: Member Services

My Maywood Community,

My name is Maritza Cruz, and I am honored to be a candidate for the Maywood City Council. Maywood is more than the city where I have lived for the past 25 years. My community I deeply care about and the place I am committed to serving.

Maywood has been more than my hometown it's the community where I have built my life, raised my family, and developed a deep commitment to serving my neighbors.

As a current Planning Commissioner, I actively participate in community programs, city events, and volunteer opportunities because I believe strong communities are built through service, collaboration, and civic engagement.

Professionally, I have built a career in Human Resources, helping people with compassion, integrity and accountability. The same values I will bring to the City Council by, being transparent, and working together to find solutions that improve our quality of life.

If elected, I will work to strengthen public safety, keep our neighborhoods clean, expand opportunities for our youth, support local businesses, and ensure our seniors receive the resources they deserve.

I am ready to serve with honesty and dedication. I would be honored to earn your trust, support and your vote.
