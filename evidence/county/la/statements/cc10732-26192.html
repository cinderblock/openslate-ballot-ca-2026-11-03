STATEMENT OF SHAMEKA ANDRE

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

WESTSIDE UNION SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 43

Occupation: School Administrator

As a dedicated educator and school administrator, I am honored to seek a position on the Westside Union School District Board. Having served the district as both a teacher and administrator, I have experienced the opportunities and challenges facing the students, families and staff.

Throughout my career, I have been committed to creating safe, supportive learning environments where every student can succeed. I believe strong schools are built through collaboration, fiscal responsibility, transparency, and meaningful partnerships with parents, educators and the community.

As a current school administrator, I understand the importance of balancing educational excellance with responsible stewardship of district resources. My experience has given me the knowledge to make thoughtful, student-centered decisions that support academic achievement while preparing students for future success.

If elected, I will listen to our community, advocate for students and staff, and work to ensure every child has access to high-quality education. Together we can continue building on Westside Union School District's strengths while meeting the needs of today's learners and tomorrow's leaders.
