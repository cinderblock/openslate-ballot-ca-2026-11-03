STATEMENT OF SIMON WRIGHT

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 4

COVINA-VALLEY UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 49

Occupation: Property Management

As the incumbent member of the Covina-Valley Unified School District Board of Education, a parent, and local business owner, I am committed to ensuring every student has the opportunity to succeed while protecting the district's long-term financial future.

I believe our employees are the foundation of student success. I have worked to strengthen relationships with our teachers, classified staff, and administrators by promoting collaboration, respect, and open communication.

I have provided strong fiscal oversight by making responsible decisions that maximize the use of community resources. I have supported innovative strategies to leverage district property to generate ongoing revenue for our schools. Together, we have also invested more than $70 million in school facilities, modernizing campuses and creating safe, engaging learning environments where students can thrive.

I am proud of the significant academic progress our students continue to make. By maintaining high expectations and expanding innovative programs, including outstanding Career Technical Education pathways, we are preparing students for success in college, careers, and life.

I respectfully ask for your vote to continue serving as your Board Member so we can build on our successes, invest in our students, support our outstanding employees, and keep Covina-Valley among California's premier school districts.
