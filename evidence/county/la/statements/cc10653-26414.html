STATEMENT OF ALMA RUBY ANDRADE

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

LYNWOOD UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 32

Occupation: Mother/Healthcare Advocate

As a lifelong resident of Lynwood and a proud parent raising my son within our community, I am deeply invested in the success of the Lynwood Unified School District. My family has personally benefited from the district's essential programs, which have provided me with the stability to balance my career as a single mother while my son thrives in his transitional kinder program.

I am running for the Board of Education because I believe every family in Lynwood deserves access to the same life-changing opportunities that have supported us. My priority is to protect and expand the funding for the vital programs that working parents depend on. I am committed to transparency, fiscal responsibility, and ensuring that our schools remain a safe, nurturing environment where every student can succeed.

With your vote, I will serve as a dedicated advocate for our children, our educators, and our families. Together, we can build a stronger, more supported future for all Lynwood students. I humbly ask for your vote to serve on the Lynwood Unified School District board.
