STATEMENT OF ALEXANDRE "ALEX" MONTEIRO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF HAWTHORNE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 65

Occupation: Nonprofit Executive

Since 2018, I have had the privilege of serving as your Councilmember for the City of Hawthorne. I am humbly asking for your support to re-elect me for another term. I have lived in Hawthorne for three decades and have been a public servant for 20 years.

Twelve years on the school board and 8 years as your Councilmember. Since being elected, I have created policies to improve life in our city. It would be an honor to have your vote and continue to advocate for health and safety of our residents, so you can experience a higher quality of living.

I am also committed to fighting for good-paying jobs with benefits and advocating for college and vocational careers in technology and other industries that will benefit the development of our youth.

My Priorities for Hawthorne
- Continuing to support public safety and crime reduction.
- Continuing to promote economic development.
- Encouraging homeownership through first-time homebuyer programs, and advocating for affordable housing and workforce housing.
- Continuing to improve our infrastructure.
- Promoting health and well-being for all Hawthorne residents.

It would be an honor to receive your vote.
