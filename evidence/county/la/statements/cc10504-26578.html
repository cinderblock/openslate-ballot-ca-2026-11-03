STATEMENT OF MICHELE PATRICK CARBONE

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF RANCHO PALOS VERDES GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 71

Occupation: Health Care Consultant

My husband and I have proudly called Rancho Palos Verdes home since 2010. I love this community and have become increasingly involved because I believe our residents deserve to have their voices heard and their interests represented.

My professional career has been dedicated to healthcare, quality, compliance, and responsible management. I hold several degrees in Administration and Quality Assurance. Throughout my career, I have held senior leadership positions at several of Los Angeles' distinguished hospitals, gaining extensive experience in healthcare management, quality, compliance, and fiscal responsibility and now run my own healthcare consulting business.

My experience has taught me the importance of accountability, fiscal responsibility, careful decision-making, and listening to the people affected by those decisions. I understand budgets, regulations, compliance, and what it takes to identify a problem, develop a plan, and follow through.

As I care deeply about our community, I'm looking for an opportunity to serve my neighbors and bring my professional experience and perspective to City Council. I want to be a councilmember who listens to residents, asks the difficult questions, looks carefully at the facts and the financial impact, and makes decisions with the community and homeowners first.
