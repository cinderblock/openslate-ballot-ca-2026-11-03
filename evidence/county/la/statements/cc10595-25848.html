STATEMENT OF FRANCIS POLLARA

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 1

PASADENA AREA COMMUNITY COLLEGE DISTRICT

Pasadena City College was a big part of my academic journey and the opportunities I found there shaped my future. That's why I'm running–to help prepare today's students for tomorrow's careers.

I'm a lifelong resident of the district, PCC alumnus, business and economic development leader, city commissioner, and community volunteer. Throughout my career, I've helped businesses, educators, and government work together to create jobs, strengthen workforce training, and prepare communities for the future. I currently serve as Chair of the La Cañada Flintridge Sustainability and Resilience Commission, on the Board of the YMCA of the Foothills, and on the Advisory Council of the Los Angeles Cleantech Incubator.

As your Trustee, I'll help PCC prepare students for the changing workforce by expanding career education, embracing new technology that improves learning, strengthening partnerships with local employers, supporting successful transfer pathways, and ensuring every taxpayer dollar is managed responsibly.

I'm honored to be endorsed by Congresswoman Judy Chu, former State Senator Carol Liu, the Pasadena City College Faculty Association, a majority of the current PCC Board of Trustees, Vice Mayor Jess Rivas, Mayor Jeanne Hobson, the Stonewall Democratic Club, the Cañada Crescenta Democratic Club, and community leaders throughout the district.

Preparing Students. Building Opportunity. Shaping the Future.
