STATEMENT OF LARRY GRABLE

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 1

SAUGUS UNION SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Small Business Owner/Attorney/Parent

Good schools aren't made by accident. I am running because I believe good schools are built through thousands of thoughtful decisions made over many years.

As a business and patent attorney, small-business owner, husband, and father, I have spent more than twenty-five years helping inventors, entrepreneurs, and owners solve complex problems and make thoughtful decisions when the outcome matters.

My wife, Christy, and I chose Santa Clarita because of its outstanding schools and strong sense of community. Together, we raised two children here and experienced the opportunities excellent public schools create.

As your Board member, I will listen, ask thoughtful questions, respect differing viewpoints, and work collaboratively with parents, educators, staff, and the community to help every student reach their full potential.
