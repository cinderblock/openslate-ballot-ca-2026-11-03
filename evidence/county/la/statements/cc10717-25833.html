STATEMENT OF ADAM C. CARRANZA

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

MOUNTAIN VIEW SCHOOL DISTRICT

The Mountain View School District has made transformative changes to meet the needs of our students and families. We have safer schools due to the investments we've made to begin to modernize our schools.

We have strengthened the enrichment opportunities that not only make school fun but expand the opportunities students have whether it is the Disney Imagination Campus teaching students about design and engineering or students learning how to make their own podcast. Students are expanding their debate and policy-making skills through our Model United Nations program.

Test scores continue moving upwards as we focus on expanding on the science of reading and supporting half of our students who come to school knowing more than one language.

As a district we have embraced community partnerships and expanded resources to support our students and their families which include a food bank and shoes.

The world is changing at a fast pace as we use more technology and AI. The jobs of tomorrow are unknown to us today but what we will always need are students who can think critically, read with deep understanding, and navigate the world with the highest ethics. Those are the skills we need to expand on.
