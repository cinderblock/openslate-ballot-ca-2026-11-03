STATEMENT OF RICHARD BOWEN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
3rd District

CITY OF LA VERNE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 73

Occupation: Retired/Business/Professional

I am honored to run for La Verne City Council.
As a 43-year La Verne resident, married for 48 years, with 2 children, I have followed the city council actions closely over the last 10 years. I have conservative values but listen to all sides presented before making decisions.

Our citizens DESERVE to have OUR voices heard. I will vote based on information vs. the potential bias of council members or city management. The city should SERVE THE PEOPLE, not the other way around.

I support the Police, Fire and all the hard-working employees of our great city and understand that we have financial pressure to continue to support our full-service city. I will work tirelessly to keep our city prosperous, financially sound and SAFE and will work together with all council members to support our CITIZEN's needs versus private groups or city management demands. I am NOT a politician. I bring 46 years of professional business management to this position.

I am a resident wishing to make a positive difference in the city I love. To have raised my family and live a wonderful life. I appreciate your support and respectfully ask for your vote.
