STATEMENT OF FRANKLIN CARVAJAL

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF CULVER CITY GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 53

Occupation: Psychologist

I am Franklin Carvajal, a Culver City resident, psychologist, business owner, longtime renter, and homeowner. I came to the United States alone at age 16 with few resources and a determination to build a better life. Through hard work and education, I earned two PhDs from UMass Amherst and Virginia Tech. My experiences with financial hardship, public transportation, affordable housing, and homeownership taught me the importance of opportunity, perseverance, and practical leadership.

I am running for Culver City Council because local government should deliver results that improve residents' everyday lives. My priorities are simple: a Culver City that is safe, clean, quiet, beautiful, and accountable. That means responsive public safety, clean streets and parks, peaceful neighborhoods, thoughtful development and green spaces, responsible use of taxpayer dollars, transparent government, and a City Council that listens to residents.

Culver City is my home. I want to bring my professional experience, life experience, and common-sense approach to City Hall and work collaboratively to protect what makes our community special while building a strong future for everyone.
