STATEMENT OF MICHAEL YEN-TING LIN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 5
(Unexpired term ending December 1, 2028)

WALNUT VALLEY WATER DISTRICT SPECIAL ELECTION

Age: 35

Occupation: Certified Public Accountant (CPA)

"It has been an honor and privilege to serve as your appointed Director for Walnut Valley Water District Division V. I am grateful for the opportunity to serve the communities of City of Industry, Pomona, West Covina, Diamond Bar, and Walnut. As a Diamond Bar resident since 1999, business owner, Certified Public Accountant (CPA), and business advisor, I care deeply about the future of our community and the quality of services our residents and businesses receive.

I believe serving on the Board is both a responsibility and a privilege. I am committed to listening to our residents, working closely with Walnut Valley Water District staff and my fellow Board Members, and making thoughtful decisions that put the interests of our communities first.

As a CPA and business owner, I understand the importance of sound financial management, careful budgeting, and planning for the future. I believe every taxpayer dollar should be treated with respect and used responsibly. At the same time, we must continue investing in our water system, maintaining critical infrastructure, protecting our water supply, and ensuring reliable, high-quality water service for our communities.

I also believe good government starts with being accessible, transparent, and accountable. I want residents and businesses to know that their concerns are heard and that their elected representatives are working on their behalf.

I am honored to serve as your appointed incumbent and respectfully ask for your vote so I can continue serving Division V. I will bring the same integrity, professionalism, dedication, and commitment to our community that I bring to my work as a CPA and business owner.

Thank you for the opportunity to serve and for your trust and support."
