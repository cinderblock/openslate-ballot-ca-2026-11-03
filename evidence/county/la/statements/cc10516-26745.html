STATEMENT OF KERA BLADES-SNELL

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF TRUSTEES,

SANTA MONICA COMMUNITY COLLEGE DISTRICT

My own path to higher education was not a traditional one. I attended community college in Belize, immigrated to the United States, and returned to school as an adult learner; earning my bachelor's degree after age 35 and my master's after 40, while raising my family and building my business. That journey taught me that every student's path is different, and every student deserves the opportunity to succeed.

As Executive Director of the Santa Monica Black Empowerment Association, I have worked directly with hundreds of individuals and families, connecting them to housing, workforce development, and education resources. I founded the Empower Her Entrepreneurial Program, writing the curriculum myself and personally taught 3 cohorts and helped twenty seven women to build their own businesses.

I am also a small business owner, a certified life coach, and President of SMC Associates.

As your Trustee, I will bring collaborative leadership, fiscal responsibility, and sound judgment to the Board. I will listen, ask thoughtful questions, and work to keep Santa Monica College accessible, affordable, and responsive to every student; from those arriving straight from high school to those returning later in life, as I did. VOTE KERA BLADES-SNELL
