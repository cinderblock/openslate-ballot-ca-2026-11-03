STATEMENT OF STEVEN KUNG

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
5th District

CITY OF MONTEREY PARK GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 49

Occupation: Filmmaker/Community Organizer

Monterey Park made history by becoming the first city in the nation to ban data centers by popular vote; I was one of the leaders of this movement, and I'm extremely proud of what we accomplished. Together with neighbors and local organizers, I co-founded No Data Center Monterey Park. At rallies and council meetings, I pushed back against corporate lobbyists, demanded transparency from City Hall, and called out the developer's intimidation tactics. I held those in power accountable while building the coalitions needed to win.

This fight showed me what's possible when a community organizes around shared values such as protecting clean air, quiet neighborhoods, and affordability. Winning by a landslide made me dream of all the things we could accomplish with this momentum: enhance the walkability of the city, build more housing, revitalize our downtown, and develop Saturn Park into the neighborhood hub we want it to be. Our victory presents a unique opportunity to realize that vision on city council. I'm running to represent District 5 because I... you... we all love Monterey Park enough to take responsibility for it.
