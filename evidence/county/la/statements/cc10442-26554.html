STATEMENT OF CHIA-YU TENG

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
4th District

CITY OF DIAMOND BAR GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Medical Doctor

Serving on the Diamond Bar City Council has been the honor of my life. It has deepened my understanding of what matters most to our residents: quality of life, safe neighborhoods, responsible government, and a prosperous future. I am running to build on that work with proven leadership and a clear vision.

My commitment rests on four priorities. First, safety comes first. I will support well-staffed and equipped law enforcement and rapid emergency response so families feel protected on every street, park, and school.

Second, I will guard your tax dollars with discipline. Long-term stability comes from responsible budgeting, not from raising taxes on hardworking families and small businesses.

Third, a thriving local economy fuels our quality of life. I want Diamond Bar to be a place where small businesses flourish, and entrepreneurs feel welcome.

Fourth, protecting our environment safeguards our health and future. I will work to preserve the green spaces and natural beauty that make our city special.

Each of these priorities reinforces the other; together they create a Diamond Bar that is safe, financially sound, and vibrant. I would be honored to earn your vote. Join me in building a stronger Diamond Bar for generations to come.
