STATEMENT OF KURT GONSKA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE RENT CONTROL BOARD

CITY OF SANTA MONICA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 39

Occupation: Producer/Campaign Manager

Kurt Gonska currently serves as a Commissioner on the Santa Monica Rent Control Board. Elected in 2022, he served as Board Chair in 2025. Living in rent-controlled apartments in Santa Monica since 2014, Gonska is a strong supporter of rent control and has fought to protect and strengthen it because it enables tens of thousands of middle class and low income people to contribute to the vitality of Santa Monica. Rent control stabilizes our community and provides cost predictability to the people who call Santa Monica home.

During his four years on the Board, Gonska was instrumental to increasing standards for how much tenants can receive in rent decreases for loss of amenities or deteriorating conditions, banning algorithmic price fixing software, developing Charter amendments that are on the ballot this November, and hiring a new Rent Control Executive Director and General Counsel.

Gonska's professional experience includes producing live events, marketing, and political campaign management.

Kurt Gonska stands on the shoulders of the activists who delivered rent control to Santa Monica in 1979 and fought to protect it for decades. He will continue fighting to fix the housing affordability crisis so that everyone in our community can thrive.
