STATEMENT OF RAFAEL SANTOYA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
1st District

CITY OF MONTEBELLO GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Los Angeles County Employee

My name is Dr. Rafael Santoya, and Montebello is our home. I am a husband, father, first-generation college student, Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist, and Los Angeles County employee. I hold a Ph.D. and bring more than 15 years of experience in quality assurance, accountability, program improvement, and public service.

My family and I live here, our children attend school here, and we are invested in seeing our community thrive.

I am running for City Council to help keep Montebello a safe, strong place to live, work, and raise our families. My priorities include public safety, strong schools, responsible city services, supporting local businesses, and ensuring our police and fire departments have the resources they need to serve our community.

I believe good leadership starts with listening and working together. I am committed to collaborating with residents, city staff, and my fellow Councilmembers, respecting different perspectives and keeping our community at the center of decisions.

I am proud to call Montebello home and would be honored to represent District 1. In unity there is strength, and together we can continue moving Montebello forward.
