STATEMENT OF SERGIO JOSEPH CALDERON

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 4

WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICT OF SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA

In 2022, the voters honored me by electing me as their public servant and Director of the Water Replenishment District of Southern California, Division 4. Since taking office, I have worked to protect our groundwater by keeping it safe, sustainable, and affordable. Today, groundwater remains the most affordable water supply in SoCal.

As a teacher, healthcare professional, and lifelong resident who lives and works within Division 4, I understand our communities' needs firsthand.

In 2026, I was unanimously appointed Board President, reflecting the Board's confidence in my leadership, integrity, and commitment to public service. This distinction gives the communities I represent a strong voice in shaping the future of our regional water supply.

During my term, I helped secure more than $90 million in federal and state funding to address toxins known as PFAS, or "forever chemicals," improve groundwater quality, and strengthen water infrastructure.

I remain committed to government accountability, fiscal responsibility, and protecting taxpayers while safeguarding our most valuable natural resource.

Proven experience matters. I respectfully ask for your vote to continue providing experienced, honest leadership while working to ensure a safe, reliable, and affordable groundwater supply for today's families and generations to come.
