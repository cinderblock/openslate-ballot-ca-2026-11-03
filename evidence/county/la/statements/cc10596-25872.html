STATEMENT OF STEVE GIBSON

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 3

PASADENA AREA COMMUNITY COLLEGE DISTRICT

Dr. Steve Gibson has served as Area 3's Pasadena City College Trustee since 2022. A proud PCC graduate (AA, 2010), he went on to earn a Master's in Communications and a PhD in Education. PCC changed his life, and he has worked every day to ensure it does the same for the next generation.

On the Board, Gibson has fought to strengthen PCC Northwest at John Muir High School, expand career and workforce training, and keep college affordable and accessible.
When the Eaton Fire devastated Altadena and Northwest Pasadena, Gibson's family lost their home. Like so many neighbors, they chose to stay and rebuild. He knows this community's grief and resilience personally, and he championed PCC's recovery efforts, including establishing the construction trades lab and fire academy to train the workers who will rebuild and protect our community.
A lifelong educator and community organizer, Gibson has spent his career fighting for healthcare access, climate justice, housing, and community empowerment.

"It has been the honor of my life to serve this community. There is more work to do, and I respectfully ask for your vote." — Steve Gibson
