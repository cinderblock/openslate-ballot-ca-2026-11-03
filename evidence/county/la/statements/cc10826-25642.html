STATEMENT OF DAVID DEJUTE

CANDIDATE FOR JUDGE OF THE SUPERIOR COURT
Office No. 87

Since graduating from Harvard Law School, David DeJute has practiced at the highest levels of the legal profession, teaching future attorneys at Pepperdine Caruso School of Law, overseeing litigation for Sony Pictures Entertainment, directing a legal clinic providing free legal help for Californians damaged by the Woolsey, Eaton and Palisades wildfires, and serving as an Assistant United States Attorney, where he was honored to represent President Barack Obama.

David is the only candidate in this race endorsed by the Los Angeles County Democratic Party. He has also earned the support of Stonewall Democratic Club, HONOR PAC, Avance Democratic Club, CAL and a broad coalition of elected officials and community leaders throughout Los Angeles County, including his former opponent in this race, Sharee Sanders Gordon.

David believes our courts should reflect the people and communities they serve. He and his husband, Eric, have shared forty years together while raising their son, Drake. Along with his distinguished legal career, those experiences have given David a broad perspective and a deep appreciation for the diverse communities that make Los Angeles County unique.

Vote for a judge whose experience, values, and life reflect Los Angeles and the City we want it to be.
