STATEMENT OF KIMBERLY COBOS CAWTHORNE

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
1st District

CITY OF MONTEBELLO GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Community Activist/Businesswoman

Montebello has been my home my entire life. As your former Mayor and Council Member, I have dedicated years to fighting for fiscal accountability, public safety, and neighborhood empowerment. I successfully championed Measure H, securing over $48 million in dedicated, local tax revenue to protect vital city services — money that stays here in Montebello. Through my ongoing work as Founder of Moving Montebello Forward, I continue advocating for a safer, stronger, and more prosperous community.

When elected, my priorities are clear: expanding resources for our local first responders; keeping every tax dollar working for Montebello; supporting small businesses and creating good local jobs; and investing in our youth programs and senior services so every generation is cared for.

As a further commitment to fiscal responsibility, I will not accept a city council salary when elected — every dollar saved goes back to serving you.

I can not do this alone — I need your support. Together, we will move Montebello forward. I respectfully ask for your vote for District 1 Council Member.
