STATEMENT OF MAYRA AGUILUZ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF MAYWOOD GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 33

Occupation: Licensed Therapist

My Fellow Maywood Residents,

Serving as your City Councilmember over the past four years has been one of the greatest honors of my life. Thank you for placing your trust in me and allowing me to serve our community with transparency, integrity, compassion, and accountability.

Together, we have made meaningful progress. We restored fiscal stability by balancing the city's budget and rebuilding our emergency reserves. We invested in infrastructure improvements, renovated our parks, expanded our urban forestry efforts, and created more community events that bring our families together. These accomplishments were possible because we worked together with a shared vision for a stronger Maywood.

While we have achieved a great deal, our work is not finished. Important projects are already underway, and I am seeking your support for re-election to ensure they are completed while continuing to protect our city's financial future.

As a Licensed Professional Clinical Counselor, I have dedicated my career to helping others, and I bring that same commitment to public service every day. I will continue to lead with honesty, love, compassion, and the highest ethical standards because Maywood deserves leadership that puts people first.

Together, we will keep moving Maywood forward.
