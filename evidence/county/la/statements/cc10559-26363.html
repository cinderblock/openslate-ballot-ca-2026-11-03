STATEMENT OF ANTHONY J. FIDALEO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 1

WEST BASIN MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICT

As a long-time CFO CPA and resident of RPV for over 30 years, I am running for West Basin Municipal Water District Director, Division 1, to secure a safe, reliable, sustainable, and cost-effective water supply for our families. Our region faces critical challenges from climate volatility, aging infrastructure, rising costs, fires, and projected severe water shortages from Colorado River supply limits. I will bring fiscal accountability, strict transparency, and proactive leadership to the board. My professional background equips me to oversee complex water-related issues; I have served as the financial expert board member on OriginClear and as former acting CFO of Santa Clara Water Waste. My primary focus will be expanding our world-class water recycling facilities, enhancing local conservation incentives, and achieving true water independence to reduce our reliance on expensive imported water. I am committed to protecting our coastal environment while keeping rates affordable for hard-working families and small businesses across Carson, the Palos Verdes Peninsula, and Rancho Dominguez. Together, we can modernize infrastructure, implement innovative sustainability, and guarantee long-term water security. I respectfully ask for your vote to bring dedicated, common-sense leadership to the District.
