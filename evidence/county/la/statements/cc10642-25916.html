STATEMENT OF REYNA E. DIAZ

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

DUARTE UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 69

Occupation: Governing Board Member, DUSD Trustee 5

For almost three decades, it has been my privilege to serve the students, families, and community of Duarte Unified School District. Throughout my years on the Board of Education, I have remained committed to putting students first and ensuring every child has the opportunity to succeed.

One of the greatest honors has been earning the trust of families. Over the years, I have had the opportunity to listen to parents' questions, concerns, and challenges, helping families navigate the school system and connect with the resources their children need to succeed. Listening and being accessible, responsive, and accountable to our community has always been very important to me.

One of my greatest joys each year is standing on the graduation stage and presenting diplomas to our students. That moment represents years of hard work, perseverance, and the shared commitment of families, our educators, and our community. It reminds me why I chose to serve and why I remain dedicated to helping every Duarte student achieve their dreams.

I am also proud of the progress we have made in strengthening our schools. Thanks to the community's support of our bond, we have modernized campuses and created 21st-century learning environments that will benefit our students for generations to come.
