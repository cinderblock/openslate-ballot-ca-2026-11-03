STATEMENT OF MARITZA C. TRAVANTI

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

MONROVIA UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 59

Occupation: Incumbent - IRS Operations (Retired)

As your current Board President, I am honored to seek another term to continue the experienced, steady leadership our district needs as we continue implementing Measure MM and address the opportunities and challenges ahead. I have brought common sense and sound judgement to every decision while always putting students first.

As the parent of a recent Monrovia Unified graduate and the daughter of immigrants, I know firsthand how public education creates opportunity and changes lives. I have helped lead our district through some of its most difficult decisions and challenging times, including the pandemic, declining enrollment, and critical fiscal stabilization efforts that protect educational opportunities for future generations.

I am proud to have expanded student opportunities through equitable access to Advanced Placement exams, counselors at all elementary schools, strengthened partnerships with the city and community organizations, and supported programs that help every student thrive.

Public service has been my lifelong commitment. After retiring from a 35-year federal career as an IRS Chief of Operations, I have continued serving our community by applying my experience leading large organizations, managing complex operations, overseeing budgets, and advancing strategic initiatives.

I respectfully ask for your vote to continue serving our students, families, and community.
