STATEMENT OF FRE'DRISHA "SHA" DIXION

CANDIDATE FOR MAYOR

CITY OF INGLEWOOD PRIMARY NOMINATING ELECTION

As Mayor of Inglewood, I'm committed to bringing every neighborhood up to the same standard as our thriving entertainment district because every resident and business deserves safe streets, opportunity, and a high quality of life.

I'm a mother, business owner, community organizer, and lawyer who has spent over a decade advocating for our community. My experience, education, and training prepares me to lead with compassion, integrity, fiscal responsibility, and results.

As Mayor, I'll eliminate deficits, create revenue streams, revitalize Market Street, support small businesses, create youth programs, expand senior services, improve our parks, solve traffic challenges, create services for the unhoused, implement stronger rent control, and make City Hall transparent, accessible, and responsive.

Public safety means protecting our community through trust, accountability, and professionalism in our police department. I'll implement policies that protect the rights and dignity of all residents and ensure our city isn't a place where people are targeted, intimidated, or harmed by ICE or our police.

I'm not seeking this office for a title. I'm seeking it to serve the people. I'll bring transparency, competence, accountability, compassion, and solutions to City Hall. It's time for Inglewood's next chapter, and I'm the best person to usher it in.
