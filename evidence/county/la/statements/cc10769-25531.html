STATEMENT OF ANTHONY PERRY

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
2nd District

CITY OF COMPTON GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 46

Occupation: Teacher/Business Owner

I am Anthony Perry, your candidate for Compton City Council, District 2. As a proud husband, father, and citizen of the city of Compton, I am committed to delivering real results for our city.

My Fortune 500 company experience has equipped me with proven skills in strategy, operations, and attracting investments. I know how major organizations create jobs, manage resources efficiently, and execute projects that deliver lasting impact. I will bring that expertise to City Hall to strengthen Compton's future.

For over two decades, I have dedicated myself to community service as an educator, organizer, and small business owner—coaching youth, supporting families, and working alongside residents to build stronger neighborhoods.

My focus is clear: economic development that cleans up our city and brings financial resources home. I will attract responsible investments, support local businesses, revitalize neglected areas, improve infrastructure, and secure grants and partnerships that create jobs and improve quality of life—without overburdening residents.

Compton has tremendous potential. With my business-tested leadership and deep community roots, we can make our streets cleaner, our economy stronger, and our future brighter.

I ask for your vote, trust, and partnership. Together, we will move Compton forward.

Anthony Perry
