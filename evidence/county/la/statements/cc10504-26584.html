STATEMENT OF JOHN CRUIKSHANK

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF RANCHO PALOS VERDES GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Vote John Cruikshank for Rancho Palos Verdes City Council – November 3

I'm a husband, father, civil engineer, local business owner, and community volunteer. I previously served as Mayor, two-term City Councilmember, and Planning Commissioner, and hold bachelor's and master's degrees from USC.

We love Rancho Palos Verdes for its views, quiet nights, strong neighborhoods, and neighborly bonds. These qualities require practical, resident-first leadership.

As your former Councilmember I fought for the highest quality of life. As a business owner I know how to balance budgets and deliver results. As a volunteer, I've served on local boards and as an advanced soccer referee.

I'm running to protect and enhance our city with these commitments:

- Be your independent voice at City Hall
- Maintain excellent Sheriff and Fire services
- Keep the City free of budget deficits
- Hold utility companies accountable
- Strengthen ties with neighboring cities
- Simplify City processes

Proudly endorsed by past and current City leaders and neighbors, I will bring measured, determined leadership focused on results and preserving what makes RPV special.

I respectfully ask for your vote on November 3.

John Cruikshank
