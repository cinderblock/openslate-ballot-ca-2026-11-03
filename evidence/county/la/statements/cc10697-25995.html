STATEMENT OF ANOUK HARVEY

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

EASTSIDE UNION SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Teacher

The schools we build today will shape what is possible for Eastside's children tomorrow. My educational journey began in the Antelope Valley, and I have chosen to build my career in service to this community.

I attended Lancaster Baptist School and SOAR High School, earned Associate Degrees in Liberal Arts and Humanities and Social and Behavioral Sciences from Antelope Valley College, and graduated from UCLA with a Bachelor's degree in Sociology and a minor in Spanish. Since then, I have taught 4th, 6th, and currently 7th grade.

In the classroom, I have watched educators set high expectations, meet students where they are, and work tirelessly to help them reach the standard. I also know that adjusting some policies could make a huge difference.

We need greater consistency for students, stronger communication with families, greater appreciation for teachers and staff, and programs chosen for their results, not simply because they satisfy a requirement. Our schools should also help children develop confidence, emotional intelligence, and pride in learning from an early age.

As a Deputy Mayor, I am helping lead a citywide effort focused on third-grade literacy and measurable student outcomes. Eastside students deserve a district as ambitious about their futures as they are capable of becoming.
