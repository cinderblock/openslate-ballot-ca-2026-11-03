STATEMENT OF SOO YOO

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 2

ABC UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 59

Occupation: ABC Unified School District Board Member

As an incumbent ABC Unified School District Board Member and two-time Board President, I have had the privilege of serving our students, families, and community through challenging and changing times. As Board President during the COVID-19 pandemic, I helped lead our district through an unprecedented period while keeping our schools open. During my board service, campuses were secured with gates and video camera systems to strengthen school safety.

I immigrated to America from Korea at age nine and grew up in a predominantly Latino neighborhood in Los Angeles. This experience taught me the importance of respecting different cultures and working together. Spanish became my third language, and my trilingual and tricultural background has helped me connect with diverse community.

I earned a B.A. from UC Irvine and an M.A. from Westminster Theological Seminary. I am the retired founder and director of a college preparatory tutoring center, where I served families for over 30 years. I have also served as a youth pastor and pastor's wife.

As a mother of four daughters, educator, youth counselor, and longtime community volunteer, I value academic achievement, safe schools, student well-being, fiscal responsibility, and strong partnerships among parents, teachers, students, and our community.
