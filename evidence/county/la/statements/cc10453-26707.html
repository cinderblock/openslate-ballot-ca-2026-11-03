STATEMENT OF MICHAEL ALLAWOS

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
1st District

CITY OF GLENDORA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 63

Occupation: Councilmember/Business Owner

Councilman and previous Mayor Michael Allawos has spent the last nine years on Glendora's City Council protecting and enhancing our small-town way of life and hometown values. As the representative for western Glendora's District 1, and the entire city, he has kept his promises by supporting Police and Fire with needed resources, expanding government transparency and communication, and standing with Glendora students and their parents.

Councilman Allawos has upheld strong Glendora development standards, preserved low-density planning, and supported local businesses and community development. He continues to communicate Glendora's needs to Sacramento and regional agencies, ensuring our voice is heard.

Working closely with city staff, Councilman Allawos has advanced improvements to roads, streetlights, and essential infrastructure. He remains committed to community-based public safety, addressing homelessness with outreach services, temporary shelter, and long-term strategies.

Financially, Glendora is stronger than ever, with record reserves and a triple AAA bond rating.

The next four years are too important to take risks with Glendora's future. Councilman Allawos brings a steady, calm hand and a proven problem-solving approach. He has kept his word to fight for our hometown values, always putting Glendora first, asking the hard questions, and always listening.
