STATEMENT OF CYNTHIA NG MONJE

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
3rd District

CITY OF ARCADIA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Administrator

I'm a widowed mother of two sons & proudly call Arcadia home since 1999. I have lived in District 3 for 14 years, where I experienced firsthand the strength & compassion of our community.

My husband passed in 2006, Although it was a difficult time, the kindness and support of our Arcadia neighbors helped us feel safe, stay strong, and find hope.

That experience inspired my commitment to serve. I am running for Arcadia City Council because our residents deserve leadership that is transparent, accountable, and puts our community first.

I support responsible growth and will be tough on overdevelopment that threatens our neighborhoods, infrastructure, & quality of life.

I will prioritize public safety by supporting the hiring of additional police officers, improving traffic flow around our schools, and making streets safer for students, families, and pedestrians.

I also support environmental sustainability and preserving the character that makes Arcadia wonderful.

I have no affiliation with any groups that do not align with the USA. I will always serve with integrity, independence, and a commitment to representing the residents of Arcadia. It would be an honor to earn your trust and your vote.
