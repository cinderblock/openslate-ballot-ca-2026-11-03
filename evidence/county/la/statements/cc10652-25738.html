STATEMENT OF DALLAS LAWRENCE

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

LAS VIRGENES UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Governing Board Member, Las Virgenes Unified School District

Experienced Leadership:

- LVUSD School Board Member
- Former Navy Officer
- Former Presidential Appointee to the U.S. Department of Education
- Former College Instructor, USC
- BA: UC Berkeley; MA: Johns Hopkins

As your School Board Member and as a current LVUSD parent of three students, I've focused on one goal these past four years: ensuring every student has the opportunity to succeed in a safe, supportive, and academically excellent school.

Together, we've raised student achievement in math and reading, made historic investments in student mental health and counseling, strengthened campus security, and improved school facilities.

We adopted the region's most comprehensive cell phone ban policy, helping students stay engaged in the classroom while improving attendance, reducing bullying, and creating a healthier learning environment. I also helped lead efforts to implement the region's strongest safety ban for high-speed e-bikes to better protect our students.

Looking ahead, my focus is clear: continue raising academic achievement, ensure technology is truly supporting learning by reducing unnecessary classroom screen time, maintain the safest schools possible, and prepare every student for success in college, careers, and life.

It's been an honor to serve our community, and I would be grateful for your vote.
