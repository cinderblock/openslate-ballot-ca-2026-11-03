STATEMENT OF RAUL PARDO

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

EL MONTE UNION HIGH SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 64

Occupation: Longshoreman

As a teacher, coach, City Council member, Public Health Commissioner, Mayor, and board member of local health and economic development organizations, I have dedicated my career to serving our community. This experience has prepared me to be an effective and responsible member of the El Monte Union High School District Board of Trustees.

My priority will always be our students. I will support strong academic programs, modern technology, career technical education, and partnerships with colleges, labor organizations, and local employers. Every student should graduate prepared for college, an apprenticeship, or a family-supporting career.

I will also work to recruit and retain exceptional teachers and staff, maintain safe and clean campuses, and ensure our schools are free from discrimination, harassment, and intimidation. Our employees deserve the resources and support they need to help every student succeed.

As a trustee, I will insist on transparency, fiscal responsibility, and accountability. Educational dollars must be used efficiently and directly for the benefit of students—not for personal interests or political agendas.

I am prepared to lead with experience, integrity, and an unwavering commitment to our students, employees, families, and community. I respectfully ask for your vote.
