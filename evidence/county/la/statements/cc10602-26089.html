STATEMENT OF DARLENE TREVINO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF TRUSTEES,
Trustee Area 1

SANTA CLARITA COMMUNITY COLLEGE DISTRICT

As your elected Board Trustee, it's an honor to represent our families and students. I was once a College of the Canyons student and went on to build a successful law practice. Serving the community that helped shape my future is personal to me. I remain committed to providing students with an affordable, high-quality education through accountable, transparent leadership. Here's my record and priorities:

Students First

- Ensure access to an affordable, high-quality education for every student.
- Prioritize funding for more classes and the tools students need.
- Strengthen the Associated Student Government to build future leaders.

Transparency and Accountability

- Advance transparency in public funding through audits and regular status reports.
- Hold top administrators accountable and hire leaders who take ownership and act with integrity.

Delivering Results

- Spearheaded the hiring of our new Superintendent.
- Championed the development of our Advanced Technology Center to expand workforce training in advanced manufacturing, welding, and robotics.

While we've made meaningful progress, our work is far from finished. With your support, we can continue building an even stronger College of the Canyons for today's students and generations to come. I respectfully ask for your vote.
