STATEMENT OF LUZ YESENIA PAEZ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
1st District

CITY OF DUARTE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 39

Occupation: Public Utility Analyst

Endorsed by: Duarte Mayor Tera Martin Del Campo, Mayor Pro-Tem Sam Kang, Councilmember Cesar Garcia, and Councilmember Vin Truong

As a longtime resident, I believe Duarte needs a fresh breath of air. Every City reaches a point where it must ask an important question: What kind of Leadership do we need for the future? Duarte is changing and we need someone with fresh ideas and energy. The new challenges we are facing require not only experience but also a fresh perspective with leadership that reflects what Duarte is becoming. This Campaign is not about looking backward; it's about looking forward to today and the future.

For Duarte to thrive, we need a NEW LEADER who combines experience with new approaches.

- I have over 16 years of proven public service experience, an understanding of local government, and a new approach to address today's challenges;
- As the Planning & Economic Development Chair, we have approved several new businesses, including Vallarta Supermarket, but we need more options;
- We continue voting on updated codes to adapt and simplify the process for existing businesses, residents, and new businesses. However, there is still more work to get done;
- Active Member and Supporter of Kiwanis, Duarte Teen Center, and Route 66 Parade Committee. I would like to encourage our community to be more involved;
- B.A. Psychology, Cal Poly Pomona University and Master of Public Administration, University of La Verne.

Duarte has a proud history and it's time to build on that foundation. Duarte deserves a LEADER who will embrace creative solutions to the issues we face today. I will collaborate with local and neighboring leadership, listen to our constituents and business owners, and bring ENERGY to be present at ALL MEETINGS. I would like to focus on investing in our Public Safety, attracting Economic Development so we don't have to shop and dine in neighboring cities, supporting local businesses, and strengthening our community programs to enrich the lives of the next generations.

When elected, I will prioritize fiscal responsibility, public safety, park improvements, economic development, and support small businesses. I would like to see Duarte retain its small-town charm while also attracting businesses important to our community. VOTE - Luz Yesenia Paez to take a seat as your Councilmember in District 1. Together, we can build a stronger, safer, and more vibrant DUARTE.
