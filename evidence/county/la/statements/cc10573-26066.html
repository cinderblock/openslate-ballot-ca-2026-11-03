STATEMENT OF PAMELA FORD

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

ANTELOPE VALLEY COMMUNITY COLLEGE DISTRICT

For 30 years, I've devoted my life to AVC and the people it serves. I've helped students facing poverty, incarceration, and hardships who often found education beyond reach. I understand those struggles. As a single mother who once relied on public assistance, I built a successful career while raising my children, and have never forgotten what families must overcome.

I was the president of AVC's classified employees union for 18 years. I've been a determined and effective advocate: Securing multimillion-dollar relief for classified employees for unlawful working conditions through litigation that reached the California Supreme Court; pushing the current board to continue funding student recruitment visits to historically Black universities and successfully negotiating Indigenous Peoples and Farm Worker's Day holidays.

My record is not limited to one group or one issue. I've defended and advocated for students, employees, families, and the integrity of the institution itself. I believe a college succeeds when people are treated fairly, resources are used responsibly, and every student is given a genuine opportunity to succeed.

I bring courage, sound judgment, compassion, and deep institutional experience. As an AVC Board Member, I'll be a strong voice for students, employees, families, and the entire Antelope Valley community.
