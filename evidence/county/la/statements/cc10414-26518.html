STATEMENT OF EDWARD J. ALVAREZ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
3rd District

CITY OF AZUSA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 61

Occupation: Councilmember/Federal Auditor

Dear Neighbors,

I thank you for your confidence in me to serve as a Councilmember for the past 13 years. I am proud of what we have accomplished through the years, such as developing the downtown and bringing a variety of local businesses.

In those years, we have also taken a financial budget with no reserve funds, to a reserve of over $50M and have invested heavily in capital improvement projects such as our Library, Senior Center, and soon the Woman's Club, to name a few.

I continue to work as a Federal Auditor and I am a Certified Fraud Specialist, a small business owner in Azusa, and retired from the US Navy. I also am a graduate with a Masters in Business Administration.

I hope to continue to serve the Azusa community and steadily increase our reserve funds and bring more businesses to Azusa and maintain a balanced budget. We have also worked to increase community events to help make Azusa a great place to work, live, and play.

I hope to have your support on November 3, 2026.
