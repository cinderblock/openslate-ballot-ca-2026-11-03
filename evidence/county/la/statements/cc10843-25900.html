STATEMENT OF JASMIN JAUREGUI PAULSON

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 2

MONTEBELLO UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 41

Occupation: Educator/Parent

Every parent, no matter their circumstances, wants the same thing: a safe school, great teachers, and every opportunity for their child to succeed. Whether you're balancing work and family, volunteering in your child's classroom, advocating for a child with an IEP, or simply helping with homework after a long day, you deserve a voice in your child's education.

As a mother, substitute teacher, PTA leader, and former social worker, I have dedicated my life to supporting children and families. As the mother of a six-year-old attending Montebello Unified schools, I understand the hopes, challenges, and responsibilities that come with raising a child today. Like many parents, I also understand the importance of ensuring children receive the support and services they need to reach their full potential.

For nearly 12 years, my family has proudly called Montebello home. Throughout my career, I have worked alongside children, parents, and educators—first as a social worker serving at-risk youth and families, and now as a substitute teacher in our schools. As the elected Secretary of the La Merced Academy PTA, I have partnered with parents, teachers, and school leaders to strengthen family engagement and create opportunities that help students succeed.

I am running for the Montebello Unified School District Governing Board because I believe every child deserves a high-quality education, every educator deserves respect and support, and every family deserves to be heard.

As both a parent and an educator, every decision I make will begin with one simple question: Would this be the decision I would want for my own son and for every child in our district? That principle will guide every decision I make.

I will advocate for stable leadership, academic excellence, fiscal responsibility, transparency, safe schools, quality mental health and student support services, and strong partnerships between families, educators, and our community.

Together, we can restore trust, strengthen our schools, and ensure every child—regardless of their background, abilities, or circumstances—has the opportunity to learn, grow, and reach their full potential.

I have stood with families as a social worker, supported students as an educator, partnered with parents through the PTA, and now I hope to serve our entire community as your Trustee.

It would be an honor to earn your trust and your vote for Montebello Unified School District Governing Board, Trustee Area No. 2.
