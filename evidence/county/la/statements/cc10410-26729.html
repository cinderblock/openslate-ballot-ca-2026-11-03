STATEMENT OF ADELE ANDRADE STADLER

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
Fifth District

CITY OF ALHAMBRA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

I'm Adele Andrade Stadler and I'm running for re-election to Alhambra City Council District 5.

I want to build on my record of getting things done for our community.

During this term I initiated the first inclusionary housing ordinance to require developers to include affordable housing in their plan. I secured funding for the first supportive housing project with 44 units for low income families. I initiated the first community workforce agreement, including 100 Unions. I initiated the smoke-free housing ordinance and banned the sale of flavored tobacco to our schoolchildren. I secured funding for the pedestrian safety and bike plan and created a traffic calming ordinance to address neighborhood traffic and environmental impacts. I will support workforce housing for Teachers, Nurses, Construction, and essential workers.

I will continue safeguarding our families and build on our neighborhood watch successes. I will continue to support broad community engagement and collaborate with partnerships to invest in our city. I will continue to respect our diverse community, by supporting multilingual resources. Alhambra has challenges, and opportunities. We must work together to shape a better future for Alhambra.
