STATEMENT OF HENRY P. HERNANDEZ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS

LA PUENTE VALLEY COUNTY WATER DISTRICT

Henry Hernandez has been a La Puente resident for more than 65 years. He is a retired Department of Defense employee who served for 40 years as a Quality Assurance Representative. I am completing my fourth full term as a Board Director. Appointed as President and Vice President during my full terms. My goals are to see the district water plan is incorporated and accomplished. Work with fellow appointed directors on implementation of initiatives and resolution of water improvements. Supportive of the needs of the community to have a successful administration. Have helped to maintain lower rate increases to water providers other than surrounding communities. Some of district's accomplishments for the past 4 years.

1. Maintain Districts revenues and Expenses.

2. Investing in Infrastructure.

3. Final permitting steps to Water Treatment Facility (PVOU-IZ).

4. Offering Paperless Billing.

5. District's mission of providing high quality water.
