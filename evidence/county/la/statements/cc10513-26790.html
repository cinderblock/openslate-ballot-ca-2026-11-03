STATEMENT OF ANNETTE RODRIGUEZ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF SANTA FE SPRINGS GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Human Resources Manager

Dear Residents,

I am proud to call Santa Fe Springs home. My husband and I have been residents of this wonderful community for more than 33 years. We raised our children here, and they benefited from the excellent schools, dedicated teachers, and strong sense of community that make Santa Fe Springs such a great place to live.

A special thank you to all Veterans and the brave men and women serving in our Armed Forces. On behalf of all Americans, your service and commitment are deeply appreciated.

In 2018, I was honored to be elected to the Santa Fe Springs City Council. Since then, I have remained committed to listening to the needs and concerns of all residents. I work hard to represent our community with leadership, compassion and integrity.

Every resident matters, and your voice is important to me. I will continue to advocate for: Programs, Services, Services for our disabled, and Quality of Life that make Santa Fe Springs a great place for families, seniors, and future generations.

I respectfully ask for your support and vote on November 3, 2026. God Bless Santa Fe Springs!

Sincerely,

Annette Rodriguez
Mayor Pro-Tem
