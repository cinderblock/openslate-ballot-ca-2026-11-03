STATEMENT OF LETTY LOPEZ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
2nd District

CITY OF WEST COVINA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Director/Mayor/Councilwoman

As a wife, mother, and resident of West Covina, I am honored and grateful to currently serve as your Mayor and Councilwoman of West Covina.

With 25+ years in LA & Pasadena Parks and Recreation with a background as a Community Service Commissioner, I have dedicated my life to serving the people and my community. I plan on continuing that same path as your Councilwoman, using my experience to continue building:

Strong Communities: Partnering with organizations and schools that build upon great programs to serve our youth and seniors and assist those in need.

Public Safety: With populations over 100,000, we have been recognized as one of the safest cities in the US during my time in office.

Economic development: Launched "Taste of West Covina" to promote local businesses and bring in destinations for food, culture, and community like Haven City and Texas Roadhouse.

Safe & Clean Parks: Through the Parks Master Plan, I will continue my efforts to keep parks clean and safe for our residents to gather and children to play

I humbly ask for your vote to continue building a safer, stronger, and more vibrant West Covina for generations to come. Re-elect Letty Lopez on November 3rd!
