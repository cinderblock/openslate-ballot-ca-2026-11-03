STATEMENT OF ABBY VELTMAN

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

MONROVIA UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Educator

Public service is one of the greatest gifts neighbors can give one another. I am grateful to everyone who has stepped forward over the years as servant-leaders for Monrovia's students, families, staff, and schools.

Their commitment has helped shape the community we value today. As we look to the future, I hope to continue that tradition of service by bringing thoughtful oversight, careful listening, and real-world, practical classroom perspectives to the School Board.

As a parent of children currently attending schools in the District, a seasoned educator, and a long-time Monrovian, I understand the opportunities and challenges our students, families, and teachers are experiencing today because I am experiencing them alongside our community.

The decisions I would help make today are decisions my own family, along with yours, will live with tomorrow and for years to come. Together, we can provide a unified, wrap-around approach to supporting Monrovia families.

If a thoughtful, collaborative parent and educator sounds like the kind of person you want helping to lead our schools, I would be honored to earn your vote.
