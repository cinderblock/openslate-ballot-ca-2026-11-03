STATEMENT OF GUSTAVO V. CAMACHO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF PICO RIVERA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

As your Mayor, it has been the honor of my life to serve the residents of Pico Rivera. Together, we have strengthened our city's financial future by maintaining balanced budgets, building strong financial reserves, and making responsible decisions that protect taxpayer dollars while investing in our community.

Together, we have improved public safety, welcomed new businesses and investment, created a new dog park, enhanced neighborhoods through citywide landscape beautification, and launched ongoing road improvements across the city. My commitment to strong financial management and responsible planning has contributed to property values increasing at a higher rate than many neighboring cities, reflecting continued confidence in Pico Rivera's future. The new Smith Park Aquatic Center is under construction, and the revitalization of Whittier Boulevard will bring new businesses, create jobs, and expand opportunities.

Rio Hondo Park will soon begin its long-awaited reconstruction, adding another important community improvement and creating new recreational opportunities for families to enjoy.

While I am proud of what we have accomplished together, I remain committed to transparent leadership, fiscal responsibility, safe neighborhoods, and continued investment in projects that enhance our community. I respectfully ask for your vote so we can continue moving Pico Rivera forward together.
