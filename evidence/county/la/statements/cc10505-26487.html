STATEMENT OF PAT WILSON

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF ROLLING HILLS GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 70

Occupation: Business Owner/Councilmember

My name is Pat Wilson, and I am a candidate for City Council.

My family moved to Rolling Hills almost 30 years ago. My children were raised here, and two of them still reside here along with my three grandchildren. While my kids are grown up, I was very active in coaching them in AYSO Soccer, Rolling Hills Little League, and Palos Verdes Peninsula Girls Softball. I may have even coached some of your children! I can think of nothing more important for my family, and all the rest of Rolling Hills residents, than safety, security, and local control of our zoning authority.

That's why, as your City Councilmember, I have been actively involved in making our community the safest city in Southern California. I have been a member of the Traffic Commission for almost 20 years, working side by side with the Sheriff's Department to ensure safety in our community for motorists, pedestrians, and equestrians and managing speed limits through traffic enforcement.

Our residents also deserve security from fire danger and land movement. I have served as a member of the Palos Verdes Peninsula Regional Contract Law Committee and Palos Verdes Peninsula Public Safety Committee for many years now. These bodies strive to make the entire peninsula a safer place to live, work and recreate. Through these committees, the Automated License Reader and Peninsula- wide AI wildfire detection camera network were established.

Local control of our community development is critical. That is why I have strongly supported carefully planned residences compatible with their surroundings. It is essential that we maintain legal compliance with State law lest they attempt to usurp our local authority and impose their vision of housing on Rolling Hills.

My professional life involves running my business, Fast Lane Transportation, Inc., a trucking and container storage company which I started in 1979, creating jobs and supporting the logistics industry. I'm also a veteran having served in the United States Air Force.

Finally, you may have seen me around town playing drums with various musical groups.

All of these broad experiences help give my life balance and create the opportunities to share in our City Council deliberations. I feel that I have more to give and would be profoundly grateful if to earn your vote.

Respectfully,

Pat Wilson
