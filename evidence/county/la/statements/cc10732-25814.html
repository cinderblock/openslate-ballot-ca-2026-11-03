STATEMENT OF JENNIFER NAVARRO

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

WESTSIDE UNION SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Incumbent

WUSD has always been very special to me. I have lived in the Antelope Valley most of my life, and raised my two daughters here, both of whom attended WUSD schools. My family has owned and operated a small business in our community for nearly 50 years. Growing up in the world of small business has taught me it takes hard work and perseverance to achieve success.

WUSD students, teachers and administrators are doing amazing things, even under the most difficult of circumstances. Despite the challenges we have faced, our schools are doing noteworthy things and have received many state and national awards.

It takes a commitment and willingness to work as a team, even when respectfully disagreeing. These past two years have shown us challenges that none of us could have seen coming, but I remained committed to being a collaborative, level-headed and engaged board member representing our children, teachers, parents, and taxpayers to ensure that Westside continues to be the school district of choice in the Antelope Valley.

I remain passionate about providing a safe, positive, and quality learning environment for our children! I humbly ask for your vote to return me to the WUSD Governing Board.
