STATEMENT OF ALDEN "MARLON" GOBEL

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF WEST HOLLYWOOD GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

West Hollywood thrives on bold vision, authentic expression, and collaborative leadership. As an international fashion designer and creative director, I have spent my career turning ambitious ideas into reality by balancing artistic execution with rigorous operational strategy. My journey began with years of intensive art and design study at London's Central Saint Martins, followed by serving as Director of Operations for Thom Browne as well a Liaison to NASA's next generation space suit study.

When I launched my eponymous brand, MARLON GOBEL, it demanded absolute strategic precision along with skills that translated into immediate global partnerships with historic institutions like Bergdorf Goodman, Swarovski, and the Fashion Institute of Technology. Over a twenty-year career, my work has been recognized by The New York Times and trusted by legendary creative icons, because I deliver on bold ideas, resilience, and unique innovation.

I am running for City Council because West Hollywood deserves leadership that understands how to manage complex operations while preserving our distinct, rebellious, and authentic spirit. I will bring fiscal discipline, project management expertise, and an unwavering dedication to making our community a vibrant, sustainable, and forward-thinking destination for residents and creators alike. I would be honored to earn your vote. Let's build the future of WeHo together.
