STATEMENT OF CHRISTIAN IBARRA

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 2

LOS NIETOS SCHOOL DISTRICT

My name is Christian Ibarra. I have lived in the Los Nietos community for 16 years. My children have attended district schools from kindergarten through middle school, and one is currently enrolled.

I am a U.S. Navy veteran, statistician, data scientist, and parent of two children with special needs. These experiences taught me to listen carefully, study the facts, ask difficult questions, and work with others to solve problems.

I am running because our community deserves accountable leadership. Board members should review proposals carefully, question major expenditures, communicate with parents and employees, and base decisions on evidence.

My priorities are stronger support for special education, teachers, and school staff; better communication with families; responsible use of district funds; safe schools; and improved academic outcomes.

One board member cannot solve every problem alone. I will prepare for meetings, listen directly to parents and employees, raise their concerns publicly, and explain my decisions honestly.

I am a parent who wants every student to receive the same care, opportunity, and quality education I want for my own children. I respectfully ask for your vote.
