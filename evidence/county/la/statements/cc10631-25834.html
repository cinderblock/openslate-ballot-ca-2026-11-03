STATEMENT OF KRISTA CARSON ELHAI

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 1

CLAREMONT UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Educator

As a lifelong member of the Claremont community, a product of Claremont schools, a former CUSD teacher for 27 years, and a parent of three CUSD graduates, I understand what makes our district special - and what it needs to thrive.

For more than 40 years, I have served students and educators through public education and nonprofit leadership. As a national board member overseeing multi-million-dollar budgets, I have built consensus, asked tough questions, and made responsible decisions that strengthen organizations while keeping people at the center.

I will work to restore long-term fiscal stability, address declining enrollment by making Claremont Unified a destination district, and strengthen partnerships with educators, employee groups, families, and city leaders. I support innovative learning through thoughtful AI integration and research-based technology policies that encourage healthy screen time habits. I also believe we should prioritize student well-being, strengthen the school community, and explore environmentally sustainable improvements to our campuses.

I will bring transparency, collaboration, accountability, and practical leadership to every decision. Together, we can preserve what makes Claremont Unified exceptional while preparing our schools for the opportunities ahead.

I would be honored to earn your vote. Learn more at krista4claremont.com.
