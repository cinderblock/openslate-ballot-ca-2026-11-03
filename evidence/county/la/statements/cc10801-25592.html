STATEMENT OF AYANNA DAVIS

PARTY PREFERENCE: DEMOCRATIC

MEMBER OF THE STATE ASSEMBLY, 65th DISTRICT

Ayanna Davis is a lifelong educator who grew up in a union family in Compton that instilled in her the importance of public service.

After working her way through college, Ayanna became a classroom teacher and later a public school principal. As a member of the Compton Unified School Board, Ayanna has been a leader in the "Compton Miracle" that resulted in the number-one graduation rate in L.A. County and top test scores for similar districts statewide. She has created apprenticeship and workforce development programs to train local residents for good-paying jobs in construction, healthcare, auto repair and the food industry.

Ayanna is running for State Assembly to fight for more affordable communities for all of us. She will tackle the affordability crisis head-on by working to lower the cost of groceries and utilities, create local jobs in our communities, upgrade local infrastructure, and build more affordable housing for working families.

Ayanna will also fight back against the Trump Administration's attacks on our communities by protecting access to affordable healthcare and high-quality education.

As our Assemblymember, Ayanna will use her extensive background as an educator to fully fund our public schools, invest in teacher training, and expand student access to tutoring, after-school programs, and mental health services.

That's why Ayanna Davis is endorsed by:

Nurses and Healthcare Workers,

Teachers and Educators,

Local Firefighters and First Responders,

And the California Democratic Party.

By Tuesday, November 3, Vote Ayanna Davis for State Assembly.
