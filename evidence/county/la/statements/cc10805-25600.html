STATEMENT OF TESSA LYNN HODGE

PARTY PREFERENCE: DEMOCRATIC

UNITED STATES REPRESENTATIVE, 23rd DISTRICT

We need new leadership in Washington. We need someone who will tackle the affordability crisis. We need someone who will fight for homeowners when homeowner insurance companies refuse to do business here in our state. We need someone who will fight for our rural communities as we see hospitals and schools closing.

I get it. I'm a lifelong resident of our district and my husband and I have called this community home our entire lives, so I know the struggles we're all dealing with.

That's why I'm in this fight. To be our voice in Washington.
I'm proud to be endorsed by Republican and Democratic leaders, as well as many independents. I will be our voice in Washington.
As a Licensed Clinical Social Worker, I have seen the struggles of those in our community, and have worked to make our communities a safer place for all.

As a small business owner, I know what it's like to struggle with your bills. I know how hard it is to support your family. As the great granddaughter of immigrants from Mexico, I understand the pain and fear a lot of us are facing now.
We desperately need change here in our communities. We cannot go with the status quo.

I would be honored to earn your vote.
Tessa is a Licensed Clinical Social Worker who has worked in community based mental health, medical social work, and policy and legislation.
