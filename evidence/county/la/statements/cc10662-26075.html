STATEMENT OF JEREMY VANDERHAL

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

PALOS VERDES PENINSULA UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

My wife and I moved to Palos Verdes ten years ago for the outstanding schools. As a lifelong educator, including faculty at master's-level Test Pilot School and flight instructor, I'm running for School Board to maintain and strengthen the quality education our community expects and deserves.

To ensure my focus remains solely on our students, I created the Palos Verdes Peninsula Pledge: "I will not accept financial contributions from politicians, political parties, partisan PACs, or political organizations (including unions). If elected, I will serve my full term and will not run for any other office while on the Board." I invite all candidates to join this pledge and put students first.

My priorities are clear: high academic standards, safe and respectful campuses, fiscal responsibility paired with strong teacher support, and full parental transparency and involvement.

Our district receives $4,500 less per student than the state average — nearly $45 million each year. We need Board members who will actively advocate at the state level for fair funding so our students and teachers are not shortchanged.

I'm committed to practical governance that puts students first and to securing the funding needed to attract and retain the outstanding teachers our students deserve.
