STATEMENT OF DANIELLE ROMERO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
4th District
(Unexpired term ending November 7, 2028)

CITY OF MONTEBELLO SPECIAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Veteran Advocate/Councilmember

Serving as your appointed District 4 Councilmember has been the greatest honor of my life. I am running to continue serving The City of Montebello with integrity, transparency, and a commitment to putting residents first.

As an Army veteran, Accredited American Legion Veteran Service Officer, mother, and community advocate, I have dedicated my life to serving others. On the Council, I have worked collaboratively to improve our parks, strengthen public safety, invest in infrastructure, support local businesses, and protect taxpayer dollars through responsible fiscal decisions.

I believe every resident deserves to be heard and represented. My decisions are guided by what is best for our community today while planning responsibly for the future.

I respectfully ask for your vote to continue serving District 4 and all of The City of Montebello. Together, we can build on our progress, address future challenges with thoughtful leadership, and preserve the values that make our community a great place to live, work, and raise a family.

It would be my privilege to earn your trust and serve as your elected Councilmember.
