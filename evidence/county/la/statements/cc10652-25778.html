STATEMENT OF SANDRA POPE

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

LAS VIRGENES UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

I am running for the Las Virgenes Unified School District Board because I believe every student deserves an outstanding public education that prepares them for success in school, careers, and life.

For 40 years, I proudly served students, families, and our community as a Las Virgenes educator. I developed and supervised the LV Outdoor Education Program, taught incoming sixth-grade math remediation summer school, and taught adult education classes for California Lutheran University and the University of Phoenix.

My career has been defined by dedication, integrity, collaboration, and a commitment to helping every student reach their full potential. I value open communication and believe strong partnerships among teachers, parents, students, administrators, and the community are essential to student success.

As your board member, I will support rigorous academic standards, critical thinking, fiscal responsibility, transparency, and responsible decision-making. I will work to strengthen communication, encourage community involvement, and ensure every decision is guided by what is best for students.

Las Virgenes has a tradition of educational excellence. With decades of classroom experience and a deep understanding of our district, I am prepared to provide thoughtful leadership, listen respectfully, and work collaboratively to ensure our schools continue to offer exceptional educational opportunities for every student.
