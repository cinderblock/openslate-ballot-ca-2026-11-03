STATEMENT OF STEPHEN J. POST

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF WEST HOLLYWOOD GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

WeHo can't wait! We need bold leaders who will protect the welcoming and diverse community that so many of us are proud to call home.

I, Stephen Post, am a renter, nonprofit advocate, business improvement manager, and West Hollywood Public Safety Commissioner. Throughout my career, I've worked to advance criminal justice reform, strengthen local communities, and bring residents, businesses, and public officials together to solve difficult problems. I've organized community dialogues on topics such as drug reform, race relations, and community policing - training more than 50 moderators and engaging nearly 500 community members, students, local officials, and law enforcement officers. As a WeHo 2040 Strategic Plan team-member, I've gathered community input from our residents that will directly shape the next 15 years of our city.

ln my current full-time role as a Business Improvement District manager, I work directly with local LA neighborhoods to support over 300 small/local businesses, strengthen neighborhood infrastructure, and help make our streets cleaner and safer. I've also spent years advocating for criminal justice reform and fairer cannabis laws, and my career in advocacy has helped seal over 200,000 records in California.

As an Area Planning Commissioner, I've advanced different development projects by prioritizing community needs and input. While earning my Master's of Public Administration at The Ohio State University, I served as President representing over 13,000 graduate students; winning annual increases of worker wages, protecting immigrant families from being evicted during COVID-19, and ensuring adequate benefits for our most vulnerable.

I'll stand up for WeHo by increasing funding to homelessness prevention, implementing a rent reduction for stabilized units, expanding public affordable housing options, improving community safety, and making City Hall more transparent and accountable.

West Hollywood was founded by LGBTQ+ rights advocates and tenants rights advocates who refused to remain invisible. We can't be afraid to build on their progress by supporting our legacy businesses, investing in climate-resilient infrastructure, and giving residents a stronger voice in how their dollars are spent. This City has always shown what progressive leadership can accomplish. The future is up to us now. I hope to earn your vote and continue building the thriving city we are supposed to be.
