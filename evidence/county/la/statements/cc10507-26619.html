STATEMENT OF SANDRA ARMENTA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF ROSEMEAD GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Mayor of Rosemead/District Representative

Rosemead is the only home I have known. I grew up here, attended our local schools, and played youth sports. Serving my beloved hometown has been the greatest honor of my life.

Thank you for trusting me to serve as your Councilwoman for the past 17 years. As your Councilwoman and Mayor, I have worked to make Rosemead safer, stronger, and more vibrant.

Together, we increased police patrols, improved emergency response times, expanded parks and recreation programs, invested in neighborhood improvements, and maintained responsible fiscal management. Because of these efforts, Rosemead was ranked number one in the San Gabriel Valley for financial stability. I am proud to celebrate our city's diversity by supporting community traditions, including the 1st Annual Taste of Rosemead, Mid-Autumn Moon Festival, Fall Fiesta/Día de los Muertos, and our Fourth of July Parade, Festival, and Fireworks Celebration. I have dedicated my life to serving Rosemead as a recreation leader, Garvey School District teacher, Mayor, and Councilwoman. I understand the challenges facing working families and remain committed to protecting public safety, maintaining financial responsibility, and preserving Rosemead's quality of life. I humbly ask for your vote to continue serving you.
