STATEMENT OF PATRICK W. ATWATER

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 5

FOOTHILL MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICT

Age: 38

Occupation: Water Resources Manager

Hi, I am running for Foothill Municipal Water District (FMWD) Board because the Eaton fire showed how much is at stake. FMWD serves Altadena, La Cañada Flintridge, and La Crescenta. La Crescenta was spared from last January's fires; next time we may not be, and the recovery next door has been far too slow. I grew up in this community, am raising my two daughters here and have almost two decades of experience in water management and infrastructure financing. Currently, I lead the innovation program for one of the world's largest water utilities. If elected, I will focus on three priorities. First, coordinate more proactively with first responders on future disasters and speed up the Altadena fire recovery. Second, invest in local water supplies like water recycling and stormwater recharge to save ratepayer money. Third, stop letting paperwork block progress. I bring an insider's knowledge of how water agencies work, but an outsider's mind for how they should work better, faster, and cheaper. Last year's fires exposed real failures, but I firmly believe that together we can turn this breakdown into a breakthrough. Learn more at: https://bit.ly/future_of_the_foothills
