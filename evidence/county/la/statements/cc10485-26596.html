STATEMENT OF DAVID MATANGA

CANDIDATE FOR CITY TREASURER

CITY OF MONTEBELLO GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: City Treasurer

It has been my honor and privilege to serve as your Montebello City Treasurer, and I respectfully ask for your support for re-election.

Growing up in East Los Angeles and graduation from Garfield High School, Class of 1970, I have proudly called Montebello home for more than 40 years. My wife, Carmel, and I raised our daughter, Monique, here, built lifelong friendships, and created cherished memories. Our family's deep roots in this community inspire my commitment to Montebello's future.

My career as a Senior Tax Compliance Representative with the State of California provided me with extensive experience in financial oversight, accountability, and fiscal responsibility. I also earned an Associate of Arts degree in Labor Studies from Los Angeles Trade-Technical College and an Associate of Arts degree in Liberal Studies from East Los Angeles College.

My service as District President of SEIU 1000 and as a delegate to the Los Angeles County Federation of Labor strengthened my understanding of the needs of working families and reinforced my commitment to honest, transparent government.

As your City Treasurer, I remain dedicated to protecting taxpayer resources, ensuring fiscal transparency, and serving every resident with integrity, accountability, and respect. I would be honored to earn your vote for re-election.
