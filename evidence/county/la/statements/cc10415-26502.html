STATEMENT OF JEAN M. AYALA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF BALDWIN PARK GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 59

Occupation: Teacher/Vice Mayor

Education: Loyola Marymount University, M.Ed. -School Administration

Loyola Marymount University, B.A. – Liberal Studies

As a proud lifelong resident and 34-year veteran teacher of the Baldwin Park Unified School District, I am vested and committed to this community. Public Service continues to be a large part of who I am. In addition to being a professional and well-respected teacher to generations of families; I have been honored to serve this community as an approachable, passionate, and engaged elected official. I continue to support projects which provide a positive quality of life including park growth, infrastructure progress, and funding for resident programs.

I am honored to have served this community as Recreational Community Service Commissioner, City Clerk, Councilmember and more recently as Mayor Pro Tem. I have the confidence of residents, business owners, colleagues and staff.

I will continue to work collaboratively to provide the best opportunities for our residents and community members and continue to address issues affecting our community such as: public safety, housing, homeless and the economy. Baldwin Park is not immune to these concerns. With your support, I will continue to give, serve and promote that which provides for the quality of life in our great community!
