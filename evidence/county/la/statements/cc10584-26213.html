STATEMENT OF KEICY CHUNG

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

EL CAMINO COMMUNITY COLLEGE DISTRICT

Age: 62

Occupation: Business Owner/Student

Education should not begin at 18 or end at retirement.

As the owner of an IT consulting business for more than 30 years and an El Camino College student for over 25 years, I understand the important role El Camino College plays in serving students, working adults, and retirees.

As our population ages, El Camino College has an opportunity to expand access and participation by serving more learners at every stage of life.

For high school students, I support stronger dual-enrollment opportunities and enrichment programs, including musical instruments, performing arts, athletics, robotics, academic competitions, and engineering challenges. These pursuits often require years of dedication and can lead to scholarships, higher education, professional achievement, and lifelong careers.

For seniors and retirees, I support lifelong learning focused on health, wellness, technology literacy, personal enrichment, and hobbies.

I support workforce programs that provide hands-on experience with campus technology infrastructure and IT operations, preparing students for high-demand careers.

As El Camino College considers future capital investments and bond-funded projects, I will advocate for rigorous oversight, clear priorities, measurable outcomes, and transparent reporting to ensure taxpayer dollars deliver meaningful value to students and the community.

I respectfully ask for your vote.
