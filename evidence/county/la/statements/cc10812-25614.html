STATEMENT OF LARRY THOMPSON

PARTY PREFERENCE: REPUBLICAN

UNITED STATES REPRESENTATIVE, 32nd DISTRICT

The future is going to happen either with us—or to us.

I am running for United States Congress to make sure California leads the right way.

I am Larry Thompson, "The Sanity Man," a Malibu resident, Hollywood talent lawyer, film producer, businessman, and lifelong problem-solver. My career has been built on recognizing what comes next, bringing creative people together, and turning bold ideas into measurable results.

I do not want our children competing for yesterday's jobs. I want them creating tomorrow's industries. Artificial intelligence will transform nearly every profession, and California should command that transformation while protecting privacy, workers, creators, children, and human accountability.

My priorities are lowering the cost of living; protecting Social Security and Medicare; improving public safety; reducing homelessness; preventing wildfires and accelerating responsible rebuilding; reducing traffic congestion with a futuristic, high-speed monorail along the 405 Freeway connecting the Valley with the Westside; supporting small businesses; and bringing film, television, technology, and other good-paying jobs back to California.

I support secure borders with due process, strong national defense, and government that is honest, accountable, and effective.

Government should work at the speed of modern technology, not the speed of outdated bureaucracy. I will work with anyone who has an innovative idea and is willing to put people ahead of politics.

History remembers the generations that built the future, not those that feared it.

Vote for the person, not the party, who you believe will best represent you and your family.

The next 30 years begin now.
