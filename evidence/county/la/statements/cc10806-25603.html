STATEMENT OF JACQUI IRWIN

PARTY PREFERENCE: DEMOCRATIC

UNITED STATES REPRESENTATIVE, 26th DISTRICT

Jacqui Irwin will stand up to the Trump Administration and protect our community, our democracy, and our values.

Jacqui has been fighting for our community for decades. Her track record is why she is supported by working families, community leaders, and firefighters.

She stands up for our values:

-funding mental health services for veterans
-modernizing and improving local schools and colleges
-bringing good jobs to our communities
-reducing pollution and protect our open space

Jacqui Irwin will stand up for our values in Congress, and ensure the needs of our community are met.

As our member in Congress, Jacqui Irwin will:

-fight to make everyday necessities affordable
-help create good jobs that will support a family
-protect our democracy, civil rights, and the integrity of our elections
-make sure we prioritize the health and safety of neighborhoods over politics

Jacqui Irwin is the candidate with a REAL record of serving our community and delivering on the issues we care about.

She listens, thinks, and ACTS. We need leaders like Jacqui in Congress.
