STATEMENT OF DAVID COHER

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 3

PASADENA AREA COMMUNITY COLLEGE DISTRICT

Pasadena City College is a door we must keep open. I walked through one like it.

I began my education at a community college, then transferred on scholarship to USC and earned my law degree from Georgetown. I worked my way through school, and I have never forgotten who held those doors open for me.

Today I work as a mediator, helping people who deeply disagree hear each other and build agreements that last. As a business leader, I led large organizations and answered for balancing budgets. I have served our community on the Pasadena Planning Commission, the Pasadena Board of Zoning Appeals, and the 2021 Pasadena Redistricting Task Force, and today I serve as Board President of the Arroyos and Foothills Conservancy (for identification purposes only).

As your Trustee, I will keep three commitments. I will measure affordability by the true cost of attending, including books, transportation, food and housing, because those costs push students out before they finish. I will expand career training that leads directly to good jobs. And I will insist on transparent budgets and plain answers to taxpayers.

Our neighborhoods are still rebuilding. PCC should help lead that work.

I would be honored to earn your vote.

— David
