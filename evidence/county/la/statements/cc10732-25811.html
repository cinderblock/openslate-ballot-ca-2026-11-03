STATEMENT OF JOHN CURIEL

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

WESTSIDE UNION SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Westside Union Governing Board Member

I have been your representative on the Westside Governing Board since 2005, with many accomplishments. All 12 Westside schools have received the Western Association of Schools and Colleges (WASC) accreditation. Westside has 5 Distinguished Schools and I have dedicated 3 new schools. We survived the GREAT RECESSION with no teacher layoffs. We continue to recruit the best and brightest staff and as a result, we have no teacher shortages. We have the highest scores in the Antelope Valley.

I am fiscally responsible and have a proven track record as a leader.

I promised that there was no room in our classrooms for politics and I have kept that pledge. I believe that all of our children in our community deserve a quality education. I have remained focused on education and will not gamble with your hard-earned taxpayer monies.

I believe parents are partners in our children's education. I believe in secured campuses to ensure that our students and staff are safe.

I listened to our stakeholders and will continue to do so, to make the best-informed decisions for all.
