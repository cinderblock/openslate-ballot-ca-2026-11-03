STATEMENT OF JAMES T. BUTTS

CANDIDATE FOR MAYOR

CITY OF INGLEWOOD PRIMARY NOMINATING ELECTION

Mayor James T. Butts respectfully asks for your vote for reelection as Mayor of Inglewood. Under my leadership, Inglewood has undergone a historic transformation, emerging as a city of opportunity, growth, and renewed pride. Mayor Butts has worked to strengthen public safety, provide services for youth and seniors, revitalize neighborhoods, and attract major economic development that has brought jobs, investment, and international recognition to our community.

During my tenure, Inglewood has seen new housing, rent control, improved city services, upgraded infrastructure, and the arrival of world-class sports and entertainment venues that have expanded the city's tax base without raising taxes on residents. Mayor Butts has focused on fiscal responsibility, balanced budgets, and long-term planning to ensure Inglewood's progress benefits both current residents and future generations.

A long-term public servant with decades of leadership experience, Mayor Butts remains committed to safe streets, economic opportunity, and a high quality of life for every family in Inglewood. I believe Inglewood's best days are still ahead. I am dedicated to building on the momentum that has made Inglewood a model for urban progress.

Vote James T. Butts for Mayor of Inglewood — experienced leadership, proven results, and a stronger future for all.
