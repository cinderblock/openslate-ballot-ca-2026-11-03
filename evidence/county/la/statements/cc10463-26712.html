STATEMENT OF SUBRINA MILLER

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
2nd District

CITY OF INGLEWOOD PRIMARY NOMINATING ELECTION

Age: 63

Occupation: Executive Director

My name is Subrina Miller. As an employee of the Inglewood School District for over 25 years, I've seen Inglewood change where school enrollment is affected. As a resident for over 30 years, I have seen our families move. While I welcome progress, growth should improve the lives of the people who built this community not leave them behind. Our residents deserve a voice that represents them and creates opportunities for everyone. Our community deserves leadership that listens, acts with integrity, and puts residents first. That is why I am running for Inglewood City Council, District 2. I have dedicated my life to serving this community. I've spent 30 years serving Inglewood—not from the sidelines, but in the community, standing with families, businesses, and neighbors. As a mother of six and grandmother of five, I have a personal stake in the future of our city. Our families, seniors, small businesses, and young people deserve to share in the opportunities that growth brings. I am committed to ensuring that every decision made reflects the needs of our community and the future we all deserve. Together, we can build an Inglewood where progress benefits everyone.

Subrina Miller, City Council District 2
