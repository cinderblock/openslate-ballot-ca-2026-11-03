STATEMENT OF HECTOR "MANUEL" ORTIZ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF IRWINDALE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

As your Council Member, I have always served with honesty, transparency, integrity, and a commitment in prioritizing the residents of Irwindale first. Together, we have strengthened our community by supporting public safety, maintaining quality city services, improving our parks and infrastructure, and protecting the small-town values that make Irwindale such a special place to call home. I believe in responsible leadership, careful financial stewardship, and making decisions that benefit both today's residents and future generations. While we have accomplished much, there is still more important work ahead. I remain committed to listening to our community, working collaboratively, and ensuring Irwindale continues to thrive. I respectfully ask for your vote and together, we can build an even stronger City of Irwindale.
