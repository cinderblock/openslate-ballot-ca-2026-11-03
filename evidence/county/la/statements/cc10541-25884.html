STATEMENT OF STEPHANIE A. CAPPS

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF TRUSTEES,
Trustee Area No. 3

ALTADENA LIBRARY DISTRICT

Age: 53

Occupation: Emergency Nurse Educator

As a lifelong learner, I have relied on libraries throughout my academic and professional journey. For the past 15 years, I have worked as a registered nurse, a profession that values continuous learning and service.

I began my nursing education at L.A. General Medical Center, where I discovered my passion for serving the underrepresented communities.

After graduation, I joined the Emergency Department and later earned a Bachelor of Science in Nursing, a Master of Science as an Acute Care Nurse Practitioner, and a post-master's certificate as a Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner. Today, I serve as the Program Coordinator and Lead Educator for the Emergency Nurse Training Program.

My experience caring for marginalized populations has reinforced the essential role public libraries play in strengthening communities. This is especially true as Altadena recovers from the Eaton Fire. Libraries provide access to information, technology, educational opportunities, and welcoming spaces that foster connection, resilience, and lifelong learning.

I would be honored to serve as a Trustee for the Altadena Library Board. I am committed to supporting equitable access to library resources and ensuring our libraries continue to meet the evolving needs of our diverse community as we rebuild and move forward together.
