STATEMENT OF BRIAN WORTHINGTON

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 2

WALNUT VALLEY WATER DISTRICT

Age: 60

Occupation: Chair, Diamond Bar Planning Commission

As Chair of the Diamond Bar Planning Commission and a long-time Diamond Bar resident, I have dedicated my public service to ensuring our community grows responsibly. Now, I am running for the Walnut Valley Water District Board, Division 2, to bring that same balanced, strategic leadership to our water management.

Safe, reliable, and affordable water is fundamental to our local economy and quality of life. My experience of vetting infrastructure projects equips me to protect your tax dollars and address our region's complex water challenges.

On the Water Board, my priorities will be:

- Strategic Infrastructure: Modernizing aging pipelines and pump systems systematically to prevent expensive emergency repairs.
- Fiscal Oversight: Exercising strict financial discipline to keep water rates fair, transparent, and predictable.
- Supply Resilience: Strengthening partnerships to diversify our imported water supply and expand local conservation.

I am proudly endorsed by Division 2 Director Ed Hilden, Diamond Bar Mayor Steve Tye, Mayor Pro-Tem Ruth Low, and Former Mayor Nancy Lyons.

I would be honored to receive your vote.
