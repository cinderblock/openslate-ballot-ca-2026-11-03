STATEMENT OF EDITH RODRIGUEZ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF MAYWOOD GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Chief Financial Officer

My name is Edith Rodriguez, I grew up in the City of Maywood and I have lived here since 1969. I graduated from Bell High School and attended college.

Professionally, I work as a Chief Financial Officer in a corporation, where I have developed skills in finance, budgeting, communication, project management, legal compliance and leadership. I also bring 25 years of financial banking experience. I believe these skills would allow me to contribute meaningfully to our community. In addition, I value transparency, accountability, and respectful communication and careful decision-making. I am prepared to make that commitment to the community.

If elected, my priorities would be to strengthen the security of our community, where our residents and business owners feel safe, invest in resources and programs for our youth.

Improve communication with our residents to ensure that their concerns are being heard and addressed, and that housing is being fairly met.

Together we can make Maywood a place where our businesses can thrive, our youth can build a future, and our families can feel safe.

Serving on the city council is an opportunity to give back to the community that I am proud to be part of. I would be honored to have your vote.
