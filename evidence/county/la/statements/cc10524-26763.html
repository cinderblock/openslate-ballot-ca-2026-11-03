STATEMENT OF NEVIN STANTON-TREHAN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
4th District

CITY OF SOUTH PASADENA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

South Pasadena is where my family chose to build our life and raise our children. I am running for City Council to bring leadership to the community we love. As Chair of the South Pasadena Finance Commission, I have worked to strengthen financial oversight, promote transparency, and protect taxpayer dollars. As Executive Vice President, Chief People Officer and General Counsel for the YMCA of Metropolitan Los Angeles, I help oversee legal, financial, workforce, and operational matters for an organization with a budget and complexity comparable to our City.

Beyond my work, I coach Jr. Clippers basketball and Little League, chaired the Marengo Parent Party, serve on the AbilityFirst and SPEF boards, and am a member of South Pasadena DUDES and the Oneonta Club.

On the City Council, I will focus on restoring financial stability, repairing roads and sidewalks, improving neighborhood safety, investing in parks for every generation, and creating a walkable downtown that supports local businesses and welcomes new ones.

I am honored to have support from leaders affiliated with SPEF, Marengo PTA, the Oneonta Club, South Pasadena DUDES, and the Chinese American Society. I will listen, act with urgency, unite the community, and deliver results. I ask for your vote.
