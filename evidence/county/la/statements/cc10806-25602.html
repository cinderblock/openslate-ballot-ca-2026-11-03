STATEMENT OF SAM GALLUCCI

PARTY PREFERENCE: REPUBLICAN

UNITED STATES REPRESENTATIVE, 26th DISTRICT

As a business executive, nonprofit founder, and lifelong community leader, I believe government should focus on solving problems–not creating more of them.

For decades, I've led large nonprofit organizations, managed billion-dollar budgets, and built teams that delivered measurable results. Throughout our region, I've worked to strengthen communities by helping thousands of women and children find safe and stable homes through The Kingdom Center, while helping launch Revive Academies to expand high-quality educational opportunities for families.

I'm running for Congress because Washington needs more leaders with real-world experience and fewer career politicians. Families deserve representatives who listen, work across differences, and put practical solutions ahead of partisan politics.

My priorities are straightforward: lowering the cost of living, improving public safety, addressing homelessness and the border issue with compassion and accountability, protecting parents' role in their children's education, supporting small businesses, and making government more transparent and accountable.

As a husband, father of three, and grandfather of ten, I understand the challenges families face every day. I will bring executive leadership, fiscal responsibility, and a commitment to putting people before politics.

If you're looking for independent leadership focused on results instead of political division, I respectfully ask for your vote.

Sam Gallucci
A Leader, Not a Politician.
