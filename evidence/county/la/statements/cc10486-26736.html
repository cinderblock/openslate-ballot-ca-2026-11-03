STATEMENT OF TAMALA P. KELLY

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF MONROVIA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Dr. Tamala P. Kelly

Serving as your Monrovia City Council Member has been one of the greatest honors of my life.

In 2022, you placed your trust in me to help lead our city. Ever since, I have worked to earn that trust with integrity, purpose, and a commitment to putting our community first. Monrovia has become stronger, more vibrant, and more resilient. Together, we have accomplished so much.
- Reopening Canyon Park
- Investing in the award-winning SGVCares program
- Strengthening our city's financial future without reducing programs and services

Seeing Monrovia recognized as an All-America City was one of my proudest moments—a reminder that together, through partnership and investment in one another, our community can achieve anything.

I proudly represent you on these regional boards:
- California Cities Housing, Community and Economic Development Committee
- San Gabriel Valley Council of Governments Energy, Environment and National Resources Committee
- 2028 Paralympic Games Committee

While I am proud of what we've accomplished, there is more to do, more opportunities to create, and more progress to be made such as reimagining economic development in our city.

I am ready to continue serving you and our city. I would be honored to earn your vote once again.

Vote Dr. Tamala Kelly for Monrovia City Council on November 3, 2026.
