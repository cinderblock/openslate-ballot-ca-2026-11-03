STATEMENT OF JAY OBERNOLTE

PARTY PREFERENCE: REPUBLICAN

UNITED STATES REPRESENTATIVE, 23rd DISTRICT

I started a successful business from my college dorm room over 30 years ago. Since then, I've created hundreds of high-paying jobs and raised my family in our community. I ran for office because I worried my children couldn't live the same American Dream, even if they worked as hard as I did, because our government isn't working for us. I'm fighting to change that in Washington.

Hardworking Californians suffered when reckless government spending drove up inflation to a 40-year high. That's why I've fought to reduce inflation by passing commonsense reforms to eliminate fraud and abuse, cutting over $1.6 trillion in wasteful spending.

While Sacramento politicians raised our taxes on gas and healthcare, I fought for the Working Families Tax Cuts. That stopped a $1,500 per year tax increase on taxpayers, eliminated taxes on overtime, tips, and Social Security, and increased the child tax credit for families.

When soft-on-crime politicians allowed human trafficking and drugs to pour into our community, I fought to secure our border and passed laws holding fentanyl poisoners accountable. Since then, drug overdoses have fallen 30%.

I'm also deeply committed to fighting for those who fought for us. That's why I worked with both Republicans and Democrats to expand resources for Veterans and give our soldiers a well-deserved raise.

I believe that America's best days are still ahead of us. I would be honored to earn your vote and continue fighting for you in Washington. Learn more about me and my values at ElectJay.com.
