STATEMENT OF LILIIA CHERNOVA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
5th District

CITY OF SANTA CLARITA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

I am an IT executive with an MBA and a Master of Education, with over fifteen years building revenue and leading teams. I know how to manage growth responsibly.

Uncontrolled development threatens our neighborhoods. I will demand scrutiny of projects like Sand Canyon Village, fight for cleaner parks and more green space, and oppose placing homeless service centers in District 5 without reasonable planning.

Wildfire safety cannot wait. I will push for proactive brush and shrub removal to protect our homes before fire season, not after.

Families here are stretched thin. I will bring financial literacy workshops and educational resources to our residents and enable a path toward debt reduction.

As an immigrant, I built my life and career by following legal protocol, and I believe firmly in legal immigration and the rule of law.

District 5 has never had our own voice on the Council. I intend to provide it for us.
