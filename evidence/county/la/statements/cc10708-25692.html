STATEMENT OF BONNIE J. CORONADO

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 1

LAWNDALE SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Incumbent

It has been said that stable district leadership leads to student success. I am proud of the accomplishments achieved in the Lawndale Elementary School District during my many years of service on the Board of Trustees.

Together, we have remained focused on academic excellence while working diligently to support the social emotional well-being of every student. Addams, Rogers, Anderson, and Smith Schools have each earned recognition as a state or national school of excellence.

The successful passage of Bond Measure LL reflects our community's trust to modernize our facilities in a responsible manner.

Representing Trustee Area 1, I remain committed to serving every stakeholder member throughout the Lawndale Elementary School District, regardless of trustee area boundaries.

Having been raised by a single parent, I understand the challenges of this family dynamic. I proudly support the rich diversity of our district, including my own Hispanic heritage. Our exceptional retention of both certificated and classified employees reflects that LESD is a district where professionals choose to build their careers and prosper.

As an elementary school teacher, and long- time board member, I believe my experiences have prepared me to continue serving on the Lawndale Elementary School Board of Trustees.
