STATEMENT OF RICARDO "RIC" ALONZO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
5th District

CITY OF MONTEBELLO GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 42

Occupation: Engineer/Councilmember

18 months ago, on January 29th, 2025, I was appointed to represent District 5 in South Montebello. Here is a list of our accomplishments you can see for yourself in South Montebello:

A police community resource center. Park rangers. Refurbished park restrooms. A community garden. Repaved streets. The Bluff Road Fence Project. The city's first sponsored Christmas event down here, plus the ones I put on myself. Before me, this side of town waited for necessities, while the north was bathed in luxuries. I also voted against a development that would have added residents without the services to carry them.

I do not perform. I do not run this seat from a phone screen. I read every staff report, I ask my questions in open session where the record catches them, and I vote my own way. No theatrics. No dramatics. Just policy. That was the deal when I came in and it has not changed.

This is what I have done. I can do more.
