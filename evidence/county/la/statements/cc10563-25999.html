STATEMENT OF BRIAN GORRINDO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 3

ANTELOPE VALLEY-EAST KERN WATER AGENCY

My name is Brian Gorrindo, and I am honored to be a candidate for the Antelope Valley-East Kern Water Agency Board of Directors, District 3.

I am a nearly lifelong resident of Leona Valley and have deep roots in our rural community. For the past 27 years, I have proudly served in the fire service and currently work as a Fire Captain, where I have built a career centered on leadership, accountability, and serving others. My wife and I raised our two children in Leona Valley, where we also own and operate a local cattle business.

Like many of you, I value the rural lifestyle that makes our community such a special place to call home. I understand the importance of protecting our agricultural heritage while ensuring a reliable and sustainable water supply for future generations.

If elected, I will work to keep AVEK fiscally responsible, promote long-term water sustainability, and advocate for policies that support our agricultural community and rural residents. I believe thoughtful planning, sound financial stewardship, and transparent decision-making are essential to meeting the water needs of our growing region.

I would be honored to earn your vote and serve the residents of District 3.
