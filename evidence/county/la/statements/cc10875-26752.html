STATEMENT OF ELKA WORNER

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF HERMOSA BEACH GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Hermosa Beach has been my home since I was seven years old. When my family immigrated from Germany, this community welcomed us and gave us the opportunity to build a life here. I grew up here, raised my daughter here, and I've spent my life giving back to the community that has given so much to my family.

Leadership isn't measured by campaign promises. It's measured by showing up, solving problems, and getting things done.

Here's what I've already done:

- Led a campaign to stop a proposed 50-foot development near the beach
- Founded HB SAFE, Hermosa Beach's first homeless outreach program
- Serve as a Parks and Recreation Commissioner
- Work in a downtown Hermosa small business and support policies that help businesses succeed.

As an award-winning journalist and former press secretary for the LA District Attorney's Office, I've built my career by listening, asking tough questions, and finding practical solutions.

As your councilmember, I'll keep Hermosa safe, make smart decisions with your tax dollars, and plan responsibly for the future. I believe Hermosa's best days are still ahead. I'd be honored to earn your vote.
