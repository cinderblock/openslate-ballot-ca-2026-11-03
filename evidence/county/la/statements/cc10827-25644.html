STATEMENT OF DONNA TRYFMAN

CANDIDATE FOR JUDGE OF THE SUPERIOR COURT
Office No. 131

DONNA TRYFMAN has devoted more than 30 years to serving the people of Los Angeles County with integrity, fairness, and an unwavering commitment to justice. A veteran Deputy Public Defender in the Major Cases Unit, she has handled the most serious and complex felony cases, supervised attorneys at the nation's largest juvenile courthouse, and currently serves as a Temporary Judge.

Donna's distinguished career earned her the 2025 Gideon Award as Felony Attorney of the Year, recognizing excellence in felony defense and public service. She has the experience, judgment, and temperament to serve effectively from day one.

Donna has earned an exceptionally broad coalition of support from judges, prosecutors, defense attorneys, labor organizations, community leaders, and organizations committed to a fair and ethical judiciary. She is proudly endorsed by the Association of Deputy District Attorneys, Mexican American Bar Association, Western States Regional Council of Carpenters, UNITE HERE Local 11, Stonewall Democratic Club, Citizens for Accountable Leadership, and many others.

Independent newspaper editorial boards also concluded Donna is the best choice, endorsing her in the Los Angeles Daily News, San Gabriel Valley Tribune, Press-Telegram, The Sun, and the Metropolitan News-Enterprise.
