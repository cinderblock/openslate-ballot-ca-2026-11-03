STATEMENT OF JAMES SANTANA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
5th District

CITY OF MONTEBELLO GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Caretaker/Business Owner

Montebello is my community. It gave me my education, my first job, and my first chance to serve. Today I own a home here, run a small business here, and hope to raise a family here.

I started in local politics as a teenager, organizing students for a local school bond. Since then, I have chaired the Parks and Recreation Commission, founded Montebello United, where neighbors gather to understand the decisions shaping our city, and built more than twenty years of business and community service experience.

District 5 carries more than its share. Homes sit beside small shops and heavy industry, and major projects are coming. We deserve a councilmember chosen by residents and present in our neighborhoods.

On every contract and every budget, I will ask one question first: does this benefit the people who live and work here?

I will advocate for development that grows with residents, not around them. I will establish a commission for seniors, caregivers, and aging families. I will hold monthly District 5 office hours. I will support small businesses, renters, and working families.

I am ready to serve District 5 and all of Montebello. I would be honored to earn your vote.
