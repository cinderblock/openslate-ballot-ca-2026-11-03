STATEMENT OF MARIELA RENTERIA

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

LYNWOOD UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: School Community Liaison

I am Mariela Renteria, born and raised in Lynwood and a proud product of Lynwood Schools. Lynwood gave me so much, and I've spent my career finding ways to give back. For the last three years, I've worked in a neighboring community helping schools and families connect and support one another. This work has shown me exactly how school systems function, where they fall short, and what it takes to fix them.

That understanding started early. As a high school student here, Lynwood alumni mentored me and taught me the value of community service. I went on to pursue my degree at UC Riverside, and everything I've learned since has been in service of one goal: making sure Lynwood families have the resources and access they deserve.

I know how school systems work from the inside — the policies, the budgets, the barriers families face — better than most. Now I'm ready to bring that knowledge home, to serve Lynwood the way it once served me.

Mariela Renteria: For Lynwood. For our students. For our future.
