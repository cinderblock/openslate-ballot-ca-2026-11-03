STATEMENT OF DAN ROCHA

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF ARTESIA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Candidate: Dan Rocha
Ballot Designation: Family Caregiver
Age: 39

I am Dan Rocha, a Son of Artesia. I grew up here and attended Elliott Elementary, Faye Ross Middle School, and Gahr High School. I have spent years serving the community that raised me.

I am a family caregiver and proud union member, former Artesia Parks and Recreation Commissioner, former Beautification and Maintenance Commissioner, President of the Cerritos-Artesia Rotary Club, and a Friends of the Artesia Library board member.

As a business owner and digital project manager, I have spent nearly two decades managing projects, budgets, technology, and client relationships. Those experiences taught me how to solve problems, bring people together, and deliver results.

I believe in leadership that is present, accessible, and accountable. I am running to strengthen public safety, address illegal dumping and neglected properties, support local businesses, improve parks and streets, and promote transparent, responsive government. I also support responsible development that protects neighborhoods while preparing Artesia for the future.

I believe the best days of Artesia are still ahead of us. Together, we can bring new ideas, practical solutions, and real results while preserving the values that make our city special.

I respectfully ask for your vote for Artesia City Council.
