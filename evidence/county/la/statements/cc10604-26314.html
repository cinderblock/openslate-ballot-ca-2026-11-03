STATEMENT OF AL BARLEVY

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 2

ABC UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 57

Occupation: Economic Development Commissioner

I decided to run for two reasons:

1) Like it or not, Artificial Intelligence (AI) is here to stay. We therefore need to integrate AI into our curriculum, but it must be done in a responsible way that enhances the education experience for the students, and makes the job of the teachers easier. We absolutely do not want AI to take over for the teachers. Teachers are an invaluable part of the education experience.

2) We must keep public money in our public schools, and not divert the money to homeschooling. Parents who choose to pull their children out of the public school and homeschool their children ought to do that at their own expense, not at the school district expense.

If you have any questions or concerns, please email me: ab.for.abc.schools@gmail.com

I would be honored to earn your vote.
