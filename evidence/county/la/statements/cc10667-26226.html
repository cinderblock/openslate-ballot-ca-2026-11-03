STATEMENT OF CARY C. CHEN

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 3

ROWLAND UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: RUSD Governing Board President

My name is Cary C. Chen, and I am running for re-election to the Rowland Unified School District Board of Education. It has been an honor to serve this community for the past sixteen years.

As a first-generation immigrant from Taiwan, I am proud to share the journey of how I established my roots in this community–finding stability, building a career, raising my family, and giving back. My path began with two years of military service as a second lieutenant officer in the Taiwan Marine Corps. I then moved to the United States to attend the University of Akron in Ohio, where I earned a Master of Science degree in Earth Science with an emphasis in Geophysics. During my graduate studies, I gained valuable classroom experience teaching physical and historical geology labs to undergraduate students.

In 1981, an opportunity to work for UNOCAL, a major California-based oil company, allowed me to settle my family in the RUSD community. All of my children were educated within RUSD, achieved their goals, and are now thriving in their careers and raising families of their own.

For over sixteen years, serving our community on the RUSD Board of Education has been a deeply meaningful commitment. Since 2009, I have worked as a dedicated parent, volunteer, and trustee, including serving four terms as Board President. My leadership extends across our community, having served on the Joint Board of La Puente Valley ROP (2015–2016), as President of the Rowland Heights Buckboard Days Parade Committee (2015), and as President of the Rowland Heights Community Coordinating Council (2022–2024).

As the longest-serving member of the board, my priority has always been the success, safety, and well-being of our students and families. Whether drawing on my background as a geophysicist, a classroom instructor, or a local business founder, my focus remains entirely on ensuring our schools provide a high-quality education that prepares every student for a bright future. I would be honored to earn your vote to continue this vital work.
