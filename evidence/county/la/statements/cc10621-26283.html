STATEMENT OF SAMUEL BROWN-VAZQUEZ

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

BASSETT UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Bassett deserves a champion who loves our community, believes in our students, and has a vision for our schools. I am running for the Bassett Unified School District Governing Board to help make Bassett a destination district families choose.

As an educator and community organizer, I teach environmental literacy and leadership curriculum that I helped develop. As a Bassett Personnel Commissioner, I helped lead the first comprehensive update of Commission bylaws since 2012 to comply with current law and strengthen employee representation.

My focus is the fundamentals: improving academic achievement, getting every student reading and writing at grade level, and creating safe, healthy schools.

Education must also open doors. I want students graduating with real choices through stronger Career Technical Education, skilled trades, expanded community college dual enrollment, FFA, and renewed partnerships like our Health Academy with Kaiser.

I want students proud to be part of Bassett. We can strengthen that connection through mariachi, folklórico, environmental education, and a Green Schools Initiative.

My vision is a Bassett where employees are valued, families are proud to enroll, and every student graduates prepared for college, a career, or a skilled trade.

I would be honored to have your vote.
