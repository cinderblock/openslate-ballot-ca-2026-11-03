STATEMENT OF CHUKWUDUBEM "CHIEDU" EGBUNIWE

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF WEST HOLLYWOOD GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

I'm seeking to be elected to the City Council of the City of West Hollywood. I'm a proud West Hollywood resident for 22 years. It's been an honor to serve my community as a nonprofit leader, as a technology leader, as an activist and volunteer. I bring decades of experience in strategic thinking, creating change and listening with compassion. In the corporate world, I've created project plans and led teams of various sizes. In the nonprofit word, I've helped launch new programs and increased membership, while navigating economic instability to create protections for the future. In both fields, I've helped create growth, stability and a culture of belonging.

I couldn't have done any of this without my West Hollywood community. Countless coffee runs with friends, marches, community gatherings, and walks around our neighborhood to clear my head. We have created something truly special in West Hollywood – a community that needs to be valued and preserved.

In order to do so, we must listen to residents. Listening does not end after Election Day. It is my job and a great privilege to listen to the residents of West Hollywood, to build consensus around goals, to find common ground when there are disagreements and to be transparent when a goal can't be achieved.

We've witnessed many changes over the years, and more changes are coming. It is critical that we prepare for growth, while preserving the core community values that have made West Hollywood a beacon of hope.

Lastly: my name is Nigerian and is pronounced CHEE-ed-oo ee-BOO-nee-way.

Don't ever be afraid to ask me how to say it. I won't be offended.
