STATEMENT OF JONATHAN BEUTLER

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF TRUSTEES

PALOS VERDES LIBRARY DISTRICT

Occupation: President, Board of Trustees, Palos Verdes Library District

It is an honor to serve as a Trustee of the Palos Verdes Library District, currently as President of the Board. I am grateful for the opportunity to lead this important community asset since 2022 and hope to earn your vote.

I care deeply about our community and believe a well-run public library is a place where every resident, regardless of background or age, can learn, connect, and grow. During my tenure, I have focused on fiscal discipline, responsibly overseen our roughly $12 million budget, advocated for expanded programming and accessibility, and delivered prudent capital improvements that are transparent to taxpayers. Palos Verdes is a special community that deserves a well-led public agency delivering world-class services.

Good governance means careful stewardship of the public trust and treasure. I take that seriously by asking thorough questions, weighing decisions against their long-term impact, and listening to residents across the political spectrum. I am truly committed to representing all constituents, and bring a passion for stakeholder engagement, bridge-building, and public service to this role.

I would be honored to continue as your Trustee: protecting this institution, supporting our dedicated staff, and ensuring that PVLD remain strong, accessible, and well-managed for generations to come.
