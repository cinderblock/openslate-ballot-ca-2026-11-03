STATEMENT OF LARRY BLOCK

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF WEST HOLLYWOOD GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Thank you to all 4,281 voters who supported our campaign in 2024. We spent $80,000 against $2.5 million in outside spending and almost beat Goliath.

I've lived and worked in West Hollywood since 1986. I've served the City of West Hollywood in many capacities, including Chairman of the Public Facilities and Infrastructure Commission and Chairman of the Disability Advisory Board. A renter's rights initiative I proposed in 2015, allowing a senior or disabled renter to move from a second-floor to a first-floor unit without a rent increase, was signed into law by Governor Newsom in 2023 as AB 1620.

I'm honored to be the 2026 recipient of the City of West Hollywood Older Adult Service Award, and the 2025 recipient of the City of West Hollywood Disability Service Award.

Special interests and dark money in independent expenditures are working to sweep the remaining seats on our City Council. I'm fighting for us and a City Council that listens to residents and works for the people.

As Publisher of WEHOonline, I have worked to provide the community with a platform to discuss and debate the issues that matter. We've held City Hall and the crony politicians accountable. It only takes one Council member to ask the tough questions, and I'm not afraid to stand up for what's right.

As the owner of BlockParty WeHo, we have celebrated West Hollywood and survived as a community-serving business for over 17 years. The pulse of the city is in my blood and I feel the problems on our streets.

The problems are real: failing infrastructure, wasted spending, politicians using a Council seat as a stepping stone, public safety pushed down the priority list, a City Council that adopted Sacramento's housing plan instead of writing our own.

It's crazy that a city founded on rent control now has the highest rents in the region. The highest sales tax in the region. The highest cost of living in the region. Their policies have put self-interest ahead of our collective interest.

I'm running to make West Hollywood safer, to protect residents and neighborhoods, to be a catalyst for a small business rebound, and to spend your tax dollars on public safety and resident services.

I'd be honored to have your vote, and bring a friend. Let's win one for the people, and hold the city accountable to the residents who live here.
