STATEMENT OF EMMA SHARIF

CANDIDATE FOR MAYOR

CITY OF COMPTON GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

I am honored to seek re-election as Mayor of Compton. Together, we have delivered real results that strengthen our infrastructure, improve public safety, and expand opportunity for every resident.

Over the past term, I fought for and secured $12 million in state funding to restore the Artesia Bridge–protecting a vital corridor for families and businesses. We enhanced the Compton Fire Department with a new fire engine and improvements to our fire station, ensuring faster response times and better protection.

Public safety remains a top priority. Through collaboration with the Los Angeles County Sheriff's Department, we brought enhanced patrols and strengthened safety measures throughout our neighborhoods. We also launched a $35 million infrastructure improvement program focused on long-overdue road repairs.

Understanding that opportunity begins with access, we launched a groundbreaking digital equity initiative to expand high-speed internet citywide. We created Compton's first Arts Commission to uplift culture and creativity, built hundreds of new housing units, and worked with regional partners to address homelessness with compassion and accountability.

Our progress is real–but our work is not done. I am committed to building a safer, stronger, and more connected Compton for all. For more information-reelectmayorsharif.com
