STATEMENT OF MARK BURTON

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF MANHATTAN BEACH GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Attorney

Dear Neighbor,

I am seeking a 2nd term to the City Council to continue putting the residents of Manhattan Beach always first.

Our community deserves a government that listens to the people-not special interests. With a proven record of fighting to ensure city business was conducted with absolute transparency, I remain deeply committed to that mission while demanding fiscal accountability that reduces wasteful spending by 10% and protects our local tax dollars.

- Protect Our Small-Town Character: Stop high-rise developments; defend local zoning control; and protect our neighborhoods from severe traffic and infrastructure strain.

- Restore Fiscal Accountability: We must end irresponsible deficit spending.

- Implement Strict Term Limits Local: Government thrives on new ideas, not entrenched power. Two four-year terms is enough.

Having served as your former Mayor and Councilmember, alongside a career as a Senior Assistant City Attorney for the City of Los Angeles, I understand municipal law and how city government works...how to deliver tangible results, manage balanced budgets, and provide the collaborative leadership our community deserves to ensure Manhattan Beach remains a safe, beautiful, and thriving place to live.

With a lifelong career dedicated to public service, I respectfully ask for your vote by November 3rd.

Thank you.
