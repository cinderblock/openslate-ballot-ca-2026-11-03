STATEMENT OF NATALIE EPSTEIN

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 1

SAUGUS UNION SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Small Business Owner

I'm a motherof four kids in this district, and every morning when I drop my school aged children off, I think about whether they're truly safe. That's why I'm running, because our schools need real safety: working cameras that are actually monitored, visible security guards, a true single point of entry, and those same standards carried into after-school programs too, where the same level of care isn't always guaranteed. I also want to bring more focus to mental health resources, enough counselors so no child falls through the cracks, and real enforcement of anti-bullying policies, not just words in a handbook. Just as important, I want to actually listen to parents and teachers on issues that have gone unanswered for too long. I work in corporate tech and know how to turn that lived experience into real, enforceable policy, not just talking points. I'm running because I want our kids to feel as safe as we all assume they already are.
