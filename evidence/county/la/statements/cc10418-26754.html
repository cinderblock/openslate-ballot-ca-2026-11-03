STATEMENT OF JORGE O. VERDIN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF BELL GARDENS GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

As a Bell Gardens resident and a father of three, I have felt inspired to serve my community and its people. I, Jorge O. Verdin, not only love the City of Bell Gardens, but I truly care about each and every one of our residents.

I am constantly thinking about how I can make a positive impact on our community. Some of my earliest memories are of walking to Garfield Elementary School, picking up pinecones along the way, and learning the importance of taking pride in the place I call home. Those early experiences helped shape my commitment to serving others.

I have always believed in supporting our local businesses and strengthening the community that has given so much to my family. It would be an incredible honor to serve on the Bell Gardens City Council with integrity, honesty, and dedication. My goal is to listen to our residents, represent them faithfully, and work together to build an even stronger, safer, and more united Bell Gardens for future generations.
