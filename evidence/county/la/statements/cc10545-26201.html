STATEMENT OF JOHN VRSALOVICH

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF DIRECTORS,
Division 2

FOOTHILL MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICT

Age: 62

Occupation: Civil Engineer

As a licensed Professional Engineer and water agency senior manager with more than three decades of experience delivering critical infrastructure projects throughout Southern California, I am committed to protecting our region's water future through responsible leadership, sound fiscal management, and transparent decision-making.

Throughout my career, I have managed complex public works programs, negotiated major construction contracts, and helped deliver projects that strengthen water reliability, public safety, and system resilience.

If elected to represent Division 2 on the Foothill Municipal Water District Board, I will work to ensure a reliable, high-quality water supply for all constituents, including the communities of Altadena and the retail agencies served by Foothill, such as Rubio Cañon Land & Water Association, Las Flores Water Company, and Kinneloa Irrigation District.

Having witnessed firsthand the devastating impacts of the Eaton Fire on our community, I understand the critical importance of resilient water infrastructure, emergency preparedness, and dependable public service.

Effective public service requires listening to the community, making decisions based on sound engineering, fiscal responsibility, and long-term planning. It would be an honor to continue serving Division 2 and help ensure a safe, reliable, and sustainable water future for our communities.
