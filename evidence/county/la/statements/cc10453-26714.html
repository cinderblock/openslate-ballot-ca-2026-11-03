STATEMENT OF TERI MERRICK

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
1st District

CITY OF GLENDORA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 66

Occupation: Retired Philosophy Professor/Substitute Teacher

As a life-long Glendoran, community advocate and current candidate, I've heard the concerns of District 1. Fix our roads. Maintain the library and public places. Help with unaffordable, inequitable housing costs. Make sure all kids, families and residents are respected, protected and heard. Don't raise taxes.

Unfortunately, we all realize these can be competing concerns. To address them, we must talk, think, and work together. We must reject polarizing rhetoric that creates mistrust, pits neighbor against neighbor and distracts us from day-to-day life issues.

A quick internet search shows I have a proven track record bringing Glendora together. As a department chair and professor at Azusa Pacific University and a substitute teacher and aide in Glendora Unified, I've helped students and co-workers wrestle with difficult problems and controversies, while learning to better understand and appreciate various perspectives and solutions. As an office manager for a family-owned manufacturing company and a woman whose husband built a construction company from the ground up, I understand what it takes to ensure both working families and small businesses can thrive.

To elect someone experienced in building coalitions capable of responding to our shared community concerns, vote for me.
