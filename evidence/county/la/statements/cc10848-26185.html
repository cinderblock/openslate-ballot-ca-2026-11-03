STATEMENT OF STACY FORTNER

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

WILLIAM S. HART UNION HIGH SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Mother/Technology Consultant

As a resident of Valencia, daughter of a school bus driver, and a committed leader in our community, I am running for William S. Hart Union High School District Governing Board to ensure every student has access to a high-quality education that prepares them for success in college, careers, and life.

Our schools should provide strong academics, safe learning environments, and opportunities that help students develop the skills needed to thrive in an ever-changing world. As a trustee, I will focus on academic excellence, responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars, transparency in decision-making, and meaningful engagement with parents, teachers, students, and community members.

I believe our district's success depends on supporting outstanding educators, maintaining fiscal responsibility, and keeping students at the center of every decision. By working collaboratively and listening to the diverse voices of our community, we can continue to strengthen our schools and build confidence in district leadership.

I am committed to thoughtful leadership, accountability, and ensuring that William S. Hart Union High School District remains a place where all students can learn, grow, and achieve their potential.

I respectfully ask for your support and vote.
