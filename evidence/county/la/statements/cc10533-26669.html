STATEMENT OF CHELSEA BYERS

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF WEST HOLLYWOOD GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

I'm Councilmember Chelsea Byers, and in 2022, I ran on the belief that a transformative agenda could deliver a more livable, caring, and connected community. Over my four years as Councilmember and Mayor, I have worked with our community to get real wins on affordability, sustainability, and safety while building a city that's diverse and inclusive for generations to come.

During my first term, I've built relationships with decision-makers across Los Angeles County to tackle the urgent issues facing our residents. These regional connections have delivered funding, partnerships, and new opportunities back to West Hollywood while ensuring our city has a voice in the decisions that affect us. I'm proud of the track record I have as a result:

Housing + Renter Protections: Led the effort to strengthen our rent stabilization ordinance and accelerated the creation of new affordable housing.

Metro Expansion: Helped launch the financial mechanism that will generate $2.2 billion for the creation of Metro's K Line Northern Extension, bringing three Metro stations to West Hollywood

Social Services and Childcare: Championed the expansion of West Hollywood's child care initiatives and increased social services funding by $1 million.

ICE-Free Zones: Co-authored a motion establishing ICE-free zones on city-owned property.

Safer Streets: Enhanced streetscape projects by adding protected bike lanes and expanding mobility options to improve safety for pedestrians and cyclists.

Supporting Local Businesses: Launched the "WeHo Loves Locals Gift Card" program, increasing investment in West Hollywood's business community.

With state funding cuts, families feeling the strain of the affordability crisis, and a Trump administration threatening our rights and freedoms, we can't afford business as usual. As a young, queer renter and transit user, I know we need leaders who will take on tough fights: to lower housing costs, strengthen social services, modernize our aging infrastructure, make our streets safer and easier to navigate, and tackle the climate crisis with the urgency it demands.

We must protect what we have built, fix what is broken, and create new opportunities for all those who call West Hollywood home. I would be honored to earn your vote once more.

I am proud to be supported by a broad coalition of individuals and organizations including Los Angeles County Supervisor Lindsey Horvath, Congressmember Laura Friedman, Los Angeles County Assessor Jeff Prang, Los Angeles/Orange Counties Building & Construction Trades Council, the California Working Families Party, and Stonewall Democratic Club among others.
