STATEMENT OF GABRIEL RAMIREZ

CANDIDATE FOR CITY CLERK

CITY OF EL MONTE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: City Clerk, City of El Monte

- Proud lifelong resident of El Monte
- Proud son of working-class, immigrant parents from México.
- Proud son of a union household (my dad, a retired grocery worker, is a Teamster)
- Proud product of public schools: Shirpser, Rio Vista, Gidley, Rosemead High School, Citrus College, Cal State LA.

- Community volunteer: El Monte Police Department Explorers, El Monte Fireworks Committee, local food banks, homeless shelters, union organizing, immigrant rights.

EI Monte is my hometown, and I care deeply about our community. I'm here to serve you, not corporate interests or others seeking special favors from City Hall. I refuse their money. I'm a clean-money, good-government candidate. I'm not part of the corrupt political Establishment that dominates El Monte politics and politics in general. I owe no one except you, my conscience, and future generations.

Serving as your City Clerk the past four years has been the honor of my life. I have worked to uphold the responsibilities of this office with honor, integrity, and ethics. I remain committed to protecting the integrity of our elections, maintaining public records, and providing responsive service to our community.

I respectfully ask for your vote so I can continue serving you as El Monte City Clerk with professionalism, transparency, and dedication everyday.
