STATEMENT OF RUDY BOJORQUEZ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF SOUTH EL MONTE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Councilmember

I am honored to serve the residents of South El Monte and humbly ask for your support to continue representing our community on City Council.

During my time on the Council, I have worked to make our city safer, stronger, and provide a better quality of life for all residents. I have supported investments in senior services, community programs, business development, and improvements that enhance the quality of life for our families.

Experience matters, and our community deserves leadership with a proven record of delivering measurable results. I have remained committed to providing that leadership for South El Monte. Putting residents first, making responsible decisions that protect our city's future, and ensuring every taxpayer dollar delivers real value for our community.

South El Monte is a special community built on hardworking families with a strong sense of pride. I will continue fighting to preserve what makes our city unique, support responsible economic development, and ensure residents have a voice in the decisions that affect them.

I respectfully ask for your vote so we can continue moving South El Monte forward together.
