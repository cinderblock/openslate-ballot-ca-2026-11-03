STATEMENT OF ADRIANA C. MITRE

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 2

LITTLE LAKE CITY SCHOOL DISTRICT

I have over 30 years of experience in construction accounting with added experience in human resources. In my current position as HR and payroll, I have learned to interact, communicate and team up with employees to build courses of action. I can bring this valuable set of skills to our district. Especially after the 10-day strike that demoralized our teachers and students. Little Lake deserves better and I will fight for transparency and collaboration.

I will bring to the school board an open mind and an honest commitment for a better future. I grew up in Norwalk, attended Little Lake schools and have lived in Santa Fe Springs for over 20 years.

I am the only candidate running in Area 2 that is endorsed by Little Lake Teachers.

As a concerned community member, taxpayer and mother, I recognize that our entire community shares in the responsibility of providing an exceptional education to our children and grandchildren to prepare them for the future. I truly believe that the current voter perspective in California, supports an increase to awareness. If elected, I will work together with the board, district administration and parents to bring action and transparency to our district.
