STATEMENT OF LISA BLANK

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER, BOARD OF TRUSTEES

PALOS VERDES LIBRARY DISTRICT

"Access for all." "The freedom to read." These were the principles of the "free public library." But what did that mean? First, the library was to be free, open to all, not "members of the club" or a particular group. Second, the library was to be a public institution, its funding coming from whatever government entity established it. Finally, as it served all, at public expense, it would offer the broadest array of subject matter, with something for the widest variety of tastes, interests or beliefs. Today, when information seems to be literally in the air, these concepts feel almost quaint. Yet who among us would be able to purchase every book, subscribe to every magazine and periodical, enroll in every video and audio streaming service? That is what the free public library does, for everyone, at no cost to anyone.

With experience as a user, volunteer, and paid staffer, and with a degree in Library and Information Science, I believe I will bring a unique knowledge base to the PVLD Board of Trustees, one that will help fulfill the library's mandate to maintain the widest collection, the most varied programs, with the most expert staff, supporting those original public library precepts of "access for all" and "the freedom to read."
