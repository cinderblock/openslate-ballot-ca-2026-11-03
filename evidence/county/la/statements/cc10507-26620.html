STATEMENT OF STEVEN LY

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF ROSEMEAD GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 41

Occupation: Councilmember, City of Rosemead/Army Reserve/Taxpayer Advocate

I grew up in Rosemead, attended Rosemead schools, and have spent my life serving the public. As your Rosemead Council Member since 2009, I have delivered results: better police response times by up to 27%, brought in over $8 million in state and federal funds for our community, and created five new parks.

As a UCLA graduate, the first in my family to earn a college degree, I understand what it means to sacrifice, work hard, and invest in the future. As an officer in the United States Army Reserves, I served overseas, commanding 148 soldiers, clearing potentially explosive devices and managing large-scale construction projects in Iraq and Syria. I understand leading under pressure.

As Taxpayer Advocate, I helped over 700 taxpayers resolve tax problems and ensured they received back every tax dollar due to them, helping make their lives little more affordable. I bring that same commitment to every City Council meeting.

My priorities are: safe and secure neighborhoods, protect your hard-earned tax dollars, build more quality parks and recreation, and make sure Rosemead values are protected in state and regional planning.

Rosemead is my home. I am asking for the honor of continuing to serve.
