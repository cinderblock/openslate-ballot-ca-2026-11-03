STATEMENT OF ALFONSO MORALES

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF LYNWOOD GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 47

Occupation: Attorney

I am proud to be a lifelong advocate for Lynwood families. More than four times you've entrusted me with your vote to serve on the Lynwood school board working to improve educational opportunities and invest in the future of our children.

When the Lynwood High School campus collapsed, I helped lead our district's advocacy efforts in Sacramento as Board President. Working with educators, families, administrators, and state leaders, we secured more than $250 million in state funding to rebuild the campus, creating the learning environment our students deserve. Ensuring our children have access to safe, high-quality schools has always been a top priority.

As an attorney, I help keep families together and guide individuals through a difficult legal system. At a time when many families face fear and uncertainty, Lynwood needs leaders who will protect residents, communicate clearly, and stand up for working families.

I am running for Lynwood City Council because there is more work to do. I will bring my experience as a school leader and attorney to strengthen public safety, support local businesses, improve city services, and protect the quality-of-life Lynwood families deserve.

I respectfully ask for your vote
