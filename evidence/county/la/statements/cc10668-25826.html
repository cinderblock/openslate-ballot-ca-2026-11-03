STATEMENT OF KEVIN T. HAYAKAWA

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

ROWLAND UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Physics Professor/Scientist

My name is Dr. Kevin T. Hayakawa. I'm a Cal State Physics Professor and NASA Principal Investigator. As a professor, I train the next generation of doctors, scientists, and engineers. I'm running for re-election to the Rowland Unified School District Board of Education this November. Serving on the school board over the past 4 years has been one of the greatest honors of my life. Rowland Unified isn't just the district I represent—it's the district that raised me. Here's my story:

- RUSD K–12 Graduate: Ybarra Academy, Telesis Academy, and Rowland High School,
- Bachelor's degrees in Physics and Astrophysics from UC Berkeley,
- Master's degree in Astronomy from UCLA,
- Ph.D. in Astronomy and Astrophysics from UCLA,
- 11+ years of teaching experience in all three segments of California's public higher education system: the University of California (UC), the California State University (CSU), and the California Community Colleges (CCC),
- 5+ years of local government experience at both the school district and water district,
- 25+ year resident of Rowland Heights.

Highlights from the past four years include:
- 3 New Advanced Placement courses: AP Physics, AP Precalculus, and AP Seminar,
- Implemented Universal Transitional Kindergarten for all 4-year-olds,
- Free breakfast and lunch for every student year-round,
- Free Expanded Learning Opportunities after school and during summer, winter, and spring breaks,
- Expanded our electric school bus fleet,
- New Nogales High School Performing Arts and Aquatics Facilities,
- Started video/audio recordings of all school board meetings.

My second-term platform centers around 5 themes:
1. STEM Education—Expand Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics education at every grade level.
2. College and Career Readiness—Expand access to Advanced Placement (AP), International Baccalaureate (IB), Career Technical Education, and other pathways that prepare students for college, military service, skilled trades, and careers.
3. Supporting Great Employees—Recruit and retain outstanding teachers, classified professionals, and administrators through competitive compensation and benefits.
4. Fiscal Responsibility—Maintain long-term financial stability while preparing for future emergencies and continuing to invest in students.
5. Community Engagement—Continue engaging students, families, employees, and community members through accessible public meetings and outreach.

Community involvement:
- Buckboard Days Parade Planning Committee, Member
- Rowland Heights Community Coordinating Council, Member
- Walnut Valley Water District, Former Director

I would be honored to once again earn your support.
