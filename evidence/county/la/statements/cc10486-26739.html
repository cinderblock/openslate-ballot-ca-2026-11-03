STATEMENT OF JESUS ROJAS

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF MONROVIA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Dedicated First Responder/Community Leader

As a first responder & police officer, I have dedicated my career to protecting our community through service, integrity, & accountability. I am running for Monrovia City Council to bring that same commitment to transparent leadership, responsible budgeting, & practical solutions that keep our neighborhoods safe & preserve the quality of life that makes Monrovia special.

I proudly serve on the Rio Hondo Watershed Area Steering Committee (WASC), supporting safe, clean water programs, the Rio Hondo College Foundation Board, which provides scholarships, a credit union board, & a local committee dedicated to raising scholarship funds. In these roles, I work collaboratively to protect our environment, strengthen educational opportunities, & serve our region.

The loss of Blondie the bear reminded many of us that Monrovia's natural environment is part of our identity. If elected, I will advocate for stronger partnerships with wildlife experts & state agencies so every reasonable humane option is explored before lethal measures are considered, while always protecting public safety. I will also advocate for the creation of a stand alone senior center in Monrovia.

As your Councilmember, I will listen, lead with integrity, spend taxpayer dollars responsibly, support local businesses, maintain our parks & infrastructure, & preserve the character of our neighborhoods. I respectfully ask for your vote.
