STATEMENT OF AMY HOWORTH

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF MANHATTAN BEACH GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 61

Occupation: Incumbent

I'm running for City Council to use my twenty years of elected experience as both a MBUSD Trustee and City Councilmember to navigate future challenges while preserving our small beach town character. I've worked hard to build consensus, earn trust, and deliver for our residents.

Sacramento's housing mandates raise real concerns about impacts to our quality of life. Among other mitigation strategies, I supported the City's first residential developer fees to alleviate stress on our infrastructure and will keep pressuring state representatives to seek legislative changes.

Public safety remains a priority. I backed hiring seven additional police officers, a drone first responder program, saw the completion of Fire Station 2, and secured funding for critical Sepulveda Boulevard safety improvements. I will continue advocating for sensible E bike legislation and enforcement.

Our local small businesses are vital to our community. I've championed solutions that support them, such as the Circuit Waverider, expansion of our beloved Farmers Market, and free holiday parking.

I'm running for City Council because I love Manhattan Beach, and after two decades of service, I know how to serve it well.

I ask for your vote on November 3rd.

Thank You!
