STATEMENT OF CHRISTOPHER STAPLES

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 6

NORWALK-LA MIRADA UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 53

Occupation: Business Owner

My name is Chris Staples and I respectfully ask for your vote.

I believe education is a partnership between the parents, teachers, and school staff. Living in the NLMUSD for 23 years, I have been involved at all our campuses and it is my belief that if there is something lacking or can be done better, you do not move to a different school, you step up and make improvements.

While on the Board, I championed the new Hutchinson Band Room, promoted expansion of services and opportunities for all students, and worked constantly to support all parents, all students and all teachers.

When schools and parents work together, students are supported from all around. I've always prioritized parent-school partnerships, and I want to continue strengthening and uplifting parents' voices in their child's education.

My goals are to continue increasing CTE (Career Technical Education) opportunities, build upon partnerships with local Community Colleges and Trade Organizations to expose our students to trades and its internship opportunities, and keep increasing opportunities and career options for all our students.

Thank you for your consideration, I look forward to serving our parents and students.
