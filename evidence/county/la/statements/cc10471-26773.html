STATEMENT OF THOMAS D. ALLISON

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
1st District

CITY OF LA VERNE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 41

Occupation: Attorney/Law Professor

My priorities are simple: make La Verne safer, strengthen community life, preserve our small-town character, spend wisely, support local businesses and organizations, and ensure every tax dollar delivers measurable value.

Professionally, I am an attorney, professor, and governance expert. My experience owning a business and nonprofit organization, practicing family law, and earning degrees in business, law, and public administration have prepared me to make thoughtful, fiscally responsible decisions.

I have and will continue to serve our community. Examples of my service include Planning Commission, Committee on Cultural Awareness and Social Inclusion, General Plan Advisory Committee, coaching AYSO soccer, and teaching at the University of La Verne.

I will listen first, work collaboratively, study the facts, vote independently, explain every vote, and faithfully represent District 1.

I am running for City Council because I love this city and believe our best days are ahead. For more than twenty years, La Verne has been my home. My wife and I are raising our young family here, and our children inspire me every day to devote my best talents to preserving and strengthening the community they will inherit. Learn more at ThomasForLaVerne.com (http://thomasforlaverne.com/). It would be my honor to earn your vote.
