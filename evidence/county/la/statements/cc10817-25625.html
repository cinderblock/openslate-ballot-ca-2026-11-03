STATEMENT OF HILDA SOLIS

PARTY PREFERENCE: DEMOCRATIC

UNITED STATES REPRESENTATIVE, 38th DISTRICT

The working people of Southern California deserve a strong advocate in Congress. County Supervisor Hilda Solis is a proven fighter, and we can count on her to fight to create local jobs and economic growth, make neighborhoods safer, and tackle inflation and the high cost of health care, groceries, gasoline, insurance, housing and rent.

That's why Hilda Solis is the candidate in the 38th District endorsed by nurses, firefighters, deputies, small business owners, Planned Parenthood Action Fund, and unions representing tens of thousands of working families.

Hilda Solis has demonstrated that she stands up for the people – not the powerful – as an educator, lawmaker, U.S. Representative, President Barack Obama's Secretary of Labor, and county supervisor. She works across party lines to get things done, and is endorsed by Independents and the Democratic Party.

Raised in the San Gabriel Valley as the daughter of immigrants, Hilda Solis fights for affordable healthcare for all. She's fighting back against the Trump Administration as they slash Medi-Cal for millions of Southern California residents. Hilda Solis fights for solutions to save the health and emergency care systems – and that's why she's supported by United Healthcare Workers.

Hilda Solis works to make our neighborhoods safer. That's why she's supported by Sheriff Robert Luna and Los Angeles County Firefighters Local #1014.

Hilda Solis works to improve our local economy so that people can earn more at work and get a fair chance to buy or rent an affordable home. She secured grants for small businesses to create local jobs. And Hilda Solis fights to lower the cost of living for all.

We need Hilda Solis in Congress, fighting for us.
