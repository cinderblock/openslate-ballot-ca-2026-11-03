STATEMENT OF ERIC OHLSEN

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
4th District

CITY OF PALMDALE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 43

Occupation: Councilmember

Palmdale is my home. It's where I'm raising my child.

I enlisted into the military right out of high school and served through September 11th and the years that followed. Service taught me something I've carried ever since: show up, do the work, and finish what you start.

Before I ever held office, I was out in our neighborhoods hauling away illegal dumping and painting over graffiti, not because it was my job, but because it needed to be done. As your Councilmember, I turned that work into the city's Community Preservation Program, helping ensure no neighborhood gets left behind.

Working families here are stretched thin. I've fought to hold the line on costs, bring good-paying jobs closer to home, and secure our fair share of transportation and infrastructure funding so Palmdale residents spend less time commuting and more time with their families.

I believe in safe neighborhoods, clean streets, and a city government that listens, responds, and respects the people it serves.

We've made progress, but there is more work to do. I'll keep showing up, doing the work, and fighting for Palmdale.

I respectfully ask for your vote.
