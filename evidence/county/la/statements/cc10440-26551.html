STATEMENT OF STEVE TYE

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
1st District

CITY OF DIAMOND BAR GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Diamond Bar is a wonderful place to live, work and play. It has been my privilege to be part of the fabric of Diamond Bar since we moved here in 1983. I have worked diligently to ensure that public safety will continue to be the top priority in Diamond Bar – you can count on me to be sure it always will be. Traffic is an issue in Diamond Bar, and we are seeing progress with the 57/60 freeway "Big Fix" that will keep cars on the freeway and off our streets. A lot has been done, and there is still a lot to do. People are seeking courage and leadership in elected officials - your vote and support can help me continue to provide this in Diamond Bar. I would appreciate your vote on November 3. Thank you for your continued support and consideration.
