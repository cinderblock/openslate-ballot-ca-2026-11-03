STATEMENT OF STEVE RHEE

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
5th District

CITY OF ARCADIA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Small Business Owner

Arcadia is more than where my family lives; it is the community we are proud to call home. As a husband, father, small business owner, and longtime volunteer, I know strong cities depend on leaders who will listen and are transparent.

For 17 years, I have put this belief into action. As President of the Arcadia Education Foundation, I support the top-ranked district in North America. Following the 2025 Eaton Fire, I led a fundraiser raising over $154,000 for 14 displaced teachers. I chaired our Lunar New Year Festival for the last three years and a member with the Chamber of Commerce, Champions Lions Club, and Tournament of Roses.

As a dedicated problem-solver, I am committed to keeping crime out of Arcadia, addressing homelessness effectively, and maintaining fiscal responsibility.

My goal is to unite Arcadia around transparency and to listen closely to our community.

I respectfully ask for your vote to keep our city safe, strong, and thriving.
