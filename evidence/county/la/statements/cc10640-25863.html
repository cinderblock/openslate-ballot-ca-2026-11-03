STATEMENT OF JOSEPH MANACMUL

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 7

DOWNEY UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 43

Occupation: Appointed Incumbent

I'm Joseph Manacmul. Downey isn't just where I live. Ward Elementary, South Middle (Sussman), and Downey High are where I learned to read, made my first friends, and fell in love with this community. Today, my three children attend Downey Unified schools, and my wife teaches in our district. This place raised me. Now I have the honor of giving back to the schools and community that shaped me.

I was appointed to the Board during a difficult district transition. Since then, I've studied budgets closely, walked Measure K construction sites, and pressed for clearer answers on spending because parents deserve facts, not guesswork.

I earned a bachelor's degree from UCLA and a master's from Pepperdine. Managing small businesses and a local nonprofit gave me hands-on experience balancing budgets under real risk—and uniting parents, educators, and community partners.

As parents and neighbors, you deserve my continued focus on safe schools, strong reading and math fundamentals, expanded career pathways, less digital distraction, and responsible use of taxpayer dollars.

I respectfully ask for your vote so we can stay rooted in this community, help every student grow, and keep Downey strong.
