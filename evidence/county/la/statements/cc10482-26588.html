STATEMENT OF ELVA "SAMMY" OSORIO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL,
3rd District

CITY OF MONTEBELLO GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Nonprofit Technical Consultant

As a local business owner on Whittier Boulevard, nonprofit technical consultant, and proud neighbor, I want to build a Montebello where people can stay, invest, and thrive for generations.

Our neighbors shouldn't feel forced out by high costs or broken streets. My goal is keeping our community together through practical leadership. I will address homelessness with balance: combining mental health help and shelter with real safety rules.

We must fix our city's basics. I will prioritize repairing local roads, stopping unfair parking tickets, and fixing traffic lights on Whittier and Beverly so drivers aren't stuck at red lights. To support local families, I will focus on quicker emergency response times, Dial-a-Ride, and expanding programs for youth and seniors.

Using my technical background, I will audit city spending, demand accountability across city services, cut red tape for small businesses, and make downtown a safe, active hub.

I don't answer to career politicians. My door is always open at my shop in the Goddess Mercado Bazaar. I respectfully ask for your vote for District 3.

Elva "Sammy" Osorio
