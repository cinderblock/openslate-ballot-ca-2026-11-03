STATEMENT OF EDGAR PELAYO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF SOUTH GATE GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 47

Occupation: Real Estate Broker

Edgar Pelayo, a lifelong resident and proud product of South Gate, is running for South Gate City Council to bring transparent leadership, accountability, and practical economic insight to City Hall. Local government works best when residents have a seat at the table, and I am committed to fostering an open, inclusive environment where community voices drive city decisions.

As a Real Estate Broker with over 20 years of experience and a dedicated community advocate, I balance my career with active leadership as vice president of a local nonprofit supporting youth and families. The skills I've built throughout my career such as strategic negotiation, budget management, and collaborative problem-solving, will be instrumental in representing our community effectively.

Through my nonprofit work and my service as a South Gate Parks and Recreation Commissioner, I've seen firsthand how powerful our community is when we come together.

I am running for City Council to ensure our local government operates with transparency, efficiency, and true representation. I am ready to stand up for our neighborhoods and families, and guide South Gate toward a brighter future. I would be honored to earn your trust and your vote.
