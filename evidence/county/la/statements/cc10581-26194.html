STATEMENT OF SHARONI DENISE LITTLE

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

COMPTON COMMUNITY COLLEGE DISTRICT

As a current Board Trustee, life-long Compton resident, former Compton College student/3rd Generation Community College family, Mother, Grandmother, and 30-year Servant Leader, I am seeking your vote to continue the vital work of educating and empowering Compton College students, faculty, and staff, and broader community, and to maintain its legacy of educational excellence and societal transformation.

I seek to continue the hard work and perseverance of our entire community, including, increasing enrollment, student success, and graduation, providing responsible extensive basic needs assistance, including free meals, laptop loans, Wi-Fi access, and localized financial aid, receiving a 7-year reaffirmed accreditation status from the Accrediting Commission for Community and Junior Colleges, launching a new Firefighter Academy and other Career, and Technical Educational programs, transforming the campus landscape with new or in construction, student instructional, Athletics complex, Arts facility, and other academic buildings, partnering with St. John's, to build a full-service, on campus Urgent Care Center by 2028, and becoming the only College in LA County to receive funding ($80 million) for on-campus housing, addressing food/housing insecurity.

Representing Compton College, I Co-Chair the statewide California Community College (CCC) diversity, equity, inclusion, and accessibility steering committee, former E-Board member of the CCC Women's Caucus, ACCT Black Caucus, and Trustee Fellow for the CCC/Aspen Institute, College Excellence Program. Educationally, I have earned a Ph.D. (Indiana University), an Ed.D. (University of Southern California), and MA/BA degrees from CSULA.

As we continue the vital work for our community, support me for Compton Community College Board Trustee District #5—Home Grown Leadership!
