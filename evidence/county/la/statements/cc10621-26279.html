STATEMENT OF PATRICE STANZIONE

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

BASSETT UNIFIED SCHOOL DISTRICT

Occupation: Special Education Teacher

As a Bassett USD Board incumbent, I have been a resident of this community for 70 years. I am a teacher with over four decades of K-12 experience and have always been committed to advocating for the needs of all Bassett students, particularly those in special education and English language learning programs, including Foster and Homeless youth. Addressing at-risk students is critical as well. My extensive background in education has equipped me with a deep understanding of the challenges our schools face. I continue to prioritize student safety and well-being, recognizing that a secure and supportive environment is essential for academic achievement. I advocate for higher academic standards because I am convinced that every student can improve with the right resources and support.

Additionally, I am dedicated to supporting our staff, as I am a teacher Union officer, where I teach, ensuring that educators have the tools and conditions necessary to foster a thriving learning atmosphere for our children. I am confident in setting Bassett Unified goals, visions, and policies. I understand the importance of a balanced budget, accountability, and complete transparency. I bring proven integrity, experience, and lifelong commitment to education for the Board position. Vote Patrice Stanzione
