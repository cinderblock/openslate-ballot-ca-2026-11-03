STATEMENT OF GABRIELA A. BERNAL

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF MAYWOOD GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Age: 50

Occupation: Business Owner

For more than 20 years, I have owned and operated a successful transportation company built on hard work, integrity and responsible leadership. Throughout my career, I have created employment opportunities for members of our community while earning a reputation for fairness, accountability, and sound decisions-making. As a business owner, I understand the importance of managing budgets, solving problems, and making decisions that serve the best interests of employees, customers, and the community. These experiences have prepared me to provide thoughtful and effective leadership.

I have proudly called Maywood home for the past 27 years and have been a homeowner for the last 8 years. I am committed to serving our community and working to improve the quality of life for all the residents.
