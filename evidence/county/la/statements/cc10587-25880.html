STATEMENT OF SUSANA GONZALEZ EDMOND

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 5

LONG BEACH COMMUNITY COLLEGE DISTRICT

Age: 53

Occupation: Mother/Business Owner

Dear Neighbor,

Education has been the foundation of my career and my commitment to public service.

As former President of the Long Beach Council PTA, a higher education leader, public commissioner, and small business owner, I have spent more than 20 years building partnerships that strengthen education, expand opportunity, and serve our community.

I am running for the Long Beach Community College District Board of Trustees Area 5 because I believe Long Beach City College changes lives. A strong community college prepares students for university transfer, workforce training, and meaningful careers while strengthening our local economy.

As your Trustee, I will provide thoughtful, collaborative leadership focused on three priorities: student success, fiscal transparency, and community engagement. I will work to ensure every decision puts students first, protects taxpayers' investment, and strengthens collaboration with local schools, employers, labor, and community organizations.

I am proud to have earned the support of a strong coalition of leaders including outgoing Trustee Dr. Virginia Baxter, Congressman Robert Garcia, Assemblymember Josh Lowenthal and Jose L Solache, and dozens more.

I have dedicated my career to bringing people together, solving problems, and serving our community with integrity. I would be honored to earn your vote on November 3rd. You can learn more about my campaign at joinsusana.com
