STATEMENT OF MICHAEL RAMOS

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER,
Trustee Area No. 3

EAST WHITTIER CITY SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 47

Occupation: District Sales Rep

My name is Michael "Mike" Ramos, and I am running for the East Whittier School Board. East Whittier is part of my story: As I am a former East Whittier School District student, and my family's connection continued when my nephew attended district schools.

I currently work as a state employee and serve as a union leader and community advocate. For six years, I have served on my union's District Labor Council and participated in collective bargaining. That experience has taught me to listen carefully, review information, ask difficult questions, work through disagreements, and make responsible decisions.

As a board member, I would focus on student achievement, safe and supportive schools, and responsible use of district resources. I would ask for clear information before major decisions, support transparent communication with families and employees, and review whether district programs and spending are producing meaningful results for students.

I believe board members should be prepared, accessible, and accountable to the community they serve.

I am seeking the opportunity to give back through public service. I would bring experience, perspective, and a commitment to thoughtful decision.

Vote Michael Ramos East Whittier School Board
