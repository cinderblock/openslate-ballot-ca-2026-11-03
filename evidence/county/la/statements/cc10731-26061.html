STATEMENT OF JACQUELINE J. RUBIO

CANDIDATE FOR GOVERNING BOARD MEMBER

VALLE LINDO SCHOOL DISTRICT

Age: 53

Occupation: Business Owner

As a proud South El Monte resident for 53 years, it has been an honor to serve as your Valle Lindo School District Trustee. I am committed to putting students first, supporting our dedicated teachers and staff, maintaining safe schools, and ensuring every child receives the high-quality education they deserve.

Throughout my term, I have led with integrity, transparency, and fiscal responsibility. I proudly championed the effort to place our district's first school bond before voters, creating an opportunity to improve and modernize our schools for future generations. Together, we have strengthened our district's financial stability, expanded community partnerships, and continued investing in opportunities that help every student succeed. I have also built partnerships with local trade organizations, businesses, and community leaders to expand educational opportunities and valuable resources for our students.

Beyond the Board, I have spent many years volunteering with local wrestling clubs, the El Monte/South El Monte Jets youth football and cheer organization, and our local Little League programs because I believe strong communities build strong schools.

I respectfully ask for your vote so we can continue moving Valle Lindo forward and preparing every student for a bright future.
