STATEMENT OF GENARO MORENO

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF PICO RIVERA GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Service has been the foundation of my life, and I believe leadership begins with honesty, listening, serving, and bringing people together.

For more than 20 years, I have proudly served as a law enforcement officer with the Los Angeles School Police Department. As a longtime Pico Rivera resident, father, Realtor, and founder of Angela's Wings, I have helped families achieve homeownership, supported efforts that helped make Pico Rivera's dog park a reality, improved the lives of animals, and provided scholarships to local students pursuing careers in veterinary medicine.

My faith in God has taught me the values of compassion, humility, integrity, and service.

If elected, I will work to:
- Reduce crime and strengthen public safety.
- Increase transparency and accountability in city government.
- Support our small businesses and create opportunities for our youth.
- Promote responsible animal welfare.
- Protect the quality of life that makes Pico Rivera a wonderful place to call home.

Most importantly, I will listen, be accessible, and work alongside residents to solve problems. Every voice deserves to be heard, respected, and represented. Together, we can build a safer, stronger, and more united Pico Rivera.
