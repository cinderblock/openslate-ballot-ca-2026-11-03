STATEMENT OF LUIS GONZALEZ

CANDIDATE FOR MEMBER OF THE CITY COUNCIL

CITY OF HAWAIIAN GARDENS GENERAL MUNICIPAL ELECTION

Occupation: Accounting Technician

As a lifelong public servant and 35-year resident of Hawaiian Gardens, I am committed to serving our community with integrity, accountability, and common-sense leadership. As an Accounting Technician, I understand the importance of fiscal responsibility, transparency, and ensuring taxpayer dollars are spent wisely.

For more than 10 years, I have proudly volunteered as a Hawaiian Gardens Little League coach, mentoring our youth and teaching teamwork, responsibility, respect, and good sportsmanship while building lasting relationships with local families.

My priorities are improving public safety, supporting local businesses, attracting responsible new businesses, and enhancing the quality of life for every resident.

Safe neighborhoods are the foundation of a strong community. I will support our law enforcement and first responders while encouraging partnerships that help keep our streets, parks, neighborhoods, and businesses safe.

I will work to strengthen our local economy by supporting small businesses, encouraging responsible investment, creating opportunities for job growth, and preserving the character of Hawaiian Gardens.

I will listen to residents, work collaboratively, and focus on practical solutions that improve our city today while preparing for tomorrow.

I respectfully ask for your vote for Hawaiian Gardens City Council.
