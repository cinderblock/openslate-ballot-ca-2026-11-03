Education and Qualifications:

I am running for the Mountain View Whisman School District Board because our schools need leadership that listens to parents, respects the community, and puts students first.

As a parent in this district, I believe families deserve a meaningful voice in decisions affecting their children. The Board should actively seek community feedback, communicate openly, and make sure parents are heard before major decisions are made.

MVWSD has a history of wasting taxpayer money. I will push for greater fiscal responsibility, transparency, and accountability. Every dollar should be carefully evaluated and prioritized for the direct benefit of students. We should eliminate unnecessary spending and closely scrutinize expensive consultants and outside contracts to ensure they provide real value.

I also believe in a well-rounded education that supports the whole child. Students deserve regular physical education, art, music, and hands-on learning such as shop classes in junior high. Schools should also promote healthy living through nutritious school lunches without excessive added sugar and meaningful opportunities for physical activity.

I will bring a parent's perspective, ask difficult questions, listen to community feedback, and advocate for responsible decisions that improve our schools.
