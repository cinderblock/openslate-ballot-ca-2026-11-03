Occupation: Community Volunteer

Education and Qualifications:

As the mother of two college students who attended Sunnyvale public schools from childhood through high school, I bring perspective of both a parent and an educator. My experience as an Early Intervention Special Educator and preschool teacher been dedicated to supporting students and their families. I volunteered hundreds of hours in Sunnyvale School District as a room parent, art teacher, PTA member and supporter of school events. I led a parent support group for families of children with special needs, organized workshops, and invited guest speakers. Through the SELPA Community Advisory Committee, I advocated for students and helped families navigate the school system.

I am running to bring transparency and accountability by ensuring decisions are made openly, taxpayer dollars are managed responsibly, and families are informed and heard. I will work to raise academic achievements, remove barriers to education, strengthen student support services, improve student safety, expand access to mental health resources, and build stronger partnerships among families, teachers, staff and the Board. I am committed to preparing every graduate for success in college, vocation, and life path. I will listen, lead with integrity, and make thoughtful decisions that put students first. I respectfully ask for your vote.
