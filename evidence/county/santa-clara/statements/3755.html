Our family celebrated 100 years in Los Gatos in 2024. I am a lifelong resident, Planning Commissioner, and community volunteer who loves our small-Town character. I am running for Town Council to bring experienced, common-sense leadership to local government.

Public safety is first. One-third of Los Gatos sit in hazardous fire zones. As Chair of our wildfire committee, I led efforts to place a Wildland Fire Specialist and expand roadside vegetation management.

Weekend beach gridlock impacts everyone. Of special concern, emergency response and downtown businesses. I will push for immediate solutions: traffic control and holding Caltrans accountable on Highway 17.

On Builder's Remedy, we must follow the law while asserting local control. As Planning Commissioner, I apply objective design standards as State law allows. On Council, I will keep our Housing Element compliant, so we decide where new homes belong.

Fiscal discipline means a truly balanced budget: funding core priorities and long-term liabilities while living within our means.

I respectfully ask for your vote to keep Los Gatos the place we love.
