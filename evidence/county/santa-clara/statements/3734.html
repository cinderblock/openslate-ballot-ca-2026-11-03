Occupation: City Commissioner; Teacher; Mentor; Technology Executive (AI)

Education and Qualifications:

I have dedicated my life to investing in people because I believe the strongest communities are built by connecting people. For 25+ years, I have served our community as a teacher, mentor, and volunteer.

During the past 10 years, I proudly served on Cupertino's Parks and Recreation Commission, Library Commission, and Cupertino Library Foundation. I helped advance the Parks and Recreation System Master Plan, supported library expansion, created the City's all-inclusive playground, expanded the dog off-leash area, strengthened recreation programs, and recommended funding for local nonprofits.

Every weekend for 25 years, I have taught preschool children, mentored students, parents, and professionals, and helped seniors improve digital literacy and stay safe from online scams. I also serve on the Board of Hope Services, supporting individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities and mental health needs.

Professionally, I have led global technology organizations and serve as an executive advisor on AI and digital transformation.

My family has proudly called Cupertino home for many years, and our children attended Cupertino schools.

If elected, I will put Residents First, bring people together, preserve quality of life, and strengthen opportunities for students, seniors, families, local businesses and City staff. www.Gopal4Cupertino.org
