It is an honor to represent you in the California State Assembly.

While our democracy is under attack at the national level, I've led the effort to protect voting rights in California. I've championed dozens of laws that strengthen our democracy—from permanently expanding vote-by-mail, to creating the nation's first Office of Elections Cybersecurity to protect against cyberattacks, to developing the Where's My Ballot service so you can track your ballot and make sure it's counted.

I've fought hard for consumers, preventing utilities like PG&E from using your ratepayer dollars for lobbying and promotional advertising that benefits shareholders, not customers. I wrote the law to make California the first state to require businesses to let you cancel subscriptions online—as easily as you signed up. I cracked down on deceptive hotel junk fees so that the price you see is the price you pay. No more surprises at checkout.

Youth mental health is personal to me. I've seen the devastating toll of youth suicide in our own communities. That's why I led the effort to offer free suicide prevention training for every student 13 and older, and for all K-12 teachers, administrators, and families in California. Training and prevention will save lives.

I'm proud of my record of commonsense, results-driven leadership that puts people first. I know there's more to do, and I humbly ask for your support so I can keep fighting for you.
