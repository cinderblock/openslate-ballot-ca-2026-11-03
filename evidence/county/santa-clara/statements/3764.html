Occupation: Milpitas Planning Commissioner

Education and Qualifications:

Milpitas is a city of opportunity, innovation, and hardworking families. I am an immigrant and a proud 30-year Milpitas resident. I serve as your Planning Commissioner and formerly served on the Economic Development and Trade Commission, which allowed me to apply successful business strategies to government bureaucracy.

As a successful retired small-business owner, I know how to promote and grow businesses. I operated an essential market for more than a decade. During COVID, I worked every day on the retail floor to keep families supplied, supported my employees, and later volunteered to pick up litter on Milpitas streets.

I bring real-world experience in government and business, sound budgeting, and financial responsibility. As a Planning Commissioner, I helped advance responsible business growth, well-paying jobs, smart infrastructure, and safe neighborhoods.

As your Councilmember, I will focus on essential services: clean streets, well-maintained parks, safer neighborhoods, and infrastructure that works. I will streamline permits, help deliver affordable housing, and address homelessness with dignity and compassion.

Milpitas deserves an accessible and accountable Councilmember who understands residents, public safety, and local businesses.

I humbly ask for your vote. Endorsed by Mayor Carmen Montano and Councilmembers Evelyn Chua and William Lam. www.dipakawasthi.org
