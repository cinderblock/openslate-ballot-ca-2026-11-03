It has been an honor to serve the communities of California's 28th Assembly District, representing portions of Santa Clara and Santa Cruz counties. I am proud of what we've accomplished together. I'm running for re-election because our communities deserve experienced leadership that delivers results.

As Chair of the Assembly Elections Committee, and with more than 27 years of experience as Santa Cruz County's former Chief Elections Official, I am working to ensure California remains the gold standard for secure, accurate, transparent, and accessible elections. Protecting democracy is foundational to addressing every other challenge we face.

I serve on the Appropriations, Business and Professions, Education, Natural Resources and Privacy and Consumer Protection committees, and as Chair of the Select Committee on California's Mental Health Crisis. From strengthening oversight and accountability of state programs, to advancing consumer protections, protecting our children from the evolving influence of Al, safeguarding our coast and natural resources, and expanding access to affordable, quality mental health care—my focus is on making government work better for the people it serves.

Our communities are facing urgent challenges: housing and homelessness, affordability and cost-of-living pressures, access to health and mental health care, economic opportunity, climate and wildfire resilience, water security, and public safety—made even more difficult by federal funding cuts that threaten our most vulnerable residents. I will continue to fight for responsible budgets, smart investments, and strong accountability to ensure resources reach those who need them most.

I respectfully ask for your vote so I can continue partnering with you to deliver real results for the 28th Assembly District—www.gailpellerinforassembly.com.
