As a Santa Clara resident for 21 years, I am committed to our City's future. I have a proven record of leadership as a former Santa Clara City Councilmember and Library Trustee and now a Santa Clara Unified School District Board Trustee. Bringing people together, solving problems and serving our community has been my focus.

During my service on the City Council and School Board, I strengthened public safety, supported students and families, improved quality of life and promoted fiscal responsibility. As a Library Trustee, I lead the community effort bringing the Northside Library to a reality expanding access to educational resources and community services.

My priority as Mayor will be to strengthen our local economy, support responsible redevelopment, attract quality businesses and ensure growth pays for infrastructure and city services. I support innovative programs like worker cooperative models that help local small businesses succeed.

Under my leadership, we will invest in parks, libraries, youth and senior programs, restore resident leadership programs and expand opportunities for residents of all ages to serve Santa Clara. I will lead with integrity, accountability, transparency, fiscal responsibility and thoughtful planning.
