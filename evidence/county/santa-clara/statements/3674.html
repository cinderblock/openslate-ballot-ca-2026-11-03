Education and Qualifications:

As a Palo Alto Unified School District teacher and mom, I believe in the potential of every child. I earned my Master of Science in Elementary and Special Education, and found my calling helping students develop their math skills. That strong foundation gives them the confidence they need to thrive. I want to bring my leadership to the PAUSD Board to help all students thrive by providing an excellent education for every student, nurturing an environment where all students matter and belong, and rebuilding trust across the district and within the community.

All students deserve excellence, which includes critical thinking, problem solving, leadership and teamwork skills that prepare them to embrace their futures. When students discover their passions, they find strong motivation to go deep into subjects they love. I will advocate for students to develop these skills and pursue their passions in AP, Honors, Advanced, College Prep, Vocational and Elective classes.

Students who know they matter and belong become happy, confident, resilient, and willing to share their unique perspectives. I will work to create a culture of belonging, where every student is truly seen and valued.

Trust allows teachers, staff, and families to bring their knowledge and expertise to solve problems together and deliver excellence. I will rebuild trust by listening respectfully; following fair, consistent, and transparent processes; and clearly explaining my votes.

My family is deeply invested in PAUSD, with one graduate and one current student. As an award-winning PTA leader, I've created programs, developed relationships, and advocated for every child because that's how strong schools are built.

Beyond our public schools, I helped restart TrackWatch, a local suicide prevention group, so that community members can learn how to provide care and support to our vulnerable young people. My service on several executive boards gives me experience managing people and budgets.

As a school board trustee, I will bring my classroom experiences, governance skills, and steadfast belief in the potential of every child to advocate for all students. Students will be at the center of every vote I cast.
