Occupation: Governing Board Member, Alum Rock School District

Education and Qualifications:

Serving on the Alum Rock School Board has been one of the greatest honors of my life. I have always put students first, making tough but necessary decisions to strengthen our district.

Together, we improved our financial standing by moving from a qualified budget to a positive budget. I established an Audit Committee, led audits of our bond program, and increased transparency and accountability.

I authored a policy banning political contributions from district contractors to reduce special-interest influence. I also opposed selling district land and helped save the former Fischer Middle School, securing a $12 million investment to transform it into a state-of-the-art campus.

I also led the effort to develop teacher workforce housing so we can recruit and retain outstanding educators.

Alum Rock is moving in the right direction, and our best days are ahead. I will continue to put students first, protect taxpayer dollars, and provide every child with a high-quality, dignified education. www.AndresQuintero.com
