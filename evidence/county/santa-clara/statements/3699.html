Occupation: Retired Teacher
Age: 60

Education and Qualifications:

I have 30 years experience working as a high school teacher in East Side Union High School District and was a Union Rep for East Side Teachers Association. I served as Benefits Committee Chair and was on the Financing Public Education Committee and State Legislation Committee for California Teachers Association State Council. Attending several summer institute trainings on school finance, budget and bargaining essentials helped me in my role on the bargaining team negotiating for better conditions for our students and staff. I also lobbied in Sacramento advocating for fair laws for charter schools, while pushing for increased school funding for public schools.

I have a Bachelor's from UC Santa Cruz, a teaching credential from SJSU, and a Master's in Teacher Leadership from National University.

I value cultural diversity, equity, equality and inclusion. I believe in the need for strong academic and socio-emotional support for students so they can achieve their full potential. My goal as a Board member would be to listen to all stakeholders in the community and create needed change.
