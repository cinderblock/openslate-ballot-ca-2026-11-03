Education and Qualifications:

I am running for Trustee, Area 1, to provide the experienced, steady leadership our students deserve. My priorities are clear:

Put Students First: Center every board decision on student success and well-being.

Elevate Academics: Raise the bar for educational excellence across all grade levels.

Support Educators: Protect and empower the teachers and staff who show up for our kids daily.

Fiscal Integrity: Manage our budget transparently so every dollar directly reaches the classroom.

As a professional with an MS in Electrical Engineering, an MBA, and over 20 years of experience solving complex problems in engineering, product management, and business, I know how to cut through gridlock, unite people, and deliver results. I will bring that same practical, get-it-done approach to the school board.

Santa Clara has given my family so much. I am ready to give back with the time, focus, and dedication this community deserves. I would be honored to earn your vote.
