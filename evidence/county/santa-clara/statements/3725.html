Occupation: Community Volunteer

Education and Qualifications:

I am honored to run for the Saratoga Union School District Board of Trustees. As a mother of four children in our elementary and middle schools, and as Saratoga Elementary PTA President from 2024-2026, I have worked closely with families, teachers, and district leadership, and I have seen firsthand what makes this district exceptional.

I hold a Master's in Special Education and Human Development and served as an educator for nearly a decade. I am committed to supporting every child academically, socially, and emotionally.

Saratoga's schools are already excellent, built on the dedication of outstanding teachers, staff, and community involvement. My priority is not change for its own sake, but preserving excellence and strengthening what works: recruiting and retaining top-tier teaching and administrative leadership across STEM, humanities, art, and athletics, supporting educators with the tools needed for success, and maintaining strong partnerships between families and the district.

As a trustee, I will listen carefully, engage thoughtfully, and always decide with a students-first mindset. I will work to ensure Saratoga continues to attract and retain the best educators and leaders in the state, so every child continues to thrive.

I ask for your support so I can continue serving the community I love and trust.
