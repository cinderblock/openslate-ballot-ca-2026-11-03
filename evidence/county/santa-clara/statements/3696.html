Occupation: Retired Software Engineer/Former EMT

Education and Qualifications:

I have spent over 40 years working for public education locally as a volunteer, PTA leader, employee and now MVLA trustee. Elected in 2018, reelected in 2022, I am currently the longest-serving trustee, having served as clerk, vice president, then president. Experience matters. I have calmly, collaboratively and with compromise advocated for and voted to guide MVLA.

My constant priorities: excellent, equitable academics that open doors to college and careers; fiscal responsibility; continued enhancement of facilities and technologies; safe campuses; and student social and mental wellness. During my tenure, MVLA has weathered COVID-19, completed $290 million Measure E bond, installed stadium lights, made our equity statement a classroom reality, launched an ethnic studies course, added positions in wellness, communications, inclusion, and CTE, expanded alternative learning programs, and added wellness areas. Eight years have seen 3 different superintendents and all new principals.

My background includes BS/MS Purdue University, former EMT, honored software engineer, various offices with Bubb, Graham and MVHS PTAs and LAMV PTA Council, school site council, substitute teacher, and MVLA IT consultant. I continually and diligently work to provide stability and innovation at MVLA. I ask for and would be honored to have your vote. www.vonne.org
