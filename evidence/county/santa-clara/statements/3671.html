Ten years ago, I moved to Area 3, drawn by its diversity and strong sense of community. When it was time to enroll my oldest child in school, I was encouraged to look outside our neighborhood. Instead, I chose our neighborhood public school, P.A. Walsh.

That decision changed the direction of my life.

I became involved in my children's school and began advocating for students and families across our district. As your appointed Area 3 Trustee, I have brought my community experience, a Social Justice degree from Gavilan College, and my ongoing social work training at San José State to every decision on the board.

I believe public schools are one of our most powerful institutions for creating opportunity. Our students have diverse backgrounds and diverse needs, and academic success requires supporting the whole child, because children cannot learn when their basic needs go unmet.

I was appointed to the board two years ago to represent Area 3 in support of our students, educators, and public schools. I am running to continue building inclusive, welcoming schools where every child is supported, where every family belongs, and where every student has the opportunity to thrive.
