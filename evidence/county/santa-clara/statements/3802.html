Occupation: Government Relations Director

Education and Qualifications:

As a lifelong Santa Clara resident, I'm running because our city deserves leaders who are accountable, practical, and honest about how government spends your money.

For years I've worked with local governments to solve problems and build partnerships that strengthen our community and improve lives.

As a Santa Clara Historical and Landmarks Commissioner, I've helped protect what makes our city unique while supporting thoughtful, responsible growth that respects our neighborhoods and history.

My service on the Chief's Advisory Committee, the Economic Development and Marketing Committee, and the West Valley-Mission College Foundation Board has provided firsthand experience with public safety, economic opportunity, education, and collaboration.

On the Council, I'll support transparent decision-making, well-maintained neighborhoods, reliable infrastructure, and responsible spending that delivers measurable results.

Government works best when decisions are based on facts, fiscal responsibility, meaningful public input, and long-term planning. Santa Clara can continue growing and innovating without losing the character, history, and values that make it our home.

I would be honored to earn your vote and serve on the Santa Clara City Council with integrity and dedication.
