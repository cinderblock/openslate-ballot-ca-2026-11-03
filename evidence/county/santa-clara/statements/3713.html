Occupation: Teacher

Education and Qualifications:

I'm a 23-year experienced teacher, parent, veteran, and Christian dedicated to preparing the next generation of leaders. Married to an immigrant, I'm also a descendant of the Blackfoot Tribe. Raised by my mother through abusive relationships while my family relied on welfare, I understand the challenges many families face. Today, I serve as a Special Educator committed to improving Franklin McKinley School District student academic achievement, protecting parents' rights, and increasing transparency between the district, schools, teachers, and families.

Franklin McKinley School District states every 8th-grade graduate will be prepared for a college-preparatory curriculum. Yet only 35% of Franklin McKinley School District students are proficient in English Language Arts (reading and writing) and 28% in math, with scores declining over the past 14 years. Our children deserve better!

As your Board Trustee, I've answered hundreds of communications from parents and community members because transparency and accountability matter. I represent you!

I will continue fighting for safe schools with equality for all, secure campuses, and boys staying out of girls' sports, locker rooms, and bathrooms; parents' rights to direct their children's education; transparency between families, schools, and the district; and academic excellence by raising test scores and strengthening educational quality.
