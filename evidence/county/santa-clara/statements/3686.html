Occupation: Governing Board Member, East Side Union High School District

Education and Qualifications:

I'm a teacher, principal, mom, proud graduate of East Side Union High School District, and serve as your Trustee. I'm running for re-election because I believe every student deserves an excellent public education and the opportunity to reach their full potential.

During my eight years on the Board, I've worked to keep our schools safe, expand mental health supports, strengthen college and career pathways, and secure more than $10 million to expand digital access, bringing free internet to 300,000 people. We also expanded dual enrollment, so more students can earn college credit in high school, reduce college costs, and graduate better prepared for college, careers, and life.

As the daughter of immigrants, I watched my parents work tirelessly to give my brothers and me opportunities they could only dream of. One teacher believed in me, challenged me, and changed my life. I became the first in my family to graduate from college, earning two bachelor's degrees from UC Davis, and came home to East San José to serve the community that shaped me.

I remain committed to keeping our schools safe, academically strong, and preparing every student for success. Thank you for your vote.
