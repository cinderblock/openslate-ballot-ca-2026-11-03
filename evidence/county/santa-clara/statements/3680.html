Occupation: Retired Educator
Age: 58

Education and Qualifications:

Mary Grunthaner is a retired educator. In that capacity for more than three decades, the issues of ensuring safety and mental health support for students and staff, accountability for those in leadership positions, and academic progress for all students in San Jose Unified have been at the forefront of Mary Grunthaner's professional career. Those issues will be the focus of her time after her being elected as Trustee for Area 4 of San Jose Unified.

Mary's safety and mental health priorities are to support and expand Wellness Centers throughout the district, to ensure positive transitions at consolidated elementary schools, and protect the right of all students to be safe from bullying, especially LGBTQ+ students.

Mary's accountability priorities are to provide transparency in decision-making, to engage in open communication with families and community members, and to hold district leadership accountable.

Mary's progress priorities are to support high-quality public education, student achievement at the highest levels, and to ensure access to academic counselors and instructional coaches at each school site.

Recently, San Jose Unified's community has been impacted by the closure of five elementary schools, nearly 19% of all district elementary schools. As Trustee, Mary will diligently work to support students, families, and staff who have had to make this transition. Mary looks forward to continuing her life's work of public service as trustee.

Mary Grunthaner earned her Bachelor's degree in History and Social Science Teacher Prep from Humboldt State University (HSU) in 1991, her Teaching Credential from HSU in 1992, and her Master's degree in Educational Leadership from San Jose State University in 2002. With 25 years experience as a classroom teacher and 7 years as an Assistant Principal in San Jose Unified, she retired in 2024. Mary has been married to her wife Sheri for 18 years and enjoys spending time with their three grandchildren.

As a Social Studies Teacher at San Jose High School and John Muir Middle School, Mary invested her energy in helping her students recognize that they are part of the story of the United States of America through civic engagement. As an Assistant Principal at Willow Glen High School, Mary utilized her Master's degree in Educational Leadership in helping students from varied backgrounds to achieve their goals and collaborating with teachers to enhance their teaching.
