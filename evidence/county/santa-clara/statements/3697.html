Occupation: Community Volunteer

Education and Qualifications:

I am running for the Alum Rock School Board because I believe every student deserves an excellent education, a safe learning environment, and the opportunity to reach their full potential.

As a PTA President, I have had the privilege of working alongside parents, teachers, administrators, and community members to strengthen our school and support student success.

I led organized school programs, coordinated volunteers, advocated for student needs, and built partnerships that benefit our school community. These experiences gave me a firsthand understanding of the challenges our schools face and the importance of listening to diverse perspectives before making decisions. After beating cancer this last year, I am determined to reinvigorate this district and make it the positive and wonderful Alum Rock students deserve.

I am currently the inaugural Chair of the district's Bonds Committee after 5 years of the district having no citizen oversight. For the first time in half a decade we are complying with state law.

I am committed to making informed, balanced decisions that reflect the needs of all students. It would be an honor to serve our community, advocate for our children, and help ensure our schools continue to provide high-quality educational opportunities for every student.
