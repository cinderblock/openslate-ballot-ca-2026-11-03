Occupation: Chair of Palo Alto Planning & Transportation Commission
Age: 50

Education and Qualifications:

MBA, Stanford; B.S. Biology, Stanford

Palo Alto needs councilmembers who know our neighborhoods, respond to resident feedback, and have the experience and judgment to deliver results.

I've been part of this community nearly my entire life, and I love our city. As the daughter of Chinese immigrants, my lifelong devotion began when I attended PAUSD schools (Nixon, JLS and Gunn). I've proudly raised my own family here, and have volunteered for 20+ years in our schools, local nonprofits, and city government.

Since 2021, I have served on the Planning Commission, bringing business, strategy, and finance experience to help guide Palo Alto through tough decisions on housing, transportation, and development. Listening carefully to residents, I've helped the city plan for housing growth, strengthen renter protections, protect nature, streamline permitting for small businesses, and make our streets safer for cyclists and pedestrians.

Palo Alto is growing. The question is not whether change is coming, but how we manage it: I believe in practical priorities, responsible budgeting, and decisions that strengthen our neighborhoods rather than strain them. My only goal is to serve Palo Alto—I will not seek higher office. I would be honored to earn your vote.
