I was born and raised in Santa Clara and have dedicated my life to giving back to the community that shaped me. My wife and I are grateful to be raising our family here, and I am committed to preserving the neighborhoods, schools, and quality of life that make Santa Clara such a special place to call home.

After completing college, I built a career as a CEO, advisor, and board member, working with organizations to strengthen leadership, improve financial stewardship, and plan for the future. Those experiences have taught me the importance of listening, building consensus, and making thoughtful, responsible decisions.

I believe strong communities are built through service, strong families, and opportunities for our youth. For the past twelve years, I have served on the Briarwood Little League Board of Directors and currently serve as President. I also serve on the Board of the Santa Clara Youth Soccer League and have coached youth, high school, and collegiate baseball and soccer.

If elected, I will work hard to provide thoughtful leadership, fiscal responsibility, transparency, and a collaborative approach to City Hall. My commitment is to always listen, put residents first, and do what is best for our community.
