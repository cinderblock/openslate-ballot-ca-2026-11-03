Occupation: Governing Board Member, Los Altos School District
Age: 52

Education and Qualifications:

I have been active in the Los Altos School District community for twenty years, and have had the privilege of serving on the LASD Board of Trustees for the last ten, including two terms as Board President.

Over that time I have had thousands of conversations with parents, teachers, and community members, and I understand both what makes our schools so special and the urgent work required to ensure that they continue to thrive in the years to come.

During my time on the board I have worked to:

Increase the mental health resources available to our students, teachers, staff, and families.

Protect and reinforce our school communities as safe places for learning and as the vital centers of our neighborhoods.

Implement a financially responsible facilities plan which will provide improved learning environments for all students.

Promote transparency and open communication between the district, our parents, and the broader community.

My time on the board has shaped me into an effective, thoughtful advocate for our community as a whole, and with your support I look forward to continuing that work.
