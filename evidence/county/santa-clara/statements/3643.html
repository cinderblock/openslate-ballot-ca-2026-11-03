Occupation: Councilmember/Navy Officer
Age: 41

Education and Qualifications:

In the Navy, we live by a commitment to leave nobody behind. I believe our communities should live by these same words. That's why I've dedicated my life to service as a Union City Councilmember, Navy Reserve officer, and housing leader.

My grandparents were sent to Japanese-American internment camps during World War 2. Their history grounds my commitment to protecting immigrants across California being targeted by Trump and ICE.

As a Vice-Mayor and County Housing Authority Chair, I've helped make it easier to build affordable housing, secured funding for homelessness services, expanded public transit, and supported renters.

I've served in an intelligence role in the Navy Reserve and graduated from Harvard Law School. I'm also an elected Democratic Central Committee member and interned in the Obama White House.

Today, my wife—a public school teacher and union member—and I are raising three kids. Our two oldest attend the same public school where my wife and I first met as kids.

I feel a strong sense of duty to my family, community, and state. Californians are living without shelter, can't afford groceries, make rent, or buy a home.

We must do better.

My platform: lower the cost of housing, utilities, and groceries; achieve universal healthcare and childcare; raise wages and protect workers; fully fund public schools; prepare communities for wildfire and climate threats; stop ICE; and protect our most vulnerable from Trump.

Endorsements: California Democratic Party; California AAPI Legislative Caucus; Planned Parenthood Advocates Mar Monte; California Professional Firefighters; United Nurses Associations of California/Union of Health Care Professionals; California Environmental Voters; State Senator Aisha Wahab; Assemblymember Alex Lee; and Alameda County Supervisor Elisa Márquez.

Let's build a California with opportunity for all.
