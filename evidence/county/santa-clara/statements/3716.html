Occupation: Attorney, Appointed Incumbent
Age: 47

Education and Qualifications:

LASD schools have always been part of my identity. I grew up in Los Altos and attended LASD schools. My mother also taught in LASD for many years. As an adult, I returned to Los Altos with my family so that our children could also attend LASD schools. My older daughter is a junior at MVHS and my younger one is an eighth grader at Blach.

Throughout their LASD journey, I have been deeply involved in their schools and with the district. I have served for 6 years on the PTA Boards of Springer and Blach─5 of those years as President. And I have played a meaningful role in forging an alliance between the Los Altos Educational Foundation (LAEF) and each of the PTA's that has blossomed into LASD Education Partners.

In those endeavors, my guiding principle has been making our schools and organizations work better for all LASD families, accentuating the attributes that make LASD great, and building consensus around how we want our schools to be.

In March, I was appointed to the LASD Board of Trustees. I now hope to continue my work with the Board in furtherance of my lifetime of dedication to LASD.
