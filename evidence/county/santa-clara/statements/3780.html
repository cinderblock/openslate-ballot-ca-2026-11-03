I'm running for City Council to stand up for you, bring people together, turn ideas into action, and plan boldly for our future. For more than 25 years, I've worked to make Mountain View stronger, welcoming, and ready for new challenges. I've served on the Parks and Recreation Commission, Human Relations Commission, and Environmental Sustainability Task Force, as a volunteer City mediator, and in leadership roles with nonprofits supporting families, the environment, mental health, and our history.

During the pandemic, I founded Appetite for Good, saving dozens of restaurants while feeding hundreds of families. I built a coalition that advanced cleaner building standards across the Bay Area. I've also led pro-democracy events drawing tens of thousands of people together to protect our rights.

On Council, my priorities will be housing that enables people to stay and thrive, strong local businesses, thoughtful and effective leadership, safer and more connected neighborhoods, and a greener, more sustainable city. I listen deeply, find common ground, build consensus, and get things done. Together, we can meet today's needs and shape Mountain View's future with hope and confidence.
