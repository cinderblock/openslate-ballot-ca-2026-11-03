Occupation: Mayor/Attorney/Mediator

Education and Qualifications:

As your Councilmember and now Mayor, I work every day to make this extraordinary city an even better place to live by addressing housing, climate, mental health, and economic vibrancy. I am running for re-election because there is more work to do. Palo Alto has been my home for over 30 years, and like you, I care about improving quality of life. So I'll champion parks, libraries, and public safety.

Palo Alto is a climate and sustainability leader. While Climate Chair, we improved EV charging and home electrification options, launched a nature-based levee to combat sea level rise, and more. I'll promote continued progress. Palo Alto needs more housing for people who work here. I'll pursue solutions that enhance and balance our community. Palo Altans deserve affordability. As our Northern California Power Agency Commissioner, I'll continue to ensure our city-owned electric utility has rates lower than PG&E, while remaining resilient and green.

Professionally, I'm an intellectual property attorney and mediator for federal courts and the International Trade Commission. I co-founded Women's Professional Soccer and Sibling Cities USA. My experienced leadership in Palo Alto has earned endorsements from Council colleagues, State Senator Becker, Assemblymember Berman, former State Senator Simitian, and Supervisor Abe-Koga. I respectfully request your vote. vickiforcouncil.com.
