Occupation: Business Owner

Education and Qualifications:

Independently-minded Pete Verbica is a fiscal conservative endorsed by the Howard Jarvis Taxpayers Association PAC for his defense of Proposition 13, protecting homeowners and seniors from rising property taxes. He supports water infrastructure, insurance reform, and affordable coverage for California homeowners.

Pete's family roots in California run deep, including a multi-generational link to Henry Coe State Park. He grew up on his family's cattle ranch, learning what it takes to make a living from the land, meet a payroll, and keep a promise, and remains an advocate for open space and wildfire resilience.

A graduate of Bellarmine College Preparatory, Santa Clara University, and MIT, and a Certified Financial Planner® with business experience, Pete brings financial discipline to public service.

In his statewide race for the California Board of Equalization, Pete advanced to the top two and earned 925,000 votes, proving broad support.

Pete believes families deserve relief from the rising cost of living through gas, utility, and sales tax cuts; he also believes that inflation, fueled by excess money printing, is the worst tax of all. He believes in working across the aisle to hold Washington accountable for its core duties—national security, immigration reform, and eliminating waste, such as the $126 billion train boondoggle.

A father of four daughters, including one who served in the U.S. Navy, Pete has long supported servicemembers, chairing a military care committee that sent hundreds of packages to sailors and Marines. He also champions education and youth arts.
