Occupation: San José District 5 Councilmember

Education and Qualifications:

Born and raised in East San José, I know firsthand the challenges many families face. Growing up in a working-class household with a single mother, I experienced poverty, housing instability, and youth violence. Those experiences shaped my commitment to public service and continue to drive my work to create safer neighborhoods, expand opportunity, and build a stronger East San José for everyone.

Since my election to represent District 5 in 2022, I have focused on delivering real results for our community. I championed a $200 million investment to strengthen East San José, led efforts to combat illegal dumping, secured funding for FIFA World Cup activations in East San José, advanced the East San José Revitalization Plan to support local businesses, and invested in the City's Youth Master Plan. I also worked to keep Regional Medical Center open, expand affordable housing, improve public safety, regulate the over-concentration of smoke shops, prevent new problematic liquor stores, and secure funding for unhoused residents and those experiencing mental health crises.

I am proud to be endorsed by Assemblymember Ash Kalra, Santa Clara County Supervisor Betty Duong, the South Bay Labor Council, and local firefighters and nurses. I respectfully ask for your vote to continue building a safer, cleaner, and stronger East San José together.
