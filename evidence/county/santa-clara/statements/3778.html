Living and working in Mountain View is a true privilege. It is one of the most innovative cities in the world. It is also clear from talking with neighbors in our city that most have the same burning question: can working families, young people, seniors, veterans, teachers, service workers, and small businesses still build a future here?

I'm running for city Council because I have the background and the commitment to help ensure that answer is yes.

My priorities are clear: expand workforce and affordable housing through practical public-private partnerships; support local jobs, apprenticeships, and career pathways; protect our parks and our environment; improve public transportation options and neighborhood safety, and ensure City Hall continues to serve its residents by remaining a welcoming community for all.

My background includes service as a U.S. Army Officer specializing in public policy, administration, and human relations, public sector experience up to the highest levels of government, and a lifelong commitment to community leadership. Most importantly, I believe in service before self and I have a hard-won experience bringing people together to solve difficult problems, both at home and abroad.

I respectfully ask for your vote. Together, let's build the Mountain View of tomorrow.
