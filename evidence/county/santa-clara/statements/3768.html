Leadership begins with listening. Every week I meet with residents, business leaders, nonprofit organizations, community leaders, and our faith community to learn their ideas and concerns, then I act on them. That approach delivers results. I introduced an ordinance addressing blighted residential and commercial properties, launched the Read 20 initiative to improve children's reading proficiency, helped secure funding to end the flooding of Llagas Creek, protecting homes and businesses. I secured a dedicated public art funding mechanism, launched the community-wide Healthy Morgan Hill initiative, and have helped raise more than $1 million for local non-profit organizations.

I will: 1) Continue working to foster an environment that provides opportunities for businesses to create new jobs and opens the door for companies to expand or relocate here; 2) Continue supporting our youth sports programs and expand recreational tourism; 3) Ensure public safety gets the resources needed to keep us safe.
