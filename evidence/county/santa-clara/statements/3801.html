My husband and I chose to raise our family in Santa Clara for its strong neighborhoods and engaged community. As a parent and education advocate, I know the decisions our City Council make today will shape Santa Clara's future for my child, and all of us.

For more than 15 years, I've worked within university programs learning to bring people together with different priorities to find lasting solutions. I've brought that approach to Santa Clara through PTA leadership from our local schools to the state Capitol, and now as President of the Santa Clara Schools Foundation. Good leadership starts with listening, and that's how I'll approach the work of City Council.

Santa Clara faces critical decisions on housing, infrastructure, and public safety. I support smart, intentional growth: housing families can afford, paired with the infrastructure and development our communities need, so growth strengthens Santa Clara rather than strains it.

Strong communities aren't built by government alone. Every resident—youth, families, and seniors play a role. I'll build a Council defined by trust, transparency, and participation.
