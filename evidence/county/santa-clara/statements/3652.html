Occupation: Educator

Education and Qualifications:

As a trustee for the Fremont Union High School District, mathematics instructor at De Anza College, and board member of the Foothill-De Anza Foundation, I have seen how vital our community colleges are to students, families, employers, and the broader community. Foothill and De Anza provide an affordable pathway for students beginning their college journey, help local businesses find skilled workers, and give community members opportunities to gain new skills throughout their lives.

I am running for the Foothill-De Anza District board to help the district meet today's challenges while preparing for tomorrow's opportunities. College affordability remains essential, employers need graduates with rapidly evolving skills, and artificial intelligence is changing both the classroom and the workplace. The district must respond thoughtfully by supporting innovation and ensuring students receive the resources they need.

My experience as an educator, trustee, and foundation board member gives me a deep appreciation for the work of faculty, staff, administrators, and community partners. I will bring these voices together, find resources that support teaching and student services, and keep Foothill and De Anza focused on providing excellent, accessible education for a diverse student body.
