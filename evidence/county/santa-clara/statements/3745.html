From 2020 to 2024, I had the honor of serving as your City Councilmember from east side Gilroy, now District 6. I am a fourth generation Gilroyan, and chose to raise my own family here, because I am deeply committed to ensuring that our hardworking families feel proud and secure in their neighborhoods. I believe our City needs to invest in our Districts infrastructure, such as improved street lighting and landscaping, and pedestrian and traffic safety initiatives. When we are proud of our neighborhood, no matter which side of town we live on, our whole City benefits. As a co-founder of Gilroy Growing Smarter, I advocate for building housing responsibly, so younger generations and long-time residents can afford to stay and put down roots here, too. As part of CARAS, I have the privilege of working with young people on life skills, leadership development and positive decision making. Throughout my life, I have been a dedicated advocate for our community, and I am asking for your support to continue this work, for our District and for our City. Please join me in shaping our future—I respectfully ask for your vote for Rebeca Armendariz for Gilroy City Council, District 6.
