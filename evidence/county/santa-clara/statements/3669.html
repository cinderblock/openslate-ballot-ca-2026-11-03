Occupation: Homemaker

Education and Qualifications:

As the mother of three children attending Morgan Hill Unified Schools—including students at San Martin/Gwinn and Sobrato High School—I have experienced firsthand the opportunities and challenges facing our district. I'm running for the Board of Education because I believe every student deserves an excellent education, every educator deserves support, and every family deserves confidence that decisions are made openly and thoughtfully.

Through years of volunteering in our schools and serving with nonprofit organizations throughout our community, I have worked alongside teachers, staff, and families. These experiences have given me an appreciation for the dedication of our educators and reinforced my belief that strong schools are built through partnership and shared responsibility.

If elected, I will lead with transparency, collaboration, and integrity. I will be accessible, communicate openly, and ensure that parents, teachers, staff, students, and community members know their voices are heard and respected.

I believe strong schools are built through responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars, academic excellence, meaningful partnerships with families, and supporting the outstanding educators and staff.

It would be an honor to earn your trust and your vote as Trustee for Area 2. Together, we can build a stronger future for every student in Morgan Hill Unified.
