Education and Qualifications:

As a decade-long district parent, former corporate executive, and community leader, I am running for the Campbell Union School District Board of Trustees (Area 5) to bring practical oversight, fiscal responsibility, and inclusive leadership.

My priorities are ensuring strong fundamentals in reading, math, and science, smaller class sizes, and a well-rounded education with arts, music, and physical education—equipping elementary students with practical skills to succeed in high school.

To achieve this, I bring a Post Graduate Diploma in Management and expertise in budget oversight to ensure public funds directly support classrooms. In elected roles on the School Site Council and PTA Executive Board, I scaled hands-on learning labs, spearheaded multicultural events, and earned recognition as Forest Hill's Volunteer of the Year. Both of my children attended CUSD from preschool onward.

I will bring a strategic business mindset and a mother's heart to foster open, transparent communication between the board, educators, and families.

Proudly endorsed by local teachers, neighboring school board trustees, and over 300 parents and community members, I respectfully ask for your vote.

Backed by parents. Guided by educators. Focused on students.
