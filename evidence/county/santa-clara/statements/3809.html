Occupation: Sunnyvale Councilmember, District 1

Education and Qualifications:

It is my honor to represent Sunnyvale District 1. I'm proud to have worked with my colleagues, staff and the community to achieve a high quality of life for Sunnyvale residents. For over 15 years, I have worked to strengthen our schools, promote a healthy environment, and bring people together for a better Sunnyvale. My public service began when budget cuts threatened 107 local teachers. Working with parents, educators, and community leaders, I helped raise $2.5 million in eight weeks to prevent cuts to our schools. That experience showed me what is possible when our community comes together, and I have brought that same approach to the city council.

As your Councilmember, I have listened and delivered results: supported public safety; improved neighborhood parking; championed safer walking and biking routes for students; proposed new community green spaces on underused land; and represented Sunnyvale at VTA to improve transit and reduce congestion. I will continue working for safe neighborhoods, reliable city services, and a healthier environment.

I am endorsed by Mayor Larry Klein, 5 fellow Sunnyvale Councilmembers, Congressman Ro Khanna, Santa Clara County Board of Supervisors President Otto Lee, and California State Board of Equalization Chair Sally Lieber. I would be honored to earn your vote. Visit LindaForSunnyvale.com
