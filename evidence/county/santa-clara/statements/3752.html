Occupation: Town of Los Altos Hills Planning Commissioner
Age: 64

Education and Qualifications:

Six years on the Planning Commission has given me direct experience with housing law, land use, open space, pathways, tree and view protection, multifamily development, and the decisions that shape and protect the Town's semi-rural character. California's housing laws are creating mandates for increased density in town, and we can meet our housing obligations with adequate additional development that doesn't surrender what makes Los Altos Hills special, if we respond strategically.

Beyond housing, fiscally responsible, effective Town government must remain the Town's primary focus, including providing core services: public safety, emergency preparedness, infrastructure, and resident services. As a business owner and engineer, I have spent my career evaluating complex tradeoffs, building consensus, planning, budgeting, and being accountable─skills that translate directly to effective Town leadership.

My wife and I have lived in Los Altos Hills for 28 years, raised our children here, and now look forward to sharing with our grandchildren the same cherished open spaces, pathways, equestrian traditions, and semi-rural character that we moved here to find.
