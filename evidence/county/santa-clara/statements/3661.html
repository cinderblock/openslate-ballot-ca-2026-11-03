Occupation: Retired Registered Nurse
Age: 70

Education and Qualifications:

For 46 years, Gilroy has been my home─the community where I raised my two daughters, built a 26-year career as a registered nurse, and watched my children and grandchildren put down roots.

My nursing career included caring for newborns in intensive care, emergency room patients, expectant mothers, and people managing diabetes. For my final 13 years, I served patients at Kaiser Permanente in Gilroy. Nursing taught me to listen, advocate, solve problems, and care for people in challenging moments.

Service has always mattered to me. I have served through Girl Scouts, Lions Club, our schools, and as a docent at Gilroy Gardens. Today, I advocate for two grandsons with special needs. This has shown me how important it is for every child to receive the support needed to succeed.

As a trustee, I will advocate for student resources, support our teachers, and ensure students with special needs receive the services and opportunities they deserve. I will listen to parents, educators, and our community while making thoughtful decisions centered on students.

I love Gilroy─our families, our schools, and our sense of community. I respectfully ask for your vote and the opportunity to serve students, families, and community as your school board trustee.
