Education and Qualifications:

My name is Erik Poicon, I am running for Mountain View City Council to build a city where everyone can belong and thrive. I have served on the county Human Rights Commission and Mountain View's Human Relations Committee, and I work as a community outreach worker for our county libraries.

I understand how our city's decisions affect residents, how trust is earned, and how listening becomes action. I have experienced homelessness, relied on rental and food assistance, and helped neighbors access the same resources that once supported my family. On the Council, I will Champion affordability at every stage of life, including attainable childcare, strong youth programs, renter protections, senior housing, and diverse homes near transit. I will advance safer streets through Vision Zero, practical transportation choices, and improved accessibility. I will support workers, seniors, and small businesses in every neighborhood.

I will also expand neighborhood briefings, multilingual outreach, and accessible City Hall services so residents can shape decisions before they are made.

Mountain View needs leadership grounded in experience, service, and accountability. It needs decisions that let people stay, work, and build lives here. That is the work I will do for you on Council.
