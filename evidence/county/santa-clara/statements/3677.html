Occupation: Public School Teacher
Age: 35

Education and Qualifications:

I'm running to be your school board member for San Jose Unified School District because I know what our public schools need in order for every student to succeed. As a public school teacher for 12 years, I've gained experience and learned how to provide what schools need to be for all students to be successful!

As a Special Education Teacher for 12 years, I've advocated for students and educators to make our schools the best places of learning they can be. I've seen what happens when students get consistency, dedicated services for every need, and robust programming: they grow into their full potential. That's the future I'm running for: a district where every dollar and every decision moves us closer to welcoming schools where all our kids can learn and feel seen.

Families in Trustee Area 2 believe their children can thrive. What they want are neighborhood schools they can count on to serve their children's needs. I will be a board member who works alongside educators to build those schools.

My lived experience as a Black and Mexican woman who grew up with very little and made the most of every opportunity taught me that all students deserve a real shot at success. I will keep Black, brown, disabled, and low-income students at the heart of the district's decisions.

As an active union member, I've served as a Representative in multiple roles, and I now serve as my teacher union's Equity Chair. In that work, I collaborate with teachers and administrators to identify problems, research solutions, and implement change with an equitable approach to education. As your board member, I'll rebuild trust through shared responsibility with educators and families, listening, communicating openly, and responding to what students actually need based on my many years of experience as an educator. I graciously ask that you vote Tomara Hall for San Jose Unified School District, Trustee Area 2.
