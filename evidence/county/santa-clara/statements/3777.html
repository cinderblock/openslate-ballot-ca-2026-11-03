Occupation: Security Specialist

Education and Qualifications:

I was born and raised in Mountain View and proudly attended Slater Elementary School, Graham Middle School, and Mountain View High School. This city has always been my home, and I am grateful for the opportunities and sense of community it gave my family.

Growing up in a single-parent household, I watched my mother work tirelessly to make ends meet. Like many families, we faced challenges, but Mountain View gave us hope, opportunity, and a place where we could build a better future. When my mother passed away, it strengthened my commitment to give back to the community that gave so much to us. My career in security has taught me the importance of leadership, accountability, and making calm, thoughtful decisions during emergencies. Every day, I work to protect people, solve problems, and serve others with integrity.

If elected, I will focus on expanding affordable housing, improving public safety, supporting local businesses, strengthening community events that bring neighbors together, and promoting environmentally responsible policies that preserve what makes Mountain View special. I believe in listening first, leading with integrity, and making decisions that put our community first. It would be an honor to earn your trust and your vote.
