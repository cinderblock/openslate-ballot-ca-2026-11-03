Two years ago, you elected me to the Los Altos City Council, and I've since had the honor of serving as Vice Mayor. Now, as our city moves from at-large to district-based elections, I'm running to represent our newly created District 4 seat.

Since taking office, I've valued learning directly from residents at council meetings, office hours, gatherings like the farmers' market, and even chats in supermarket aisles. Those conversations shape my priorities. I pushed for measures like our new e-bike ordinance, because residents deserve streets safe for drivers, riders, and pedestrians of all ages. We've enhanced and renovated parks across the city. And we've advanced public safety, including our Drone as First Responder program, giving officers faster situational awareness while safeguarding resident privacy.

My technology career taught me the value of thoughtful innovation, and my earlier service as Chair of the Los Altos Historical Commission and President of the Los Altos History Museum deepened my respect for what makes Los Altos special. I bring both to build a safer, more connected community.

I respectfully ask for your vote to continue serving Los Altos.
