A great city creates joy, not frustration. I believe City Hall should use common sense, solve problems, spend taxpayer dollars wisely, and never make simple things harder than necessary.

I am a small business owner with a master's degree in economics. I have served our community as a Parks Commissioner, board member of the Police Foundation, PTA, Cubberley, Rotary, local nonprofit and Palo Alto Sister Cities. I bring people together and get things done.

Permitting delays can take months or years, driving businesses into debt before opening and keeping families waiting to improve their homes. My Green Lights plan will create predictable permit timelines, faster inspections, and easier approval for backyard ADUs of up to 1,200 square feet for our children, young families, or aging parents.

On the Council, I will improve quality of life by reducing event-related traffic, cutting red tape and unnecessary bureaucratic costs so we can add more community amenities faster and for less. Palo Alto is a wonderful place. I will protect what we love, fix what makes life harder, find common ground, and build a shared future where the next generation has the opportunities we had and can afford to stay here. www.yudy4paloalto.com
