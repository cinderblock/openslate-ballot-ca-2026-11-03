Occupation: Governing Board Member, Mountain View Whisman School District

Education and Qualifications:

Serving our students and community as a member of the Mountain View Whisman School Board for nearly eight years has been a true honor. I am running for re-election to continue the work we've started, with a focus on improving outcomes and opportunities for all students, supporting our teachers in the classroom, and strengthening community partnerships.

Since I was first elected in 2018, we have: promoted student learning by implementing a new literacy curriculum and creating an award winning literacy intervention program; supported our teachers by consistently raising teacher salaries and completing an innovative workforce housing project; strengthened community partnerships by making our school fields open as parks outside school hours through a new agreement with the City of Mountain View; and, strengthened district leadership and stewardship by hiring a new Superintendent, implementing fiscal reforms to ensure every dollar counts for students, and conducting a systems analysis to sharpen our focus on student learning.

I envision a district where every student thrives. I look forward to ensuring top-tier classroom instruction with a new strategic plan, supporting teachers through deeper collaboration on standards, assessment, and instruction, and building stronger partnerships with our community.
