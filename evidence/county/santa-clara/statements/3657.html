I wish to serve as a trustee of the West Valley-Mission Community College District because I want the District to put more emphasis on academics and the needs of students. My parents were dedicated community college teachers, and it would be my honor to continue that legacy by making sure teachers and staff have what they need to be effective.

The central mission of the community college is to provide quality classes and training to community members, so that students can realize their academic and professional goals. It is supported by taxpayer funds, which are entrusted to the District with the expectation of fulfilling that mission. West Valley-Mission CCD is well funded, yet the District holds $125 million in reserves, even as it drops classes and removes useful infrastructure. As a trustee, I will focus on good stewardship of your tax dollars by increasing transparency, improving student services, reducing administrative bloat, and investing in vital educational programs. I will champion initiatives for improving mental health services and for accommodations for neurodiverse and disabled students. The community college continues to be a driver for upward mobility: It is essential that courses and services are available to every student.
