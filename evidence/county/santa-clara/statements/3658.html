Occupation: College Professor
Age: 66

Education and Qualifications:

My name is Doug Robb and I am running for the Board of Trustees of the West Valley-Mission Community College District (WVMCCD), Area 7. I am a product of the California Community College System, first as a student at College of San Mateo and then as a professor at San Jose City College for 32 years. I bring a unique perspective to WVMCCD, from my experience as a professor, the Head Baseball Coach, and a member of the Academic Senate and other college committees.

With its abundant resources and outstanding faculty and staff, WVMCCD is well position to serve all its students and the community. Students come from diverse experiences but share a common desire for a quality education that will set them up for future success. Students deserve convenient access to an enriching college experience.

If elected, I will work hard to ensure that WVMCCD students receive a high-quality education and the opportunity to study a major that is right for them. The district should offer classes that students want and need, while using a practical and flexible structure to address enrollment issues quickly. I am committed to eliminating barriers so that students can complete their goals on time.
