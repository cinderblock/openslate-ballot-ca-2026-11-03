My wife, Jenny, and I are public school graduates, and today we're raising a 7th grader in Palo Alto's public schools. I am running because every student deserves an exceptional education and every family deserves a district that is accountable and transparent to families at every level. I come from a family of educators, judges, and journalists who taught me the value of learning, integrity, asking difficult questions, and taking action. The elected Board must rebuild trust through action and accountability.

When my family and others saw that the current system could be substantially improved, I did not stand by. I spoke up, helped organize families, and got more involved. I challenged opaque superintendent separation agreements totaling more than $1 million and successfully advocated to preserve teaching assistants and drama education, support sexual harassment survivors, and provide advanced courses. I have seen how much our students accomplish and how much more they could if parents, students, teachers, and administrators collaborate more effectively. We need to improve the culture in Palo Alto schools, and I'm ready to do the hard work to make that happen.

My career has centered on leading multinational and multicultural teams through complex change. As an operations-focused executive, I have scaled organizations, launched startups, and worked with businesses, nonprofits, and government agencies. I have learned to listen to different perspectives, manage resources transparently and responsibly, and turn ambitious goals into measured results.

On the Board, I will focus on four priorities.

Transparent and collaborative governance: rigorous, independent Board oversight, direct channels for public input, and accountability for every decision and dollar.

Comprehensive academic excellence: supporting and challenging every student according to their abilities, interests, and needs while offering broad options across the arts, athletics, career education, and enrichment.

Mental health and safety: thoughtful coordination among schools, families, local and state agencies, and our broader community.

Practical technology: preparing students to use powerful new tools effectively and responsibly, requiring evidence before adoption, and evaluating results after implementation.

I offer independent judgment, results-oriented executive discipline, a parent's perspective, and a willingness to speak up and solve difficult problems. Together, we can build schools that deliver for every student and earn our community's confidence. Your vote is a sacred trust, and I would be honored to uphold it.
