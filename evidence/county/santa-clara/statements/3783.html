Occupation: Palo Alto City Councilmember

Education and Qualifications:

Palo Alto faces complex challenges. I offer proven leadership, sound judgment, and a deep track record of bringing people together to craft sensible solutions for our community.

During my first term, I served as Mayor, Vice Mayor, and Chair of the Finance and Housing Committees. I also led work on public safety, retail, and homelessness. Before Council, I served four terms on the Parks and Recreation and Planning and Transportation commissions. My colleagues trusted me to lead as Chair five times as we addressed issues central to our community's vitality and quality of life.

Beyond City Hall, my extensive service on local nonprofit boards has earned me a reputation for working effectively and respectfully to deliver results. As a three-time software start-up CEO and executive recruiter, I also bring experience managing partners, budgets, risk, and uncertainty.

In a second term, I will continue to increase affordable housing, further strengthen public safety, expand parklands, and champion fiscal responsibility.

My service is driven by love for our community. In a second term I will sustain and enhance our many unique amenities, drive new initiatives, and responsibly manage growth. I ask for your vote to continue serving.
