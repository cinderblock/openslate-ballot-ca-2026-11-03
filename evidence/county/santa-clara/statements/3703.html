Occupation: Trustee

Education and Qualifications:

For the past 12 years, it has been my honor to serve the Cambrian community on the Board of Trustees. As a parent, neighbor, and longtime advocate for our schools, I care deeply about ensuring every student has the support, opportunities, and resources they need to thrive.

My service extends beyond our local board because Cambrian's voice must be heard wherever public education decisions are made. Through the Santa Clara County School Boards Association, its Legislative Action Committee, the California School Boards Association, and the County Committee on School District Organization, I have brought Cambrian's priorities directly to county, state, and elected government leaders.

Education funding can be unpredictable, especially in a district that is funded on enrollment. Since we have seen enrollment declines which negatively impact our funding. I have worked to create stable revenue sources through Extended Day Programs, better use of district facilities, and land development agreements that continue to support staff and students.

On the Board, I have served as Board President, on bond oversight, and contributed to policy work. My focus remains strong schools, thoughtful governance, responsible budgets, and support for students' academic, social, and emotional needs.
