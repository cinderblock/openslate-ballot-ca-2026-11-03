Occupation: Governing Board Member

Education and Qualifications:

I moved to San José to teach kindergarten. Nearly fifteen years later, I am running for re-election to the San José Unified School District Board of Education because I still believe what I believed in that classroom: every child deserves a world-class education, including my daughters, who attend our neighborhood public school. We have made real progress these last four years, but there is more work to do for our students, families, and community.

My teaching experience took me further than I expected. I went from my own students to classrooms nationwide, coaching teachers in mathematics and literacy. Most recently, I served as executive director of an education policy nonprofit that trains school leaders across California.

As your trustee these last four years, I put my Certificate in Education Finance, my experience as a teacher, and a proven track record to work. During my term, we:

Partnered with Stanford and SJSU to secure free student resources and staff training.

Won a raise for teachers and staff, and launched a workforce housing initiative so they can afford to live in the community they serve.

Secured $800+ million in funding for school facility upgrades.

Earned the district's highest credit rating, saving taxpayers millions of dollars.

With another four years, I will keep building partnerships that invest in student safety, academics, STEM, and extracurriculars. I will make sure students are ready to compete in an economy reshaped by AI, whether they choose college, a trade, or starting their own business. I will continue protecting the mental health resources our students rely on and the policies that make every student welcome on campus—even as those resources and policies are threatened by our current political climate. Now more than ever, this board needs a proven policymaker and an experienced educator who keeps the focus on students, not politics.

This work is personal. My daughters attend our neighborhood public school, so every vote I take on this board is a vote about their education and your child's. I want for every student in Area 2 what I want for them—educators who are supported, campuses that are safe, and a diploma that opens doors.
