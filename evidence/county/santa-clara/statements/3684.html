Public education is in a period of rapid change: recovery from the pandemic, mental health needs, evolving college and career pathways, and new demands on teachers and staff. In this new landscape, we need a fresh lens on the school board—one that centers equity, representation, and student voice in every decision.

I am an ESUHSD alumni and a longtime advocate for education and our community. For over two decades, I've supported students and adults in accessing college and career pathways. My work has always been about listening to people and working together to find solutions. I have experience in leadership, working with diverse groups, and shaping policies that reflect the values of the East Side and support student success.
