As an immigrant child, I watched my parents work multiple jobs and long shifts to build our family's American Dream in Milpitas. They taught me that our community is built on hard work and looking out for one another. As a mother, caregiver, frontline healthcare worker, union member, and labor leader, I stand with working families. Today, as my children walk the same high school halls I once did, I feel a responsibility to protect and uplift the home that gave my family everything. Like many residents, I want safe neighborhoods with adequate lighting; well-maintained parks and fields where sports can be played safely; reliable public services supported by the staff who deliver them; and affordable, stable housing for seniors, people with special needs, city employees, and public safety workers who commute hours to serve Milpitas. As a healthcare worker, I lead with compassion and conviction and see families' needs firsthand. City decisions affect families, seniors, small businesses, and workers, and those perspectives should guide policy choices. Our city government should listen to the people it serves. I will collaborate transparently, seek residents' input, and lead with solutions. Join me in keeping Milpitas safe, welcoming, and affordable for everyone.
