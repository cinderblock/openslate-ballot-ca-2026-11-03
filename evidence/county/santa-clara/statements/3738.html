Education and Qualifications:

I'm a third-generation Cupertino resident. My family arrived here in the Valley of Heart's Delight in 1952. I grew up here, attended our schools, graduated from UCLA, and returned to Silicon Valley. That thriving Cupertino gave residents so much. I'm running for reelection to ensure future generations get that same chance.

As your councilmember, I've used my expertise as an attorney to get real results for residents. I expanded housing for teachers, seniors, and young families─while keeping our small-town feel─by winning state approval for Cupertino's housing plan. I helped negotiate a state tax deal that saved our city millions securing a balanced budget. I fought for better infrastructure, greater public safety, and a government that follows the law and listens to you.

We need strong partnerships to accomplish big things for Cupertino─and I've built them as Vice President of the Cities Association of Santa Clara County and as our representative to Silicon Valley Clean Energy, and the Santa Clara County Library District.

Our residents deserve a future where every generation, at every income level, can afford to stay and thrive in Cupertino. That's why Assemblymembers Patrick Ahrens and Alex Lee, Senator Josh Becker, Supervisor Margaret Abe-Koga, Councilmember Sheila Mohan, and nine former Cupertino mayors support me.
