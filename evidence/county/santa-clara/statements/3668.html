Occupation: Governing Board President

Education and Qualifications:

Since you first elected me to the Morgan Hill Unified School District Board in 2018 we have increased high school graduation rates from 84% to 96%, increased teacher pay by 30%, tripled Career and Technical Education participation, and integrated the Science of Reading (including phonics) in elementary school. Our graduates newly enjoy guaranteed acceptance to San Jose State University along with robust College Dual Enrollment and Advanced Placement programs.

We have also expanded music and arts education, added fourteen wellness counselors to a system which previously had none, and restored physical education to all grades. Meanwhile, our student nutrition department has become a national model for providing healthy meals.

Increasing the funding to do these things has required my relentless advocacy in Sacramento and Washington, DC. That work has, for example, resulted in $3M per year of additional Special Education funding for Morgan Hill Unified. Through both advocacy and smart budget choices we have sustained strong financial reserves without school closures or mass layoffs.

Previously I brought a unique education/workforce development focus to the Morgan Hill Chamber of Commerce, was a founding board member of three local foundations, and owned Thinker Toys Morgan Hill.

With your vote we can continue moving our schools forward together.
