Occupation: Student Services Professional

Education and Qualifications:

I am a parent and former president for the Parent Teacher Organization of San Jose Unified's largest elementary school. I have worked within our community to increase access to after school programming, support the safety of students walking and biking to schools, and create systems to better support our classroom teachers.

Fostering a culture of collaboration and communication begins with the district, from school board members to administrators and teachers. Crucially, parents need to be involved in their children's education. Historically the district has failed to meaningfully engage, as they did in the recent closure of five schools, and the community believes that their input and engagement is not valued. We can do better for all students in our district. The district should provide the tools and leadership to help parents participate in their students' success.

Our schools should be appropriately staffed. Every kindergarten classroom needs support staff to manage behaviors so our teachers can teach. Inconsistent staffing of substitute teaching positions leaves gaps in our students' education and school safety plans.

We need to carefully evaluate the use of technology in our classrooms. For our youngest students, we should consider if any technology has a place in the classroom. For our older students, we should ensure that the software we use can provide results consistent with other measures of achievement. Our district needs a policy on the use of AI that ensures that students, teachers, and staff are meaningfully engaging with content.

Our curriculum needs to provide appropriate instruction for all students. Expecting teachers to provide differentiation in the classroom with the resources they currently have is unreasonable. Each student should be provided an opportunity to grow their abilities, and curriculum and classroom resources should reflect that goal.

Our district needs leadership. I have served as PTO president, substitute teacher across several SJUSD schools, Girl Scout troop leader to over forty girls, and executive treasurer for a nonprofit serving children and their families in Santa Clara County. Professionally, I work in student services for universities helping implement educational programs. I have the skills and commitment to move our district forward.
