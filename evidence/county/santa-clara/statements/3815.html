Education and Qualifications:

I am running for the Valley Water Board to bring my executive experience to keep water rates affordable while rebuilding our aging water pipelines and dams.

The Coyote Creek Flood Protection Project and the Anderson Dam Seismic Retrofit Project must be completed on time and within budget. These critical projects have taken far too long to get going, and I will provide active oversight to ensure they move forward efficiently while protecting taxpayers and keeping water rates as affordable as possible.

I will continue to support and expand efforts to remove trash and debris from our creeks and waterways. Valley Water and the City of San Jose should make this work a higher priority. We must also strengthen outreach and engagement with the diverse communities we serve so that every resident has a voice in decisions that affect our water resources. My master's degree in public administration, along with four years of service on a local school board, has given me valuable experience in bringing people together, building consensus, and setting priorities that deliver results.

Our campaign for affordable, reliable water is endorsed by respected local leaders, including County Supervisor Betty Duong, School Board Member George Sanchez, Councilmember Peter Ortiz, Valley Water Directors Shiloh Ballard and Tony Estremera, Assemblymember Ash Kalra, and former Valley Water Next Gen Intern Asjah Naquin.
