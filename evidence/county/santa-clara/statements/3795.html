Occupation: Councilmember/Retired Teacher
Age: 65

Education and Qualifications:

Affordability is Santa Clara's greatest concern. I've been both tenant and homeowner. My priorities: Housing options, lower utility bills, and city fees while maintaining high quality service.

My governance style. Engagement, respect, and decency. When we miss the mark, we acknowledge it and apologize. Courtesy should not have to compete with disdain.

As a teacher at Wilcox High for over 18 years, I originated the Computer Science Program, co-founded and coach the internationally recognized Robotics Team. I also play organ at my Spanish-speaking church.

Code.org founder Hadi Partovi nominated me to President Obama's 2015 White House Champions of Change.

I've lived in Santa Clara for 40 years and served on the Planning and Historical Commissions over 13 years. Currently eight years on City Council and serve as Vice Chair of the Bay Area Water Supply and Conservation Agency.
