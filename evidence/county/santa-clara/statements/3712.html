Occupation: Retired ESD Secretary

Education and Qualifications:

My name is Corinna Diener. I was born in San Jose and have been an Evergreen resident since 1992. My three children and two grandchildren attended the Evergreen School District. For nearly 20 years, I served the district in Child Nutrition Services, as a Health Aide, and as a School Secretary, working closely with students, families, and staff to support a safe and positive learning environment. I also served as PTA President and volunteered with local swim and youth soccer programs, giving me firsthand experience in the importance of strong schools, engaged families, and community partnerships.

The Evergreen School District faces important challenges in the years ahead. We need practical leadership that is fiscally responsible while maintaining the high standards our students, staff, and community deserve. By working together with a transparent, collaborative, and unified Board of Trustees, we can make thoughtful decisions that strengthen our schools and build trust throughout our community.

I would be honored to earn your vote so we can continue building a stronger, more supportive educational community for every student. Thank you for your support.
