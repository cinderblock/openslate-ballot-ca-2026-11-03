Occupation: Director, Santa Clara Valley Water District 1

Education and Qualifications:

Protecting your safety and ensuring access to clean, safe drinking water are my top priorities. I've fought to keep water affordable because families cannot continue to absorb rising rates.

That's why I voted to include senior and low-income exemption from the Measure S parcel tax. Our Board recently awarded the contract to rebuild the critical Anderson Dam, where we store water that recharges our ground water basin. The new Anderson Outlet Tunnel will improve Valley Water's ability to manage atmospheric rivers and reduce downstream flooding. I led the efforts to move multiple phases of the long-stalled Llagas Creek Flood Protection Project Foward. By adjusting local funding to leverage an $80 million federal grant, we put the project on track for completion this year. Improving local trails and roads during construction.

There is still more work to do. I would be honored to earn your vote and continue serving you.

I have been endorsed by Morgan Hill Mayor Mark Turner, Santa Clara County Supervisor Sylvia Arenas, retired State Senator Bill Monning, and Tim Chiala, President Santa Clara County Farm Bureau and many others.
