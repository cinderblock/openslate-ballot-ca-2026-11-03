Occupation: Former City Manager
Age: 53

Education and Qualifications:

For more than 25 years, I've served local communities by solving problems, building consensus, and delivering results. I'm running to bring decades of executive experience managing public utilities, protecting taxpayer dollars, and solving complex infrastructure challenges.

I've served as Gilroy's City Administrator, Finance Director for the cities of Gilroy and Monterey, Authority Manager for the South County Regional Wastewater Authority, and managed water and wastewater utilities serving south Santa Clara County communities. I've overseen major public infrastructure projects, managed budgets exceeding $200 million, and delivered reliable public services while safeguarding taxpayer dollars.

I earned a Master of Public Administration from San José State University, a bachelor's degree in Recreation Administration, and obtained the Certified Public Finance Officer designation.

Experience matters. Our community deserves a Valley Water Board that is transparent, accountable, and focused on serving the public. Good leadership starts by listening, asking tough questions, and making informed decisions based on facts.

As your Director, I'll work to protect our long-term water supply, reduce flood risk, responsibly maintain our reservoirs and trails, safeguard taxpayer dollars, and ensure the public clearly understands how decisions are made and how public funds are spent.
