I am honored to have the opportunity to become your representative for District 6.

Utilizing with my business experience, I can partner with current leaders to make common sense decisions that will have generational impacts. Furthermore, I believe I can help represent our younger citizens and provide a new outlook in the Council Chambers.

As part of my studies, I was blessed with the opportunity to work with our youth through National Night Out and Youth Taskforce events and hope to be the first of many young people becoming involved in our community's leadership roles.

I am humbly asking for your vote in order to usher in a new generation of leaders and moving our city forward. I hope to represent our district as a whole with common sense stances while building trust and protecting future generations.
