Occupation: Los Altos City Council Member

Education and Qualifications:

I'm Pete Dailey, candidate for the Santa Clara Valley Water District. I moved to Silicon Valley in 1993, and have lived or worked in Los Gatos, Mountain View, Palo Alto, and Los Altos, where I recently served as Mayor, and continue to serve on the City Council. I have an undergraduate degree from Berkeley, and master's degrees from Pepperdine and Stanford.

In addition to balancing the city's budget after years of deficits, lowering crime and improving public safety, I also delivered on promises to build housing and improve environmental sustainability. I built this success on teamwork. Los Altos even earned the State of California's Prohousing designation while I was Mayor.

I have served on the Valley Water Commission for four years and was elected Chair in 2025. I am committed to the agency's complex three-part mission to protect our vital water supply, prevent flooding, and steward the habitat in our watershed.

I'm running to deliver on this mission for the residents of the district, who deserve to be represented by a Director known for teamwork, collegiality, professionalism, and ethical public service.

For more information, go to www.pete4water.com. Vote Pete Dailey for a more effective board.
