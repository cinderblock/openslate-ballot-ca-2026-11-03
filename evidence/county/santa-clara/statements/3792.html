Occupation: Councilmember, City of San José District 7

Education and Qualifications:

District 7 is safer, cleaner, and receiving more responsive services than when I took office four years ago. As your Councilmember and a 22-year San José Fire Captain, I have delivered practical results on public safety, homelessness, housing affordability, youth services, and reliable city services.

Together, we restored parks like the Japanese Friendship Garden, protected after-school programs, addressed problematic RVs, and fought for more police and fire funding. I demanded accountability for homelessness spending, protected taxpayer dollars, strengthened neighborhood services, supported responsible housing growth, defended renters, and worked to keep mobile homes affordable.

As an immigrant and father, I believe in uniting our district and getting results, so every resident, regardless of background or neighborhood, is heard, respected, and represented.

I am honored to be supported by business, labor, police officers, firefighters, mobile home residents, Mayor Matt Mahan, Congressman Sam Liccardo, District Attorney Jeff Rosen, and leaders across our county. We have made real progress, and I respectfully ask for your vote so we can continue making neighborhoods safer, improving city services, addressing homelessness, and lowering costs for working families.
