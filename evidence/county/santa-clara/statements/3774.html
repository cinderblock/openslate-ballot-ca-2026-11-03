I am your "Supporting Our Neighborhoods" candidate, a 17-year community leader and a 31-year Mountain View resident. I fought, and will continue to fight, for strong neighborhoods, thoughtful growth, more parks and open space, a thriving downtown and economy, and affordable housing. I offer practical environmental leadership. When new development has been proposed, I have worked to retain small businesses and parks. Above all, I want residents to determine the future of the neighborhoods in which they live. I oppose policies that will worsen traffic and parking issues.

On the Environmental Planning Commission, I recommended approval of over 4200 new residential units, 640 of which are affordable. On my neighborhood association board, I hosted over 20 neighborhood meetings, educating my neighbors on proposed developments. I have served in leadership positions in our local Sierra Club chapter, Mountain View Historical Association, and Kiwanis. I currently serve as Chair of the city's Rental Housing Committee and Chair of my neighborhood association. As a retired software technical lead and engineer who minored in economics, I understand the Silicon Valley economy. I am committed to supporting our quality of life and building Mountain View's bright future. I respectfully request your vote.
