Occupation: Retired Educator

Education and Qualifications:

I am proud to be a candidate for the Cambrian School District Board of Trustees. With more than 30 years of experience in education, I have served as a teacher, site administrator, district administrator, and county office administrator. I also opened a brand-new school as its founding principal, gaining strong experience in budgeting, personnel, curriculum, and operations. These roles have given me a deep understanding of how schools function and how effective leadership supports student success.

In addition to my professional background, I bring the perspective of a parent. My two children attended Cambrian schools, and my family has experienced firsthand the value of a strong public education and a supportive school community.

I am committed to ensuring that every student has access to high-quality learning opportunities, safe and inclusive schools, and the support needed to thrive. I believe in responsible decision-making, open communication, and collaboration with families, educators, and community members.

If elected, I will use my experience and dedication to help sustain Cambrian's tradition of excellence while planning thoughtfully for the future.
