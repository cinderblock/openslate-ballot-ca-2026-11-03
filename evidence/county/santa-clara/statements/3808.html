Occupation: Commissioner, Saratoga Library and Public Arts Commission

Education and Qualifications:

I'm a longtime Saratoga resident, dedicated to serving our community. I currently serve on Saratoga's Library & Public Arts Commission.

I recently retired after 38 years with Santa Clara Valley Transportation Authority, where I rose to manage 50+ employees across five locations—all while raising two sons as a single mother. I've learned that broken systems don't fix themselves until good people step up.

My roles have included Executive Board Member, Treasurer, and Political Director. I know how to lead, manage budgets, negotiate, and hold organizations accountable.

I'm running to protect Saratoga's quality of life, so it remains a safe, welcoming place to live, work, raise a family, and retire. I'll prioritize public safety, livable neighborhoods, our kids' education, and our parks and infrastructure. I'll make sure seniors have the resources they need to remain part of the community they helped build.

My endorsements include Assemblymember Patrick Ahrens, Santa Clara County District Attorney Jeff Rosen, Santa Clara County Supervisor Margaret Abe-Koga, and many more.

I'll bring transparency and accountability to City Hall, listen to residents before decisions are made, and ensure taxpayer dollars are spent responsibly. I hope to earn your vote.
