Let me finish what we started together.

For over thirty years, serving the students, families, and educators of Trustee Area 1 has been the honor of my life. I am asking for the privilege of another term.

In 1996, I played an integral role in the development of Don Callejon School, the first step in our vision that the district's future growth lay north of Bayshore. That vision became reality. I testified in Sacramento as an advocate for the acquisition of the Agnew campus property. Abrams Elementary, Huerta Middle School, and McDonald High School stand as the fulfillment of that promise, two decades in the making.

I have fought to protect our neighborhoods, keep our schools strong, and ensure Alviso and our First Street communities always have a voice at the table.

Our work is not finished.

Endorsed by: U.S. Congressman Ro Khanna (CA-17); State Senator Aisha Wahab (SD-10); Assemblymember Patrick Ahrens (AD-26); Assemblymember Alex Lee (AD-24); County Supervisor Otto Lee; City Councilmember David Cohen; Water District Director Richard Santos.
