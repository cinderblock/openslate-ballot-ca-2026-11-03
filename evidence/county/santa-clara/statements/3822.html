Education and Qualifications:

It has been my honor to serve the LASD community since I was first elected in 2018. As a former LASD parent, I've seen the impact of the strong education provided by LASD, and as your school board member, I've worked hard to ensure that every student receives an educational experience that prepares them for success in any path they choose. My guiding ethos has always been to keep students and families at the center of every decision, and I believe strongly in the whole child model: looking at improving education and wellness outcomes from a holistic perspective. I have the experience, insight, and proven commitment to improving outcomes for all youth, having worked on education issues at the district, county, and state levels. As LASD Board President in 2021, 2025, and 2026, I'm proud to have supported the partnership between district administration, teachers/staff, and families. In addition to serving on various nonprofit boards and the SCC Juvenile Justice Commission, I was president of the SCC School Boards Association and now represent the needs of our students and families at the state level through the CA School Boards Association.
