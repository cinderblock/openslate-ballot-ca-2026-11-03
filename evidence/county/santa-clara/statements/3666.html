Occupation: Retired Educator

Education and Qualifications:

I was a Special Education teacher for 19 years and Assistant Principal for 4 years at Britton Middle School. While a teacher, I was head girls' basketball coach for 15 years, department chair for 16 years, and served as union rep for 12 years. As a parent, I've experienced the schools from Kindergarten through high school graduation and faced the issue of having one child whose needs were not met by the schools. One goal as a board member is to set policy for schools to adequately meet all students' needs, academically and socially/emotionally. Our schools must allow all students to be on a pathway to achieving their fullest potential as adults by being prepared for college, military service, a job, or career training.

As an incumbent, I have opposed the perpetuation of deficit spending, which imperils opportunities for our students' future education. I am working for fiscal transparency and meaningful parent/community/staff and student engagement. As I am a strong supporter of teachers, Morgan Hill Federation of Teachers (teacher union) has again endorsed me for trustee. I stand for: Student success, Fiscal responsibility, Transparency, Staff and Community Engagement.
