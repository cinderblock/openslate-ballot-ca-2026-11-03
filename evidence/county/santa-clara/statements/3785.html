I'm running for Palo Alto City Council because our city must work for all of us, not just the wealthy or well connected. As a former Community Health Worker, woodland firefighter by trade, community advocate, and Palo Alto resident, I have stood with those too often pushed aside, including working families, immigrants, unhoused neighbors, and communities denied a real voice in the decisions that shape their lives.

I believe government should be a force for dignity, fairness, and collective care. A city should be judged by how it treats those with the least power. In Palo Alto, taking care of our neighbors means keeping our streets clean, safe, and welcoming while addressing the root causes of homelessness, displacement, and inequality.

I have spoken out for marginalized communities, supported efforts to cover the racist courthouse mural, and called on local leaders to confront hate through education, inclusion, and accountability. I will fight to protect our immigrant community, create more affordable housing, preserve Palo Alto's natural surroundings, and make sure every resident is treated with dignity and respect
