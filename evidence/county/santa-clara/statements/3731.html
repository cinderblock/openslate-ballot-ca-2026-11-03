I chair Campbell's Planning Commission and have served on two city commissions since 2020, working to preserve what makes Campbell special while preparing it for the future. My wife and I chose Campbell to raise our twins because neighbors know each other, kids thrive, and iconic parks, events, and small businesses bring people together.

I'm a Bay Area native with a degree in Political Science and Communications. As a Product Manager, I bring teams together to solve complex problems. On Campbell's commissions, I improved housing projects based on neighborhood feedback, led the permit audit that became Campbell's Conditional Use Permit Streamlining Ordinance, and helped expand the downtown mural program.

In both my career and commission service, I dig into complex plans, weigh tradeoffs, ask tough questions, and bring people together to fix what's not working. On Council, I will support well-planned housing while preserving neighborhood character. I will invest in public safety, streets, sidewalks, parks, and public spaces. I will help small businesses open faster and succeed, manage Campbell's budget responsibly, and develop a long-term plan for aging facilities.

I listen, show up prepared, and sweat the details. I would be honored to earn your vote and serve District 4. www.davis4campbell.com
