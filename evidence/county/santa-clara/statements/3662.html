Occupation: Regional Director

Education and Qualifications:

As a proud graduate of the Milpitas Unified School District, I am honored to run for the Milpitas School Board. I attended Spangler Elementary School, Thomas Russell Middle School, and graduated from Milpitas High School. My experiences helped shape the person and leader I am today. I'm committed to giving back to the community that invested in me by helping ensure every student has the opportunity to succeed.

I hold a Master's degree in Education and have spent more than 15 years serving adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities. Throughout my career, I have focused on creating opportunities, building strong community partnerships, and helping individuals reach their full potential.

I have also had the privilege of serving as Chair of the Silicon Valley Chamber of Commerce, where I collaborated with business and community leaders to strengthen our region. Through my service with the Milpitas Knights football and cheer programs, I have supported local youth by promoting teamwork, leadership, and community involvement.

I will bring thoughtful leadership, fiscal responsibility, transparency, and a collaborative approach to every decision. My commitment is to listen, put students first, support our educators, and ensure Milpitas schools continue to provide every student with the opportunity to learn, grow, and thrive. I would be honored to earn your trust and your vote.
