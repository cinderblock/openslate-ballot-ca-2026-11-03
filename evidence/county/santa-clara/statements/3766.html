Education and Qualifications:

I've lived in this town my entire life and am raising my children in the house I grew up in. For the last two decades, I've worked as a carpenter in the Bay Area. I've watched this town grow, struggle, and change. I've seen families pushed out, small businesses squeezed, and neighborhoods reshaped by decisions made far from the people they affect. I'm running for mayor because our town deserves leadership rooted in everyday experience, not corporate interests or private investors.

Too often, investors come in, buy up land, and reshape our community─while longtime residents have little voice in the process.

I'm stepping forward because I believe leadership should be accountable, transparent, and grounded in the lives of the people who actually live here. My priorities are straightforward: protect our town from reckless development, support local workers and small businesses, invest in infrastructure that serves residents, and make sure every decision puts people first.

I'm not a politician─I'm just a blue-collar worker who wants to see this town thrive. I want to help rebuild trust, opportunity, and a future where our town belongs to the people who call it home─not the corporations and private investors.
