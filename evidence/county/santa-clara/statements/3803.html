Santa Clara has been my home since 1984, and I'm proud to have raised my children here. At Wilcox High School, I introduced students to cybersecurity through CyberPatriot and judged robotics competitions.

Serving on the Planning Commission and Charter Review Committee gives me firsthand experience with decisions shaping our neighborhoods and finances. We must maintain what we own, protect reliable and affordable power, and make growth work for residents.

City staff identified over $600 million in infrastructure needs. Years of deferred maintenance brought us here. I'll push for disciplined asset planning so we maintain what we own and prevent today's maintenance needs from becoming tomorrow's costly emergencies.

Sacramento's one-size-fits-all mandates for housing are taking decisions away from the communities that understand local needs best. I'll work with neighboring cities to help restore local authority over how building codes are created around the needs of our communities.
