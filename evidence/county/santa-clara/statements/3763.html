Occupation: Engineer/Small Business Owner/Father
Age: 56

Education and Qualifications:

Serving on the Milpitas Planning Commission from 2019 to 2024, including as Chair and Vice-Chair, gave me firsthand experience balancing responsible growth, fiscal responsibility, and community needs. Today, I serve on the Valley Water Independent Monitoring Committee, Milpitas Veterans Commission, Milpitas CERT, and Milpitas Kiwanis, while chairing the MUSD Community Board Advisory Council and Parcel Tax Oversight Committee.

Raised in an immigrant family with six children, I learned hard work, discipline, and service. My commitment began as a Boy Scout and Boys State delegate, continued through active duty in the United States Army as an armor crewman, and led to a Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering from Cal Poly, San Luis Obispo.

A proud Milpitas resident for nearly 20 years, my family is deeply rooted here. My wife serves on the MUSD Board of Education. Two of our children attend Milpitas High, and one attends UC Santa Cruz. As your Councilmember, my priority is public safety, advocating for responsible affordable housing, and investing in infrastructure to improve our quality of life. Amid staffing challenges and budget uncertainty at City Hall, I will apply sound financial management and practical problem-solving while being transparent to protect taxpayer dollars.

I respectfully ask for your vote. Visit billchuanformilpitas.com.
