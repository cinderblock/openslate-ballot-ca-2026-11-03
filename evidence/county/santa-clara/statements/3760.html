Milpitas needs new leadership. As Mayor, we will work together to address the challenges facing our city. Milpitas has lost 10 Senior Executives in the past 5 years, resulting in significant costs. The city has violated numerous Municipal codes, and this must stop. We must strengthen our public safety units, our economic development, and our infrastructure. Inflation is a concern for our residents, and one way to address it is by accelerating existing development projects, which can help reduce the tax burden on our residents. We need to strengthen our traffic enforcement and create a parking enforcement division. To support the health and well-being of our community, we should expand recreation programs that serve residents of all ethnicities. State mandated housing requirements present challenges. As we meet these requirements, our priority must be protecting the character of our neighborhoods. It is time to build a stronger partnership with our School District to provide training in new technologies. Most importantly, the Mayor should support the City Manager and Staff while working to secure additional funding and relationships. The City Council should remain focused on its policymaking role and avoid involvement in the City's day-to-day operations. Please consider voting for me.
