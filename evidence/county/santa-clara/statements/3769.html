For more than 20 years, Morgan Hill has been my home. My wife and I are raising our two daughters here, and I am committed to protecting the safe, welcoming community we love.

Service Above Self has guided my life. As a Fire Captain/Paramedic, I have dedicated my career to protecting lives, leading emergency responders, and serving others with integrity. I also represent California firefighters at the state level and serve on the California Earthquake Authority Board, working with state leaders to strengthen disaster preparedness, resilience, and public policy.

As your City Councilmember, I will support strong police, fire, and emergency medical services, fiscal responsibility, transparent government, well-maintained roads and infrastructure, thriving local businesses, and parks and open spaces that enhance our quality of life.

Development must serve our community, not overwhelm it. I will protect Morgan Hill's character, ensure infrastructure and public safety keep pace with growth, and hold developers accountable.

I believe city government should be accessible, accountable, and focused on serving every resident. Please visit electstevenhayes.com to learn more. I would be honored to earn your trust and vote.
