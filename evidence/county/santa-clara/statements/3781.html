I was born and raised in Palo Alto. My wife and I met in preschool here. I love the Gamble Garden, farmers' market, and Rinconada Park. I believe Palo Alto is the best place in the world to live, work, and raise a family, and I'm running to keep it that way.

But make no mistake: the Palo Alto we cherish is under threat. Outside developers see our neighborhoods as profit opportunities. Sacramento politicians believe they can plan our housing better than we can. And our own city government has grown too large, too expensive, and too disconnected from the issues residents actually care about: safe streets, efficient transportation, sensible housing policy, reliable and affordable utility service.

I will fight for thoughtful housing policy that protects our single-family neighborhoods. I will champion undergrounding the rail corridor, not because it's easy or cheap, but because it's the right investment for the next century of Palo Altans. And I will demand a city government that listens to and answers to residents.

Palo Alto is my home. I'm asking for your vote to help me defend it.
