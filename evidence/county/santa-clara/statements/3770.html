Education and Qualifications:

I am a Morgan Hill Councilwoman, Mother of 3, have been a small business owner, and lifelong Morgan Hill resident. I attended local schools, graduated from Live Oak High School, and earned a New York University Master's in Urban Planning in Housing and Economic Development. Serving 10 years with the City─8 on the Morgan Hill City Council and 2 on the Planning Commission─is an honor and built me into a strong homegrown leader.

Your vote, your voice, and our future guide me. Together we achieved so much. I secured resources and investments for Morgan Hill, championed funding public safety officers and another Fire House, and supported resident-initiated referendums. I led Climate Action efforts, helped bring our 4th Caltrain, and advocated for agritourism, healthcare, community outreach, and inclusion. I am known for championing local businesses, nonprofits, community organizations, local leaders, and residents. I remain committed to Public Safety despite economic challenges, support affordable and sustainable housing and infrastructure, smart use of City funds, business development elevating Morgan Hill, youth sports, recreation, mental health access, and outdoor activities.
