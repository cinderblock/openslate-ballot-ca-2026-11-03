Hello, Milpitas! As your Vice Mayor, attorney, and fellow resident, I am Garry Barbadillo, and I humbly ask for your vote. My journey from immigrant to public servant is rooted in gratitude. Milpitas is where my wife and I chose to raise our children, and serving our community is my way of giving back.

Bringing legal expertise, practical governance experience, and over a decade of pro bono community service, I was honored to be named Milpitas Citizen of the Year in 2018.

During my Council tenure, I have consistently protected your budget—managing tax dollars responsibly without increasing costs on families. Public safety remains my top priority; I consistently fund police and emergency services to keep our streets safe while protecting senior programs and neighborhood quality of life. Regionally, I advocate for Milpitas on key water, green energy, and emergency communications boards and I have represented our city in Valley Water, Silicon Valley Clean Energy, and the Silicon Valley Regional Interoperability Authority.

I remain dedicated to transparent governance, responsive public services, and building a prosperous future for all residents. I respectfully ask for your continued trust and your vote.
