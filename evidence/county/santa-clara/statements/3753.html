Occupation: Physician

Education and Qualifications:

I have called Los Altos Hills home for 22 years, where my husband and I have raised our two children. Serving as Chair of the Los Altos Hills Parks, Recreation, and Community Events Committee has deepened my appreciation for the values that make our town a special place to live. I treasure the unique character of Los Altos Hills, its open space, scenic pathways, and strong volunteers. As a physician specializing in neurohospital medicine, I have built my career on listening carefully, evaluating information thoughtfully, and making decisions with integrity and compassion.

I am running for Town Council to help preserve the unique character of Los Altos Hills while planning responsibly for its future. I am committed to maintaining our rural environment, protecting our beautiful pathway network, and prioritizing safety. I also believe in fostering opportunities for residents to connect through activities that promote health, wellness, and community.

I will be a thoughtful, accessible, and dedicated voice for our residents. I believe effective leadership begins with listening, and I will work hard to ensure every perspective is heard. By doing my homework, asking thoughtful questions, and making informed decisions, I will always put the long-term interests of our community first. I would be honored to earn your support and the opportunity to serve Los Altos Hills.
