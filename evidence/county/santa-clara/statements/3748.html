I am former Mayor Lynette Lee Eng, and I humbly ask for your vote for Los Altos City Council, District 2.

After two terms on the City Council, eleven years as a Park Commissioner, and two terms on the Santa Clara County Civil Grand Jury, I've always believed in listening first and leading with integrity. Serving our community has been the honor of my life, and I have never taken your trust for granted.

I initiated the Grant Park Senior Program because our seniors deserve a community that cares for them. I was instrumental in the Los Altos Summer Concert Series to bring neighbors together and strengthen our community.

I will continue to support thoughtful growth that respects our community, provides adequate infrastructure, and preserves the character of our neighborhoods. I will work to ensure transparent government, responsible stewardship of community assets, and strong local decision-making.

I will continue to serve every member of our community, not special interests, with integrity, experience, and steady leadership.

Together, we can keep Los Altos financially strong, beautiful and a welcoming place that everyone is proud to call home.
