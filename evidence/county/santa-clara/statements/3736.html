Education and Qualifications:

As a school parent and community volunteer, I have worked with residents, seniors, and schools to improve quality of life and ensure local government listens to our community.

If elected to the Cupertino City Council, I will work to maximize quality of life, strengthen public safety, support excellent schools, invest wisely in infrastructure, protect our neighborhoods, and spend taxpayer dollars responsibly.
