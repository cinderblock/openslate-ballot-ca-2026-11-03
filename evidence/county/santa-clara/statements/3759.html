I am honored to serve as Mayor of Los Gatos. I'm running for re-election to the Los Gatos Town Council to continue serving the community I grew up in and love.

Since first elected in 2022, I have prioritized transparency and communication, keeping residents informed through a monthly newsletter, community events, social media, and coffee meetings.

I led the passage of a strong, balanced budget without raising taxes, voted to pass a certified Housing Element to curb overdevelopment, and restricted illegal e-motorcycles. I have been a strong voice for wildfire preparedness and safer streets, created programs to mentor our youth, advocated for seniors, and supported rental assistance for families struggling to make ends meet.

I have delivered results, and you can trust me to keep delivering: supporting public safety, working to reduce traffic, and taking a balanced approach to housing that gives Los Gatos maximum local control while delivering more affordable homes. I will continue to support our downtown and small businesses, protect our parks and environment, and communicate both ways─sharing information while always staying open to feedback, ideas, and concerns.

Serving on the Town Council has been the greatest honor of my professional life. I humbly ask for your vote.
