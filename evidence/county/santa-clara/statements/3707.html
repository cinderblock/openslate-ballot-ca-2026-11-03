Occupation: Parent & Technology Executive
Age: 44

Education and Qualifications:

My kids attended CUSD schools for 9 years. In 2024, the district transferred two respected teachers without adequate explanation. As parents, we organized, spoke up at the board meeting. But the board did not hold the administration accountable. Teachers have the most important job in the world. We owe them more, not less. This broke my trust in the district. I could not tolerate disrespect to teachers. We moved our kids to a private school. I'm running to help transform CUSD to be strongly values driven. So that no teacher feels disrespected and every family feels welcome.

I served four years on the Forest Hill PTA board, two as President. Under my leadership, we rebuilt community programs after COVID, maximized financial transparency, and simplified several processes.

I've run large organizations at Yahoo! and Netflix. And have co-founded OKhuman, which lets students disclose the human effort behind their writing. I bring years of operational experience, both in school parent bodies, and corporate organizations.

Our classrooms should mean less screen time and more time with teachers. We must keep student-teacher ratios low so teachers are able to recognize where every child is and help them grow and be challenged.

Endorsed by teachers.
