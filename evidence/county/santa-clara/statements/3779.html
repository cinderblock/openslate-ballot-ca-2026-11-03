Mountain View has been my home for 32 years. It's where I've been a long-term renter and first-time homeowner. It's where we raised our two kids. It's where I served our community on the city's Parks and Recreation Commission, Downtown Committee, Library Board, Environmental Planning Commission, and on a regional land use commission. My experience is broad and deep.

An engineer by training, I dive into analysis and listen to all voices to make thoughtful decisions. I'll bring this work ethic to the City Council.

I'm proud of our city and I believe we can make it even better by making smart choices. That means building affordable housing for our diverse community of families, seniors, and essential workers. It means expanding parks and tree canopy, especially in the neighborhoods that need them most. It means protecting the downtown we cherish and backing the small businesses that keep it thriving. It means confronting climate change today, with an eye on tomorrow. It means always maintaining a balanced budget.

Mountain View deserves thoughtful, experienced leadership. I've delivered that for decades and respectfully ask for your vote so I can continue to do so on the City Council.
