Occupation: Higher Education Administrator/Community Advocate

Education and Qualifications:

For over 20 years, I've dedicated myself to serving Gilroy through education and public service. During this time I've served on the Gilroy Unified School District Board, the Gilroy City Council, and the San José/Evergreen Community College Board.

On Council, I prioritized public safety, downtown revitalization, support for small business and responsible fiscal stewardship. I championed Gilroy's downtown pop-up park and lighting enhancements. I also established Farm Workers Day, helped open a school health clinic and advocated for School Resource Officers in our schools.

In 2025 I was honored to receive the Gilroy Police Officers' Association Stanley P. Delvin Award and to have earned the endorsement of the Gilroy Police Officers' Association and the IAFF Local 2805 Gilroy Firefighters Association during my service on Council.

If elected, I will bring forward solutions that benefit our community. I'll work collaboratively to improve public safety and safer neighborhoods; support measures that reduce red tape for small businesses; fight for transparency and accountability from local government, better city services and responsible growth. I respectfully ask for your vote to represent District 4. Gilroy deserves experienced leadership that listens, delivers results, represents your needs and puts our community first.
