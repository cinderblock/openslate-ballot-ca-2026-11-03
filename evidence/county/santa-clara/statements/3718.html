Occupation: Incumbent/Attorney

Education and Qualifications:

Mountain View Whisman is home to one of the most diverse student bodies in the Bay Area. Every child, whatever their starting point, deserves to be challenged, supported, and prepared for what comes next.

I have served this district since 2012 in many ways, including as Trustee through 2016, and again since 2022. The current board inherited real challenges from prior leadership, and we now have new leadership in place. Progress is real; the work has just started.

My commitment is straightforward: every student leaving 8th grade should be academically prepared to pursue a college-preparatory path—not because every student will, but because every student should be able to choose it. As a district and community we must commit to high expectations for all students.

Targeted support for our students cannot be viewed as remediation. It is the bridge to grade-level instruction. Serving our most vulnerable learners well and challenging every other student cannot be competing priorities. Both are imperative.

Every dollar must reach the classroom in the hands of teachers who believe in the intrinsic value of every child.

I bring a scientist's rigor and a lawyer's thoughtfulness to this work. I ask for your continued trust.
