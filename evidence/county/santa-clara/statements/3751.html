For 26 years, Indu Kadambi has called Los Altos Hills home and worked to protect the qualities that make it exceptional, natural beauty, rural character, strong neighborhoods, and thoughtful local leadership.

She brings a proven record of service as a Los Altos Hills County Fire District Commissioner, educator, small business owner, published author, PTA leader, and longtime school volunteer. Through her leadership with Hills 2000 and Friends of Los Altos Hills, Indu Kadambi has helped residents stay informed, engaged, and heard in decisions that shape the town's future.

With a master's degree in clinical psychology, she leads with empathy, sound judgment, and collaboration. Her experience reflects a deep commitment to public safety, fiscal responsibility, transparency, and accountable government.

Guided by an Indigenous proverb, "We do not inherit the Earth from our ancestors; we borrow it from our children," Indu believes we have a responsibility to preserve Los Altos Hills for today's residents and future generations. She is committed to protecting, preserving, and enriching the community we all value, for the families who live here today and those who will call Los Altos Hills home in the years ahead.
