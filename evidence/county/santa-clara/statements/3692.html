Occupation: Educational Advocate

Education and Qualifications:

As a graduate of LGSUHSD, I took advantage of our district's Middle College program to challenge myself academically, ultimately earning my degree from UCLA. Our schools have a proud tradition of preparing students for success in higher education, meaningful careers, and engaged lives; I am committed to carrying that legacy forward.

As an educational advocate, I have represented students and families navigating special education, disability accommodations, discrimination, and access to public services. My work has shown me both where our schools excel and where families sometimes struggle to access the support they need.

The strongest schools are built on partnerships between educators, students, and families. As your trustee, I will work to strengthen those partnerships, improve access to district resources, and ensure that every decision is guided by what is best for our students.

I am honored to be endorsed by both the Mayor of Saratoga, Chuck Page, and the Former Mayor of Monte Sereno, Liz Lawler.

I respectfully ask for your vote. (www.IshaanPrasad.org)
