Occupation: Program Specialist/Parent
Age: 42

Education and Qualifications:

Declining enrollment, aging infrastructure, and several fiscal challenges are facing the Cambrian School District. Letting go of qualified teachers, and school closures are things that negatively impact us all.

The Board's decisions are not just numbers to me─they affect the families we interact with every day. As the parent of three young children, I bring insight into the realities of raising family currently in this community, this economic climate, and these ever changing times.

With over a decade of experience facilitating Parenting Education groups, one of my greatest strengths is listening to the needs of the community with understanding and compassion. Working as a Behavior Therapist across several school districts has given me insight into how districts operate, and how funding cuts can be leveraged to minimize impact on students and teachers. It has also given me practice in advocating for parents and students who may have support needs that differ from the rest of the population.

In this role I will work to give more avenues for community input before decisions are made. I will increase communication with families in the district, and advocate for those who feel their concerns are not being heard.
