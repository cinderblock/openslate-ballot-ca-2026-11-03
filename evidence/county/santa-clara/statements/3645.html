As Chair of the Legislative Progressive Caucus, I'm proud to lead the fight to build social housing, establish universal healthcare, combat climate change, defend everyone's civil rights (especially LGBTQ+ families, trans folks, and POC), uplift our unhoused neighbors, support immigrant families, and root out corporate special interest money from politics.

Growing up in Milpitas and San José, I've seen too many friends and families priced out of the South Bay.

Corporations and their billionaire executives have squeezed every tax break out of us taxpayers while profiting from skyrocketing rents, home prices, and essential goods. I've been consistently leading the fight to tax the rich and fight against corporate greed.

Since day one, I have rejected all campaign contributions from corporations because I am accountable to you, not the corporate interests. Above all else, I believe in the integrity of our democracy—that the government should serve all people, not the rich and powerful.

I'm running for re-election to continue the fight against a fascist federal regime, fix the housing crisis, and rein in corporate greed.

Humbly, I ask for your vote to continue representing you.
