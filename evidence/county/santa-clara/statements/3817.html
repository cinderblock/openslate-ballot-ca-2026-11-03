It is an honor serving you on the Valley Water Board. I take your trust seriously and am proud to have fulfilled the promises I made when I last sought your vote.

You asked me to eliminate irresponsible projects that would raise water rates without sufficient benefits. I partnered with the community, nonprofits, and property owners, and ended the troubled Pacheco Dam project, saving billions of dollars.

You asked me to deliver accountability from District leadership. I worked with staff and the community to ensure that two six-figure executives accused of wrongdoing no longer serve the District.

You asked me to protect your tax dollars. I preserved funding for critical flood-control projects to keep our homes and communities safe.

Throughout my term, as circumstances shifted, I did not waver. I stood up for you, fought for you, and involved you in decisions about you.

The Water District is entering a new chapter. As your experienced, independent, and hard-working Director, I will deliver responsible, local and lower-cost solutions including water capture and recycling.

With your vote, I will continue to bring competence, transparency, and highest integrity to Valley Water to ensure affordable clean water today and in the future.
