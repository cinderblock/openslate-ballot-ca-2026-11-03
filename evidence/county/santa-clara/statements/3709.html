Occupation: Associate Finance Director

Education and Qualifications:

I am a proud product of California's public education system. From elementary school through my master's degree, my journey showed me the transformative power of accessible, quality public education. Now, as a parent, I want my son and every student in Campbell Union School District to have that same opportunity.

I will fight for our children to have the best education and support possible. I will ensure that the district is fiscally responsible and doesn't waste your tax dollars.

At Stanford University, I work with project managers on diverse construction projects, giving me experience in budgeting, long-range planning, and facilities management. As the Chair of Stanford's LBRE Engagement Council, I know how to be an inclusive, forward-thinking leader.

Public education faces several challenges, but with CUSD's homegrown leadership, we have a strong foundation. Kids learn best when they are supported, both in and out of the classroom. The goal is progress, not perfection. I want students to be inspired to be lifelong learners and not just learn for the sake of testing. I will put students first and ensure our schools empower and inspire every child to grow and succeed.
