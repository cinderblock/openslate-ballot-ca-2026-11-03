Occupation: Parent/Commissioner/Consultant

Education and Qualifications:

I am running for the Morgan Hill Unified School Board to ensure every student has access to safe, supportive schools and a high-quality education that prepares them for college, and/or a career, and life.

For more than 25 years, I have worked in technology, business development, public policy, and education advocacy. I bring experience managing budgets, building partnerships, and delivering results with accountability.

Education is personal to me. My wife has taught in public schools for 20+ years, and our sons attended Morgan Hill schools. As a classroom volunteer and member of the Sobrato High School Site Council, I have seen firsthand the value of how strong collaboration between parents, educators, and staff improves student outcomes.

As your trustee, I will focus on strong academics, transparent budgeting, and ensuring resources reach classrooms. I am proud to be endorsed by the Morgan Hill Federation of Teachers for my commitment to attracting and retaining excellent teachers, expanding college and career pathways, and strengthening investment in special education and student support services.

Building Better Schools Together means listening, partnering with all education stakeholders and keeping students at the center of every decision. I respectfully ask for your vote.
