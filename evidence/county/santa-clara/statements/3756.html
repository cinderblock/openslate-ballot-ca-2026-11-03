Occupation: Economic Analyst, Town Commissioner

Education and Qualifications:

I grew up in Los Gatos. I was born in Good Samaritan Hospital and attended Los Gatos public schools. I love this town. For the last six years, I have dedicated thousands of unpaid hours to our community as a volunteer.

As a former Planning Commissioner, I understand the gravity of our housing situation. I served as a leading advocate for the certified Housing Element that ended the wave of Builder's Remedy applications in Los Gatos, restoring local control.

Currently, I serve on the Complete Streets and Transportation Commission, where I designed and evaluated policies to reduce our school and beach traffic. I recently worked with VTA to design a program that would provide all Los Gatos High School students and staff with free transit passes. As the founder and president of the Los Gatos Anti-Racism Coalition, a volunteer nonprofit organization, I launched a homelessness prevention program that has provided more than 150 families at risk of eviction with over $100,000 of rental assistance to help them stay housed.

Los Gatos has serious challenges ahead—we need forward-thinking, proactive leadership. Those raised here and those who work here should be able to afford to live here. If elected to our Town Council, I will work to build a community where everyone feels represented, safe, and proud to live in Los Gatos.
