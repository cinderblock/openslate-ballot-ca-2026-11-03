For the past year, it has been my privilege to serve on the Berryessa Union School District Board of Trustees. During a year of significant transition, I listened to families, responded to community concerns, and helped guide thoughtful decisions for our students and staff. I am proud of the progress we've made, including advancing campus improvements, transforming the Toyon and Laneview campuses to serve our community, and beginning the development of a long-term strategic plan.

This district has shaped my life as a student, parent, and now Trustee. My years as a PTA leader and School Site Council member reinforced my belief that schools thrive when families, educators and the community work together.

I bring more than 20 years of leadership and organizational strategy experience. Today, I serve on the Board's strategic planning subcommittee, helping lead a community-wide effort to shape our district's vision for the next seven to ten years.

As your Trustee, I will continue working collaboratively, making thoughtful, fiscally responsible decisions, and keeping students at the center of every decision.

It would be an honor to earn your vote and continue serving our Berryessa community.
