Occupation: Engineer/City Commissioner

Education and Qualifications:

I am Anuradha Sankaran, a Milpitas resident, an engineer with 20 years of technology experience, City Commissioner, education volunteer, and parent of a 2026 MUSD graduate, seeking to serve on the MUSD Governing Board.

Throughout my career, I have led projects, managed resources, and developed practical solutions. I will bring accountability and sound decision-making to MUSD governance.

My service includes two years on Rancho Middle School's Community Board Advisory Council, part of the School Site Council; two years as Fundraising Chair for the Milpitas High School PTSA; and three years organizing educational and cultural programs at a nonprofit community language school. As an Environmental and Sustainability Commissioner, I support the Commission's environmental awareness efforts.

My priority is ensuring every student has opportunities to pursue college and career pathways and succeed in a technology-driven world, while closing achievement gaps and strengthening readiness for admission to competitive colleges. Drawing on my STEM experience, I will promote partnerships with colleges and technology companies to expand hands-on learning, internships, digital skills, and responsible artificial intelligence education. I will champion safe, inclusive schools, student mental health, qualified teachers, and responsible budgeting that protects educational quality. I respectfully ask for your vote.
