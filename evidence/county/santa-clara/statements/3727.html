Occupation: Governing Board Member, Sunnyvale School District

Education and Qualifications:

I am grateful to have served the students and community of the Sunnyvale School District as a School Board member for the last ten years, including two years as Board President.

As a lifelong resident who attended Cumberland, Sunnyvale Middle, and Fremont High School, and as a parent in the district, my commitment to our community is deeply-rooted.

I will continue to fight for our youth and ensure we are actively addressing the needs of all of our students.

As a Board Member, I have successfully advocated for programs and resources that prepare our students for high school, college, and beyond. I have championed our children's needs locally, as well as at the State and Federal levels.

I am energized by our progress and the direction we are headed. If re-elected, I will remain fiercely committed to eliminating the persistent achievement gaps, ensuring fiscal responsibility, and empowering family/parent engagement. Together, we can ensure every student's well-being and academic success.

I would be honored to receive your support and your vote.
