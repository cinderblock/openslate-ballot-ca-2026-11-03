Education and Qualifications:

I'm honored to have served as your Ward 1 Director on the Midpeninsula Regional Open Space District Board since 2023, representing Los Gatos, Saratoga, Monte Sereno, parts of Cupertino, and the open lands south to Mt. Umunhum and Loma Prieta. In this time, Ward 1 has made significant progress on Measure AA projects—restoration and public access at Bear Creek Redwoods Open Space Preserve and advancing planning and design for the Highway 17 wildlife and trail crossings.

I advocate for high-quality public access, including accessible trails, and for projects that pair ecological restoration with great outdoor experiences. As a frequent volunteer for land and trail stewardship, I work directly alongside Midpen staff to understand our lands and priorities. Having hiked and/or ridden every trail in the District, I bring firsthand knowledge of the preserves to Board discussions.

I'm committed to fulfilling Measure AA's "Old Trees, New Trails" vision: Protecting and restoring open space, and connecting people to nature. I ask for your vote to continue this work.
