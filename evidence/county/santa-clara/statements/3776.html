Occupation: High School Teacher/Mother/Engineer
Age: 43

Education and Qualifications:

As a 43-year-old mom, science teacher, and engineer, I am running for City Council because I care about Mountain View. I love living here and am eager to make the city safer, greener, and more resilient in the face of climate change. My engineering background has equipped me with analytical tools needed to solve complex urban challenges. My daily work in the classroom keeps me grounded in the immediate needs of our diverse families and youth. Mountain View is a vibrant, growing community, and keeping it accessible and livable requires proactive, dedicated leadership.

I don't just advocate for our community; I take action to build it. I created the Pollinator Habitat in Cuesta Park and reinstated the Varsity Park Neighborhood Association. I will prioritize expanding safe infrastructure for our kids, building on our volunteer-led Bike Bus initiative at Bubb School, so all our residents can travel safely. My commitment is centered on building a stronger, more connected city for everyone. I will advocate for smart growth that balances urgent housing needs with environmental stewardship, fostering transparent governance responsive to every neighborhood. I am dedicated to keeping Mountain View safe, sustainable, and inclusive for every resident.

I would be honored to earn your vote.
