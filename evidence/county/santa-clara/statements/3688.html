Occupation: Governing Board Member

Education and Qualifications:

For seven years, I have served on the Fremont Union High School District Board of Trustees, including as Board President and Vice President. Working collaboratively with fellow trustees, district staff, teachers, students, families, and community partners, I have helped strengthen our comprehensive high schools by supporting academic excellence, advancing student wellness, elevating student voice, and building an inclusive school community where every student has the opportunity to thrive.

As a parent who came to the U.S. with a belief in the promise of public education, I understand the transformative power of schools and our responsibility to serve every student. My experiences as a parent, educator, and community leader have strengthened my commitment to thoughtful governance, transparent communication, and collaborative leadership. While FUHSD continues to be recognized as a high-achieving district, we must continue working to close opportunity gaps and ensure every student experiences a sense of belonging, growth, and success.

In my next term, I will focus on student-centered decision-making, student wellness, college and career readiness, and strong community partnerships while ensuring every student develops the critical thinking, collaboration, problem-solving, and social-emotional skills needed for future success.
