Occupation: Teacher

Education and Qualifications:

As a classroom teacher, I see every day how important strong schools are to our children, families, and community. I understand the needs of students, the dedication of teachers, and the partnership families want with their schools.

My years in special education and working with multilingual students have reinforced a simple belief that every student is capable of growth and achievement. When schools combine high expectations with the right support, students of every background and ability can thrive.

I am running to help ensure that every student has a safe, supportive, and high-quality learning environment. I believe in strong academic foundations, student well-being, transparent communication, and responsible use of district resources. Our schools should prepare children not only to succeed in the classroom, but also to grow with confidence and respect for others.

School board decisions shape what happens in our classrooms every day. I will bring the perspective of someone who understands those realities because I have lived them as an educator.
