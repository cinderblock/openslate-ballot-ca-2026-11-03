Water bills keep rising while Valley Water wasted millions on risky projects, cost overruns and golden parachutes. Meanwhile, our neighborhoods still live with neglected creeks, flooding, and illegal dumping. I am running to be your voice and hold Valley Water accountable to the working families of East San José.

As District Representative for State Senator Dave Cortese, I have spent years helping residents cut through government red tape and navigate complex systems to get results. As a community organizer, I coordinated the distribution of protective equipment during the COVID-19 pandemic, registered new voters, and helped low-income families access food, housing, and essential benefits they needed most. I host a public affairs program on Net Viet TV, where I connect Vietnamese-speaking communities with their government.

My wife and I have been married 21 years and raised our son and daughter here. I spent years working warehouse night shifts. I know what every increase on a water bill means to a working family.

As your Director, I will demand an independent audit before any rate increase, end excessive executive payouts, and make Valley Water clean up and protect our neighborhood creeks.

Safe, clean, affordable water. Honest, accountable leadership. I would be honored to earn your vote.
