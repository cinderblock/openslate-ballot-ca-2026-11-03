For more than 25 years, I have proudly called Sunnyvale home and have been honored to serve our community as a volunteer, public servant, and your District 3 Councilmember. My priorities have always been keeping our neighborhoods safe, managing taxpayer dollars responsibly, improving quality of life, and accountable government.

I currently serve as Chair of the Valley Water Commission and Chair of the Finance and Administration Committee of Silicon Valley Clean Energy. I represent Sunnyvale on the Information Technology Committee of the National League of Cities, helping shape policy discussions on AI, cybersecurity and emerging technologies in local government.

I founded Sunnyvale Youth Advisory to inspire students through community service and civic engagement.

In 2021, I was honored to be named Sunnyvale's Distinguished Resident.

As a technology executive and entrepreneur who has led four startup companies, with master's degrees from Virginia Tech and Stanford University, I bring leadership to solving Sunnyvale's challenges.

I will continue promoting transparency, data-driven decision-making, responsible fiscal management, and greater resident engagement.

I respectfully ask for your vote so we can continue building a safe, fiscally strong, innovative, and exceptional Sunnyvale together.
