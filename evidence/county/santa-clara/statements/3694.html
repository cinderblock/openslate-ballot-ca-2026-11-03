Occupation: MVLA Governing Board Member

Education and Qualifications:

I am running for reelection to ensure every student is seen, supported, and set up to succeed.

As a disabled Anglo-Burmese immigrant and parent of two, I experienced firsthand the opportunities and obstacles in education and strongly believe that all students—from our highest-achieving to those facing challenges—deserve an excellent education that prepares them for college, careers, and lifelong success.

After serving MVLA for the past four years, and this year as Board President, I would be honored to continue my focus on academic excellence and opportunities for all, connection and belonging, and a transparent, fiscally responsible Board.

During my tenure on the Board, I have ensured academic excellence for all by expanding AP access, career technical education pathways, dual enrollment, inclusive classrooms for students with disabilities, and increasing English Learners' performance. I supported connection and belonging with new student wellness programs. I strengthened transparency through regular community engagement and fiscal responsibility by delivering classrooms, gyms, and Student Services facilities on budget.

I will build on my Board experience and professional and community leadership to continue delivering results for all students and ensure MVLA remains a world-class school district.

I respectfully ask for your vote.
