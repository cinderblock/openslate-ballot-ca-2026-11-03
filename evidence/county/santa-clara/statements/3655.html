I serve as your San José Evergreen Community College District Trustee for Area 2. I ask for your vote to continue building a district where accountability and student success come first.

As a mother and wife in a blended family of seven, married for 28 years, I understand the power of opportunity. My family includes union trade workers, an educator, and my youngest, preparing to become an attorney. This guides me: never give up, invest in people, and be part of the solution.

I championed middle school college access, K-12 and nonprofit partnerships, transfer pathways, workforce education and apprenticeships, and college opportunities for students in local correctional facilities. I supported the Milpitas College expansion, Career Education Complex, nursing and automotive programs, and high tech partnerships connecting students to Silicon Valley careers.

As Chair of the Budget, Finance and Audit Committee, I fought for transparency, balanced budgets, responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars, a strong district credit rating, and timely union agreement ratification. I also helped advance a permanent Chancellor and executive team focused on results.

My record reflects action through policymaking, tough questions, fiscal stewardship, and student focused decisions. I am ready to continue the work with your support.
