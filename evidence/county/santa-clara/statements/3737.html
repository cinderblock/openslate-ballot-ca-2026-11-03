Occupation: Civil Engineer

Education and Qualifications:

I am a California professional engineer with degrees from UC Davis and San Jose State University.

I bring more than 50 years of engineering design experience serving communities throughout the Bay Area.

I am running for Cupertino City Council because I believe our city government has lost sight of common-sense solutions to the issues facing Cupertino.

Government authority is held in trust for the people and must remain accountable to the residents it serves.

After attending planning and council hearings, I have observed residents dismissed when expressing concerns about development in their neighborhoods.

I believe state housing mandates are steadily reducing local control and weakening zoning protections that residents have relied upon for decades.

I oppose policies that place growth ahead of neighborhood protections, public safety, and property rights. I will fight for common sense, local control, and respect for Cupertino residents.
