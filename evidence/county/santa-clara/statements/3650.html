Occupation: Governing Board Member, Santa Clara Board of Education

Education and Qualifications:

Elected in 2022 to the Santa Clara County Board of Education, it has been a privilege to serve my community. I respectfully ask for your vote as I seek re-election to continue the important work of improving student outcomes, empowering educators, and ensuring accountability and fiscal responsibility at every level.

Research is clear: early learning bridges equity gaps and leads to stronger long-term academic success. I support strong fiscal oversight of early childhood education and building upon the Board's Strong Start Initiative to ensure every child begins school ready to learn.

Preparing students for the future remains my priority. Every student deserves a quality education to be college and/or career ready when they graduate, with access to pathways that reflect diverse learning styles, prioritizes health and wellness, and a changing economy. Expanding high-quality career technical education and workforce opportunities is essential.

I am proud to be endorsed by the California Teachers Association, reflecting my commitment to attracting, supporting, and retaining outstanding educators. When we invest in educators, we improve outcomes for students.

With a focus on sound stewardship, I will continue working to ensure every public dollar directly benefits students and classrooms.

I respectfully ask for your vote. For more info, visit voteraeenalari.com.
