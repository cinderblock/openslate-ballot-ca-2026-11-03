Occupation: Council Member

Education and Qualifications:

Los Gatos is unlike anywhere else—a small town with its own charm. Preserving that has been my work as your Council Member.

I was raised here and graduated from Los Gatos High School. For 40 years, I owned a local insurance agency advising families and businesses. For 12 years, I have been serving the town as Mayor, Vice Mayor, Planning Commissioner, and Council Member.

I voted to protect our single-family residential zones, against housing numbers that exceed State mandates, and against the first Builder's Remedy project approved in our small town.

I have consistently supported our Police Department and worked to balance the town's budget without raising taxes, while protecting 911 response times and streets without potholes. I support our Senior Services Roadmap and will work to fund adult day care, senior transportation, and nutrition programs. Residents are clear about what comes next: traffic relief, safer neighborhoods, and holding Builder's Remedy projects to every limit the law allows.

I have worked to earn your trust—the work is not finished.
I ask for your vote to finish it.
