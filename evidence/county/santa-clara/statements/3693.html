Occupation: Developmental Psychologist/Parent Volunteer
Age: 54

Education and Qualifications:

Nearly 20 years ago, my family chose the Saratoga/Monte Sereno neighborhood because of its exceptional schools and community. Every year since has reinforced that we made the right decision. My son, who has a developmental disability, received the support he needed to thrive and is now a college freshman. My daughter is currently a high school sophomore, giving me firsthand experience with the opportunities and challenges facing students and families today.

I am a developmental psychologist in the Neonatal Intensive Care Unit at a hospital, where I support families facing medical, financial, and social challenges. My doctoral research focused on children with developmental disabilities, their families, and the impact of public policy.

Serving our schools has always been my way of giving back to the community that has given my family so much. For more than 14 years, I have volunteered in leadership roles, including PTA President at Redwood MS, President of the Saratoga HS School Site Council, and honored as Los Gatos-Saratoga Union High School District's 2026 Volunteer of the Year.

As your Trustee, I will listen, lead with integrity, and make thoughtful decisions while strengthening social-emotional learning, fiscal responsibility, and strong values that make our district a place we are all proud to call home.
