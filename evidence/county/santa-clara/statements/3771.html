Occupation: Chief Executive Officer

Education and Qualifications:

I am running for City Council, District D because I would like to "give back" to the city in which I have lived since 1994.

I believe that the process of city government begins with the community's voice; thus, I will first engage in learning more about current issues facing working families, seniors, and young people. I will make decisions rooted in equity, common sense, city government, and the community's input. I will use my experience in government to help Morgan Hill stay safe, welcoming, and sustainable for all of us.

My experience as an executive manager with the County's Public Health Department and Community Health Partnership translates directly to the work of the City Council. Morgan Hill is facing big questions about traffic and safety, housing and growth, and how we fund and protect the services that keep our city livable. I know how to listen to different perspectives, work across institutions, and craft practical solutions instead of slogans. I also understand that health is about more than clinics and hospitals—it is about safe streets, clean and welcoming public spaces, affordable homes, and a sense of belonging and fairness in how decisions are made.
