Santa Clara and San Jose voters: It has been an honor to serve as your College Trustee. I ask for your vote so I may continue safeguarding the life-changing educational opportunities Mission College and West Valley College provide.

Careful fiscal stewardship and unwavering commitment to equity and social justice enable our colleges to expand access and student success. We provide outstanding academics, free tuition, parking, health services, childcare, and meals; partner with local high schools on dual enrollment; and create innovative internships and career pathways. We welcome students at every stage of life and work.

California public education—and community college in particular—transformed my life. Raised in West San Jose, I earned degrees from De Anza College and San Jose State University before attending UC San Francisco School of Law. I have lived in Santa Clara for more than 30 years where we have raised three children, who attended public schools. As a local business owner and instructor of continuing education and law courses, I understand what students, educators and employers need to thrive.

My priorities remain clear: remove barriers to access, protect educational quality and make college success attainable for every student and family. Learn more at annekepner.org.
