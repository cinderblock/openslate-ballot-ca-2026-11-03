Decades working in family-owned businesses have given me a strong work ethic, and my experience in the nonprofit sector has taught me that every problem has a solution if you're creative enough. Both of those traits will be essential for City Council as we secure Campbell's financial future.

Fresh perspectives will enable us to responsibly adapt the state's housing mandates to fit our existing small-town charm. New ideas are necessary to make sure we have the revenue not just to maintain our excellent community service programs, but also to fund ambitious projects like the new aquatics center. Let's keep Campbell dollars in Campbell by attracting new businesses and better supporting the needs of those already here. This way we can sustainably increase our revenue base without raising tax rates.

If elected, I will work to ensure that our city remains the welcoming environment to raise a family that we all enjoy. I am committed to prioritizing public safety, championing our small business community, and transparent, principled government that puts the needs of residents first.

I grew up in this town learning how to build as a tradesman. Campbell, let's see what we can build together.
