As a hostage negotiator and sexual assault detective, I had no choice but to get the job done in someone's hour of crisis. But right now, the City Hall status quo isn't getting the job done for our families on housing, homelessness, and crime.

This is personal. I was raised in San Jose, and since I was a kid, I've seen friends move away as housing became unaffordable. I've seen our homelessness crisis get worse over the decades, despite billions of dollars being spent to "fix" it. And I've seen special interests stand in the way of solutions.

Now, I'm raising my daughters here. That's why I'm running for City Council. Everyone deserves the opportunity to stay in the city they grew up in, in a house of their own. They deserve parks and streets that are safe to walk at night, and real economic opportunity.

Mayor Matt Mahan has made real progress, but he needs a partner who will stand up to the status quo and get tough jobs done. I'm proud to have his endorsement alongside the Mercury News, Planned Parenthood, San Jose police officers, Congressman Sam Liccardo, and thousands of neighbors. Together, we'll make sure San Jose gets the job done for you.
