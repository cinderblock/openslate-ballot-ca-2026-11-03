Occupation: Director of Operations

Education and Qualifications:

I am an environmental scientist with a specialty in wildlife and marine science, and I am dedicated to preserving, protecting, restoring, and acquiring public open space land for all to enjoy.

I hold a Master's degree in Environmental Science with a focus in Marine Ecology from the University of Sydney, a Bachelor's degree in Law and Earth Sciences (Geology) from UC Santa Cruz, and studied Economics and Real Estate at De Anza College.

I have volunteered in our community for many years, including more than a decade as a docent at the Monterey Bay Aquarium, the Marine Science Institute, Ano Nuevo State Park, The Marine Mammal Center, and the California Academy of Sciences. I currently volunteer with a regional youth group.

I formerly lived on land that is now part of the Sierra Azul Open Space Preserve and supported its sale for permanent public preservation. The District stewards approximately 1,600 acres of San Francisco Bay frontage and Pacific Ocean access. My training in marine and coastal ecology will help ensure that board decisions are guided by science. As a disabled regular hiker of our preserves, I will work to make open spaces more accessible to everyone.
