Occupation: Educator, Coach, Administrator and Parent

Education and Qualifications:

I champion Public Education. Father of three Morgan Hill Unified School District graduates, forty years of MHUSD service as a Teacher, Athletic Coach, Principal, Director, Leadership Coach, and husband to a career teacher; education is my passion. Board Trustee is my next opportunity to serve.

I have the confidence of teachers, public service and community leaders. I believe in honesty, transparency and engaging our community. My loyalty is to students, the law and extends with mutual respect to those with the common mission of making our schools distinguished. My priorities include grade level reading by third grade, math proficiency by fifth, college and career readiness by graduation, and fiscal responsibility to sustain robust academic and extracurricular programs.

Twice recognized by the California School Boards Association for excellence in strategic planning, I led Britton as the only district secondary school to surpass the 800 API distinguished school mark. I have authored the district's LCAP; I know accountability, compliance, data analysis and program evaluation for return on investment.

I know our schools and the responsibilities of the Governing Board. I have provided training and presented to past Boards many times. I will do all I can to ensure effective leadership. In that, you can trust. Thank you for your vote!
