Occupation: Governing Board Member/Educator

Education and Qualifications:

As Vice-President of the Milpitas Unified School District Board, I am honored to serve our students, families, and community. A proud Milpitas resident for nearly 20 years, I have provided unwavering leadership as Board President, three-term Vice-President, and two-term Board Clerk. My civic commitment extends across Milpitas as past president of both the Milpitas Chamber of Commerce and Milpitas Rotary Club.

I deeply value public education, and our vital work is far from finished. As a mother of two current Milpitas High School students and an MHS graduate now at UC Santa Cruz, my dedication is personal. As a credentialed educator who has taught in our local classrooms and served on the PTA, School Site Council, and Community Board Advisory Council, I bring firsthand insight into classroom needs. I earned my Bachelor's Degree in Law & Society from UC Santa Barbara.

As a local real estate and mortgage business owner, I apply sharp financial management and entrepreneurial problem-solving to protect taxpayer dollars and maximize resources for our schools. My top priorities are advancing student achievement, expanding teacher recruitment and retention, enhancing campus safety, and delivering transparent fiscal oversight.
