Occupation: Educator/Engineer/Executive

Education and Qualifications:

Cupertino has been my home for 17 years and the longest I have lived anywhere in one place. My wife and I are proud to raise our two sons here. As a refugee, I am deeply grateful to call Cupertino home.

A licensed Professional Engineer with an MBA from Columbia University, my 30-year career spans executive leadership in engineering, finance, and public affairs. My service to residents and the community at large includes serving as Cupertino Chamber of Commerce President and Pack 492 Cubmaster, and teaching engineering as an adjunct professor at San José State University for nearly two decades.

Here's why I'm running: Cupertino faces a critical financial and development crossroads. We need transparent, accountable leadership that puts residents first. I know firsthand how to secure infrastructure, manage multimillion-dollar budgets, and unite diverse leaders to solve shared challenges. I will champion policies that put residents first by creating housing for every stage of life, upgrading our water system, roads and facilities, and strengthening our local economy.

I am endorsed by Congressman Sam Liccardo, Assemblymembers Patrick Ahrens and Alex Lee, Supervisor Margaret Abe-Koga, Assessor Neysa Fligor, Councilmember and former Mayor Sheila Mohan, and ten other former Cupertino Mayors.

I respectfully ask for your vote.
