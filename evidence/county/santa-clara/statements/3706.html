Occupation: Sr. Human Resources Manager
Age: 40

Education and Qualifications:

I am running to serve a full term on the Cambrian School District Board of Trustees because I believe every child deserves an education that nurtures both their learning and their well-being. Our students thrive when they feel safe, supported, and seen, and I am committed to ensuring our district continues to prioritize high-quality academics alongside strong mental and emotional health resources.

As an immigrant and the youngest of seven children, I've experienced firsthand how transformative a great education can be. That journey eventually led me to Santa Clara University and brought me to the Bay Area 22 years ago—a community my family is proud to call home.

My wife and I have three children. One currently attends a Cambrian school, and our older two recently graduated from Price Middle School. Our youngest daughter has moderate autism, and through her we've developed a deep, personal commitment to advocating for students with special needs. Living with ADHD myself, I also understand how essential it is for schools to support children facing mental health and neurodiversity challenges.

If elected, my goal is to continue working closely with parents, teachers, and administrators to create thoughtful, sensible policies that help every child feel included, capable, and supported.
