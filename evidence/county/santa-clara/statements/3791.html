Occupation: Trustee, East Side Union High School District

Education and Qualifications:

I am Van Le, a refugee, small business owner, and four-term East Side Union High School District Trustee. For more than 40 years, I have served San José families, schools, and neighborhoods. As Board President, I helped reduce district costs by more than $1 million annually, protected essential services, support hiring teachers, and staff, and strengthened public oversight. I founded the Children's Moon Festival in 1992 and have worked to support homeless students, immigrant families, and survivors of domestic violence.

My experience in public service and the private sector has taught me that effective leadership means listening, bringing people together, making fiscally responsible decisions, and delivering results with transparency and accountability.

District 7 deserves leadership that is present, responsive, and accountable. I will work for safer neighborhoods through smart police deployment, crime prevention, and emergency response. I will fight illegal dumping and blight, improve streets and parks, support small businesses, expand opportunities for youth, help seniors access services, and protect neighborhood character while supporting housing choices that working families can afford.

I will lead with integrity, fiscal responsibility, and respect for the community. Together, we can build a safer, strong District 7. I respectfully ask for your vote—www.vanlefordistrict7.com
