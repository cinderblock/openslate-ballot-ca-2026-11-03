Age: 52

Education and Qualifications:

Service has always been at the heart of my life in Los Gatos. I've raised my daughters here and served the community as a Scout leader, youth sports coach, school volunteer, and mentor to young women. After earning my MBA and spending a decade in technology, I chose a life in public service. I was appointed as Chair of the County Consumer Advisory Commission and served on the Town Bicycle and Pedestrian Advisory Commission. For three years, I served as a Policy Aide to a County Supervisor, working on the County budget and operations. Today, I am a Senior Management Analyst with California Children's Services, a program for children with serious medical conditions.

I'm running for Town Council because I want to put that experience to work for the community I love. That means safeguarding taxpayer dollars, protecting the historic neighborhoods and natural beauty that make Los Gatos special, and supporting public safety and local businesses.

I will listen, do my homework, seek different perspectives, and bring people together to find practical solutions. I am a problem-solver and someone who shows up, listens, and gets to work.

I would be honored to earn your vote and serve you on the Los Gatos Town Council.
