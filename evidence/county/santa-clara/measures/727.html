CITY ATTORNEY'S IMPARTIAL ANALYSIS OF MEASURE F

Measure "F" was placed on the ballot by a unanimous vote of the Mountain View City Council. This measure asks the voters to approve an ordinance authorizing the City Council to increase the Transient Occupancy Tax to a maximum rate of 15%. Measure "F" must be approved by a majority of voters.

The City established a Transient Occupancy Tax in 1967. Chapter 33 of the Mountain View City Code requires customers who stay in a hotel in the City for less than thirty (30) days to pay a Transient Occupancy Tax, also known as hotel tax. The operators of these lodgings collect this tax as part of the room bills and pay the tax to the city. A hotel includes any hotel, inn, tourist home or house, motel or similar lodging. This hotel tax also applies to short-term rentals.

The voters last approved an increase to the hotel tax in 1991. The current Transient Occupancy Tax rate is 10%. The majority of the cities in Santa Clara County have Transient Occupancy Taxes with tax rates ranging between 12% and 15.5%

All revenue generated by this tax would be placed in the City's general fund and could be used for any general governmental purpose. Examples of projects performed for general governmental purpose include, but are not limited to, repairing potholes, streets and sidewalks, improving police, fire and 911 emergency response services, supporting affordable housing, adding new parks and providing general government services.

Based on historical Transient Occupancy Tax revenues, the city estimates each 1% increase in Transient Occupancy Tax could generate an additional $1,100,000.00 in funding revenue for general governmental purposes each year.

A "yes" vote would amend the Mountain View City Code to authorize the City Council to increase the Transient Occupancy Tax rate up to a maximum of 15%. A "no" vote would reject the proposed ordinance, and the Transient Occupancy Tax rate would remain at 10%.

The above statement is an impartial analysis of Measure F. If you desire a copy of the ordinance or measure, please call the City Clerk's office at (650) 903-6304 and a copy will be mailed at no cost to you.

ARGUMENT IN FAVOR OF MEASURE F

Vote YES on Measure F!

The Mountain View City Council voted unanimously to place Measure F on the ballot.

If approved by Mountain View voters, Measure F allows the Mountain View City Council to increase the Transient Occupancy Tax (TOT), which is paid by hotel and other short-term rental guests, from 10% up to 15%. Measure F requires annual independent audits.

Mountain View's 10% TOT rate is lower than rates in most Santa Clara County cities. If approved by voters, the maximum allowable TOT rate would become comparable to many neighboring jurisdictions. The TOT rate was last amended in 1991, increasing from 8% to 10%.

At the maximum 15% rate, the increase can generate approximately $5.5 million per year for essential City infrastructure and services, including fixing potholes, repairing streets and sidewalks, expanding library hours, enhancing emergency response services, supporting affordable housing, and adding new parks.

If voters approve Measure F, the City Council will consider implementing any new TOT rate, not to exceed the 15% cap, at a public meeting after the election.

Please join the Mountain View City Council, the League of Women Voters of the Los Altos-Mountain View Area, and neighborhood and community leaders in voting YES on Measure F!
